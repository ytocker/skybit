# Skull-King PILLARS — NEW 10 (P11–P20) Brainstorm

BRAINSTORM MODE — concept *directions* only, NO full renders this round. Theses +
draft RECIPEs (token lists, focal first) + per-column knobs. All ten are plain
stacked totems: **WITH_SKEWER = False for every P11–P20** (no skewers in this set).

The existing pool (P1–P10) is now visibly repetitive in the routes figure, so this
doubles it. Per the `distinct-design-variants` skill, each new column is a UNIQUE
bottom-up composition — a different idea of *what the totem is* — not P1–P10 with a
swapped ornament or lean. They differ from each other AND from P1–P10 on: skull
FAMILY mix, COLLAR on/off, LEAN, ornament CHOICE + PLACEMENT, and focal family.

Token legend (engine grammar): `crown:0-5` royal bare relic (crown:2 = heart/lit
focal type) · `palm:0-5` ornamented cradled (palm:4 = forehead gem) · `r9:0` unlit /
`r9:1` lit earlier-round crown · `new:` simple-skull, antler-stag, sabertooth-maw,
longjaw-relic, cyclops-brow, keyhole-relic, cracked-half, flat-slab · `classic:`
round-cap, square-jaw, broad-zygo, egg-dome, gaunt-hollow, calvaria, flat-brow-robust,
child-skull · `orn:` bead_white, bead_gold, bead_cyan, bead_darkblue, gem_thirdeye,
ornament_necklace. Knobs: `COLLAR` (gold seam beads on/off), `LEAN` (−0.42..+0.42).

**Mandate parts** — must FEATURE in the new set (none of P1–P10 use these):
`classic:child-skull`, `orn:bead_darkblue`, `r9:0` (unlit crown).
**Under-used** (once in P1–P10), lean on them: `palm:3`, `classic:broad-zygo`,
`orn:gem_thirdeye`, `orn:ornament_necklace`, `new:cyclops-brow`,
`new:sabertooth-maw`, `new:longjaw-relic`, `new:cracked-half`.

## P11 — `runt-cairn-taper`
**Thesis:** An *inverted nursery* — a fat broad-cheeked base that shrinks course by
course up to a tiny child-skull crown, so the blackout is a bottom-heavy ziggurat
that pinches to a nub (the opposite taper to P4's gaunt spire, and the only column
that ends on `child-skull`). All-classic, no metal, COLLAR off — pure bone masonry.
**Word-thumbnail:** wide ▇ → ▆ → ▅ → ▂ pinch.
- **RECIPE (focal→far):** `classic:broad-zygo` (FOCAL, lit) → `classic:square-jaw`
  → `classic:round-cap` → `orn:bead_darkblue` → `classic:child-skull`
- **COLLAR:** OFF · **LEAN:** 0 (upright; the taper, not a lean, is the read)
- **Showcases:** `classic:child-skull` (focal nursery payoff at the far nub),
  `orn:bead_darkblue` (lone cold pip as the only ornament), `classic:broad-zygo`
  (wide focal base).

## P12 — `thirdeye-watchtower`
**Thesis:** The *all-seeing* column — three faceted third-eye GEMS seated as their
own keystone tiers between cyclops single-socket skulls, so the silhouette is a
vertical ladder of glinting eyes: skull-eye, gem, skull-eye, gem. Heaviest
`gem_thirdeye` use in the whole pool; reads as a row of lit lenses, not skulls.
**Word-thumbnail:** ⊙ ◆ ⊙ ◆ ⊙ stacked.
- **RECIPE (focal→far):** `new:cyclops-brow` (FOCAL, lit) → `orn:gem_thirdeye`
  → `classic:calvaria` → `orn:gem_thirdeye` → `new:cyclops-brow` → `orn:gem_thirdeye`
- **COLLAR:** OFF (gems own the glints; a bead collar would muddy them)
- **LEAN:** 0
- **Showcases:** `orn:gem_thirdeye` (×3 as keystone tiers — the defining motif),
  `new:cyclops-brow` (the rhyming eye-sockets).

## P13 — `lopsided-fang-lean`
**Thesis:** A *snarling jaw-stack tilted hard left* — sabertooth maws and longjaw
relics whose fangs all hang the same way down a strong NEGATIVE lean, so the column
looks like it's lunging/sliding off its own base. The set's only negative-lean
column and the only fang-heavy *plain* totem (P9's fangs were skewered).
**Word-thumbnail:** ◣ leaning fangs cascading down-left.
- **RECIPE (focal→far):** `new:sabertooth-maw` (FOCAL, lit) → `new:longjaw-relic`
  → `new:sabertooth-maw` → `new:longjaw-relic` → `classic:gaunt-hollow`
- **COLLAR:** ON (gold seam beads strap the off-axis tiers like a holding strap) ·
  **LEAN:** −0.34 (negative — leans opposite to P5's +0.42) · **MARGIN_R:** 2.4
  (seat the focal off the gap edge so the sabre fangs clear the rim)
- **Showcases:** `new:sabertooth-maw` (×2) + `new:longjaw-relic` (×2, plain,
  un-skewered), negative LEAN, COLLAR-on fang strap.

## P14 — `child-relic-shrine`
**Thesis:** A *small reliquary shrine* — a child-skull cradled and venerated at the
gap (focal), with a single forehead-gem palm relic as a guardian above and an unlit
r9 crown standing in vigil; a dark-blue votive bead marks the subject. The
tender/devotional read: tiny focal, larger protectors. Mixed family, COLLAR on.
**Word-thumbnail:** ● tiny → · bead → ◉ gem-palm → ○ unlit crown.
- **RECIPE (focal→far):** `classic:child-skull` (FOCAL, lit) → `orn:bead_darkblue`
  → `palm:4` → `r9:0` → `palm:3`
- **COLLAR:** ON (warm gold seam beads frame the shrine)
- **LEAN:** 0
- **Showcases:** `classic:child-skull` AS the focal (distinct from P11's far-end use),
  `r9:0` (unlit crown), `orn:bead_darkblue` (votive accent), `palm:3`.
- *AD revision:* dropped the draped `ornament_necklace` (it twinned the gold-ringed
  halo with P19, the warlord that owns that drape) for a single dark-blue votive bead;
  P14's read now rests on the child-skull focal + size-shrine idea alone.

## P15 — `darkblue-bone-rosary`
**Thesis:** A *cold rosary* of bare classic bone domes threaded on dark-blue beads —
every seam carries a `bead_darkblue` so the column reads as a deep-indigo prayer strand
of relic domes (the cool, midnight counterpart to P10's warm multi-colour strand, but
PLAIN — no skewer underneath). Built from classic domes (not royal crowns) so it stays
distinct from P19's crown family. Heaviest `bead_darkblue` use in the pool.
**Word-thumbnail:** ◗•◖•◗•◖ domes split by blue pips.
- **RECIPE (focal→far):** `classic:egg-dome` (FOCAL, lit) → `orn:bead_darkblue`
  → `classic:round-cap` → `orn:bead_darkblue` → `classic:calvaria`
  → `orn:bead_darkblue` → `r9:0`
- **COLLAR:** OFF (the dark-blue pips ARE the seam rhythm; gold collar would fight them)
- **LEAN:** 0
- **Showcases:** `orn:bead_darkblue` (×3, the defining strand), `r9:0` (unlit crown
  as the far terminal), classic bone-dome family.

## P16 — `broad-block-bastion`
**Thesis:** A *bulging fortress wall* — broad-zygo wide-cheek skulls stacked nearly
edge-to-edge so the blackout is the WIDEST, most barrel-sided column of the pool, a
solid bastion that bows outward at the cheeks. All-classic, COLLAR off, a lone third-
eye gem as the single keystone glint. The girth answer to P4/P11's slim tapers.
**Word-thumbnail:** ◖▇◗ ◖▇◗ barrel courses.
- **RECIPE (focal→far):** `classic:broad-zygo` (FOCAL, lit) → `classic:flat-brow-robust`
  → `orn:gem_thirdeye` → `classic:broad-zygo` → `classic:square-jaw`
- **COLLAR:** OFF · **LEAN:** 0
- **Showcases:** `classic:broad-zygo` (×2, defining the wide silhouette),
  `orn:gem_thirdeye` (lone keystone).

## P17 — `cracked-ruin-lean`
**Thesis:** A *collapsing ruin pile* leaning slightly the OTHER way — cracked-half and
keyhole voids stacked at a gentle positive lean so the broken faces all slump toward
the gap; a dark-blue bead marks the fracture line. Decayed like P5 but a different
break-family mix, gentler lean, and a cold (not necklace) ornament — so it doesn't
twin P5.
**Word-thumbnail:** ◗╱ broken halves slumping right.
- **RECIPE (focal→far):** `new:cracked-half` (FOCAL, lit) → `new:keyhole-relic`
  → `orn:bead_darkblue` → `new:cracked-half` → `classic:gaunt-hollow`
- **COLLAR:** OFF · **LEAN:** +0.22 (mild positive — distinct from P5's hard +0.42
  and P13's negative)
- **Showcases:** `new:cracked-half` (×2), `orn:bead_darkblue`, mild positive lean.

## P18 — `palm-jewel-pagoda`
**Thesis:** A *jewel-box pagoda* of the ornamented cradled palm skulls only, COLLAR
on for a warm gold-pip metronome at every seam — the richest *plain* (un-skewered)
reliquary, where P7's gold scepter rod is gone and the gold lives entirely in the
collar + the palm gems. Leads with the forehead-gem palm:4 as the lit focal.
**Word-thumbnail:** ◉•◉•◉•◉ gem-domes on gold pips.
- **RECIPE (focal→far):** `palm:4` (FOCAL, lit) → `palm:3` → `palm:5` → `palm:0`
  → `palm:2`
- **COLLAR:** ON (gold seam beads under every palm — the warm pulse)
- **LEAN:** 0
- **Showcases:** `palm:3` (mid-stack), all-palm family un-skewered, COLLAR-on richness.

## P19 — `necklace-draped-warlord`
**Thesis:** A *garlanded war-totem* — a single antler-stag head crowns a stack of bare
royal crowns, all swagged by TWO necklace-ring garlands draped between courses, so
the column reads as a beast-skull trophy hung with looped chains over relic domes.
Mixes one wild appendage into an otherwise royal body; heaviest `ornament_necklace`
use.
**Word-thumbnail:** ⩕ antler over ◗◯◗ domes with hung loops.
- **RECIPE (focal→far):** `crown:0` (FOCAL, lit) → `orn:ornament_necklace`
  → `crown:3` → `orn:ornament_necklace` → `new:antler-stag`
- **COLLAR:** OFF (the two big ring-garlands are the ornament; beads would clutter)
- **LEAN:** +0.12 (a slight swagger; the draped loops sway off-axis)
- **Showcases:** `orn:ornament_necklace` (×2 garlands — defining drape),
  antler-over-royal mix.

## P20 — `mongrel-generations-totem`
**Thesis:** A *museum of skull lineages* — one skull from every FAMILY in a single
column (new / r9 / palm / classic), so the blackout is a deliberately mismatched
mongrel stack, each tier a different shape-language. The "one of each" sampler totem;
the most heterogeneous silhouette of all twenty. Mixed beads, no two seams alike. Leads
with the long longjaw muzzle and climbs a loose size-ladder spine; the unlit r9 crown
is demoted to a mid-body course and the terminal is a cracked-half (so cracked-half
recurs across two columns).
**Word-thumbnail:** ⩕ longjaw → ◉ gem-dome → ⊙ eye → ◯ unlit → ◗ cracked — all different.
- **RECIPE (focal→far):** `new:longjaw-relic` (FOCAL, lit) → `orn:bead_darkblue`
  → `palm:3` → `orn:gem_thirdeye` → `r9:0` → `new:cracked-half`
- **COLLAR:** OFF (each seam ornament is chosen per-tier, not a uniform collar)
- **LEAN:** −0.16 (slight negative; the mongrel stack leans casually left)
- **Showcases:** `new:longjaw-relic` AS the lit focal (plain), `palm:3`, `r9:0` (unlit
  crown demoted to a mid-body course), `new:cracked-half` (far terminal),
  `orn:bead_darkblue` + `orn:gem_thirdeye` mixed seams, negative lean.

## Distinctness self-audit (vs each other AND P1–P10)

**Blackout silhouettes are all different:** P11 bottom-heavy ziggurat pinching to a
nub · P12 ladder of eyes+gems · P13 left-leaning fang cascade · P14 tiny-focal shrine
with hung loop · P15 indigo dome rosary · P16 widest barrel bastion · P17 mild-right
slumping ruin · P18 even gem-dome pagoda · P19 antler-over-domes with swag loops ·
P20 mongrel one-of-each. None repeats a P1–P10 outline (P11 inverts P4; P13 is plain
where P9 was skewered + negative lean; P15 is plain/cold where P10 was warm strand;
P17 differs from P5 in break-mix, lean magnitude, and ornament).

**No single skull defines two new columns as the IDENTITY:** child-skull is a far-nub
in P11 but the FOCAL shrine subject in P14 (different role); broad-zygo is a taper
base in P11 vs the repeated barrel course in P16; cracked-half anchors P17's ruin (×2)
and recurs as P20's terminal, while P13/P20 lead with fang/lineage ideas. P15 (cold
classic bone-dome rosary) and P19 (warm royal-crown garland) no longer twin — P15 is
the classic-dome family, P19 the royal-crown family. Each thesis rests on a different
axis (taper, eye-ladder, lean, devotion, cold-strand, girth, ruin, pagoda, garland,
mongrel).

**Lever spread:** family mix — all-classic (P11, P16), all-palm (P18), classic-dome
rosary (P15), all-crown+antler (P19), new-heavy (P12, P13), mixed (P14, P20); COLLAR
on (P13, P14, P18) / off (rest); LEAN — upright (P11, P12, P14, P15, P16, P18),
positive (P17 +0.22, P19 +0.12), negative (P13 −0.34, P20 −0.16); ornament placement —
focal-adjacent (P14, P15), mid-stack keystone (P11, P16), as-its-own-tier ladder (P12,
P19), per-tier mixed seams (P20), none (P13 plain, collar only); focal family —
classic (P11, P14, P15, P16, P18→palm), new (P12, P13, P17, P20), crown (P19), palm
(P18).
