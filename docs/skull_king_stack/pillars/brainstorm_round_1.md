# Skull-King PILLARS — Brainstorm Round 1 (10 distinct stacked-skull towers)

BRAINSTORM MODE — concept *directions* only, no full renders. Each pillar is an
ordered RECIPE of element tokens read gap-edge → far end, ~4–6 tiers, with a lit
focal skull at the gap, an ornament role (collar/seam OR its own tier), and — for
the skewered five — a distinct skewer treatment. Judged at the real 58px pillar
width: silhouette + ornament rhythm must carry; interior detail dies.

Token legend (from `skulls_individual.png` / `ornaments/showcase.png`):
crown:0-5 (#1-6 domed reliquary, can be lit) · palm:0-5 (#7-12 jewelled reliquary)
· r9:0/r9:1 (#13-14 plain earlier crowns) · WILD #15-22 (horned-ram, antler-stag,
sabertooth-maw, cyclops-brow, longjaw-relic, cracked-half, flat-slab, keyhole-relic)
· CLASSIC #23-30 (round-cap, egg-dome, broad-zygo, square-jaw, calvaria,
gaunt-hollow, child-skull, flat-brow-robust) · ORN #31-36 (bead_white, bead_gold,
bead_cyan, bead_darkblue, gem_thirdeye, ornament_necklace).

The precedent (`stack.png` / `skewer.png`) is ONE recipe: crown↔palm interleave,
even 34px pitch, a thin gold bead collar under every skull, centred gold-cored rod.
All 10 below deliberately break away from that single look on family, rhythm,
ornament role, and skewer style.

---

## PLAIN PILLARS (P1–P5) — no skewer

### P1 — `relic-reliquary-totem`
**Thesis:** The "pure royalty" tower — only the domed crown + jewelled palm
reliquary skulls, tightly overlapped pagoda-style, every seam clasped by a gold-pip
collar. This is the canonical Skull-King relic, the baseline of dignity the other
nine deviate from.
- **Recipe (gap→far):** `crown:2 (FOCAL, lit)` → [collar `orn:bead_gold`] → `palm:4`
  → [collar `orn:bead_gold`] → `crown:0` → [collar `orn:bead_gold`] → `palm:2` →
  [collar `orn:bead_gold`] → `crown:5`.
- **Collar/spacer:** uniform gold-pip (#32) seam-clasp under every skull — a steady
  warm dotted rhythm.
- **Silhouette + rhythm:** dense, rounded, near-uniform-width dome stack with a
  metronome of bright pips at each seam. Reads as a smooth bumpy column — the
  "regular" totem. Tight 30px pitch (heavy overlap).

### P2 — `horned-warband`
**Thesis:** The aggressive wild-beast tower — antlered/horned skulls whose
appendages punch OUT of the column so the SILHOUETTE itself is spiky, not the
interior. Ornaments are forced into their own small bead-tier so the horns stay the
read.
- **Recipe (gap→far):** `new:antler-stag (FOCAL, lit)` → [tier `orn:bead_white` pair]
  → `new:horned-ram` → [tier `orn:bead_white` pair] → `new:sabertooth-maw` →
  [tier `orn:bead_white` pair] → `new:longjaw-relic`.
- **Collar/spacer:** white-bead (#31) PAIR as its own thin seam-tier (cool, dim),
  letting the bone horns own the gold/value.
- **Silhouette + rhythm:** ragged, antler-up / horn-out / fang-down jagged outline —
  the only pillar whose edge is broken by bone spurs. Widest, most irregular
  blackout shape of the set. Loose 36px pitch so the appendages have air.

### P3 — `keystone-cairn`
**Thesis:** The blocky "stone cairn" tower — flat-slab and square-jaw and
flat-brow-robust skulls stacked like masonry blocks with almost no overlap, a
gem seated in its OWN tier between two courses like a keystone jewel.
- **Recipe (gap→far):** `new:flat-slab (FOCAL, lit)` → [tier `orn:gem_thirdeye`] →
  `classic:square-jaw` → [collar `orn:bead_darkblue`] → `new:keyhole-relic` →
  [tier `orn:gem_thirdeye`] → `classic:flat-brow-robust`.
- **Collar/spacer:** alternating — a faceted third-eye GEM (#35) as its own keystone
  tier between the two block courses, dark-blue bead (#34) as the minor seam.
- **Silhouette + rhythm:** squared, architectural, vertical-walled column with hard
  shoulders — the antithesis of P1's rounded dome. Wide gem-glints punctuate the
  flat masonry. Square 38px pitch (blocks butt, barely overlap).

### P4 — `gaunt-hollow-spire`
**Thesis:** The starving/ascetic tower — narrow, tall, hollow-cheeked skulls
(gaunt-hollow, calvaria jawless cap, egg-dome, child-skull) tapering toward the gap
so the column reads as a thin pointed SPIRE, threaded by a single cyan-bead spine.
- **Recipe (gap→far):** `classic:egg-dome (FOCAL, lit)` → [collar `orn:bead_cyan`] →
  `classic:gaunt-hollow` → [collar `orn:bead_cyan`] → `classic:calvaria` →
  [collar `orn:bead_cyan`] → `classic:child-skull` (smallest, at far end).
- **Collar/spacer:** cyan-bead (#33) single under each — a cold blue spine of dots.
- **Silhouette + rhythm:** the SLIMMEST pillar — tall egg-domes and a jawless cap
  give a smooth tapering candle/spire profile, child-skull pinching the far end. The
  one pillar that narrows. Tight 31px pitch.

### P5 — `broken-bone-pile`
**Thesis:** The decayed battlefield tower — cracked-half and chipped/asymmetric
skulls stacked at deliberately uneven seams (alternating left/right lean of the
break) so the column looks like a precarious pile of broken bone, garlanded by the
necklace ring-eye ornament draped between tiers.
- **Recipe (gap→far):** `new:cracked-half (FOCAL, lit)` → [tier `orn:ornament_necklace`]
  → `classic:broad-zygo` → [collar `orn:bead_darkblue`] → `new:cracked-half` (mirror) →
  [tier `orn:ornament_necklace`] → `classic:round-cap`.
- **Collar/spacer:** the necklace ring-eye (#36) draped as its own garland tier
  between the broken courses; dark-blue bead (#34) for the minor seam.
- **Silhouette + rhythm:** asymmetric, lopsided — each cracked-half offsets the
  centreline so the outline zig-zags. Broad-zygo's wide cheeks bulge mid-stack. The
  one OFF-AXIS, wobbling pile. Uneven 33/35px alternating pitch.

## SKEWERED PILLARS (P6–P10) — rod threaded through, each a DIFFERENT skewer

### P6 — `plain-bone-spit`
**Thesis:** The crudest skewer — a plain raw bone rod (no gold core) rammed straight
down through round classic skulls, no ornament tier except a single bone-white bead
threaded ON the rod at each seam. The "rough field trophy" spit.
- **Recipe (gap→far):** `classic:round-cap (FOCAL, lit)` → [rod-bead `orn:bead_white`]
  → `classic:square-jaw` → [rod-bead `orn:bead_white`] → `classic:broad-zygo` →
  [rod-bead `orn:bead_white`] → `classic:flat-brow-robust`.
- **Skewer style:** PLAIN BONE ROD — pale bone shaft, NO gold marrow seam, blunt
  bound-tail nub at the far end, no barbed tip (just a rounded bone cap into the gap).
- **Collar/spacer:** white bead (#31) threaded on the rod at each seam (bead-on-spit).
- **Silhouette + rhythm:** plain rounded skulls on a pale straight stick, bone beads
  marking each seam. Reads as the simplest, most utilitarian skewer — quiet shaft.
  Even 34px pitch.

### P7 — `gold-cored-scepter`
**Thesis:** The royal scepter skewer — the design's signature gold-marrow rod (the
precedent's rod), but elevated: it runs through the jewelled palm reliquary skulls
and terminates in a gem-tipped point, framing this as the King's own staff.
- **Recipe (gap→far):** `palm:0 (FOCAL, lit)` → [collar `orn:bead_gold`] → `palm:3` →
  [collar `orn:bead_gold`] → `palm:1` → [collar `orn:bead_gold`] → `palm:5`.
- **Skewer style:** GOLD-CORED BONE ROD (bone shaft + bright gold marrow seam) with
  a GEM-TIPPED point — third-eye gem (#35) set at the barbed tip jutting into the gap.
- **Collar/spacer:** gold-pip (#32) seam-clasp under each palm skull.
- **Silhouette + rhythm:** smooth jewelled reliquary skulls on a bright twin-line
  (bone+gold) shaft, capped by a glinting gem point. The richest, most regal spit.
  Tight 31px pitch.

### P8 — `ring-eye-washer-axle`
**Thesis:** The mechanical "axle" skewer — between every two skulls the rod passes
through a flat necklace ring-eye washer (#36) seated like a spacer-disc on an axle,
so the column reads as skulls strung on a beaded mechanical spindle.
- **Recipe (gap→far):** `new:cyclops-brow (FOCAL, lit)` → [washer `orn:ornament_necklace`]
  → `classic:calvaria` → [washer `orn:ornament_necklace`] → `new:keyhole-relic` →
  [washer `orn:ornament_necklace`] → `classic:round-cap`.
- **Skewer style:** RING-EYE WASHER AXLE — a thin dark rod with a flat necklace
  ring-eye washer disc clamped at every seam (the rod visibly passes through each
  ring's eye); the cyclops single-socket + keyhole voids echo the ring motif so the
  whole pillar rhymes "round holes on a rod."
- **Collar/spacer:** necklace ring-eye (#36) AS the washer — ornament IS the seam.
- **Silhouette + rhythm:** stark round voids (cyclops eye, keyhole, calvaria cap)
  punched by flat washer discs at each seam — a clean "beads-on-an-axle" read. Even
  34px pitch, the discs widening the seam silhouette.

### P9 — `barbed-fang-harpoon`
**Thesis:** The savage harpoon skewer — a rod with a long barbed gem-tipped point and
recurved barbs, threaded through fanged/maw skulls so the whole pillar reads as a
weaponised trophy harpoon, point lunging into the gap.
- **Recipe (gap→far):** `new:sabertooth-maw (FOCAL, lit)` → [collar `orn:bead_cyan`] →
  `new:longjaw-relic` → [collar `orn:bead_cyan`] → `new:horned-ram` →
  [collar `orn:bead_cyan`] → `new:antler-stag`.
- **Skewer style:** BARBED HARPOON POINT — long gold-cored rod ending in an
  exaggerated multi-barb head with a cyan gem (#33-tone) at the very tip jutting deep
  into the gap; small recurved barb nubs at each seam instead of plain rod nubs.
- **Collar/spacer:** cyan bead (#33) under each — cool dots that read as the harpoon's
  rivets.
- **Silhouette + rhythm:** fangs-down / antler-up wild silhouette PLUS a long spear
  point — the most aggressive, directional shape, clearly "pointing" into the gap.
  Loose 36px pitch to seat the appendages and the long tip.

### P10 — `bead-threaded-strand-spindle`
**Thesis:** The prayer-strand skewer — the rod is almost hidden, dressed instead as a
continuous threaded bead strand (alternating white/gold/cyan beads) running the full
length BETWEEN the skulls, so it reads as skulls knotted onto a rosary, not a stick.
- **Recipe (gap→far):** `crown:3 (FOCAL, lit)` → [strand-beads `orn:bead_white` +
  `orn:bead_gold`] → `r9:0` → [strand-beads `orn:bead_cyan` + `orn:bead_white`] →
  `palm:2` → [strand-beads `orn:bead_gold` + `orn:bead_cyan`] → `r9:1 (lit pip)`.
- **Skewer style:** BEAD-THREADED STRAND — the rod core is thin/dark and mostly
  covered by a dense run of alternating multi-colour beads filling every inter-skull
  gap (no barbed point; the strand simply terminates in a knotted bead cluster in the
  gap). Mixes crown + r9 + palm relics so the strand ties three relic generations.
- **Collar/spacer:** the bead strand IS the spacer — multi-colour rosary run, the
  busiest ornament rhythm of all ten.
- **Silhouette + rhythm:** relic domes separated by fat segments of beadwork — a
  knobbly "beads-and-skulls rosary" column, the visibly beadiest seam rhythm; the
  far end carries the only lit r9 brow-pip beside the focal. Even 33px pitch.

## Self-audit against the four distinctness tests

**Blackout (silhouette only):** P1 smooth rounded dome stack · P2 ragged horn/antler
spurs · P3 squared masonry wall · P4 slim tapering spire · P5 lopsided off-axis pile
· P6 plain round skulls on a quiet stick · P7 smooth reliquary on bright twin-rod +
gem tip · P8 round voids with flat washer discs · P9 fang/antler + long spear point ·
P10 knobbly beads-between-domes rosary. Ten different blackout shapes — PASS.

**Swap (move one element, identity survives?):** Each pillar's identity rests on a
DIFFERENT axis — P1 family-purity, P2 appendage silhouette, P3 block stacking, P4
taper, P5 asymmetry, P6 plain-rod material, P7 royal gold+gem, P8 washer mechanism,
P9 weaponised point, P10 strand-as-spacer. Swapping a single skull doesn't collapse
one into another. PASS.

**Cover-label (no captions, still tell apart?):** Skull FAMILY differs (pure-relic /
wild-horned / blocky / gaunt / cracked / classic-round / palm-jewelled / void-eyed /
fanged / multi-gen) AND ornament ROLE differs (uniform collar / bead-tier / keystone
gem / cyan spine / draped garland / rod-bead / gold collar / washer / harpoon rivet /
full strand) AND the five skewers are each a distinct mechanism. PASS.

**One-sentence (each has a unique thesis):** Yes — every pillar above leads with a
single distinct "what it IS" sentence; none is a recolour of another. PASS.

### Coverage map (no twins)
- Skull families spread: pure crown+palm (P1,P7), wild-appendage (P2,P9), blocky
  (P3), gaunt/tall (P4), cracked/decayed (P5), plain classic-round (P6), void/ring
  (P8), multi-generation relic mix (P10).
- Ornament ROLES spread: uniform seam-collar (P1,P7), own bead-tier (P2), keystone
  gem-tier (P3), cold bead spine (P4), draped necklace garland (P5), bead-on-rod
  (P6,P10), washer-disc (P8), harpoon rivet (P9).
- Skewer styles (P6-P10) all distinct: plain bone rod · gold-cored gem-tipped scepter
  · ring-eye washer axle · barbed gem harpoon · bead-threaded rosary strand.
- Stacking rhythm spread: tight overlap (P1,P4,P7,P10), loose w/ air (P2,P9), butting
  blocks (P3), uneven off-axis (P5), even (P6,P8).
