# mukha_citipati_court_ii — art-director brainstorm cull / critique

VERDICT: ITERATE

Strong, disciplined set. The register shift (charnel-ascetic / mountain-cave vs brood I's
temple-treasury) is the right strategic move and reads apart at a glance. Body-split 3 Citipati /
2 Mukha is sensible. All five hold the locked motif and are mutually distinct (five distinct
palettes, ornament classes, crowns, focals — no internal collapse). Lock these 5 — but only with
the fixes below. No re-roll, no replacement; the set composition is correct.

## Ranking (strongest → most-at-risk)
1. **vyaghra_charma** — freshest idea in either brood; pelt is a new ornament class, tawny+black-
   stripe an unclaimed palette, stripe-sash a textbook 32px tell. Cleanest collision profile.
2. **lekha_dakini** — gilt mantra-script + prayer-wheels is a real new class; held back by two
   live collisions (palette vs near-black+gold; finial vs gabled reliquary-shrine).
3. **bhasma_yogini** — ash-grey+saffron is the standout fresh palette; seed-bead-vs-bone-bead
   distinction is real but SUBTLE — must be a hard silhouette/texture difference, not a recolor.
4. **hima_kapalini** — good cold counterweight but sits in the most crowded zone (cold-blue +
   faceted crowns heavily TAKEN); the dried-blood plum + matte-frost-vs-cut-gem must be LOUD.
5. **jvala_nirmala** — cold-cobalt full-body flame is good but carries the heaviest single
   collision (shares flame-grammar with vajra_rakta ring, ratna_padmini halo, the Citipati
   reference's own ring). "Cold is enough" is NOT enough alone.

## Locked-item audit
Six-arm fan + six palm-skulls asserted on all five — but jvala's mantle and hima's shard clusters
risk OCCLUDING outer palm-skulls; route costume mass BEHIND/BETWEEN the six arms. Fused crown
(5-skull arc + tiara-band) must stay present UNDER each new superstructure:
- vyaghra (tiger-ears flank): SAFE. bhasma (jata behind): SAFE if knot sits behind/above the arc.
- lekha (stupa finial): AT RISK — spire can read as REPLACING the arc; spring it from BEHIND the
  centre skull, full arc+band unbroken in front.
- hima (ice-shard cluster): AT RISK — shards rise from behind, arc+band frontmost.
- jvala (cobalt flame-crest): HIGHEST RISK — same-cobalt tongues fuse with the body mantle and
  erase the arc; skull-arc MUST be opaque bone reading IN FRONT, value-separated from the flame.
Value ladder: jvala's white-blue centre skull + white-blue third-eye will fight — enforce a wide
value GAP (third-eye out-glows crown-centre). jvala density: flame must SHEET densely (overlapping
tongues) so the body never reads naked through the gaps.

## COLLISION CHECK (vs brood I + mukha_devi_kin + skull_kings + skull_kings_ii)
1. **vyaghra_charma — CLEAR.** No pelt / tawny+black-stripe / tiger-ear crown anywhere. Hold the
   green-gold focal (unclaimed, cool focal on warm mass).
2. **bhasma_yogini — MOSTLY CLEAR.** Ash-grey+saffron genuinely fresh. FIX: seed beads differ in
   SILHOUETTE (knobbly/irregular matte warm-brown) not just hue, and a single fat U-swag — NOT
   asthi's multi-strand smooth-pip lattice. Promote the jata topknot as dominant read.
3. **jvala_nirmala — HIGHEST COLLISION.** FIX: distinction in PLACEMENT+SHAPE, not hue — (a)
   FULL-BODY MANTLE wrapping shoulders/arms/knee, explicitly NOT a head-ring or closed halo;
   (b) SHEETING/draped flame grammar (overlapping tongues like a shawl), not the radiating
   spike-ring of vajra/ratna; (c) cobalt = deep saturated FIELD, not a thin glow rim. If it still
   reads "blue vajra_rakta" after a render, that's a fail. Watch most closely.
4. **lekha_dakini — TWO live collisions.** (a) Palette vs near-black+gold (Obsidian) — push the
   indigo materially BLUER + a touch lighter so it never collapses to black at 32px night, and make
   the vermillion seal a reliable visible accent. (b) Stupa finial vs the gabled reliquary-shrine
   (Garnet) — make the stupa a smooth ROUND-domed bumpa + stacked rings + sun-moon tip (the
   anti-gable; roundness is the separator).
5. **hima_kapalini — CROWDED zone.** (a) Cold-blue is heavily TAKEN (asthi/Obsidian/Verdigris/
   Starlit/Opal/Bismuth/Lapis) — the dried-blood plum MUST be a deliberate visible second note
   (lip + cord + maybe sockets): brood-read = "frost + a wound of dried blood." (b) Faceted/crystal
   crown vs Bismuth/Amethyst/Opal — shards ABSOLUTELY MATTE, irregular, frost-rimed, NO facet-
   sheen / NO refraction / NO regularity. Any specular glint = collision.

## 32px carriers (named per sister)
- vyaghra: stripe-sash band (SAFE). bhasma: matted-hair jata topknot silhouette (primary), swag
  secondary. jvala: full-body cobalt mantle MASS (filled blue figure, not spiky rim). lekha: stupa
  finial spire (pale-gold round-dome+spire); script mushes to a gold band (fine); prayer-wheels
  hero-only. hima: dried-blood plum colour-tell + shard cluster.

## Reverence
All five dignified (canonical wrathful/ascetic iconography). Watch: bhasma's jata + ash must read
as ascetic GRAVITAS (sculpted ordered dreadlocks, clean tri-band forehead), not messy/comedic.

## Net
Lock exactly these 5 (3 Citipati / 2 Mukha) with the fixes folded into each per-concept brief.
The risk is entirely outward (vs TAKEN), not inward.
