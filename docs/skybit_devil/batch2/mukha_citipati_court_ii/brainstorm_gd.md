# mukha_citipati_court_ii — BRAINSTORM (5 sister directions)

A SECOND brood beside the shipped `mukha_citipati_court`. Same locked skeleton on
all five (six-arm radial fan; six open palms each cradling a tiny skull; the FUSED
crown = Citipati 5-skull arc-sweep + Mukha tiara-band across the brow, TOGETHER;
dense never-naked ornament; value ladder third-eye → palm-skulls → crown/secondary;
two-scale HERO + true-32px with one named silhouette element; SS=8 + hi-res hero
export). Reverent + ornate; chibi cuteness in PROPORTION only; procedural-only.

DISTINCTION is the whole point. Where brood I sat in the *temple-treasury* register
(jewels, brocade, bone-beads, garlands, naga-thread), brood II deliberately moves to
the **charnel-ascetic / mountain-cave register**: flayed hides, ash and seed-beads,
cold wisdom-flame, gilt scripture, and hoar-frost. Every ornament SET, palette, and
crown superstructure below is checked COLLISION-FREE against the TAKEN lists. No two
of the five share a palette or an ornament set.

**Body-base split: 3 Citipati (tall dancer) / 2 Mukha (squat + lotus).**
(Meets the ≥2 Citipati AND ≥2 Mukha floor.)
- Citipati bodies: vyaghra_charma, jvala_nirmala, lekha_dakini
- Mukha bodies: bhasma_yogini, hima_kapalini

### 1. vyaghra_charma — tiger-pelt charnel adept  (CITIPATI body)
- **Thesis:** the cremation-ground hunter — a striped beast-hide is the whole costume,
  bold tawny bands doing the ornament work no bead or silk can.
- **Ornament SET:** **animal-pelt wrap (vyaghra-charma)** — a tiger-skin loincloth knotted
  at the hip with the striped pelt slung as a sash across the rib-barrel, four dangling
  paws (claw-tipped) at the corners, the flat striped tail curling round one thigh, and
  the small flattened tiger-face mask as a belt-buckle plaque. Stripe-bands ARE the
  surface texture across hide + skirt. (No silk, no beads, no garland cord — pelt only.)
- **Palette:** **tawny-gold + black tiger-stripe + warm cream bone** — fill `~(214,158,72)`
  tawny, hard black `~(30,24,22)` stripes, bone mass kept warm-cream `~(238,226,196)` so
  the striped hide reads as the saturated mass against the lighter skeleton. Single
  cool **green-gold** glow accent on the focal only. (No ivory+amethyst, no bronze+blood,
  no gilded+chartreuse — this is the only tawny+black-stripe palette in either brood.)
- **Crown superstructure + skulls:** fused 5-skull arc + Mukha tiara-band, and ON TOP a
  fresh **paired tiger-EAR crest** flanking the centre skull (two short rounded striped
  ears rising from the tiara — never the brood-I rearing-naga hood or any fan/spike).
  Crown skulls warm-cream, dimmer than the palms; centre skull glows.
- **Focal hue:** **green-gold** third-eye slit (cool against all the warm tawny).
- **32px tell:** the **bold tiger-stripe sash band** across the torso — a chunky
  tawny-and-black diagonal that survives downscale where fine ornament can't.
- **Most at risk of resembling:** brood-I **vajra_rakta** (CITIPATI body + a diagonal
  sash mass). Stays distinct: vajra_rakta's sash is *brocade silk + flame-ring*, warm
  cinnabar; vyaghra_charma's is a *striped animal pelt with dangling claw-paws + a
  tiger-face buckle + ear-crest*, tawny/black — pelt vs cloth, and no flame-ring at all.

### 2. bhasma_yogini — ash-ascetic seed-bead mother  (MUKHA body, lotus throne)
- **Thesis:** the renunciate on the lotus — grey cremation-ash skin, brown rudraksha
  SEED malas (deliberately NOT bone beads), and a wild matted-hair crown.
- **Ornament SET:** **ash-smear + rudraksha-SEED bead malas + matted-hair jata** — long
  brown wrinkled seed-bead strands (knobbly, warm-brown, distinct from brood-I's smooth
  pale bone-beads) loop as a chest swag, wrist/ankle wraps and a counting-mala over the
  arm; the lotus throne and rib-barrel carry a dry **ash-smear stipple** texture (tri-band
  forehead lines too); twisted matted dreadlocks fall past the shoulders. (Brown seed
  beads + ash texture — explicitly not the smooth bone-bead lattice, not silk, not garland.)
- **Palette:** **ash-grey + saffron + rudraksha-brown** — skin/throne ash-grey
  `~(178,176,168)`, warm saffron `~(232,168,64)` for the brow-bands/mala spacers, deep
  knobbly **rudraksha-brown** `~(120,78,46)` beads. Saffron is the one warm pop. (Not the
  cool moonlit-bone+cyan, not mauve rose-bone, not malachite/jade — ash-grey + saffron is
  fresh to both broods.)
- **Crown superstructure + skulls:** fused 6-skull arc (low, wide) + tiara-band, with a
  fresh **matted-hair jata-knot topknot** rising behind the centre skull — a coiled
  dreadlock bun finialed with a small saffron seed (never a flame-ring, dome, or halo).
  Crown skulls ash-grey, dimmest tier; centre skull glows saffron.
- **Focal hue:** **hot saffron-amber** third-eye slit (warmest pixel against grey).
- **32px tell:** the **brown seed-bead chest swag** — a heavy knobbly U-loop reading as
  one dark warm arc across the pale-grey torso.
- **Most at risk of resembling:** brood-I **asthi_dakini** (a bead jewelry set) and
  **mundamala_mata** (Mukha lotus + a framing swag). Stays distinct: asthi_dakini is
  *smooth pale bone-beads* on cool blue; bhasma_yogini is *knobbly warm-brown seed beads*
  on ash-grey — bead material, texture, and palette all differ. vs mundamala_mata: that
  swag is *severed heads/skulls*, oxblood; this swag is *plain round seeds*, brown — and
  the matted-hair topknot crown is unique here.

### 3. jvala_nirmala — cool wisdom-flame dancer  (CITIPATI body)
- **Thesis:** the only sister cloaked in cold BLUE wisdom-fire — a flickering flame
  MANTLE wrapping the whole dancing body, frost-clean and luminous, not an ember ring.
- **Ornament SET:** **blazing cool-BLUE wisdom-flame MANTLE** — separated cobalt flame
  tongues sheet UP and OUT around the cocked-hip dancer as a body-wrapping mantle/shawl
  (over the shoulders, down the arms, flaring off the kicked-out knee), with thin
  white-blue hot edges. The flame IS the garment — it cloaks every surface so the figure
  never reads naked. (Distinct from brood-I vajra_rakta's *open ember flame-RING* behind
  the head and from ratna_padmini's *enclosing ember halo* — this is cool-blue, full-body
  mantle, not a head ring or a closed prabhamandala.)
- **Palette:** **cobalt + ice-white + pale wisdom-bone** — flame cobalt `~(58,110,224)`,
  hot edge ice-white `~(214,232,255)`, bone pushed cool-pale `~(222,228,238)`. The whole
  figure is cold; ONE warm note is forbidden (purity of the wisdom-fire read).
  (Not the indigo+cyan-moon, not bright-teal opal, not lapis+gold — a saturated cobalt
  flame field on pale bone is its own palette.)
- **Crown superstructure + skulls:** fused 5-skull arc + tiara-band, and a fresh
  **triple cobalt flame-CREST** licking straight up off the centre of the crown (three
  tall thin tongues — distinct from the brood-I flame-RING which encircles, and from any
  fanned-crest/sun-ray; this is a vertical flame finial). Crown skulls cool-pale, dim;
  centre skull glows white-blue.
- **Focal hue:** **white-blue** third-eye (the single brightest pixel — pure hot core).
- **32px tell:** the **cobalt flame-mantle silhouette** — the spiky cool-blue body
  outline itself; a jagged blue aura no other sister carries.
- **Most at risk of resembling:** brood-I **vajra_rakta** and **ratna_padmini** (both
  use ember fire). Stays distinct by HUE and PLACEMENT: theirs is warm ember at the head
  (ring / halo); this is cold cobalt wrapping the entire body as the costume — a blue
  dancing flame-figure reads instantly apart from a bone-and-fire one. Also vs Citipati's
  own ember halo reference — colour-inverted on purpose.

### 4. lekha_dakini — gilt-scripture mantra adept  (CITIPATI body)
- **Thesis:** the scholar-deity — gold-on-dark mantra script and prayer-wheel cylinders
  are the ornament, a chorten finial crowning the head.
- **Ornament SET:** **mantra/scripture-gilt** — gold lantsa-script glyph rows banding the
  rib-barrel, sash and limb-wraps (engraved-text texture), with small **prayer-wheel
  cylinders** hung as pendants at the hip and wrists (gold cylinder + cap) and a folded
  scripture-leaf plaque on the chest. Script-bands cover every surface. (Distinct from
  brood-I's brocade vajra-silk print and bone-bead lattice — this is engraved gilt TEXT
  + cylinder pendants, no fabric mass, no beads, no garland.)
- **Palette:** **indigo-ink + vermillion-seal + gold-leaf** — body/wraps deep indigo-ink
  `~(40,44,82)`, gold-leaf script `~(226,188,96)`, a single **vermillion seal-red**
  `~(196,52,46)` cartouche/dot accent. Dark-ground sister; gold text reads bright on it.
  (Not the near-black+gold, not lapis+gold, not bronze+blood — indigo-ink + vermillion-
  seal + gold is fresh; the vermillion is a tiny seal dot, never a mass.)
- **Crown superstructure + skulls:** fused 5-skull arc + tiara-band, topped by a fresh
  **chorten/stupa finial** — a small stepped reliquary spire with a sun-moon tip rising
  from the centre (a Buddhist scripture-shrine form; NOT the brood-I reliquary-shrine
  pile, NOT a dome/spike/crystal-capstone — a clean stepped stupa silhouette). Crown
  skulls indigo with gold socket-rims, dimmest; centre skull glows gold.
- **Focal hue:** **gold** third-eye slit (brightest pixel; pure gold-leaf, hotter than
  the script).
- **32px tell:** the **stupa finial spire** over the head — a stepped pale-gold cone
  reading as the crown silhouette where the script collapses.
- **Most at risk of resembling:** the TAKEN **near-black+gold** palette family and the
  TAKEN reliquary-shrine crown. Stays distinct: the ground is *indigo-ink* (blue-leaning),
  not near-black, and carries a *vermillion seal* no other sister has; the crown is a
  single clean *stupa spire*, not a cluttered reliquary-shrine pile. And the ornament —
  readable gilt SCRIPT + prayer-wheel cylinders — exists nowhere in either brood.

### 5. hima_kapalini — hoar-frost crystal mother  (MUKHA body, lotus throne)
- **Thesis:** the glacier-cave deity — sheathed in frost-rimed bone and faceted ice
  shards, a crystalline cousin to the warm sisters.
- **Ornament SET:** **hoar-frost + crystal-shard sheathing** — angular faceted ice
  crystals cluster on the shoulders, rib-barrel and lotus throne (frost-rime crusting
  every edge, fine white needle-spikes along the bands), with a frost-crystal collar and
  shard-tipped wrist cuffs. Crystal facets ARE the dense surface. (Distinct from brood-I
  jewel-inlay/gem-cabochon and from any nacre/crystal-capstone — these are matte frost +
  raw ice shards, not cut/polished gems or pearl.)
- **Palette:** **frost-white + pale slate-blue + dried-blood plum** — bone/ice
  frost-white `~(236,242,248)`, shadow pale slate-blue `~(150,170,196)`, and a single
  muted **dried-blood plum** `~(132,58,66)` cord/lip accent for the wrathful note. Cold,
  desaturated, with one dusty-dark warm. (Not teal+pearl, not turquoise+gold+coral, not
  ivory+amethyst — frost-white + slate-blue + dried-blood is its own cold palette;
  the plum is muted, far from the rejected magenta.)
- **Crown superstructure + skulls:** fused 6-skull arc (low, wide) + tiara-band, topped
  by a fresh **upthrust ice-shard cluster** — three tall faceted frost crystals spiking
  from the crown centre (NOT a spike/cone metal finial, NOT a crystal-ziggurat/capstone —
  irregular raw frost shards, frost-white with pale-blue cores). Crown skulls frost-white
  rimed darker, dimmest; centre skull glows pale-cyan.
- **Focal hue:** **pale-cyan-white** third-eye (cold brightest pixel).
- **32px tell:** the **shard-crown spike cluster** — the jagged frost crystals over the
  head reading as a spiky pale crown.
- **Most at risk of resembling:** brood-I **ratna_padmini** (Mukha lotus + a faceted,
  jewel-bright surface). Stays distinct: ratna_padmini is *warm cut gems + tassels + gold
  + a full ember halo*, turquoise/coral; hima_kapalini is *cold raw frost-shards + ice
  crust*, frost-white/slate with a dried-blood note and NO halo at all. Polished warm
  jewels vs matte cold ice — opposite temperature and surface.
