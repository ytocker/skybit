# KING SKULL II — the SECOND royal brood (brainstorm, no full render)

## The thesis
A **second royal court of skeleton-kings** sworn into the same Skybit chibi
scary-CUTE grammar as the shipped six — flat triad fills (dark-core → flat-fill →
top-left rim-sheen), a hard 1–2px ink keyline `(28,22,30)`, a 1px alpha-grown
outline, big-head chibi proportions, SS=6 supersample → smoothscale,
procedural-only (no gradients-as-shading, no PNGs), ONE dominant bone mass + thin
accents, a single brightest-pixel focal. This brood must NOT relitigate the first
court's poles. The first court owns: seated-pyramid (Koschei), radial-4-arm fan
(Amethyst), armoured-wedge + planted sword (Carnelian), tall smooth column
(Obsidian), soft organic slump + fronds (Verdigris), short round egg (Rose-Gold) —
plus two never-built poles I am told I may revive as fully-matured members:
tall-candle triple-mitre (Pontiff) and wide flaring A-frame robe (Frost).

So **eight silhouette KINDs and eight palette poles are spoken for.** Every one of
my ten must own a NEW silhouette KIND, a NEW above-head skull-crown FORM, and a NEW
palette pole — and must also dodge the wider skeleton roster: Citipati (5-skull
dancer), Nagaraja (serpent-coil), Stupika (stacked-tower), Mukha-Devi (radial-skull
fan), Asthi-Garuda (spread-wing), and the Mukha-Devi versions (tall-almond mandorla,
lobed hood-ring, wide crescent-cup, dendritic canopy, rigid arm-grid pylon),
Catrina/Mariachi/Necrarch/reapers.

### The unifying NEW motif (what makes this a brood, not scattered kings)
Every king in court II carries an **above-head SKULL-CROWN** — a Citipati-lineage
skull read ABOVE the head — but each invents its OWN crown FORM (a single great
skull finial, a halo-ring of tiny skulls, a skull-and-arches dome, a skull atop a
spire, paired flanking skulls, a skull throned in a sunburst, etc.). No king copies
Citipati's 5-skull arc or Mukha's 3-skull tiara. This skull-crown is the lineage
through-line that the first court only hinted at with tiny-skull regalia — here it
is promoted to the literal crown, which is itself a fresh brood-identity the first
six don't share.

### The cradle mandate
**Exactly 5 of the 10 are MANDATORY-CRADLE** — several palms cupping/offering a
glowing skull at chest or lap, leaning into multi-arms so the cradle reads as a
clear 32px tell. The other 5 are DISCRETION (cradle only if it strengthens that
king). I have assigned the cradle to the five whose archetype is *devotional /
offering / nurturing / hoarding* (where cupped hands are natural), and withheld it
from the five whose archetype is *martial / regnal-aloof / locomotive* (where extra
cupping hands would dilute a stronger silhouette tell).

### Palette spread (ten NEW poles, none colliding with the eight reserved)
emerald-jade · ruby/garnet · sunfire amber-gold · midnight-indigo/starlit ·
ash-charcoal + ember · opal iridescent-pearl · malachite-banded green ·
oxblood + antique-copper · bismuth-iridescent · lapis/cobalt-ultramarine.

## The 10 directions

### 1. JADE EMPRESS DOWAGER  *(MANDATORY-CRADLE)*
- **Archetype + silhouette KIND:** a **broad kneeling matriarch** — a wide,
  ground-hugging trapezoid: a heavy carved-jade lap/skirt block at the base, a
  squat torso, an over-large head. The only *kneeling-trapezoid* mass (distinct
  from Koschei's seated *throne-pyramid*: no throne frame, no back-fan; she is a
  low solid carved bell of a body, like a tomb-jade figurine come alive).
- **Above-head skull-crown FORM:** a **jade phoenix-comb arch** rising from behind
  the skull into a single **carved jade skull finial** at the apex (a skull set in
  a fanned jade headdress comb). Skull-above-head via finial, not arc.
- **Arm count + why:** **SIX arms** — lower four cup the cradle, upper two splay a
  jade fan and a ribbon-scroll. Multi-arm earns the cradle read at 32px.
- **Palette:** **emerald-jade** carved-stone bone `~(110,168,128)` (cool spinach
  jade) + polished pale **mutton-fat jade** highlights `~(214,228,206)` + thin
  **gold** filigree `~(214,176,96)`; jade core focal skull glows pale `~(196,236,214)`.
- **Single 32px tell:** a low green carved-jade bell-body, four cupped hands holding
  a pale glowing skull at the lap, a jade skull-comb crown above.
- **Distinct from 6 + roster:** only kneeling-trapezoid + only jade pole; vs Koschei
  no throne frame and green-not-tallow; vs Mukha-Devi her radial elements are skulls,
  this is a grounded green matriarch cradling a single skull.

### 2. GARNET CARDINAL-INQUISITOR  *(DISCRETION — NO cradle)*
- **Archetype + silhouette KIND:** a **narrow stooping hooded clergyman** — a thin
  forward-leaning hunch with a deep peaked cowl that drops the head, a long
  vertical reading-stole, one bony hand raised in a pointing benediction. A
  *hooded-stoop* hairpin silhouette (cowl-peak forward of the feet). Distinct from
  Obsidian's straight smooth column (he leans/hunches; cowl breaks the vertical)
  and from the revived tall-candle Pontiff (Pontiff stands rigidly erect with a
  ground-staff; this one stoops and points, cowl-peaked not mitre-tiered).
- **Above-head skull-crown FORM:** the cowl-peak resolves into a **gabled skull
  reliquary** — a tiny pointed-arch shrine perched atop the hood with a single
  garnet-eyed skull enthroned inside it (skull-and-gable above the head).
- **Arm count + why:** **two-armed** — the lone pointing hand is the menace; extra
  arms would soften the severe ascetic read. No cradle (he condemns, not offers).
- **Palette:** **ruby/garnet** — deep oxblood-garnet robe `~(120,28,46)` over a
  cool bone face `~(214,206,196)` + thin **antique silver** stole-chains
  `~(176,180,188)`; one garnet eye-gem `~(196,40,72)` is the focal.
- **Single 32px tell:** a thin dark-red stooping hood with a pointing finger and a
  tiny garnet-skull shrine peaked above the cowl.
- **Distinct from 6 + roster:** only hooded-stoop KIND + only garnet pole; reads as
  a sinister cardinal, not a skeleton-man; the gabled reliquary crown is unique.

### 3. SUNFIRE SOLAR-KHAN  *(DISCRETION — NO cradle)*
- **Archetype + silhouette KIND:** a **disc-haloed enthroned sun-monarch** —
  the dominant shape is a **vast circular sun-disc halo** filling the frame behind
  a compact seated torso, so the blackout reads as a near-perfect CIRCLE with a
  small king at its centre. The only *disc/circle* silhouette in either court.
- **Above-head skull-crown FORM:** at the disc's zenith, a **skull throned in a
  rayed sunburst** — a single gold skull set as the topmost ray-boss of the halo
  (skull-as-sun above the head). The lower rays are plain gold flames.
- **Arm count + why:** **two-armed** — palms open in a low presenting gesture
  against the disc; the disc IS the spectacle, more arms would clutter the circle.
- **Palette:** **sunfire amber-gold** — molten amber bone `~(232,176,84)` + deep
  **ember-orange** ray cores `~(214,108,40)` + thin **white-gold** rim
  `~(250,232,180)`; the brightest pixel is a white-hot focal at the disc centre.
- **Single 32px tell:** a glowing amber sun-disc ring with a small king inside and a
  single gold skull at the top ray.
- **Distinct from 6 + roster:** only circular-disc KIND + only amber-sunfire pole;
  vs Amethyst's radial *arms*, this is a radial *disc of rays*; vs Asthi-Garuda's
  feathered wings, this is a solid sun-ring.

### 4. STARLIT NIGHT-SHEPHERD  *(MANDATORY-CRADLE)*

  Distinct from Frost's wide *triangular A-frame* (his hem is a straight flat

- **Arm count + why:** **FOUR arms** — lower two cradle a glowing moon-skull at the
  chest, upper two hold a star-map scroll and a tiny astrolabe. Cradle reads clearly.
- **Palette:** **midnight-indigo / starlit** — deep indigo cloak `~(40,44,92)` over
  cool blue-bone `~(176,188,214)` + thin **silver-white** star-ticks `~(238,240,250)`;

- **Distinct from 6 + roster:** only teardrop-bell KIND + only midnight-indigo pole;

### 5. EMBER ASH-WALKER  *(DISCRETION — NO cradle)*

### 9. BISMUTH PRISM-ARCHITECT  *(MANDATORY-CRADLE)*

  faceted rectangular bismuth crystals stacking up to the head (hard right-angle

  set-square. Cradle reads against the geometric mass.
- **Palette:** **bismuth-iridescent** — the famous oil-slick rainbow on grey crystal:
  base steel-grey bone `~(132,138,150)` with **iridescent facet edges** cycling
  magenta/teal/gold `~(206,96,168)/~(96,196,196)/~(214,184,96)`; the cradled skull is
  the brightest iridescent focal.
- **Single 32px tell:** a stepped grey-crystal pyramid with rainbow facet edges, four

- **Distinct from 6 + roster:** only stepped-ziggurat KIND + only bismuth-iridescent

### 10. LAPIS COBALT NAVIGATOR-KING  *(MANDATORY-CRADLE)*

- **Arm count + why:** **FOUR arms** — outer two grip the wide ring at its poles

- **Palette:** **lapis / cobalt-ultramarine** — deep lapis-blue robe `~(36,64,148)`

## Self-audit (distinct-design-variants doctrine)
- **Blackout:** kneeling-trapezoid / hooded-stoop-hairpin / solid-circle / teardrop-bell /
  walking-strider / floating-tiered-rings / anvil-T-yoke / blocky-machine / stepped-ziggurat /
  wide-ring-ellipse — ten clearly different solid shapes, none matching the eight reserved poles
  (seated-pyramid, radial-4-arm, armoured-wedge, smooth-column, organic-slump, round-egg,
  tall-candle, wide-A-frame) or the roster KINDs (serpent-coil, stacked-tower, radial-skull-fan,
  spread-wing, mandorla, hood-ring, crescent-cup, dendritic-canopy, arm-grid).
- **Swap:** no pair convertible by recolour+repose+accessory — each has its own construction
  (e.g. the circle, the ring-ellipse, the ziggurate terraces, the anvil-yoke, the machine boxes
  are different builders, not one body re-dressed).
- **Cover-the-label:** each row's 32px tell is describable without the name.
- **One-sentence:** ten distinct theses (matriarch / inquisitor / sun-monarch / astronomer /
  pilgrim / pearl-queen / judge / automaton / architect / navigator), no rephrasings.
- **Skull-crown variety:** finial-comb / gabled-reliquary / sunburst-ray-boss / halo-ring-of-tiny /
  helm-crest-skull / paired-flanking / dome-skull-boss / gear-spire / capstone / ring-apex — ten
  distinct above-head FORMs, none copying Citipati's 5-arc or Mukha's 3-tiara.
- **Palette poles:** jade / garnet / amber-sunfire / midnight-indigo / charcoal-ember / opal /
  malachite / oxblood-copper / bismuth / lapis-cobalt — ten new poles, none among the reserved
  eight (tallow-chartreuse, ivory-amethyst, bronze-carnelian, onyx-gold, verdigris-pearl,
  rose-gold-pink, marble-sapphire, frost-cyan).

**Note for the art-director:** watch two adjacency risks at 32px — (a) Sunfire's solid sun-DISC
vs Lapis's wide ring-ELLIPSE: keep Sunfire a filled solid circle and Lapis a thin open horizontal

vs the Oxblood automaton's blockiness: keep #9 an architectural step-mass (no limbs reading as a
figure) and #8 a clearly humanoid machine (peg legs, gear-arms). Both are flagged for the per-king
locks.
