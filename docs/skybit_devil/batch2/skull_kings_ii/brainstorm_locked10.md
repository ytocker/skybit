# KING SKULL II — LOCKED 10 (per-concept briefs)

A brand-new SECOND royal court of **ten** skeleton KINGS — slick, royal, high-end —
sworn into the shipped Skybit chibi scary-CUTE grammar and standing apart from the
first court of 6 + the two reserved poles + the skeleton-dense roster. Folds in the
art-director's brainstorm cull (`brainstorm_ad.md`): Jade crown de-collided from
Sunfire; cradle swap (Bismuth → discretion, Sunfire → mandatory @ 4 arms); the eight
per-king render locks.

**Brood through-line:** every king carries an **above-head SKULL-CROWN** (a
Citipati-lineage skull read ABOVE the head) in its OWN crown FORM — promoting to the
literal crown what the first court only hinted at as tiny-skull regalia.

**Mandatory-cradle FIVE** (several palms cupping a glowing skull, leaning into
multi-arms so the cradle reads at 32px): **Jade Empress, Sunfire Solar-Khan,
Starlit Night-Shepherd, Opal Pearl-Diver, Lapis Navigator.** **Discretion FIVE**
(stronger non-cradle tell, no cradle): Garnet Cardinal, Ember Ash-Walker, Malachite
Magistrate, Oxblood Automaton, Bismuth Prism-Architect.

**House grammar (clone helpers from `jiangshi_epic/citipati/render_citipati.py` +
`citipati_versions/koschei/render_koschei.py`; mirror `skull_kings/regent_koschei/
render_regent_koschei.py` structure):** chibi scary-cute, flat triad
(`triad_blob`/`triad_circle`: dark-core→fill→top-left sheen) + 1–2px ink keyline
`(28,22,30)` + 1px grown outline; `SS=6`→smoothscale; standalone pygame, FONT five
`..` up. Each sheet = hero + top↔bottom-mirrored pillar (grounded in the king's own
forms) + true-32px DAY & NIGHT chips + blacked-out silhouette proof + palette strip.

**GATE every round:** reads as a ROYAL skull-KING; the above-head skull-crown + ONE
32px tell land day+night; ONE dominant bone/body mass + thin accents; focal stays
the single brightest pixel; clean mirrored pillar; no realism drift; distinct from
the other nine + the first 6 + roster. Push each per-concept loop GD→AD→GD… to
`VERDICT: SHIP-READY` (safety cap ≈5 designer rounds; surface a staller, don't ship it).

### 1. jade_empress_dowager — kneeling jade matriarch  *(MANDATORY-CRADLE, 6 arms)*
- **KIND:** broad **kneeling trapezoid / solid carved bell-body** sunk to the ground
  (NO throne frame, NO back-fan — those make it Koschei).
- **Crown:** a **vertical jade phoenix-PLUME finial** — one tall carved jade blade
  rising to a single jade **skull finial** at the apex (no fan-spread; de-collides
  from Sunfire's radial burst).
- **Arms:** SIX — lower four cup the cradle, upper two splay a jade fan + ribbon-scroll.
- **Palette:** spinach-jade carved-stone body `(110,168,128)` + mutton-fat jade
  highlight `(214,228,206)` + thin gold filigree `(214,176,96)`; cradled skull glows
  pale jade `(196,236,214)` = single brightest focal (filigree must not out-shine it).
- **32px tell:** low green carved-jade bell, 4 cupped hands holding a pale glowing
  skull at the lap, vertical jade skull-plume above.

### 2. garnet_cardinal_inquisitor — stooping clergyman  *(DISCRETION — no cradle, 2 arms)*
- **KIND:** thin **hooded forward-stoop HAIRPIN** — cowl-peak clearly forward of the
  toe-line, head dropped below the shoulder crest (a leaning hook, never a candle —
  de-collides from the Pontiff).
- **Crown:** a **gabled skull-reliquary shrine** atop the hood — a tiny pointed-arch
  shrine with a single garnet-eyed skull enthroned inside.
- **Arms:** TWO — one bony hand raised in a horizontal pointing jab (the menace).
- **Palette:** oxblood-garnet robe `(120,28,46)` + cool bone face `(214,206,196)` +
  thin antique-silver stole-chains `(176,180,188)`; one garnet eye-gem `(196,40,72)`
  focal. Bone face + silver must lift value at NIGHT (don't rely on dark-red alone).
- **32px tell:** thin dark-red stooping hood, pointing finger, garnet-skull gable
  peaked above the cowl.

### 3. sunfire_solar_khan — disc-haloed sun-monarch  *(MANDATORY-CRADLE, 4 arms)*
- **KIND:** **FILLED near-perfect VERTICAL circle** — a solid amber sun-disc halo
  filling the frame behind a compact seated torso; rays render as a textured FILL,
  not an open ring (de-collides from Lapis's open ellipse).
- **Crown:** a **skull throned in a rayed sunburst** — one gold skull as the topmost
  ray-boss at the disc's zenith; lower rays plain gold flame.
- **Arms:** FOUR — two presenting a glowing sun-skull in cupped palms at the disc
  center, two open against the disc.
- **Palette:** molten amber body `(232,176,84)` + ember-orange ray cores `(214,108,40)`
  + white-gold rim `(250,232,180)`; cupped sun-skull = white-hot focal (disc glow must
  not blow it out).
- **32px tell:** glowing amber sun-disc ring, small king inside, gold skull at the
  top ray, a cupped skull at center. Lock: king/crown overlap the rim so it never
  reads as a coin/collectible.

### 4. starlit_night_shepherd — mantled astronomer-king  *(MANDATORY-CRADLE, 4 arms)*
- **KIND:** tall **teardrop / candle-flame BELL** — narrow head/shoulders belling
  into a rounded teardrop hem, NO shoulder-flare (de-collides from Frost's straight
  A-frame).
- **Crown:** a **hovering halo-RING** above the head reading as "ring + ONE dominant
  zenith skull" (don't try to resolve 7 micro-skulls at 32px — one big + a studded ring).
- **Arms:** FOUR — lower two cradle a glowing moon-skull at the chest, upper two hold
  a star-map scroll + astrolabe.
- **Palette:** deep indigo cloak `(40,44,92)` + cool blue-bone `(176,188,214)` + tiny
  cool-silver star-ticks `(238,240,250)` clustered on the cloak (NOT scattered like
  coin sparkles); cradled moon-skull glows pale-cyan `(196,224,236)` focal.
- **32px tell:** teardrop indigo cloak with star-ticks, thin skull-ring halo, glowing
  pale skull cupped at the chest.

### 5. ember_ash_walker — striding cinder-pilgrim  *(DISCRETION — no cradle, 2 arms)*
- **KIND:** **mid-stride forward WALKER** — lifted leg + tattered ash-cloak streaming
  back off the trailing shoulder, staff swung forward; asymmetric, leaning (the only
  king in MOTION — lean hard into it).
- **Crown:** one **great charred skull worn crest-forward as a war-helm** over the brow
  (single-great-skull, worn low/forward).
- **Arms:** TWO — swung staff + streaming cloak carry the motion (a cradle would freeze
  the stride).
- **Palette:** matte charcoal body `(72,68,74)` with ember-orange cracks `(232,120,48)`
  + grey ash-rime ticks `(170,166,172)`; ONE bright ember crack at the chest = focal
  (rest dimmer). Ember cracks + rime carry the silhouette at NIGHT.
- **32px tell:** charcoal figure mid-stride with glowing orange cracks, streaming
  cloak, big ember-cracked skull crested forward.

### 6. opal_pearl_diver_queen — floating tiered sovereign  *(MANDATORY-CRADLE, 6 arms)*
- **KIND:** **floating tier-stack** — feet hidden, soft scalloped shell-tier rings
  widening DOWNWARD like an inverted nacre pagoda, soft rounded base (de-collides from
  Stupika narrows-up + Verdigris asymmetric slump).
- **Crown:** **paired flanking nacre skulls** on curved shell-horns either side above
  the head, framing a central pearl.
- **Arms:** SIX — four lower hands cup a luminous opal skull, upper two hold a conch +
  pearl-strand.
- **Palette:** pale nacre body `(228,224,232)` + a FIXED 3-fleck iridescence
  pink/teal/gold `(196,170,214)`/`(150,212,206)`/`(214,184,96)` (not animated) +
  mother-of-pearl rim; cupped opal skull glows shifting-pastel focal, flanking
  pearl-skulls small.
- **32px tell:** hovering nacre tier-stack, no feet, four hands cupping a shimmering
  opal skull, two small pearl-skulls flanking the head.

### 7. malachite_magistrate — banded judge  *(DISCRETION — no cradle, 2 arms)*
- **KIND:** **wide flat anvil-T shoulder-YOKE** — a massive horizontal squared collar
  far wider than the head over a narrow hidden body, arms folded into the yoke; widest
  at the SHOULDERS (widening at the base = Carnelian). No serpent coil.
- **Crown:** a **banded-green dome with a skull boss** — a low malachite dome of two
  crossed bands meeting at a single skull boss at the apex.
- **Arms:** TWO — folded sternly into the yoke (immovable judge).
- **Palette:** concentric malachite banding `(64,128,86)`/`(120,184,128)` in 2–3 BOLD
  bands (fine stripes vanish at 32px) + cool bone face `(208,212,200)` + thin brass
  `(196,168,92)`; one malachite eye-band focal.
- **32px tell:** wide flat banded-green yoke wider than the head, green dome + skull
  boss above a stern bone face.

### 8. oxblood_automaton_king — clockwork monarch  *(DISCRETION — no cradle, 2 arms)*
- **KIND:** **rigid blocky HUMANOID machine** — riveted rectangular copper plates,
  boxy torso, square pauldrons, PEG LEGS, gear-rings at the joints (clearly humanoid —
  de-collides from Bismuth's limbless step-mass).
- **Crown:** a **skull atop a clockwork gear-spindle spire** rising from the head
  (small brass skull finial, mechanical).
- **Arms:** TWO — stiff geometric arms with gear-elbows.
- **Palette:** patinated copper-bronze body `(178,120,78)` + oxblood-lacquer inlays
  `(116,36,40)` + thin verdigris ticks `(120,168,150)` (must NOT cool it toward
  Malachite green); brass gear-eye `(232,196,108)` focal. ≥1 prominent circular
  gear-ring (the de-collider from Bismuth's facets).
- **32px tell:** blocky copper-and-oxblood machine, gear-rings at joints, tiny brass
  skull on a spire.

### 9. bismuth_prism_architect — crystal ziggurat  *(DISCRETION — no cradle, limbless)*
- **KIND:** **stepped crystal ZIGGURAT** — faceted rectangular bismuth crystals in
  right-angle terraces that step OUT toward the base; LIMBLESS architectural mass
  (suppress figural arms — this is why the cradle is dropped; de-collides from the
  automaton).
- **Crown:** a **faceted bismuth skull CAPSTONE** as the pyramid's topmost step.
- **Arms:** none read as limbs (architectural).
- **Palette:** steel-grey crystal body `(132,138,150)` with rainbow facet-EDGES
  cycling magenta/teal/gold `(206,96,168)`/`(96,196,196)`/`(214,184,96)` as discrete
  per-edge colored strokes (NOT a gradient — confirm buildable); facet edges are the
  identity/focal, not a glowing core.
- **32px tell:** stepped grey-crystal pyramid with rainbow facet edges, faceted skull
  capstone on top.

### 10. lapis_navigator_king — armillary cosmographer  *(MANDATORY-CRADLE, 4 arms)*
- **KIND:** **wide flattened horizontal ELLIPSE** — a king inside an immense armillary
  ring of THIN crossing bands, aspect ≤ ~1.6:1, sky showing through (open ellipse —
  de-collides from Sunfire's filled vertical disc).
- **Crown:** a **lapis skull at the ring-apex** (the celestial pole-star) directly
  above the head.
- **Arms:** FOUR — outer two grip the wide ring at its poles (the armspan), inner two
  cup a glowing star-skull at the chest. Rings few/thin so the chest cradle is the
  bright focal inside the lens (if the frame crowds it out, demote from mandatory).
- **Palette:** deep lapis robe `(36,64,148)` with fine gold-pyrite ticks `(228,196,108)`
  (robe texture, NOT scattered coin-sparkles) + cool bone `(200,206,216)` + thin
  ultramarine ring `(64,108,210)`; cradled star-skull glows white-gold focal.
- **32px tell:** king inside a wide ring-ellipse, gold-flecked lapis robe, inner hands
  cupping a glowing skull, small blue skull at the ring apex.
