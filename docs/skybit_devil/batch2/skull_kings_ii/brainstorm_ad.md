## Distinctness tests (10 + reserved 8 + roster)

**Blackout:** ten distinct solid shapes — kneeling-trapezoid (1), hooded-stoop
hairpin (2), solid-circle (3), teardrop-bell (4), forward-strider (5),
floating-tier-stack (6), anvil-T yoke (7), blocky-machine (8), stepped-ziggurat
(9), wide-ring-ellipse (10). None collides with the reserved 8
(throne-pyramid/column/wedge/slump/egg/radial-fan/tall-candle/A-frame) or the
roster (serpent-coil/stacked-tower/radial-skull-fan/spread-wing/mandorla/hood-ring/
crescent-cup/dendritic-canopy/arm-grid).

**Adjudicated adjacencies:**
- **Sunfire disc (3) vs Lapis ring-ellipse (10):** RESOLVABLE but a HARD render
  lock on both — Sunfire = FILLED near-perfect VERTICAL circle, amber interior,
  rays as textured fill (not an open ring); Lapis = WIDE FLATTENED HORIZONTAL
  ellipse (aspect ≤ ~1.6:1) of THIN open crossing bands with sky showing through.
  Disc-vs-lens + filled-vs-open + vertical-vs-horizontal — all three mandatory.
- **Bismuth ziggurat (9) vs Oxblood automaton (8):** separate well in blackout
  (limbless step-mass vs humanoid box-figure). Real risk is shared "hard
  rectangles" surface vocab at 32px DAY → fix by color/value: Bismuth = rainbow
  facet-EDGES on cool grey; Oxblood = warm copper+oxblood with circular gear
  motifs. Keep #9 limbless-architectural, #8 obviously humanoid.

**Collision the GD missed:**
- **Garnet Cardinal (2) vs reserved Marble Pontiff:** bodies differ, but both are
  "thin vertical clergyman + peaked head-shrine." Survives ONLY if Garnet's stoop
  is a TRUE HAIRPIN — cowl-peak clearly forward of the toe-line, head dropped well
  below the shoulder crest — so the blackout is an unmistakable leaning hook, never
  a candle. Per-king lock, not a replace.
- **Jade kneeling-trapezoid (1) vs Koschei seated-pyramid:** adequate, but hold the
  line — NO chair, NO radiating back-fan (a back-fan = a throne = Koschei). Solid
  carved bell only.

**Crown-FORM near-duplicate:** Jade's "phoenix-comb arch → skull finial" (1) twins
Sunfire's "skull throned in a rayed sunburst" (3) — both a single skull at the apex
of a radial fan. Sunfire OWNS the radial-burst crown (intrinsic to the sun thesis);
**Jade's crown → a vertical jade phoenix-PLUME finial** (one tall carved blade
rising to the skull, no fan-spread).

## Cradle assignments (the 5 mandatory)

GD logic is right (devotional/offering → cradle; martial/aloof/locomotive → no
cradle) but two instances are wrong:
- **Bismuth (#9) → REMOVE from mandatory.** A soft cupped-hand cradle fights a
  hard crystalline architectural mass, and you can't suppress limbs for the
  limbless-blackout AND grow four cradle-arms. Make it DISCRETION; its tell is the
  stepped pyramid + facet edges + capstone skull.
- **Sunfire (#3) → PROMOTE to mandatory-cradle at 4 ARMS.** A sun-monarch
  presenting a glowing sun-skull in cupped palms at the disc center is utterly
  natural and gives the disc interior the bright focal it needs. Two arms won't
  carry a cradle at scale → 4 arms (two presenting the skull, two open at the disc).

**Locked mandatory five:** Jade (1, 6 arms), Starlit (4, 4 arms), Opal (6, 6 arms),
Lapis (10, 4 arms — weakest keeper; if the ring frame crowds the chest cradle out,
demote), Sunfire (3, 4 arms). All true offering-poses. **Discretion five:** Garnet,
Ember, Malachite, Oxblood, Bismuth — each has a stronger non-cradle tell.

## Per-king punch lists (round 1 must nail)

1. **Jade Empress** — solid carved bell/trapezoid, NO throne frame/back-fan; crown
   = vertical phoenix-plume finial (de-collide from Sunfire); 6 hands cup ONE pale
   glowing skull as the single brightest focal (gold filigree must not out-shine).
2. **Garnet Cardinal** — stoop = TRUE hairpin, cowl-peak forward of toes (de-collide
   from Pontiff); pointing finger reads as a clear horizontal jab; bone face +
   antique-silver lift the value at NIGHT (don't rely on the dark-red hue alone).
3. **Sunfire Solar-Khan** *(MANDATORY-CRADLE, 4 arms)* — FILLED solid vertical
   circle, rays as textured fill (de-collide from Lapis); cupped sun-skull = the
   white-hot focal; break the disc's perfection with the king/crown overlapping the
   rim so it never reads as a coin/collectible.
4. **Starlit Night-Shepherd** — rounded teardrop-bell hem, no shoulder-flare
   (de-collide from Frost A-frame); halo-RING reads as "ring + one dominant zenith
   skull" (7 micro-skulls mush at 32px); star-ticks tiny/cool-silver, clustered on
   the cloak (not scattered like coin-sparkle FX).
5. **Ember Ash-Walker** — unmistakable forward stride in blackout (lifted leg +
   trailing-cloak asymmetry; only moving king); ONE bright ember crack at the chest
   as focal, rest dimmer; ember cracks + ash-rime rim carry the silhouette at NIGHT.
6. **Opal Pearl-Diver Queen** — soft scalloped rings widening downward, no feet
   (de-collide from Stupika narrows-up + Verdigris slump); iridescence = a FIXED
   3-fleck pattern (pink/teal/gold on nacre), not animated; cupped opal skull is
   focal, flanking pearl-skulls small.
7. **Malachite Magistrate** — yoke clearly widest at the SHOULDERS (horizontal bar
   = the blackout tell; widening at the base = Carnelian); 2–3 BOLD malachite bands
   (fine stripes vanish at 32px); no serpent coil — the magistrate is the read.
8. **Oxblood Automaton** — clearly humanoid machine (peg legs, square pauldrons,
   gear-rings) to separate from Bismuth; ≥1 prominent circular gear-ring as the
   de-collider; warm copper+oxblood (don't let verdigris ticks cool it to Malachite).
9. **Bismuth Prism-Architect** *(DISCRETION — NO cradle)* — limbless architectural
   step-mass, terraces step OUT toward the base, capstone skull; rainbow facet-EDGES
   over cool grey body are the identity (not a glowing core); "oil-slick" = discrete
   colored edge-strokes per facet boundary, not a gradient (confirm buildable).
10. **Lapis Navigator** — WIDE flattened horizontal ellipse, aspect ≤1.6:1, thin
    crossing rings with sky through (de-collide from Sunfire); rings few/thin so the
    inner cupped star-skull is the bright focal; gold pyrite = fine robe texture, not
    scattered coin-sparkles.
