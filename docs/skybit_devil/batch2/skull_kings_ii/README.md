# King Skull II — a second royal court of ten skeleton-kings

![King Skull II showcase](./showcase.png)

A brand-new **second** royal brood standing alongside the first court of six
([../skull_kings](../skull_kings)) — slick, high-end skeleton MONARCHS in the shipped
Skybit chibi scary-CUTE procedural grammar (flat triad fills, 1–2px ink keyline, SS=6
supersample → smoothscale, procedural-only). It carries the **Citipati** house-grammar
forward and adds the brood's own through-line:

- **Above-head SKULL-CROWN on all ten** — every king wears a Citipati-lineage skull
  read ABOVE the head, each in its own crown FORM (promoting to the literal crown what
  the first court only hinted at as tiny-skull regalia).
- **Multiple hands cradling a glowing skull on five** (the *mandatory-cradle* kings),
  leaning into Mukha-style multi-arms so the cradle reads at true 32px.
- The other five use a **stronger non-cradle tell** (a pointing jab, a stride, a folded
  yoke, machine rigidity, an architectural step-mass).

**All ten are SHIP-READY** — every one pushed to a genuine art-director sign-off (the
focal-stubborn three, Ember / Opal / Oxblood, matured through the full safety-cap budget;
Opal took a round-6 focal re-architecture and Oxblood a round-6 day-only warm-mass fix).

## The five mandatory-cradle kings (multiple palms cupping a skull)

- **Jade Empress Dowager** — a broad kneeling carved-jade bell; vertical jade phoenix-plume
  + skull finial; six arms, four cupping a pale-jade skull. — [./jade_empress_dowager](./jade_empress_dowager/round_4.png)
- **Sunfire Solar-Khan** — a filled vertical amber sun-disc; skull as the top sunburst
  ray-boss; four arms presenting a white-hot sun-skull. — [./sunfire_solar_khan](./sunfire_solar_khan/round_5.png)
- **Starlit Night-Shepherd** — a rounded teardrop indigo cloak; halo-ring + zenith skull;
  four arms cradling a pale-cyan moon-skull. — [./starlit_night_shepherd](./starlit_night_shepherd/round_3.png)
- **Opal Pearl-Diver Queen** — a floating inverted nacre tier-stack (no feet); paired
  flanking nacre skulls; four hands cupping a saturated-teal opal skull. — [./opal_pearl_diver_queen](./opal_pearl_diver_queen/round_6.png)
- **Lapis Navigator-King** — a wide horizontal armillary-ring ellipse; lapis skull at the
  ring-apex; four arms, inner two cupping a white-gold star-skull. — [./lapis_navigator_king](./lapis_navigator_king/round_2.png)

## The five discretion kings (stronger non-cradle tell)

- **Garnet Cardinal-Inquisitor** — a thin hooded forward-stoop hairpin; gabled skull-reliquary
  shrine; one hand in a horizontal pointing jab. — [./garnet_cardinal_inquisitor](./garnet_cardinal_inquisitor/round_4.png)
- **Ember Ash-Walker** — the only king in motion; a mid-stride charcoal cinder-pilgrim,
  one great ember-cracked skull worn crest-forward as a war-helm. — [./ember_ash_walker](./ember_ash_walker/round_5.png)
- **Malachite Magistrate** — a wide flat anvil-T shoulder-yoke; banded-green dome + skull
  boss; arms folded sternly. — [./malachite_magistrate](./malachite_magistrate/round_4.png)
- **Oxblood Automaton-King** — a rigid blocky clockwork humanoid with gear-rings; a brass
  skull on a gear-spindle spire. — [./oxblood_automaton_king](./oxblood_automaton_king/round_6.png)
- **Bismuth Prism-Architect** — a limbless stepped crystal ziggurat; rainbow facet-edges;
  a faceted bismuth skull capstone. — [./bismuth_prism_architect](./bismuth_prism_architect/round_3.png)
