## Strongest → weakest aspects

1. **Two-wing BIRD construction (FIXED — the gate, now STRONG).** Each side is
   now ONE wing: long leading primaries rising up-and-out, short coverts folded
   under and behind the SAME wing — not a second pair thrusting down at equal
   weight. The 4-equal-blade dragonfly gestalt is gone. The blacked-out chip
   reads bird, not fly. Exactly the round-2 FIX 1 ask. Keeper.
2. **Beak focal + bone sockets (FIXED).** The heavy hooked raptor beak is now
   the unmistakable face focal and the single biggest tell in silhouette. The
   sockets are angular keyhole BONE recesses with a hard rim + dark hollow, NOT
   the round forward compound lenses of r2 — the camera-goggle read is killed.
   Blood-orange beak + eye-glow is the lone warm focal; desaturated violet-grey
   bone holds the violet in the BONE, never in a glow. Clean of Necrarch. Don't
   touch the hue work.
3. **Pillar winged cap (FIXED).** The gap-cap is now a bird-skull with the
   blades half-FOLDED into a tight scalloped fan sweeping down the shaft,
   beak/eyes glowing, talons gripping toward the gap. It finally says "wings,"
   separating the prop from a generic spine totem and from Nagaraja's rib-hood.
   Bottom-weighted, clean mirror.
4. **Talons (FIXED).** Three fat chisel-toes per foot, triad-lit to match the
   blade construction, gripping forward — they survive at 32px instead of
   dissolving into the r2 thread-scribble fuzz.
5. **Lift angle (minor, accepted).** Per GD's own note, the wings sit closer to
   horizontal-droop than a steep upswept V. This is a lift-angle nicety, not a
   read failure — the bird identity and X-spread both hold regardless. Noted as
   the single remaining polish lever, not a blocker.
