# Asthi-Garuda — bone-winged charnel sky-eater · ROUND 2 critique (final budgeted round)

VERDICT: ITERATE

The round-1 gate landed: the needle-fan is dead, the wings are now fat rigid
bone-BLADES, each triad-lit as its own plank, and the blacked-out 32px finally
reads as a wide X rather than a dandelion-puff. Palette, beak focal and the
grumpy charm are all still on-brief. But the rebuild over-corrected into a NEW
distinctness failure that the brief explicitly polices: at 32px and in the
blacked-out box this now reads as a four-wing INSECT (dragonfly / housefly),
not a beaked BIRD-skull with spread wings. That is the one tell the whole
concept exists to own ("ONLY winged/aerial X-silhouette AND ONLY beaked
skull"), and it is now ambiguous. Because this was the final budgeted round and
the silhouette read regressed in kind even as it improved in crispness, I can't
sign it off — but the gap is narrow and specific, not a re-roll.

## Ranking — strongest → weakest aspects

1. **Wing BLADE language (FIXED, STRONG).** Fat, rigid, chisel-tipped bone
   splines, each its own dark-core→fill→sheen plank. Exactly the round-1 ask.
   The membrane/needle problem is gone; clearly NOT Pazul. Keeper.
2. **Face / palette / focal (STILL STRONG).** Desaturated lavender-grey bone,
   blood-orange beak+sockets as the lone focal, hard ink keyline. On-brief,
   clear of Necrarch and the three-pale neighbours. Do not touch the hue work.
3. **Pillar mirror (OK, half-fixed).** Bottom-rooted, banded perch-pole shaft,
   talons gripping the gap — good anchor. But the cap STILL doesn't carry the
   half-folded WINGED tell (see FIX 3).
4. **Silhouette READS AS INSECT, not bird (NEW top fix).** Four equal splayed
   blade-fans + two round lensed eyes = a fly/dragonfly gestalt. The X is bold
   but it's a bug's X. This is the gate now.
5. **Talons (NOT fixed — still failing).** Round-1 FIX 6 was ignored: the legs
   are still two scribbled thread-bundles. At 32px they vanish to fuzz under the
   body, the same noise problem the wings just escaped.

## KEEP
- The fat rigid blade construction and per-blade triad lighting. Locked.
- The face, beak triangle, ringed orange sockets, lavender-grey desaturated
  bone, ink keyline. All on-brief.
- Bottom-weighted perch-pole shaft with banded quill-rings.

## FIX (most important first — narrow, do all five)

1. **Break the four-equal-wing INSECT read — make it a BIRD X, not a bug X.**
   The two upper and two lower blade-fans are currently near-equal length and
   symmetric, which is exactly a dragonfly. Birds don't have four wings:
   - Collapse to TWO dominant wing masses (one per side), each a clean
     up-and-out blade-FAN, so the silhouette is a 2-arm spread-wing V/X, not a
     4-arm cross. The lower blades should read as the BOTTOM EDGE/trailing
     coverts of the SAME wing, tucked behind and shorter — not a second pair of
     wings thrusting down-and-out at equal weight.
   - Net: upper primaries long and rising; lower coverts short and folded under.
     One wing per side. Re-shoot the blacked-out chip — it must read bird, not
     fly.
2. **Kill the goggle/fly-eye read on the sockets.** Round-1 FIX 4 only half
   landed — the bridge bar is gone but the two sockets are now big round
   equal lensed discs sitting forward, which is the other half of the insect
   gestalt (compound eyes). Make them BONE sockets: angle them slightly,
   recess the orange into a hard bone rim + dark hollow (not a flat forward
   lens), and let the beak/brow break the round-disc symmetry. Asymmetric
   skull bone, not a pair of camera lenses.
3. **Pillar gap-cap must carry the WINGED tell (round-1 FIX 5 still open).**
   The cap is still just "more vertebrae + a small head." Make it a bird-skull
   with the same fat blades half-FOLDED into a tight hard SCALLOPED fan
   sweeping down the shaft, beak/eye glowing. Right now nothing on the prop
   says "wings," so it doesn't separate from a generic spine totem.
4. **Talons → 3 fat triad-lit toes per foot (round-1 FIX 6, still ignored).**
   The thread-scribble legs are the weakest pixels in the piece and dissolve at
   32px. Three solid chisel claws per foot, gripping forward — match the blade
   construction so the grip reads small.
5. **Shoulder "ball joints."** The two grey ring-knuckles where wings meet body
   read as bolts/rivets and reinforce the mechanical-insect-thorax look. Either
   integrate them as bone scapula knobs (triad-lit, irregular) or drop them;
   don't leave them as two symmetric round rivets.

## Distinctness police (cross-set)
- vs **insects generally (NEW risk):** four equal splayed blades + round lenses
  = fly. FIX 1+2 resolve it. This must be unmistakably a winged bird-SKULL.
- vs **Pazul:** clean now — hard bone blades, not faceted membranes. Hold.
- vs **Mukha-Devi:** the directional 2-wing V (FIX 1) keeps it off her symmetric
  radial fan. The current 4-arm cross drifts toward radial — another reason to
  collapse to two wings.
- vs **source Citipati / Nagaraja:** beak + violet-grey hold; the half-folded
  blade-fan cap (FIX 3) is what keeps the pillar off Nagaraja's rib-spline hood.

## Remaining gap (final-round honesty)
Two of the five round-1 fixes (talons, pillar winged-tell) were not done, and
the wing rebuild introduced an insect-silhouette regression that the brief
specifically forbids. The blade language and palette are ship-quality; the
SILHOUETTE IDENTITY is not yet locked as "beaked bird, spread wings." This is a
focused one-pass fix (two wings not four, bone sockets not lenses, fat talons,
winged cap) — not a re-roll — but it has not earned SHIP-READY as drawn.
