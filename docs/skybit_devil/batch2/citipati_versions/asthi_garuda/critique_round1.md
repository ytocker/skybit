# Asthi-Garuda — bone-winged charnel sky-eater · ROUND 1 critique

VERDICT: ITERATE

A genuinely charming little undertaker — face, palette and beak focal are
already on-brief. But the wings, its WHOLE distinctness (only winged form in
the brood), fail the one test that matters: the GD's own flag is correct and
the fix has not yet landed. As drawn the wings are a fan of thin needle-quills
that collapse into a fuzzy fringe at 32px; the bold X is not there. This is the
gate, and it's open. Iterate.

## Ranking — strongest → weakest aspects

1. **Face / character read (STRONG).** Grumpy beaked skull, ringed orange
   sockets, agape little beak — reads instantly as a cranky bone-vulture. This
   is the keeper. Charm is locked.
2. **Palette / house-style fidelity (STRONG).** Desaturated dusk-violet-grey
   bone, blood-orange beak+eye as the single focal, hard ink keyline, flat
   triad — all on-brief. Violet lives in the bone, not a glow; clear of
   Necrarch. Good.
3. **Pillar mirror (OK, needs a tell pass).** Bottom-rooted, clean spine shaft,
   gap-cap skull present — but the cap reads as "another vertebra knot," not as
   a half-folded WINGED skull. The winged tell is absent from the prop too.
4. **Wings / X-silhouette (FAILING — top fix).** Thin radiating needles, ~14+
   per side, that thread to nothing at the tips. At 32px the bird is a round
   dandelion-puff with orange eyes; the X is soft, the blade language is gone,
   and it could be mistaken for a fuzzy ball or a many-armed shape. NOT yet the
   only-winged-form silhouette.

## KEEP
- The grumpy beaked-skull face and ringed blood-orange sockets — exactly the
  "cutest undertaker of the sky" hook. Do not touch the face.
- Beak as the warm focal; the lone agape triangle reads on day AND night chips.
- Desaturated lavender-cast bone — correctly NOT robe-violet, correctly NOT a
  glow. Three-pale separation from Nagaraja/Stupika holds via the orange accent.
- Bottom-weighted talons gripping toward the gap line on the prop. Good anchor.

## FIX (most important first)

1. **Re-build the wings as FEWER, FATTER, RIGID quill-BLADES — this is the
   whole boss.** Kill the needle-fan. Target **4–5 hard quill-blades per wing,
   max**, each a wide bone-spline that stays a solid BLADE to its tip (a stubby
   chisel/leaf end, never a hair-thread). Each blade triad-lit as its own form
   (dark-core spine → flat-fill → top-left sheen) so it reads as a plank of
   bone, not a line. Rule on the GD's flag: **fat rigid blades, confirmed — thin
   quills are rejected.**

2. **Force a BOLD X-silhouette.** Two clean diagonal thrusts up-and-out from the
   shoulders, two down-and-out — a hard capital X, wider than the body is tall.
   The longest blade in each of the four arms defines the X-limb; shorter blades
   tuck behind it, they do NOT splay into a radial sunburst. Test: blacked-out
   at 32px it must read as an X / spread-wing bird and as NOTHING else (not a
   ball, not Mukha-Devi's radial fan, not a starburst). If it doesn't, fewer
   blades / fatter blades.

3. **Tier the blades into TWO clean ranks, not one fringe.** Brief calls for
   "two tiered fans." Make it two: a longer outer primary rank (3 blades)
   forming the X-arms, a shorter inner covert rank (1–2 fatter stubs) at the
   shoulder. Distinct lengths, distinct ranks — reads as a wing, not a halo.

4. **Lose the goggle/spectacle read.** The two sockets currently sit in flat
   bone "lenses" joined by a bridge that reads as eyeglasses — pushing it toward
   a goofy steampunk owl, away from raw bone-vulture. Set the orange eyes
   directly into hard bone sockets (rim of bone, dark hollow, orange glow
   nested), drop the bridge bar.

5. **Pillar must carry the WINGED tell.** The shaft is fine as a vertebral
   perch-pole, but the gap-cap must read as a bird-skull with wings half-FOLDED
   into a HARD SCALLOPED FAN (use the same 4–5 fat blades, folded down to a
   tight scalloped cap), beak/eye glowing. Right now nothing on the prop says
   "wings." Make the half-folded blade-fan the unmistakable cap so the pillar is
   distinct from Nagaraja's rib-spline cap and from the source khatvanga.

6. **Tidy the talons.** At hero scale the legs read as two thin scribbled
   bundles of threads — the same noise problem as the quills, in miniature.
   Drop to **3 fat toes per foot**, each a solid triad-lit claw, so the grip
   reads at 32px instead of dissolving.

7. **Body banding.** The torso rib-grooves are fine; just confirm they're 2–3
   bold seams, not a fine hatch that turns to noise at gameplay scale.

## Distinctness police (cross-set)
- vs **Pazul** (sibling winged-ish): Pazul = faceted turquoise MEMBRANES,
  double folded wing-pairs, scorpion sting over the head, ochre body. Asthi =
  hard bone-BLADE X, desaturated violet-grey, beaked skull, no sting. As long as
  the blades read as bone splines (not membranes) and the X reads as wings (not
  Pazul's folded back-decks), they're clean. Keep the blades hard-edged and
  beak-forward.
- vs **Mukha-Devi** (radial-fan): the current needle-fan is dangerously close to
  a radial arm-starburst. The X-rebuild (fix 2) is what separates them — a
  directional X, not a symmetric radial fan.
- vs **source Citipati** / Nagaraja: beaked skull + X-wing + violet-grey cast
  keep it clear; just make sure the pillar cap doesn't echo Nagaraja's splayed
  rib-spline hood — the half-folded blade-fan must read as folded WINGS.

## Iteration directives (punch list)
1. Wings → 4–5 FAT RIGID bone-blades per wing, each solid to a chisel tip; kill
   all thread-quills.
2. Arrange into a BOLD capital-X silhouette wider than body-height; verify the
   blacked-out 32px reads only as X / spread-wing.
3. Two distinct blade RANKS (long outer primaries + short inner coverts), not
   one fringe.
4. Eyes into hard bone sockets; remove the goggle bridge-bar.
5. Talons → 3 fat triad-lit toes per foot.
6. Pillar gap-cap → bird-skull with wings half-folded into a HARD SCALLOPED
   blade-fan, beak/eye glowing; shaft stays the vertebral perch-pole.
7. Re-shoot both 32px chips (day + night) and confirm the X survives at true
   gameplay scale before next submission.
