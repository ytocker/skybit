# Nagaraja — bone-cobra serpent-king · ROUND 1 critique

VERDICT: ITERATE

A genuinely strong, instantly-likable first pass — the reared-cobra read, the cool-pearl
bone, the brass hood-trim and the regal jewel are all on-brief and on-style. It is held off
SHIP-READY by ONE confirmed legibility defect at 32px (the three-emerald cluster) plus two
smaller polish items. Fix the eye-gems and this likely ships next round.

---

## Ranking — strongest → weakest aspect

1. **STRONGEST: silhouette + KIND distinctness.** The reared S-coil with the wide rigid hood
   and bottom-rooted base loop is unmistakable and is the ONLY legless/armless serpentine read
   in the whole 15-boss roster. At 32px on both day and night the keyhole shape (broad
   hood up top, single spine-stalk down to a loop) is legible and unlike anything else. This is
   the design's reason to exist and it lands.
2. **Charm + house-style fidelity.** Scary-CUTE not grim: the big jewel, the little fang pair,
   the friendly hood proportions. Flat triad + hard ink keyline + 1px outline all present.
   Cool-pearl bone reads correctly as the LIGHTEST pale bone. Elevated, not morbid. Good.
3. **WEAKEST: the three-emerald focal hierarchy at 32px (the GD's flag — CONFIRMED).** At true
   32px the two eye-gems and the brow cabochon glow merge into ONE bright green smear under the
   hood. You cannot count three points; you read "a glowing hood." The brief wants a SINGLE
   cabochon focal. RULING below.

---

## RULING on the emerald-eyes question (item 3 — top priority)

**Desaturate the eyes to flat deep-jade `(34,118,92)` with NO glow halo. Reserve the bright
emerald glow `(72,196,142)` + sheen `(244,250,250)` for the brow cabochon ALONE.**

Confirmed by the 32px crops: the brow gem and both eyes currently share the same emerald glow
value, so at gameplay scale they bloom into a single horizontal green band — the crown gem does
NOT stand alone as the single focal the brief calls for. Making the eyes flat jade (a darker,
matte, lower-chroma version of the same hue family — so the snake still reads "emerald-themed")
drops them out of the glow tier and lets the brow cabochon be the one point that blooms. This
also strengthens scary-CUTE: matte jade eyes under one glowing crown-jewel reads as a crowned
king; three equal glows read as a bug. Keep the eyes as clearly-shaped almond sockets so they
still read as a FACE at 32px on shape alone (accessibility: face read must not depend on the
glow). Do NOT go all the way to ink — the eyes vanishing entirely would flatten the face; jade
keeps the socket structure visible.

---

## KEEP (carry forward unchanged)

- The reared S-coil + bottom loop. Do not touch the silhouette.
- Cool-pearl bone value — it is correctly the lightest of the brood and pops on the night chip.
- The 7-spline RIGID hood: it is rigid and rib-fan-like, clearly NOT a bushy Kitsune tail-fan
  and NOT Asthi-Garuda's quill-blades. On-brief.
- Brass hood-trim as a thin rib-edge accent — restrained, doesn't fight the emerald. Good.
- The brow cabochon as a single domed gem (not a facet-field). Construction is correct; it just
  needs to be the ONLY glowing point.

## FIX (this round)

1. **(focal) Eyes → flat deep-jade, no halo; emerald glow + sheen reserved to the brow gem.**
   See ruling. This is the gate to ship.
2. **(hood — 32px) The hood splines lose their count and read as a solid pale disc at 32px.**
   On both chips the 7 ribs merge into one scalloped fan with no internal separation — fine for
   silhouette, but the design's "rigid rib-cobra-hood" tell evaporates. Deepen the inter-spline
   ink grooves ~1px and let the slate-pearl shade `(150,166,176)` sit in the valleys so 3–4 rib
   divisions survive the downscale at the hood edge. You don't need all 7 to read at 32px — you
   need the hood to read as *ribbed*, not as a smooth platter, so it never drifts toward a
   Catrina hat-brim or Mukha-Devi fan silhouette.
3. **(pillar) The gap-cap hooded-skull is currently too SMALL / floaty relative to the vertebral
   shaft, and the brow-gem on it suffers the same merge.** In the pillar chips the cap reads as a
   tiny knob on a long bead-string — it doesn't mirror the creature's hood mass. Widen the cap
   hood to ~shaft-width +40% so the mirror clearly echoes the hero's hood, keep it bottom-rooted
   and on-axis (it is — good), and apply the same single-glow rule: cap brow-gem glows, cap eye-
   sockets matte jade. The stacked-vertebra shaft banding is good and tileable; keep it.
4. **(polish) Fang pair is nearly invisible at 32px.** Optional, low priority — the fangs are a
   nice charm beat in the hero but contribute nothing at gameplay scale. Don't spend ribs on
   them; just make sure they don't muddy the jaw silhouette. Leave as-is if it costs hood clarity.
