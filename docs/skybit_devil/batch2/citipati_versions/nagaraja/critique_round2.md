# Nagaraja — bone-cobra serpent-king · ROUND 2 critique (final budgeted round)

VERDICT: SHIP-READY

Round 1's single ship-gate (the three-emerald focal smear) is fixed, the two polish
items landed, and nothing regressed. At true 32px on both day and night the design now
reads as a crowned hissing cobra-skull on a single spine — ONE glowing emerald crown over
two matte jade sockets, exactly the hierarchy the brief and the source Citipati call for.
This is an EPIC, instantly-likable, unmistakably-serpentine boss. Ship it.

## Round-1 fixes — all verified landed

1. **(GATE) Eyes → flat deep-jade, no halo; glow reserved to brow gem. DONE — confirmed.**
   Hero head: the two almond sockets are now matte deep-jade `(34,118,92)` with a flat
   sheen-flick, no bloom; the crown cabochon is the sole point carrying the bright emerald
   glow + white sheen. On the 32px day AND night chips the gem reads as one bright point and
   the sockets read as dark structure, not light — you now count ONE focal, never a green
   band. The crowned-king read is achieved; the "bug with three eyes" risk is gone. This was
   the gate, and it's clean.
2. **(hood ribs at 32px) DONE.** The inter-spline ink grooves are deepened and the slate-pearl
   shade sits in the valleys; on both chips the hood reads as a *ribbed* fan with 3–4 visible
   divisions, not a smooth platter. It holds clear of a Catrina hat-brim / Mukha-Devi fan.
   Rigid, not bushy — still cleanly distinct from the Kitsune tail-fan and Garuda quill-blades.
3. **(pillar gap-cap) DONE.** The cap hood is widened to clearly mirror the hero's hood mass
   (no longer a tiny floaty knob), stays on-axis and bottom-rooted, and applies the single-glow
   rule (cap brow-gem glows, cap sockets matte). The vertebral-disc shaft banding is preserved
   and tileable. The mirror now genuinely echoes the creature.
4. **(fangs, optional) Resolved correctly.** The fang pair stays a charm beat in the hero and
   does not muddy the jaw silhouette; it costs nothing at 32px. Right call to leave it.

## KEEP (locked — do not touch when integrating)

- The reared S-coil + bottom-rooted base loop. The unique legless/armless serpentine
  silhouette is the design's reason to exist and it is razor-clear at 32px on both skies.
- Cool-pearl bone as the LIGHTEST of the three pale bones — confirmed on the night chip where
  it pops hardest. Cross-set pin holds.
- Emerald glow as the single signature accent; deep-jade as its matte partner. On-brief, and
  not on any owned roster hue.
- Brass hood-rib trim as a thin restrained edge-accent — it frames without competing with the
  gem. Good.

## Minor notes — integration-time only, NOT ship blockers

1. **Hood-edge brass arc reads a touch heavy on the day chip.** At 32px on the bright day sky
   the gilt rim is the second-brightest thing after the gem and slightly competes for the eye.
   Not a defect — it survives — but if a free pass comes up during code integration, drop the
   brass value ~8-10% toward slate so the emerald is even more solitary as the focal. Optional.
2. **Hero neck/spine vs. the loop.** The transition from the single spine-stalk into the base
   coil is clean; just ensure the procedural disc-banding spacing on the hero matches the
   pillar shaft so the two read as the same skeleton. Cosmetic continuity, not a redesign.
