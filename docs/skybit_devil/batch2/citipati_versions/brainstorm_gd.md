# Citipati-versions — GD brainstorm (8 directions, no full render)

**Lineage anchor (DNA to loosely share, NOT repeat):** shipped **Citipati** — a
cocked-hip DANCING charnel skeleton-lord; FIVE-SKULL CROWN ARC + thin FLAME-HALO
RING; warm-ivory bone dominant + cinnabar accent + thin ember ring; khatvanga
vertebra-bead spine-staff pillar. NO hat / instrument / scythe. The shared DNA is
the *theme* — charnel-ground / crowned / lord-of-the-dead / ritual-skeleton spirit
— NOT the silhouette. Each direction below is its OWN folklore creature, its OWN
silhouette KIND, anatomy, and palette.

**House style (every direction inherits, ELEVATED/EPIC):** CHIBI proportions (big
head, short weight-shifted body) pushed to boss scale; FLAT procedural triad
(dark-core → flat-fill → top-left rim-sheen); 1–2px ink keyline `(28,22,30)`; 1px
grown silhouette outline; supersample → smoothscale. Procedural-only — NO
gradients, NO PNGs. EPIC via bigger render scale, SS=5–6, more geometry, richer
triad, stronger glow. Each has a mirrorable pillar grounded in its OWN forms (soft
requirement — character first). Scary-CUTE menace, never grim realism.

**Hardest-brood distinctness flag (skeleton-dense roster):** the roster already
carries Catrina (couture flower-hat dame), Mariachi + 5 warm-skeleton variants
(Comelona/Papelito/Zapateada/Rison/Charro Jinete), batch-1 reapers, and the source
Citipati itself. So EVERY direction below: (a) a genuinely distinct skeleton
silhouette KIND — explicitly NOT all upright humanoids (serpentine, seated-throne,
multi-armed, drum-bound, stupa-stacked, inverted-meditant, etc.); (b) carries NO
sombrero, NO instrument, NO scythe/bident, NO couture-hat; (c) owns a DISTINCT
bone-tone + a single distinct accent/glow so the 8 don't all read
warm-ivory-and-fire. **Owned hues to avoid:** Citipati warm-ivory+cinnabar+ember,
Necrarch violet, Draugr ice-cyan, Raijin storm-grey, Yurei blue-cyan, Kitsune mint,
Mariachi/Catrina warm-bone+marigold/teal. Each below claims an unclaimed bone-tone
+ accent lane.

## 1. Nagaraja — the bone-cobra serpent-king of the under-river
- **Thesis.** The ONLY serpentine skeleton in the roster — a coiled vertebral
  SNAKE-spine rising to a crowned skull, hood spread from rib-fans. The
  anti-Citipati: no legs, no dance — a vertical coil that IS its own pillar.
- **32px read.** A tall S-coil of stacked vertebrae rising to a small fanged skull
  topped by a SPREAD HOOD of seven thin rib-spines (a peacock-of-bone fan, distinct
  from Kitsune's tail-fan by being rigid bone splines, not bushy tails). Coil at the
  base anchors it bottom-heavy; the hood-fan is the unmistakable crown read.
- **Chibi + triad anatomy (elevated).** A big chibi serpent-skull (round cranium,
  short fanged snout, two slit eye-glows) on a neck of beaded vertebrae; the "hood"
  is 7 curved rib-splines radiating behind the skull, each a hard triad-lit blade
  (dark-core spine → flat fill → top-left sheen edge); body tapers into a tight
  double-coil base of overlapping vertebral discs. No arms. Elevated detail: facet
  every vertebra disc, rib-spline hood ribbing, a single gem-cabochon set in the
  brow (naga jewel).
- **Pillar (grounded).** The vertebral-COIL column: stacked vertebra discs =
  repeatable shaft (disc banding, the body continued); cap = the hooded skull
  itself, hood-fan splayed at the gap with the brow-gem glowing. On-axis, the coil
  reads as a snake reared up a post — clean mirror, bottom-rooted.
- **Palette (own lane).** COOL-PEARL bone `(214,222,224)` base (a bluish marble bone,
  clearly cooler than warm-ivory but NOT Draugr's frost-cyan/rust block) · slate-pearl
  shade `(150,166,176)` · **emerald serpent-glow accent `(72,196,142)`** (the naga
  jewel + eye glow — a cool jade-green nobody owns: clear of Kitsune mint, Kappa
  yellow-green, Cernun pine) · deep-jade shade `(34,118,92)` · brass hood-rib trim
  `(206,170,86)` · ink `(26,30,32)` · sheen `(244,250,250)`. Reads pearl-bone +
  emerald — the serpent-jewel lane.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook.** A reared cobra made of one long spine, crowned by a
  glowing jewel, hissing tiny fangs — regal and venomous but pocket-sized.

## 2. Koschei — the deathless bone-sorcerer on a marrow-throne
- **Thesis.** The SEATED skeleton-lord (Slavic Koschei the Deathless: "kost" =
  bone) — emaciated to bone, slouched on a throne, cradling the egg that hides his
  death-soul. The only enthroned silhouette in the brood; a wide bottom-heavy throne
  block, the anti-dancer.
- **32px read.** A narrow gaunt skull-and-ribs torso slumped inside a wide,
  bottom-heavy THRONE of fused bones (femur uprights, a fan of rib-spires as the
  backrest), both bony hands cupping a single glowing EGG-ORB at the lap. Throne
  block + cupped orb is the tell — distinct from Necrarch's floating tall robe by
  being seated and bone-built, not robed.
- **Chibi + triad anatomy (elevated).** Big chibi skull, sunken hollow sockets with
  pin-point cold glow, a tall iron tomb-CROWN of thin spikes (not a skull-arc — bent
  blackened-iron points, clearly off Citipati's 5-skull crown); skeletal ribcage
  torso, skin-over-bone forearms; the throne is fused femurs + a rib-spire backrest,
  triad-lit (dark-core bone → flat fill → sheen). Elevated detail: rib-spire backrest
  fan, knuckle-jointed throne arms, the egg-orb caustic glow rim, crown-spike facets.
- **Pillar (grounded).** The femur-THRONE-COLUMN: stacked paired femurs lashed into a
  column = repeatable shaft (joint banding); cap = the rib-spire throne-back fan with
  the egg-orb cradled at the gap, glowing. Bottom-rooted, symmetric on-axis.
- **Palette (own lane).** SALLOW-PARCHMENT bone `(208,196,160)` base (a sickly tallow
  yellow-bone — distinct from warm-ivory and from Necrarch parchment by being
  greener-sallow) · olive-bone shade `(146,130,92)` · **toxic-chartreuse soul-egg glow
  accent `(190,222,96)`** (the hidden-death egg + socket pin-glow — a poison
  yellow-green nobody owns at this value) · blackened-iron crown/throne `(58,54,60)` ·
  cold-jade socket pin `(120,200,176)` (a literal sliver) · ink `(26,26,24)` · sheen
  `(238,232,206)`. Reads sallow-bone + poison-green egg — the deathless lane.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook.** A skinny, sulking skeleton king clutching his secret
  death like a too-precious egg — grumpy immortal, dangerously smug.

## 3. Stupika — the walking skull-stupa reliquary lord
- **Thesis.** The STACKED-SKULL TOWER boss — a tiered reliquary-stupa built of skulls
  that has woken up and walks. The only architectural/stacked silhouette; creature ≈
  pillar (like Karakasa is for its parasol, but built of skulls).
- **32px read.** A tapering TIERED TOWER of 3–4 stacked skull-bands narrowing upward
  to a domed kapala-finial with a spire; tiny stub feet at the base. A bone-ziggurat
  read — unmistakable, top-tapering, bottom-rooted (no top-heavy risk; mass is at the
  base tiers).
- **Chibi + triad anatomy (elevated).** Each tier is a ring of fused chibi skulls
  (the LOWEST tier has the one "live" face — big eyes, the boss expression — so it
  reads as a creature, not just masonry); tiers separated by hard triad-lit cornice
  bands; topped by an inverted-kapala dome + a stacked-disc spire (a stupa's harmika
  + chattra). Elevated detail: per-tier skull facets, cornice grooves, a string of
  bone-bead garland swagged between tiers, the spire's stacked rings.
- **Pillar (grounded).** Creature IS the pillar: the skull-tier tower = repeatable
  shaft (each skull-band a module); cap = the kapala-dome + spire finial glowing at
  the gap. Cleanest mirror in the brood — a true stacked column, symmetric on-axis.
- **Palette (own lane).** WARM-SANDSTONE bone `(224,202,162)` base WAIT — too close to
  ivory; instead **CHALK-GREY bone `(206,202,196)` base** (a cool dry chalk, distinct
  from warm-ivory and from cool-pearl Nagaraja by being neutral-grey, lower chroma) ·
  slate-chalk shade `(146,144,142)` · **saffron-gold cornice + spire accent
  `(228,176,72)`** (the gilt reliquary trim — a warm gold focal, distinct from
  Citipati's gold *scepter sliver* by being the structural banding) · vermilion
  prayer-cord swag `(196,72,58)` (thin linear) · butter-lamp glow `(255,214,128)` at
  the spire/dome · ink `(28,26,26)` · sheen `(244,242,238)`. Reads chalk-bone + gilt
  cornice — the gilded-reliquary lane.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook.** A holy bone-skyscraper that grew a face on its ground
  floor and toddled off — sacred, top-heavy-looking but stomping, weirdly endearing.

## 4. Aghora — the ash-smeared revenant ascetic in lotus
- **Thesis.** The SEATED MEDITANT skeleton (the aghori cremation-ascetic) — cross-
  legged in serene lotus, ash-grey, third eye lit, a skull-cup (kapala) in the lap.
  The only calm/still silhouette in a brood of motion-and-menace; stillness reads as
  uncanny here.
- **32px read.** A wide grey triangular SEATED block — crossed legs forming the broad
  base, a slim bone torso, a calm chibi skull with a glowing THIRD-EYE dot and a
  top-knot of matted bone-ash hair; both hands resting palm-up, one cupping a small
  glowing kapala-bowl. Triangle-meditant block, bottom-heavy.
- **Chibi + triad anatomy (elevated).** Big serene chibi skull, half-lidded eye-glow
  + a vertical third-eye flame on the brow; matted top-knot rendered as hard flat
  ash-locks (triad-lit, NOT soft hair); ash-dusted ribcage torso; crossed bone legs
  as the wide base; kapala skull-bowl in the lap with a tiny soul-flame. Elevated
  detail: tripundra ash-stripe brow marks, rib shading, bead mala draped across the
  chest, third-eye flame lobes.
- **Pillar (grounded).** The trident-PILLAR (trishula, the aghori's emblem — a STAFF
  not a scythe/bident: a 3-prong fork is distinct from a 2-prong bident): ash-wrapped
  shaft with mala-bead banding = repeatable body; cap = the trishula three-prong head
  cradling the glowing kapala at the gap. On-axis, symmetric.
- **Palette (own lane).** ASH-GREY bone `(196,188,184)` base (a smoky cremation-ash
  grey, warmer/dustier than Stupika's chalk; distinct from cool-pearl Nagaraja) ·
  charcoal-ash shade `(120,112,112)` · **indigo-flame third-eye + kapala glow accent
  `(110,118,212)`** (a deep ritual indigo nobody owns — NOT Necrarch's magenta-violet,
  this is bluer/cooler, a calm dusk-indigo) · vibhuti-white ash-stripes `(238,236,230)`
  · rudraksha-bead brown mala `(132,84,52)` · ink `(26,24,28)` · sheen `(232,228,224)`.
  Reads ash-grey + indigo-flame — the cremation-ascetic lane.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook.** A tiny dead monk so deep in trance he forgot to stop
  existing — eerily peaceful, third eye burning, completely unbothered by being a
  skeleton.

## 5. Mukha-Devi — the six-armed wrathful bone-mother
- **Thesis.** The MULTI-ARMED bone-deity (a wrathful dakini / charnel-mother) — a
  fan of six skeletal arms each holding a ritual relic, a fierce three-eyed skull.
  The only radial/many-armed silhouette; a wide fan of arms reads instantly, distinct
  from every single-pair humanoid skeleton.
- **32px read.** A compact chibi skull-torso with SIX thin bone arms fanned out
  symmetric (3 per side) like a peacock of limbs, each tipped with a tiny relic
  (bell, kapala, drum, flame, lasso, gem); a third eye and a small skull-tiara. The
  six-arm starburst is the unmistakable read — wide, radial, bottom-anchored on a
  small lotus.
- **Chibi + triad anatomy (elevated).** Fierce chibi skull, three eye-glows, bared
  little fangs, a low skull-bead tiara (3 skulls, fewer than Citipati's 5 — distinct
  count + lower arc); slim ribcage torso; six triad-lit bone arms radiating, each
  hand a distinct micro-prop; perched on a small lotus base. Elevated detail: each
  relic individually triad-lit, finger joints, a sash of tiny skull-beads, third-eye
  flame.
- **Pillar (grounded).** The relic-CHAKRA-STAFF: a banded bone shaft hung with the
  same six micro-relics as ring-pendants = repeatable body; cap = a small radiating
  hand-fan / lotus-burst with the bell relic glowing at the gap. On-axis, symmetric
  (the radial motif suits a symmetric cap perfectly).
- **Palette (own lane).** ROSE-BONE base `(224,200,196)` (a faint dusty-rose bone — a
  warm-but-pink ivory distinct from yellow warm-ivory and from rose-bone NOT used
  elsewhere) · mauve-bone shade `(168,134,138)` · **hot-magenta-rose relic glow accent
  `(232,86,150)`** (the dakini's lotus-fire — a hot pink-rose nobody owns: clear of
  Leyak's hot-pink TRAIL by being on a bone radial deity, and the geometry differs) ·
  deep-rose shade `(160,40,86)` · gold relic-trim `(224,182,84)` · teal bell-sliver
  `(64,170,166)` · ink `(28,22,26)` · sheen `(248,236,234)`. Reads rose-bone +
  magenta lotus-fire — the wrathful-mother lane.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook.** A pint-sized many-armed death-goddess juggling six holy
  weapons at once — furious, three-eyed, and absolutely showing off.

## 6. Damaru-Heruka — the bone-drum-bound dance-of-time lord
- **Thesis.** The DRUM-BOUND skeleton — a charnel lord whose torso is encircled by a
  RING of bone hand-drums (damaru) on cords, beating the rhythm of impermanence.
  Drum-ring-halo silhouette, but bone-built and worn at the WAIST (distinct from
  Raijin's overhead taiko-halo and storm-grey palette).
- **32px read.** A fierce chibi skull over a torso girdled by a RING of small
  hourglass-shaped bone drums on a swung cord (a low drum-belt halo, not overhead);
  arms crossed beating two drums; squat braced stance. Waist-ring of hourglass drums
  is the tell — a low ring vs Raijin's high ring.
- **Chibi + triad anatomy (elevated).** Big chibi skull, fierce wide grin, a single
  bone top-knot (no crown — distinct), flame-brow; ribcage torso girdled by 5–6
  hourglass damaru drums strung on a swagged cord; crossed bony arms gripping a beater
  each; braced bone legs. Elevated detail: each drum's pinched-waist hourglass facets,
  taut drum-skin sheen, cord beads, sound-line tick marks radiating (a hint of
  rhythm/motion).
- **Pillar (grounded).** The damaru-TOTEM: stacked hourglass bone-drums threaded on a
  spine-cord = repeatable shaft (each pinched drum a module — naturally banded); cap =
  one larger damaru with its cord-beads swinging into the gap, drum-skin glowing.
  On-axis, symmetric (the hourglass stack mirrors beautifully).
- **Palette (own lane).** BRONZE-BONE base `(212,180,140)` (a tan-bronzed bone, warmer
  and browner than ivory, distinct from Mariachi warm-bone by leaning bronze not
  cream) · umber-bone shade `(150,112,72)` · **copper-fire drum-skin glow accent
  `(232,128,64)`** WAIT — too near Citipati ember; instead **deep-teal drum-skin +
  glow accent `(40,160,158)`** (a saturated peacock-teal nobody owns as a glow:
  clear of Pazul's lighter turquoise wings by value/saturation and by being a
  glow not a membrane mass) · pine-teal shade `(22,104,104)` · brass cord-beads
  `(206,168,84)` · ink `(26,24,22)` · sheen `(240,224,196)`. Reads bronze-bone +
  deep-teal drumskin — the rhythm-of-time lane.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook.** A skeleton drummer-monk wrapped in tiny rattle-drums,
  beating out the universe's countdown with a grin — manic, hypnotic, hard to look
  away from.

## 7. Kapalin — the bone-lantern grave-monk of the lamp-road
- **Thesis.** The LANTERN-BEARER skeleton — a hunched charnel-monk shuffling the
  funeral road, holding aloft a glowing kapala skull-LANTERN that lights the way for
  the dead. The only forward-hunched, prop-thrust-overhead silhouette; soft and sad,
  not wrathful — the gentle psychopomp of the brood.
- **32px read.** A small hunched cloaked skeleton leaning forward, one long arm
  raised to hold a glowing skull-LANTERN out front/up, a string of tiny bone-bell
  lamps trailing behind; a stooped pilgrim read. The raised glowing skull-lantern is
  the focal blob.
- **Chibi + triad anatomy (elevated).** Gentle chibi skull with soft droop-sockets
  (kind, not fierce), a low monk's hood-cowl of bone-cloth (NOT a couture/court hat —
  a plain cremation shroud cowl), stooped ribcage, one arm raised gripping a wire-
  hung kapala-lantern (a skull with eye/mouth holes lit from within), the other
  steadying a walking bone-staff. Elevated detail: lantern's glow-caustic spill on
  the skull face, shroud-cloth fold facets, trailing bell-lamp string, staff banding.
- **Pillar (grounded).** The lamp-STAFF / lantern-pole: a banded bone walking-staff
  strung with hanging kapala-lanterns = repeatable shaft (each lantern a glowing
  module); cap = the largest skull-lantern blazing at the gap, wire-hung. Slim,
  symmetric on-axis — clean mirror.
- **Palette (own lane).** DRIFTWOOD-GREY bone `(200,194,182)` base (a weathered grey-
  taupe bone, dustier and warmer-grey than Stupika chalk / Aghora ash — leans taupe) ·
  taupe-bone shade `(140,130,116)` · **warm-amber lantern glow accent `(252,196,108)`**
  WAIT — risks Citipati ember; pin it as a SOFTER, GREENER soul-amber: **pale-gold
  soul-lantern glow `(244,224,150)`** with a faint **moss-green flame core
  `(160,206,118)`** sliver (a gentle will-o'-wisp green nobody owns — clear of Kitsune
  mint by being warmer/yellower, and it's a lantern flame not a foxfire orb) ·
  shroud-cloth indigo-grey wrap `(96,104,124)` (the cowl, a cool mass) · ink
  `(26,26,28)` · sheen `(238,236,228)`. Reads driftwood-bone + pale-gold soul-lantern
  with a moss-green flame — the psychopomp lamp lane.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook.** A weary little dead monk lighting the long road home for
  lost souls, lantern held high — heartbreakingly gentle, glowing in the dark.

## 8. Asthi-Garuda — the bone-winged charnel sky-eater
- **Thesis.** The WINGED skeleton — a vulture/garuda psychopomp of the sky-burial
  ground, vast bone-feathered wings spread, a beaked bird-skull. The only spread-wing
  / aerial silhouette in the brood; a wide X-of-wings read no other skeleton has, and
  it ties straight to the sky-burial folklore (jhator — "alms to the birds").
- **32px read.** A small fierce BEAKED bird-skull body with two vast spread WINGS of
  bone-quills fanning wide (a broad X/diamond silhouette), taloned feet gripping a
  perch-bone below. Spread bone-wings + hooked beak = unmistakable, wide and aerial,
  anchored by the gripping talons (not top-heavy — the wings frame, the talons root).
- **Chibi + triad anatomy (elevated).** Chibi vulture-skull with a big hooked beak,
  round fierce eye-glows, a small ruff of bone-down; two wide wings built of hard
  triad-lit bone-QUILL blades fanned in tiers (primary/secondary rows); a stub
  ribcage body; long taloned skeletal feet clutching a bone perch. Elevated detail:
  tiered quill-blade rows each individually triad-lit, beak ridges, talon hooks, a
  single funeral-bead pendant at the throat.
- **Pillar (grounded).** The perch-PILLAR / quill-totem: a banded bone perch-pole
  wound with feather-quill rings = repeatable shaft (quill banding); cap = the
  bird-skull with wings half-folded into a hard scalloped fan, beak/eye glowing at the
  gap. On-axis; the folded-wing fan mirrors cleanly, talons grip toward the gap line
  (bottom-weighted — no top-heavy risk).
- **Palette (own lane).** STORM-SLATE bone WAIT — collides with Raijin; instead
  **DUSK-VIOLET-GREY bone `(186,176,196)` base** (a faint cool lavender-grey bone, a
  twilit feather-bone — distinct from Necrarch's saturated robe-violet by being a
  desaturated GREY-violet bone tone, and clear of cool-pearl/chalk/ash by its violet
  cast) · deep-slate-violet shade `(118,108,134)` · **blood-orange beak + eye glow
  accent `(238,114,52)`** (the carrion focal — a hot orange that, on a violet-grey
  bone, reads completely unlike Citipati's ember on ivory) · rust-shade `(170,66,34)`
  · pale-quill highlight `(232,228,238)` · ink `(26,24,30)` · sheen `(244,240,248)`.
  Reads dusk-violet bone + blood-orange beak — the sky-burial carrion lane.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook.** A grumpy little bone-vulture spirit who eats the dead so
  they can fly — wings flared, beak agape, the cutest possible undertaker of the sky.

## Distinctness self-map (for the AD cull — silhouette KIND + bone-tone + accent)
- 1 Nagaraja — SERPENT COIL · cool-pearl bone · emerald jewel-glow
- 2 Koschei — SEATED THRONE · sallow-parchment bone · poison-chartreuse egg
- 3 Stupika — STACKED TOWER · chalk-grey bone · gilt-saffron cornice
- 4 Aghora — SEATED LOTUS MEDITANT · ash-grey bone · indigo third-eye flame
- 5 Mukha-Devi — SIX-ARM RADIAL · rose-bone · magenta lotus-fire
- 6 Damaru-Heruka — WAIST DRUM-RING · bronze-bone · deep-teal drumskin glow
- 7 Kapalin — HUNCHED LANTERN-BEARER · driftwood-grey bone · pale-gold + moss-green lamp
- 8 Asthi-Garuda — SPREAD-WING BIRD · dusk-violet-grey bone · blood-orange beak

Eight distinct silhouette KINDs (serpent / throne / tower / lotus / radial / drum-ring /
hunched-lantern / winged), eight distinct bone-tones, eight distinct accent glows — none
warm-ivory-and-fire, none on an owned hue. Each carries NO sombrero, NO instrument-as-
identity, NO scythe/bident, NO couture-hat. Pillars are all grounded in the creature's
own forms and mirror on-axis (towers/coils/throne-columns are bottom-rooted; wing/lotus/
drum caps stay modest at the gap). Over-proposed by design (8 → AD culls to 5).

Folklore grounding: Citipati / charnel ornaments (Rubin, Rigpa Wiki, termatree), Naga /
Nagaraja (Wikipedia, Britannica), Aghori + kapala (mahakaltimes, Tibetan Buddhist Encyc.,
Ripley's), Yama/underworld throne, Koschei "kost"=bone (Mythlok, Wikipedia), sky-burial
jhator "alms to the birds" (Tibetan Buddhist Encyc.).
