# Citipati-versions — AD brainstorm cull (8 → LOCKED 5)

VERDICT: ITERATE

This is the hardest cull in the batch and the GD set earned it — eight genuinely
distinct silhouette KINDs, eight bone-tones, eight accent lanes, and the
self-map is honest. But the constraint that bites here is not "are these
distinct from each other" — most are — it is **silhouette-KIND SPREAD vs. the
skeleton-dense roster** plus **two latent collisions the GD already half-flagged
and then kept anyway** (Damaru→Raijin, the grey-bone triple). The 5 survivors
have to read as five different CREATURES at 32px, and the set cannot spend its
spread budget on two seated forms or two grey-bone forms. Locking 5 with the
re-specs below; ITERATE (not SHIP) because three survivors need a HARD pin to
clear an existing-roster collision and the grey/ash clash must be resolved
before these enter per-concept loops.

---

## THE LOCKED 5 (keeps)

1. **Nagaraja** (serpent coil · cool-pearl bone · emerald jewel-glow) — the only
   serpentine, legless, no-arms silhouette across BOTH batches; the creature IS a
   vertical coil so the pillar is free. Strongest distinctness pick in the set.
2. **Stupika** (stacked skull-tower · chalk-grey bone · gilt-saffron cornice) —
   the only architectural/stacked-mass kind; cleanest creature=prop=pillar mirror
   in the brood, bottom-rooted by construction. Keep.
3. **Mukha-Devi** (six-arm radial · rose-bone · magenta lotus-fire) — the only
   radial/many-armed silhouette; a wide symmetric arm-fan no single-pair humanoid
   skeleton can collide with. Keep.
4. **Asthi-Garuda** (spread-wing bird · dusk-violet-grey bone · blood-orange beak)
   — the only winged/aerial AND only beaked bird-skull; a wide X-of-wings read
   nothing else owns, and it ties to the sky-burial folklore. Keep.
5. **Koschei** (seated bone-throne · sallow-parchment bone · poison-chartreuse
   egg) — the only enthroned, wide-bottom throne-block; a genuinely different mass
   from the upright dancers, and the cupped-egg hook is a memorable character. Keep
   — but see the re-spec below; this is the survivor I went back and forth on.

Silhouette KINDs in the locked set: SERPENT-COIL / STACKED-TOWER / RADIAL-FAN /
SPREAD-WING / SEATED-THRONE. Five different creatures at 32px, zero upright
skull-men — exactly the spread the brief demanded. None carries a sombrero,
instrument, scythe/bident, or couture-hat.

## THE CUTS

- **CUT #6 Damaru-Heruka** (waist drum-ring · bronze-bone · deep-teal). The GD
  flagged the Raijin collision itself, twice (palette WAIT on copper-fire, the
  "low ring vs high ring" caveat) — and that is the tell that it is fighting an
  existing boss. **Raijin already owns "fierce demon ringed by drums beating
  rhythm with raised beaters."** Moving the ring from overhead to the waist and
  swapping storm-grey for bronze-bone is a re-pose + recolor of Raijin's THESIS,
  not a new creature kind. It also reads closest to the source Citipati's "dancing
  charnel lord" energy (drums + grin + brace stance). Two collisions, both with
  shipped/locked bosses. Cut.
- **CUT #7 Kapalin** (hunched lantern-bearer · driftwood-grey · pale-gold+moss).
  Two problems. (a) Silhouette KIND: a stooped upright humanoid holding a prop
  aloft is the LEAST distinct kind in the eight — it is a skull-man with a lantern,
  and the brood is explicitly trying NOT to keep skull-men. (b) The GD's own hook
  ("heartbreakingly gentle, the psychopomp") is the ANTI-epic of the set; the user's
  priority is EPIC, memorable characters, and Kapalin is the one direction that
  trades menace for melancholy. Its lantern motif also overlaps Yurei's hitodama-
  lantern pole and Jiangshi's lantern-cap pillar. Cut — but harvest the kapala-
  lantern glow idea into Aghora's lap-bowl if wanted.
- **CUT #4-as-proposed... NO — Aghora is cut, Koschei kept.** Two SEATED forms
  (Koschei throne, Aghora lotus) cannot both survive — keeping both spends the
  spread budget twice on "seated" and they share a wide bottom-heavy triangular
  block at 32px (throne block vs lotus-leg triangle read alike when small). I keep
  **Koschei** over **Aghora** because: (1) the egg-cradle + iron tomb-crown is a
  more EPIC, more memorable character hook than a serene meditant (the user's
  priority is epic-character, and "calm/still" is the one hook that risks reading as
  inert at gameplay scale and in motion); (2) Koschei's femur-throne-column is a
  more distinct pillar than Aghora's trishula-staff, which edges toward the
  staff/scythe-family the brief is steering away from; (3) the throne mass is more
  clearly NON-humanoid than a cross-legged figure. **CUT Aghora** — but see the
  re-spec: its INDIGO third-eye flame is the cleaner accent and should migrate
  onto Koschei (read on below).

So: cut Damaru-Heruka, Kapalin, Aghora.

## CROSS-SET PALETTE CLASHES (must resolve before per-concept loops)

The eight bone-tones were fine; the FIVE survivors are mostly clean, with two
live issues:

- **CLASH A — Koschei sallow-parchment vs. roster parchment.** Koschei's base
  `(208,196,160)` sits dangerously near Necrarch's `(224,212,186)` bone-parchment
  AND in the same yellow-bone family as Mariachi warm-bone `(236,226,202)`. The GD
  leaned it "greener-sallow" but at 32px a sallow-yellow skeleton on a throne can
  read as "another warm-bone calaca." **FIX:** push Koschei's base distinctly
  COOLER and GREENER-grey — a corpse-tallow grey-green, e.g. `(190,192,158)` —
  away from both warm-ivory and warm-parchment, and let the **poison-chartreuse
  egg carry the warmth**. This makes Koschei read tallow-grey-green + poison-egg,
  a lane nobody owns.
- **CLASH B — the GREY-BONE pile-up is GONE once Aghora + Kapalin are cut.**
  Stupika (chalk-grey) is now the ONLY neutral-grey bone in the five — good. But
  confirm: Stupika chalk-grey `(206,202,196)` vs. Asthi-Garuda dusk-violet-grey
  `(186,176,196)` vs. Nagaraja cool-pearl `(214,222,224)` are THREE cool-pale
  bones close in value. They separate by HUE CAST (neutral-grey / violet-grey /
  blue-pearl) but that cast is fragile at 32px against a busy sky. **FIX:** widen
  the value/chroma gaps — keep Nagaraja cool-pearl as the LIGHTEST and let its
  emerald glow do the work; pull Stupika's chalk a touch DARKER and warmer-neutral
  so its gilt-saffron cornice reads as the focal; push Asthi-Garuda's violet-grey
  more clearly LAVENDER-cast (raise the violet so the blood-orange beak pops
  against it). Three pale bones is acceptable ONLY because their KINDs (tower /
  serpent / bird) and their ACCENTS (saffron / emerald / blood-orange) are wholly
  distinct — lean hard on accent + silhouette to carry the separation, not on the
  bone cast alone.
- **Accent lanes are clean:** emerald (Nagaraja) / poison-chartreuse (Koschei) /
  gilt-saffron (Stupika) / magenta-rose (Mukha-Devi) / blood-orange (Asthi-Garuda).
  No two share a glow, none lands on an owned hue (clear of Necrarch violet, Draugr
  ice-cyan, Raijin gold+crimson, Kitsune mint, Yurei blue-cyan, source cinnabar+
  ember). Mukha-Devi's magenta-rose vs. Leyak's hot-pink trail: confirmed distinct
  by geometry (radial deity vs. bodiless head-trail) AND base (rose-bone vs.
  ash-white) — keep, but DON'T let Mukha-Devi's rose-bone drift pink enough to
  echo Leyak's ash-white-with-pink read; keep the bone clearly warm-DUSTY-rose,
  not white.

## LOCKING BRIEFS (per survivor)

### 1. Nagaraja — bone-cobra serpent-king
- **Palette:** cool-pearl bone `(214,222,224)` (keep as the LIGHTEST bone in the
  five) · slate-pearl shade `(150,166,176)` · emerald serpent-glow `(72,196,142)`
  · deep-jade `(34,118,92)` · brass hood-rib trim `(206,170,86)` · ink `(26,30,32)`
  · sheen `(244,250,250)`.
- **Pillar:** vertebral-COIL column — stacked vertebra discs = shaft (disc
  banding); cap = hooded skull, 7 rib-splines splayed at the gap, brow-gem glowing.
  Bottom-rooted coil. Clean.
- **Character hook:** a reared cobra made of one long spine, crowned by a glowing
  jewel, hissing tiny fangs — regal, venomous, pocket-sized.
- **Distinct-skeleton-tell:** the ONLY legless/armless serpentine coil; 7 RIGID
  rib-spline hood (not bushy — keeps it clear of Kitsune's tail-fan and Asthi-
  Garuda's quill-wings). Cap hood-fan ≤7 splines so it reads as a fan, not noise.
- **Re-spec:** none. Watch the brow-gem stays a single cabochon focal, not a
  field of facets at 1×.

### 2. Stupika — walking skull-stupa reliquary lord
- **Palette:** chalk-grey bone `(206,202,196)` (pull a hair DARKER/warmer-neutral
  per Clash B so the gilt reads as focal) · slate-chalk shade `(146,144,142)` ·
  gilt-saffron cornice + spire `(228,176,72)` · vermilion prayer-cord swag
  `(196,72,58)` (thin linear only) · butter-lamp glow `(255,214,128)` at spire/dome
  · ink `(28,26,26)` · sheen `(244,242,238)`.
- **Pillar:** creature IS the pillar — skull-tier tower = shaft (each band a
  module); cap = kapala-dome + spire finial glowing at the gap. Cleanest mirror in
  the brood.
- **Character hook:** a holy bone-skyscraper that grew a face on its ground floor
  and toddled off — sacred, stomping, weirdly endearing.
- **Distinct-skeleton-tell:** the ONLY stacked/architectural mass; the ONE live
  face is the LOWEST tier (big eyes) so it reads as a creature, not masonry, and so
  mass sits at the base (never top-heavy). 3–4 tiers MAX so each skull-band reads
  at 1×.
- **Re-spec:** keep gilt-saffron strictly to the CORNICE BANDS + spire (structural
  focal), and vermilion strictly LINEAR — neither may grow into a second mass or it
  drifts toward the source's gold-scepter / cinnabar read.

### 3. Mukha-Devi — six-armed wrathful bone-mother
- **Palette:** dusty rose-bone `(224,200,196)` (keep clearly WARM-dusty, NOT white
  — Clash note vs. Leyak) · mauve-bone shade `(168,134,138)` · magenta-rose relic
  glow `(232,86,150)` · deep-rose `(160,40,86)` · gold relic-trim `(224,182,84)` ·
  teal bell-sliver `(64,170,166)` · ink `(28,22,26)` · sheen `(248,236,234)`.
- **Pillar:** relic-CHAKRA-STAFF — banded bone shaft hung with the six micro-relics
  as ring-pendants = shaft; cap = small radiating hand-fan / lotus-burst, bell relic
  glowing at the gap. Symmetric — suits the radial motif.
- **Character hook:** a pint-sized many-armed death-goddess juggling six holy
  weapons — furious, three-eyed, showing off.
- **Distinct-skeleton-tell:** the ONLY radial six-arm fan; LOW 3-skull tiara (not
  Citipati's 5-skull arc — keep the count + arc demonstrably lower). The six relics
  must read as six distinct micro-blobs, not a fringe — keep arms few-and-fat,
  fingers minimal.
- **Re-spec:** none, but pin the third eye + tiara so the FACE still reads under the
  arm-starburst at 32px (the radial fan must frame the face, not swallow it).

### 4. Asthi-Garuda — bone-winged charnel sky-eater
- **Palette:** dusk-violet-grey bone `(186,176,196)` (push the LAVENDER cast UP per
  Clash B so the beak pops) · deep-slate-violet shade `(118,108,134)` · blood-orange
  beak + eye glow `(238,114,52)` · rust-shade `(170,66,34)` · pale-quill highlight
  `(232,228,238)` · ink `(26,24,30)` · sheen `(244,240,248)`.
- **Pillar:** perch-PILLAR / quill-totem — banded bone perch-pole wound with quill
  rings = shaft; cap = bird-skull with wings half-FOLDED into a hard scalloped fan,
  beak/eye glowing, talons gripping toward the gap line (bottom-weighted).
- **Character hook:** a grumpy little bone-vulture spirit who eats the dead so they
  can fly — wings flared, beak agape, the cutest undertaker of the sky.
- **Distinct-skeleton-tell:** the ONLY winged/aerial X-silhouette + the ONLY beaked
  skull. Wings = tiered hard quill-BLADES (rigid, distinct from Pazul's faceted
  membranes by being bone splines not webbing; the violet-grey + blood-orange lane
  also clears Pazul's ochre+turquoise and Necrarch's saturated robe-violet).
- **Re-spec:** confirm the violet-grey bone stays DESATURATED so it never reads as
  Necrarch's robe-violet; the violet lives in the BONE, not as a glow.

### 5. Koschei — deathless bone-sorcerer on a marrow-throne
- **Palette (RE-SPEC, Clash A):** corpse-tallow grey-green bone `(190,192,158)`
  (cooler/greener than warm-parchment — NOT Necrarch, NOT Mariachi) · olive-bone
  shade `(146,130,92)` · poison-chartreuse soul-egg glow `(190,222,96)` (carries the
  warmth; the cupped-egg focal) · blackened-iron crown/throne `(58,54,60)` · **indigo
  socket pin-glow `(110,118,212)`** (migrated from the cut Aghora — replaces the
  cold-jade sliver; a deeper, more memorable socket glow that doesn't echo Draugr/
  Yurei cyan) · ink `(26,26,24)` · sheen `(238,232,206)`.
- **Pillar:** femur-THRONE-COLUMN — stacked paired femurs lashed into a column =
  shaft (joint banding); cap = rib-spire throne-back fan with the egg-orb cradled at
  the gap, glowing. Bottom-rooted, symmetric.
- **Character hook:** a skinny, sulking skeleton king clutching his secret death
  like a too-precious egg — grumpy immortal, dangerously smug. (This is the EPIC-
  character lift over the cut Aghora.)
- **Distinct-skeleton-tell:** the ONLY enthroned/seated wide-base block; the tall
  BLACKENED-IRON tomb-crown of thin bent spikes (explicitly NOT a skull-arc — keep it
  spiky iron, never skulls, so it reads off Citipati's 5-skull crown and Mukha-Devi's
  3-skull tiara).
- **Re-spec:** apply the tallow grey-green base + indigo socket pin (Clash A + the
  Aghora-accent migration). Keep the egg-orb as the SINGLE warm focal so the cool
  body recedes and the egg + indigo sockets pop against day AND night biomes.

## WHY THIS SET CLEARS (once the re-specs land)

- Five distinct silhouette KINDs at 32px (coil / tower / radial / wing / throne) —
  no upright skull-man, no two-seated-form overlap, no drum-ring Raijin clone.
- Five distinct bone-tone + accent lanes, none on an owned hue, the three pale
  bones held apart by KIND + accent (and widened value/chroma per Clash B).
- Every survivor yields a clean bottom-rooted, on-axis mirror pillar grounded in
  its own forms (coil / skull-stupa / relic-staff / perch-quill / femur-throne) —
  no scythe/bident/instrument/couture-hat anywhere.
- Each is an epic, memorable CHARACTER (regal venom / sacred skyscraper / showoff
  goddess / sky-undertaker / smug immortal) — the melancholy/inert risk (Kapalin,
  Aghora) is cut.

ITERATE → after a single GD pass folding the three re-specs (Koschei base+socket
migration, Stupika gilt/value tighten, the three-pale-bone value spread), this set
is locked and ready to enter five independent per-concept loops.
