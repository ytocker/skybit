# Citipati-versions — LOCKED 5 (per-concept briefs)

Spin-off epic bosses off the shipped **Citipati** (dancing charnel skeleton-lord). The roster is
skeleton-DENSE (Catrina, Mariachi + 5 variants, batch-1 reapers, source Citipati) — so the five
are deliberately five different CREATURE KINDS, zero upright skull-men, none carrying a sombrero /
instrument / scythe-bident / couture-hat. Full rationale + cuts in `brainstorm_ad.md` (canonical).

**House style (elevated):** chibi + scary-CUTE + flat procedural triad (dark-core→flat-fill→
top-left rim-sheen) + 1–2px ink keyline + 1px outline; procedural-only; pushed EPIC at SS=5–6 →
smoothscale. Lineage renderer template: `docs/skybit_devil/batch2/jiangshi_epic/citipati/render_citipati.py`
(STANDALONE pygame). Pillar grounded in each creature's own bones; mirror clean, bottom-rooted,
no top-heavy cap. Legible at true 32px day+night.

**CROSS-SET PINS (police every round):**
- KIND spread: SERPENT-COIL / STACKED-TOWER / RADIAL-FAN / SPREAD-WING / SEATED-THRONE — keep
  each unmistakable at 32px.
- Three pale bones (Nagaraja cool-pearl LIGHTEST / Stupika chalk a hair DARKER-warmer-neutral /
  Asthi-Garuda lavender-cast UP) separate by KIND + ACCENT, not bone-cast alone — lean on the
  accents (emerald / gilt-saffron / blood-orange).
- Distinct accent per boss; none on an owned hue (no Necrarch violet-glow, Draugr/Yurei cyan,
  Raijin gold+crimson, Kitsune mint, source cinnabar+ember).

### 1. nagaraja — bone-cobra serpent-king  (KIND: serpent-coil)
- **Palette.** Cool-pearl bone `(214,222,224)` (LIGHTEST of the five) / slate-pearl sh `(150,166,176)`;
  emerald serpent-glow `(72,196,142)`; deep-jade `(34,118,92)`; brass hood-rib trim `(206,170,86)`;
  ink `(26,30,32)`; sheen `(244,250,250)`.
- **Pillar.** Vertebral-COIL column — stacked vertebra discs = shaft (disc banding); cap = hooded
  skull, ≤7 RIGID rib-splines splayed at the gap, brow-gem glowing. Bottom-rooted coil.
- **Hook.** A reared cobra of one long spine, crowned by a glowing jewel, hissing tiny fangs —
  regal, venomous, pocket-sized.
- **Tell / re-spec.** ONLY legless/armless serpentine coil; 7-spline RIGID hood (not bushy — clear
  of Kitsune fan & Garuda quills). Brow-gem = single cabochon focal, not a facet-field at 1×.

### 2. stupika — walking skull-stupa reliquary lord  (KIND: stacked-tower)
- **Palette.** Chalk-grey bone `(206,202,196)` (a hair DARKER/warmer-neutral so gilt reads focal) /
  slate-chalk sh `(146,144,142)`; gilt-saffron cornice+spire `(228,176,72)`; vermilion prayer-cord
  swag `(196,72,58)` (THIN linear only); butter-lamp glow `(255,214,128)` at spire/dome; ink
  `(28,26,26)`; sheen `(244,242,238)`.
- **Pillar.** Creature IS the pillar — skull-tier tower = shaft (each band a module); cap =
  kapala-dome + spire finial glowing at the gap. Cleanest mirror in the brood.
- **Hook.** A holy bone-skyscraper that grew a face on its ground floor and toddled off — sacred,
  stomping, weirdly endearing.
- **Tell / re-spec.** ONLY stacked/architectural mass; the ONE live face is the LOWEST tier (big
  eyes) so mass sits at base, never top-heavy; 3–4 tiers MAX. Gilt strictly to CORNICE BANDS +
  spire; vermilion strictly LINEAR — neither grows into a second mass (anti source gold/cinnabar).

### 3. mukha-devi — six-armed wrathful bone-mother  (KIND: radial-fan)
- **Palette.** Dusty rose-bone `(224,200,196)` (clearly WARM-dusty, NOT white — anti-Leyak ash) /
  mauve-bone sh `(168,134,138)`; magenta-rose relic glow `(232,86,150)`; deep-rose `(160,40,86)`;
  gold relic-trim `(224,182,84)`; teal bell-sliver `(64,170,166)`; ink `(28,22,26)`; sheen `(248,236,234)`.
- **Pillar.** Relic-CHAKRA-STAFF — banded bone shaft hung with six micro-relics as ring-pendants =
  shaft; cap = small radiating hand-fan / lotus-burst, bell relic glowing at the gap. Symmetric.
- **Hook.** A pint-sized many-armed death-goddess juggling six holy weapons — furious, three-eyed,
  showing off.
- **Tell / re-spec.** ONLY radial six-arm fan; LOW 3-skull tiara (demonstrably lower count/arc than
  Citipati's 5-skull crown). Arms few-and-fat, six relics = six distinct micro-blobs (not a fringe);
  pin the third-eye + tiara so the FACE still reads under the arm-starburst at 32px.

### 4. asthi-garuda — bone-winged charnel sky-eater  (KIND: spread-wing)
- **Palette.** Dusk-violet-grey bone `(186,176,196)` (push LAVENDER cast UP so beak pops; stays
  DESATURATED — the violet lives in the BONE, never a glow, anti-Necrarch robe-violet) / deep-
  slate-violet sh `(118,108,134)`; blood-orange beak+eye glow `(238,114,52)`; rust-sh `(170,66,34)`;
  pale-quill highlight `(232,228,238)`; ink `(26,24,30)`; sheen `(244,240,248)`.
- **Pillar.** Perch-PILLAR / quill-totem — banded bone perch-pole wound with quill rings = shaft;
  cap = bird-skull with wings half-FOLDED into a hard scalloped fan, beak/eye glowing, talons
  gripping toward the gap line (bottom-weighted).
- **Hook.** A grumpy little bone-vulture spirit who eats the dead so they can fly — wings flared,
  beak agape, the cutest undertaker of the sky.
- **Tell / re-spec.** ONLY winged/aerial X-silhouette + ONLY beaked skull; wings = tiered hard
  quill-BLADES (bone splines, not Pazul's faceted membranes). Keep violet-grey desaturated.

### 5. koschei — deathless bone-sorcerer on a marrow-throne  (KIND: seated-throne)
- **Palette (RE-SPEC).** Corpse-tallow grey-green bone `(190,192,158)` (cooler/greener than warm-
  parchment — NOT Necrarch, NOT Mariachi) / olive-bone sh `(146,130,92)`; poison-chartreuse soul-egg
  glow `(190,222,96)` (the SINGLE warm focal — cupped egg); blackened-iron crown/throne `(58,54,60)`;
  INDIGO socket pin-glow `(110,118,212)` (migrated from cut Aghora; not Draugr/Yurei cyan); ink
  `(26,26,24)`; sheen `(238,232,206)`.
- **Pillar.** Femur-THRONE-COLUMN — stacked paired femurs lashed into a column = shaft (joint
  banding); cap = rib-spire throne-back fan with the egg-orb cradled at the gap, glowing. Bottom-
  rooted, symmetric.
- **Hook.** A skinny, sulking skeleton king clutching his secret death like a too-precious egg —
  grumpy immortal, dangerously smug.
- **Tell / re-spec.** ONLY enthroned/seated wide-base block; tall BLACKENED-IRON tomb-crown of thin
  bent spikes (NEVER skulls — reads off Citipati's 5-skull crown & Mukha-Devi's tiara). Egg-orb =
  the single warm focal so the cool body recedes; egg + indigo sockets pop on day AND night.
