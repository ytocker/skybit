# Koschei — round 1 critique (art-director)

Strong foundation. The seated wide-base block is unmistakable, the blackened-iron
spike crown lands the tell cleanly (zero skull confusion vs Citipati / Mukha-Devi),
and the corpse-tallow grey-green bone is correctly cooler/greener than the warm-bone
cousins. Indigo sockets pop on both day and night. This is one ITERATE pass from
ship-ready. The whole pass hinges on fixing the character hook: the cupped egg.

## Ranking of this round's aspects
- STRONGEST: spike-crown silhouette + seated wide-base block. Reads as "enthroned"
  at 32px instantly; the only seated mass in the roster — KIND is secure.
- WEAKEST: the lap soul-egg. The GD's flag is CORRECT and is the gating issue.

## RULING ON THE EGG-BLUR FLAG (gating — fix first)
The GD is right. At true 32px day the egg is a desaturated smudge that bleeds into
the tallow lap and effectively vanishes against the bright sky; at night it reads as
a tiny green leak, not a cradled egg. The cradling-finger arcs are pure noise at 1×.
Right now the character hook ("clutching his secret death like a too-precious egg")
does NOT survive to gameplay scale. This must read. Prescription:

1. **Dark socket behind the egg.** Set the egg into a hard ink/blackened-iron
   recess in the lap — a dark crescent or full dark cup BEHIND the egg so its rim
   has a high-contrast frame on every side. The dark gap is what makes "held object"
   read at 1×; the chartreuse alone won't separate from tallow bone.
2. **Clean ovoid + hard rim.** Redraw the egg as a single clean vertical ovoid with
   a crisp 1px ink rim and a tighter, brighter `egg hot core` highlight pinned
   top-left. Kill the lumpy contour it has now. At 32px it must read as an egg shape,
   not a round blob — push the vertical egg axis so it's taller than wide.
3. **Simplify the cradle to two thumbs.** Drop the finger-arc filigree entirely.
   Two fat, simple thumb/hand shapes meeting UNDER the egg (a cupped bracket) is all
   that's needed — at 32px those read as "hands holding," the thin arcs read as dirt.
   Bottom-cradle, not a side-claw wrap.
4. **Raise egg size + saturation ~15–20%.** It's the SINGLE warm focal; it's
   currently outgunned by the indigo eyes for attention. Make the egg the brightest,
   most-saturated thing in the figure so the cool body recedes as briefed. Verify the
   eyes do NOT out-glow the egg at 32px (right now on day they do).

## KEEP

- Bone cast + indigo sockets — palette is on-pins, distinct from the brood.

## FIX (beyond the egg)
- **Throne armrests read as detached bars at 32px** — the two vertical capped posts
  float free of the figure and look like external pillars, not arms-of-a-throne.
  Connect them to the seat base with a visible cross-member or bring them inboard so
  the throne reads as one mass, not skeleton + 2 sticks.
- **Face is a touch generic** — give the sulk one clear cue (downturned jaw line or
  heavier indigo brow shadow) so "grumpy immortal" reads, not neutral skull.
- **Night value:** the iron crown + armrests nearly vanish on the night sky (ink on
  indigo-dark). Add a hair of the olive-bone or a 1px cool rim-light to the crown
  spikes so the silhouette survives at night 32px.

## PILLAR
