# Skybit BOSS — BATCH 2 LOCKED BRIEFS (15 survivors, per-concept ready)

Final brainstorm lock. The AD culled 24→15 (5/5/5) and asked for surgical
palette/face fixes so survivors don't drift toward each other at 32px. Every
must-fix below is applied as a HARD pin, not a suggestion.

House style every brief inherits (unchanged from batch 1's warren-clown /
Big-Reapy grammar): CHIBI proportions (big head, short wide weight-shifted
body); FLAT saturated fills + hard 1–2px ink keylines `(28,22,30)`; form via
the **dark-core → flat-fill → top-left rim-sheen** TRIAD; silhouette POP via a
1px outline grown from the alpha mask; supersample → smoothscale. Scary-CUTE
menace, never grim realism. Each tall prop mirrors top↔bottom into a PILLAR
(repeatable shaft body + detachable gap-edge cap), the way Big Reapy's
bone-bident does.

AD must-fixes pinned globally:
- **Green-band separation** (Cernun / Tlaloc-Tiki / Kappa) — three unmistakable
  greens at 32px: deep desaturated PINE / greyer slate-jade-with-coral-focal /
  bright YELLOW-green.
- **Cool glows** — Yurei hitodama = blue-CYAN; Kitsune foxfire = MINT-green
  (two distinct cool glows, never traded).
- **Pazul** — turquoise wing membranes stay prominent as the cool counterweight
  to its desert-ochre.
- **Raijin** — grey body RECEDES; gold lightning + crimson drum-rims are the
  focal so it reads on dark-blue night biomes.
- **Mirror discipline** — all 15 props confirmed to mirror into a clean
  repeatable pillar; the two top-heavy risks (Cernun antler-staff, Raijin
  mallet+drum) are flagged with their fix below.

## SECTION 1 — DEVILISH

### Ifra — the smokeless-fire genie devil
- **Thesis.** An ifrit (Arabian fire-jinn) cuted to a cocky ember-imp whose
  lower half dissolves into a smoke-flame curl instead of legs. The bottle-genie
  devil: pure heat and swagger.
- **32px read.** Broad crossed-arms upper torso with two flat back-swept
  flame-horns, tapering at the waist into a billowing legless SMOKE-CURL base —
  the no-legs flame-wisp bottom is the unmistakable blob.
- **Construction.** Chibi ember torso, crossed beefy arms (classic genie pose),
  square smug face, glowing coal-eyes, single gold nose-ring; two flat horns;
  plume legs are HARD flat flame-lobes, triad-lit (dark-core / coral fill /
  saffron top-left sheen) — never a soft gradient. Floats, bobbing.
- **Prop → pillar.** Genie LAMP-PILLAR: fluted oil-lamp body = repeatable shaft
  (fluting banding); wick-spout puffing a fire-curl = gap-edge cap. Clean
  vertical mirror — lamp body is symmetric; no top-heavy risk.
- **PINNED PALETTE.** coral-ember body `(238,108,72)` · deep-rust shade
  `(176,62,42)` · violet smoke-plume accent `(118,86,150)` (the cool
  counterweight) · hot-saffron flame-core `(255,196,96)` · brass lamp + nose-ring
  `(214,168,72)` · ink `(34,20,24)` · sheen `(255,182,138)`. Dominant family:
  coral+violet+saffron — sole "live fire" hue in the set.
