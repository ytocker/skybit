# Catrina — round 3 critique (AD, confirming pass)

Round 2's gate is closed. The over-pinched neck that made the gown read as a
severed float is gone: the new shawl-collar bridges skull to body as one
continuous mass, the waist-pinch has dropped to a gentle mid-gown point, the
shoulders are broadened, and the hem now flares for bottom weight. In the
pure-black silhouette the figure reads as a single unbroken hat-to-hem column —
no sub-3px bridge anywhere, the skull sits ON a clear collar mass instead of
floating in a gap. The two top-risk failure modes (lollipop-on-a-stalk wobble,
vanishing skull on shrink) are both resolved. Catrina clears the bar.

## What landed (KEEP — do not touch)
- **Single hat-to-hem mass.** Continuous silhouette from brim to hem; the
  shawl-collar shoulder line gives the skull a stable platform. No detachment in
  silhouette or at the 32px / 48px chips. This was the whole job and it's done.
- **Plumed-couture read, distinct from Mariachi.** The 4-plume bouquet on the
  tilted cavalier brim erupts past the disc outline; at 32px and in pure black it
  says "plumed hat," never "sombrero disc." Sibling-drift stays resolved — the GD
  correctly did NOT re-open the solved hat.
- **Pinned palette.** Ash-white bone, marigold + hot-pink plumes, teal gown, gold
  trim — dead-on the pin and cleanly cool-couture against Mariachi's warm-bone
  family. Holds focal hierarchy day and night; the marigold hatband draws the eye
  without competing with the plume mass.
- **Parasol-pillar mirror.** Ribbed canopy + pointed ferrule reads unmistakably as
  a parasol at gap scale, on-axis and symmetric — not a lollipop/coin/dome. No
  top-heavy risk. Clean repeatable shaft.
- **Accessibility tell.** Wide-brim-plume + hourglass-gown shape carries the read
  with zero reliance on the marigold hue; value structure separates head/collar/
  gown without color. Solid for colorblind play.

## Strongest aspect
The body re-anchor. The shawl-collar is the right structural answer — it widens
the head-to-gown join into a couture shoulder line that is both on-thesis
(high-society dame) AND fixes the silhouette continuity, rather than just
fattening a neck. The hem flare counterweights the dominant hat so the figure
reads as a stable, slightly bottom-heavy chibi rather than top-heavy.

## Weakest aspect (polish-only, NOT blocking)
The gloved fan hands are a touch busy where they meet the gown at 32px — they
read as a small ambiguous fleck rather than a clear fan tell at the smallest
chip. This does not threaten the silhouette or the sibling distinction and is
well inside ship tolerance. If a free polish pass ever opens during integration,
simplify the fan to a single clean wedge; otherwise ship as-is.

## Bottom line
The defining problem (plume vs. sombrero) was solved in round 2 and the
side-effect (severed-float neck) is solved here without disturbing anything that
was working. Continuous figure, distinct couture read, pinned palette, clean
parasol mirror, hue-independent accessibility tell — all landed. SHIP-READY.
