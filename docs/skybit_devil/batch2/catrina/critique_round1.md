VERDICT: ITERATE

# Catrina — round 1 critique (AD)

Strong first pass on charm and palette. But the ONE thing this concept exists
to nail — a plumed couture brim that cannot be confused with Mariachi's flat
sombrero disc — is not yet solved at 32px. That gates the verdict.

## Strongest aspect
The palette is dead-on the pin: ash-white bone, marigold cluster, hot-pink
blooms, and the teal gown read as genuine "couture festive," cleanly separated
from Mariachi's warm-bone/ochre family. The hourglass gown + high collar + fan
silhouette is elegant and on-thesis. Triad lighting and ink keyline are
house-correct. Charm is there at hero scale.

## Weakest aspect (the gate)
The HAT is a flat disc, not a plumed brim. Side-by-side with the Mariachi
sibling at 32px the two brims are nearly the same shape: a flat circular
platter with a small bump on top (Catrina = marigold cluster, Mariachi =
sombrero crown). The brief's CRITICAL clause — "the plumed brim must read
distinct from Mariachi's flat sombrero disc" — fails. The single pink plume is
one thin diagonal stroke that disappears entirely by 48px and is gone at 32px.
Without the plume, Catrina IS a sombrero skeleton. This is the make-or-break.

## Ranked, actionable fixes for round 2

1. **Make the PLUME the silhouette, not a garnish.** The hat's defining mass
   must be feathers breaking the disc outline — not a flat platter with a stripe
   on top. Replace the single thin diagonal feather with a BOUQUET of 3–4 bold
   ostrich-style plumes arcing UP and OUT past the brim edge (asymmetric, tallest
   at back), each a fat triad-lit lobe with a soft barb-notched edge. Target: the
   plume cluster adds ~40% to the hat's bounding height and clearly breaks the
   circular outline so the 1px silhouette alone says "plumed hat," never "disc."
   This is the priority above all others.

2. **Tilt and shape the brim away from a flat platter.** Mariachi owns the
   perfectly flat horizontal disc. Give Catrina an asymmetric, slightly upswept
   couture brim — one side curled/dipped (cavalier tilt), an elliptical not
   circular footprint. A non-flat, non-symmetric brim edge is half the
   distinction even before the plumes land.

3. **Demote the marigolds from a centered crown-bump to a band.** Right now the
   marigold cluster sits dead-center on top like a sombrero crown — reinforcing
   the wrong read. Move blooms to a hatband nestled at the brim/crown join, off
   to one side, with the plumes rising behind them. Marigolds should accent, not
   form the top profile.

4. **Hold the brim-to-head ratio but verify it post-plume.** Brim width vs.
   head is good. After adding plumes, re-check the 32px chip: hat (brim + plume)
   should still be the dominant mass, clearly wider/taller than the gown.

5. **Parasol prop: differentiate the canopy from the sombrero too.** The prop
   canopy currently reads as a teal dome that rhymes with the hat-disc AND with
   Mariachi's round guitarrón cap. Give the parasol a scalloped/ribbed
   lace-edge (visible rib spokes + a pointed ferrule finial at top) so the
   gap-cap is unmistakably a parasol, not a generic dome. The marigold fringe is
   good — keep it.

6. **Pillar-mirror cap legibility at gap scale.** In the pillar mirror the
   canopy shrinks to a small teal blob with a dotted rim — fine for a clean
   mirror, but push the rib spokes and pointed finial so it still reads as
   "parasol" and not "lollipop/coin" against a busy sky.

7. **Face: protect the flower-socket tell.** The painted petal eye-sockets and
   stitched smile are charming at hero scale but mushy by 48px. Simplify to 2–3
   bold petal points per socket and a high-contrast stitch line so the
   sugar-skull read survives shrink — it's a key Catrina signature.

8. **Minor — gown sheen.** The top-left sheen on the teal gown is a touch broad
   and flattens the hourglass. Tighten it to a narrower collarbone/shoulder
   highlight to keep the couture-slim read.

## Accessibility note
Once the plume breaks the outline (fix #1), the shape tell is solid: plumed-hat
+ hourglass-gown carries the read with zero reliance on the marigold hue. Until
then, the disc+gown reads too close to Mariachi for colorblind separation. Fix
#1 resolves both the sibling-drift and the accessibility tell at once.

## House-style / batch drift
No batch-1 grammar drift — chibi proportions, flat fills, ink keyline, triad,
and outline POP are all consistent. Sibling drift with Mariachi is the only
real conflict, and it lives entirely in the hat. Solve the plume and Catrina
clears the bar.
