# Catrina — round 2 critique (AD, final critique for this concept)

The make-or-break from round 1 is SOLVED: the plumed couture brim now reads
unmistakably distinct from Mariachi's flat sombrero disc, and it survives all
the way down to 32px and into the pure-black silhouette. That clears the gate
that held the verdict. One genuine new problem surfaced as a side effect of the
narrowed body — the GD's own flag is correct — and it is a one-pass fix. I'm
holding the verdict at ITERATE only for that, framed below as the LAST round-3
brief. This is close.

## Strongest aspect
The hat. The 4-plume bouquet on the upswept, tilted (cavalier-dip) brim is
exactly the round-1 ask delivered: the plumes break the outline with fat
triad-lit lobes and barb-notched tips, the brim is an asymmetric ellipse with a
clear lift on one side, and the marigold cluster is demoted to a side hatband at
the brim/crown join instead of a centered sombrero-crown bump. In the black
silhouette the top mass reads "plumed hat" with zero color information — the
accessibility tell is now solid. Side-by-side with the Mariachi sibling at 32px
the two are no longer confusable: Mariachi = flat horizontal disc + crown bump;
Catrina = tilted brim erupting in a feather fan. Sibling-drift resolved. Palette
stays dead-on the pin (ash-white bone, marigold + hot-pink plumes, teal gown),
and the parasol prop now carries visible rib spokes + a pointed ferrule so the
gap-cap reads as a parasol, not a dome/coin. Charm and couture intact.

## Weakest aspect (the gate for round 3)
The gown-disconnect the GD flagged is REAL and it's the one thing keeping this
from ship-ready. In the pure-black silhouette the figure reads as three stacked
masses — wide hat / near-invisible neck pinch / gown — joined by a sliver so
thin the gown looks like it's about to detach and the head nearly disappears.
The hourglass got over-pinched in service of "couture-slim": at gameplay scale
and in motion the silhouette risks a "lollipop on a stalk" wobble, and the
skull (Catrina's signature face) is the narrowest point of the whole figure, so
it's the first thing to vanish on shrink. The hat read is so dominant now that
the body has lost the structural weight to anchor it.

## Ranked, actionable fixes for round 3 (final pass)

1. **Re-widen the neck/collar join so the gown never disconnects from the head.**
   This is the priority. In the silhouette the hat-to-body bridge must read as a
   continuous column, not a pinch-point. Widen the high collar and shoulders
   ~25–30% so the skull sits ON a clear collar mass rather than floating in a
   gap. Keep the hourglass, but move the waist-pinch DOWN to mid-gown and make it
   gentler — the narrowest point of the figure should be the waist, never the
   neck. Target: a single unbroken silhouette from brim to hem with no sub-3px
   bridge anywhere.

2. **Give the gown more bottom weight to counter the hat.** The hat is (correctly)
   the dominant mass, but the gown is now too slight to balance it — flare the
   hem/skirt wider at the base so the figure reads as a stable couture
   silhouette, not top-heavy. Bottom-heavy-ish body + wide hat is the chibi
   grammar; lean into it.

3. **Protect the skull at the pinch.** Once the collar widens (fix 1), re-check
   the 32px chip: the skull + flower-socket eyes must still read as the face,
   not get swallowed by the brim shadow above and the collar below. If the brim
   undershadow is crowding the face, lift the head 1–2px clear of the brim's
   back edge so the skull has air.

4. **Hold everything else — do not touch the hat.** The plume bouquet, tilt,
   hatband, palette, parasol ribs + ferrule, and triad/ink are all landed. This
   is a body-only revision; resist re-opening the solved hat.

5. **Minor — verify the gloved fan hands survive the body re-width.** The folding
   fan is a nice Catrina tell at hero scale; just confirm it doesn't muddy the
   gown silhouette once shoulders widen. If it adds noise at 32px, simplify to a
   single clean fan wedge.

## Pillar mirror
The parasol mirror is clean and on-axis — ribbed canopy + ferrule reads as a
parasol at gap scale, not a lollipop. No top-heavy risk. No change needed.

## Bottom line
The concept's defining problem is solved and the work is genuinely appealing.
ITERATE is purely to fix the over-pinched neck so the gown stays attached to the
head in silhouette — fixes 1–2 are the whole job. Land those and Catrina ships.
