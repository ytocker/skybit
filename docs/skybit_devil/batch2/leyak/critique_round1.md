VERDICT: ITERATE

# Leyak — Round 1 critique (Art Director)

A strong, on-brief first pass. The showcase head is genuinely scary-CUTE,
the palette is dead-on the pin, and the prop→pillar mirror is the cleanest
in either batch. But the design is FAILING its single most important job:
the **32px read inverts** — the head shrinks to an unreadable nub while the
trail dominates and goes to noise. The brief's whole thesis is "head on a
streamer," and right now it reads as "streamer with a speck." That gates a
RE-ROLL-adjacent fix, but the bones are right, so: ITERATE.

## Ranking of aspects (strongest → weakest)

1. **Palette match (excellent).** Ash-white face, hot-pink trail, deep-rose
   shade, violet bruise-rim, amber eyes — all reading as pinned. The
   ash-white/hot-pink pairing is unmistakably the bodiless-head lane; no
   collision with Yurei (white+ink-black+blue-cyan) or Necrarch (violet+bronze).
2. **House style (excellent).** Flat saturated fills, hard ink keyline, the
   dark-core→fill→top-left sheen triad is visible on every lobe, 1px outline
   pops the silhouette. Sits naturally beside Big Reapy's grammar.
3. **Prop→pillar mirror (excellent).** Rib-bead shaft tiles cleanly, the
   heart-pendant lantern is a convincing on-axis glowing gap-cap, and the 2x
   cap-band blow-up sells "radiates INTO the gap." No top-heavy risk. Ship this
   part nearly as-is.
4. **Showcase charm (very good).** The big-eyed tusk-grin lolling-tongue head
   is delightful and clearly devil-skeleton hybrid, not gore. Keep it.
5. **Distinctiveness (good).** Bodiless-head-on-streamer is one-of-a-kind in
   the set; the grayscale chip confirms a shape-led read at the showcase size.
6. **32px silhouette read (FAILING — the gate).** At true 32px the head is a
   3–4px ash blob with no legible face; the eyes/grin vanish and the icon reads
   as a pink squiggle with a dot. The thesis silhouette does not survive.
7. **Accessibility tell (WEAK at scale).** The grayscale + dusk chips prove the
   tell works at showcase size, but the brief pins the tell to carry at GAMEPLAY
   scale. At true 32px the trailing-ribbon shape is there but the head — the
   thing that makes it a CREATURE and not a rope — disappears. Value contrast
   head-vs-trail is fine; head-internal contrast (face features) is lost.

## KEEP (do not lose these next round)
- The exact pinned palette and the triad lighting on every lobe.
- The heart-pendant lantern cap and rib-bead shaft mirror — basically final.
- The showcase head's eye scale, tusk-grin width, and bat-ear flaps.
- The day/night showcase pair: the head holds value on both biomes there.

## FIX (prioritized punch list for GD round 2)

1. **Rebalance head:trail mass for the 32px icon — this is the whole brief.**
   At gameplay scale the HEAD must be the dominant, instantly-legible blob, not
   the trail. Target roughly **55–60% head / 40–45% trail** in the icon's
   vertical budget (it is currently ~30/70). Either grow the head or shorten the
   icon trail to 3–4 lobes max. The showcase can keep a longer trail; the
   delivered 32px must privilege the head.

2. **Give the 32px head a 1px "face tell" that survives downscale.** At true
   32px the eyes+grin are mush. Bake a deliberate low-res face: two dark
   eye-dots + a single dark grin-bar on the ash field, hinted at supersample so
   smoothscale preserves a recognizable skull-face at 32px. Test by viewing the
   true-32 chip at 1x BEFORE the 4x blow-up — if you can't tell it's a face,
   it's not done.

3. **Cut trail lobe-count and noise at 1x.** The trail currently carries ~8–9
   beads that alias into a speckled mess in the night chip. Drop the rendered
   trail to **5–6 hard lobes** with clearly alternating value (rose/hot-pink) so
   it reads as a deliberate wavy ribbon, not dithered noise, when scrolling.

4. **Fix the NIGHT face read.** In the night showcase the violet bruise-rim +
   dark sockets let the upper face partially sink into the dark-blue sky. Push
   the ash-white face value up ~10–15% and keep a thin sheen on the cranium top
   so the head stays a clean light blob against night biomes (mirror Raijin's
   "focal must pop on night" discipline).

5. **The amber pendant and amber eyes are competing.** Two amber focal glows
   (eyes + heart-lantern) split attention at small scale. Keep amber for the
   EYES (face is the hero), and let the pendant glow read via the hot-pink/rose
   heart shape with only a small warm core — so the eye is drawn to the face
   first, then the trail, then the cap.

6. **Tongue legibility.** The lolling tongue is charming at showcase but is the
   first thing to disappear/merge into the trail at 32px. Either tuck it shorter
   so it doesn't blur into the first trail-lobe, or drop it from the 32px icon
   variant entirely and keep it showcase-only.

7. **Minor polish.** The bat-ear flaps are slightly too long/sharp and read as
   horns rather than little ear-flaps at mid scale — round and shorten them ~15%
   so the "goofy" beats the "pointy-menacing." Keep the asymmetry subtle.

## Re-deliver
Next round's sheet must lead with a **true-32 head-dominant icon that reads as
a grinning skull on a short ribbon at 1x without the blow-up**. If that single
chip works, everything else here is already at ship quality.
