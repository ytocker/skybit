# Kitsune — round 2 critique (art-director, final)

The gate is cleared. Round 1's fatal flaw — the nine-tail fan reading as a
hedgehog sunburst instead of a peacock arc — is fully resolved. At the 32px
actual render (bottom-left), the dominant read is now unmistakably "wide
scalloped grey fan + small white face dot," exactly the brief's one-of-a-kind
silhouette. This ships.

## Strongest aspects
- THE FAN. Rebuilt as a wide bushy scalloped peacock arc that spreads clearly
  wider than the body footprint, with ~5 broad readable lobes (9 rendered,
  grouped). It is now the dominant mass and the icon of the design. This was
  the whole gate and it passes.
- VALUE HIERARCHY. The fan is pushed greyer (sampled lobes ~168,152,149 down
  to dark ~67,68,77 in the lower lobes) against the snow-white face
  (~194,189,190 at 32px / brighter at large size). The fox face pops FORWARD
  off a receded fan — the "small face in front of a wide fan" read the brief
  demanded. Internal silhouette hierarchy now exists where round 1 had one
  undifferentiated white blob.
- CORAL EYE-SPOT RHYTHM. The coral tips now read as a deliberate matched
  rhythm along the fan edge (the peacock eye-spots), not scattered frost
  spikes. Clean coordinated comb.
- MINT FOXFIRE. Still on-pin and unmistakably MINT, drop-shadowed by one ear,
  and now that the fan is darker it survives shrink as the one cool accent —
  clear daylight from Yurei's blue-cyan. Cool-glow separation: PASS.
- Face, ears, brow-blaze, amber eyes, torii pillar mirror — all the round-1
  keeps carried through intact.

## Weakest aspect (minor, not blocking)
- At 32px the foxfire orb is small and sits close to the face value-wise; it
  reads but is the first thing to soften in heavy weather. Not a ship blocker
  — the fan + face + coral rhythm carry the read on their own.
