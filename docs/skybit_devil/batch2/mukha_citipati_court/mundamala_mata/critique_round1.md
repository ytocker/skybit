VERDICT: ITERATE

The strongest concept of the brood, and it shows — the garland-framing device works, the
blackout/silhouette proof is the best in the set (garland + fan + crown collapse to one coherent
rounded mass), and it is emphatically NOT naked. Locked census passes: six-arm fan (low-origin,
sky open), six palms each cradling a tiny skull, fused crown (arc-sweep AND distinct tiara-band),
double-loop mundamala, carved bone apron, squat Mukha body + wide lotus base. But three
measurable problems keep it off SHIP-READY: the value ladder is collapsed, the garland reads
INVERTED at 32px (light pips, not a dark wreath), and palette dominance is inverted (gold out-
pacing oxblood; rose crept onto the eye sockets). All fixable in one round.

## FIX (prioritized)
1. **Fix the value ladder (the gate).** Measured: central skull L≈175, palm-skull L≈172, garland
   heads L≈145 — palms and central skull are the SAME value, garland only ~30L below. Target
   three separated bands: third-eye = single brightest (~255, keep) → six palm-skulls MID, pull
   DOWN to ~L150-160 + warm them slightly → garland heads + crown skulls DIMMEST, drop to ~L110-120
   (a visible 30-40L gap below the palms). Don't let four skull-types share one value.
2. **Garland reads INVERTED at 32px.** In both chips the swag shows as a ring of small LIGHT pips
   around a dark core — the brief wants ONE bold DARK wreath. Once #1 drops garland heads to ~L110
   the wreath reads dark against the day sky, with gold spacers the only sparkle (not confetti).
3. **Palette dominance inverted — oxblood must win, not gold.** Measured gold px (1212) ≈ 2×
   oxblood px (642). Thicken/darken the oxblood cord so the strung loop reads as a continuous dark
   line at hero; cut gold to true spacer cadence (~every 3rd-4th bead, not near-continuous). Gold
   accents the oxblood, doesn't replace it.
4. **Rose leaked onto the eye sockets — pull back to the third-eye only.** Sockets are filled
   (214,74,110) deep-rose = a 2nd/3rd rose focal flanking the brow. Make sockets neutral dark
   (ink/oxblood-shadow ~L30-45, empty wrathful sockets) and reserve rose for the brow gem alone.
   NOTE: hue has NOT drifted to the rejected magenta — your rose (225,136,159)/(214,74,110) is a
   warm dusky in-family. The problem is QUANTITY + PLACEMENT, not hue. Keep the hue, use it once.
5. **Two-scale: crown mushing at 32px.** Tiara-band (the fusion tell) vanishes. Carry it as one
   horizontal darker line across the brow at 32px so the fusion still reads; let the arc skulls
   collapse to a lumpy dark cap. The garland swag is your named 32px carrier — once #2 lands it
   carries the silhouette.
6. **Minor — palm placement.** The two lowest palms overlap the apron-edge garland heads and
   muddle. Nudge them ~4-6px outward so each palm-skull has clean negative space and the apron's
   chest-loop reads as a distinct lower band.
7. **Pillar — clean, keep it.** Grounded in the sister's forms, clean on-axis mirror. Once #3
   lands (less gold), re-cadence the pillar spacers to match so the shaft isn't more gold-heavy
   than the hero garland.

## KEEP (do not lose)
- The blackout silhouette — best in the brood. Don't let the value/cadence fixes break the
  unified swag-fan-crown mass.
- The carved bone apron with vertical ribbing — the decisive "not naked" element; keep its density.
- The dusky-rose/oxblood hue family (correctly Mukha's moodier family, NOT magenta).
- Reverence — the severed-head garland reads dignified; cuteness in proportion only.

## Net
One round on the value ladder (#1), the dark-wreath read (#2), and the oxblood/gold rebalance
(#3) should clear this to SHIP-READY.
