# mukha_citipati_court — GD BRAINSTORM (5 sister directions)

Five **sister** bone-deities of one form — the **Mukha-Citipati Court** — fusing the
two references: **Mukha-Devi's six-arm radial fan** (the brood silhouette) with
**Citipati's danced charnel-lord vocabulary** (crown arc + flame-halo + cocked-hip
body). They are SISTERS, not distinct KINDs: the silhouette, the fan, and the
scary-CUTE chibi grammar are shared. Novelty lives ONLY on the four open axes —
**body base / dominant ornament SET / palette direction / crown-&-skull treatment.**

The prior brood read **"NAKED."** These deliberately go **DENSER than either
reference** — each sister wears a fully realised Tibetan wrathful-deity ornament
SET drawn from the researched charnel-ground vocabulary (the "five-or-six bone
ornaments": crown, earrings, neck/chest ornament, apron/girdle, armlets/anklets;
plus mundamala, naga sacred-thread, silk vajra-scarf, prabhamandala flame-halo,
lotus throne, jewel-inlay + tassels + beadwork). Gold-on-bone is the high-end tell
on every one. No two sisters share a palette OR an ornament-set.

---

## LOCKED on all five (non-negotiable)

1. **Mukha-Devi's SIX-ARM radial fan** (`draw_arm_fan`: low shoulder origin,
   ±100/64/28° off vertical, two-segment fat bone limbs, none aimed straight up so
   a clean wedge of open sky sits above the crown).
2. **All six arm-ends are open PALMS, each cradling a TINY SKULL** (a `tiara_skull`
   cranium per hand) — this is the core motif and REPLACES Mukha's relic-discs.
3. **A fused above-head SKULL-CROWN** = Citipati's 5-skull crown arc + Mukha's
   3-skull tiara → ONE richer multi-skull crown seated in the open sky over the head.
4. **Body base is split across the five**: ~3 **Citipati-bodied** (tall rib-barrel
   dancing skeleton, cocked hip) / ~2 **Mukha-bodied** (squat chibi rib-barrel +
   wide lotus base). Made explicit per sister below.
5. **DENSE ornament** — each picks a DOMINANT set from the wrathful vocabulary so no
   sister reads naked; gold accents on bone everywhere.
6. **Higher resolution**: `SS=8`, larger hero canvas + a standalone hi-res hero
   export. (Noted here; the per-sister loops implement it.)

Third eye stays the single **brightest pixel** on every sister (the face must win
inside the fan). Pipeline cloned from the two reference scripts: flat triad
(`triad_blob`/`triad_circle`) + ink keyline (28,22,26) + 1px grown outline.

**BODY-BASE SPLIT (confirmed): 3 Citipati / 2 Mukha.**

- Mukha-bodied:  **mundamala_mata**, **ratna_padmini**

### 1. asthi_dakini — the bone-jewel sky-dancer  (CITIPATI body)
- **One-line thesis.** A flamenco-cocked skeleton-dancer whose whole body is
  *strung* in white bone-bead lattice — the six-ornament dakini, jewelled in her
  own kind.
- **Body base.** **Citipati** — tall rib-barrel, cocked hip, one knee kicked out,
  the danced silhouette.
- **Dominant ornament SET.** **Multi-strand bone-bead jewelry** (the densest "rus
  rgyan" read): a tight 3-row bead **choker**, a long looping **chest necklace** that
  swags between the shoulders, beaded **armlets + bracelets + anklets** ringing every
  limb, big **wheel earrings**, and a beaded **girdle** at the hip. Carved-bone discs
  with tiny gold spacer-pips — lattice, not blobs. This is what kills "naked": every
  bone surface is ringed in beadwork.
- **Palette direction.** **Cool moonlit bone** — blue-white bone (BONE pushed
  toward ~(214,222,234) / shade ~(150,164,190)), **icy-cyan** focal glow
  (~(120,224,224)), **pale-gold** spacer beads (~(232,206,128)). Reads silver-and-ice
  — distinct from every warm sister.
- **Crown/skull treatment.** A **wide airy ~6-skull fan-crown** (Citipati arc widened,
  +1 skull), each crown-skull bead-ringed at its base; lit centre skull in cyan.
  Airy/light so it never clumps.
- **Third-eye / focal.** Vertical **icy-cyan** slit, hot-white core — the one cool
  pixel that says "looking at you."
- **32px tell.** A cool blue-white dancer *crusted in bead-rows*, six skull-palms,
  wide light skull-fan over the head.
- **Non-naked guarantee.** Bead-strands wrap EVERY limb + torso + hip + ears — the
  busiest surface texture of the five.

### 2. vajra_rakta — the wrathful blood-scarf adept  (CITIPATI body)
- **One-line thesis.** The fiercest sister: a dancing charnel-lord wrapped in
  storm-billowing brocade silk scarves shot through with vajra-cross motifs.
- **Body base.** **Citipati** — cocked-hip dance, but arms read more *thrusting/wrathful*
  than balletic; the silks add the storm of motion.
- **Dominant ornament SET.** **Silk scarves / sashes with a brocade pattern motif**
  (the billowing **vajra-scarf**): two long crossed sashes loop shoulder-to-hip and
  **flare out past the fan** in flame-edged tails, each carrying a repeated **gold
  vajra-cross / dot brocade** print; a wrap-sash at the waist with hanging brocade
  tassels. The flying silk is the dominant mass — fabric, not bone, dominates the
  outline. Add a thin **flame-halo** behind the head (prabhamandala) for the wrathful
  field, kept an OPEN ring so it doesn't swamp the crown.
- **Palette direction.** **Cinnabar-&-saffron wrath** — warm-ivory bone, **deep
  cinnabar** silk (~(196,52,46)) with **saffron-gold** brocade (~(232,186,84)), a thin
  **turquoise** scarf-trim sliver. Reads red-and-gold — the saturated-warm sister.
- **Crown/skull treatment.** Tall **5-skull crown arc** (Citipati-true) backed by the
  open flame-ring; centre skull lit ember. Densest *vertical* crown of the set.
- **Third-eye / focal.** Vertical **violet** wisdom slit (cool against all the warm
  silk) with a white core — kept the brightest by sitting in the calm bone face amid
  the chaos.
- **32px tell.** A red-and-gold whirl of patterned silk with a tall skull-crown,
  flame-rim, six skull-palms.
- **Non-naked guarantee.** Crossed billowing brocade sashes + waist wrap + tassels +
  flame-halo fill all negative space around the dance.

### 3. naga_kapali — the serpent-thread skull-priest  (CITIPATI body)
- **One-line thesis.** A charnel-lord girt by living naga-serpents — the sacred
  thread is a coiling snake, and skull-cups hang where the coils knot.
- **Body base.** **Citipati** — cocked-hip dance, but quieter/grounded so the
  serpent coils read as the moving line.
- **Dominant ornament SET.** **Naga sacred-thread + skull-cup hangings**: a green
  serpent loops as the diagonal **brahmin-thread (upavita)** across the chest, head
  rearing at one shoulder with a tiny gold crown, tail wrapping the opposite hip; a
  second smaller naga rings the neck as a **choker**. At the thread's low knots hang
  small **gold-rimmed kapala skull-cups** with a glow inside. Coiled-rope texture +
  scale-pips everywhere give the dense read.
- **Palette direction.** **Jade-naga & verdigris** — warm-bone, **jade-green** serpent
  (~(64,168,120)) with **darker-jade** scale shade (~(36,108,80)), **gold** crown/cup
  rims, an **amber** glow (~(240,176,84)) in the skull-cups. Reads bone-and-green —
  the only cool-green sister.
- **Crown/skull treatment.** **5-skull crown arc** with a **rearing-naga centerpiece**
  hooding the middle skull (serpent + skull fused at the crown apex) — the loudest
  single crown silhouette of the set.
- **Third-eye / focal.** Vertical **amber** slit, white core (warm focal against the
  green so it pops).
- **32px tell.** A green snake coiling diagonally across a bone dancer, hooded-snake
  crown, six skull-palms, green choker.
- **Non-naked guarantee.** Two serpents (thread + choker) plus hanging skull-cups plus
  scale-texture wrap the torso, neck and hips — coiled line everywhere.

### 4. mundamala_mata — the severed-head garland mother  (MUKHA body)
- **One-line thesis.** The squat lotus-throned mother who literally *wears the dead*:
  a heavy looping mundamala of severed heads is her dominant garment.
- **Body base.** **Mukha** — squat chibi rib-barrel on a wide 6-petal lotus base
  (her grounded, throne-seated read).
- **Dominant ornament SET.** **Mundamala (skull/severed-head garland collar)** as the
  hero ornament: a long double-loop garland of alternating fresh-head + bare-skull
  beads on a red cord, swagging deep across the chest and dipping into the open sky
  beside the crown to FRAME the face in a hammock of heads; gold spacer-discs and a
  central gold clasp. Backed by a heavy **bone apron/girdle** of carved plaques over
  the lotus. Garland + apron together = wall-to-wall ornament.
- **Palette direction.** **Dusky rose & oxblood** (Mukha's family, pushed darker) —
  dusty rose-bone, **oxblood-red** cord (~(150,44,52)), **rose** head-glow
  (~(232,86,150)) used sparingly, **gold** spacers. Reads rose-and-oxblood — Mukha's
  own family but moodier; no other sister uses rose.
- **Crown/skull treatment.** A **fused 6-skull crown** (Mukha 3-tiara grown by +3, the
  garland's "graduation" up onto the head); modest height, wide, seated low — the
  garland is the loud element, not the crown.
- **Third-eye / focal.** Vertical **rose** slit, hot white-rose core (Mukha-true; the
  garland heads kept a notch dimmer so the brow eye wins).
- **32px tell.** A squat lotus mother draped in a heavy swag of head-beads, six
  skull-palms, low wide skull-crown.
- **Non-naked guarantee.** The garland fills the whole chest + frames the face; the
  apron plaques cover the lotus front — almost no bare bone shows.

### 5. ratna_padmini — the jewel-lotus throne mother  (MUKHA body)
- **One-line thesis.** The opulent, gentlest sister — a jewel-encrusted bodhisattva-
  empress enthroned on a prabhamandala-haloed lotus, dripping tassels and inlay.
- **Body base.** **Mukha** — squat chibi rib-barrel + wide lotus base, here grown into
  a full **tiered lotus throne**.
- **Dominant ornament SET.** **Jewel inlay + tassels + beadwork + lotus throne +
  flame-halo** (the regalia set): a teardrop **gem-pendant necklace** of inlaid
  cabochons, **jewelled armlets/bracelets**, a **tasselled girdle** with strings of
  beads + bells swinging at the hips, a tiered **multi-petal lotus throne** with a gem
  at each petal tip, and a full **prabhamandala flame-halo** ringing the whole figure
  (the only sister with a complete enclosing halo). Inlay + tassels everywhere = dense
  and rich rather than wrathful.
- **Palette direction.** **Turquoise-&-gold treasure** — warm-bone, **turquoise**
  cabochons (~(64,176,170)) as the cool jewel focal, lavish **gold** inlay/halo
  (~(224,182,84) → bright), a thin **coral** tassel-tip accent (~(244,128,110), a hair
  warm of rose, clear of Mukha's magenta and KFC red). Reads gold-and-turquoise — the
  treasure sister.
- **Crown/skull treatment.** A **gem-studded ~5-skull crown** — each crown-skull set
  with a turquoise brow-cabochon and ringed in gold; softest, most jewelled crown.
- **Third-eye / focal.** Vertical **turquoise** slit with white core — the cool jewel
  that matches the cabochons and stays brightest.
- **32px tell.** A gold-and-turquoise enthroned mother inside a full flame-halo ring,
  tassels swinging, six skull-palms, jewelled skull-crown.
- **Non-naked guarantee.** Full enclosing halo + tiered gem-tipped throne + tasselled
  girdle + cabochon necklace leave essentially no empty bone or empty sky.
