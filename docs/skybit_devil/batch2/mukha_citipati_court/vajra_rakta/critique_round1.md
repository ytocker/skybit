## FIX (prioritized)
1. **Kill the bare upper-torso zone (top priority).** Silk starts at the waist; rib-barrel +
   shoulders are exposed bone. Bring the crossed sashes UP and OVER the shoulders — two diagonal
   brocade bands crossing the chest (classic vajra-silk X), passing behind the arm-fan origins,
   meeting at the waist knot. The sternum X reads at 32px as a bright chest mark. Target: ≤1 rib-
   band of bare bone between collar and belt.
2. **Make the silk BILLOW, not hang flat.** Replace the two straight-edged triangles with 3-4
   curved overlapping fabric lobes per side, scalloped/wave trailing edges flaring OUTWARD past
   the widest fan-arm so the lower silhouette is WIDER than the fan. Add 1-2 inner fold-shade
   planes (the `silk fold-shade` palette entry is barely used) for volume. Blackout must read as
   a bell/skirt, not a tapering wedge.
3. **Rescue the flame-ring — an OPEN ring of distinct tongues.** Currently a featureless saffron
   disc behind the crown. Convert to 5-7 separated ember tongues (use `ember flame-ring` orange,
   hotter than the saffron brocade so it separates by hue+value) arcing around the crown's outer
   edge L+R, explicitly NOT closing across the top (keep the open-sky wedge). Carry 2-3 tongues
   down each side; each a clear pointed lick with the ink keyline, not a blob.
4. **Fix the 32px silk read (named carrier).** Once FIX 2 widens the silhouette, verify the
   blackout reads as a wide skirt at 32px. Brighten the 32px silk base ~10-15% and place the gold
   lattice on a regular grid of ~3-4 visible dots (deliberate ornament, not noise). Add a 1px
   warmer rim on the silk outer edge so it doesn't bleed into the night chip.
5. **Night-chip legibility.** Lower body + tassels go near black-on-navy. After FIX 4 confirm
   the silhouette reads at arm's length; the grown outline may need 1px lighter (toward bone-
   ivory) on the silk outer contour for night separation. Crown/fan/face survive night fine.
6. **Pillar (low priority).** Clean, on-axis, grounded in the sister's forms. Once hero silk
   softens (FIX 2), echo a hint of that curve on the pillar's red silk tabs so they read as cloth.
7. **Minor — eye sockets.** Keep the socket red pure shadow-value; don't let it creep brighter
   than the violet third-eye (currently the third-eye correctly wins — hold it).
