# Jiangshi-epic — LOCKED 5 (per-concept loop briefs)

Lineage anchor: shipped **Jiangshi** — rigid arms-out hopping Qing corpse with a vertical
talisman-pole pillar. These 5 are distinct NEW bosses sharing that DNA loosely (rigid / sealed
/ bound undead or revenant, ceremonial East-Asian flavor, vertical ceremonial pole/scroll/
banner pillar). Elevated house style: chibi + scary-CUTE + flat triad (dark-core → flat-fill →
top-left rim-sheen) + 1–2px ink keyline `(28,22,30)` + 1px grown outline; procedural-only, no
gradients/PNGs. Push EPIC via bigger render scale, SS=5–6, more geometry, richer triad, stronger
glow. Each pillar grounded in the creature's own forms; clean top↔bottom mirror; no top-heavy
cap.

Folder per concept: `docs/skybit_devil/batch2/jiangshi_epic/<slug>/round_N.png` +
`critique_roundN.md` + `render_<slug>.py`. (Jiangshi lineage → standalone pygame renderers;
run with `SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy`.)

**Cross-set fix (HARD):** Citipati, Xinniang, Vetala all sit in the red→amber arc — separate by
VALUE/SATURATION not hue: Xinniang owns the ONE saturated-red mass; Citipati ivory-dominant,
red only as accent + thin ember ring; Vetala pale bone with a DARK desaturated ox-blood. No
concept lets its red grow into a second saturated mass. The three cinnabar accents
(Citipati crown / Jangseung hanja / Zhenmushou horns) stay thin LINEAR accents on different
bases — never a mass.

---

## 1. citipati — dancing charnel skeleton-lord
- **Thesis:** the ONLY motion silhouette in the roster — a cocked-hip dancing skeleton-lord;
  the perfect anti-Jiangshi (rigid → kinetic).
- **Silhouette:** wide cocked-hip dancing stance, one knee kicked out, arms in a flamenco
  flourish; FIVE-SKULL CROWN ARC + thin FLAME-HALO RING are the unmistakable top read. No hat,
  no instrument, no scythe/bident (off Mariachi + reapers).
- **Palette:** warm-IVORY bone dominant; cinnabar crown/sash ACCENT only; ember-orange a thin
  HALO RING (not a mass); turquoise sash-trim a sliver; gold scepter accent. Reads ivory-and-fire.
- **Grounded pillar:** khatvanga spine-staff = stacked vertebra beads (the torso's rib bands
  continued); cap = single crown-skull with a small flame-ring at the gap. Spine on-axis,
  symmetric.
- **Elevated detail:** five distinct crown skulls, lobed flame-halo ring, rib-band shading,
  vertebra bead facets.
- **Watch:** flame-halo a clean lobed RING not a busy fire field; crown reads as five skulls
  at 1×.

## 2. zhenmushou — antlered sancai tomb-guardian beast
- **Thesis:** the only quadruped BEAST-block in the lineage; bottom-heavy seated mass reads
  instantly, never top-heavy.
- **Silhouette:** squat seated beast on straight forelegs, wide lion-mask face, two upswept red
  horns, a back-fan of exactly 5 triangular spine-plates. Bottom-rooted.
- **Palette:** cream-glaze base + amber-glaze mid-band (earned extra band) + brown-OLIVE
  ceramic accent (NOT leaf green — clear of Cernun pine / Kappa yellow-green / source jade);
  oxidized-red horns; kiln-amber mouth glow. Sancai lane nobody owns.
- **Grounded pillar:** spine-spike stela = column of the same triad-lit spike-plates stacked
  edge-on with crackle-glaze banding; cap = smaller mirrored beast-mask with a glowing amber
  kiln-mouth. Bottom-rooted, on-axis.
- **Elevated detail:** crackle-glaze seams as hard hairline triad grooves (sparse), glaze
  pooling bands, mane curls, horn ridges.
- **Watch:** 5 spikes max so the fan reads as a fan not fringe; olive stays brown-ceramic;
  seams sparse.

## 3. xinniang — vermilion ghost-marriage corpse bride
- **Thesis:** the only all-red, hidden-face BELL silhouette; the single most distinct color
  block in the set and the strongest pure-charm/dread hook.
- **Silhouette:** tall narrow bell-robe to the floor; SQUARE hanging veil over a hidden face
  with two faint glowing dead-eye dots; gold fengguan phoenix-crown scalloped arc on top; two
  bound silk hands clasped at the waist. Square-veil-under-crown is the tell.
- **Palette:** SATURATED VERMILION robe dominant (the warmest MASS in the set) + gold hero
  metal; jade hairpin a literal sliver (lineage tell); faint warm veil-glow. No bone shown.
- **Grounded pillar:** wedding-lantern dowry pole = red silk bands + hanging gold
  double-happiness medallions (echo the hem); cap = round wedding-lantern with a gold tassel.
  Round on-axis.
- **Elevated detail:** scalloped phoenix-crown filigree, gold hem motif, veil weave, medallion
  glyphs.
- **Watch:** gold crown-arc + two eye-dots + gold hem must carry the read so the red bell isn't
  a plain cone; pin gold value-contrast against the red.

## 4. jangseung — carved guardian totem-post
- **Thesis:** the purest creature = prop = pillar mirror (as Karakasa is for its parasol), with
  the cleanest folklore-true twin-face cap.
- **Silhouette:** tall narrow carved POST; oversized comic-fierce face filling the top third
  (bug eyes, fat bulb-nose, fanged grin), tall official's hat-block; a vertical column of
  cinnabar hanja down the front; no limbs.
- **Palette:** weathered SLATE-WOOD base (cool grey-brown driftwood — clearly cooler/greyer than
  Zhenmushou's warm cream-wood); cinnabar hanja/face-paint focal; moss-TEAL patches (aged jade
  tell); warm-cream eye glow.
- **Grounded pillar:** creature IS the pillar = more carved-wood column + cinnabar hanja + moss;
  cap = twin mirrored partner-face (folklore-true pair), eyes glowing, mouth lit at the gap.
  On-axis, symmetric — cleanest mirror in the set.
- **Elevated detail:** carved bevel grooves, grain lines, moss-patch stippling, hanja column.
- **Watch:** bulb-nose + bug-eyes BIG and few (one-face read); resist piling cracks that fuzz
  the face at 1×.

## 5. vetala — inverted bat-hung charnel revenant
- **Thesis:** the single UPENDED silhouette across both batches — head-down is a 32px read no
  other boss can collide with. The distinctness pick.
- **Silhouette:** UPSIDE-DOWN — clawed grip-feet hooked at TOP, wrapped tapering body hanging
  down, fanged chibi head at the BOTTOM (eyes read right-way-up), arms folded up as furled
  bat-wing membranes; tongue points UP (gravity gag). Bottom-weighted pendulum.
- **Palette:** CONFIRMED maroon lane. Dusty bone-wrap dominant; blood-MAROON membrane/accent
  kept DARK + DESATURATED (duller than Xinniang's vermilion); hot-amber eyes + gap glow; slate
  branch (prop only).
- **Grounded pillar:** charnel-branch = wrapped branch with the same hard wrap-banding + hooked
  claw-marks; cap = the bat-wings UNFURLED into a hard scalloped fan with a glowing focal at the
  gap. Wing-fan on-axis; pendulum hangs toward the gap (bottom-weighted, not top-heavy).
- **Elevated detail:** wrap-band wraps, membrane rib-struts, claw hooks, fang grin.
- **Watch:** inverted face must read as a FACE first at 1× when flipped; maroon stays dark
  desaturated ox-blood (never vermilion).
