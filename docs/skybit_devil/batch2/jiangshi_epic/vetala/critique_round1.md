VERDICT: ITERATE

# Vetala — Round 1 critique (inverted bat-hung charnel revenant)

Strong, genuinely distinct lead. The inverted-hang silhouette is the set's
real differentiator and round 1 commits to it hard — clawed grip-feet hook the
branch at top, the wrapped body tapers DOWN, the fanged chibi face sits at the
bottom with eyes right-way-up, and the pendulum hangs toward the gap. That is
exactly the brief. House style reads true: flat triad fills, hard ink keyline,
1px outline, no gradient creep. Maroon is correctly DARK desaturated ox-blood
and stays well duller than Xinniang's vermilion. This clears RE-ROLL easily.
It does NOT yet clear SHIP-READY — the single most important promise (the
inverted face must read as a FACE FIRST at true 32px) is only half-landed, and
the right-side panels are clipped off the canvas. ITERATE.

## Strongest aspect
The hero pose + bottom-weighted distinctness. At a glance, full-size, it is
unmistakably its own creature: head-down, wing-furled, nothing else in either
batch collides with it. The wrap-banding down the body and the furled-membrane
arms with rib-struts are clean, characterful, and on-medium.

## Weakest aspect
The true-32px FACE read. On the day chip the bottom face collapses to a small
pale-amber pill — you read "amber blob hanging off a maroon mass," not "fanged
upside-down face." Two amber eye-dots are there but the fang grin and the
right-way-up orientation do not survive the downscale. At night it is worse:
the maroon membrane mass nearly merges with the dark-blue sky, the bone-wrap
column becomes the whole read, and the face is a faint dot. The brief's gate —
FACE first at 1x, day AND night — is not met yet.

## KEEP
1. Inverted construction + bottom-weighted pendulum. Do not touch the gross
   pose; it is the concept.
2. DARK desaturated ox-blood. `108,40,...` membrane + `70,26,32` shade are
   correctly duller than Xinniang. Hold this line — do NOT brighten the maroon
   to win the night read (see FIX 2 for the correct lever).
3. Amber restricted to eyes + gap focal. Correct and on-brief.
4. Wrap-band + rib-strut + claw-hook detail on the hero. Reads as elevated, not
   noisy, at hero scale.
5. Flat-triad + ink keyline discipline. No gradient drift. Good.

## FIX (prioritized punch list)
1. **Sheet width / clipping (HARD, do first).** The palette panel, the night
   32px chip, and the entire "Face read" aid panel run off the 1040px right
   edge — swatch labels, the night gameplay strip, and the face-aid caption are
   truncated. Widen the canvas (≈1240–1280px) OR reflow the right column so
   nothing clips. I cannot fully sign off a face-aid panel I can't see.
2. **Win the 32px face by VALUE + GEOMETRY, not hue.** Keep maroon dark; instead
   (a) enlarge the bottom head ~15–20% relative to the body so the face owns
   more pixels at 1x; (b) push the bone/ivory face value brighter and the ink
   keyline around the face heavier so the head pops off both sky and the maroon
   wings; (c) give the two amber eyes a hard 1px dark socket rim so they stay
   two distinct dots, not a single smear, when downscaled. The fang grin must
   survive — see FIX 4.
3. **Night read: separate the maroon membrane from the dark-blue sky.** Right
   now the wings sink into night. Don't brighten the maroon — instead add a
   thin (1px) cooler-bone or top-left rim-sheen edge on the wing membranes so
   their SILHOUETTE edge survives against `~20,30,55` night sky. The bone-wrap
   column already separates fine; it's the dark membrane that vanishes.
4. **Fang grin geometry at 1x.** At true 32px the mouth reads as a flat band.
   Drop to 2–3 BIG triangular fangs with a dark mouth-gap behind them so the
   grin reads as a grin (the same lesson as Karakasa's tongue and Citipati's
   skull). The lolling-UP tongue gag is charming at hero scale but is the first
   thing to sacrifice pixels for — let the fangs + eyes carry the face, keep the
   tongue as a small amber-pink nub only if it survives the downscale.
5. **Pillar bottom-weight verification.** Panel (b) is partly clipped, but from
   what's visible the wrap-banding + amber gap-focal cap read correctly and the
   pendulum hangs toward the gap. Confirm in the reflowed sheet that BOTH the
   top cap and bottom cap mirror cleanly and that neither cap reads top-heavy —
   the amber focal should sit AT the gap line, mass dropping toward it.
6. **Bat-wing scalloped-fan cap distinctness.** Make sure the unfurled
   scalloped-fan cap reads as bat-wings-unfurled, not as a generic fan — the
   hooked claw-marks + scallop count are the tell that grounds the pillar in the
   creature's own forms. Show it un-clipped next round.

## Distinctness check (PASS)
Clearly distinct from Necrarch (violet upright lich, orb-cradling, feet-up
not head-down) and Karakasa (single-eye upright parasol). The inverted hang is
the unique axis. No notes — hold it.

## Cross-set color check (PASS, hold)
Bone-wrap dominant: yes. Maroon dark desaturated ox-blood, duller than
Xinniang's vermilion: yes. Amber to eyes + gap only: yes. The only risk is FIX
3 tempting a maroon brighten for the night read — explicitly do NOT; use rim
value, not hue.
