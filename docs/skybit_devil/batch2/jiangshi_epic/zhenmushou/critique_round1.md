# Zhenmushou — Round 1 critique (antlered sancai tomb-guardian beast)

Strong bones. The seated-beast block is the right read, the sancai brown-olive
lane is correctly distinct from the green trio, and the hero clears the
distinctness gate against Tlaloc-Tiki and Jangseung. But three brief-mandated
reads are NOT landing yet — the **5-plate back-fan is invisible**, the **pillar
inverts the brief's mass logic (it's a top-heavy descending-arrow column, not a
bottom-rooted stela)**, and the **horns are too thin/limp to read as horns at
32px**. Solid foundation needing a targeted second pass, not a re-roll.

---

## Ranking — strongest -> weakest aspect

1. **Palette / sancai fidelity (STRONGEST).** Cream-glaze base + amber mid-band +
   brown-olive accent sits exactly in the ceramic lane the brief wants. Clearly
   warm-ceramic, NOT leaf-green, and reads nothing like Cernun pine, Kappa
   yellow-green, or source jade. The kiln-amber mouth glow is a genuine focal.
   Shippable as-is.
2. **Seated-beast silhouette / bottom-heaviness.** The squat block on straight
   forelegs reads as a grounded quadruped beast at gameplay scale — the
   "never top-heavy" creature goal is met on the hero.
3. **Face / lion-mask.** Wide, fierce-cute, good amber eyes, fanged grin with
   ivory fangs. Charming and legible. Minor fixes only.
4. **Horns.** Present and correctly oxidized-red as a THIN linear accent
   (cross-set rule honored), but too whippy/thin to survive 32px — they thin to
   near-nothing at the chip scale.
5. **Back-fan of 5 spine-plates (WEAKEST on hero).** Cannot find the fan. Olive
   mass at the shoulders/sides reads as a hunched mane or pauldrons, not as a
   discrete 5-plate fan rising behind the beast. The brief's signature epic read
   is effectively absent at every scale.
6. **Pillar (WEAKEST overall).** The shaft is a stack of downward-pointing
   chevron/arrow plates that visually FLOW DOWNWARD and pile mass at the TOP —
   the exact opposite of the brief's "bottom-rooted spine-spike stela." The
   crackle-glaze banding is missing, and the two caps don't read as a clean
   mirrored pair.

---

## Per-aspect: KEEP / FIX

### Hero — seated antlered beast
- **KEEP:** the bottom-heavy seated block; the amber kiln-mouth glow; the
  cream->amber->olive value structure; fierce-cute face and fang read.
- **FIX — the 5-plate back-fan does not exist as a fan.** #1 issue. At 1x and at
  32px the back reads as a blobby mane/shoulder mass. Brief is explicit: "5-plate
  back-fan must read as a FAN not fringe." Build five DISCRETE hard-edged
  triangular plates rising behind the head/shoulders, each separated by an
  ink-keyline gap, fanned (center plate tallest, outer plates shorter and raked
  outward) so the negative-space notches between plates are visible. Triad-light
  each plate independently (olive dark-core -> amber fill -> cream top-left sheen)
  so the plate count survives downscale. At 32px you should still count distinct
  top-edges, not a serrated lump.
- **FIX — horns too thin.** They taper to a 1px wisp and vanish on the night chip.
  Thicken the base ~40-60%, keep two clean upswept tapered prongs (still thin
  LINEAR cinnabar accent — do NOT let red grow into a mass), and give each a
  single ridge groove for the "horn ridges" elevated detail. They should read as
  two firm antler-horns at 32px, not antennae.
- **FIX — olive shoulder blocks are ambiguous and not cleanly mirrored.** They
  read as mane? ears? pauldrons? Commit to mane-curls (brief lists them as
  elevated detail) as a tidy symmetric scallop frame around the lion-face, and
  let the back-fan own the spiky job. One spiky system, not two competing ones.
- **FIX — forelegs read a touch spindly** for a beast-block. Widen them slightly
  and root the paws with clear ground-contact so the bottom-heavy mass is
  unambiguous.

### Pillar — spine-spike stela
- **FIX — invert the mass logic (critical).** The current shaft is a column of
  downward-pointing arrowheads; the eye reads DOWNWARD flow and the widest mass
  sits at the top — the top-heavy failure the brief forbids. Rebuild the shaft as
  specified: **stacked spike-PLATES edge-on** — the hero's back-fan plate motif
  repeated up the column, each plate UPSWEPT (pointing up/outward, echoing the
  horns and fan), widest plates toward the BOTTOM tapering as they rise, so the
  stela reads bottom-rooted and grounded.
- **FIX — add the crackle-glaze banding.** Absent. Per brief and cross-set rule:
  SPARSE hairline triad grooves (a few per shaft segment, hard 1px), NOT a
  granulate mesh — too many granulate to noise at 1x. ~2-3 hairline seams per
  plate band, irregularly placed, dark-core groove + cream catch-light.
- **FIX — the two caps must mirror cleanly.** Top and bottom caps currently differ
  in size/framing and the gap-edge isn't clearly the amber kiln-mouth focal. Make
  both caps the same smaller mirrored beast-mask, each with the glowing amber
  kiln-mouth seated AT the gap line, mass dropped toward the gap (not flaring
  away) — clean top<->bottom mirror on-axis, neither cap top-heavy.
- **KEEP:** the warm sancai column palette; the beast-mask-cap concept; on-axis
  symmetry of the shaft centerline.

### 32px gameplay chip (day + night)
- **KEEP:** the seated-beast block survives and reads as a creature at true scale
  on BOTH biomes — silhouette and value structure hold. Good.
- **FIX:** at 32px the fan is a lump, the horns nearly disappear (especially
  night), and the pillar reads as a generic spiky stack — all downstream of the
  hero/pillar fixes. Re-cap the chip after those land. On the NIGHT chip verify
  the cinnabar horns still read (lowest-contrast element vs a dark sky) — a thin
  cream/amber rim-sheen on the horn top-left edge rescues them without growing
  the red mass.

## Iteration directives (prioritized punch list)

1. **Build the 5-plate back-fan as five DISCRETE triangular plates** behind the
   head — center tallest, raked outward, ink-keyline gaps between, each
   triad-lit. Must count as 5 distinct top-edges at 32px. (Signature epic read —
   top priority.)
2. **Invert the pillar to bottom-rooted:** stacked UPSWEPT spike-plates, widest at
   the base tapering upward — kill the descending-arrow flow that puts mass at
   the top.
3. **Add SPARSE hairline crackle-glaze seams** to the pillar plates (2-3 per band,
   hard 1px triad grooves) — no granulate mesh.
4. **Mirror the two pillar caps cleanly:** identical smaller beast-masks, amber
   kiln-mouth seated AT the gap, mass dropped toward the gap on both ends.
5. **Thicken the horns ~50% at the base**, add one ridge groove each, add a
   top-left rim-sheen so they read at night — keep a thin linear cinnabar accent,
   never a mass.
6. **Resolve the olive shoulder/mane ambiguity:** commit to a tidy symmetric
   mane-curl frame around the face; let the fan own the spikes. Mirror it L/R.
7. **Widen forelegs slightly and root the paws** for an unambiguous bottom-heavy
   beast block.
8. **Re-render the 32px day+night chips** after 1-7 and confirm: fan = 5 plates,
   horns visible on night, pillar reads bottom-rooted.

## Distinctness & cross-set check (passing — hold the line)
- **vs Tlaloc-Tiki:** clear. Warm amber-ceramic sancai vs grey-slate goggle-eyed
  mask. Keep the eyes as warm amber irises (NOT concentric goggle rings).
- **vs Jangseung:** clear. Glazed ceramic with a kiln glow vs carved/weathered
  slate-wood. Keep the glaze sheen and amber mouth-glow — they read as fired
  ceramic, not grain.
- **Cross-set red discipline:** PASSING — horns are a thin linear cinnabar accent,
  not a second saturated mass. When thickening (directive 5), add width not
  saturation/area.
- **Olive lane:** PASSING — brown-olive ceramic, clear of leaf-green / Cernun
  pine / Kappa yellow-green / source jade.
