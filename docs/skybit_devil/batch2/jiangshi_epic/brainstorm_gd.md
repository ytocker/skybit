# Skybit BOSS — JIANGSHI-LINEAGE "EPIC" SPINOFF BRAINSTORM (GD, ~8 directions)

Brainstorm only. No render. ~8 distinct NEW boss directions INSPIRED BY the
shipped **Jiangshi** (the talisman-sealed hopping Qing corpse) — each shares
its DNA *loosely* (a rigid / sealed / bound undead-revenant, ceremonial
East-Asian-folklore flavor, with a vertical ceremonial POLE/scroll/banner
pillar grounded in the creature's own forms) but is a DISTINCT new creature:
different legend, silhouette, anatomy, palette. NOT five recolored Jiangshis.

## House style inherited (unchanged), pushed to EPIC

CHIBI proportions (big head, short weight-shifted body) + scary-CUTE tone +
FLAT saturated fills + hard 1–2px ink keylines `(24,30,26)`-family; form via
the **dark-core → flat-fill → top-left rim-sheen** TRIAD; silhouette POP via a
1px outline grown from the alpha mask; supersample → smoothscale. Every tall
prop mirrors top↔bottom into a PILLAR (repeatable shaft body + detachable
glowing gap-edge cap), the way Jiangshi's talisman-pole / lantern does.

**ELEVATED = epic, not different grammar.** Keep flat fills + 1–2px ink + the
triad. Push: a BIGGER / more imposing 32px read (taller or broader mass, more
geometric internal detail — carved seams, plate facets, banding), RICHER triad
shading (one extra mid-band where it earns it), STRONGER glow/menace at the
gap-cap. Procedural-only — no gradients, no PNGs.

## Hard separation lines (must not collide)

- **Source Jiangshi** owns: jade-corpse + rust-plum + cinnabar; rigid
  arms-OUT-forward T silhouette; talisman-scroll/lantern pole. None of the 8
  may repeat that exact arms-forward stiffness AS its whole silhouette, nor the
  jade+rust-plum+cinnabar combination as its dominant family.
- **Shipped roster to dodge:** Catrina (couture flower-hat skeleton dame) ·
  Draugr (ice brute warrior, frost-bone+ice-cyan) · Necrarch (floating crowned
  violet lich) · Kitsune / Yurei / Kappa / Raijin / Karakasa (the Japanese set)
  · Mariachi (warm-bone musician) · Leyak (bodiless flying head) · Cernun /
  Tlaloc / Pazul (the green-band + ochre devils).
- **Owned hue families OFF-LIMITS as a dominant:** violet (Necrarch),
  ice-cyan / frost-bone (Draugr), storm-grey (Raijin), mint-green &
  blue-cyan glows (Kitsune/Yurei), bright yellow-green (Kappa), deep pine
  (Cernun), grey-slate-jade (Tlaloc), couture marigold+teal (Catrina),
  oxblood-paper (Karakasa). Each of the 8 below owns a fresh, unused lane.
- Jade-corpse green is the *lineage* tell, allowed as a SECONDARY accent on a
  couple of these, but never the dominant family of more than one — and never
  paired with rust-plum+cinnabar (that's the source).

## DIRECTION 1 — RO-LANGS, the joint-locked Himalayan risen corpse  [TURQUOISE-CHARNEL + BONE-WHITE]

- **Thesis.** Tibet's *ro-langs* ("risen corpse"): a body that cannot bend ANY
  joint, lurching stiff-LEGGED with a long lolling tongue it wags to mark prey.
  Where Jiangshi locks its arms forward, this one is locked at the KNEES + neck
  — a plank-stiff, board-straight vertical pillar of a body, tongue out.
- **32px read.** A tall RIGID PLANK figure — board-straight torso+legs fused
  into one column (the un-bending-joint gag), arms pinned flat down the sides,
  head tipped slightly back with a long flat TONGUE hanging straight down past
  the chin. A vertical domino of a creature; the down-tongue is the focal blob.
  Distinct from Jiangshi (arms forward, hopping feet-together) — this is
  arms-DOWN, plank-rigid, tongue-down.
- **Construction (chibi + elevated triad).** One fused board torso-leg column
  (no visible knee — render as a single triad-lit slab with two faint shin
  seams); arms as flat side-pinned planks; chunky square head, half-lidded dead
  eyes, the long flat tongue a hard triad shape (dark-core / flat / sheen, NOT
  a wet gradient); ragged charnel burial-wrap banding wound around the column
  in 3–4 hard bands (the extra mid-band = the elevated detail); a small ritual
  cord knotted at the throat. Tiny bound feet poke out the base.
- **Prop → pillar (grounded in its OWN forms).** The **prayer-flag PRABHA
  POLE**: the creature's own burial-wrap bands continue down the pole as
  repeatable shaft banding (each band a folded prayer-flag, ink-mantra
  glyphs) — the shaft is literally MORE of the wrapped-corpse body. Gap-edge
  cap = a **butter-lamp flame in a bone-cup finial**, the same lolling-tongue
  SHAPE flipped up into a flame so the cap is a flame-tongue mirroring the
  creature's tongue. Clean top↔bottom mirror; round cup on-axis, no top-heavy.
- **Palette (fresh lane: cold charnel turquoise + bone, NOT ice-cyan).**
  ash-bone wrap `(214,210,196)` base · cold-stone shade `(150,158,150)` ·
  **charnel-TURQUOISE flame/glow `(72,170,158)`** (a deep teal-turquoise, not
  Draugr's pale ice-cyan, not Kappa dish-turquoise — pushed darker + greener) ·
  dried-blood cord accent `(150,52,44)` · butter-lamp gold `(226,182,84)` ·
  ink `(26,30,28)` · sheen `(238,238,224)`. Dominant: bone-ash + charnel
  turquoise — the SOLE plank-rigid Himalayan corpse.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook.** A door-tall stiff plank that CAN'T bend, wobbling
  upright like a tipping domino, tongue flapped straight out — comedy via total
  rigidity, menace via the cold turquoise charnel glow climbing the wrap-bands.
