VERDICT: ITERATE

# Jangseung — carved guardian totem-post — ROUND 1 critique

A strong, confident first round. The creature=pillar mirror is the cleanest in
the set as promised, the slate-wood reads clearly cooler/greyer than a warm
cream-wood survivor, and the big bug-eye / bulb-nose / fang face carries the
32px read on both day and night sky. It is NOT yet ship-ready: the FACE is
doing 95% of the work while the SHAFT and the all-important cinnabar HANJA have
collapsed into noise/near-invisibility at gameplay scale, and the cap's
fang-bone mouth is the weakest read on the sheet. Fix the hanja legibility, the
shaft rhythm, and the cap mouth and this locks.

## Ranking — strongest to weakest aspect

1. **Hero face (top third)** — STRONGEST. Genuinely comic-fierce + cute: the two
   big ringed bug-eyes with cream glow, the fat slate bulb-nose, and the
   cinnabar-lipped fang grin land instantly. This is the epic-at-a-glance read
   the brief demanded, and it survives the 32px chip on both skies. Keep almost
   exactly as-is.
2. **Cross-set wood separation** — STRONG. The cool grey-slate base sits clearly
   apart from a warm cream-wood lineage-mate on value AND temperature, not just
   hue. The two carved survivors will not converge. Holds.
3. **Mirror / pillar construction** — STRONG. On-axis, symmetric, twin partner-
   face cap is folklore-true and NOT top-heavy — the cap sits below the gap with
   its mass low. Cleanest mirror in the set, as briefed.
4. **Moss-teal patches** — GOOD but currently reading as loose floating blobs
   that drift OFF the silhouette into the sky (see chip), costing the clean
   carved-column read. Clustered lobes are right; placement/anchoring is off.
5. **Cinnabar hanja column** — WEAK at scale. This is the headline problem (see
   below). The GD flagged it; my ruling is it is NOT fine as-is.
6. **Cap fang-bone mouth** — WEAKEST. The lower partner-face mouth reads as a
   muddy fang-bone smear at 32px; it lacks the cinnabar lip-line that makes the
   hero grin pop, so the cap face is noticeably less legible than the top face.

## KEEP

- The hero face geometry, scale, and feature count (2 eyes, 1 bulb-nose, 5
  fangs) — textbook "big & few." Do not add features.
- Warm-cream eye glow as the single warm focal — it correctly lifts the cap face
  against the night sky. The brief's instinct here is dead right.
- Slate-wood temperature/value vs the cream-wood survivor. Locked.
- The mirrored twin-face cap concept and its low, non-top-heavy seating.
- Cinnabar as a thin LINEAR accent philosophy (brow, lip, hanja) — never a
  second red mass. Correct per the cross-set HARD fix.

## FIX

- **Hanja vanishes at 32px (RULING: not acceptable as-is).** The GD guessed the
  face would carry it so the disappearing hanja is fine. The face DOES carry the
  silhouette — but the hanja is the totem's identity tell and the only thing
  giving the long shaft any read; right now the shaft between the two faces is a
  dead grey bar with faint red ticks that smear to mud at gameplay scale. It
  doesn't need to be *readable as glyphs* at 32px, but it must persist as a
  rhythmic cinnabar SPINE so the column doesn't read as blank. Thicken the
  strokes ~30–40%, drop to FEWER, BOLDER glyph-blocks (3–4 chunky marks, not a
  dense ladder of thin ticks), and give each a 1px ink keyline so it holds its
  edge on downscale instead of bleeding into the slate.
- **Moss blobs float off-silhouette.** In the chip the teal lobes drift into the
  sky beyond the post edge and read as detached particles, not carved-on aged
  jade. Pull every moss cluster INSIDE the column silhouette, anchor them at the
  bevel grooves / glyph edges (where moss actually collects), and cut the count
  so they read as 2–3 deliberate patches, not scattered noise.
- **Cap mouth is the weak face.** Give the lower partner-face the same cinnabar
  lip-line the hero grin has so its fang-row reads as a GRIN and not a bone
  smudge at 32px. It should mirror the top face's legibility — right now it's a
  tier below.
- **Shaft is too inert.** Between the faces the post is a flat grey rectangle.
  Add a sparse, hard-edged bevel-groove rhythm (vertical grain + a couple of
  carved horizontal banding seams, triad-lit) so the shaft tiles read as carved
  WOOD, reinforcing the totem distinctness vs a stone-mask survivor. Keep it
  SPARSE — this is texture rhythm, not detail piled on.
- **Day-sky value.** On the day chip the slate body is close in value to a pale
  blue sky and the silhouette edge softens. Nudge the deep-slate shade ~10%
  darker / push the 1px grown outline so the post pops off a bright day sky as
  hard as it does at night.

## Distinctness check (PASS, with one watch)

- vs Karakasa (parasol yokai): no risk — vertical RIGID carved post vs round
  ribbed canopy-on-leg. Clear.
- vs a stone-mask survivor: the carved-WOOD grain + bevel grooves + twin-face
  totem read keep it distinct — but ONLY if you actually land the wood-grain
  rhythm in the FIX above. A featureless grey shaft drifts toward "stone." Earn
  the wood read.
- Cinnabar stays a thin linear accent on a cool slate base — no second red mass.
  Honors the cross-set HARD fix.

## Iteration directives (prioritized punch list)

1. Rebuild the hanja as 3–4 BOLD keylined cinnabar glyph-blocks (strokes +30–40%
   thicker) so the column reads as a cinnabar spine at 32px, not a dead bar.
2. Pull all moss-teal INSIDE the silhouette, anchor to grooves/glyph edges, cut
   to 2–3 deliberate clustered patches.
3. Add the cinnabar lip-line to the CAP partner-face mouth so it reads as a grin,
   matching the hero face's legibility.
4. Add a sparse hard-edged wood-grain + 1–2 carved banding seams to the shaft so
   it reads as carved WOOD (locks distinctness vs stone-mask).
5. Darken deep-slate shade ~10% / strengthen outline so the post holds its
   silhouette against a bright day sky as well as it does at night.

Hold the hero face, the cap seating, the wood temperature, and the cream eye
glow exactly as they are.
