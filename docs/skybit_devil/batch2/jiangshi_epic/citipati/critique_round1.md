VERDICT: ITERATE

# Citipati — Round 1 critique (Jiangshi-epic set, concept #1)

Strong, charming hero pose and a genuinely distinct concept. But it fails its
own stated gate: at true 32px the FIVE-SKULL CROWN ARC + thin FLAME-HALO RING
do NOT read as the silhouette tell — the halo collapses into a solid orange
sunflower blob and the ember dominates ivory. The cross-set fix is also
violated (ember reads as a MASS, not a thin ring). Fixable in a round, but
these are the load-bearing notes.

## Ranking of aspects (strongest → weakest)
1. **Concept / pose distinctness** — the cocked-hip flamenco dance is the only
   motion silhouette in the roster; reads as nothing else. Keep wholesale.
2. **House-style fidelity at hero scale** — flat triad, clean ink keyline,
   crisp procedural facets. On-style.
3. **Pillar** — clean vertebra-bead shaft, on-axis, not top-heavy. Closest to
   ship of all four elements; only minor cap notes.
4. **Color separation / cross-set discipline** — FAIL as drawn: ember is a
   mass, ivory is not dominant at small size; collides toward Xinniang/Vetala.
5. **32px legibility of the crown+halo tell** — FAIL: the structural read the
   whole concept is sold on is the first thing that dies when scaled.

## KEEP
- The dancing silhouette and flamenco-flourish arms. Best-in-set kinetic read.
- Skull face: third-eye + the two big round eyes are warm, scary-CUTE, on-brand.
- Cinnabar handled correctly as a thin LINEAR sash/limb accent — not a mass. Good.
- Turquoise as a single sliver. Restraint is right.
- Vertebra-bead pillar shaft and clean top↔bottom mirror.

## FIX (priority order)
1. **The flame-halo is a fire FIELD, not a lobed RING.** At 32px (both chips)
   it's a solid orange sunflower disc behind the head — exactly the "busy fire
   field" the brief warns against, and it makes ember the dominant MASS, which
   breaks the cross-set rule (must separate from Xinniang's saturated-red mass
   by keeping ember a thin ring). Re-build the halo as an OPEN RING: ivory/sky
   must show THROUGH the gap between the head and the flame lobes, and between
   the lobes themselves. Negative space is the whole point.

2. **Crown skulls are invisible at gameplay scale** — they merge into the
   orange petals and read as zero skulls at 32px, so the primary "Citipati"
   tell is gone. Either (a) make the five skulls the OUTER arc silhouette
   (skulls break the outline; flame sits as a thin ring INSIDE/behind them), or
   (b) drop to a cleaner count and pin high ivory-on-ember value contrast so
   each skull punches a hole in the orange. Right now the orange wins and the
   skulls lose — invert that hierarchy.

3. **Ivory is NOT dominant at small size.** The 32px chips read "orange thing,"
   not "ivory-and-fire." Shrink the ember ring's total area, push the bone
   value brighter, and let the warm-ivory body+crown carry the mass. Ember
   should be the thin accent halo, cinnabar the sliver — ivory the field.

4. **Two competing orange languages.** The petal-flames AND the gold scepter
   AND the ember halo all sit in the same warm-orange/gold band, so the head
   reads as one undifferentiated warm clump. Separate value/chroma: keep the
   gold scepter, but the flame ring needs to be hotter/lighter and OPEN so it
   doesn't fuse with the gold and the skulls.

5. **Lower-leg / foot read at 32px.** The kicked-out knee is great in the hero,
   but at 32px the legs read as a thin tangle. Thicken the leg masses slightly
   and keep the cocked-hip stance the clear bottom-silhouette tell.

6. **Pillar gap-cap is slightly faint.** The crown-skull + flame-ring cap at
   the gap edge is legible but small against the night chip. Bump the cap skull
   ~15% and give its flame-ring the same OPEN-ring treatment as the hero (so
   shaft and cap share one flame language). Shaft beads are good — leave them.

7. **Night-biome check:** ember currently glows fine on night, but once the
   ring is opened and ivory is pushed brighter, re-verify the bone holds value
   against the dark-blue night sky (it should improve). Keep the third-eye dot
   bright enough to register at 32px on both biomes.

## Distinctness (PASS, hold the line)
No hat, no instrument, no scythe — clean of Mariachi / Catrina / batch-1
reapers. The dance pose is unique. Cross-set color is the only collision risk,
and it lives entirely in FIX #1 and #3: turn the ember mass into a thin open
ring and re-assert ivory dominance, and Citipati separates cleanly from
Xinniang (saturated-red mass) and Vetala (dark ox-blood).

## Next-round target
A 32px chip where you can COUNT skulls in the crown arc and SEE sky through the
flame ring, and where the first-read color is ivory — not orange.
