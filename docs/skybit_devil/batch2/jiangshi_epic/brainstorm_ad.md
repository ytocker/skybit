# JIANGSHI-EPIC BRAINSTORM — AD CULL 8→5 (brainstorm-critique mode)

VERDICT: ITERATE

The set is genuinely strong: 8 distinct silhouette KINDs, 8 separated lanes,
every prop grounded in its own forms. This is a re-roll-proof brainstorm. But
the locked 5 are NOT the GD's suggested 5 — and three survivors need a hard
re-spec before they enter per-concept loops. Hence ITERATE: lock the 5 below,
apply the pins, then proceed.

## THE LOCKED 5 (KEEP)

1. **Citipati** — the dancing charnel skeleton-lord. The ONLY motion silhouette
   in the entire boss roster; cocked-hip dance is unmistakable at 32px and is
   the perfect anti-Jiangshi (rigid → kinetic). Warm-bone+cinnabar+ember-HALO
   lane is clean. Strongest of the eight.
2. **Zhenmushou** — the antlered sancai tomb-guardian beast. The only quadruped
   BEAST-block in the lineage set; bottom-heavy seated mass reads instantly and
   never goes top-heavy. Cream/amber/olive sancai is a lane nobody owns.
3. **Xinniang** — the vermilion ghost-marriage corpse bride. The only all-red,
   hidden-face BELL silhouette; warm vermilion+gold is the single most distinct
   color block in the set and the strongest pure-charm/dread hook.
4. **Jangseung** — the carved guardian totem-post. The purest creature=prop=
   pillar mirror, the way Karakasa is for its parasol — and the cleanest
   folklore-true twin-face cap. Slate-wood+cinnabar+moss-teal is unowned.
5. **Vetala** — the inverted bat-hung charnel revenant. The single UPENDED
   silhouette across BOTH batches; head-down is a 32px read no other boss can
   ever collide with. This is the distinctness pick and it earns the slot.

## THE 3 CUTS

- **Ro-langs (CUT).** Strong concept, but plank-rigid + tongue-down is the
  closest neighbor to the source Jiangshi (both stiff vertical corpses; the
  down-tongue echoes Jiangshi's hopping-stiff comedy). It's the one survivor-
  candidate whose silhouette risk overlaps the parent. Charnel-turquoise lane is
  nice but not worth the collision. Cut.
- **Kokdu (CUT, loses the carved-wood duel to Jangseung).** Kokdu vs Jangseung
  was the flagged near-pair. RULING: keep Jangseung, cut Kokdu. Jangseung is the
  PURER lineage idea (creature IS the pillar) and a cleaner 32px read (one big
  carved face vs a busy doll-on-a-mount with a beast + crown + arms — too many
  sub-shapes to survive at gameplay scale). Kokdu's doll-on-a-tiger also flirts
  with "rider" reads not present elsewhere and the peony-pink+indigo+jade is
  three accents fighting for one tiny body. Cut.
- **Joss-effigy (CUT, with regret).** RULING on the boldness flag: the flat
  paper-cutout is the most ORIGINAL idea here and the black-led lane is unique —
  but a deliberately 2D flat-cutout fights the dark-core→fill→rim-sheen TRIAD
  that is the house form language; a flat puppet has almost no form to triad-
  shade, so it would read as the one boss that "isn't built like the others."
  Lowest corpse-read, as GD flagged. It's a feature in isolation, a liability in
  a cohesive roster. Hold as the top alternate if any survivor fails its loop.

## RULINGS ON GD'S WATCH-ITEMS

- **Kokdu vs Jangseung carved-wood overlap** → resolved: cut Kokdu, keep
  Jangseung (above). The carved-wood lane holds ONE, and it should be the post.
- **Vetala hue** → CONFIRM MAROON, reject the cooler slate-led alt. Slate-grey
  body would drift toward Raijin's storm-grey and Jangseung's slate-wood (now
  both in/near the set). Blood-maroon wrap + bone + hot-amber eyes is clean
  against everything shipped. Keep slate to the BRANCH prop only, not the body.
- **Joss boldness** → ruled a cut (above): too off-grammar for the triad to
  carry. Alternate, not a survivor.
- **Citipati vs Mariachi / batch-1 reapers** → SAFE, but pin the guard: Mariachi
  is sombrero + guitarrón + warm-bone musician; Citipati must lead with the
  FIVE-SKULL CROWN ARC + FLAME-HALO RING + khatvanga — no hat, no instrument, no
  scythe/bident (off the reapers). The crown-arc + halo is the silhouette tell.

## PALETTE-CLASH CHECK AMONG THE 5

The risk is WARM convergence: Citipati (warm-bone + cinnabar + ember), Xinniang
(vermilion + gold), Vetala (bone + maroon + hot-amber) all sit in the red→amber
arc. They DON'T collide as silhouettes, but to keep 32px color separation:
- Citipati = WARM-BONE dominant, cinnabar is crown/sash ACCENT only, glow is a
  thin ember RING. Reads ivory-and-fire.
- Xinniang = SATURATED VERMILION dominant (a full red robe), gold hero metal.
  Reads solid red bell — the warmest MASS.
- Vetala = BONE-WRAP dominant, maroon is membrane ACCENT, amber is eyes/gap
  only. Reads pale-and-oxblood, DESATURATED — must stay duller than Xinniang's
  hot red. PIN: keep Vetala's maroon a dark desaturated ox-blood, never creep
  toward Xinniang's vermilion.
Zhenmushou (cream/amber/olive) and Jangseung (slate-wood/cinnabar/moss-teal)
anchor the cool/neutral half — good spread. One small flag: Jangseung's cinnabar
hanja vs Zhenmushou's oxidized-red horns vs Citipati's cinnabar crown are three
cinnabar-family reds — fine because each is a thin LINEAR/ACCENT use on a
different base (slate vs cream vs ivory), but the designer must not let any of
them grow into a mass.

## ROSTER-COLLISION CHECK (vs shipped 15 + source)

- All 5 clear the source Jiangshi: none is jade-dominant, none is the arms-
  forward T, none pairs jade+rust-plum+cinnabar.
- Citipati ≠ Catrina (couture hat/gown) ≠ Mariachi (sombrero/guitar) ≠ batch-1
  reapers (scythe) — crown-arc+halo dancer is its own thing.
- Zhenmushou ≠ Tlaloc-Tiki: both carved/idol, but Tlaloc is a grey-slate-jade
  goggle-eyed humanoid MASK; Zhenmushou is a seated four-leg amber-ceramic beast
  with a back-spike fan. Different KIND + lane. SAFE.
- Xinniang ≠ Catrina (hidden face vs painted skull + couture hat) ≠ Yurei
  (visible mournful face, legless wisp vs hidden face, grounded bell). SAFE.
- Jangseung ≠ Karakasa (object-yokai parasol) ≠ Tlaloc (stone mask): carved-WOOD
  totem with a bulb-nose comic-fierce mug + twin-face pair. SAFE.
- Vetala ≠ Necrarch (violet, upright floating lich) ≠ Karakasa (oxblood PAPER):
  maroon body wrap, inverted hang. SAFE.

## LOCKING BRIEFS (re-spec each before per-concept loops)

### 1. CITIPATI — locked brief
- Silhouette: wide cocked-hip DANCING stance, one knee kicked out, arms in a
  flamenco flourish; FIVE-SKULL CROWN ARC + thin FLAME-HALO RING are the
  unmistakable top read. No hat, no instrument, no scythe.
- Palette: warm-ivory bone dominant; cinnabar crown/sash ACCENT only; ember-
  orange a thin HALO RING (not a mass); turquoise sash-trim a sliver; gold
  scepter accent.
- Pillar: khatvanga spine-staff — stacked vertebra beads (the torso's rib bands
  continued), single crown-skull cap with a small flame-ring at the gap. Spine
  on-axis, symmetric. Good as specced.
- Watch: keep the flame-halo a clean lobed RING, not a busy fire field, or it
  turns to noise at 1×.

### 2. ZHENMUSHOU — locked brief
- Silhouette: squat seated beast on straight forelegs, wide lion-mask face, two
  upswept red horns, a back-fan of exactly 5 triangular spine-plates. Bottom-
  rooted. Drop to 5 spikes max so the fan reads as a fan, not fringe, at 1×.
- Palette: cream-glaze base + amber-glaze mid-band (the earned extra band) +
  YELLOW-olive accent (must stay brown-olive ceramic, NOT a leaf green — keep it
  clear of Cernun pine / Kappa yellow-green / source jade); oxidized red horns;
  kiln-amber mouth glow.
- Pillar: spine-spike stela — column of the same triad-lit spike-plates stacked
  edge-on, crackle-glaze banding, smaller mirrored beast-mask cap with a glowing
  amber kiln-mouth. Bottom-rooted, on-axis. Good.
- Watch: crackle-glaze seams are the detail push — render as hard hairline triad
  grooves, sparse; too many and they granulate at 1×.

### 3. XINNIANG — locked brief
- Silhouette: tall narrow bell-robe to the floor; SQUARE hanging veil over a
  hidden face with two faint glowing dead-eye dots; gold fengguan phoenix-crown
  scalloped arc on top; two bound silk hands clasped at the waist. The square-
  veil-under-crown is the tell.
- Palette: saturated vermilion robe dominant (the warmest MASS in the set) +
  gold hero metal; jade hairpin kept to a literal sliver (the lineage tell);
  faint warm veil-glow. No bone shown.
- Pillar: wedding-lantern dowry pole — red silk bands + hanging gold double-
  happiness medallions (echoing the hem), round wedding-lantern cap with a gold
  tassel. Round on-axis. Good.
- Watch: at 32px a featureless red bell risks reading as a plain cone — the gold
  crown-arc + the two eye-dots through the veil + the gold hem motif must carry
  the read; pin enough gold value-contrast against the red that the silhouette
  isn't just a red blob.

### 4. JANGSEUNG — locked brief
- Silhouette: tall narrow carved POST; oversized comic-fierce face filling the
  top third (bug eyes, fat bulb-nose, fanged grin), tall official's hat-block; a
  vertical column of cinnabar hanja down the front; no limbs.
- Palette: weathered SLATE-WOOD base (cool grey-brown driftwood — keep clearly
  cooler/greyer than Zhenmushou's warm cream-wood so the two carved survivors
  don't converge); cinnabar hanja/face-paint focal; moss-TEAL patches (aged jade
  tell); warm-cream eye glow.
- Pillar: creature IS the pillar — more carved-wood column + cinnabar hanja +
  moss; twin mirrored partner-face cap (folklore-true male/female pair), eyes
  glowing, mouth lit at the gap. On-axis, symmetric. Cleanest mirror in the set.
- Watch: bulb-nose + bug-eyes must stay BIG and few — this is a one-face read;
  resist piling weather-checking cracks that fuzz the face at 1×.

### 5. VETALA — locked brief
- Silhouette: UPSIDE-DOWN — clawed grip-feet hooked at TOP, wrapped tapering
  body hanging down, fanged chibi head at the BOTTOM (eyes read right-way-up),
  arms folded up as furled bat-wing membranes; tongue points UP (gravity gag).
  Bottom-weighted pendulum mass.
- Palette: CONFIRMED maroon lane. Dusty bone-wrap dominant; blood-MAROON
  membrane/accent kept DARK + DESATURATED (must stay duller than Xinniang's
  vermilion); hot-amber eyes + gap glow; slate branch (prop only).
- Pillar: charnel-branch — wrapped branch with the same hard wrap-banding +
  hooked claw-marks; cap = the bat-wings UNFURLED into a hard scalloped fan with
  a glowing focal at the gap. Wing-fan on-axis; pendulum hangs toward the gap so
  it reads bottom-weighted, not top-heavy. Good.
- Watch: the inverted face is the whole gag — make sure the eyes/grin read as a
  FACE first at 1× when flipped, or the silhouette reads as an ambiguous bundle.

## MOST IMPORTANT CROSS-SET FIX

Three of the five (Citipati, Xinniang, Vetala) live in the red→amber arc — hold
their color separation by VALUE and SATURATION, not hue: Xinniang owns the one
saturated-red MASS, Citipati stays ivory-dominant with red only as accent + a
thin ember ring, Vetala stays pale bone with a DARK desaturated ox-blood. If any
of the three lets its red grow into a second saturated mass, two bosses will
read alike at 32px.

2. Ro-langs (re-lane away from Jiangshi's stiffness if promoted)
3. Kokdu (only if the doll-on-mount read can be simplified for 32px)
