# Cernun — Round 1 critique (art-director)

Strong, confident first pass. The big read is right: bone-white branching
antler rack over a narrow stag-muzzle, seated pine-fur body, torc at the
throat — it scans as a woodland stag-druid at a glance and could not be
confused with any sibling. The PINNED PALETTE is essentially nailed
(measured fur base `(52,91,67)`, shade `(31,61,45)` — within 2 of the
pinned `(54,92,68)`/`(32,62,46)`); the deep desaturated pine is dark,
low-chroma and blue-leaning, and sits unmistakably apart from a bright
yellow-green Kappa or a grey-jade Tlaloc. The top-heavy antler-staff fix
is genuinely applied. This is an ITERATE, not a RE-ROLL — the lead is good;
the fixes are about focal hierarchy at 32px and a few polish/identity slips.

## Strongest aspects
1. **Silhouette / tree-crown read.** The wide bone antler fan with the
   forking 3-tine branches is a true crown read, never a horn pair. Holds
   at 32px (panel d) on both skies.
2. **Palette / green-band separation.** Measured pine is locked. On the
   night sky the bone antlers carry the silhouette while the pine body
   recedes gracefully — exactly the value structure we want.
3. **Mirror discipline.** The pillar (panel b) is the best surprise: the
   bark-umber shaft is a clean repeatable body, the cap is a slim two-tine
   crook tucked on-axis with the torc-ring hung below the fork. It does
   NOT read as a second antler head — flag resolved.

## Weakest aspects
1. **Face/focal hierarchy at gameplay scale.** At 32px (panel d) the head
   collapses into a thin pale stalk between the antlers; the green eye-spots
   are the only thing telling you "there's a creature here." The muzzle,
   torc and body all sit in the same dark-pine/bone value band, so the
   face — the charm carrier — loses the fight to the rack.
2. **Menace vs. cute balance.** Right now it reads more eerie-totem than
   scary-CUTE. The eyes are flat green discs with no warmth; there is no
   clear friendly tell. Big Reapy / warren-clown grammar wants a beat of
   personality the face isn't yet delivering.

## KEEP
- Pine palette and the bone-vs-pine value split. Do not touch the hues.
- Antler rack construction and the wide tree-crown fan.
- The pillar shaft + compact two-tine crook cap and below-fork torc.
- Cross-legged seated mound posture; the coiled serpent at the base is a
  nice batch-1-consistent flourish.

## FIX (prioritized punch list for GD round 2)
1. **Lift the face out of the antler stalk at 32px.** The single most
   important fix. Widen and shorten the skull-muzzle ~15–20% so the face
   is a distinct mass below the rack, not a continuation of it. Add a 1px
   ink keyline gap (or a hair-darker shade band) where antler bases meet
   the brow so the crown visually detaches from the head. Confirm in the
   32px name-test, not just the hero.
2. **Give the eyes warmth + a friendly beat.** The flat green eye-discs are
   the only face focal and they read cold/totemic. Try a small warm catch
   (torc-gold pupil dot or birch-bone inner ring + top-left sheen) so the
   eyes pop against the pine and gain the scary-CUTE glint. This is the
   charm lever — push it.
3. **Raise the torc's focal value.** The throat-torc is the brief's sanctioned
   warm accent but at 32px it muddies into the body. Keep it a THIN ring (do
   not broaden toward Pazul ochre) but bump its value/contrast so the gold
   reads as a clean smile-curve focal at gameplay scale — currently it nearly
   disappears on the night card.
4. **Antler tine noise at 1×.** The outermost fine twig-tines (panel a, far
   left/right) turn to disconnected speckle at 32px (panel d). Drop to the
   3-tine fork the brief specifies per antler and thicken each tine ~1px so
   every branch survives the downscale as a deliberate shape, not anti-alias
   fuzz. Fewer, fatter, cleaner forks.
5. **Body silhouette is a bit slab-like.** The seated fur body currently
   reads as a flat green pentagon. Add one weight-shift / shoulder asymmetry
   and a clearer coin-pouch shape in the lap so the chibi body has the
   weight-shifted house posture rather than a symmetric block.
6. **Triad-light the antlers.** The rack is mostly flat bone right now. Apply
   the dark-core → flat-fill → top-left rim-sheen triad to the antler forks
   (sheen `(150,186,150)` is pinned) so they get the same dimensional read
   as the body and don't look like cut paper next to the lit torso.
7. **Pillar cap polish.** The crook cap (panel b) is correct but the torc-ring
   sits dead-center and slightly large — nudge it a touch smaller and confirm
   at 32px (panel d, top/bot caps) that the moss-tuft band markers are still
   legible; right now the moss flecks read as random green noise on the bark.

## Distinctness / sibling-drift check
No drift. Against Tlaloc (grey-jade + coral focal) and Kappa (bright
yellow-green) the deep-pine + bone-antler combo is unambiguous in hue, value
and silhouette KIND. Jiangshi's jade-corpse is lighter/yellower and on a
skeleton body — no conflict. Serpent-at-base is a consistent batch-1 motif,
not a duplicate of any sibling element.
