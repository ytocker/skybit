## Polish notes for integration (non-blocking)
1. **Wing SHEEN edge vs day sky (the GD's flag — confirmed, minor).** The cool
   top-left sheen highlight on the upper wing panels sits close in value to the
   pale day-sky blue, so the membrane's outer rim can momentarily soften against
   a bright day biome. The membrane FILL holds separation fine; it's only the
   thin sheen lip that blends. Fix at integration: either drop the sheen lip
   ~one value step cooler/darker, or rely on the 1px alpha-grown outline
   `(30,22,20)` to re-assert the edge against light sky. Do NOT darken the whole
   membrane — the fill value is correct; this is an edge-only tweak.
2. **Double-deck legibility.** The two pairs read as a four-panel fan more than
   a clearly staggered upper/lower deck at the smallest scale. It's acceptable
   as-is (the four-panel turquoise frame is the win), but a slightly larger
   value/overlap step between the upper and lower pair would make the
   "double-deck — the one read no sibling has" survive even at 1x. Optional.
3. **Lower-wing/leg crowding.** The bottom wing panels and the bird-claw limbs
   share the lower-left mass; at 32px they marginally merge. A hair more dark
   ink gap between the lowest wing tip and the claws keeps the silhouette crisp.
