# Jiangshi — Round 1 critique (Art Director)

Strong palette execution and a likable face, but the silhouette misses the
ONE signature read the brief is built around: **both arms locked straight
FORWARD (the T-of-arms)**. As shipped, only the left arm extends; the right
side is occupied by the swinging queue braid, so the figure reads as a
one-armed pointing robot, not a stiff hopping vampire. That is the gate this
concept must clear. Everything else (palette, hat, face) is close to right.

## Ranking — strongest / weakest

- **Strongest:** palette discipline. The pinned jade-corpse skin
  `(120,158,118)` is dead-on and reads as a clearly LIGHTER, yellower, lower-
  chroma green than Cernun's pine `(54,92,68)` (V 62 vs 36, S 25 vs 41) — no
  collision risk. Rust-plum robe + cinnabar rank-badge + gold trim are warm and
  correct; the brief's "NOT indigo" pin is fully respected. The face (red eyes,
  brow talisman, stitched mouth) is scary-cute and on-brief.
- **Weakest:** the silhouette. The defining "T-of-arms" (both arms thrust
  forward) is not delivered — only one arm extends. This is the whole concept;
  it cannot ship without it.

## KEEP

- Jade-corpse skin hue/value — locked, do not touch.
- Rust-plum robe + cinnabar circular rank-badge focal — good warm anchor,
  reads at 32px.
- Brow talisman (cream + cinnabar strokes) and the winged mandarin hat with
  gold wing-tips — both legible in the 24px read; classic Qing silhouette.
- Face: round red eyes + dark nose + stitched mouth = correct scary-CUTE.
- Pillar palette: the talisman-band shaft + red lantern gap-cap reads as a
  clean, symmetric, repeatable mirror. No top-heavy risk. Banding rhythm is good.

## FIX (prioritized punch list)

1. **BOTH arms straight forward — the #1 fix.** Render the canonical jiangshi
   pose: two stiff arms thrust horizontally FORWARD, parallel, at the SAME
   height, sleeves and hands matching. At 32px this is the entire silhouette
   tell and the brief's whole reason to exist. Right now it reads as a
   one-armed point. Both sleeves should be the rust-plum robe tube with jade
   hands at the tips.
2. **Move the queue braid to read clearly as a tail, not a second arm.** Pull
   the black queue to CENTER-behind (trailing down/behind the body) or to a
   single side as a thin swing, but it must not sit where the second arm
   belongs. At 32px the current braid masquerades as the missing right arm —
   that ambiguity is what breaks the silhouette. Thin it ~30% and tuck it so
   the two forward arms own the horizontal axis.
3. **Arms read as length, not stubs, at 24px.** In the 24px chip the single
   arm nearly disappears. Give the paired forward arms enough length (roughly
   0.6–0.7× body height each) and a 1px ink keyline so the T-shape survives the
   smoothscale. The arms are the silhouette — they must be the most confident
   masses after the head.
4. **Feet together, mid-hop.** Brief calls for "tiny feet together mid-hop."
   Current feet are a small splayed pair reading as a stand. Bring them
   together into a single small block lifted slightly off the baseline to sell
   the hop and to keep the rigid-column read.
5. **Hat wings vs arms — avoid a competing horizontal.** The hat's gold wing-
   tips currently throw a wide horizontal that, combined with one forward arm,
   muddles the read. Once both arms go forward, narrow the hat wings ~15% so
   the ARMS are the dominant horizontal and the hat is a secondary, taller
   accent. Keep the hat tall and vertical to preserve the mandarin read.
6. **Add the rank-badge/queue contrast check at night.** Rust-plum robe is
   mid-value; against a dark-blue night biome the body may sink. Confirm the
   cinnabar badge + gold trim keep enough contrast to anchor the focal at
   night, the way the brief leans on warm accents elsewhere. If the robe sinks,
   nudge the gold hem/trim brighter rather than touching the skin.

## Distinctness / drift check

- **No green-band conflict:** jade-corpse is unambiguously separated from
  Cernun pine and Kappa yellow-green by value and chroma. Confirmed safe.
- **No sibling drift:** the rust-plum + cinnabar warm family is unique among
  the skeleton section (Catrina = cool ash/teal, Mariachi = warm bone/ochre,
  Necrarch = violet, Draugr = ice). Jiangshi's jade skin + rust robe is its own
  lane. Good.
- The ONLY distinctness lever this concept owns is the T-of-arms silhouette —
  which is exactly what round 1 failed to deliver. Land the two forward arms
  and this becomes the most unmistakable silhouette in the section.

## References

- Standard jiangshi reference pose: arms rigid and parallel straight forward,
  feet together hopping — this paired-arm read is the genre's instant tell and
  is what every casual-recognizable jiangshi leans on.
