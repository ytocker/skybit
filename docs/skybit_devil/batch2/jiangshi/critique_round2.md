VERDICT: ITERATE

# Jiangshi — Round 2 critique (Art Director, final critique)

Round 2 fixed the right things conceptually — both sleeves are now present and
the queue is centered-behind — but the chosen FRONTAL foreshortening of the
arms defeats the concept's only distinctness lever. With both rust-plum tubes
pointed straight AT the camera, they have almost no horizontal extent, so at
32px (and worse at 24px) they collapse INTO the torso block. The signature
"T-of-arms" — the one silhouette this concept owns — does not land. The figure
reads as a stocky robed box with a chest-sash X, not a stiff arms-out hopper.
This is a profile/staging problem, not a palette problem, and it is the gate.

## Strongest / weakest

- **Strongest:** palette + face, unchanged from R1 and still excellent. Jade-
  corpse skin reads clearly lighter/yellower than Cernun pine; rust-plum robe,
  cinnabar rank-badge, gold hat-trim and brow talisman are all on-brief and
  legible at 24px. Queue is now correctly center-behind — the R1 "second-arm"
  ambiguity is gone. Night chip holds: badge + gold hem keep the warm focal
  anchored on dark-blue.
- **Weakest:** the arm STAGING. Frontal foreshortened tubes give the T-of-arms
  zero silhouette width. At 32px the arms vanish into the body; the defining
  read still isn't there. R1 had one arm too few; R2 has two arms with no
  horizontal reach — same net failure of the silhouette tell.

## KEEP

- Jade-corpse skin hue/value — locked, do not touch.
- Rust-plum robe + circular cinnabar rank-badge focal — strong warm anchor.
- Brow talisman, winged mandarin hat (now correctly secondary to the body),
  red eyes + stitched mouth — scary-cute, on-brief.
- Queue center-behind — correct, resolved.
- Pillar (talisman-band shaft + red lantern gap-cap) — clean symmetric mirror,
  no notes.

## FIX — round-3 brief (final pass)

1. **Stage the arms as a true horizontal T, not frontal tubes.** Re-pose the
   two sleeves so they read as length ACROSS the silhouette, not depth toward
   the camera. Use a 3/4-frontal body so each rust-plum sleeve projects clearly
   sideways-and-forward, each roughly 0.5-0.6x body width PAST the torso edge,
   both at the same shoulder height, with a jade hand block at each tip. The
   total arm-to-arm span should be the widest horizontal in the silhouette —
   wider than the hat wings.
2. **Break the arms off the torso with value + a 1px ink keyline.** Right now
   the arm tubes and robe are the same rust-plum mass with no separation, so
   even where they extend they merge into the body block. Drop a sleeve shade
   (or a thin ink gap) where each arm meets the shoulder so the two arms read as
   distinct masses at 32px.
3. **Kill the chest-sash X read.** The crossed sash currently throws a bright
   diagonal that competes with (and at small size replaces) the arm read. Either
   simplify it to a single horizontal sash band under the badge, or drop its
   contrast, so the eye reads ARMS-OUT first and badge second.
4. **Verify at 24px, not just 32px.** Confirm the T-shape survives smoothscale
   at the 24px chip — that is where R1 and R2 both broke. If the arms don't
   survive there, lengthen them and thin the hat wings ~15% further until the
   arms are unambiguously the dominant horizontal.
5. **Feet:** the mid-hop block is fine; keep it lifted slightly to sell the hop.

## Distinctness

No green-band or sibling drift — jade-corpse + rust-plum is its own lane,
confirmed safe. The T-of-arms remains the SOLE distinctness lever and still
isn't delivered. Land arms that read as a horizontal T at 24-32px on this final
pass and the concept ships; frontal tubes do not clear the bar.

## Reference

Genre tell: every casual-recognizable jiangshi reads as arms thrust OUT to a
clear horizontal span with feet together — the width of the arms IS the
recognition. Foreshortening that span away loses the read.
