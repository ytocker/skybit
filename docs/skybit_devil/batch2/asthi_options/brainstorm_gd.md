# ASTHI-DAKINI — 5 VERSION directions (brainstorm, no render)

**Shared fixed base (do NOT propose changing):** larger faceted cyan HERO gem at the
necklace centre (single brightest), a SMALLER dimmer same-gem third-eye on the brow,
six palm-skulls sized to match the six crown skulls. Warm aged-bone palette
(`BONE 212,202,186` / `BEAD` highlight chain / `GOLD` hue-separator pips / `CYAN`
focal / `CROWN_BONE` dimmest tier / `INK 28,22,26` keyline). Locked value ladder for
every version: **necklace hero gem brightest → third-eye → skull cyan → crown dimmest.**

**What each version owns:** a distinct *skull-content / expression LANGUAGE* across its
12 skulls (6 palm `PROFILE` + 6 crown `CROWN_PROFILE`) — eyes, gaze, expression, AND
gem-colour content — plus its own answer to WHERE/HOW-MUCH cyan lives in the skulls.
All skulls stay SKULL-STYLE: expression is carried by bone grammar (socket shape,
gaze-direction of the cyan socket-light, jaw set, tooth count/chips, brow ridge, tilt,
suture, cracks, inlays) — never cartoon faces. The base already parameterises all of
these per `idx`, so each language is a re-authoring of those two tables (+ palette/
ornament/gem twist for the two broader ones), not a structural rebuild of the figure.

Mix per the brief: **3 skull-focused** (differ mainly via the 12 skulls on the
otherwise-shared base) + **2 broader-styling** (also push palette accent / ornament
density / gem treatment for a wider spread).

---

## 1. `serene-relic` — SKULL-FOCUSED
**Thesis:** A meditative, at-peace brood — every skull is calm, eyes downcast or closed,
the whole court reads as sleeping reliquaries rather than grinning trophies. The "so
what": Asthi the *consoler*, soft and devotional.

**Skull-content / expression language (the 12):** Uniform CALM register, individuated by
how each rests. Sockets are NARROW, lidded crescents (a thin bone-rim arc closing the
top of each pit) rather than wide round pits — half-lowered "lids". Gaze is DOWNCAST:
the dim cyan socket-glints sit low in the pit and slightly inward, so the eyes read as
cast-down in reverence. Jaws are almost all CLOSED or softly parted (no agape, no
cracks, no missing teeth — this is the *unbroken* brood); tooth rows are even and full.
Brow ridges low and smooth; sutures are the quiet `line` median seam, never zig.
Skull-to-skull difference is in TILT and dome — a few heads gently bowed forward, one or
two tipped to rest on a temple, cranium widths ranging round→tall — so they read as a
row of individuals dozing, not one stamp.

**Cyan-emphasis approach:** Cyan is INTERNAL and soft — it pools as a low glow in the
*sockets* (the gaze-light), not as brow cabochons. Most skulls carry only a faint
`CYAN_D` socket pool. The MORE-cyan skulls (~2–3): the crown-CENTRE relic and the two
lowest palm-skulls get a fuller `CYAN`→`CYAN_BR`-rim socket pool (a "lit from within"
read), like the inner light is strongest at the heart of the fan. No competing brow
gems, so the third-eye/hero stay unchallenged.

**Stays skull-style + ladder:** all expression via lidded sockets / jaw / tilt; socket
cyan capped at `CYAN_BR` rim only (no white core), held below the third-eye.

---

## 2. `wrathful-grin` — SKULL-FOCUSED
**Thesis:** The opposite pole — a fierce, charnel-ground brood, all bared teeth, wide
glares and battle-scars. The "so what": Asthi the *protector-destroyer*, the scary half
of scary-cute turned up, every skull snarling outward.

**Skull-content / expression language (the 12):** WRATHFUL register. Sockets wide and
OVAL, slightly canted *outward and up* (angry-brow geometry: the inner top corner
pinched down by a heavy brow ridge on every skull). Gaze is OUTWARD/UP — cyan glints
ride the upper-outer socket so the court glares away from centre, fanning aggression.
Jaws mostly AGAPE in a roar with FULL fanged tooth rows (longer corner teeth = tiny
canines via wider end tooth-slits); several `cracked` jaws and `chip` gaps as old-battle
scars. Sutures are all jagged `zig`; add a hairline CRACK line across one temple on
2–3 craniums. Difference skull-to-skull: degree of roar (wide vs half), which side is
cracked, lean of the snarl — a chorus of distinct furies.

**Cyan-emphasis approach:** Cyan is the WRATH-FIRE in the eyes. Every skull's sockets
get a `CYAN_D` ember; the MORE-cyan skulls (~3) — the two outermost palm-skulls (tips of
the fan, the "lead" snarls) and the crown-centre — burn brighter with a `CYAN`-core
ember and a `CYAN_BR` upper rim glint, reading as eyes flaring with power. Keep them
ember-in-socket (no brow cabochons) so the hero gem still wins.

**Stays skull-style + ladder:** anger is pure bone grammar (brow ridge + socket cant +
fang slits + cracks); cyan is socket-bound, capped under the third-eye.

---

## 3. `gem-eyed-oracle` — SKULL-FOCUSED
**Thesis:** A brood of seers — instead of hollow pits, the skulls have JEWELLED eyes:
faceted cyan cabochons set INTO the sockets, so the court is a ring of staring oracles.
The "so what": Asthi the *all-seeing*, twelve little jewel-stares echoing her third-eye.

**Skull-content / expression language (the 12):** The defining move — sockets are not
empty ink pits but bone rings holding a small GEM (gold-bezel cyan cabochon seated where
the pupil would be). Expression comes from how the gem-eyes are SET and aimed: a pair
staring dead-ahead (alert), a single gem with the other socket dark and lidded
(one-eyed seer / "winking" relic), gems set high (wide-eyed) vs low (knowing), and gem
SIZE varying (big-eyed innocent → pinpoint-eyed sage). Jaws kept mild (closed or barely
parted) so the EYES carry the read; even tooth rows; smooth brows; quiet `dots`/`line`
sutures so nothing competes with the stares. Skull-to-skull difference lives entirely in
the eye-set + which socket is jewelled vs dark.

**Cyan-emphasis approach:** This is the version where cyan lives MOST in the skulls by
design — most skulls show paired cabochon eyes, so cyan is everywhere as small
contained stones. The MORE-cyan skulls (~2–3): the crown-centre and the two innermost
palm-skulls get the largest, brightest gem-eyes (still capped at `CYAN_BR` rim, gold
bezel). The discipline: many SMALL gems, none rivalling the necklace hero's faceted
size/brightness — quantity of cyan, not a brighter single stone, so the ladder holds.

**Stays skull-style + ladder:** gems sit in real bone sockets with carved rims (it's
still a skull with jewelled eyes, not a face); every gem-eye capped below the third-eye;
the hero remains the single faceted, white-cored, largest stone.

## 4. `verdigris-reliquary` — BROADER STYLING
**Thesis:** Aged temple bronze — the bone is the same warm ivory, but the metalwork has
gone to GREEN-PATINA (verdigris) and the cyan reads as oxidised-copper bloom, not icy
jewel. The "so what": an ancient, dug-up Asthi; the court looks centuries-old, mineral
crept into the bone. A genuinely different *material world* from the icy-jewel base.

**Palette / ornament / gem twist:** Keep `BONE`/`BEAD` untouched; swap the warm `GOLD`
spacer-pips and bezels for AGED-BRONZE (a darker, greener-gold: ~`158,140,70` body with
`120,150,110` patina edges) so the hue-separator now reads bronze-verdigris on ivory.
The cyan keeps its hue but is treated as VERDIGRIS BLOOM — matte, slightly green-shifted
in the dimmer steps, fewer hard glints, so skull-cyan looks like mineral crust in the
bone rather than cut gemstone. Ornament density UP a notch on the metalwork (bronze
suture-studs on more skulls) to sell "encrusted relic". The faceted necklace hero gem
and third-eye stay ICY and crisp — they are the only *living* jewels, which makes the
oxidised court read by contrast.

**Skull-content / expression language (the 12):** Register of EROSION — neutral
expressions, but each skull weathered differently: green crust creeping from one socket
edge, a patina streak down a temple, bronze suture-studs gone green on some. Sockets
plain-dark with a thin verdigris RIM-bloom (the bone "rusting"). Jaws mixed neutral; a
couple cracked as age-damage. Difference is in WHERE the patina has crept on each — a
map of decay, no two alike.

**Cyan-emphasis approach:** Cyan = mineral bloom. The MORE-cyan skulls (~2–3): the
crown-centre and the two lowest palm-skulls have the heaviest verdigris bloom — bloom
ringing both sockets + a crust patch on the cranium — reading as the most oxidised,
oldest relics. All matte, green-shifted, well below the icy third-eye.

**Stays skull-style + ladder:** patina is applied as socket-rim/suture/crust marks on
otherwise normal skulls; bronze and verdigris-cyan both sit dim; icy faceted hero gem
remains brightest, third-eye second.

## 5. `dawn-lotus-court` — BROADER STYLING
**Thesis:** A blessing-bringer rather than a charnel queen — warm DAWN accents (rose-gold
+ a soft lotus-pink secondary) lift the palette, and the skulls carry tiny bloom/petal
ornament, so the brood reads as a serene, auspicious court at sunrise. The "so what":
the festive, devotional, *celebratory* calavera read — Asthi the auspicious one. A
warmer, lighter world than every other version.

**Palette / ornament / gem twist:** Shift `GOLD` toward ROSE-GOLD (`216,156,118`,
sheen `248,210,186`) for the pips/bezels, and introduce a sparing LOTUS-PINK secondary
accent (`232,166,182`) used ONLY as a small petal-mark / lower-third blush — a third hue
that no other version uses, kept low-saturation so it stays house-safe on the light
field. Ornament: a tiny incised LOTUS / bud mark on the forehead of several skulls
(carved bone relief, not paint) and a single pink-tinted petal-tick on the brow band.
Bead lattice slightly daintier. The cyan jewels keep their hue but sit in rose-gold
bezels (warm setting, cool stone) for a sunrise jewel-box feel. Hero gem + third-eye
unchanged in cut/brightness.

**Skull-content / expression language (the 12):** GENTLE-AUSPICIOUS register — soft,
content expressions (mild closed/parted jaws, even teeth, no cracks), but each blessed
differently by its ornament: a forehead lotus-bud on some, a rose-gold tilaka dot on
others, a petal-tick by one socket, sockets calm with a small low cyan glint (a kind,
half-lit gaze). Difference is in the ornament-mark each carries + gentle tilt — a court
of distinct, smiling-but-skeletal blessings.

**Cyan-emphasis approach:** Cyan stays cool against all the warmth. The MORE-cyan skulls
(~2–3): the crown-centre and two flanking palm-skulls get a slightly larger
rose-gold-bezelled cyan brow drop (a "blessing jewel"), the cool counterpoint to the
warm court. Cyan elsewhere is just the small low socket glint. All capped under the
third-eye.

**Stays skull-style + ladder:** lotus/tilaka marks are incised bone relief + tiny
ornament, not faces; pink is a sparing low-sat accent, never a fill; rose-gold and
skull-cyan both dim; faceted icy hero gem brightest, third-eye second.

### Distinctness check (different "so what" each)
1. **serene-relic** — calm/sleeping, downcast lidded eyes, cyan pooled *inside* sockets.
2. **wrathful-grin** — fierce/snarling, glaring outward, cyan as wrath-fire embers.
3. **gem-eyed-oracle** — all-seeing, JEWELLED eyes replace pits, cyan as many small stares.
4. **verdigris-reliquary** — ancient bronze/patina material world, cyan as oxidised bloom.
5. **dawn-lotus-court** — auspicious sunrise, rose-gold + lotus-pink, cyan as cool blessing.

Three are pure expression/cyan languages on the shared base (1–3); two also re-tune the
material/palette world for a wider spread (4–5). No version restyles the figure,
silhouette, or the locked hero-gem / third-eye / value ladder — each is a re-authoring
of the two skull profile tables (plus, for 4–5, the metal/accent palette and a small
ornament set).
