# Necrarch — round 2 critique (art-director, FINAL pass before round-3)

Big structural win. The two-piece "orb in a poncho" disease is cured: this is now
ONE continuous vertical figure — skull on bone-shoulders bridging straight into
the robe, no sky gap. The orb is correctly shrunk to a chest accent, the 3 crown
spikes notch the silhouette, and the single wisp-curl hem replaced the leg-lobes.
The brief's four pinned tells (hooded skull / spiked crown / cradled orb /

two of those tells and drifts one notch toward Hollow — fixable, and exactly what
the last round-3 pass is for.

## Strongest / weakest

- **Strongest:** the unification fix + the bone shoulder-bridge — the figure now

- Violet+bronze palette and orb-glow triad — locked, no drift.

- Single asymmetric wisp-curl hem — the "no feet, floats" pin is satisfied.

## FIX (round-3 punch list — prioritized)

1. **Make the 3 crown spikes survive at 32px.** On the DAY/NIGHT chips the crown
   reads as a single bronze blob — the spikes are too thin/short and the same
   bronze value as the band, so they fill in. Make each spike taller and BONE-
   colored (parchment, not bronze) with a bronze base-band, so the spikes break
   the head outline by ~3-4px against the sky as three distinct notches. The crown
   is the #1 Hollow separator — it must notch the alpha outline at 32px or the
   read collapses to "hood."

2. **Lift the SKULL above the orb in the value hierarchy.** At 32px the orb-glow
   is still the brightest single mass and the eye goes to the chest first. The
   skull is the hero; raise the bone face's value/contrast against the robe by
   ~15% and pull the orb's bloom in another notch so "skull mage" reads before
   "glowing dot." Confirm on both chips that the FACE wins the first glance.

3. **Sleeve-cuffs still evaporate at 32px.** They read at large size but not on
   the chips — so the orb looks free-floating again, weakening the "cradled
   phylactery" gesture. Give the two cuffs a 1px bronze rim and enough mass to
   register as two bracket-shapes flanking the orb at 32px. This also reinforces
   #2 (orb reads HELD, not loose).

4. **Eye-sockets: confirm rounded glow, not the round-1 visor tiles.** Hard to
   judge at this size — verify the sockets are teardrop with a contained violet
   inner-glow and a plum rim so bone reads first, glow second. Don't let the
   socket-glow re-blow to white.

5. **Night-biome contrast.** Deep-plum robe `(60,34,92)` on dark-blue night sky is
   low-contrast — lean on the 1px alpha-grown outline and keep the high-value bone
   skull+spikes as the bright anchor at top. The NIGHT chip robe edge is borderline
   vanishing along the lower-right; verify the outline holds the whole silhouette.

6. **Polish (low):** crozier finial prongs are still near-symmetric/stiff — a touch
   of inward curl + a 1px upper-left sheen lifts it to AAA-casual. Confirm the
   cap-orb glow falloff matches the now-smaller creature orb so prop and creature
   read as the same magic.

A 32px read where I can name skull + THREE crown spikes + cradled orb + wisp on
