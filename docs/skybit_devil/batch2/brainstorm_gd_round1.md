# Skybit BOSS brainstorm — BATCH 2 concept directions (no full render)

House style every direction below obeys (same bar as batch 1's
warren-clown / Big-Reapy grammar): CHIBI proportions (big head, short
wide weight-shifted body); FLAT saturated fills + hard 1–2px ink keylines
`(28,22,30)`; form via the **dark-core → flat-fill → top-left rim-sheen**
triad; silhouette POP via a 1px outline grown from the alpha mask;
supersample → smoothscale. Scary-CUTE menace, never grim realism. Each
tall vertical prop is designed to mirror top↔bottom into a PILLAR
(repeatable shaft body + detachable gap-edge cap), the way Big Reapy's
bone-bident does.

**The batch-1 roster these 24 must NOT collide with** (KIND = tradition ×
silhouette × anatomy × dominant palette):
- *Devils:* Lil Nick (classic red), Baalgoat (Baphomet goat), Glitchfiend
  (neon synthwave), Ao-Oni (blue oni brawler), Implet (acid-green gremlin),
  Brimstone (faceted magma-skull), Soulforge (soot blacksmith-skull),
  Twinface (split skull/devil), Pyrecrown (green candle-crown skull),
  Clovenpate (bone-imp on huge hooves).
- *Also shipped:* a warren clown/jester + 5 reapers (Grim Sprout tiny imp
  great-scythe, Big Reapy giant jack-o-skull bone-bident ember/ash-blue,
  Dr. Quill plague-doctor BIRD vial-staff, Tick-Tock smug clerk
  hourglass-staff teal/brass/amber, The Hollow faceless hooded cloak
  snuffer-pole indigo/cyan/soul-pink).
- *Dominant hue families ALREADY OWNED, do not reuse:* cherry-red,
  cobalt-blue, indigo+torch-gold, magenta/cyan neon, acid-green,
  charcoal-basalt+magma, soot/iron+forge-orange, bone-cream|devil-red|gold,
  emerald soul-fire, bone+oxblood+brass, teal/brass/amber (Tick-Tock),
  indigo/cyan/soul-pink (Hollow). Reach for: violet, turquoise, hot-pink,
  ochre/tan, jade, plum, ash-white, bronze, coral, rust-orange, mint, etc.

The AD will cull each section of ~8 to **5** (15 total). Breadth + genuine
distinctness over polish. Web traditions cited from the WebSearch digest
(sources at foot).

## SECTION 1 — DEVILISH (cull to 5)

Designer's-call MIX of fresh devil archetypes AND devil–skeleton hybrids.
New traditions only — no ram-horn (cut from batch 1), no Baphomet, no oni,
no classic-red, no neon.

### D1 — PAZUL ("the scorpion-tailed plague-wind demon")
1. **Thesis.** Mesopotamia's Pazuzu cuted down — a lion-dog-headed wind
   demon with a curling scorpion sting for a tail. The oldest devil on
   record, rendered as a pocket gremlin.
2. **Silhouette (32px black read).** Small square-jawed snarling muzzle-head,
   TWO pairs of stubby folded wings stacked on the back (a double-deck
   wing read no other take has), and one fat scorpion tail arcing UP over
   the head with a bulb-sting tip — the over-the-head sting hook is the
   unmistakable blob.
3. **Construction.** Chibi: blocky canine-lion head with a square open
   snarl and tiny up-fangs, a stumpy emaciated rib-torso, four little
   bird-claw limbs, the double wing pairs as hard faceted membranes, and
   the segmented scorpion tail (4–5 triad-lit drum segments) curling
   forward. Its OWN tail primitive (segmented, not the spade-flick of any
   classic devil tail).
4. **Prop → pillar.** The **scorpion-sting STING-SPEAR** held vertical: a
   segmented chitin shaft = repeatable pillar body (segment banding), the
   hooked venom-bulb sting = detachable gap-edge cap dripping a bead of
   green venom into the gap.
5. **Palette.** desert-ochre hide `(206,158,84)` base · burnt-tan shade
   `(150,104,52)` · turquoise wing-membrane `(64,176,168)` accent ·
   venom-lime `(176,224,72)` sting-glow · amber slit-eye `(244,196,64)` ·
   ink `(30,22,20)` · sheen `(244,214,150)`.
6. **Scary-cute.** The big scorpion sting droops over like a too-heavy
   party-hat; the snarl is actually a nervous over-bite. Four tiny feet
   patter.
7. **Web tradition.** Pazuzu — lion/dog head + horns, emaciated body,
   clawed hands, scorpion tail, two sets of wings. (Getty, d20pfsrd.)
8. **Distinct vs nearest roster.** Nearest is Ao-Oni (brawler devil) — but
   Pazul is lithe + four-limbed + double-winged + scorpion-tailed in
   ochre/turquoise, not a cobalt sumo slab with a club. Different KIND.

### D2 — CERNUN ("the antlered forest stag-devil")
1. **Thesis.** The Celtic Horned God Cernunnos as a wee woodland devil —
   a crowned STAG-skull druid ringed with antlers and torcs. Nature's
   devil, mossy and ancient.
2. **Silhouette.** A narrow stag-skull face under a colossal branching
   ANTLER rack that fans wide (the branching tree-crown read is wholly
   unlike any horn pair — it reads "antlers," not "horns"), seated
   cross-legged on a little mound. Wide top, compact body.
3. **Construction.** Chibi: long narrow cervine skull-muzzle, a fat brow
   torc (gold neck-ring) at the throat, big branching antlers (each a
   hard triad-lit fork of 3 tines), a small fur-druid body sitting
   cross-legged clutching a coin-pouch (Cernunnos' cornucopia/wealth
   tell). A ram-horned serpent coils at the base.
4. **Prop → pillar.** The **living ANTLER-STAFF / world-branch**: a
   bark-banded wooden shaft = repeatable pillar body, a forked antler
   crook hung with a torc-ring = gap-edge cap; moss tufts mark the bands.
5. **Palette.** mossy forest-jade `(72,128,86)` base fur · deep-pine shade
   `(40,84,58)` · birch-bone antler `(228,214,180)` accent · bark-umber
   `(108,78,48)` staff · torc-gold `(224,184,84)` · ink `(26,28,22)` ·
   sheen `(176,214,150)`.
6. **Scary-cute.** The antlers are so wide it can barely balance; a little
   bird could nest in them. Sleepy half-closed deer eyes; chewing a leaf.
7. **Web tradition.** Cernunnos — antlered aged figure, torcs, ram-horned
   serpent, money-bags/cornucopia, cross-legged pose. (Wikipedia,
   Mythopedia, World History Encyclopedia.)
8. **Distinct vs nearest roster.** No roster member uses antlers or a jade
   forest palette; the cross-legged seated build and branching crown set

### D3 — CHARRO-CHARUN ("the blue boatman hammer-devil")
1. **Thesis.** Etruscan Charun — the corpse-blue underworld ferryman who
   does his work with a big HAMMER, snake-haired and tusked. The
   psychopomp devil (a death-warden, not a tormentor).
2. **Silhouette.** A round corpse-blue head with a hooked vulture-NOSE and
   two boar TUSKS curving up, topped by writhing snake-hair tendrils that
   break the outline into a medusa fringe, and one big mallet held over
   the shoulder. The hooked-nose-plus-snake-fringe read is unique.
3. **Construction.** Chibi: round bluish head, big hooked beak-nose, two
   ivory boar tusks from the lower lip, 4–5 short snake-hair tendrils
   (each a triad S-tube with a dot head), heavy brow, a little ferryman's
   tunic, one mallet-arm. Wide fiery eyes.
4. **Prop → pillar.** Charun's signature **death-MALLET / maul** stood on
   its head: a banded timber haft = repeatable pillar body, the big square
   mallet-head (with a coffin-nail stud) = gap-edge cap that "knocks" at
   the gap. Distinct from A8 Soulforge's anvil-headed sledge (this is a
   square timber maul, blue palette, no forge sparks).
5. **Palette.** corpse-slate-blue `(96,128,150)` base · deep-slate shade
   `(58,84,108)` · bone-tusk `(230,220,196)` accent · bile-green
   snake-hair `(120,168,92)` · ember-eye `(244,128,72)` · timber-umber
   maul `(120,86,54)` · ink `(24,28,34)` · sheen `(150,184,206)`.
6. **Scary-cute.** A weary just-clocked-in ferryman slump; the tusks give
   it an accidental underbite-pout. One snake-hair has tied itself in a
   bow.
7. **Web tradition.** Charun — blue/grey corpse skin, hooked vulture nose,
   boar tusks, snake hair, fiery eyes, signature hammer/mallet. (Charun
   Wikipedia, mythlok, Tomb of the Blue Demons.)
8. **Distinct vs nearest roster.** Slate-BLUE but cooler/greener than
   Ao-Oni's cobalt, and a snake-haired tusked psychopomp with a timber

### D4 — IFRA ("the smokeless-fire genie devil")
1. **Thesis.** An ifrit — the Arabian fire-jinn — as a chibi ember-imp
   whose lower half dissolves into a curl of SMOKE-FLAME instead of legs.
   The bottle-genie devil; pure heat and swagger.
2. **Silhouette.** A muscular little upper body with crossed arms and two
   back-swept flame-horns, tapering at the waist into a SMOKE-CURL plume
   (no legs — a genie-wisp base read nothing else has). The legless
   flame-tail base is the hook.
3. **Construction.** Chibi: broad-shouldered ember torso (crossed beefy
   arms, the classic genie pose), a square smug face with glowing
   coal-eyes and a single gold nose-ring, two flat back-swept flame-horns,
   and a billowing smoke-and-fire plume where legs would be (hard flat
   flame-lobes, triad-lit, NOT a soft gradient). Floats.
4. **Prop → pillar.** The **genie LAMP-PILLAR**: a tall ornate oil-lamp on
   its spout = repeatable pillar body (the lamp's fluted body banding),
   the flame-wick spout = gap-edge cap puffing a curl of fire into the
   gap. The only "container" prop in the set.
5. **Palette.** coral-ember `(238,108,72)` base · deep-rust shade
   `(176,62,42)` · violet smoke-plume `(118,86,150)` accent · hot-saffron
   flame-core `(255,196,96)` · brass lamp + nose-ring `(214,168,72)` ·
   ink `(34,20,24)` · sheen `(255,182,138)`.
6. **Scary-cute.** Tries to look intimidating with crossed arms but the

   smirk and one cocked eyebrow.
7. **Web tradition.** Ifrit/jinn — smokeless flame, horns, fangs, colossal
   winged fire-being, born of fire and chaos. (mythology.net, hellslore,
   thecontentauthority.)
8. **Distinct vs nearest roster.** Brimstone is COOLED lava-rock (a solid

### D5 — LEYAK ("the flying-head trailing-entrails devil")
1. **Thesis.** Bali's Leyak / SE-Asian penanggalan — a grinning detached
   HEAD that flies with a trailing comet of dangling viscera. Pure body-
   horror reframed as a goofy floating jack-o-head. A devil-skeleton
   hybrid: skull-grin head, no body at all.
2. **Silhouette.** A single big floating head with a wide tusk-grin and a
   long wavy TRAIL streaming down beneath it (the comet-tail of dangling
   bits) — a "head on a streamer" read shared by nothing in the roster.
   The trail is also the pillar.
3. **Construction.** Chibi: one oversized head — round skull-ish face,
   huge bug eyes, a wide tongue-lolling tusk-grin, little bat-ear flaps —
   and BELOW it a tapering ribbon of dangling lobes (heart, ribbon,
   bead-drips) rendered as hard flat triad shapes, not gore-realistic.
   No torso, no limbs. Floats and bobs.
4. **Prop → pillar.** The **trailing VISCERA-RIBBON itself** is the pillar:
   the dangling organ-ribbon = repeatable pillar body (rib-bead banding),
   a heart-pendant lantern = gap-edge cap glowing at the gap. The set's
   only "prop = the creature's own body" pillar.
5. **Palette.** ash-white face `(232,224,214)` base · cool-grey shade
   `(168,160,160)` · hot-pink trail `(228,84,128)` accent · deep-rose
   shade `(168,44,84)` · violet bruise-rim `(120,82,140)` · amber eye
   `(244,196,72)` · ink `(30,22,28)` · sheen `(255,238,232)`.
6. **Scary-cute.** It's having the time of its life flying around — gleeful
   wide grin, the dangling bits trail like party streamers, not horror.
7. **Web tradition.** Leyak / manananggal / penanggalan — detached flying
   head trailing entrails, fanged grin, SE-Asian folklore. (thecoolist,
   Manananggal via demon digest.)
8. **Distinct vs nearest roster.** No roster member is a bodiless floating
   head; the hot-pink trailing ribbon and ash-white face are a fresh
   palette and a one-of-a-kind silhouette.

### D7 — MARROWMAW ("the devil-jawed bone-serpent")  [devil–skeleton hybrid]
1. **Thesis.** A serpentine LOWER-JAW given a devil's life — a giant
   floating SKULL-JAW (the maw is the whole creature) with two horns and a

   tooth-lined mandibles), small horn pair on the upper skull-cap, two

4. **Prop → pillar.** The **vertebra SPINE-COLUMN tail** is the pillar: the
   stacked-vertebra spine = repeatable pillar body (bone-bead banding),

### D8 — TLALOC-TIKI ("the rain-fanged jade idol devil")
1. **Thesis.** A Mesoamerican goggle-eyed rain-deity IDOL turned imp — a
   carved jade-stone TEMPLE-MASK devil with goggle eye-rings and big curved

2. **Silhouette.** A wide squarish carved-mask head dominated by two huge

4. **Prop → pillar.** A **carved TOTEM / stela column**: a stacked-face

7. **Web tradition.** Tlaloc rain-god goggle eyes + fangs + Mesoamerican

### S1 — CATRINA ("the elegant flower-crowned Day-of-the-Dead lady")
1. **Thesis.** La Calavera Catrina — the Posada/Rivera high-society
   skeleton dame in a vast feathered HAT and a flower-painted face. The
   ELEGANT undead; festive, not grim.
2. **Silhouette.** A delicate skull under an enormous wide-brimmed
   plumed HAT (the platter-brim is the dominant read, far wider than the
   head), a long-necked hourglass gown silhouette below — a couture
   read nothing else in the roster has.
3. **Construction.** Chibi: small sugar-skull face with painted flower-
   petal eye-sockets and a stitched smile, a colossal brimmed hat heaped
   with marigolds and a feather, a slim high-collared gown body, gloved
   bone hands holding a folding fan. Marigold-petal motifs as flat
   triad shapes.
4. **Prop → pillar.** The **lace PARASOL / folding-fan staff**: a fluted
   parasol pole = repeatable pillar body (rib banding), the scalloped
   open parasol canopy = gap-edge cap fringed with marigolds at the gap.
5. **Palette.** ash-white bone `(238,230,222)` base · cool-grey shade
   `(176,168,166)` · marigold-orange `(244,150,44)` accent · hot-pink
   bloom `(228,86,140)` · teal gown `(54,150,150)` · gold trim
   `(226,186,84)` · ink `(28,22,26)` · sheen `(255,248,242)`.
6. **Scary-cute.** Impossibly poised and prim, but the giant hat keeps
   tipping over her eye-sockets; a tiny dignified nose-in-the-air tilt.
7. **Web tradition.** La Calavera Catrina — Posada/Rivera elegant
   flower-faced skeleton lady, marigolds, fancy hat. (dailyartmagazine,
   mexicoinmypocket, Calavera Wikipedia.)
8. **Distinct vs nearest roster.** Every shipped skull is bare-faced and
   grim; Catrina is a flower-painted couture dame in marigold/teal — fully

### J7 — HANNYA ("the jealous-mask kabuki she-demon")
1. **Thesis.** A hannya — the Noh/kabuki MASK of a jealousy-maddened woman

2. **Silhouette.** A stylized triangular MASK-face (wide brow tapering to a
   pointed chin) with two straight upswept HORNS and long flowing hair, a
   fanned kimono sleeve raised dramatically — the angular theatre-mask read
   (sharp, symmetrical, mask-flat) is distinct from every rounded skull.

   hard arched brows, gold metallic almond eyes, a wide fanged grin), two
   straight forward-swept horns, long flat hair-panels, a kimono body with

   tall theatre **MASK-POLE**: a lacquer pole = repeatable pillar body

### J8 — NAMAHAGE ("the straw-cloaked ogre-mask reveler")
1. **Thesis.** A namahage — the Akita New-Year visitor: a fierce demon-MASK
   under a shaggy STRAW cape, brandishing a ritual cleaver, stomping door to
   door scaring off laziness. The festival-ogre; straw-skirted and loud.

   (a haystack with a face).
3. **Construction.** Chibi: a wide conical straw-mino cape (hard hatched

## CROSS-COLLISION GUARDS (within batch 2 + against batch 1)

- **No reused horn primitive.** Devil/yokai horns here are all distinct
  KINDS: Pazul has NO classic horns (scorpion sting is the crown), Cernun =
  branching ANTLERS (a tree-crown, not a horn pair), Charun = boar TUSKS +
  snake-hair, Ifra = flat back-swept FLAME-horns, Marrowmaw = bronze bone-
  horns over a profile jaw, Tlaloc = none (goggle-mask), Hannya = straight
  upswept theatre-mask horns, Namahage = single stub mask-horn, Necrarch =
  spiked bone CROWN (not horns). No two are the ram/curved pair the AD cut
  from batch 1, and none repeats Lil-Nick's upturned pair or Baalgoat's
  back-swept goat horns.
- **Skull-face de-duplication.** Batch-1 skulls are front-facing domes
  (Big Reapy / Twinface / Pyrecrown). Batch-2 skeletons avoid that:
  Catrina = flower-PAINTED dressed dame, Mariachi = moustached dancer,
  Jiangshi = green stiff hopper, Draugr = HELMETED armored, Necrarch =
  CROWNED floating mage, Gladibone = HELMETED behind a shield, Boneminstrel
  = lanky DANCER face-in-fiddle, PlagueMarrow = BANDAGED hunched. Marrowmaw
  (devil section) is a PROFILE jaw, not a front dome. None is a bare grim
  front skull.
- **Flame/glow family kept apart.** Ifra = coral fire-plume, Pyrecrown
  (shipped) = green candle, Necrarch = violet soul-orb, Yurei = cyan
  hitodama, Kitsune = mint foxfire, Raijin = gold lightning. Six different
  glow hues + six different geometries (plume / candle / orb / will-o /
  fox-flame / lightning-zag). No two converge.
- **Prop family de-duplication.** No fork/scythe/hourglass/club repeats
  from batch 1. New prop families: sting-spear, antler-staff, maul, lamp,
  viscera-ribbon, cane, spine-column, totem, parasol, guitarrón, talisman-
  scroll, axe, crozier, scutum-shield, fiddle-bow, plague-bell, feather-fan,
  torii-gate, hitodama-lantern, bamboo-spout, mallet+drum, umbrella-shaft,
  mask-pole, cleaver. All 24 distinct.
- **Palette firewall (no two of the 24 share a dominant family; none
  reuses a batch-1 owned family).** Dominant hues span ochre, jade,
  slate-blue, coral, ash-white+pink, plum, rust-bone, jade-teal (devils);
  ash-white, warm-rust, jade-corpse, frost+ice, violet+bronze, sand-bronze,
  plum-grey, bandage-tan (skeletons); vermilion+teal, snow+coral,
  pale+cyan, river-green+bronze, storm-grey+gold, oxblood, crimson+plum,
  straw-ochre (japanese). Watch-flags below.
- **Existing-roster firewall.** Yurei ≠ Hollow (faced vs faceless;
  white/cyan vs indigo/pink). PlagueMarrow ≠ Dr. Quill (bandaged victim vs
  beaked doctor; tan/mint vs apothecary). Kappa ≠ Implet (turtle yokai vs
  bat-wing devil; river-green/bronze vs acid-green/plum). Marrowmaw ≠ Big
  Reapy (profile jaw+spine vs front dome). Mariachi/Boneminstrel are
  distinguished from each other by warm-vs-cold palette + guitarrón-vs-
