# Mariachi — round 1 critique (AD)

Strong first pass. The warm-festive palette is locked dead-on, the hero pose
is charming, and the sombrero-disc is the right hero read. But at the size and
motion this ships in, two pinned silhouette beats are NOT landing: the
guitarron disappears, and the dance-kick legs collapse. This is a clear
ITERATE — fix the 32px read, don't re-roll.

## Ranking — strongest / weakest
- **Strongest:** PINNED PALETTE match. warm-bone + tan + rust-red jacket +
  ochre sombrero + turquoise rosette + silver botonadura are all present and
  correctly weighted; this is unmistakably the "warm festive" lane and reads
  clearly apart from Catrina's cool couture. Hero-scale charm/menace is genuinely
  scary-CUTE: the grinning skull + curled moustache under the brim is appealing.
- **Weakest:** the 32px silhouette. The brief's three load-bearing cues —
  (1) vast FLAT sombrero disc, (2) legs mid-zapateado KICK, (3) round guitarron
  hugged to chest — only #1 survives at gameplay scale. #2 reads as an
  undifferentiated dark blob, and #3 vanishes entirely (the guitar is occluded
  by / fused into the torso). At 32px this currently reads as "disc-hat skeleton
  with a dark lump," which is dangerously close to a generic calaca and loses
  the musician identity that justifies the slot.

## KEEP
- Palette weighting and the turquoise rosette as the single cool spark on a warm
  body — exactly the brief's intent. Do not touch the hue set.
- Ink keyline weight and the dark-core → flat-fill → top-left sheen triad on the
  sombrero brim; brim form reads correct at 4x.
- Curled-moustache skull and silver botonadura down the jacket sides — great
  hero-scale charm, on-brief.
- Guitarron-NECK pillar shaft: the fret banding repeats cleanly and the round
  sound-hole + turquoise rosette cap mirrors on-axis. The mirror itself is sound.

## FIX (prioritized)
1. **Recover the guitarron at 32px (top priority).** Right now the round
   guitarron is hugged dead-center and reads as a shield/drum lump fused to the
   ribcage — at 32px it's invisible and at 4x it's ambiguous (drifts toward
   Tlaloc's stone mass / Draugr's round shield). Push the guitarron OFF-AXIS and
   forward: angle it across the body held in a strumming tilt so its round body
   breaks the torso's left or right edge of the silhouette. The goal is a
   secondary round bump that reads as "held instrument," distinct from the body
   oval. Give it a clearly different VALUE from the warm-bone ribcage (it's
   already darker rust-brown — good — but separate it with an ink gap + sheen so
   the disc edge survives the alpha-outline at 1x).
2. **Make the dance-kick legs read as a KICK, not a blob.** At 32px the legs are
   a dark clump under the body. Exaggerate the asymmetry hard: one bone leg
   planted/bent, the other kicked OUT to the side at a strong diagonal with the
   red boot-toe as the silhouette's lowest, outermost point. The zapateado kick
   has to be legible in the OUTLINE alone — a single splayed diagonal leg is the
   accessibility/shape tell here, the way the brief wants. The red boot-tips are
   your value anchors; keep them, but spread them apart.
3. **Flatten the sombrero CROWN.** The crown is currently a tall, near-vertical
   square trapezoid — at 32px@4x it reads like a couture block and edges toward
   Catrina's plumed-brim/Jiangshi's mandarin-hat territory. The brief pins a
   FLAT DISC distinct from Catrina. Drop the crown height ~30–40% to a low rounded
   peak so the dominant read stays "wide flat plate," and let the embroidered brim
   band (the ochre/turquoise/rust ring) do the decorative work it's already doing
   well.
4. **Widen the silhouette stance for 32px stability.** Combined with fix #2, the
   creature should make a wide, weight-shifted triangle (chibi brief: short wide
   weight-shifted body). Currently it's vertically stacked and narrow below the
   brim — the brim overhangs an empty waist. Broaden the jacket shoulders and
   throw the limbs out so the body fills under the disc.
5. **Brim-overhang contrast at night.** The ochre brim over warm-bone face risks
   low value separation against day desert/ochre skies. Add a thin ink underside
   to the brim (a 1px shadow band) so the disc edge and the face beneath it stay
   distinct on warm AND dark biomes — verify against both before next round.
6. **Pillar cap mass.** The guitarron sound-hole cap is good but slightly large
   vs. the neck shaft; nudge the round body to ~shaft +30–40% so it doesn't go
   top-heavy at the gap line, matching the discipline applied to Cernun/Raijin.
   Keep the rosette turquoise as the glowing focal at the gap.
