# MAHA-KAPALI — Round 1 critique (LOOSE · skull-motif corner anchor)

Strong, confident, the boldest "final-boss" silhouette of the brood, and unmistakably Mukha's sister.
Clears most of the GATE. But the single most important criterion for THIS concept — the 32px crown↔arm
negative gap + the crown reading as 5 — is not proven, and the DAY chip shows the exact mush the brief
flagged as highest-risk. ITERATE.

## KEEP
- Six-arm fan + face + palette unchanged — sister read locked. Trophy-strand construction (gold rod + 2
  skulls + rose tassel, hanging lower than Mukha). Pillar panel + gap-cap. Third-eye brightest at hero.
- NIGHT chip already reads as "tall skull-crown + skulls dripping off the arms" with the crown↔arm gap
  surviving — proof the design CAN work at 32px.

## FIX (ranked)
1. **[BLOCKER] DAY chip mushes — crown↔arm gap does NOT survive there.** Night passes, day fails (bright
   sky floods the thin dark wedge; crown + upper trophies fuse into one pale blob). Widen the literal
   vertical air-gap between the lowest crown skull and the top trophy at source so a dark wedge survives
   downscale on a LIGHT sky. Escape hatch: drop arm trophies to 1 skull + tassel on the TWO TOP arms only
   (keep 2 on the lower four). Pass only when the wedge shows on day AND night AND in the blackout.
2. **[MAJOR] Crown doesn't confidently read as 5.** Stagger into a clear tall TRIANGULAR fan — centre
   tallest + brightest keystone, pairs stepping down/forward, a hair of ink keyline between skulls so the
   count is unambiguous at hero scale (silhouette = a tall crest, not a wide cabbage/dome).
3. **[MAJOR] Lit-centre keystone competes with the third-eye.** Keep the crown centre a clear notch BELOW
   the brow third-eye's value/chroma; brow must be the single pixel the eye lands on first (both chips).
4. **[MINOR] Six rose tassel-dots pull focus** — dim to deep-rose so they don't compete with the focal;
   confirm a desaturated value pass still reads crown/face hierarchy.
5. Blackout proof is the truth-test: once the gap widens, sky must punch through between crown and arms.

## Round-2 punch list
1. Widen crown↔arm source gap so a dark wedge survives on LIGHT sky; if needed, top-two arms → 1 skull +
   tassel. Re-render both chips + blackout. (BLOCKING)
2. Crown = exactly 5, tall triangular stepped fan, ink keyline between skulls, count legible at hero.
3. Crown-centre keystone a notch below the third-eye; brow brightest on both chips.
4. Dim tassel-dots to deep-rose.
5. Pass only when day AND night show: surviving crown↔arm wedge + 5-read crest + brow brightest; blackout shows the gap.
