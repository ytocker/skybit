# Mukha-Devi KIN — LOCKED 5 (per-concept briefs)

Five new bosses spun off the shipped **Mukha-Devi** (six-armed wrathful bone-mother), as her **grounded
SISTERS** — not divergent silhouette KINDs. Mukha-Devi is the brood's **"Original."** Every concept KEEPS
her **six-arm radial fan**, her **rose/gold/teal bone palette**, and her **central chibi skull-face + rose
third-eye (the single brightest pixel)**. Distinctness lives ONLY in (a) a far more elaborate **arm-end
ornament SET** (replacing her two simple alternating relics) and (b) a pushed-up **skull treatment**. The
five cover both user-requested spreads: tight↔loose and prominent↔skull-motif.

**The `distinct-design-variants` doctrine is deliberately RELAXED here at the user's direction** — sisters,
not KINDs. Do not penalize shared silhouette.

**House style / pipeline (clone from `citipati_versions/mukha_devi/render_mukha_devi.py`):** chibi scary-CUTE,
flat triad (`triad_blob`/`triad_circle`: dark-core→flat-fill→top-left sheen) + ink keyline + 1px grown
outline; `SS=6` → smoothscale. Reuse `draw_arm_fan` (±100/64/28° off vertical, two-segment limbs, low
shoulder origin), `tiara_skull` (for arm-end skulls + richer crowns), `draw_pillar`. Each sheet: hero +
top↔bottom-mirrored pillar panel + true-32px DAY & NIGHT chips + blacked-out silhouette proof + palette
strip. Palette: `BONE (224,200,196)`, `BONE_D (168,134,138)`, `BONE_DD (120,90,98)`, `BONE_SH (248,236,234)`,
`ROSE (232,86,150)`, `ROSE_BR (255,160,200)`, `ROSE_D (160,40,86)`, `GOLD (224,182,84)`, `GOLD_BR`, `GOLD_D`,
`TEAL (64,170,166)`, `TEAL_BR`, `INK (28,22,26)`.

**GATE every round:** still reads as Mukha's sister (six-arm fan + palette + face); arm-ends visibly RICHER
than her disc/flame; skulls more dominant than her 3-skull tiara; legible at true 32px DAY+NIGHT (the
brow third-eye stays the single brightest pixel); clean mirrored pillar panel; stay in the rose/gold/teal
family + Skybit chibi grammar (no realism drift); police the two push-apart pairs.

---

### 1. kapala_devi — six skull-cup wrath  (TIGHT · skull-motif)
- **Arm-ends.** Six upturned `tiara_skull` craniums used as BOWLS, rim-up; `GOLD` rim-band at each cup mouth;
  pooled `ROSE`→`ROSE_BR` offering-glow inside. Keep Mukha's A-B-A-B rhythm by GLOW SIZE only (A = fat bright
  rose pool, B = small deep-`ROSE_D` ember). Teal a hairline on the upper-pair rims only.
- **Skulls.** Six arm skull-cups + kept LOW 3-skull tiara = halo of nine; the arm-ends literally ARE skulls.
- **Crown/pose.** None — identical body + crown (the tight anchor).
- **32px.** Strongest of the set. Watch: cap each rose offering-pool a notch BELOW `THIRD_BR` so the brow
  third-eye stays the single brightest pixel (six rim dots must not out-glow the one focal).
- **Push-apart vs Mala.** Skulls sit UP as cups WITH internal rose glow, evenly spaced (vs Mala's down-beads).

### 2. maha_kapali — great skull-crowned dread  (LOOSE · skull-motif — corner anchor)
- **Arm-ends.** Six skull-trophy strands: a short `GOLD`-capped rod, each hung with TWO stacked `tiara_skull`
  trophies + a `ROSE` tassel-dot below.
- **Skulls.** Tall fanned MEGA-CROWN locked to **5 skulls** (centre lit `ROSE_BR`) + twelve arm-trophy skulls.
- **Crown/pose.** LOOSE — taller head/upper mass; trophy-strands hang lower than Mukha's caps.
- **32px (highest risk).** ~17 skulls courts the "mush" failure. Crown LOCKED to 5; arm trophies LOCKED to 2
  per strand (drop to 1 + tassel if the chip mushes). The GD MUST show a 32px chip proving crown skulls do
  NOT merge into the topmost arm strands. Target read = "tall skull-crown + skulls dripping off the arms"
  value-clump, NOT countable skulls.
- **Push-apart vs Nritya.** Dense tight/tall 5-stack + lit centre + arm-trophies (motif) vs Nritya's airy fan.

### 3. nritya_devi — dancing wrath  (LOOSE · prominent — the lone pose-mover)
- **Arm-ends.** Asymmetric dance ornaments: 2 hands trail thin `ROSE` scarf-ribbons (flicked gap-ward), 2
  shake `GOLD` ankle-bell clusters, 1 spins a `TEAL`-headed `GOLD` damaru drum (rose cord), 1 holds a single
  `tiara_skull` rattle. Asymmetric L/R on purpose.
- **Skulls.** Taller fanned ~4–5-skull crest + the one skull-rattle (prominent, body/face still lead).
- **Crown/pose.** LOOSE — asymmetric arm angles + a slight tribhanga torso tilt + taller fanned crest.
- **32px.** Fatten the rose scarves to short, FAT, 2-segment ink-keylined ribbons (thin flicks vanish on busy
  sky). Keep the tilt subtle — dance, not a rendering bug.
- **Push-apart vs Maha.** Hold crest ≤5 and keep it WIDE/airy/light so it never reads as Maha's mega-crown.

### 4. mala_mata — skull-garland mother  (MID · skull-motif — connected rim)
- **Arm-ends.** Six small `GOLD` ring-clasps, each hanging a `tiara_skull`, JOINED hand-to-hand by a sagging
  `ROSE`-cord garland (a `mundamala`) with tiny gold spacer-dots. The three upper swags dip into the open
  sky beside the crown, framing the face in a hammock of skulls.
- **Skulls.** 6 garland skulls + 3 tiara ≈ 9 ringing the face; "she wears the dead."
- **Crown/pose.** Tiara kept; the connected garland is the loud element (MID on tight↔loose).
- **32px.** Cord = a 2px ink-keylined rose line; let the SKULL BEADS carry the read (a sag of skull-dots under
  the arms). Protect the negative-space wedge above the crown so the garland FRAMES rather than fills it.
- **Push-apart vs Kapala.** Skulls hang DOWN as inert beads on a visible cord, NO internal glow (vs up-cups).

### 5. padma_mata — bloom-and-relic mother  (TIGHT · prominent — tonal counterweight)
- **Arm-ends.** Six bloom-clusters: short `BONE`/`GOLD` petal-blobs opening around a central relic. A = `ROSE`
  disc cradled in petals; B = `GOLD` seed-pod with a `TEAL` dewdrop. TWO of the six (mid arms, one per side)
  tuck a tiny `tiara_skull` BUD among the petals.
- **Skulls.** 3 tiara + 2 hidden skull-buds = 5, soft/hidden — stays PROMINENT, not motif.
- **Crown/pose.** None. Faint accent warmth only — nudge `ROSE` a hair coral in the bloom hearts (WITHIN
  family; clear of KFC red / any owned warm hue).
- **32px.** LOW petal count (4–5 FAT petals per bloom, not a many-petal rosette) so each reads as a chunky
  flower-mass. Size the hidden skull-buds ≥ tiara-skull footprint and let them peek clearly above the petals
  on those two arms — if they vanish, the whole "scary-CUTE hidden skull" hook is lost.
- **The non-wrathful note** — keep it the gentlest, most charming sister so the brood isn't one flavor.
