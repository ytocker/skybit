# Mukha-Devi KIN — grounded sister-brood (brainstorm, no full render)

## The thesis: sisters, not strangers

Mukha-Devi is the **Original**. These ~7 directions are her **SISTERS** — same
bloodline, same body. Every one **keeps her shipped DNA, untouched**:

- the **six-arm radial fan** (few-and-fat bone limbs splaying in a wide symmetric
  starburst around a tiny chibi torso, low-origin so the crown stays open);
- the **rose / gold / teal bone palette** (`BONE 224,200,196` dusty rose-bone mass;
  `ROSE 232,86,150` magenta relic-glow; `GOLD 224,182,84` trim; `TEAL 64,170,166`
  bell-sliver; `INK 28,22,26`) — only small per-sister **accent shifts** are allowed,
  never a clashing new hue;
- the central **chibi skull-face + the single brightest rose third-eye** on the brow.

So distinctness here does NOT come from silhouette (the skill is relaxed by the
user). It comes from **two levers only**:

1. **The arm-end ornament SET** — Mukha today carries only TWO simple alternating
   relics (a rose-disc-in-gold-ring `kind A`, and a gold flame-triangle with a rose
   ember `kind B`). Each sister replaces those six caps with her own **elaborate,
   distinct ornament set**, built only from the existing chunky triad grammar
   (`triad_circle` / `triad_blob` / `tiara_skull`) so it still reads at 32px.
2. **The skull treatment** — Mukha has a LOW 3-skull tiara. Each sister pushes
   skulls further: more of them, bigger, garlanded, or worked into the arm-ends
   themselves (reusing the existing `tiara_skull()` mass).

Some sisters touch **crown / pose / proportion** too (the "loose" ones); the rest
vary **only ornaments + skulls** (the "tight" ones). All six arms + the palette
survive in every case.

### The two spreads every sister is placed on

- **Tight ↔ Loose** — tight = arm-ends + skulls only; loose = also crown / proportion / pose.
- **Prominent ↔ Skull-is-the-motif** — *prominent* = bone-body + rose third-eye still
  lead, skulls are strong supporting beats; *skull-motif* = skulls become the defining
  read of the whole sprite (skull-tipped arms, garlands, dominant skull-crown).

The closing table lays all 7 on both spreads so the final 5 can guarantee coverage
(≥1 tight, ≥1 loose; ≥1 prominent, ≥1 skull-motif).

## 1. GHANTA-MATA — the bell-reliquary mother  *(TIGHT · prominent)*

**Thesis.** The quietest sister: she changes nothing of the body, only trades
Mukha's two plain relics for six **ornate bell-reliquaries** — tiny ceremonial
hand-bells, each a different ring of detail. She is the "control" sister that proves
how much richness the arm-ends alone can carry.

**Arm-end set.** Six **gold ghanta bells**: a `triad_circle` gold dome-body with a
hard `BONE_DD` mouth-arc at the bottom (reuse the pillar's existing bell arc), a thin
`GOLD_BR` collar ring at the crown, and a swinging `ROSE` clapper-dot inside the
mouth. The three **upper** bells (the near-horizontal arms, most visible) get a
`TEAL` rim-stripe on the lip — Mukha's literal teal sliver, promoted to a recurring
bell accent. Alternate ornament rhythm A-B-A-B-A-B: A-bells hang a single rose
disc-charm below the lip; B-bells hang a **tiny tiara_skull charm** below the lip.
Gold = body, rose = clapper + charm, teal = three lip-stripes.

**Skull treatment.** Mukha's LOW 3-skull tiara, kept as-is, PLUS three tiny
`tiara_skull` clapper-charms on the B-bells — so skulls quietly double (3 crown + 3
arm-charms) without becoming the motif.

**Crown / pose / proportion.** None — tight. Identical body to the Original.

**Position.** Tight end of Tight↔Loose; prominent end of the skull spread (face +
third-eye still lead; bells, not skulls, are the headline).

**Why a distinct sister.** Six richly-detailed bells with teal-lipped, clapper-belled
ornament beats read completely unlike two flat alt-relics — pure arm-end + charm work.

## 2. ASTRA-DEVI — the weapon-bearer  *(TIGHT-to-MID · prominent)*

**Thesis.** The wrathful armory sister. Each of the six hands now grips a **distinct
ceremonial WEAPON** instead of a glow-cap — the classic devi astras — but rendered as
six chunky equal-footprint blobs so the rim still reads as a clean ring, not a fringe.

**Arm-end set.** Six small gold weapons, one per hand, each a `triad_blob`/
`triad_circle` mass sized to the same footprint as Mukha's relic_r so the starburst
stays even: **vajra** (double-ended gold dumbbell with rose core), **trishula**
(three-prong gold trident, rose ember at the socket), **katar** (short gold push-
dagger triangle, teal guard-bar), **shankha** (rose-mouthed gold conch spiral),
**gada** (gold mace-ball on a stub, `BONE_DD` flange grooves), and a **chakra** (gold
ring with hard spokes, rose hub — basically a richer version of Mukha's existing
disc-relic). Gold is every weapon body; rose is each weapon's single glow-heart; teal
is one accent (the katar guard, the conch lip).

**Skull treatment.** The 3-skull tiara stays. Skulls do NOT migrate to the arms here —
the weapons own the rim — so it sits at the prominent end.

**Crown / pose / proportion.** Tiny pose nudge only: the two lowest arms angle their
weapons slightly **gap-ward** (vajra and gada pointing down-out) for a "ready" stance;
body + crown unchanged. Borderline tight — call it tight-to-mid.

**Why a distinct sister.** Six legible different weapons (vs two alt-relics) make her
the obvious "armed wrath" read while every weapon stays inside the chunky triad
grammar; no silhouette change needed.

## 3. MALA-MATA — the skull-garland mother  *(MID · skull-motif)*

**Thesis.** The first true skull-motif sister: she strings a **mundamala (skull
garland)** through all six hands, so the rim ceases to be six separate caps and
becomes one continuous **rope of tiny skulls** swagging arm-to-arm. Skulls are now
the headline of the whole sprite.

**Arm-end set.** Each hand grips a **gold ring-clasp** (small `triad_circle` gold
loop) from which a `tiara_skull` hangs, and a **thin rose cord** (Mukha's prayer-cord
linear accent, promoted) swags between adjacent hands — so the outer arc reads as six
gold clasps joined by a sagging garland of ~6 skull-beads with rose cord and tiny gold
spacer-dots between beads. Gold = clasps + spacers, rose = cord, the bone skulls = the
beads. The three upper swags dip into the open sky beside the crown, framing the face
in a hammock of skulls.

**Skull treatment.** DOMINANT. Six garland skulls on the arms + the 3 on the tiara =
nine skulls ringing the face; the tiny rose third-eye still wins as brightest pixel but
the *read* is "she wears the dead."

**Crown / pose / proportion.** Tiara kept (3 skulls) but the garland is the loud
element; arms unchanged. Mid on tight↔loose (no body change, but the connective
garland is a real ornament-system change, more than a per-cap swap).

**Why a distinct sister.** Connected garland-of-skulls vs six discrete relics is an
entirely different rim logic, and it flips her to skull-motif without altering the
six-arm silhouette at all.

## 4. KAPALA-DEVI — the six-skull-cup wrath  *(TIGHT · skull-motif)*

**Thesis.** The tight-but-terrifying sister: keep the exact body, but every hand now
**holds up a skull** instead of a relic — the kapala (skull-cup) offering. Six skulls
ring the rim like Mukha's six glow-caps, so it's the cleanest possible skull-motif:
just swap the cap type, change nothing else.

**Arm-end set.** Six **upturned skull-cups**: each is a `tiara_skull` cranium used as a
bowl, rim-up, with a `GOLD` rim-band around the cup mouth and a pooled `ROSE` glow
(`ROSE`→`ROSE_BR`) sitting inside the cup as the "offering" — so each arm-end is a
rose-lit gilt-rimmed skull. Alternate A-B by glow size: A-cups brim with a fat rose
pool (brighter), B-cups hold a smaller deep-`ROSE_D` ember — keeping Mukha's A-B-A-B
rhythm. Gold = six rim-bands; rose = six offering-glows; teal optional as a single
hairline on the upper-pair rims.

**Skull treatment.** DOMINANT and clean: six skull-cups on the arms + 3-skull tiara.
The arm-ends literally ARE skulls, so the silhouette's outer ring reads as a halo of
nine skulls. No new skull *system* (no garland) — just the cap type swapped, hence
tight.

**Crown / pose / proportion.** None — tight. Identical body and crown to the Original.

**Why a distinct sister.** Same construction effort as Mukha (six caps) but the cap is
a skull-cup, not a disc/flame — the minimal edit that maximally flips prominent→
skull-motif. Pairs as the tight counterpart to Mala-Mata's loose garland.

## 5. PADMA-MATA — the bloom-and-relic mother  *(TIGHT · prominent)*

**Thesis.** The gentlest, most ornamental sister — and the palette-accent showcase.
Each hand cradles a **lotus-bloom cradling a relic**, turning the rim into six little
flowers. She proves the brood can carry beauty as well as wrath while staying scary-
cute.

**Arm-end set.** Six **bloom-clusters**: a ring of short `BONE`/`GOLD` petal-blobs
(reuse the pillar's lotus-burst petal loop) opening around a central relic. Alternate
A-B: A-blooms cradle a `ROSE` disc (Mukha's type-A relic, now nested in petals);
B-blooms cradle a `GOLD` seed-pod with a `TEAL` dewdrop highlight — the teal sliver as
a recurring petal-dew. Two of the six blooms (one per side, the mid arms) tuck a
**tiny `tiara_skull` bud** among the petals — a soft hint of death inside the flower.
Gold = petal tips + pods, rose = bloom hearts, teal = dew accents.

**Skull treatment.** Tiara kept (3 skulls) + two tiny skull-buds nestled in arm
blooms = 5 skulls, but soft and hidden — she stays prominent (face/third-eye lead),
NOT skull-motif. Slight accent shift: nudge `ROSE` a touch warmer-coral in the bloom
hearts (within family) for a "blossom" read.

**Crown / pose / proportion.** None — tight body. Only the arm-ends and a faint
accent-warmth change.

**Why a distinct sister.** Six floral relic-clusters read totally unlike two hard
alt-relics; the buried skull-buds give her a unique "flower with a skull in it" charm
no other sister has.

## 6. NRITYA-DEVI — the dancing wrath  *(LOOSE · prominent)*

**Thesis.** The first genuinely LOOSE sister: she keeps six arms + palette, but the
fan is thrown into an **asymmetric dancing pose** and she gains a **taller fanned
crown**, so her proportion and silhouette-feel shift while staying unmistakably
Mukha's kin. Her arm-ends are **dance-attributes**: scarves, anklet-bells, a drum.

**Arm-end set.** Six **dance ornaments**: two hands trail thin `ROSE` scarf-ribbons
(linear cord accent, flicked gap-ward), two hands shake `GOLD` ankle-bell clusters
(a tight `triad_circle` rose-belled cluster — three tiny gold bells per hand), one
hand spins a small `TEAL`-skinned damaru hand-drum (gold body, teal heads, rose
cord), and one hand holds a single **`tiara_skull` rattle** on a stick. Gold = bells +
drum body, rose = scarves + drum cord, teal = drum heads (promoting the bell-sliver to
a drumskin). Asymmetric on purpose — left and right arms carry different attributes —
so the dance reads.

**Skull treatment.** Tiara kept but **taller, fanned to ~5 small skulls** in a peacock
crest (still her bone palette, still scary-cute) — a looser crown than Mukha's low 3,
plus the one skull-rattle in hand. Skulls grow but the body/face still lead, so she
lands prominent.

**Crown / pose / proportion.** LOOSE — asymmetric dance pose (arms at uneven angles,
torso given a slight tribhanga tilt), taller fanned 5-skull crest. Six arms + palette
intact.

**Why a distinct sister.** The only sister whose *pose* moves; the dance-attribute
arm-ends (scarves/bells/drum) are a unique ornament family, and the tilt + taller
crest read as motion no static sister has.

## 7. MAHA-KAPALI — the great skull-crowned dread  *(LOOSE · skull-motif)*

**Thesis.** The most extreme sister and the brood's "final boss" energy: skulls are
the entire identity. A **towering skull-crown** dominates the head, the arm-ends are
**skull-trophy strands**, and her proportions push the head/crown taller. Still six
arms, still the rose-bone palette, still the third-eye — but everything else is skulls.

**Arm-end set.** Six **bone-trophy strands**: each hand grips a short vertical
`GOLD`-capped rod hung with a stack of 2 tiny `tiara_skull` trophies and a `ROSE`
tassel-dot at the bottom — so each arm-end is a little hanging totem of stacked
skulls. Twelve arm-skulls total around the rim. Gold = the rod caps + a hairline ring
between stacked skulls, rose = the tassels, teal = a single tassel-bead on the two
upper strands for palette balance.

**Skull treatment.** MAXIMALLY dominant: a **tall fanned 5-or-7 skull mega-crown**
arcing high over the head (vs Mukha's low 3), centre skull lit `ROSE_BR`, PLUS the
twelve arm-trophy skulls. The face's own skull + third-eye remain the anchor, but the
sprite reads as a storm of skulls — the clear skull-motif extreme.

**Crown / pose / proportion.** LOOSE — taller head + tall mega-crown raises the whole
silhouette's upper mass; arms unchanged in count/origin but their trophy-strands hang
lower. Six arms + palette intact.

**Why a distinct sister.** The only sister stacking skulls on BOTH the crown (mega-
arc) and every arm (trophy strands) — the unmistakable skull-everything counterweight
to tight, prominent Ghanta-Mata at the other end of both spreads.
