# Mukha-Devi KIN — five grounded sister-bosses

![Mukha-Devi kin showcase](./showcase.png)

Five new bosses spun off the shipped **Mukha-Devi** (the six-armed wrathful bone-mother / radial-fan member
of the Citipati brood) — as her **grounded SISTERS**. Unlike the earlier
[`mukha_devi_versions/`](../mukha_devi_versions/README.md) brood, which deliberately diverged into five
distinct silhouette KINDs, this set stays **tightly grounded in Mukha herself**: every sister keeps her
**six-arm radial fan**, her **rose / gold / teal bone palette**, and her **central chibi skull-face + rose
third-eye (the single brightest pixel)**. Distinctness lives only in (a) a far more **elaborate arm-end
ornament SET** — replacing her two simple alternating relics — and (b) a pushed-up **skull treatment**.

> **Deliberate doctrine note.** The `distinct-design-variants` rule (five unique bottom-up silhouettes) is
> intentionally **relaxed** here at the user's direction. These are sisters of one goddess, not five
> creature KINDs — distinctness is carried by ornament + skull treatment, and by a **mixture** across two
> spreads: tight↔loose (some vary only arm-ends + skulls; some also vary crown/pose) and
> prominent↔skull-is-the-motif.

The five (tight → loose):

- **Kapala-Devi** — six upturned skull-CUPS (rim-up, gold rims, rose offering-glow); halo of nine skulls.
  *(tight · skull-motif)* — **SHIP-READY** (round 2) — [./kapala_devi](./kapala_devi)
- **Padma-Mata** — six fat 5-petal bloom-cradles, two hiding a half-buried skull-bud; the gentle
  counterweight. *(tight · prominent)* — final round 3 — [./padma_mata](./padma_mata)
- **Mala-Mata** — a connected skull-garland (mundamala) swagged hand-to-hand, framing the face.
  *(mid · skull-motif)* — final round 3 — [./mala_mata](./mala_mata)
- **Nritya-Devi** — dancing wrath: asymmetric tribhanga pose, airy crest, dance-attribute arm-ends
  (scarves / bells / damaru / skull-rattle). *(loose · prominent)* — final round 3 — [./nritya_devi](./nritya_devi)
- **Maha-Kapali** — great skull-crowned dread: tall 5-skull mega-crown + six skull-trophy strands.
  *(loose · skull-motif — the corner anchor)* — **SHIP-READY** (round 2) — [./maha_kapali](./maha_kapali)

**Ship-status:** Kapala-Devi and Maha-Kapali earned genuine `SHIP-READY` verdicts at round 2. Nritya-Devi,
Mala-Mata, and Padma-Mata ran the full GD3/AD2 budget — their round-2 critiques judged each "one clean pass
from ship," and the round-3 self-checks confirmed those exact gates landed (Nritya's night third-eye now
out-luminates the bone sheen + scarves separate on day; Mala's sky-wedge punches through the crown, verified
by mask row-scan; Padma's skull-buds now read as half-buried emerging skulls). The loop ends on the designer
revision, so those three carry no post-round-3 sign-off — see each concept's `critique_round*.md`.
