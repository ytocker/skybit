VERDICT: ITERATE

# Calaca Comelona — round 1 critique (AD)

Most important fix: the SIGNATURE tell (open ribcage-pantry with a visible interior
food cluster) does not survive to 32px — at true gameplay size it reads as a
generic horizontally-striped skeleton, identical in kind to the shipped Mariachi
and the other warm-bone variants. The whole concept rides on that read; it must be
re-engineered as a bold dark interior cavity + 3 high-contrast food lobes, not
thin rib-banding.

## Verdict rationale
The palette and house style are genuinely on-brief and good. But this is an
EXPOSED-ANATOMY lead facet, and the one thing that makes it exposed-anatomy (the
food-stuffed open cupboard) is the first thing that disappears at scale. That is a
core-thesis miss, not a polish miss — hence ITERATE, not SHIP-READY.

## What's measured (so the designer keeps it)
- **Red-split: PASS, keep exactly as is.** Sampled non-bg pixels:
  large creature red ~1.8% / gold ~18% / bone ~33%; 32px-on-sky red ~1.1% /
  gold ~46%. Chile-red `(214,86,44)` is confined to the hatband + the single
  cigar-chile in the jaw — never a red-dominant read. Zero collision risk with
  Zapateada's pink-rose or Jinete's brick-rust. This is the gold-led/red-demoted
  read the brief pinned. Do NOT add more red in revision.
- **Palette is on-pin.** masa-gold, ochre hat, warm-bone, tan-bone shade, sheen,
  ink all match the pinned hexes. "Kitchen, not warrior" read lands.

## RANKING — strongest → weakest aspect
1. **Color / red-split (STRONGEST).** Textbook execution of the hardest pin.
2. **House-style triad.** Dark-core/flat-fill/top-left sheen is present and flat,
   no gradients. Ink keylines clean at large scale.
3. **Scary-CUTE.** The big-head/short-body chibi + cigar-chile grin is likable.
4. **Prop->pillar mirror.** Functional but generic (see FIX 4).
5. **32px silhouette + signature tell (WEAKEST).** Fails the gate read.

## KEEP
- Gold-led palette and red restraint — locked, don't touch.
- Cigar-chile clenched in the jaw — great cute/comedy tell, reads even small.
- Offered-tamale hand asymmetry — keep; it's the "vendor" + asymmetry tell.
- Flat triad banding on the large render.

## FIX (prioritized punch list)
1. **Re-engineer the ribcage as a CAVITY, not stripes.** At 32px the current
   even rib-banding is indistinguishable from any skeleton's chest. Make the
   belly a single bold DARK interior pocket (deep ink-core rectangle/arch),
   framed by only ~3 thick rib bands per side spread open like cupboard doors.
   The dark hole is the silhouette tell — negative space does the work, the way
   Risón's dropped-jaw and Papelito's punched sockets are pinned to.
2. **Make the 3 food lobes POP out of the cavity at 32px.** Right now the interior
   cluster vanishes. Give each lobe max contrast against the dark interior:
   masa-gold tamale + cream pan-dulce + the one chile-red lobe, each a fat round
   blob with its own sheen dot, sitting PROUD of the dark pocket. 3 lobes MAX
   (brief already says drop the 4th garnish — hold that line). They should read
   as "stuff inside the chest" at 1x, not texture.
3. **Fix the silhouette weight — it's bottom-LIGHT, not bottom-heavy.** The black
   silhouette chip reads top-heavy (hat + chest mass) with spindly legs and a
   pinched waist; the brief calls a "squat BOTTOM-HEAVY pear." Widen the
   pelvis/lower body or bow the femurs outward so the lower mass anchors the
   shape. Currently it silhouettes closer to the shipped Mariachi than it should.
4. **Differentiate the hat from Mariachi.** Both wear a flat straw disc; at 32px
   the hat+skull cap is near-identical to the anchor. Distinguish via a FLATTER,
   wider vendor-brim profile and/or a distinct hatband notch, so the gap-read
   between the two skeletons is the belly cavity, not just the body below an
   identical hat. The belly tell must carry it — see FIX 1.
5. **Pillar cap is generic.** The comal+tamale-dome cap currently reads as just
   another round bead on the shaft, near-identical to the body banding. Give the
   comal cap a clearly distinct flat-disc griddle silhouette (wider than the
   shaft, with the stacked-tamale dome breaking the round) so the gap-edge cap is
   legible as a separate element. Mirror axis is clean — keep that.
6. **Polish:** the large render's rib lip sheen is slightly noisy on the
   patient's right side; once you drop to ~3 thick ribs (FIX 1) this resolves.

## Re-roll guard
Not a re-roll — the lead, palette, and charm are right. This is a focused
re-construction of the belly-cavity read so the EXPOSED-ANATOMY facet survives
to gameplay scale. Next round: show the true 32px chip on BOTH a day-ochre and a
night sky, with the dark-cavity-plus-3-lobes read holding.
