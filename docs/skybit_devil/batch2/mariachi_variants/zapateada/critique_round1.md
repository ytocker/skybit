# Zapateada — Round 1 critique (lead facet: DANCE / MOTION)

A strong, on-thesis foundation: the swirling-skirt KIND is unmistakably its own
silhouette, the skull is genuinely scary-CUTE (lash-flicks + temple-rose +
joyful grin land), and the prop-mirror is correctly resolved as a symmetric
radial rosette. But it FAILS two of the three CRITICAL pins: the skirt hue is
NOT rose-magenta (it has drifted into a dusty salmon/brick that will converge
with Jinete's rust), and the figure does NOT read as MOTION at a glance — the
lean is too timid and the raised arm vanishes. Both are fixable in one round;
neither is a re-roll.

## Ranking — strongest to weakest aspect
1. **Prop → pillar mirror (KEEP, ship-grade).** The radial ribbon-rosette is
   genuinely symmetric on the pole axis, castanets read, wound-ribbon banding
   repeats cleanly, gap-edge cap detaches well. This is the strongest panel and
   needs no change beyond inheriting the corrected hero hue.
2. **Skull / scary-cute character (KEEP).** Lash-flicks, pink eye-glow, temple
   rose, open grin — charming and on-house. Holds at 48px.
3. **Skirt construction (PARTIAL).** Three tiers + comma-curve sweep is right,
   but the energy is leaking out (see FIX 2).
4. **Silhouette MOTION read (FIX — critical).** The black-silhouette panel reads
   as a near-UPRIGHT figure with a sweeping hem — closer to a curtsey than a
   twirl. It does not announce "this is the moving one" against the other four.
5. **PINNED PALETTE / red-split (FIX — critical).** Skirt is sampling ~(174,118,
   115)–(203,127,127): a desaturated salmon-brick, well off the pinned
   rose-magenta (214,72,86). At 32px this will blur straight into Jinete's rust
   (176,70,56) — defeating the entire cross-set mandate.

## FIX (prioritised)
1. **[CRITICAL — palette] Repaint the skirt to the pinned ROSE-MAGENTA
   (214,72,86).** The current fill is far too desaturated and too warm/brown —
   it is sliding into Jinete's lane. Push saturation up hard and the hue COOLER
   (toward pink). Verify the flat-fill mid-tone samples within a few points of
   (214,72,86), with the triad dark-core cooler still and the top-left rim-sheen
   the lightest pink. Put the corrected hero swatch and a Comelona-chile +
   Jinete-rust comparison chip side by side at 32px to PROVE the three-way split.
2. **[CRITICAL — silhouette/motion] Sell the twirl.** Right now the torso is too
   vertical and the raised arm merges into the head, so the silhouette reads
   static. (a) Tilt the whole torso+skull ~15–20 degrees INTO the spin so the
   spine is a clear diagonal in the black panel. (b) Lift and SEPARATE the raised
   arm so it arcs clear of the cranium with sky-gap between arm and head — that
   negative space is what reads as "arm thrown overhead" at 32px. (c) Exaggerate
   the skirt's comma-curve so the hem trails on the OPPOSITE side from the lean
   (counter-swing) — that asymmetry is the motion tell.
3. **[silhouette legibility] The ribcage/arm tangle mid-body turns to noise.**
   The crossed bone-arms over the ribs read as a knot at 48px and worse at 32px.
   Simplify to a clean readable ribcage + one clearly-arced raised arm + one
   trailing arm; drop interior rib-line count so the torso reads as bone, not
   scribble.
4. **[motion line] The turquoise ribbon is good but currently short and stiff.**
   Let it trail in a longer curve following the spin direction — a cheap, strong
   motion cue that also re-states the cool accent that helps separate E from the
   warm-red siblings.
5. **[value] Skirt value structure is flat/hazy.** The semi-transparent skirt
   overlay is washing the whole lower mass to one mid-value. Restore the
   dark-core → flat-fill → rim-sheen triad as hard BANDS per ruffle tier so each
   tier reads as a distinct lit lobe, not a single soft pink blob.
