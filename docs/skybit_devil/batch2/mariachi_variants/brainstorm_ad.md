# AD BRAINSTORM-CRITIQUE — Mariachi-lineage "warm-skeleton family" (8 → LOCKED 5)

VERDICT: ITERATE

The set is strong and genuinely bottom-up — eight different ideas of WHAT the
skeleton IS, not eight re-dresses. Distinctness across silhouette KINDs is real
and the warm-lane separation is mostly thought through. But the facet structure
forces the pairs, and in two of the three pairs the *more novel / lower-collision*
candidate is the keep, which knocks out the two members that drift toward shipped
bosses (Florita→Catrina, Tamborero→Mariachi/Raijin). One cross-set palette fix is
mandatory before the per-concept loops or three of the survivors crowd the red/rose
lane. Locking briefs below; GD does one final pass to pin them, then per-concept.

---

## THE LOCKED 5 (one per lead facet + the blend)

1. **B — Calaca Comelona** [EXPOSED ANATOMY] — open-ribcage food-pantry belly is a
   one-of-a-kind silhouette KIND (bottom-heavy pear with an interior cluster) and
   the warmest, most-separated palette in the set (bone + chile-red + masa-gold,
   "kitchen not warrior"). Beats A on novelty and on staying clear of Mariachi's
   rust-on-bone.
2. **D — Papelito** [CALACA DECORATION] — the ONLY non-volumetric, flat cut-paper
   body in the entire roster; scalloped punched-face rectangle reads as PAPER at
   32px against any other bone boss. Zero collision, maximum KIND-distance. Trivial
   keep.
3. **E — Zapateada** [DANCE / MOTION] — the only ASYMMETRIC, in-motion silhouette
   in the whole family (off-axis swirling bell-skirt + lean). Motion is carried by
   the shape, not just pose. Beats F on distinctness and on dodging the
   Mariachi-musician and Raijin-drum collisions.
4. **G — Risón** [FACIAL CHARACTER] — singleton; the head-back, dropped-open-jaw
   belly-laugh is the most face-forward read in the set and the warm amber/terracotta
   clay lane is unoccupied. Keep, with a reaper-skull collision guard (below).
5. **H — El Charro Jinete** [BLEND] — singleton; the rearing two-body horse+rider is
   the tallest, most dramatic, "boss-iest" silhouette and the only multi-body KIND.
   Keep; it gets the full house triad as the grand-boss anchor.

## THE 3 CUTS

- **A — Costillas (luchador)** — cut. Loses the anatomy pair to B: a barrel ribcage
  flex is a more conventional skeleton read than B's open-belly-with-food, and A's
  CRIMSON-mask-on-bone sits one step from Mariachi's rust-red-on-bone — the redder
  the survivor pool, the more A crowds it. B is the braver, warmer, less-collidable
  anatomy lead. (A is the strongest cut — a viable re-roll fallback if B proves
  too busy at 32px in its per-concept loop.)
- **C — Florita (sugar-skull bride)** — cut on COLLISION. Catrina is already the
  shipped "flower-crowned festive female DotD calaca." At 32px "skull + heaped
  flowers on the crown + festive dame body" reads as Catrina regardless of
  wreath-halo-vs-platter-brim and marigold-vs-teal nuance. The decoration facet is
  better served by D, which collides with nothing. Florita's flower-saturation
  duplicates Catrina's whole pitch.
- **F — Tamborero (drummer)** — cut on DOUBLE COLLISION. Pulls against Mariachi
  (same musician-calaca lineage, the exact thing this batch was told to push PAST)
  AND against Raijin's drums. GD's "marching strut + slung drum vs seated strummer
  vs overhead drum-halo" defense is real but thin at 32px, where "festive calaca +
  prominent drum" blurs into the shipped musician. E carries the motion facet with
  a silhouette that collides with nothing.

## NEAR-DUPLICATE / PALETTE / COLLISION FLAGS

**Palette — the one mandatory cross-set fix.** With A and F cut, the surviving reds
are B chile-red `(208,72,48)`, E rose-red `(210,64,72)`, and H rust-red `(186,60,48)`
— three warm reds that risk reading alike at 32px. They must split on a different
axis each:
- B chile-red is a small *accent* (hatband + a chile lobe), NOT a hero mass — its
  hero is masa-GOLD + the food cluster. Pin B so red never becomes the dominant read;
  if it creeps up, push the chile toward a more orange `(214,86,44)` so it can't be
  confused with E's pink-rose.
- E rose-red `(210,64,72)` is the only PINK-leaning red and it IS the hero mass
  (the swept skirt). Pin it pinker/cooler if needed `(214,72,86)` to hold clear of
  H's brown-rust. The asymmetric motion silhouette already separates it, but the
  hue must not converge.
- H rust-red `(186,60,48)` is the brownest/darkest red and rides WITH ochre +
  turquoise as the full house triad — it must stay the desaturated brick lane, not
  brighten toward E.
  Net: B = gold-led (red demoted to accent), E = pink-rose hero, H = brick-rust +
  ochre + turquoise. Three different VALUES and three different hue-leans. GD must
  state this split explicitly in each locked brief.

**G — Risón skull vs the batch-1 reapers.** G's whole pitch is an oversized
expressive SKULL, and the reaper batch (Big Reapy, Twinface, Clovenpate, Pyrecrown)
is skull-led. Guard: Risón is a warm festive Mexican CALACA (painted moustache,
tipped-back sombrero, amber/clay palette, dropped-jaw LAUGH) — the expression and
the warm sombrero/sash keep it off the cool grim reaper skulls. Pin the sombrero +
moustache + amber lane as non-negotiable so it never reads as a bare reaper cranium;
the dropped-open-jaw laugh is the distinguishing geometry — make the open jaw the
clear focal negative space, not a generic grin.

**G amber vs H ochre.** G's amber/terracotta `(228,168,72)`/`(196,108,64)` and H's
ochre `(214,162,76)` are both warm yellows. They separate on silhouette KIND
(giant tilted laughing head vs rearing horse+rider) and on H carrying turquoise +
rust as co-leads while G leads on clay/terracotta with no turquoise body mass. Keep
G's turquoise to a tiny jug-fleck only so the two yellows don't both wear turquoise.

**No remaining shipped-roster collisions among the 5:** B open-belly pantry, D
flat-paper, E swirling-skirt, G laughing-head, H horse+rider — none echoes Mariachi
(seated strummer), Catrina (platter-hat dame), Necrarch (floating robe-lich), Draugr
(helm+shield brute), or Jiangshi (arms-out hopper). Florita and Tamborero — the two
that DID drift — are exactly the two cut. Good.

## LOCKING BRIEFS (GD final pass before per-concept loops)

**B — Calaca Comelona.** Lock the bottom-heavy pear + open-cupboard-ribcage with a
3–4 lobe interior food cluster as the signature read. PALETTE RE-SPEC: masa-GOLD is
the hero mass; chile-red demoted to accent only (hatband + one chile), pushed
oranger `(214,86,44)` to stay off E's pink-rose. Keep the offered-tamale hand — it
adds the "vendor" read and an asymmetry tell. Watch 32px legibility of the interior
cluster: 3 fat lobes max, hard triad, no fourth small one that turns to noise.

**D — Papelito.** Lock the scalloped punched-skull-face rectangle + bunting string.
This is the only flat-paper body — protect it: NO volumetric bone anywhere, triad
reads strictly as front-sheet / back-sheet shadow / top-edge sheen. Multi-hue paper
is fine BUT keep turquoise the single cool note and lead warm (marigold + papel-pink
+ masa-gold) so it stays in the family. Punched sockets must show sky-through at
32px — that negative-space tell is the whole gag.

**E — Zapateada.** Lock the off-axis swirling bell-skirt + lean as the silhouette;
this is the family's only asymmetric/in-motion read — do not let the per-concept
loop straighten it. PALETTE: rose-red `(210,64,72)`→ pin slightly pinker/cooler
`(214,72,86)` as the swept hero mass to hold clear of H's brick-rust; turquoise
ribbon stays the cool accent. 3 skirt ruffle tiers, all leaning one way — motion in
the lean, not in extra tiers.

**G — Risón.** Lock the oversized head-back skull + dropped-open hinged jaw as the
focal; the open-jaw negative space is the read. Pin the tipped-back sombrero +
painted moustache + amber/terracotta clay palette as the anti-reaper guard (so it
never reads as a bare grim skull). Turquoise = tiny jug-fleck only (don't share H's
turquoise mass). Body stays minimal/bony — the face does the work; keep the toasting
cup as the asymmetry + character tell.

**H — El Charro Jinete.** Lock the rearing-horse + raised-sombrero rider two-body
silhouette as the grand-boss anchor. Full house triad (rust + ochre + turquoise
zarape) is correct as the richest palette — but keep rust the BRICK/desaturated lane
so it doesn't brighten toward E's rose. Two clearly-skeletal bodies; the rear + the
raised arm carry the action. Per the house mirror rule, the prop (lance/finial)
stays slim + symmetric on-axis — the action lives in the creature, not the pillar.
