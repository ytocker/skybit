# Skybit BOSS — MARIACHI-LINEAGE "WARM-SKELETON FAMILY" (LOCKED 5, per-concept ready)

Final brainstorm lock for the warm-skeleton family. The AD culled 8→5 (one per
lead facet + the blend) and pinned ONE mandatory cross-set fix: the three
surviving warm reds (Comelona chile / Zapateada rose / Jinete rust) must NOT
converge at 32px. Every must-fix below is applied as a HARD pin, not a
suggestion.

House style every brief inherits (unchanged from the Mariachi anchor +
`brainstorm_locked15.md` grammar): CHIBI proportions (big head, short wide
weight-shifted body); FLAT saturated fills + hard 1–2px ink keylines
`(28,22,30)`-ish; form via the **dark-core → flat-fill → top-left rim-sheen**
TRIAD (never soft gradient); silhouette POP via a 1px outline grown from the
alpha mask; supersample → smoothscale. Scary-CUTE, never grim. Each tall prop
mirrors top↔bottom into a PILLAR (repeatable shaft body + detachable gap-edge
cap), the way Big Reapy's bone-bident does.

**Anchor (Mariachi, do NOT re-make):** sombrero-disc + grinning skull + short
studded charro jacket + dance-kick legs + round guitarrón; warm-bone + rust-red
+ ochre + turquoise rosette; fairly CLOTHED and cute. All 5 below push HARDER
into bone.

**AD HARD PIN — split the three warm reds on three different axes (VALUE + hue
+ role), so they never blur:**
- **B Comelona** — chile-red is a SMALL ACCENT only (hatband + one chile lobe),
  pushed oranger `(214,86,44)`; **masa-GOLD `(228,186,96)` is the hero body
  mass.** Gold-led, red demoted.
- **E Zapateada** — hero skirt mass pinned PINKER/COOLER **rose-magenta
  `(214,72,86)`**, clearly off Comelona's orange-chile and Jinete's brown-rust.
- **H Jinete** — rust held in a DESATURATED BRICK/LEATHER lane `(176,70,56)`,
  NOT bright; rides WITH ochre + turquoise as the full house triad.
  Net three lanes: B = gold-led / E = pink-rose hero / H = brick-rust + ochre +
  turquoise — three different values, three different hue-leans, three different
  roles for "red."

Mutually-distinct sub-palettes confirmed: D Papelito = paper-rainbow accents on
a CREAM body (the only non-bone body); G Risón = the AMBER/CLAY lane (terracotta
+ amber, turquoise reduced to a tiny fleck so it doesn't share H's turquoise
mass). Five distinct color lanes + five distinct silhouette KINDs.

## 1 — Calaca Comelona  [LEAD FACET: EXPOSED ANATOMY]

- **Thesis.** "La Calaca Comelona" (the eating skeleton) as a street-food
  market VENDOR whose belly is a literal open-ribcage cupboard — pan dulce, a
  chile, and a tamale stuffed between the bones. Anatomy played for comedy: you
  can SEE the food inside it.
- **32px silhouette read.** A squat BOTTOM-HEAVY pear: small skull under a flat
  vendor straw hat, a wide-open exposed RIBCAGE belly framing an interior
  CLUSTER of round food blobs, short bone legs, one bony hand thrust forward
  offering a fat tamale. The open-belly-with-stuff-inside read is one-of-a-kind.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad + 1px ink keylines, scary-cute).**
  Small round skull, big toothy grin, a chile clenched like a cigar in the jaw;
  exposed barrel ribcage spread OPEN like cupboard doors, spine visible up the
  back; INSIDE the cage 3 FAT food lobes MAX (pan dulce, one chile, a round
  tamale) — hard triad on every rib AND every lobe so the pantry reads busy but
  legible; 4th small garnish dropped to avoid 32px noise. Thin pelvis + bowed
  femur legs; offered-tamale hand keeps the "vendor" read + an asymmetry tell.
  Dark-core in rib troughs / flat bone on rib faces / sheen on the top-left rib
  lip — curvature in flat banding, never gradient.
- **Signature prop → PILLAR.** Market-stall pole / hanging ristra: a wooden
  vendor pole strung with a ristra of dried chiles = repeatable shaft (chile
  banding); a round comal griddle with a stacked-tamale dome = gap-edge cap.
  **Mirror CONFIRMED clean** — round comal sits on-axis, symmetric; the
  bottom-heavy creature is the heavy one, the prop is balanced. No top-heavy cap.
- **Lead expressive FACET.** EXPOSED ANATOMY — the open-cupboard ribcage with a
  visible interior food cluster IS the design; gluttony read entirely through
  bared bone.
- **PINNED warm sub-palette (gold-led; red is accent only).**
  masa-GOLD tamale/pan `(228,186,96)` **HERO body/food mass** ·
  pumpkin-ochre straw hat `(214,156,72)` · warm-bone ribcage `(236,222,194)` ·
  tan-bone shade `(174,148,106)` · chile-red ACCENT ONLY `(214,86,44)`
  (hatband + one chile lobe — pushed oranger to stay off E's pink-rose; never
  the dominant read) · cilantro-green garnish tiny `(120,170,80)` ·
  ink `(30,22,20)` · sheen `(250,240,218)`. Intent: warmest "kitchen, not
  warrior" read — gold + bone foreground, red incidental.

## 2 — Papelito  [LEAD FACET: CALACA DECORATION]

- **Thesis.** A calaca built from PAPEL PICADO — perforated tissue-paper banner
  bunting given a body. Torso and limbs are cut-paper sheets with stamped-out
  skull holes; it flutters like festival decoration come alive. Decoration as
  the literal MATERIAL of the creature.
- **32px silhouette read.** A SCALLOPED rectangular paper-sheet "body" (pinked
  zig-zag bottom edge) strung from a string of little bunting flags, a cut-out
  skull face PUNCHED into the top of the sheet (sky shows THROUGH the sockets),
  and tiny paper-strip hands. The fluttering-banner-with-punched-face read is
  unique — flat and papery where every other take is volumetric bone.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad + 1px ink keylines, scary-cute).**
  The "head" is a skull SHAPE punched as negative space out of the sheet-top
  (sky-through sockets — that negative-space tell is the whole gag, must hold at
  32px); torso is one or two layered papel sheets with stamped star/skull/flower
  perforations; pinked scalloped hem; thin paper-strip arms; a string of
  triangular bunting flags arcing over the top like a banner-line. PROTECT THE
  FLATNESS: NO volumetric bone ANYWHERE — triad reads strictly as front-sheet /
  back-sheet shadow / top-edge sheen. Deliberately the LEAST bony body in the
  family (decoration that gained a ghost).
- **Signature prop → PILLAR.** Bunting string / flagpole: a banner cord strung
  with stacked papel-picado flags = repeatable shaft (each flag a band); a
  larger cut-paper marigold-rosette medallion = gap-edge cap. **Mirror CONFIRMED
  clean** — on-axis flags + round rosette, naturally vertical and symmetric. No
  top-heavy cap.
- **Lead expressive FACET.** CALACA DECORATION — the creature is nothing but
  festival decoration that woke up; the punched-out face is decoration as
  anatomy.
- **PINNED warm sub-palette (paper-rainbow accents on a CREAM body — the only
  non-bone body).**
  paper-cream sheet base `(246,238,222)` **HERO body mass** ·
  marigold-orange flag `(244,150,44)` · papel-pink flag `(232,96,150)` ·
  masa-gold flag `(226,182,80)` · turquoise flag `(56,168,168)` (the SINGLE
  cool note — kept to one flag, lineage's owned accent) · rust-red bunting cord
  `(190,60,50)` · ink `(28,22,26)` · sheen `(252,246,232)`. Intent: warm-led
  multi-hue festival paper on cream — the rainbow-bunting read, the only
  non-bone-dominant palette; reads as PAPER.

## 3 — Zapateada  [LEAD FACET: DANCE / MOTION]

- **Thesis.** A BALLET FOLKLÓRICO dancer calaca caught mid-twirl: one bony arm
  arced overhead, a huge ruffled skirt fanned out by the spin, bone legs in a
  pointed zapateado stomp. Motion is the whole design — frozen at peak swirl.
- **32px silhouette read.** A dynamic DIAGONAL: a wide fanned bell-SKIRT swept
  to ONE side by the spin (asymmetric), bone torso leaning INTO the turn, one
  arm raised in an arc. The off-axis swirling-skirt + lean is the family's ONLY
  asymmetric, in-motion silhouette — do not let the per-concept loop straighten
  it.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad + 1px ink keylines, scary-cute).**
  Small skull, painted lash-flicks, a rose tucked at the temple, joyful open
  grin; slim ribcage-and-spine torso partly bare above the skirt waist; arms as
  bone with ribbon trailing; a vast layered RUFFLED skirt swept into a
  comma-curve, built from exactly 3 hard triad-lit ruffle tiers ALL LEANING ONE
  WAY (motion in the lean, NOT in extra tiers); pointed bone feet, one toe-down
  mid-stomp. Skirt ruffles carry the energy — flat fanned lobes.
- **Signature prop → PILLAR.** Ribbon-dance pole / listón maypole: a fluted
  dance pole = repeatable shaft (wound-ribbon banding); a spiral rosette of
  fanned skirt-ruffle + a castanet pair = gap-edge cap. **TOP-HEAVY / OFF-AXIS
  FLAG (AD): the creature's skirt is deliberately asymmetric and off-axis — the
  PROP must NOT inherit that swing.** Fix: the creature stays the asymmetric one;
  the pillar resolves the swept skirt into a SYMMETRIC round ribbon-rosette
  centered on the pole axis (a radial fan, not a one-sided sweep), so the mirror
  reads balanced on-axis. Mirror CONFIRMED clean once the rosette is radial.
- **Lead expressive FACET.** DANCE / MOTION — the spin is the silhouette; the
  whole figure is a frozen frame of a twirl.
- **PINNED warm sub-palette (pink-rose hero — pinker/cooler than B and H).**
  ROSE-MAGENTA ruffle tiers `(214,72,86)` **HERO swept skirt mass** (pinned
  pinker/cooler than Comelona's orange-chile and Jinete's brown-rust — the only
  PINK-leaning red in the family) · warm-bone `(236,224,200)` · tan-bone shade
  `(178,154,112)` · marigold-gold ruffle-trim `(232,184,80)` · turquoise ribbon
  `(58,172,166)` (cool accent) · coral temple-rose `(238,116,92)` ·
  ink `(28,22,24)` · sheen `(250,242,222)`. Intent: bright pink-rose skirt +
  bone + turquoise ribbon — the cool-pink red lane; asymmetric motion AND hue
  hold it clear of B and H.

## 4 — Risón  [LEAD FACET: FACIAL CHARACTER / EMOTION]

- **Thesis.** A cantina merrymaker calaca whose whole personality lives in the
  SKULL: a head thrown back in a huge belly-laugh, jaw cracked wide, sockets
  crinkled with mirth, a sombrero tipped back off the brow. Emotion-first — the
  face does all the work; body is minimal and bony.
- **32px silhouette read.** An OVERSIZED skull tilted back in a laugh (head is
  the dominant mass, jaw dropped FULLY open), sombrero pushed back so the whole
  grinning face shows, a small bony body below toasting with a raised cup. The
  head-back + dropped-open-jaw read is the most face-forward in the family.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad + 1px ink keylines, scary-cute).**
  Big tilted skull: wide rounded cranium, deep crescent-crinkled eye sockets
  (laugh-lines as ink ticks at the corners), a dropped hinged JAW swung fully
  open showing tooth rows + a curled tongue-tick — make the OPEN-JAW NEGATIVE
  SPACE the clear focal, not a generic grin; a bushy painted moustache bouncing;
  sombrero shoved BACK off the forehead (brim behind the head, not shading it);
  small exposed ribby torso, one bone hand raising a clay pulque cup (asymmetry
  + character tell), the other slapping a femur knee. Triad on cranium dome +
  jaw.
- **ANTI-REAPER GUARD (AD HARD PIN).** Risón is an oversized expressive SKULL,
  so it must be pinned OFF the cool grim batch-1 reapers (Big Reapy, Twinface,
  Clovenpate, Pyrecrown). NON-NEGOTIABLE tells: the tipped-back SOMBRERO + the
  painted MOUSTACHE + the warm AMBER/CLAY palette + the dropped-open-jaw LAUGH
  geometry. It is a warm festive Mexican CALACA, never a bare neutral reaper
  cranium.
- **Signature prop → PILLAR.** Pulque jug / botellón pole: a fat-bellied clay
  jug = repeatable shaft (glaze banding); a round cup-and-saucer / cork =
  gap-edge cap. **Mirror CONFIRMED clean** — round jug body on-axis, symmetric.
  No top-heavy cap.
- **Lead expressive FACET.** FACIAL CHARACTER / EMOTION — pure expression at
  32px; the dropped-jaw belly-laugh carries the whole read.
- **PINNED warm sub-palette (the AMBER / CLAY lane — distinct from H's ochre).**
  AMBER pulque/jug glaze `(228,168,72)` · terracotta jug-body `(196,108,64)`
  (amber + terracotta = the clay HERO lane) · warm-bone skull `(238,226,202)` ·
  deep tan-bone socket-shade `(168,140,98)` (deep sockets for expression) ·
  rust-red sombrero-band + sash `(192,62,50)` accent · turquoise jug-glaze fleck
  `(60,170,166)` TINY ONLY (no turquoise body mass — keeps clear of H's
  turquoise) · ink `(30,22,20)` · sheen `(252,242,218)`. Intent: warm clay/amber
  read carried by EXPRESSION, not a loud body hue; separates from H on KIND
  (giant laughing head vs horse+rider) AND on no-turquoise-mass.

## 5 — El Charro Jinete  [LEAD FACET: BLEND (anatomy + decoration + motion)]

- **Thesis.** A Linares-style skeletal CHARRO RIDER on a small rearing alebrije
  bone-horse: blends bold exposed anatomy (rider AND mount are bone), painted
  calaca decoration (embroidered charro suit + sugar-skull horse), AND
  performance motion (the horse rears, the rider waves a sombrero). A two-body
  parade centaur-of-bones — the grandest, "boss-iest" silhouette.
- **32px silhouette read.** A REARING bone-HORSE on hind legs (front hooves
  pawing up, arched neck + skull-muzzle) with a small charro rider skeleton
  seated high, one arm thrust up waving a small sombrero. The
  rearing-horse-plus-rider read is wholly unique — the only multi-body KIND, a
  tall dramatic top-action silhouette.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad + 1px ink keylines, scary-cute).**
  MOUNT: a chibi bone-horse, big triad-lit skull-muzzle, arched cervical spine,
  exposed ribcage barrel, slim bone legs rearing (two planted, two pawing); a
  painted sugar-skull motif on the brow + a marigold browband + a rust
  saddle-blanket (zarape) with hard woven-stripe banding. RIDER: tiny charro
  skeleton, embroidered short jacket + botonadura, bone hand up waving a small
  sombrero, the other on a rein. Two clearly SKELETAL stacked bodies; the rear +
  the raised arm carry the action.
- **Signature prop → PILLAR.** Lance / charro reata-pole: a wrapped lance/quirt
  shaft = repeatable shaft (binding banding); a furled banner-pennant + a
  horse-skull finial with a marigold = gap-edge cap. **TWO-BODY TOP-HEAVY FLAG
  (AD): the rearing horse+rider is the only stacked-mass creature — the prop
  must NOT echo that double-mass cap.** Fix: the action lives ENTIRELY in the
  creature; the PILLAR prop stays SLIM + SYMMETRIC on-axis — a single narrow
  lance shaft with a compact horse-skull finial seated tight on the shaft axis
  (NOT a second rider-and-mount). Mass kept on-axis and modest so the mirror
  reads balanced. Mirror CONFIRMED clean.
- **Lead expressive FACET.** BLEND — anatomy (both bodies bone) + decoration
  (embroidered suit + sugar-skull horse + zarape) + motion (rear + raised hat)
  read at once; the grand-boss anchor of the family.
- **PINNED warm sub-palette (the FULL house triad — richest — with rust HELD in
  the brick/leather lane).**
  BRICK/LEATHER rust zarape + jacket `(176,70,56)` (DESATURATED — held in the
  brick lane, NOT brightened toward E's rose) · ochre saddle + hat `(214,162,76)`
  · turquoise zarape-stripe + browband `(58,172,164)` (co-lead cool accent) ·
  warm bone (both bodies) `(236,224,198)` · tan-bone shade `(176,150,108)` ·
  marigold finial `(240,150,46)` · ink `(28,22,22)` · sheen `(250,240,216)`.
  Intent: the fullest warm Mariachi-lineage palette at its richest, on the most
  complex body, to read as a grander boss — but rust stays brick-brown so it
  reads as the darkest/brownest red, clear of B's orange-chile and E's pink-rose.
