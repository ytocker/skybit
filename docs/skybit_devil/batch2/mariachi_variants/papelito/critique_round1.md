VERDICT: ITERATE

# Papelito — round 1 critique (lead facet: CALACA DECORATION / punched negative-space face)

## Verdict rationale
Strong, charming, on-palette sheet — but it MISSES its single defining gag. The
brief pins the punched-out skull face (sky shows THROUGH the sockets) as "the
whole gag, must hold at 32px." In this round the sockets are NOT holes: they are
drawn-in shapes. On the white-card mockups they read as solid dark/ink circles;
on the on-sky panel the socket interiors sample CREAM `(246,238,222)`, identical
to the body — the sky behind (sampled `(95,155,212)`) does not come through.
So the lead expressive facet is currently faked, not built. Everything else is
close to ship; this one fix is mandatory and is why it's ITERATE not SHIP-READY.

## Ranking — strongest / weakest aspect
- STRONGEST: palette + material read. The paper-rainbow bunting on cream, single
  turquoise flag, pinked scalloped hem, and the diagonal top-left fold corner
  genuinely read as papel picado — the only non-bone body in the family,
  unmistakably distinct from the shipped batch-2 skeletons and the other 4
  variants. Prop→pillar mirror (bunting-flag shaft + marigold-rosette cap) is
  clean, on-axis, repeatable.
- WEAKEST: the punched face. The lead facet doesn't actually punch. Secondary:
  the 32px@4x read collapses (see below).

## KEEP (working — do not lose these)
1. Cream-body + paper-rainbow accent palette is bang-on the pin. Marigold/pink/
   masa/turquoise flags all present; turquoise correctly held to a SINGLE flag.
2. The papery FLATNESS holds — no volumetric bone anywhere. Triad reads as
   front-sheet / fold-shadow / top-edge sheen, exactly as briefed.
3. Pinked zig-zag scalloped hem + the curled top-left corner fold = instant
   "tissue paper" tell. Excellent material cue.
4. Prop→pillar: flag-string shaft + marigold-rosette cap is symmetric, on-axis,
   and tiles cleanly. No top-heavy cap. Ship this part.
5. The on-sky panel framing (showing it against an actual day-sky blue) is the
   right way to prove the gag — keep doing this, it's exactly what exposed the
   miss.

## FIX (prioritized punch list for round 2)
1. **PUNCH THE SOCKETS — #1, non-negotiable.** The eye sockets (and ideally the
   nose triangle) must be true negative space cut out of the alpha, so the sky/
   background shows through. Right now socket interior == body cream on the sky
   panel; it should == sky. Cut them from the mask, ink the rim 1px, done. This
   IS the design — without it Papelito is just a paper rectangle with a printed
   face, and loses its reason to exist next to the other 4.
2. **Make the punched holes survive 32px.** At 32px@4x the current face is mush —
   the two sockets merge into one dark blob and the mouth/teeth cluster reads as
   noise. With real cut-through holes this gets EASIER: two clean sky-colored
   sockets punched into a cream sheet is a far stronger small-scale tell than
   dark-on-cream. Size each socket to a clear ~3–4px void at 32px, with at least
   2px of cream bridge between them so they never fuse. Drop the lash-tick detail
   at small scale.
3. **Simplify the mouth.** The grape-cluster teeth + radiating ink ticks
   (currently the busiest element) turn to grit at 32px. Reduce to a single
   punched/stamped mouth shape or a short row of 3–4 holes. Let the mouth be the
   THIRD punch, reinforcing the negative-space language rather than fighting it
   with positive ink dots.
4. **Resolve the eyelash/painted-rim treatment.** The little radiating ticks
   around the sockets read as drawn paint on the large render but as fuzz at
   32px. Either drop them or convert to 1–2 hard pinked notches in the socket
   rim (a cut detail, not a painted one) so they stay on-language and on-medium.
5. **Punched perforation field.** The body's decorative dots (the row of small
   dots + the star sparkles) should also be true stamped-OUT holes, not painted
   marks, to sell "papel picado is perforated paper." Even 4–6 visible
   punch-holes in the sheet reinforce the gag the moment the silhouette moves
   against sky. Keep the count low so it doesn't noise up at 32px.
6. **Arms.** The pink/orange paper-strip chevron hands are cute and read as
   folded paper — keep, but thicken the connection to the body by ~1px so they
   don't detach into floating confetti at gameplay scale.

## Distinctness check (vs shipped batch-2 skeletons + the other 4 variants)
- PASS on KIND and palette: scalloped punched-paper rectangle on cream is unlike
  Comelona's pear, Zapateada's swept skirt, Risón's giant head, Jinete's
  horse+rider, and unlike any volumetric bone reaper. Once the sockets actually
  punch through, the flat-paper-with-sky-holes silhouette is unique in the whole
  roster — the strongest distinctness tell available, currently unspent.

## So-what
Round 1 nailed the material, palette, and pillar; it has not yet built the one
thing the brief says IS the design. Fix #1 alone moves this from "nice paper
rectangle" to "the festival decoration that woke up." Everything else is minor
small-scale legibility cleanup that the punch-through actually makes easier.
