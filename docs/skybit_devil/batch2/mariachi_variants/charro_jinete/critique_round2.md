# El Charro Jinete — round 2 critique (FINAL / AD pass 2; lead facet: BLEND)

Round 2 moved the needle hard on the things I flagged — the silhouette panel
now genuinely reads as a reared form with a hat-spike, the palette pin still
holds dead-on, and the night crop survives. But the central deliverable, the
**two-BODY (horse + rider) read at color 32px**, still does not land: the rider
has been absorbed into the mount and now reads as the horse's own chest/bib
rather than "a skeleton seated high on a rearing horse." The grand-boss KIND
that justifies this entry is still only half-earned. This is the LAST round —
fixes below are the round-3 brief.

## KEEP (locked — do not touch)
- **Raised-sombrero arm spike (fix #4): LANDED.** In the black-silhouette panel
  the up-thrust hat now breaks the outline as a clean separated lollipop against
  sky. This is the motion tell and it survives at 32px. Hold it exactly.
- **Rearing diagonal (fix #3): improved.** The silhouette reads as an upright,
  weight-back form with the neck/forelegs lifting off the baseline — no longer a
  lumpy upright quadruped. Good.
- **Palette pin: still nailed.** Rust holds at brick `(176,70,56)` — browner and
  darker than B's orange-chile and E's pink-rose. The split panel proves it.
- **Night 32px crop (fix #7): PASS.** The bone bodies still carry the read on the
  navy night sky and the brick zarape does not go muddy. Both biomes confirmed.
- **Barrel value step (fix #6): partially landed.** The barrel is a touch deeper
  than the rider bone — but see FIX 2, it isn't yet buying the separation it
  should because the wrong two masses are being separated.

## WEAKEST aspect — the rider/horse FUSION at color 32px (CRITICAL, still open)
- **The rider stopped being a second body.** In round 1 the two masses merged
  into one lump; round 2 over-corrected by welding the rider DOWN onto the
  horse's front so the red sash now reads as the HORSE's chest-blanket/bib, not
  as a charro torso seated on the back. A first-time viewer at 32px sees "one
  rearing bone-creature with a red bib," not "rider + mount." The unique KIND is
  still not unambiguous in color.
- **The ink channel went to the wrong seam.** Fix #1's 1–2px dark gap appears to
  have been spent separating the horse's NECK from its barrel, not separating the
  RIDER-pelvis from the horse-BACK. The channel that matters — a horizontal dark
  break under the rider so it sits ON something — is still missing.
- **Silhouette panel passes, color build doesn't — same diagnosis as round 1.**
  The black shape reads "reared horse + rider" because the hat-spike + leg gaps
  carry it; the moment color fills in, the same-value bone of rider and mount
  re-fuses. Internal value structure is still fighting you at the rider seam.

## Iteration directives — round-3 punch list (prioritized; the LAST pass)
1. **Put the rider clearly ON TOP, not in front.** Lift the rider mass up and
   back so its pelvis sits on the horse's spine/back line, with a hard 1–2px ink
   channel UNDER the rider (between rider-pelvis and horse-back). The red sash
   must read as the rider's torso, not the horse's chest. This is the single
   fix that earns the KIND — if only one thing changes, change this.
2. **Re-aim the value step to the RIGHT seam (revises fix #6).** The deeper
   tan-core should sit on the horse's BACK directly beneath the rider, so the
   lighter rider bone pops UP off a darker saddle zone — not a generic
   neck-vs-barrel split. ~15–20% value drop in that one band.
3. **Give the rider a legible torso block + skull dot above the saddle line.**
   At 32px the rider can afford exactly: skull dot, torso/sash block, raised-arm
   spike. Right now the skull reads as part of the horse's withers cluster — pull
   it up so a distinct rider head sits above the horse's back, clear of the
   mount's own skull.
4. **Resolve the two-skull ambiguity.** The horse skull-muzzle (pointing up-left)
   and the rider skull must not cluster into one blob on the right. Keep the
   mount's muzzle low-left on the rearing diagonal and the rider's skull high on
   the spike side, with sky or ink between them.
5. **Thin the barrel speckle.** The mid-barrel still carries scattered light
   bone flecks (old ribcage/zarape detail) that dither into noise and eat the
   contrast budget the rider seam needs. Let the barrel read as one bone mass
   with a single dark-core trough at 32px; keep the woven banding on the 160px
   only.

## Why ITERATE, not SHIP-READY or RE-ROLL
The concept, palette, prop, and silhouette skeleton are all RIGHT and most of
round 1's punch list landed — this is not a rethink, so RE-ROLL is unwarranted.
But the one non-negotiable promise of this entry, an unmistakable two-BODY
horse-and-rider at gameplay scale, still isn't legible in color. One focused
round-3 pass on the rider-on-back seam (directives 1–4) should clear it; if it
lands, this is the family's grand boss. As the final AD pass, the GD's next
sheet is its last — fold these in and ship it.
