# Skybit BOSS — MARIACHI-LINEAGE "WARM-SKELETON FAMILY" (brainstorm, 8 directions)

Lane: warm-bone / rust-red / ochre / turquoise / marigold festive energy in
the Mariachi calaca lineage, pushed HARDER into bone than Mariachi's clothed
cuteness. NOT all musicians. Each direction LEADS on a different expressive
facet of skeleton-ness so the AD has room to cull 8 → 5.

House style inherited (from `brainstorm_locked15.md` header + Mariachi anchor):
CHIBI proportions (big head, short wide weight-shifted body); FLAT saturated
fills + hard 1–2px ink keylines `(28,22,30)`-ish; form via the
**dark-core → flat-fill → top-left rim-sheen** TRIAD; silhouette POP via a 1px
alpha-grown outline; supersample → smoothscale. Scary-CUTE, never grim. Each
tall prop mirrors top↔bottom into a PILLAR (repeatable shaft + detachable
gap-edge cap), the way Big Reapy's bone-bident does.

**Anchor recap (Mariachi, do NOT re-make):** sombrero-disc + grinning skull +
short studded charro jacket + dance-kick legs + round guitarrón. Warm-bone +
rust-red + ochre, turquoise rosette. It is fairly CLOTHED and cute. Everything
below shows MORE BONE.

**Hard collision avoids.** Must not collide with shipped batch-2 skeletons —
Mariachi (musician calaca) / Catrina (couture flower-hat dame, wide-brim plumed
HAT + gown hourglass) / Necrarch (floating crowned lich, robe-wisp + soul-orb +
violet) / Draugr (ice brute warrior, helm + round shield + frost-cyan) / Jiangshi
(rigid arms-out hopper, jade-corpse + talisman) — nor batch-1 skull reapers
(Big Reapy, Brimstone, Soulforge, Twinface, Pyrecrown, Clovenpate). Avoid owned
non-warm hues: violet (Necrarch), ice-cyan (Draugr), the three green-bands +
jade-corpse, storm-grey (Raijin), mint/blue-cyan glows (Kitsune/Yurei).

**Facet coverage target across the 8:** (1) EXPOSED ANATOMY ×2, (2) CALACA
DECORATION ×2, (3) DANCE/PERFORMANCE MOTION ×2, (4) FACIAL CHARACTER/EMOTION ×1,
(5) deliberate BLEND ×1. Eight bottom-up bases, not one re-dressed.

## (1) EXPOSED ANATOMY

### A — Costillas, the bare-ribcage luchador  [FACET: EXPOSED ANATOMY]
- **Thesis.** A Día-de-Muertos LUCHADOR stripped to the bone: a barrel ribcage
  and bony fists thrown up in a flexed victory pose, head sealed in a laced
  wrestling mask. Anatomy as muscle — the skeleton IS the physique.
- **32px silhouette read.** A bottom-light, TOP-HEAVY barrel: a huge round
  laced-mask HEAD over an exposed wedge RIBCAGE torso, two stubby arms crooked
  up in a double-bicep flex, thin pelvis-and-bone legs in a wide planted stance.
  The flexed double-fist-up shape is shared by nothing in either batch.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad).** Oversized round head fully wrapped
  in a laced lucha mask (eye/mouth holes show skull-grin + socket beneath);
  exposed barrel RIBCAGE rendered as 4–5 hard triad-lit rib bands curving off a
  visible spine-keel; bony ball-joint shoulders; forearms as paired
  radius/ulna bones with knuckle-fist; slim pelvis ring; femur/tibia legs planted
  wide. Dark-core in the rib troughs, flat bone fill on the rib faces, sheen on
  the top-left rib lip — ribcage curvature done in flat banding, never gradient.
- **Signature prop → pillar.** Title CHAMPIONSHIP-BELT pole: studded leather
  strap = repeatable shaft (rivet banding); big oval championship medallion
  embossed with a skull = gap-edge cap. Round medallion sits on-axis — clean
  symmetric mirror, no top-heavy risk on the prop (the creature is the top-heavy
  one, not the pillar).
- **Sub-palette (warm, must read distinct @32px).** warm-bone ribcage
  `(238,224,196)` · tan-bone shade `(176,150,108)` · CRIMSON laced mask
  `(196,46,52)` base (deep, blood-bright — hero mass) · mask-lacing cream
  `(240,228,200)` · gold belt + medallion `(224,178,72)` · turquoise mask-trim
  flash `(60,170,170)` · ink `(28,20,22)` · sheen `(252,242,220)`. Dominant
  family: bone + CRIMSON mask + gold — the saturated red-mask-on-bone read.
- **Scary-CUTE hook.** A jacked tiny skeleton throwing a flex so hard its ribs
  pop — pure swagger, zero menace. Goofy-tough.

### B — Calaca Comelona, the gluttonous vendor skeleton  [FACET: EXPOSED ANATOMY]
- **Thesis.** "La Calaca Comelona" (the eating skeleton) reframed as a
  street-food market VENDOR whose belly is a literal open ribcage cupboard —
  pan dulce and chiles stuffed between the bones. Anatomy played for comedy: you
  can SEE the food inside it.
- **32px silhouette read.** A squat BOTTOM-HEAVY pear: small skull under a flat
  market-vendor straw hat, a wide-open exposed RIBCAGE belly framing a cluster
  of round food blobs, short bone legs, one bony hand offering up a fat tamale.
  The "open-belly-with-stuff-inside" read is one-of-a-kind.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad).** Small round skull, big toothy
  grin, a chile-pepper clenched like a cigar in the jaw; exposed barrel ribcage
  spread OPEN like cupboard doors, the spine visible up the back; inside the cage
  3–4 hard food lobes (pan dulce, a chile, a round tamale); thin pelvis + bowed
  femur legs; one full bone-arm/hand thrust forward offering food. Triad on every
  rib AND every food lobe so the inside-cluster reads as a busy little pantry.
- **Signature prop → pillar.** Market-STALL pole / hanging-ristra: a wooden
  vendor pole strung with a ristra of dried chiles = repeatable shaft (chile
  banding); a round comal griddle with a stacked-tamale dome = gap-edge cap.
  Round comal on-axis — clean mirror.
- **Sub-palette.** warm-bone `(236,222,194)` · tan-bone shade `(174,148,106)` ·
  CHILE-red food + hatband `(208,72,48)` accent · masa-gold tamale/pan
  `(228,186,96)` · pumpkin-ochre straw hat `(214,156,72)` · cilantro-green chile
  garnish small `(120,170,80)` · ink `(30,22,20)` · sheen `(250,240,218)`.
  Dominant family: bone + chile-red + masa-gold — the warmest, food-saturated
  read; reads "kitchen" not "warrior."
- **Scary-CUTE hook.** A skeleton so hungry it stores snacks in its own ribcage,
  beaming as it hands you a tamale. Adorable gluttony.

## (2) CALACA DECORATION

### C — Florita, the marigold-crowned sugar-skull bride  [FACET: CALACA DECORATION]
- **Thesis.** A Día-de-Muertos BRIDE calaca: a sugar-skull face painted in full
  filigree under a heaped MARIGOLD flower-crown, trailing a lace veil. Decoration
  as identity — every bone is painted, petaled, or laced. (Differs from couture
  Catrina: NO wide plumed platter-hat, NO gown hourglass — this is a low ROUND
  marigold wreath + a long straight veil-column, and a visible painted skull
  body, not a couture dame.)
- **32px silhouette read.** A round marigold WREATH ring hugging the crown of the
  skull (a halo, not a brim), with a long straight VEIL falling dead-vertical
  behind, over a slim bouquet-clutching body. Wreath-halo + vertical-veil read,
  unlike Catrina's wide horizontal brim.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad).** Sugar-skull face heavily PAINTED:
  petal-rimmed eye sockets, a dotted-filigree brow, a stitched-vine smile, a
  flower on the nasal aperture; a tight wreath of 6–8 marigold blooms ringing the
  skull-crown (each bloom a hard triad-lit rosette); a long lace veil as flat
  scalloped triad panels falling straight down; slim bone torso in a simple
  bodice; bone hands cupping a round bouquet. Decoration carried in FLAT painted
  shapes on the bone, not engraved relief.
- **Signature prop → pillar.** Bouquet / floral-CANDELABRA stem: a wrapped
  flower-stalk stem = repeatable shaft (ribbon/leaf banding); a round
  marigold-and-baby's-breath bouquet-ball = gap-edge cap. Round bouquet on-axis —
  clean mirror.
- **Sub-palette.** ash-bone painted-face `(240,230,214)` · warm-grey shade
  `(186,172,158)` · MARIGOLD-orange blooms `(244,148,40)` base (the hero mass) ·
  hot-pink filigree + smile `(226,84,128)` accent · rust-red rose focal
  `(186,58,52)` · sage-green leaf small `(122,158,96)` · veil-cream `(248,240,228)`
  · ink `(28,22,26)`. Dominant family: bone + MARIGOLD + hot-pink filigree — the
  flower-saturated, painted-decoration read (warm, NOT couture-teal like Catrina).
- **Scary-CUTE hook.** A blushing dead bride so covered in flowers she's more
  bouquet than corpse — sweet, never grim.

### D — Papelito, the papel-picado banner-spirit  [FACET: CALACA DECORATION]
- **Thesis.** A calaca built from PAPEL PICADO — perforated tissue-paper banner
  bunting given a body. Its torso and limbs are cut-paper sheets with stamped-out
  skull holes; it flutters like festival decoration come alive. Decoration as the
  literal material of the creature.
- **32px silhouette read.** A SCALLOPED rectangular paper-sheet "body" (pinked
  zig-zag bottom edge) strung from a string of little bunting flags, with a
  cut-out skull face punched into the top of the sheet and tiny paper hands. The
  rectangular fluttering-banner-with-punched-face read is unique — flat and
  papery where every other take is volumetric bone.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad).** The "head" is a skull SHAPE punched
  as negative space out of the top of a paper sheet (the sky shows through the
  sockets); the torso is one or two layered papel-picado sheets with stamped
  star/skull/flower perforations; a pinked scalloped hem; thin paper-strip arms;
  a string of triangular bunting flags arcing over the top like a banner-line.
  Triad reads as front-sheet / back-sheet shadow / top-edge sheen — flat layered
  paper, deliberately the LEAST bony body (it's decoration that gained a ghost).
- **Signature prop → pillar.** Bunting-STRING / flagpole: a banner cord strung
  with stacked papel-picado flags = repeatable shaft (each flag a band); a larger
  cut-paper marigold-rosette medallion = gap-edge cap. On-axis flags — clean
  vertical mirror.
- **Sub-palette.** Each papel flag a different festival hue but TIED warm:
  marigold-orange `(244,150,44)` · papel-pink `(232,96,150)` · turquoise
  `(56,168,168)` · masa-gold `(226,182,80)` · paper-cream sheet base
  `(246,238,222)` · rust-red bunting cord `(190,60,50)` · ink `(28,22,26)` ·
  sheen `(252,246,232)`. Dominant family: multi-hue festival paper on cream —
  the rainbow-bunting read (warm-led, turquoise as the single cool note, NO
  violet/ice). Reads as PAPER, the only non-bone-dominant body.
- **Scary-CUTE hook.** A length of party bunting that woke up, fluttering and
  peeking through its own punched-out eye-holes. Pure festive whimsy.

## (3) DANCE / PERFORMANCE MOTION

### E — Zapateada, the folklórico ballet skeleton  [FACET: DANCE/PERFORMANCE MOTION]
- **Thesis.** A BALLET FOLKLÓRICO dancer calaca caught mid-twirl: one bony arm
  arced overhead, a huge ruffled skirt fanned out by the spin, bone legs in a
  pointed zapateado stomp. Motion is the whole design — frozen at peak swirl.
- **32px silhouette read.** A dynamic DIAGONAL: a wide fanned bell-SKIRT swept to
  one side by the spin (asymmetric, motion-blurred fan), bone torso leaning into
  the turn, one arm raised in an arc, the other flaring the skirt hem. The
  off-axis swirling-skirt read is unmistakable — the only asymmetric, in-motion
  silhouette in the family.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad).** Small skull, painted lash-flicks
  and a rose tucked at the temple, joyful open grin; slim ribcage-and-spine torso
  partly bare above the skirt waist; arms as bone with ribbon trailing; a vast
  layered RUFFLED skirt swept into a comma-curve, built from 3 hard triad-lit
  ruffle tiers (motion implied by the lean of the tiers); pointed bone feet, one
  toe-down mid-stomp. Skirt ruffles carry the energy — flat fanned lobes, lean
  all one way.
- **Signature prop → pillar.** Ribbon-DANCE pole / listón maypole: a fluted dance
  pole = repeatable shaft (wound-ribbon banding); a spiral rosette of fanned
  skirt-ruffle + a castanet pair = gap-edge cap. Round rosette on-axis — clean
  mirror (the creature is asymmetric; the pillar stays symmetric).
- **Sub-palette.** warm-bone `(236,224,200)` · tan-bone shade `(178,154,112)` ·
  ROSE-red ruffle tiers `(210,64,72)` base (the swept hero mass) · marigold-gold
  ruffle-trim `(232,184,80)` · turquoise ribbon `(58,172,166)` accent · coral
  temple-rose `(238,116,92)` · ink `(28,22,24)` · sheen `(250,242,222)`. Dominant
  family: bone + ROSE-red ruffles + turquoise ribbon — a brighter, pinker red
  than Costillas' crimson mask, and the motion read keeps them apart.
- **Scary-CUTE hook.** A skeleton so lost in the dance her skirt is still
  catching up to her spin — pure festive joy, bones be damned.

### F — Tamborero, the parade dancing-drummer skeleton  [FACET: DANCE/PERFORMANCE MOTION]
- **Thesis.** A street-PARADE percussionist calaca high-stepping in a comparsa: a
  big shoulder-slung barrel DRUM, two mallets mid-strike, knees pumping in a
  marching strut. Motion via the marching-strut + drumstrike — a percussion
  performer, NOT a strummer (clearly off Mariachi's seated guitarrón).
- **32px silhouette read.** A wide barrel DRUM slung across the chest dominating
  the torso, two stick-mallets crossed mid-strike above it, one bone knee kicked
  high in a march, plumed cap bobbing. The slung-drum + high-knee-march read is
  distinct from Mariachi's hugged round guitar and from Raijin's overhead
  drum-ring halo.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad).** Round skull under a small plumed
  parade cap; exposed clavicle + upper ribs above a barrel drum slung on a strap
  across the body (drum head = a taut triad-lit disc with a painted skull-sun);
  two bone arms with mallets caught mid-strike (motion lines); slim spine; one
  femur kicked high mid-march, the other planted. Drum is the focal disc; bone
  limbs splay with energy.
- **Signature prop → pillar.** Parade DRUM-MAJOR pole / mace: a beribboned baton
  shaft = repeatable shaft (ring banding); a barrel drum laid head-on with
  crossed mallets = gap-edge cap. Front-on round drumhead on-axis (≈shaft-width,
  modest, mass dropped toward gap) — clean mirror, explicitly kept off Raijin's
  top-heavy risk.
- **Sub-palette.** warm-bone `(236,224,198)` · tan-bone shade `(176,150,110)` ·
  burnt-ORANGE drum-shell `(224,124,52)` base · rust-red drum-rim + strap
  `(190,58,48)` accent · cream drumhead `(244,232,208)` w/ ochre painted sun
  `(220,168,72)` · turquoise mallet-ribbon `(60,172,164)` · ink `(28,22,22)` ·
  sheen `(250,240,216)`. Dominant family: bone + burnt-ORANGE drum + rust-rim —
  an orange-led warm read (vs Costillas crimson, Florita rose, Zapateada rose) to
  hold its own value lane.
- **Scary-CUTE hook.** A skeleton marching so hard it's about to drum itself
  apart, grinning at the parade crowd. Joyous racket.

## (4) FACIAL CHARACTER / EMOTION

### G — Risón, the laughing-jaw cantina skeleton  [FACET: FACIAL CHARACTER/EMOTION]
- **Thesis.** A cantina merrymaker calaca whose whole personality lives in the
  SKULL: a head thrown back in a huge belly-laugh, jaw cracked wide, sockets
  crinkled with mirth, a sombrero tipped back off the brow. Emotion-first — the
  face does all the work; body is minimal and bony.
- **32px silhouette read.** An OVERSIZED skull tilted back in a laugh (the head
  is the dominant mass, jaw dropped wide open), sombrero pushed back so the full
  grinning face shows, a small bony body below toasting with a raised cup. The
  head-back-laughing + dropped-jaw read is the most face-forward in the family —
  reads as pure expression even at 32px.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad).** Big tilted skull: wide rounded
  cranium, deep crescent-crinkled eye sockets (laugh-lines as ink ticks at the
  corners), a dropped hinged JAW swung fully open showing tooth rows + a curled
  tongue-tick, a bushy painted moustache bouncing; sombrero shoved back off the
  forehead (brim behind the head, not shading it); small exposed ribby torso, one
  bone hand raising a clay pulque cup, the other slapping a femur knee. Triad on
  the cranium dome + jaw; the open-jaw negative space is the focal.
- **Signature prop → pillar.** Pulque JUG / botellón pole: a fat-bellied clay jug
  = repeatable shaft (glaze banding); a round cup-and-saucer / bottle-cork =
  gap-edge cap. Round jug body on-axis — clean mirror.
- **Sub-palette.** warm-bone skull `(238,226,202)` · deep tan-bone socket-shade
  `(168,140,98)` (deep sockets for expression) · AMBER pulque/jug glaze
  `(228,168,72)` base · rust-red sombrero-band + sash `(192,62,50)` accent ·
  terracotta jug-body `(196,108,64)` · turquoise jug-glaze fleck `(60,170,166)` ·
  ink `(30,22,20)` · sheen `(252,242,218)`. Dominant family: bone + AMBER +
  terracotta — the warm clay/amber read, distinct from the redder/ochre takes;
  carried by the EXPRESSION, not a loud body hue.
- **Scary-CUTE hook.** A skeleton laughing so hard at his own joke his jaw nearly
  falls off — the most lovable, least menacing boss in the set.

## (5) DELIBERATE BLEND

### H — El Charro Jinete, the skeletal alebrije-horse rider  [FACET: BLEND of anatomy + decoration + motion]
- **Thesis.** A Linares-style skeletal CHARRO RIDER on a small rearing alebrije
  bone-horse: blends bold exposed anatomy (both rider and mount are bone),
  painted calaca decoration (embroidered charro suit + sugar-skull horse), AND
  performance motion (the horse rears, the rider waves a sombrero). A
  two-body parade centaur-of-bones — the most complex, "boss-iest" silhouette.
- **32px silhouette read.** A REARING bone-HORSE on hind legs (front hooves
  pawing up, arched neck + skull-muzzle) with a small charro rider skeleton
  seated high, one arm thrust up waving a sombrero. The rearing-horse-plus-rider
  read is wholly unique — a tall, dramatic, top-action silhouette no single-figure
  take has.
- **Construction / anatomy (chibi + triad).** MOUNT: a chibi bone-horse, big
  triad-lit skull-muzzle, arched cervical spine, exposed ribcage barrel, slim
  bone legs rearing (two planted, two pawing); a painted sugar-skull motif on the
  horse's brow + a marigold browband + a rust saddle-blanket (zarape) with hard
  woven-stripe banding. RIDER: tiny charro skeleton, embroidered short jacket +
  botonadura, bone hand up waving a small sombrero, the other on a rein. Two
  stacked bodies, both clearly skeletal; motion in the rear + the raised arm.
- **Signature prop → pillar.** LANCE / charro reata-pole: a wrapped lance/quirt
  shaft = repeatable shaft (binding banding); a furled banner-pennant + a
  horse-skull finial with a marigold = gap-edge cap. The mount is asymmetric/
  top-action; the PILLAR prop stays slim and symmetric on-axis — clean mirror,
  the action lives in the creature not the pillar.
- **Sub-palette.** warm bone (both bodies) `(236,224,198)` · tan-bone shade
  `(176,150,108)` · RUST-red zarape/jacket `(186,60,48)` base · ochre saddle +
  hat `(214,162,76)` · turquoise zarape-stripe + browband `(58,172,164)` accent ·
  marigold finial `(240,150,46)` · ink `(28,22,22)` · sheen `(250,240,216)`.
  Dominant family: bone + rust-red + ochre + turquoise zarape — the FULLEST warm
  Mariachi-lineage palette (deliberately the "house" triad at its richest, on the
  most complex body, to read as a grander boss).
- **Scary-CUTE hook.** A pint-sized dead charro on a galloping bone-pony, hat in
  the air mid-yeehaw — heroic and ridiculous at once.

## DISTINCTNESS / COLLISION CROSS-CHECK

- **Eight bottom-up bases, no re-dress.** Anatomy-led barrel-flex (A) vs
  open-belly pantry (B); decoration-led flower-bride wreath+veil (C) vs
  woke-up-bunting flat-paper (D); motion-led swirling-skirt diagonal (E) vs
  marching-drummer high-knee (F); face-led head-back belly-laugh (G);
  blend-led rearing-horse-plus-rider two-body (H). Each is a different idea of
  WHAT the skeleton is.
- **Off the shipped 5.** No wide plumed platter-hat or gown hourglass (Catrina);
  no robe-wisp/soul-orb/float (Necrarch); no helm+round-shield brute (Draugr); no
  rigid arms-out hop/talisman (Jiangshi); none is a seated guitarrón strummer
  (Mariachi). Florita's flowers sit in a low ROUND wreath + vertical veil (not
  Catrina's horizontal brim); Tamborero is a marching drummer (not Mariachi's
  seated strummer, not Raijin's overhead drum-halo).
- **Off batch-1 reapers.** No hooded scythe-reaper, no flaming/forge skulls, no
  two-faced or split-crown skull — all are festive Mexican calaca archetypes.
- **Warm-palette separation @32px (so they don't blur):**
  - A Costillas — bone + **CRIMSON** mask `(196,46,52)` + gold (red-mask hero).
  - B Comelona — bone + **CHILE-red** `(208,72,48)` + **masa-gold** (food/kitchen).
  - C Florita — bone + **MARIGOLD** `(244,148,40)` + **hot-pink** filigree (flower).
  - D Papelito — **multi-hue paper** on cream (rainbow bunting, the paper body).
  - E Zapateada — bone + **ROSE-red ruffles** `(210,64,72)` + turquoise ribbon.
  - F Tamborero — bone + **burnt-ORANGE drum** `(224,124,52)` + rust-rim.
  - G Risón — bone + **AMBER/terracotta** `(228,168,72)`/`(196,108,64)` (clay).
  - H Jinete — bone + **rust + ochre + turquoise** (the full house triad, richest).
  Reds split into crimson / chile / rose; oranges split into marigold / burnt /
  amber-terracotta; D is paper-rainbow; H is the full-house richest — eight
  distinct value+hue lanes, plus each has a distinct silhouette KIND so they hold
  apart even before color reads.
- **No owned cool hues.** Turquoise used only as the small accent the lineage
  already owns (Mariachi rosette); zero violet, ice-cyan, green-band, storm-grey,
  or mint/cyan glow.
- **Accessibility tells (shape, not hue):** A double-flex barrel; B open-belly
  pantry cluster; C wreath-halo + vertical veil; D scalloped punched-paper
  rectangle; E off-axis swirling skirt; F slung-drum + high-knee march; G
  head-back dropped-open jaw; H rearing horse + rider. All read at 32px without
  color.
- **All 8 props mirror clean.** Symmetric on-axis caps (A medallion belt, B comal,
  C bouquet, D paper-rosette, E ribbon-rosette, F front-on drum kept modest,
  G jug, H lance-finial). H's action lives in the creature; its prop stays slim/
  symmetric — no top-heavy risk on any pillar.
