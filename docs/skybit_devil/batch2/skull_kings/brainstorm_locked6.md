# KING SKULL — LOCKED 6 (per-concept briefs)

A capstone royal court of **six** skeleton KINGS — slick, royal, high-end, excellent gaming experience.
(The user chose to take BOTH the AD's recommended 5th and the blessed alternate, so the court is 6.) The set
pays **respect to Citipati** (house grammar; thin-accent discipline; purple wisdom-pin lineage) and includes
one king **evolved from Koschei**. Each king has its OWN iconic royal crown (the 5-skull corona is Citipati's
alone). Multi-arm + tiny skulls are a TOOLKIT used where they serve (not forced on all). Several distinct
royal palette directions — no two kings share one.

**House grammar (clone helpers from `jiangshi_epic/citipati/render_citipati.py` + `citipati_versions/
koschei/render_koschei.py`):** chibi scary-CUTE, flat triad (`triad_blob`/`triad_circle`: dark-core→fill→
top-left sheen) + 1–2px ink keyline `(28,22,30)` + 1px grown outline; `SS=6`→smoothscale; `bone_limb`,
`crown_skull`, `flame_halo`, `draw_pillar` available. Each sheet: hero + top↔bottom-mirrored pillar (grounded
in the king's own forms) + true-32px DAY & NIGHT chips + blacked-out silhouette proof + palette strip.

**GATE every round:** reads as a ROYAL skull-KING (regal + slick + high-end); its iconic crown + ONE 32px
tell land day+night; ONE dominant bone mass with thin accents (no unsanctioned second saturated mass); the
chosen focal stays the single brightest pixel; clean mirrored pillar; Skybit chibi-cute grammar (no realism
drift); distinct from the other kings + the skeleton-dense roster (Citipati dancer, Koschei seated, Mukha
radial-fan, the citipati_versions five, Catrina/Mariachi/reapers).

**Budget THIS round = push to SHIP-READY.** Each per-concept loop iterates GD→AD→GD→… until the AD returns
`VERDICT: SHIP-READY`. Safety cap ≈5 designer rounds; if one stalls, surface it rather than ship it.

### 1. regent_koschei — gilded throne-emperor  (the mandatory Koschei evolution)
- **Archetype / KIND:** seated throne-emperor; low wide pyramid sunk into a throne (only seated mass).
- **Crown:** gilded blackened-iron SPIKE comb + gold ferrule caps + a single chartreuse jewel at the centre tip.
- **Palette:** gilded corpse-tallow grey-green bone `(190,192,158)` + thin warm gold + chartreuse soul-egg
  focal `(196,232,72)` + indigo socket pins `(110,118,212)`.
- **Toolkit:** two-armed (cups the egg); tiny GOLD skull-chain along the throne lip = lineage tell.
- **32px tell:** low dark-iron-and-gold caged mass with one glowing chartreuse lap-jewel.
- **Lock:** keep Koschei's KIND (seated, iron-spike comb, cupped chartreuse focal, indigo pins). Royal enters
  ONLY as gilt/jewel-setting/skull-chain. GOLD = edge/ferrule/chain ONLY — never a second saturated mass; the
  chartreuse egg stays the single warm focal. Pillar = the femur-throne column, now with gilt riveted cuffs.

### 2. amethyst_god_king — four-armed radial deity
- **Archetype / KIND:** four-armed radial deity, wide symmetric fan (the only radial silhouette).
- **Crown:** tall 5-spire amethyst jeweled CONE (kirita-mukuta); purple third-eye (ties Citipati's hue).
- **Palette:** warm-ivory bone `(246,236,210)` + a LARGE amethyst/purple robe + thin gold edging.
- **Toolkit:** four arms (its whole point); tiny skulls only as armlet beads — arm-ends are HANDS/regalia,
  NEVER skull-tipped (that separates it from Mukha-Devi's radial skulls).
- **32px tell:** wide four-arm radial fan under a purple cone-crown, purple third-eye dot.
- **Lock:** SANCTIONED exception — amethyst may be a LARGE robe mass, BUT ivory head+arms must out-mass the
  purple at 32px ("ivory deity in a purple robe," not "purple blob"). Audit vs Mukha-Devi when maturing.

### 3. carnelian_warlord — armoured colossus
- **Archetype / KIND:** towering armoured colossus; top-heavy broad wedge + a vertical planted sword (the
  only heavy/broad-shouldered mass).
- **Crown:** heavy forward-spiked iron WAR-CROWN + a blood-carnelian brow gem.
- **Palette:** bronze-dirty bone `~(206,176,128)` + blood-carnelian thin accent + dark-iron pauldrons.
- **Toolkit:** two-armed; iron skull-belt + pauldron skull-faces = tell.
- **32px tell:** broad armoured wedge, forward-spiked crown, vertical planted sword down the centre.
- **Lock:** push the bronze bone clearly WARMER/dirtier than ivory (or it drifts to "ivory king with red
  trim"); confine iron to pauldrons + crown (NOT a torso plate) so bronze stays the dominant mass.

### 4. obsidian_sovereign — dark imperial monarch  (the value pole)
- **Archetype / KIND:** tall slender dark monarch; smooth columnar robe to the floor (only tall-narrow + only
  near-black).
- **Crown:** closed IMPERIAL DOME — crossed arches over a circlet + a tiny orb-and-cross finial (only smooth/
  round crown).
- **Palette:** onyx near-black bone `~(44,42,52)` + cool blue-grey rim-sheen + GOLD as the only bright accent.
- **Toolkit:** two-armed; tiny skull as the sceptre knob.
- **32px tell:** black columnar king, gold domed-arch crown, single vertical gold hem line.
- **Lock:** near-black bone vanishes on night sky — the cool blue-grey rim-sheen MUST be wide+bright enough to
  carry the whole silhouette (reuse Koschei's indigo-rim-on-iron night lesson). Keep the robe column SMOOTH/
  unbroken (no ribs that pull it toward "another skeleton-man").

### 5. verdigris_drowned_king — drowned undersea monarch
- **Archetype / KIND:** slumped drowned monarch; soft trailing organic asymmetric outline (the only organic/
  soft shape).
- **Crown:** branching CORAL-antler crown + a baroque PEARL finial.
- **Palette:** sea-bleached grey-green bone `~(168,184,166)` + verdigris-copper teal + pearl-cream (the only
  teal in the set).
- **Toolkit:** two-armed; no tiny skulls (pearls + barnacles are the texture tell).
- **32px tell:** slumped teal-green king, branching coral antlers, 2–3 trailing weed tendrils.
- **Lock:** fronds = 2–3 FAT separated tendrils with sky between (not a fringe); coral antlers = 3–4 FAT forks
  + the baroque-pearl as the high-contrast crown-tell (organic detail muds at 32px). Push the sea-bone
  LIGHTER/greener than Koschei's tallow (closest two bone hues — keep values apart).

### 6. rosegold_prince — the boy-prince  (the cute pole)
- **Archetype / KIND:** short round chibi heir; compact "egg-on-legs" (the only small/round silhouette — the
  CUTE pole of scary-cute).
- **Crown:** a dainty OPEN trefoil coronet (delicate, low) + a single pink tourmaline.
- **Palette:** palest bone `~(250,244,236)` + rose-gold + soft pink.
- **Toolkit:** two-armed (maybe clutching a tiny orb-sceptre); a single tiny skull on the coronet or as a toy.
- **32px tell:** small round king, trefoil coronet, pink gem.
- **Lock (police hard):** the AD's worry — a tiny round king risks reading as a COIN/power-up pickup at 32px.
  Make the CROWN + a clear royal silhouette read unmistakably as a tiny KING: give the coronet enough height/
  contrast and a regal posture (orb-sceptre, cape-flare or collar) so it never reads as a generic round blob.
  Keep it the gentlest/most charming of the six, but unmistakably royal. Stay clear of KFC red (rose-gold/pink
  only). Distinct from the others by being the sole small/round + sole pink king.
