# King Skull — a royal court of six skeleton-kings

![King Skull showcase](./showcase.png)

A capstone royal theme: **six** slick, high-end skeleton MONARCHS. The court pays **respect to the original
Citipati** (the dancing charnel skeleton-lord whose procedural house-grammar the whole batch2 lineage is
built on — warm-ivory bone, thin-accent discipline, the purple wisdom third-eye, the shared
`triad`/`crown_skull`/`flame_halo`/`draw_pillar` toolkit), and one king is an explicit royal **evolution of
Koschei**. Multi-arm + tiny skulls are used as a toolkit where each king benefits (not forced on all). Each
king owns its OWN iconic royal crown and a distinct royal palette direction — no two share one.

**All six are SHIP-READY** (the brief's emphasis was to push every one to a genuine art-director sign-off,
not stop at the standard budget). The two lineage tiles (Citipati + Koschei) are shown set apart at the head
of the showcase.

The six:

- **Regent Koschei** — the gilded throne-emperor; the **Koschei evolution**. Seated iron-spike-crown king
  with a gold crown-jewel + lap soul-egg (twin chartreuse points), gold tracing + a tiny skull-chain. — [./regent_koschei](./regent_koschei)
- **Amethyst God-King** — a four-armed radial deity; pale ivory body + a hollowed amethyst jewel-cone crown,
  purple third-eye; the only multi-arm king. — [./amethyst_god_king](./amethyst_god_king)
- **Carnelian Warlord** — an armoured colossus; broad bronze wedge with a vertical planted greatsword, iron
  war-crown + a single blood-carnelian brow gem. — [./carnelian_warlord](./carnelian_warlord)
- **Obsidian Sovereign** — the dark value-pole; a tall near-black robe-column edged in a cool moonlit rim,
  closed gold imperial-dome crown + orb-and-cross finial. — [./obsidian_sovereign](./obsidian_sovereign)
- **Verdigris Drowned-King** — the drowned undersea monarch; a soft organic slump, branching coral-antler
  crown + a near-white baroque pearl, sea-bleached bone + verdigris-teal (the only teal). — [./verdigris_drowned_king](./verdigris_drowned_king)
- **Rose-Gold Prince** — the cute pole; a small round boy-heir with a tall fleur coronet + a hot pink
  tourmaline, palest bone + rose-gold. — [./rosegold_prince](./rosegold_prince)
