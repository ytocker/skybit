# KING SKULL — AD brainstorm cull (8 → 5)

VERDICT: ITERATE → locks to the 5 below (one final GD pass to bake in the per-king lock notes). The
brainstorm is a genuine court (8 distinct theses / silhouettes / crowns / palettes), not 8 recolors. The
cull maximizes silhouette + palette spread, removes one real tall-thin collision, and thins the pale cluster.

## The SET read
Disciplined, bottom-up, Citipati/Koschei grammar respected throughout. Two problems force the specific cuts:
1. **Pale cluster (real, over-built).** #3 Marble Pontiff, #7 Rose-Gold Boy-Prince, #8 Frost Lich-Emperor all
   lead with a near-white bone mass — at 32px the bone mass carries before the accent hue resolves, so three
   pale kings cluster as "the white guys." Keep at most two pale, maximally separated.
2. **Tall-thin collision.** #2 Obsidian Sovereign (columnar) and #3 Marble Pontiff (candle-staff) share the
   tall-narrow vertical blackout shape — "robe vs staff" is a prop diff, not a silhouette KIND. Obsidian stays
   (only near-black king = essential value pole); Pontiff is the cleanest cut (kills the collision AND one
   pale king in one move).

## The 5 to PURSUE (ranked) — with lock notes
1. **REGENT KOSCHEI** *(force-keep; the mandatory Koschei evolution)* — seated throne-emperor, low wide
   pyramid (only seated mass). Crown: gilded blackened-iron SPIKE comb + gold ferrule caps + 1 chartreuse
   jewel-tip. Palette: gilded grey-green tallow + thin gold + chartreuse soul-egg focal + indigo socket pins.
   Two-armed; gold skull-chain along the throne lip = lineage tell. **Lock:** keep Koschei's KIND (seated,
   iron-spike comb, cupped chartreuse focal, indigo pins); royal enters ONLY as gilt/jewel-setting/skull-
   chain. Gold = edge/ferrule/chain ONLY — never a second saturated mass; the chartreuse egg is the single
   warm focal.
2. **AMETHYST GOD-KING** — four-armed radial deity, wide symmetric fan (only radial silhouette). Crown: tall
   5-spire amethyst jeweled CONE (kirita-mukuta), purple third-eye (ties Citipati's hue). Palette: warm-ivory
   bone + large amethyst robe + thin gold edge. Four arms; tiny skulls only as armlet beads. **Lock:** the
   radial silhouette is the set's most valuable shape — keep it. SANCTIONED exception: amethyst may be a
   LARGE robe mass, BUT ivory head+arms must out-mass the purple at 32px ("ivory deity in a purple robe,"
   not "purple blob"). Separate from Mukha-Devi: his radial elements are ARMS/hands/regalia, never skull-
   tipped (hers are skulls).
3. **CARNELIAN WARLORD** — towering armoured colossus, top-heavy broad wedge + vertical planted sword (only
   heavy/broad mass). Crown: heavy forward-spiked iron WAR-CROWN + blood-carnelian brow gem. Palette: bronze-
   dirty bone + carnelian thin accent + dark-iron pauldrons. Two-armed; iron skull-belt + pauldron faces =
   tell. **Lock:** push the bronze bone `~(206,176,128)` clearly WARMER/dirtier than ivory (or it drifts to
   "ivory king with red trim"); confine iron to pauldrons+crown (not a torso plate) so bronze stays dominant.
4. **OBSIDIAN SOVEREIGN** *(force-keep on value spread)* — tall slender dark monarch, smooth columnar robe
   (only tall-narrow + only near-black; value pole). Crown: closed IMPERIAL DOME (crossed arches + orb-cross
   finial) — only smooth/round crown. Palette: onyx near-black bone + cool blue-grey rim-sheen + gold as the
   only bright accent. Two-armed; tiny skull as the sceptre knob. **Lock:** near-black bone vanishes on night
   sky — the cool blue-grey rim-sheen must be wide+bright enough to carry the whole silhouette (reuse
   Koschei's indigo-rim-on-iron night lesson). Keep the robe column smooth/unbroken (no ribs that pull it
   toward "another skeleton-man").
5. **VERDIGRIS DROWNED-KING** *(the wildcard / fifth slot)* — slumped drowned monarch, soft trailing organic
   asymmetric outline (only organic/soft shape). Crown: branching CORAL-antler crown + baroque pearl finial.
   Palette: sea-bleached grey-green bone + verdigris-copper teal + pearl-cream (only teal in the set). Two-
   armed; no tiny skulls (pearls + barnacles are the texture). **Lock:** keep fronds to 2–3 FAT separated
   tendrils with sky between (not a fringe); coral antlers = 3–4 FAT forks + the baroque-pearl as the high-
   contrast crown-tell (organic detail muds at 32px — the Citipati flame-ring lesson). Push its sea-bone
   lighter/greener than Koschei's tallow (closest two bone hues — see audit).

## The 3 to CUT
- **✂ #3 MARBLE PONTIFF (strongest cut).** Tall-thin silhouette twins Obsidian (prop diff, not KIND) AND one
  of three pale kings; its sapphire ≈ Frost's cyan (most redundant accent pair). Cutting it fixes the
  collision + a pale overlap at once.
- **✂ #8 FROST LICH-EMPEROR.** Second pale-bone king; wide-A-frame competes with Carnelian's wedge at 32px,
  and glacier-cyan ≈ the sapphire it was meant to differ from. Pale + cool-blue is covered by Obsidian's rim
  + Verdigris's teal.
- **✂ #7 ROSE-GOLD BOY-PRINCE (closest call — cut WITH A FLAG).** Third (palest) pale king. Virtues: only
  short/round silhouette + only warm-pastel/cute pole. Cut because the brief prioritizes palette-direction
  spread, Verdigris is a stronger silhouette outlier + cooler-separated palette corner, and a tiny round king
  risks reading as a coin/power-up pickup at 32px. **SANCTIONED ALTERNATE:** swap Boy-Prince in for Verdigris
  IF the user wants the "cute pole" over the value/silhouette outlier — the one substitution the AD blesses.

## Silhouette-distinctness audit (blackout)
low seated pyramid (Koschei) / radial 4-arm fan (God-King) / broad wedge + planted sword (Warlord) / tall
smooth column (Obsidian) / soft organic slump + fronds (Verdigris) — five clearly different solid shapes;
the Obsidian↔Pontiff collision is removed by the cut. Five crown forms (spike-comb / cone / war-crown /
imperial dome / coral antlers) all survive 32px; coral antlers are the most detail-dependent (fat forks +
pearl finial mandated).
