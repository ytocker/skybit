# KING SKULL — the royal skeleton-court brood (brainstorm, no full render)

## The thesis
A **royal court of skeleton KINGS** — slick, royal, high-end, but locked inside
the Skybit chibi scary-CUTE procedural grammar: flat saturated triad fills
(dark-core → flat-fill → top-left rim-sheen), a hard 1–2px ink keyline `(28,22,30)`,
1px alpha-grown outline, big-headed chibi proportions, SS=6 supersample → smoothscale,
procedural-only (NO gradients-as-shading, NO PNGs). Regalia is the new vocabulary —
crowns, thrones, scepters, jeweled regalia, robes-of-state, gilt — but every piece is
built from the same flat-triad primitives the roster already uses.

**Respect for CITIPATI (the original to honour, never copy).** Citipati is the
ivory-dominant dancing charnel-lord with a warm-ivory `BONE (246,236,210)` mass,
thin-accent discipline (cinnabar crown-band, an OPEN ember halo-RING, gold/turquoise
slivers), a purple `THIRD_EYE (120,84,196)`, and fan ears. **His FIVE-skull crown arc
is his ALONE.** Every king below invents its OWN iconic royal crown form — no five-skull
arc, no three-skull tiara (Mukha-Devi owns that). Tiny skulls appear only as a *tasteful
lineage through-line* in regalia, never as a crown that re-reads as Citipati's. We borrow
Citipati's *discipline* (one dominant bone mass, thin accents, a single cool wisdom pin),
not his silhouette.

**Koschei evolution (mandatory, exactly one).** Direction #1 (REGENT KOSCHEI) is an
explicit ROYAL evolution of `citipati_versions/koschei` — it keeps his seated/enthroned
KIND, his blackened-iron crown identity, his cupped poison-chartreuse soul-egg focal and
indigo socket pins, then *elevates* them to high-end: the iron throne is gilded, the egg
is jeweled in a gold cabochon claw-setting, and tasteful tiny-skull regalia (a chain of
minute skulls along the throne lip) marks the lineage. He stays seated and two-armed.

**Palette spread (no two kings own the same royal direction).**
- #1 Regent Koschei — gilded grey-green tallow + gold + chartreuse jewel
- #2 Obsidian Sovereign — onyx/obsidian bone + gold (dark king)
- #3 Marble Pontiff — pale-marble bone + silver/platinum + cool sapphire
- #4 Amethyst God-King — warm-ivory bone + deep amethyst/purple court (ties Citipati's third-eye hue)
- #5 Carnelian Warlord — bronze-bone + blood-carnelian + iron (martial)
- #6 Verdigris Drowned-King — sea-bleached bone + verdigris-copper + pearl (aquatic court)
- #7 Rose-Gold Boy-Prince — palest bone + rose-gold + pink-tourmaline (the young heir)
- #8 Frost Lich-Emperor — blue-white frost-bone + platinum + glacier-cyan (cold court)

Multi-arm and tiny-skulls are a **toolkit, not a mandate** — used where a king benefits
(#4 the god-king is multi-armed; tiny skulls thread #1/#2/#5 as the lineage tell; #3/#6/#7/#8
stay two-armed or use other regalia tells).

## The 8 directions

### 1. REGENT KOSCHEI  *(THE KOSCHEI EVOLUTION — mandatory)*
- **Archetype + silhouette KIND:** seated throne-emperor — low, wide pyramid of mass
  sunk back into a throne, knees together, hunched over the cupped jewel. The only
  *enthroned/seated* silhouette in the set. Two-armed (keeps Koschei's identity).
- **Own royal crown form:** Koschei's tall blackened-iron SPIKE comb, now **gilded** —
  the iron spikes capped with thin gold ferrules and a single chartreuse jewel-bead at
  the centre spike tip. Still a jagged dark comb, never a ring of domes.
- **Royal palette DIRECTION:** gilded corpse-tallow grey-green bone `~(190,192,158)` +
  warm GOLD gilt on the throne frame + the poison-chartreuse `EGG (196,232,72)` jewel as
  the single warm focal; indigo socket pins `(110,118,212)`.
- **Regalia:** gilded iron throne with a rib-spire back-fan; the soul-egg upgraded into a
  faceted chartreuse CABOCHON held in a four-claw gold setting in his lap; a chain of
  minute gold skulls swagged along the throne's front lip (the lineage tiny-skull tell);
  a short gold sceptre laid across one armrest.
- **Multi-arm / tiny skulls:** two-armed (cradling the jewel); YES tiny skulls — the gold
  skull-chain along the throne lip is the tasteful lineage through-line.
- **32px tell:** the low seated dark-iron-and-gold caged mass with one glowing chartreuse
  jewel in the lap — unmistakably "the grumpy enthroned hoarder, now gilded."
- **Why distinct:** the ONLY seated mass; gilded-grey-green + chartreuse palette is owned
  by no other king; vs roster it is the matured Koschei, distinct from upright Citipati/
  Mukha/Nagaraja/etc by its low throne pyramid.

### 2. OBSIDIAN SOVEREIGN  *(the dark king)*
- **Archetype + silhouette KIND:** standing slender DARK monarch — a tall, narrow, regal
  upright with a long straight robe-of-state column to the floor and a high domed crown.
  Smooth, vertical, columnar silhouette (the "elegant tall pillar of a man").
- **Own royal crown form:** a closed IMPERIAL DOME crown — two crossed arches over a
  jeweled circlet, topped by a tiny gold orb-and-cross finial. Smooth and rounded, the
  opposite of Koschei's spikes.
- **Royal palette DIRECTION:** onyx/obsidian bone — near-black polished bone
  `~(44,42,52)` with a cool blue-grey sheen as the rim-light — + GOLD circlet/arches as
  the only bright accent. A "black king edged in gold."
- **Regalia:** floor-length robe-of-state rendered as one smooth dark flat mass with a
  thin gold-embroidered hem and vertical placket; a tall thin gold sceptre topped with a
  single skull-knob; an ermine-style collar suggested by a row of small white triad-ticks.
- **Multi-arm / tiny skulls:** two-armed; tiny skull only as the sceptre knob (subtle).
- **32px tell:** a black columnar king with a gold domed-arch crown and one vertical gold
  hem line — reads as a sleek dark chess-king at scale.
- **Why distinct:** only near-black bone mass + closed dome crown; columnar robe silhouette
  unlike every gaunt-rib king; vs roster nothing is this dark or this smooth-vertical.

### 3. MARBLE PONTIFF  *(the priest/pontiff-king)*
- **Archetype + silhouette KIND:** TALL scepter-priest — extremely tall, thin, ascetic
  upright topped by a towering vertical headpiece, both hands clasped on a long ground-staff
  held vertically in front. A "tall thin candle" silhouette dominated by vertical lines.
- **Own royal crown form:** a tall pale-marble + silver MITRE / tiara — a stacked
  triple-tier conical pontiff's crown (three rings of carved relief, narrowing to a silver
  cross-finial). Vertical and pointed, distinct from every wide/domed/spiked crown.
- **Royal palette DIRECTION:** pale-MARBLE bone — cool near-white `~(232,234,238)` with
  faint blue-grey veining ticks — + SILVER/PLATINUM trim + a single cool SAPPHIRE
  `~(64,108,210)` cabochon at the mitre's front and the staff head. Cool, pale, holy.
- **Regalia:** a long vertical crozier/staff with a sapphire-eyed skull head; a draped pale
  stole down the front with silver cross-stitching; ringed silver bands on the clasped
  finger-bones.
- **Multi-arm / tiny skulls:** two-armed (clasped on the staff); no tiny skulls (silver
  cross relief instead — keeps him holy, not charnel).
- **32px tell:** the towering tri-tier pale mitre over a thin white column gripping a tall
  blue-tipped staff — a "holy candle" read.
- **Why distinct:** only pale-marble + silver + sapphire palette; only triple-cone mitre;
  the tall-vertical-staff silhouette is unique; vs roster nothing is this cold-white-holy.

### 4. AMETHYST GOD-KING  *(the multi-armed deity-monarch)*
- **Archetype + silhouette KIND:** seated/standing MULTI-ARMED god-king — a broad,
  radially-symmetric figure with FOUR arms fanned in mudra poses, a wide symmetrical
  silhouette (the only radial-arm king). Lotus-throne base.
- **Own royal crown form:** a tall five-spire AMETHYST KIRITA-MUKUTA — a pointed conical
  deity-crown of stacked purple-jewel tiers rising to a single tall amethyst spire (NOT a
  ring of skulls; NOT Mukha's fan — a solid pointed jeweled cone). Ties Citipati's purple
  third-eye into a full court hue.
- **Royal palette DIRECTION:** warm-ivory bone `(246,236,210)` (borrowing Citipati's bone
  so the deity reads as kin) + deep AMETHYST/purple `~(120,84,196)` robe-of-state and crown
  jewels + thin gold edging. A "purple-and-gold divine" read; the purple is the dominant
  ACCENT mass (the one king where the jewel colour is allowed to be large, as a robe).
- **Regalia:** four arms holding tiny regalia (a lotus, a gold disc, a sceptre, an
  upturned blessing palm); a third eye on the brow (the Citipati wisdom pin, owned-hue);
  a broad amethyst sash-robe; gold armlets.
- **Multi-arm / tiny skulls:** YES four arms (its whole point); tiny skulls as small
  beads on the armlets only.
- **32px tell:** the wide radial four-arm fan under a tall purple jeweled cone-crown with
  a purple third-eye dot — a "purple many-armed idol."
- **Why distinct:** only multi-armed radial silhouette; only large-purple-robe palette;
  the amethyst cone-crown + third eye is unique; vs roster the radial arms separate it
  from Mukha-Devi's *radial-skull-FAN* (hers is skulls, his is arms).

### 5. CARNELIAN WARLORD  *(the crowned warlord-colossus)*
- **Archetype + silhouette KIND:** towering crowned WARLORD-colossus — broad-shouldered,
  heavy, armoured, feet planted wide, one fist on a great planted sword. A wide, top-heavy,
  aggressive triangle silhouette (heavy shoulders → planted base). The "big bruiser king."
- **Own royal crown form:** a heavy spiked WAR-CROWN of iron + carnelian — a thick battle
  circlet with forward-jutting horn-like blades and a blood-carnelian gem over the brow.
  Massive and forward-leaning, distinct from elegant/domed/conical crowns.
- **Royal palette DIRECTION:** bronze-toned BONE `~(206,176,128)` (warmer, dirtier than
  ivory) + blood-CARNELIAN `~(176,40,44)` as the thin gem/sash accent + dark IRON
  pauldrons and gauntlets. Martial, heavy, bronze-and-blood.
- **Regalia:** iron skull-faced pauldrons; a great two-handed sword planted point-down he
  leans on (the vertical anchor); a carnelian-clasped cape thrown back off the shoulders;
  a heavy belt of small iron skulls.
- **Multi-arm / tiny skulls:** two-armed; YES tiny skulls — the iron skull-belt + pauldron
  faces are the lineage tell.
- **32px tell:** the broad armoured wedge with a forward-spiked iron crown and a vertical
  planted sword down the centre — a "war boss" read.
- **Why distinct:** only heavy/armoured/top-heavy wedge silhouette + planted-sword vertical;
  only bronze-bone + carnelian + iron palette; vs roster nothing else is a bruiser.

### 6. VERDIGRIS DROWNED-KING  *(the aquatic abyssal court)*
- **Archetype + silhouette KIND:** seated/slumped DROWNED monarch — a barnacled, weed-draped
  king on a coral-throne, posture sagging sideways with trailing kelp tendrils, an irregular
  drooping silhouette broken by hanging fronds (the only "soft-edged, trailing" outline).
- **Own royal crown form:** a CORAL-BRANCH crown — asymmetric verdigris-copper coral antlers
  studded with pearls, an organic branching form (no straight comb, no smooth dome) topped
  with a single large baroque pearl.
- **Royal palette DIRECTION:** sea-bleached grey-green BONE `~(168,184,166)` + VERDIGRIS
  copper `~(78,158,134)` regalia (oxidised teal-green) + PEARL white-cream highlights.
  An oxidised undersea-treasure palette no other king touches.
- **Regalia:** a verdigris coral-and-anchor throne; a trident-sceptre wound with kelp;
  draped seaweed "robe" fronds; barnacle clusters and pearl beads along the crown and collar.
- **Multi-arm / tiny skulls:** two-armed; no tiny skulls (pearls + barnacles are the texture
  tell instead — keeps the aquatic identity clean).
- **32px tell:** the slumped verdigris-green king with branching coral antlers and trailing
  weed — a "sunken pirate-king" read with the only teal accent in the set.
- **Why distinct:** only soft trailing/organic silhouette + branching coral crown; only
  verdigris + pearl palette; vs roster nothing is aquatic or asymmetric-organic.

### 7. ROSE-GOLD BOY-PRINCE  *(the jewel-cabochon young heir)*
- **Archetype + silhouette KIND:** small, round, SHORT chibi PRINCE — over-large head,
  tiny body, the cutest and most compact silhouette of the brood (a little egg-on-legs).
  The "baby heir" — scary-cute pushed all the way to cute. The only short/round king.
- **Own royal crown form:** a dainty open CORONET — a delicate thin rose-gold band with a
  ring of small upright trefoil points, each tipped with a tiny pink-tourmaline bead. Small,
  light, and open (vs every tall/heavy crown).
- **Royal palette DIRECTION:** the PALEST bone `~(250,244,232)` (almost white, youthful) +
  ROSE-GOLD `~(228,170,150)` coronet/trim + a single large PINK-TOURMALINE
  `~(232,120,150)` heart-cut cabochon held to his chest. Soft, warm, pastel-regal.
- **Regalia:** a tiny scepter-rattle (a sceptre scaled to a toy); a short fur-trimmed cape;
  an oversized signet ring on a finger; the heart-cut gem clutched like a favourite toy.
- **Multi-arm / tiny skulls:** two-armed; no tiny skulls (a single gold skull charm on a
  neck-ribbon, optional, very subtle — keeps him sweet).
- **32px tell:** the tiny round big-headed prince clutching one pink heart-gem under a light
  trefoil coronet — a "chibi baby king" read; the only pink in the set.
- **Why distinct:** only short/round/compact silhouette; only palest-bone + rose-gold + pink
  palette; vs roster nothing else is small-and-cute — it anchors the "scary-CUTE" pole.

### 8. FROST LICH-EMPEROR  *(the lich emperor in robe-of-state, cold court)*
- **Archetype + silhouette KIND:** standing LICH EMPEROR in a vast flaring robe-of-state —
  a wide-shouldered upright whose robe flares OUT at the base into a broad triangular skirt
  (a wide A-frame / "ice-mountain" silhouette), arms spread holding a long horizontal staff.
  Imperious, frost-wreathed, broad-based but standing.
- **Own royal crown form:** a wide spreading FROST-CROWN — a flat broad imperial circlet
  fanned with thin upward ice-shard points (a splayed sunburst of pale-blue crystal spikes),
  centred on a glacier-cyan gem. Wide and fanning (vs Koschei's narrow comb, vs the dome/cone).
- **Royal palette DIRECTION:** blue-white FROST bone `~(214,228,238)` + PLATINUM/pale-silver
  regalia + glacier-CYAN `~(96,196,224)` gem/eye glow + thin frost-white rime ticks. The
  coldest, palest court — distinct from the holy-marble (#3) by its cyan (not sapphire) and
  its frost-rime texture.
- **Regalia:** a vast flaring frost-robe of state as the dominant lower mass (flat pale
  triad with thin platinum hem); a long horizontal ice-sceptre held two-handed across the
  body; frost-rime ticks crusting the shoulders; cyan socket-glow eyes.
- **Multi-arm / tiny skulls:** two-armed (staff held wide); no tiny skulls (ice-shard relief
  + rime ticks are the texture tell).
- **32px tell:** the wide A-frame robe under a fanning pale-cyan ice-shard crown with a
  horizontal staff — a "frozen emperor mountain" read; the only cyan-frost king.
- **Why distinct:** only wide flaring-robe A-frame silhouette + horizontal staff; only frost
  blue-white + cyan palette; vs roster nothing else is icy or robe-flared; separated from the
  Marble Pontiff by cold-cyan vs holy-sapphire and wide-robe vs tall-candle.

**Cull guidance for the art-director:** all 8 pass the blackout test (seated-pyramid /
tall-column / tall-candle-staff / wide-radial-fan / armoured-wedge / soft-trailing-organic /
short-round-egg / wide-A-frame are eight different solid shapes), and each owns a unique
crown FORM and palette direction. If trimming to 5, the seated Koschei-evo (#1, mandatory)
and the multi-armed god-king (#4) and the warlord-colossus (#5) are the strongest
silhouette anchors; among the pale trio (#3 marble, #7 rose-prince, #8 frost) keep at most
two to avoid a "pale court" cluster — recommend dropping whichever of #3/#8 the AD finds
closest, since the Boy-Prince (#7) is the only short/cute silhouette and earns its slot.
