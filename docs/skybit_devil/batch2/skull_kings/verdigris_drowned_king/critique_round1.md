# VERDIGRIS DROWNED-KING — Round 1 critique

A strong, genuinely original direction — the slump, coral antlers, and teal-only palette all land as
CONCEPTS. But round 1 fails two of three critical 32px gates, exactly where a drowned-king must win. Not a
re-roll — the bones are right; it needs a focused legibility pass.

## KEEP
- **Posture/silhouette** — the head-lolled, collapsed-shoulder slump is the best thing here; instantly
  "drowned, weary king," unmistakably the only soft/asymmetric organic shape in the court.
- Coral-antler crown concept (reads antler/coral, not generic spikes). Hue identity at hero scale
  (verdigris-teal + sea-bone + pearl; only teal in the set; clear of KFC red + the purple-pin lineage).

## FIX (ranked, all at 32px)
1. **[GATE] Fronds fail the 32px test** — they mud into a soft fringe; you can't count 2–3 fat tendrils with
   sky between. Rebuild as 2–3 FAT separated tendrils (drop any thin fringe); each must survive as a distinct
   shape on the 32px DAY chip. Fix this FIRST.
2. **[GATE] Pearl finial is NOT the high-contrast crown-tell** — pearl/pale-bone/teal all sit in one value
   band. Push the pearl's top-left sheen to near-white `~(245,248,250)` + slightly enlarge it so it's the
   single brightest pixel by a clear margin; eye must land on the pearl first on both chips.
3. **Sea-bone vs Koschei too close** (`(186,206,184)` vs tallow `(190,192,158)` — only "slightly greener").
   Move toward `~(196,216,196)`–`(204,224,200)`: raise luminance ~8–10% above Koschei AND push green ahead
   of R/B so it tilts cool-green. Re-pin the side-by-side; obvious value AND hue step.
4. **Rescue the NIGHT chip** — the bone mass loses value on night sky; add a cool pearl/teal RIM-sheen wide
   enough to carry the slump silhouette (Obsidian/Koschei night-rim lesson).
5. **Coral antlers: confirm 3–4 FAT forks at 32px** — each fork survives the blackout as a separate prong,
   not merged with the skull into one blob.
6. **Re-run the blackout LAST** — the slump must be identifiable with zero internal detail.
7. **Pillar:** dial barnacle beads back ~15–20% in contrast/frequency so they don't strobe while scrolling.
Lean on VALUE (the near-white pearl) for the tell, not teal-vs-green hue (the riskiest colorblind pairing).
Get fronds + pearl + bone-separation landing at 32px and it clears the gate.
