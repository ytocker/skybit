# CARNELIAN WARLORD — Round 1 critique

A characterful, menacing royal colossus — but it FAILS the brief's central lock + the GATE's "one dominant
bone mass": the iron pauldrons OUT-MASS the bronze bone at 32px (iron ≈45% of iron+bronze, and the two
pauldron blocks are the widest/darkest/highest-contrast shapes — at 32px it reads as two dark wings with a
small body between). Planted-sword + war-crown tells work; mass hierarchy + bronze warmth don't yet. Fixable
without re-roll.

## KEEP
- Planted-greatsword centre-line (best tell, reads day+night). War-crown spike comb (regal, distinct).
  Mirrored pillar (wholesale). Carnelian brow-gem placement — but it must become the SOLE red.

## FIX (ranked)
1. **Shrink iron pauldrons ~35–40%** (narrower/shorter) and pull them inboard to cap the shoulder, not form
   outboard wings; remove any iron creeping onto the torso. At 32px iron = two small shoulder caps + crown only.
2. **Widen + deepen the bronze torso barrel** so it's the clear central mass; the top-heavy wedge should come
   from bronze shoulders, not iron.
3. **Kill the pauldron skull-face RED eye-dots** — make them dark ink sockets so the ONLY red is the brow
   gem; brow gem must be the single brightest pixel day+night.
4. **Push bronze warmth/dirt up** (target `~(206,176,128)` or warmer/dirtier; drop value ~10–15, add red) so
   it clearly separates from ivory on a bright day sky; keep the triad so it stays dimensional.
5. **Consolidate the lower-body joint knobs** — fewer/larger bronze segments so the legs plant cleanly
   astride the sword, not scattered beads at 32px.
6. **Iron skull-belt = a single thin band** (accent line, not a plate, so it doesn't add to iron mass).

Day: bronze barrel is the obviously largest shape; iron = two small caps + crown; one red dot (brow).
Night: war-crown + planted sword still read; bronze doesn't go muddy-dark. Distinctness (broad wedge +
planted sword) holds — don't let shrunk pauldrons round off the wedge.
