# Skybit BOSS — BAMBOO THEME brainstorm (GD, brainstorm mode)

Fresh theme. 8 genuinely distinct boss directions, ALL unmistakably BAMBOO at
32px, each its own folklore/creature. AD will cull to 5. No full render here —
theses + thumbnail-level reads only.

## House style inherited (from `brainstorm_locked15.md`)

CHIBI proportions (big head, short weight-shifted body); FLAT saturated fills +
hard 1–2px ink keyline `(28,22,30)`; form via the **dark-core → flat-fill →
top-left rim-sheen** TRIAD; silhouette POP via a 1px outline grown from the alpha
mask; supersample → smoothscale. Scary-CUTE menace, never grim realism, but
pushed EPIC (bigger, more detail, a glow). Procedural only — NO gradients, NO
PNGs. Every prop mirrors top↔bottom into a clean repeatable PILLAR (repeating
shaft body + detachable gap-edge cap), the way Big Reapy's bone-bident does.

## The hard constraint — SPREAD THE GREEN

The shipped roster already owns the green/leaf lanes and these must NOT be
collided with:
- **Cernun** — deep desaturated PINE, birch-bone antler crown, blue-leaning.
- **Kappa** — bright YELLOW-green water-imp, turtle-bronze, turquoise dish.
- **Kitsune** — snow-white fox, MINT-green foxfire glow.
- **Haedung** — jade scale-band.
- **Tlaloc / Citipati** — grey slate-jade + coral / teal accents.

So the 8 bamboo bosses must NOT all read green. Each gets its own bamboo palette
LANE pulled from a real bamboo form, plus its own distinct ACCENT so no two read
the same at 32px:
1. **fresh-jade culm** (live madake) — held cooler/bluer + paired with a
   lacquer-red accent so it never reads as Kappa's warm yellow-green.
2. **golden-tan dry madake** (cured timber) — a warm STRAW palette, basically
   not-green; gold-leaf accent.
3. **blackened kurochiku** — near-black with a violet sheen; lacquer-red + gold.
4. **snow-laden white-green** — cool white-blue dominant, green only as cold
   accent; snow-blue glow.
5. **sulphur sprout-yellow** (takenoko husk) — warm cream-and-ochre with a
   sheath-pink tip; held clearly APART from Kappa yellow-green (it is a
   tan/cream husk, not a green body).
6. **moonlit pale-green glow** (Kaguya) — pearl-white culm with a cool MOON-glow
   (a fresh glow hue, not Kitsune mint, not Yurei cyan — a pale gold-white).
7. **woven-tan basket** (komuso) — warm wicker tan, paper-white ofuda accent.
8. **ink-wash sasa green-grey** (tanabata) — soft sage-grey grass with a
   PAPER-WHITE + festival-multicolor wish-strip accent.

No two share both a dominant family AND a silhouette KIND. Greens that DO appear
(1, 4, 8) sit at three different value/chroma lanes (saturated-cool, white-cold,
sage-grey) and on three different body KINDs.

## THE 8 DIRECTIONS

### 1. Take-Ryu — the grove-coiling bamboo dragon
- **Thesis.** An Eastern RYU whose entire serpentine body IS a single living
  green culm — node-rings band the coils, leaf-sprays burst from each joint. The
  grove given a head and made to coil skyward.
- **32px read.** A long S-COIL of jointed green segments stacked vertically, a
  small whiskered dragon-head crowning the top, leaf-fins flaring at intervals —
  a vertical serpent-of-nodes, unlike any blocky/winged shipped boss.
- **Chibi+triad anatomy.** Big-headed cute dragon skull (short snout, round
  cheeks, stubby antler-whiskers of curled leaf-shoots), tiny stub forelegs
  clutching nothing; body = 5–6 fat node-segments, each a hard triad-lit cylinder
  (dark-core groove at the node-ring, flat jade fill, top-left rim-sheen on the
  bulge); leaf-spray fins at every other joint. ELEVATED detail: each node-ring
  gets a thin gold sheath-collar and a 2-leaf sprig; eyes glow faint.
- **Bamboo pillar.** The culm-body itself is the pillar — node-segment = the
  repeat band; the dragon-head (or a single curled leaf-shoot finial) = gap-edge
  cap. Cleanest mirror in the set: a jointed culm is a pillar by nature.
- **Palette (fresh-jade culm, held COOL).** culm-jade `(86,170,108)` base ·
  deep node-green `(46,120,72)` shade · **lacquer-red accent** `(204,64,52)`
  (whisker beads / sheath-collars) · gold node-collar `(222,186,90)` · pale
  leaf-mint sheen `(168,222,170)` · ink `(24,32,26)`. Dominant: cool jade +
  lacquer-red — the red focal pulls it off Kappa's pure yellow-green.
- **Hook.** A whole bamboo grove woke up, braided into one dragon, and reared. EPIC.

### 2. Madake-Oni — the dry-timber ogre with a culm war-club
- **Thesis.** A stout ONI carved/grown from CURED golden bamboo — a timber-yellow
  brute hauling a giant knotted bamboo-culm kanabo club. Not green at all: warm
  dry STRAW.
- **32px read.** A broad bottom-heavy ogre block, two short node-stub horns,
  hoisting a thick studded CLUB taller than itself — a warm-tan brute-with-club
  read, the only non-green-bodied bamboo boss with a clear ogre kind.
- **Chibi+triad anatomy.** Square straw-tan ogre torso, big jutting under-fangs,
  fierce knot-brow, two stubby horns that are little bamboo-node stubs; thick
  arms; tiger-stripe husk loincloth. Body fills are warm timber-tan, each plane
  triad-lit (dark-core knot-groove / straw fill / pale sheen). ELEVATED: the club
  is a real fat culm with raised node-rings as the "studs," gold-leaf split-cracks
  glinting along the grain.
- **Bamboo pillar.** The kanabo CLUB is the pillar — knotted culm shaft with
  node-ring studs = repeat band; the gnarled root-ball head (or a split-culm
  crown) = gap-edge cap. Heavy mass sits low/centered on-axis — clean mirror.
- **Palette (golden-tan dry madake — basically NOT green).** timber-straw
  `(212,176,104)` base · burnt-amber shade `(158,116,56)` · **gold-leaf split
  accent** `(238,206,120)` · ember-red eye `(206,72,52)` · husk-cream
  `(236,222,178)` · ink `(34,24,18)` · sheen `(244,222,160)`. Dominant: warm
  straw + gold — the SOLE warm/non-green bamboo body, can't collide with any green.
- **Hook.** Years of curing turned the timber to muscle; it swings the grove like
  a bat.

### 3. Kurochiku-Nushi — the blackened-bamboo lacquer yokai master
- **Thesis.** A KUROCHIKU (black-bamboo) tsukumogami lord — an elegant near-black
  bamboo figure with violet-black lacquer sheen, regal and sinister-serene. The
  dark, courtly bamboo.
- **32px read.** A tall slim near-BLACK jointed figure, narrow, with a thin
  lacquer-red sash and a single gold node-ring crown — a dark vertical pillar of a
  creature; the only dark-bodied bamboo boss, pops on bright day-sky by contrast.
- **Chibi+triad anatomy.** Slim chibi noble: small smooth black-bamboo head with
  half-lidded calm eyes, body a stack of polished black node-segments, long thin
  arms ending in leaf-blade fingers, a trailing hakama-hem of split black culm.
  Triad on a DARK base reads as charcoal-core / blackened-violet fill / cool
  violet-white sheen (so it isn't a flat blob). ELEVATED: gold sheath-bands at
  each node, a lacquer-red obi, a faint violet under-glow at the feet.
- **Bamboo pillar.** A polished black-culm STAFF/flute the master leans on = the
  pillar — black node-segment shaft = repeat band; a gold-capped culm-cut + red
  tassel = gap-edge cap. Slim symmetric — clean mirror.
- **Palette (blackened kurochiku).** near-black culm `(48,42,52)` base ·
  ink-violet shade `(30,26,38)` · **violet-white sheen accent** `(168,156,196)` ·
  **lacquer-red obi/tassel** `(196,52,56)` · gold node-band `(220,182,92)` · ink
  `(20,16,22)`. Dominant: violet-black + lacquer-red + gold — the SOLE dark body,
  no green at all.
- **Hook.** The grove that aged to black for a hundred years; it does not hurry.

### 4. Yukitake — the snow-laden bowed bamboo spirit
- **Thesis.** A winter bamboo spirit — a culm bowed double under heavy SNOW, its
  spirit-face peeking from the bend, cold and gentle-eerie. The most distinct
  PALETTE: white-cold, green only as accent.
- **32px read.** A tall pale stalk arcing over in a heavy bow, a cap of rounded
  SNOW-mounds on top tilting it, a small round face nestled in the curve — a
  bowed-and-snow-capped read, instantly winter, unique posture.
- **Chibi+triad anatomy.** Slim frost-pale green culm body arced into a candy-cane
  bow; a chubby spirit-face in the bend (closed gentle eyes, rosy cold cheeks);
  heavy white snow-loaf mounds capping the head and shoulders (hard triad-lit
  snow: blue-shadow core / white fill / white-blue sheen); icicle drips at the
  leaf-tips. ELEVATED: a faint snow-blue aura, sparkle-flecks, frost rime on the
  node-rings.
- **Bamboo pillar.** The snow-laden culm itself is the pillar — pale culm with
  snow-cap ridges per node = repeat band; a heavy snow-loaf + icicle fringe =
  gap-edge cap. The bow can be drawn straight for the repeat shaft, bent only at
  the cap — clean mirror.
- **Palette (snow-laden white-green — cold).** snow-white `(232,238,244)` base ·
  blue-shadow `(150,176,200)` snow shade · **frost-green culm accent**
  `(120,178,150)` (the ONLY green, used small/cold) · **snow-blue glow**
  `(170,212,236)` · icicle-cyan `(198,232,242)` · ink `(28,34,40)` · sheen
  `(248,252,255)`. Dominant: snow-white + snow-blue — held FAR from any roster
  green; reads as a winter boss first.
- **Hook.** It has stood all winter; the weight that should snap it only makes it
  bow and watch you.

### 5. Takenoko-Bozu — the sprout-imp baby shoot
- **Thesis.** A TAKENOKO (baby bamboo shoot) come alive — a fat cone-shaped husk
  imp, all overlapping sheath-scales, tiny and bratty. Scary-CUTE leaning hard
  CUTE, but EPIC via swarm/size.
- **32px read.** A fat upright CONE of overlapping pointed husk-scales (a pinecone
  / artichoke read), a stubby face near the top, two leaf-sprout antennae — a
  squat sheath-cone, unlike anything slim or winged in the roster.
- **Chibi+triad anatomy.** Round-bottom cone body built from rows of overlapping
  triangular sheath-scales (each a hard triad-lit scale: dark-core base / cream-tan
  fill / pale sheen); cheeky scrunched face peeking between the top scales; two
  curled green leaf-sprouts as antennae; tiny root-feet. ELEVATED: a fuzzy
  husk-fur fringe on each scale-edge, a sheath-pink blush at the tip, comically
  big to sell EPIC despite being "a baby."
- **Bamboo pillar.** A stacked sprout-CLUSTER pillar — repeating rows of
  husk-scales = the repeat band (a scaled cone-column); a single curled
  leaf-sprout finial = gap-edge cap. Symmetric cone-stack — clean mirror.
- **Palette (sulphur sprout / takenoko husk — tan-cream, NOT Kappa green).**
  husk-cream `(228,210,156)` base · ochre-husk shade `(176,140,72)` · **sheath-pink
  tip accent** `(224,150,150)` · sprout-green leaf `(132,178,84)` (only the two
  antennae) · sulphur-yellow inner `(236,214,108)` · ink `(34,28,18)` · sheen
  `(244,232,186)`. Dominant: cream-tan husk + sheath-pink — a husk, not a green
  body; clearly off Kappa.
- **Hook.** It just burst from the dirt overnight and it is already the size of a
  pillar and very annoyed.

### 6. Kaguya — the moon-princess glowing inside the culm
- **Thesis.** KAGUYA-HIME at the moment of finding: a pale celestial child curled
  GLOWING inside a split, luminous bamboo stalk. The light-in-the-grove boss —
  serene, radiant, otherworldly.
- **32px read.** A tall pale culm split open down the middle revealing a small
  curled glowing FIGURE within, a halo of cool moon-light around the split — a
  glowing-stalk-with-a-figure read, totally unlike a free-standing creature.
- **Chibi+triad anatomy.** Outer culm = pale luminous bamboo (triad-lit pale jade
  segments) cracked open in a vesica/almond split; inside, a chibi moon-child
  (round face, long pale hair, closed serene eyes, tiny folded hands) cradled in
  the glow; the split edges rim-lit. ELEVATED: a soft procedural MOON-glow (flat
  stepped concentric rings of pale gold-white, no gradient), floating
  light-motes, a thin crescent above. Menace = uncanny serenity + radiance.
- **Bamboo pillar.** The luminous culm is the pillar — glowing pale node-segments
  = repeat band; the split-open glowing aperture (figure tucked at the gap) =
  gap-edge cap radiating into the gap. Symmetric stalk — clean mirror, and the
  glow makes a striking gap-edge.
- **Palette (moonlit pale-green + fresh MOON-glow hue).** pearl-culm `(214,228,210)`
  base · pale-jade shade `(150,188,158)` · **moon-glow accent** `(244,238,196)`
  (pale GOLD-white — a NEW glow hue, not Kitsune mint, not Yurei cyan) · cool
  moon-blue rim `(192,206,232)` · child-pale skin `(238,232,224)` · gold hair-tie
  `(224,196,120)` · ink `(30,32,34)` · sheen `(252,250,236)`. Dominant: pearl-jade
  + pale-gold moon-glow — its glow hue alone separates it from every roster glow.
- **Hook.** The grove found a moon inside it; the light is calm and the light is
  not from here.

### 7. Komuso-Fue — the basket-head bamboo monk of emptiness
- **Thesis.** A KOMUSO "monk of emptiness" tsukumogami — a faceless figure in a
  woven wicker BASKET-hat (tengai), playing a shakuhachi bamboo flute that bleeds
  sound-spirits. The face-hidden mendicant; pure eerie-zen.
- **32px read.** A standing robed figure topped by a big domed WOVEN-BASKET head
  (no face — a wicker dome with a faint glow seam), a long bamboo FLUTE held
  diagonally to where a mouth would be — a basket-head-with-flute read, the only
  faceless bamboo boss.
- **Chibi+triad anatomy.** Chibi robed monk (short wide hempen robe, triad-lit
  panels); the head is an over-large woven basket-dome rendered as a cross-hatch
  weave of triad-lit reed strokes (the weave is the detail); two faint glowing
  eye-slits deep in the weave; both hands cradle a node-banded shakuhachi flute
  angled up. ELEVATED: pale sound-spirit wisps (flat stepped puffs, NOT gradient)
  drifting from the flute holes; an ofuda paper-charm pinned to the robe.
- **Bamboo pillar.** The shakuhachi FLUTE is the pillar — node-banded bamboo flute
  shaft with finger-holes = repeat band; the bell-mouth end + a sound-spirit wisp
  = gap-edge cap. Slim symmetric flute — clean mirror; finger-holes read as the
  band detail.
- **Palette (woven-tan basket + paper-white ofuda).** wicker-tan `(196,164,104)`
  base · deep-reed shade `(140,108,58)` · **paper-white ofuda accent** `(238,232,214)`
  with cinnabar `(198,68,56)` strokes · hemp-grey robe `(150,146,132)` ·
  pale sound-wisp `(214,224,220)` glow · bamboo-flute green-tan `(170,176,118)` ·
  ink `(30,24,18)` · sheen `(228,206,156)`. Dominant: wicker-tan + hemp-grey +
  paper-white — a warm woven body, no creature-green; faceless silhouette is
  unique.
- **Hook.** It plays one note and the grove holds its breath; you never see what
  is under the basket.

### 8. Tanabata-Sasa — the wish-laden bamboo-grass festival spirit
- **Thesis.** A TANABATA sasa (bamboo-grass) spirit hung with paper WISH-strips
  (tanzaku) and streamers — a slender grass-bundle figure fluttering with festival
  color. The most COLORFUL bamboo boss; soft sage-grey body, color from the wishes.
- **32px read.** A slim swaying bundle of thin bamboo-grass stalks, leaves
  fanning out, draped all over with little rectangular paper STRIPS and streamers
  in bright colors, a gentle face among the leaves — a strip-draped grass-spray
  read, festive and fluttering, distinct from every solid-bodied boss.
- **Chibi+triad anatomy.** Slender sage-grey sasa-bundle body (several thin
  triad-lit grass stalks tied at a node-waist), a wide fan of pointed sasa-leaves
  as the "crown," a soft round face peeking among the lower leaves; festival
  decorations — folded paper cranes, star-streamers, and rows of tanzaku
  wish-strips — hung off the leaves as flat triad shapes. ELEVATED: many tiny
  multicolor strips fluttering, a couple of paper stars glinting, a faint warm
  festival glow. Cute-eerie: it grants wishes it overheard, slightly wrong.
- **Bamboo pillar.** The decorated sasa-STALK is the pillar — a thin grass-stalk
  with node-ties strung with hanging tanzaku strips = repeat band (each strip a
  band marker); a paper-star + leaf-spray = gap-edge cap fluttering at the gap.
  Slim symmetric stalk — clean mirror.
- **Palette (ink-wash sasa sage-grey + festival multicolor accents).** sage-grass
  `(150,176,128)` base (greyer/softer than any roster green — a muted grass, low
  chroma) · deep-sage shade `(98,128,84)` · **paper-white tanzaku accent**
  `(238,236,224)` carrying small **festival multicolor** strips (pink `(228,120,150)`,
  sky `(120,186,220)`, gold `(232,200,96)`) · warm festival glow `(244,224,176)` ·
  ink `(28,32,26)` · sheen `(196,216,176)`. Dominant: soft sage-grey grass +
  multicolor paper — its grey-green sits at a third green lane and the multicolor
  strips make the silhouette read as "festival," not "another green creature."
- **Hook.** Every wish ever tied to it is still whispering; it leans down to read
  yours.

## DISTINCTNESS CROSS-CHECK

- **8 silhouette KINDs, all different:** (1) vertical node-serpent coil ·
  (2) bottom-heavy ogre + tall club · (3) slim dark noble-stack · (4) bowed
  snow-capped stalk · (5) squat husk-scale cone · (6) split glowing stalk with
  inner figure · (7) faceless basket-head + diagonal flute · (8) strip-draped
  grass-spray. No two share a body kind.
- **Green is SPREAD across distinct lanes/value/chroma, never the same:**
  - 1 Take-Ryu — saturated COOL jade, lacquer-red focal (off Kappa's yellow-green).
  - 2 Madake-Oni — warm STRAW/gold, NOT green at all.
  - 3 Kurochiku — near-BLACK violet, NOT green at all.
  - 4 Yukitake — snow-WHITE/blue dominant, green only a cold accent.
  - 5 Takenoko — cream-TAN husk, green only on two antennae.
  - 6 Kaguya — pearl pale-jade with pale-GOLD moon-glow (glow hue is the read).
  - 7 Komuso — wicker-TAN + hemp-grey, no creature green.
  - 8 Tanabata — muted SAGE-grey grass + multicolor paper (third green lane).
  Only 1, 4, 8 carry real green, at three different lanes (saturated-cool /
  white-cold / sage-grey-muted) on three different KINDs — no collision with
  Cernun pine, Kappa yellow-green, Kitsune mint, Haedung jade, Tlaloc slate.
- **Distinct accents/glows:** lacquer-red (1,3) used on different bodies ·
  gold-leaf (2) · violet sheen (3) · snow-blue glow (4) · sheath-pink (5) ·
  pale-gold MOON-glow (6, a new glow hue) · paper-white ofuda + cinnabar (7) ·
  festival multicolor (8). New glows (snow-blue, moon-gold, festival-warm) are
  held apart from Yurei cyan and Kitsune mint.
- **Every concept HAS a real bamboo pillar grounded in a bamboo form:** the culm
  body (1,3,4,6), a culm war-club (2), a sprout-cluster (5), a flute/staff (7),
  a decorated sasa-stalk (8). Node-segments give every one a natural repeat band.
