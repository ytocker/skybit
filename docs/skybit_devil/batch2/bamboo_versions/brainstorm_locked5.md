# Bamboo-versions — LOCKED 5 (per-concept briefs)

Fresh BAMBOO-theme epic bosses. Every one is unmistakably BAMBOO at 32px and a distinct
folklore/creature. AD culled the original 8 (`brainstorm_gd.md`, `brainstorm_ad.md`) to these
five and cut **Takenoko** (silhouette-fragile husk-cone), **Komuso** (palette + flute-pillar a
near-twin of Madake/Kurochiku's tan/dark lanes), and **Tanabata** (color-fragments lost at 32px +
a third muted-green lane the roster doesn't need). The five below differ on BOTH palette family
AND silhouette KIND.

**House style (elevated):** CHIBI proportions (big head, short weight-shifted body) + scary-CUTE
menace pushed EPIC + flat procedural triad (dark-core groove → flat node fill → top-left rim-sheen)
+ 1–2px ink keyline `(28,22,30)` + 1px alpha-mask outline for silhouette POP; supersample (SS=5–6)
→ smoothscale. Procedural ONLY — NO gradients, NO PNGs, NO soft raster halos. Every prop mirrors
top↔bottom into a clean repeatable PILLAR: repeating node-segment shaft body + a detachable
gap-edge cap, bottom-rooted, the way Big Reapy's bone-bident does. Legible at true 32px, day AND
night.

**CROSS-SET PINS (police every round):**
- KIND spread: NODE-SERPENT-COIL / OGRE-WITH-CLUB / SLIM-DARK-NOBLE-STACK / BOWED-SNOW-STALK /
  SPLIT-STALK-WITH-INNER-FIGURE — each unmistakable at 32px.
- Bamboo greens are SPREAD across distinct value/chroma lanes, never collided with the shipped
  roster (Cernun pine, Kappa yellow-green + turtle-bronze, Kitsune mint, Haedung jade, Tlaloc/
  Citipati slate-jade). Only Take-Ryu carries a saturated-COOL jade body; Yukitake's green is a
  small COLD accent; the other three are non-green bodies (straw / near-black / pearl).
- Distinct accent + glow per boss; the two glow bosses must NOT collide with each other or with
  Yurei cyan / Kitsune mint — Yukitake = snow-blue glow, Kaguya = pale gold-white moon-glow.

### 1. take-ryu — grove-coiling bamboo dragon  (KIND: node-serpent-coil)
- **Thesis.** An Eastern RYU whose whole serpentine body IS one living green culm — node-rings band
  the coils, leaf-fins burst from the joints. The grove given a head and reared skyward.
- **Palette lane (fresh-jade culm, held COOL + lacquer-red focal).** culm-jade `(86,170,108)` base /
  deep node-green `(46,120,72)` shade; **lacquer-red accent** `(204,64,52)` (whisker beads,
  sheath-collars) — the saturated red focal is what pulls the cool jade clear of Kappa's pure
  yellow-green; gold node-collar `(222,186,90)`; pale leaf-mint sheen `(168,222,170)`; ink
  `(24,32,26)`.
- **Silhouette KIND.** Vertical NODE-SERPENT-COIL — an active S-COIL of 5–6 fat jointed node-segments
  with a large whiskered dragon HEAD finial crowning the top and asymmetric leaf-fin flares off the
  joints. Never a straight column.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The culm-body IS the pillar: a jointed node-segment = the repeat shaft
  band (dark-core groove at each node-ring, flat jade bulge, top-left sheen); gap-edge cap = the
  whiskered dragon-head finial (or, on the lower mirror, a single curled leaf-shoot finial). A
  jointed culm is a pillar by nature — cleanest mirror in the set; bottom-rooted.
- **AD HARD CONSTRAINT (baked in).** Push the whiskered dragon-HEAD finial HARD — large, a clearly
  CRANIAL silhouette bump at the top — and keep the body an ACTIVE S-COIL with asymmetric leaf-fin
  flares, NEVER a straight column. This cranial-head + live-coil read is exactly what must separate
  Take-Ryu from Kaguya's straight split-stalk at 32px. Hold the jade body more saturated (heavier
  value) than Kaguya's pale pearl culm.
- **Hook.** A whole bamboo grove woke up, braided into one dragon, and reared. EPIC.

### 2. madake-oni — dry-timber ogre with a culm war-club  (KIND: ogre-with-club)
- **Thesis.** A stout ONI carved from CURED golden bamboo — a timber-yellow brute hauling a giant
  knotted bamboo-culm kanabo taller than itself. Not green at all: warm dry STRAW.
- **Palette lane (golden-tan dry madake — basically NOT green).** timber-straw `(212,176,104)` base /
  burnt-amber `(158,116,56)` shade; **gold-leaf split accent** `(238,206,120)` (hard metallic split-
  cracks along the grain — the sparkle lane); **ember-red eye** `(206,72,52)` (the ONLY saturated
  accent, kept TINY); husk-cream `(236,222,178)`; ink `(34,24,18)`; sheen `(244,222,160)`.
- **Silhouette KIND.** Bottom-heavy OGRE-WITH-CLUB — a broad squat straw-tan brute (jutting under-
  fangs, knot-brow, two short bamboo-node stub horns) hoisting a thick studded CLUB taller than its
  body; heavy mass sits LOW.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The kanabo CLUB is the pillar: a fat knotted culm shaft with raised
  node-rings as the "studs" = the repeat band; gap-edge cap = the gnarled root-ball head (or a split-
  culm crown). Heavy mass low and on-axis — clean bottom-rooted mirror.
- **AD HARD CONSTRAINT (baked in).** Hold the straw GENUINELY warm and DESATURATED — never drift to
  Kappa's turtle-bronze. The ember-red eye is the ONLY saturated accent and stays TINY. The sparkle
  is carried by gold-leaf split-cracks rendered as HARD METALLIC glints (sharp stepped facets), NOT
  a soft glow — this hard-metallic gold is the deliberate contrast against Kaguya's soft stepped
  moon-halo, so the two gold accents don't collide.
- **Hook.** Years of curing turned the timber to muscle; it swings the grove like a bat.

### 3. kurochiku-nushi — blackened-bamboo lacquer noble  (KIND: slim-dark-noble-stack)
- **Thesis.** A KUROCHIKU (black-bamboo) tsukumogami lord — an elegant near-black bamboo figure with
  violet-black lacquer sheen, regal and sinister-serene. The dark, courtly bamboo.
- **Palette lane (blackened kurochiku — the SOLE dark body).** near-black culm `(48,42,52)` base /
  ink-violet `(30,26,38)` shade; **violet-white sheen accent** `(168,156,196)`; **lacquer-red obi/
  tassel** `(196,52,56)`; gold node-band `(220,182,92)`; ink `(20,16,22)`. Triad on a DARK base reads
  charcoal-core → blackened-violet fill → cool violet-white sheen — never a flat blob.
- **Silhouette KIND.** Slim courtly NOBLE-STACK — a tall narrow figure: small smooth black-bamboo head
  (half-lidded calm eyes), body a stack of polished black node-segments, long thin leaf-blade-fingered
  arms, a trailing split-culm hakama hem. A dark vertical pillar of a creature.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The polished black-culm STAFF/flute the noble leans on is the pillar:
  black node-segment shaft = repeat band; gap-edge cap = a gold-capped culm-cut + red tassel. Slim and
  symmetric — clean bottom-rooted mirror.
- **AD HARD CONSTRAINT (baked in).** Prove legibility against a BRIGHT NOON sky EARLY (round 1 must use
  a bright daytime card, not a neutral grey one): the violet-white sheen + gold node-bands + lacquer-
  red obi triad must keep this from reading as a flat black blob on the lightest sky. The three rim
  accents are load-bearing for the silhouette read, not decoration.
- **Hook.** The grove that aged to black for a hundred years; it does not hurry.

### 4. yukitake — snow-bowed bamboo spirit  (KIND: bowed-snow-stalk)
- **Thesis.** A winter bamboo spirit — a culm bowed under heavy SNOW, its gentle spirit-face peeking
  from the bend. The most distinct PALETTE in the set: white-cold dominant, green only as accent.
- **Palette lane (snow-laden white-blue — held FAR from any roster green).** snow-white `(232,238,244)`
  base / blue-shadow `(150,176,200)` snow shade; **frost-green culm accent** `(120,178,150)` (the ONLY
  green, used small and COLD); **snow-blue glow** `(170,212,236)`; icicle-cyan `(198,232,242)`; ink
  `(28,34,40)`; sheen `(248,252,255)`. Reads as a WINTER boss first.
- **Silhouette KIND.** BOWED-SNOW-STALK — the only non-vertical posture in the set: a pale shaft with a
  heavy snow-loaf cap, a chubby spirit-face (closed gentle eyes, rosy cold cheeks) nestled where the
  bow happens, icicle drips at the leaf-tips.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The snow-laden culm is the pillar: a STRAIGHT pale culm shaft with snow-cap
  ridges per node = repeat band; gap-edge cap = the heavy snow-loaf + icicle fringe. The snow-loaf cap
  IS the gap-edge cap.
- **AD HARD CONSTRAINT (baked in).** Draw the SHAFT dead STRAIGHT and bow ONLY at the CAP — the entire
  bow lives in the snow-loaf cap so the repeating shaft stays mirror-clean and tileable. The bowed read
  comes from the cap's tilt + snow mass, NOT from curving the body (a curved shaft would break the
  pillar repeat). Green stays a small cold accent only — never the body.
- **Hook.** It has stood all winter; the weight that should snap it only makes it bow and watch you.

### 5. kaguya — moon-child in the split culm  (KIND: split-stalk-with-inner-figure)
- **Thesis.** KAGUYA-HIME at the moment of finding — a pale celestial child curled GLOWING inside a
  split, luminous bamboo stalk. The light-in-the-grove boss: serene, radiant, otherworldly.
- **Palette lane (pearl culm + pale gold-white MOON-glow).** pearl-culm `(214,228,210)` base, pushed
  COOLER/PALER than spec so its value sits clearly LIGHTER than Take-Ryu's saturated jade / pale-jade
  `(150,188,158)` shade; **moon-glow accent** `(244,238,196)` — a pale GOLD-WHITE, a NEW glow hue,
  distinct from Kitsune mint and Yurei cyan; cool moon-blue rim `(192,206,232)`; child-pale skin
  `(238,232,224)`; gold hair-tie `(224,196,120)`; ink `(30,32,34)`; sheen `(252,250,236)`.
- **Silhouette KIND.** SPLIT-STALK-WITH-INNER-FIGURE — a STRAIGHT symmetric pale culm split open down
  a vesica/almond aperture revealing a small curled glowing moon-child within, light bleeding from the
  split into the gap. Totally unlike a free-standing creature.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The luminous culm is the pillar: glowing pale node-segments = repeat band;
  gap-edge cap = the split-open glowing aperture (figure tucked at the gap) radiating into the gap. The
  glow makes a striking gap-edge; symmetric stalk — clean bottom-rooted mirror.
- **AD HARD CONSTRAINT (baked in).** The vesica/almond SPLIT + its glow must be the DOMINANT 32px read
  — a luminous aperture bleeding light into the gap, not a creature in a tube. Hold the outer culm
  clearly STRAIGHT and SYMMETRIC (NO coil — this is the anti-Take-Ryu pin) and keep the pearl base
  cooler/paler so its value reads lighter than Take-Ryu's jade. The moon-glow must be FLAT STEPPED
  concentric rings (NO gradient, NO soft raster halo). Keep the gold as a SOFT STEPPED HALO — the
  deliberate foil to Madake's hard metallic gold-leaf cracks — so the two gold accents never collide.
- **Hook.** The grove found a moon inside it; the light is calm and the light is not from here.

## DISTINCTNESS MATRIX (all 5 differ on palette family AND silhouette KIND)

| # | Boss | Palette family (dominant + accent) | Silhouette KIND |
|---|------|-----------------------------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Take-Ryu | saturated-COOL jade + lacquer-red | node-serpent-coil |
| 2 | Madake-Oni | warm STRAW/gold + hard-metallic gold-leaf (tiny ember eye) | ogre-with-club |
| 3 | Kurochiku-Nushi | near-BLACK violet + violet-white sheen + lacquer-red/gold | slim-dark-noble-stack |
| 4 | Yukitake | snow-WHITE/blue + snow-blue glow (green = cold accent only) | bowed-snow-stalk |
| 5 | Kaguya | pearl-PALE + pale gold-white moon-glow (soft stepped) | split-stalk-with-inner-figure |

Five distinct palette families (saturated-jade / warm-straw / dark-violet / snow-white / pearl-pale)
× five distinct silhouette KINDs (coil / ogre+club / noble-stack / bowed-stalk / split-stalk) — no two
share either axis. The two gold accents (2, 5) are deliberately split hard-metallic vs. soft-stepped;
the two glows (4, 5) are split snow-blue vs. moon-gold; both held clear of Yurei cyan and Kitsune mint.
