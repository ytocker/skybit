# Shishi-Kadomatsu — AD critique round 2 (the pose rebuild)

VERDICT: ITERATE (recovery confirmed — no longer RE-ROLL territory)

The pose rebuild worked where round 1 failed: the winged-medallion is gone, and in COLOR at 32px (day +
night) this now reads as a planted, crowned, green-culm creature — a real low-wide body with green
leg-posts + a forward cream crown + indigo brow-cord + base-anchored plum/straw. Cut-discs are the
brightest values (round-1 condition met). The structure is genuinely present in color → ITERATE, not RE-ROLL.

**Weakest = the BLACKOUT (the acceptance gate).** Stripped to black it's a rounded DOME / temple-bell
with a bright square window — the "medallion/blob" failure in a new costume. The four must-sees:
1. Low wide body — FAIL (taller-than-wide, domed; crown's round top dominates).
2. FOUR distinct leg-posts + front/back gap — FAIL (legs fuse into one skirt with a single notch → reads
   as one mass).
3. Leg node-rings — not assessable in black (legs not separated).
4. Crown outer edge stays bright-cream scalloped — FAIL (rim goes solid black; only the interior
   face-window is bright → "dark dome with a glowing hole," brightness in exactly the wrong place).

## Round-3 punch list (final — win the BLACKOUT first, then protect color)
1. **Make the crown RIM survive blackout:** 1px ink GAP between adjacent outer cut-discs (scallop the
   OUTER contour) + hold a bright-cream 1px lip on the outermost ring so the crown reads as a notched
   scalloped arc, not a solid dome cap. Highest-value fix.
2. **Carve four legs into the black skirt:** punch THREE sky-colored negative-space slots through the
   bottom (outer-L, center front/back, outer-R) so blackout shows FOUR separate black posts. Negative
   space — not value — survives blackout.
3. **Lower + widen the body block:** drop the crown ~2-3px to sit forward/low (not a high halo); extend
   the horizontal raft wider than the crown so the black shape is wider-than-tall (komainu). Crown ≤45% mass.
4. **Kill the bright center face-window in blackout:** close the face value down, OR make the crown rim
   brighter than the face so the eye reads the RING, not a central jewel (signature = ring of cut-tips).
5. Re-confirm at least one node-ring collar steps each leg-post in the COLOR chips once separated.
6. Lowest priority: bring the recessed back leg-pair up ~10-15% value so they contribute to the black skirt.

Acceptance (unchanged): judge the BLACKOUT first — four separate posts under a low wide body + a
scalloped bright crown. If those are present in black, it ships.
