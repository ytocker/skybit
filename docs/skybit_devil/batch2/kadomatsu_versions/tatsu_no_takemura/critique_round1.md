# Tatsu-no-Takemura — AD critique round 1

VERDICT: ITERATE

Strong promising round 1 — the coil reads as a clean winding S at every scale (blackout PASSES cleanly)
and is genuinely distinct. Fails the HARD GATE on two counts: the body doesn't reliably read as BAMBOO
(node-collar rings appear only ~3× in the upper-mid coil; lower half + neck are smooth tube → a green
caterpillar at 32px), and the reared HEAD reads as a closed cream EYE/disc with a pupil-dot, not an open
diagonal-cut MAW — and at 32px the brightest cream is at the FOOT, not the head (inverts the brief).

KEEP: the generous 2.5-loop vertical S + framing (don't touch); blackout distinctness; thread-thin teal
whiskers (filament-only restraint); straw+plum+pine foot-anchor; hard-stepped bands / SS=6.

## Round-2 punch list (priority)
1. **Node-collar rings on EVERY coil-segment (GATE):** tile the two-ring madake collar at a regular
   rhythm down the WHOLE body (~every half-diameter), INCLUDING around the outside of each bend (bends
   are currently bald). Add the 1–2 small cut-nubs at the coil-overlap crossings (currently none) to
   seed cream rhythm. Belly-rings are what make it bamboo-as-scales, not a snake.
2. **Rebuild the head as the diagonal-cut CREAM MAW, brightest value:** open sogi-cut culm-end (steep
   slant, `CUT_HI` sheen ring-wall + lightened `CAVITY` as the open mouth). Kill the central pupil-dot
   (reads as a closed eye / Kappa-saucer). It must be the single brightest value + clearly an open mouth.
3. **Fix value hierarchy so the HEAD wins at 32px, not the foot:** lower/shrink the foot blessing-face,
   push the head-maw cream up → 32px read = "winding S capped by ONE bright cut-disc at the TOP." Night
   chip especially fails (head ≈ body value, disappears).
4. **Add the missing jade-teal DORSAL-fin filament** tracing the outer spine of the top loop (thread-thin,
   off the body fill).
5. Tidy the mid-coil teal blob at the upper bend (thickest teal mark, competes with whiskers) → resolve
   into a cream cut-nub or thin to filament.
6. Polish: even the neck taper into the maw (a culm doesn't neck down).

Land 1+2+3 and it clears the gate — the coil is good enough that round 2 should be close to ship.
