CULL: kadomatsu-torii, kazari-no-yama, shishi-kadomatsu, tatsu-no-takemura, moso-no-taisho

# Kadomatsu brood — AD brainstorm-critique (8 → 5)

**North Star, restated as the gate I judged against:** *"MAKE IT RELIABLE TO BAMBOO. IT IS
A BOSS BAMBOO PLANT."* Bamboo-plant reliability is a HARD GATE, not a tiebreaker. Bound /
fresh-cut culm material + cream diagonal-cut mouths must DOMINATE the read at a glance and
survive at 32px. "Epic/mythic" is flavour on top — a direction that risks reading as a
generic creature/object *wearing* green is cut or hard-tightened.

I diverge from the GD's suggested cull on one structural point and it matters: the GD kept
**three beasts (Hooh, Shishi, Tatsu)** + 2 body. Under the North Star that is the single
RISKIEST shape for this brood — Hooh and Shishi are the two weakest on bamboo-reliability of
the whole eight (a bird and a lion are the two forms most likely to read as "animal that
happens to be green"). The parent itself (Kadomatsu-Shin) is a pure BODY-is-bamboo plant;
the brood is faithful to it when it leans body-forward. So I keep **4 body + 1 beast**, and
the one beast I keep (Tatsu) is the *most* bamboo-reliable beast in the set because its body
is LITERALLY a stacked culm. I keep Shishi over Hooh only by converting its mane from "fur"
to a radial sunburst of cut-discs — which turns the riskiest beast into the set's biggest
cut-mouth-signature payoff. Details below.

## RANKING (best → worst on the bamboo gate, then epic payoff)

### KEEP

**1. moso-no-taisho — KEEP (rank 1).** The purest bamboo read of all eight and the
deliberate anti-parent. One titanic culm, one colossal cut-disc — impossible to misread as
anything but bamboo at any scale, and the cut-mouth signature is at its largest here. The
cleanest pillar mirror in the brood. This is the spine of the set.
- *Tightened brief.* Silhouette KIND: SINGLE FAT MONOLITHIC COLUMN with one giant top
  slant-cut — a thick lone standing-stone of bamboo, wider than any single element in the
  brood. Epic hook: awe through scale + restraint (megalith). Held-apart accent: **kintsugi
  gold-vein lacquer** as THIN line-work tracing the node-collars only — NOT a glow fill, so
  it never collides with the pale-gold blessing glow at the foot. Pillar: it already IS the
  hero pillar; node-segments tile, the colossal cut = gap-cap, straw-cinched plum-cream foot
  = lower mirror. MUST-FIX to guarantee the read: the giant cut-disc must fill ~the top
  quarter and stay the BRIGHTEST value on the form so at 32px it collapses to one unmissable
  cream disc; give the column 5–6 BIG node collars (this is the only direction with room for
  node geometry to read large — use it), and keep the gold veins to ~2px hairlines so they
  don't boil into noise at 32px. Risk to police: a bare monolith can read inert — let the
  foot-kit (straw + plum + a single pine sprig) carry just enough base life without going
  top-heavy.

**2. kadomatsu-torii — KEEP (rank 2).** The cleanest mirror after Moso and the only
negative-space / doorway silhouette in the brood or the siblings. Reads as bound-bamboo
architecture, not a building skinned green, because the members are obviously stepped-green
culm bundles pinned by four cream cut-discs.
- *Tightened brief.* Silhouette KIND: TALL ⛩-FRAME GATE — two vertical culm-bundle uprights
  + a heavy horizontal lintel + a smaller tie-beam; the OPEN doorway between the legs is the
  read (the only negative-space form in the set). Epic hook: monumental architectural scale,
  the doorway faintly gold-lit. Held-apart accent: a single **vermilion shrine-cord /
  shimenawa-red band** across the top beam — the only high horizontal red sash (still
  base-balanced by straw + plum below so it never goes top-heavy). Pillar: one upright leg IS
  the pillar verbatim — bound-culm shaft tiles, the cut lintel-end = slant gap-cap, straw +
  plum = lower mirror. MUST-FIX: the four corner cut-discs (two upright tops + two lintel
  ends) are the signature — make them the brightest cream and large enough that at 32px the
  form reads as "open rectangle pinned by four cream nodes." Keep the legs visibly STRIPED
  bound-culm (vertical stepped bands + a straw lashing where lintel meets leg) so the frame
  never reads as plain timber. Watch its architectural-twin (the cut Pagoda) is gone, so push
  the OPENNESS hard — wide doorway, thin members — to bank the negative-space read.

**3. tatsu-no-takemura — KEEP (rank 3).** The only kinetic/coiling form, and the most
bamboo-reliable beast in the set: its body is one immense culm, node-collars reading as
serpent belly-scales — the botany and the anatomy fuse. Distinct from every sibling (nothing
coils; the spider is a star of straight legs, the literal opposite).
- *Tightened brief.* Silhouette KIND: VERTICAL COILING S/Ω-SERPENT — a thick ribboning
  culm-body looping up the frame, head reared at the top, a continuous winding tube. Epic
  hook: motion + length, the dragon of prosperity climbing the gate. Held-apart accent:
  **jade-teal whisker / dorsal-fin glint** held DARKER + COOLER than CULM, as tiny
  filament-only accents on whiskers + a thin fin-fringe — kept off the Haedung jade lane by
  being thread-thin accent, never a body fill. MUST-FIX: the reared HEAD must be the hero
  cream cut-disc maw (cavity = open mouth) and stay the brightest value; the coil-body must
  show clear node-collar rings at every segment so it reads as bamboo-as-scales, not a plain
  green tube — at 32px the silhouette must read as a winding S with one bright cut-disc head,
  so keep the coil to 2–3 generous loops (not a tight spaghetti that blurs to a blob). Add
  1–2 small cut-nubs where coils overlap to seed the cream rhythm.

**4. kazari-no-yama — KEEP (rank 4).** Added back over the GD's cut. On the bamboo gate this
is actually one of the MOST reliable directions — it is literally a fat festival mound of
bound cut culms, so it can't read as anything but bamboo, and it carries the densest
constellation of cut-discs of any direction. It buys the set its "sheer mass / abundance"
epic register, which Moso (restraint) and Torii (architecture) don't cover.
- *Tightened brief.* Silhouette KIND: FAT TRIANGULAR MOUND-PYRE — a broad-based PEAKED pile
  of dozens of discrete bound culms, hard culm-tips bristling the upper slopes, triple straw
  bands cinching the tiers. Epic hook: the most culms of any direction — a mountain of
  offerings. Held-apart accent: **mikan-orange daidai + rice-ear gold** tucked in the
  lashings — the only warm-orange in the brood. Pillar: one tier-culm IS the pillar; tiered
  straw bands = repeat collars, an apex cut-disc = gap-cap, the broad lashed foot = lower
  mirror. **MUST-FIX (the only real watch in the set) — separate it HARD from the Takenoko
  sibling cone:** Takenoko is ONE smooth tapering husk-SHELL (continuous plates); Kazari must
  be a PACKED CLUSTER of many DISCRETE culms with a bristling, broken upper edge and a
  constellation of cream cut-discs (one big apex hero + a graded scatter down the flanks).
  The blackout silhouette must read jagged/many-tipped, never a smooth solid triangle. The
  triple horizontal straw bands + the cream-disc constellation are what kill the cone twin —
  make both unmistakable.

**5. shishi-kadomatsu — KEEP (rank 5), conditional on the mane rebuild.** The only
horizontal / quadruped silhouette in the brood or the siblings, so it earns its slot on
distinctness alone, and it gives the set its single creature-centrality entry. It is also the
direction I'm watching hardest on the gate — a round mane + four legs reads LION first. I keep
it only because the fix turns its biggest liability into the set's biggest signature win.
- *Tightened brief.* Silhouette KIND: SQUAT CROUCHED QUADRUPED with a round CUT-DISC mane —
  low, heavy, broad, four planted leg-bundles, a circular crown around a blessing-face. Epic
  hook: heraldic guardian power, coiled and grounded. Held-apart accent: **indigo-cobalt
  brow / mane-cord** — the only cool-blue accent, reads heraldic, never washes into the
  greens. Pillar: a foreleg-bundle culm IS the pillar; a mane cut-disc = gap-cap, planted paw
  + straw anklet + plum = lower mirror. **MUST-FIX (the keep condition):** the mane must NOT
  read as fur. Build it as a RADIAL SUNBURST OF CREAM CUT-DISCS — a halo ring of bright
  diagonal-cut culm-ends around the face — so the mane IS the cut-mouth signature, not animal
  hair. The four legs must be obviously bound stepped-green culm bundles (node rings visible),
  and the body a lashed-culm mass, so the blackout silhouette reads "bundle-bodied beast
  crowned with a ring of cut-tips," not "lion." If at 32px the mane still reads as a furry
  blob rather than a ring of cream nubs, this direction fails the gate — flag it back to a
  re-roll rather than ship a lion in green. Lean the cut-disc crown bright and discrete.

### CUT

**3. take-hooh — CUT.** A phoenix is a bird-made-of-green: the weakest bamboo-reliability of
the would-be beasts, and the upswept twin tail-plumes brush the Kurochiku tengu's wing-lane
at blackout (two upper splayed masses behind an upright body). Its kinetic/soaring register
is already covered, more reliably, by **Tatsu** (the serpent), and its creature-centrality
slot is filled, more reliably, by **Shishi**. The sakura-rose accent also sits closest to the
vermilion plum of any proposed accent. Cut.

**7. kasane-no-toh (pagoda) — CUT.** The most "object, not plant/beast" direction — a
five-storey building reads as architecture skinned green, the exact failure the North Star
guards against — and it is the architectural twin of **Torii** (both tall stacked-culm
buildings with cut-disc eave/corner ends). Keep the more distinctive of the two: Torii wins on
the unique negative-space doorway read. The pagoda's "sacred verticality" is covered by Moso
(monolith) and Torii (gate). Cut.

**8. kagura-no-wa (fan-crown) — CUT.** Thinnest margin vs the Tsuchigumo sibling — both are
radial splays of straight culms from a bound hub; at blackout an upward fan and a downward
leg-star are uncomfortably close (a stranger could confuse the two). Its wide-upper splay also
competes with the Sasa sibling's wide read. The susuki-silver accent is the closest of all
proposed accents to the cream cut-discs themselves, which muddies rather than sharpens the
signature. Its "radiant majesty / gold-glow stage" is covered by Moso's restraint and Torii's
gold-lit doorway. Cut.

## SET-LEVEL DISTINCTNESS NOTE (the kept 5)

**Blackout test:** open gate-frame (Torii) / fat peaked many-tipped pyre (Kazari) / squat
horizontal quadruped + ring-crown (Shishi) / vertical coiling S-tube (Tatsu) / single fat
monolith (Moso) — five clearly different black shapes. None matches the parent's slim vertical
stepped trio (Moso = one fat shaft; Torii = a frame; Kazari = a wide peaked mass; Shishi =
horizontal; Tatsu = a coil). 

**Vs the 4 siblings:** none is a smooth single husk-cone (Takenoko), a wide-LOW drift-mound
(Sasa), a winged beaked crow (Tengu), or a downward radial leg-star (Tsuchigumo). The only two
watches, both handled in-brief: Kazari (peaked, many DISCRETE culms, bristling top + straw
bands) vs Takenoko's smooth single shell; and Shishi's quadruped is clear of all four (no
sibling is horizontal/four-legged) — its only risk is reading "lion," not "sibling," and that's
the mane-rebuild must-fix.

**Swap test:** no pair converts by recolour + repose + accessory — each needs its own builder
(open frame / packed peaked cone / crouched quadruped + radial crown / coiling tube / lone
monolith).

**Accent separation (all held-apart, none on a roster green lane — Cernun pine / Kappa
yellow-green / Kitsune mint / Haedung jade):** shrine-vermilion sash (Torii) / mikan-orange
(Kazari) / indigo-cobalt (Shishi) / jade-teal filament (Tatsu, accent-only, off the jade body
lane) / kintsugi-gold vein (Moso, line-work, kept distinct from the blessing-glow gold). Five
distinct held-apart accents; the two that sit nearest other set members are Tatsu's teal
(separated from Haedung jade by being thread-thin) and Moso's gold (separated from the foot
glow by being hard line-work, not a radial fill) — both flagged in their briefs.

**Cut-mouth signature across the 5:** Moso = one giant hero disc; Torii = four corner discs;
Kazari = a constellation; Tatsu = one reared-head maw + overlap nubs; Shishi = a radial
ring-crown of discs. Every direction keeps the cream cut-disc as its brightest value and can
pop it at 32px — the signature survives the cull intact, with good variety in how it's deployed.

**Centrality:** 4 body-is-bamboo (Moso, Torii, Kazari + the body-shaped Shishi-mane-of-culms)
/ 1 clear beast (Tatsu). This leans body-forward ON PURPOSE — the parent is a pure plant, and
body-forward is the safest posture for the North Star gate while Shishi + Tatsu still carry the
mythic-beast flavour the user asked for.
