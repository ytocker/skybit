# Kadomatsu brood — GD brainstorm (8 EPIC directions, to cull to 5)

A NEW sub-brood riffing on the user's favourite boss, **Kadomatsu-Shin** (the parent: a
plain stepped tri-culm diagonal-cut cluster bound at the waist, pine + plum + blessing-face
at the foot — `../bamboo_v2_versions/kadomatsu_shin/render_round_2.py`). The user wants 5
MORE like it but **MORE EPIC**, flavoured **"mythic bamboo-beast"** and spanning **BOTH**
centralities (pure bound-culm plant-clusters → plant-shaped mythic bamboo-beasts).

## NORTH STAR (police every direction, every round)
**"MAKE IT RELIABLE TO BAMBOO. IT IS A BOSS BAMBOO PLANT."** Every direction reads
*reliably as bamboo at a glance* before anything else. "Epic/mythic" is flavour layered ON
TOP of an unmistakable bamboo-plant read — never a replacement. If the bamboo read ever
loses to the beast read, the direction has failed. Bound/fresh-cut culm material DOMINATES
the silhouette in all 8 — these are bamboo plants that have grown grand, not beasts wearing
bamboo.

## INHERITED DNA — every direction MUST keep (exact values from the parent renderer)
- **Diagonal-cut hollow-ring CREAM mouths** — the signature focal. Bright cream ring-wall
  `CUT_CREAM (224,214,170)` + sheen `CUT_HI (244,238,206)` (BRIGHTEST value on the form), a
  SMALL lightened cavity `CAVITY (118,128,92)`, steep sogi slant, the cut-oval enlarged so at
  true 32px it collapses to a BRIGHT CREAM DISC (not a hole). Every concept features cut-mouths
  prominently, with a clear front/back dominance hierarchy (one hero cut + smaller cream nubs).
- **Bound / fresh-cut culm material as the dominant body mass** — madake node-segment culms
  (two-ring collars + paired branch-stub nubs), warm fresh-culm green `CULM (124,188,104)` with
  **4–6 HARD STEPPED value bands, NO gradients** (`CULM_D 74,138,72` / `CULM_DD 50,102,56` /
  `CULM_HI 172,216,130` / `CULM_HOT 208,232,158`), warm rim-keyline `CULM_RIM (158,206,116)`
  riding the long edges (the night-hold), back-mass a value-step darker `CULM_BACK (58,116,62)`.
- **Auspicious New-Year kit, base-anchored (NEVER top-heavy):** woven straw bind `STRAW
  (206,176,104)` (a horizontal BIND, no protruding arms), pine needle-fans `PINE (58,104,62)`,
  vermilion plum `PLUM (216,80,60)`, and the SOLE radial accent — pale-gold blessing glow
  `GLOW (244,224,150)`. Hard ink keyline `INK (28,22,30)` + 1px grown outline. SS=6 → smoothscale.

## PIPELINE / pillar law (unchanged from the set)
REALISM-elevated, NOT chibi. Hard stepped bands. The signature must survive the downscale BLUR
at 32px on day AND night sky. **Pillar law:** a jointed culm IS the natural pillar — node-segment
= repeat band; shaft + a detachable **slant cut-cap gap-edge** (a fresh diagonal CUT mouth, not a
spear-point) + a bottom-rooted lower mirror (straw-bound plum-on-cream). Bottom-heavy, clean
top↔bottom mirror.

## DISTINCTNESS DOCTRINE (per `.claude/skills/distinct-design-variants/SKILL.md`)
Each direction is a UNIQUE bottom-up **silhouette KIND** — different as a solid black shape, its
own construction, not a recolour/re-pose/accessory-swap of another. All 8 must differ from:
- **the PARENT** (plain stepped tri-culm cluster — every direction goes BIGGER / structurally
  different, never just "the parent again");
- **each other** (8 distinct silhouette KINDs);
- **the 4 bamboo-v2 siblings** — Takenoko-Warashi (sheath-scale CONE-shoot), Sasa-Yuki-Onna
  (WIDE-LOW drift-mound), Kurochiku-Garasu-Tengu (WINGED beaked tengu), Take-Tsuchigumo (RADIAL
  multi-leg spider). No new direction may land on cone / wide-mound / winged-bird / radial-spider.

They all share the warm fresh-cut-bamboo palette family, so the SET is differentiated by
**FORM/silhouette + one held-apart accent each** (kept clear of roster green lanes: Cernun pine /
Kappa yellow-green / Kitsune mint / Haedung jade). Centrality spread runs pure-plant → mythic-beast.

## THE 8 DIRECTIONS

### 1. KADOMATSU-TORII — the colossal living gate of stacked cut culms  (BODY-IS-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** Not three culms but a whole SHRINE-GATE grown from bound bamboo: two massive culm
  pillars and a great cross-lintel of bound culms, every member sheared on the sogi diagonal — the
  Toshigami's own gate, the place the year walks through.
- **Silhouette KIND.** TALL ⛩-FRAME GATE — two vertical culm-bundle uprights joined by a heavy
  horizontal lintel + a second smaller tie-beam (the torii ∏/⛩ shape). A NEGATIVE-SPACE form: the
  open doorway between the legs is the read. Nothing else in the set or the siblings is a frame /
  doorway silhouette.
- **Cut-mouths + bound culms at 32px.** The two upright tops AND both lintel-ends are bright cream
  cut-discs — four cream nodes pinning the corners of an open rectangle. Bound culm-bundle legs are
  obvious vertical stripes of stepped green; straw lashings where lintel meets legs.
- **Epic/mythic hook.** Architectural scale — it reads as a monumental gate, not a tabletop sprig.
  The doorway glows faintly with the gold blessing.
- **Held-apart accent.** A single **vermilion lintel-wrap / shrine-cord (shimenawa-red) band**
  across the top beam — the only direction with a horizontal red sash high on the form (still
  base-balanced by straw+plum below).
- **Pillar note.** One upright leg IS the pillar verbatim — bound-culm shaft tiles, the cut lintel-
  end is the slant gap-cap, straw-lashing + plum the lower mirror. The cleanest mirror after parent.
- **Thumb.**  ` ╒══◗══╕ `  /  ` ◖█◗  ◖█◗ ` (two cut-topped legs under a cut-ended beam)

### 2. KAZARI-NO-YAMA — the offering-mound pyre of bundled cut culms  (BODY-IS-BAMBOO)

  fat festival mound, all mouths fanning UP and OUT like a bristling cream-tipped pyre — abundance

- **Silhouette KIND.** FAT TRIANGULAR MOUND-PYRE — a broad-based, peaked CONE-of-many (squat, wide
  at the foot, many discrete culm-tips bristling the upper slopes). Distinct from sibling Takenoko's
  smooth single sheath-CONE (that's one tapering plate-shell; this is a packed cluster of dozens of
  separate culms) and from Sasa's LOW wide drift-mound (this is TALL and peaked, all hard culm-tips).
- **Cut-mouths + bound culms at 32px.** A whole CONSTELLATION of cream cut-discs studding the cone —
  one big hero disc at the apex, a graded scatter of smaller cream nubs down the flanks. The mound
  body is unmistakably many bound green culms (vertical stepped stripes), triple straw bands cinching

- **Epic/mythic hook.** Sheer mass — a mountain of offerings, the most culms of any direction.
- **Held-apart accent.** **Mikan-orange daidai fruit + rice-ear gold** tucked in the lashings (the
  New-Year bitter-orange) — a warm orange the other directions don't carry.
- **Pillar note.** One tier-culm of the mound IS the pillar; tiered straw bands = repeat collars, an

### 3. TAKE-HOOH — the phoenix risen from the cut grove  (PLANT-SHAPED MYTHIC BEAST)

### 6. MOSO-NO-TAISHO — the great single-culm monolith with one colossal maw  (BODY-IS-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** One titanic moso culm — the biggest bamboo on earth — stood alone and sheared with a

### 7. KASANE-NO-TOH — the multi-tier pagoda of bound culms  (BODY-IS-BAMBOO)

## SELF-AUDIT (distinctness tests on the SET of 8)
- **Blackout test:** gate-frame (1) / fat peaked pyre (2) / S-curve+up-plumes (3) / crouched quadruped+
  round mane (4) / coiling S-tube (5) / one fat monolith (6) / stepped flared-eave tiers (7) / wide
  upward fan (8) — eight clearly different black shapes. None matches parent's slender stepped trio.
- **Vs siblings:** none is a smooth single cone (Takenoko), a low wide drift-mound (Sasa), a winged
  beaked crow (Tengu), or a downward radial leg-star (Tsuchigumo). Closest watches: #2 pyre vs Takenoko
  cone (split: many discrete culms + peaked vs one smooth plate-shell) and #8 fan vs Tsuchigumo radial
  (split: upward flat crown over bound base vs downward crawling fanged star).
- **Swap test:** no pair converts by recolour+repose+accessory — each needs its own builder (frame /
  packed cone / S-neck+plumes / quadruped+mane / coiling tube / monolith / tiered eaves / radiating fan).
- **Accent spread (all held-apart):** shrine-red sash / mikan-orange / sakura-rose / indigo-cobalt /
  jade-teal filament / kintsugi-gold vein / verdigris-teal / susuki-silver — eight different held-apart
  accents, none on a roster green lane.
- **Bamboo-forward gate:** all 8 keep bound/fresh-cut culm as the dominant mass + prominent cream cut-
  mouths; the mythic flavour (gate/pyre/phoenix/lion/serpent/monolith/pagoda/crown) rides ON TOP.

## SUGGESTED CULL TO 5 (AD's call)
Spread = keep BOTH centralities + a range of scale-reads. A defensible 5: **1 Torii** (architectural
gate, cleanest mirror), **3 Take-Hooh** (the soaring beast), **4 Shishi** (the heraldic quadruped),
**5 Tatsu** (the kinetic serpent), **6 Moso-Taisho** (the monolithic anti-parent). That gives gate /
phoenix / lion / serpent / monolith — 2 body + 3 beast, every silhouette distinct, the widest mythic
spread. Likeliest cuts: **2 Kazari** (closest to Takenoko cone + reads like a busier parent), **7
Pagoda** (most "object" not "plant/beast", architectural-twin of Torii), **8 Kagura-fan** (thinnest
margin vs the Tsuchigumo radial). AD to confirm/adjust.
