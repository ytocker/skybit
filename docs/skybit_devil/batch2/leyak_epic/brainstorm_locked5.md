# Leyak-epic — LOCKED 5 (per-concept loop briefs)

Lineage anchor: shipped **Leyak** — bodiless floating head whose downward trailing stream IS
the pillar. These 5 are distinct NEW bosses sharing that DNA loosely. Elevated house style:
chibi + scary-CUTE + flat triad (dark-core → flat-fill → top-left rim-sheen) + 1–2px ink
keyline `(28,22,30)` + 1px grown outline; procedural-only, no gradients/PNGs. Push EPIC via
bigger render scale, SS=5–6, more geometry, richer triad, stronger `make_glow_surface` glow.
Each pillar grounded in the creature's own forms; clean top↔bottom mirror; no top-heavy cap.

Folder per concept: `docs/skybit_devil/batch2/leyak_epic/<slug>/round_N.png` +
`critique_roundN.md` + `render_<slug>.py`. (Leyak lineage → renderers MAY import `game.draw`/
`game.config`; run with `PYTHONPATH=/home/user/skybit SDL_VIDEODRIVER=dummy`.)

---

## 1. krasue — cool lantern-skull stringing glowing gut-orbs
- **Thesis:** a sleepy floating skull-lantern that dangles its own viscera as a string of
  glowing marsh-orbs; the orb-string IS the pillar.
- **Palette (re-specced cool body / warm-glow contained):** body cool dusk-mauve base
  `(150,128,150)` / shade `(96,78,104)` / lilac rim-sheen `(214,200,220)`; firefly-gold
  `(255,224,128)` ONLY inside 5–6 discrete orbs (body cool, glow warm — opens a clean lane vs
  shipped Ifra's coral body).
- **Silhouette:** round soft-glowing head up top, a beaded vertical string of luminous orbs
  trailing straight down (orb-on-string KIND).
- **Grounded pillar:** orb-string shaft = evenly spaced glowing gut-orbs on a thin sinew;
  gap-edge cap = a single cracked bottom lantern-orb (its own form). Symmetric on-axis.
- **Elevated detail:** thin sinew threading, inner orb glow lobes, faint membrane veining on
  the head; warm glow strictly inside orbs.
- **Watch:** keep body cool — gold must not bleed onto flesh; orbs evenly tileable.

## 2. tzitzimitl — eclipse star-skull with a gold corona + doom-banners
- **Thesis:** a pocket eclipse-omen — a star-skull blazing a too-big gold corona, dropping a
  fall of doom-banners that form the pillar.
- **Palette:** midnight-INDIGO night-cloth body (blue-leaning, NOT violet — clear of Necrarch);
  bone face; gold corona `(255,214,110)` as the single brightest warm focal.
- **Silhouette:** radial starburst corona around a small skull head = unmistakable wide radial
  top (radial star-corona KIND).
- **Grounded pillar:** banner-fall shaft = 2–3 hanging doom-banner panels per repeat (indigo
  cloth + gold glyph trim); gap-edge cap = a star-disk medallion sized ~shaft+30% (no top-heavy
  medallion). On-axis.
- **Elevated detail:** corona = 8 rays max, long/short alternating, clean readable starburst;
  gold glyph stitching on banners; subtle bone cracks on skull.
- **Watch:** corona dominant warm mass; keep indigo blue-leaning; banners tileable.

## 3. umibozu — abyss sea-monk jelly-dome + tentacle curtain
- **Thesis:** a serene chubby deep-sea monk head the color of the abyss, dragging a glowing
  tentacle curtain that becomes the pillar.
- **Palette:** abyss teal-black body (cool, distinct from Tzitzimitl's indigo); warm-amber
  biolum as SMALL DOTS only (never a body wash — different glow geometry from Tzitzimitl's
  corona).
- **Silhouette:** smooth large rounded jelly-dome head, calm closed/heavy eyes (jelly-dome
  KIND).
- **Grounded pillar:** tentacle-curtain shaft = 6 tentacles max forming a clean tileable
  curtain with amber biolum dots; gap-edge cap = a modest bell-jelly (~shaft+30%). On-axis.
- **Elevated detail:** one hard rim-sheen lobe on the pate (procedural, no soft gradient);
  tentacle sucker-dots; faint inner dome translucency band.
- **Watch:** amber stays as dots; dome cap modest; calm not grim (scary-cute).

## 4. mokoi — flat painted plank-mask + bark-art strip
- **Thesis:** a flat painted plank-mask blinking big target-eyes, sliding down a strip of
  glowing bark-art that is the pillar.
- **Palette:** charcoal ground dominant `(46,42,48)`; twin-OCHRE accents `(206,150,72)` /
  `(170,108,52)`; pipeclay-white `(238,232,220)` dot-pattern as the protected hue-blind tell;
  ember glow confined to the cap only.
- **Silhouette:** flat graphic rectangular-ish plank face with concentric ring-eyes (flat
  graphic plank KIND — the FLAT one; no 3D shading, keep flat).
- **Grounded pillar:** bark-ribbon shaft = 1 pipeclay dot-band + 1 ochre hatch-band per repeat;
  gap-edge cap = a totem-plaque with ember glow confined to the cap. On-axis.
- **Elevated detail:** crisp concentric ring-eyes, dense clean dot-rows, hatch linework — detail
  via graphic pattern density, NOT shading.
- **Watch:** charcoal dominant mass; protect pipeclay dot tell; stay FLAT.

## 5. nukekubi — court-lady head, flame-collar, charm-cord
- **Thesis:** a prim court-lady's detached head, hair perfectly pinned, drifting down a string
  of paper charms with a polite flame where her neck used to be.
- **Palette (face mitigation HARD-pinned vs Leyak):** warm powder face `(236,212,196)`, face
  mass ≤25% of head; lacquer-black top-knot `(34,28,38)` + gold kanzashi pins `(228,184,84)`
  DOMINANT; coral-RED flame collar `(232,108,64)` (deepened off Ifra's coral). Black/gold crown
  + collar carry the 32px read, not the face.
- **Silhouette:** ornate coiffed head, tall lacquer top-knot with radiating gold pins, glowing
  collar beneath (ornate coiffed-head KIND).
- **Grounded pillar:** charm-cord shaft = one o-fuda paper charm + one bead-knot per repeat;
  gap-edge cap = a tassel + warding-bell on-axis.
- **Elevated detail:** fanned gold kanzashi pins, lacquer sheen lobe on the top-knot, o-fuda
  glyph marks, lobed flame collar.
- **Watch:** demure half-lid eyes + small bow-mouth carry scary-cute (NO Leyak grin, NO ash
  tone); top-knot+collar dominate, face stays minor.
