# Tzitzimitl — Leyak-epic — ROUND 1 critique

Strong first pass. The hero panel reads instantly as an EPIC eclipse-omen: a gold star-corona is the dominant warm focal, the bone star-skull is seated cleanly in the center, and the indigo banner-fall mirrors into a believable pillar. It already sits next to the Skybit roster as a member of the same family — flat triad, ink keyline, no gradient mush. It does NOT clear the bar yet: two flagged issues are real, the 32px chip is corona-legible but skull-illegible, and the medallion-vs-shaft balance drifts toward top-heavy at native scale. One iteration should land it.

## Ruling on the GD's two flags

1. **Banner panels read alternating-light — CONFIRMED, fix it.** In panel (b) the stacked doom-banner panels alternate indigo / pale-cream / indigo down the fall. That cream panel is not a triad value step, it reads as a SECOND material and breaks the "one cloth, repeating" pillar logic — at scroll speed it will strobe light/dark as it tiles, which is exactly the noise we avoid. The banners must be ONE indigo cloth value with only the dark-core→fill→top-left-sheen triad separating panels, plus the gold glyph trim. Kill the cream fill.

2. **Corona rays cropped at the zoom-box right edge — CONFIRMED, fix it.** In panel (b)'s 2x CAP-band zoom the right-hand corona ray is clipped by the frame. This is a render/compositing crop, not a design intent — it makes the medallion look lopsided in the one view meant to audit the cap. Pad the zoom box (or shrink the medallion to fit) so all 8 rays are fully inside every frame.

## Ranked actionable notes (this IS the round-2 brief)

1. **32px skull is mush — raise the face read (highest priority).** On both DAY and NIGHT chips the corona reads great but the bone skull collapses into a single light blob — no eye-sockets, no grin. At true 32px the silhouette is "gold star," which is good, but the scary-CUTE FACE is the charm and it's gone. Fix: at chip scale drop the skull to the absolute minimum legible mark — two dark socket dots + one dark grin slot, oversized and high-contrast against the bone, ink-keyline thickened to 2px on the chip render. The grayscale value panel confirms it: the skull and the inner-disk read at nearly the same value. Push the bone face ~15-20% lighter than the indigo disk so the face pops as a distinct value island.

2. **Medallion is creeping top-heavy — re-check the +30% rule.** In the 1x native (82px) strip the star-disk cap reads larger than shaft+30%; the rays push the effective cap diameter well past the banner width, so the mirror looks medallion-on-a-stick rather than banner-fall-with-a-cap. Either (a) measure cap from banner width and hold the disk+rays envelope to shaft+30% total, or (b) shorten the corona rays on the PILLAR medallion specifically (the hero corona can stay full-glory; the pillar cap is allowed a tamer ray set). Confirm the top↔bottom mirror is identical and on-axis.

3. **Indigo is blue-leaning — GOOD, hold the line, but watch the rim-sheen.** Body hue is correctly blue-indigo and clear of Necrarch's violet — keep it. One risk: the top-left rim-sheen on the indigo disk leans slightly cool-violet in the 4x blow-up. Pin the sheen to a blue-white, not a lilac, so a colorblind/violet drift can't pull the whole body toward Necrarch's lane.

4. **Corona ray rhythm — tighten the long/short alternation.** Brief calls for 8 rays, long/short alternating. In the hero panel the alternation reads, but a couple of short rays are nearly as long as the long ones, softening the starburst snap. Make the short rays decisively ~60-65% of the long-ray length so the silhouette has crisp 8-point rhythm even at 32px.

5. **Gold corona is the dominant warm focal — CONFIRMED good.** Don't add competing warm mass. The gold glyph trim on the banners is the right amount of secondary warmth; keep it thin so the corona stays the single hero warm.

6. **Polish — corona ray tips.** A few ray tips in the hero panel have a slightly soft/rounded ink terminus. For the AAA-casual snap, keep ray tips as crisp triangular points with a clean 1px outline; rounded tips at 32px read as a blurry star.

## What to KEEP
- Eclipse star-skull + gold corona concept and the radial-top silhouette KIND — distinct from source Leyak (head-on-streamer) and from every roster member.
- Banner-fall AS the pillar (not a bolted-on prop). Right call, grounded in the creature's own forms.
- Flat triad + ink keyline + procedural discipline — no gradient creep. House-style fidelity is solid.
- Indigo blue-leaning body, gold-dominant warm. Both pins respected.
