# AD brainstorm-critique — Leyak-epic cull (8 → 5)

VERDICT: ITERATE

## Locked 5
Krasue (lantern-gut orb-string) · Tzitzimitl (eclipse star-skull) · Umibozu (abyss
jelly-dome) · Mokoi (painted plank-mask) · Nukekubi (court-head + flame-collar).

**Most important cross-set fix:** warm-glow crowding at the bottom of the value range —
Krasue's amber-coral body + firefly-gold glow sits on top of shipped Ifra (coral body +
saffron flame). Krasue MUST be re-specced to a COOL dusk-mauve/indigo lantern-flesh with the
gold glow contained strictly inside the orbs (body cool, glow warm) to open a clean lane.

## Cuts
- **Baykok** — bone-creature traffic jam (Draugr frost-bone, Catrina ash-bone, Mariachi
  warm-bone, Necrarch bone-parchment already ship); hollow-rib head leans grim vs scary-cute.
  Bank the spine-arrow pillar idea for a future skeleton slot.
- **Pontianak** — Yurei-adjacent at gameplay scale (sorrowful drooping white-ish floating
  female head, soft downward fall) even with petals; maroon/rose brushes Leyak + Karakasa;
  least-epic top-silhouette (soft bud reads small).
- **Chonchon** — russet+slate-blue is the riskiest palette (slate-blue → Raijin storm-grey /
  Yurei cool-blue; chartreuse bead → Pazul venom-lime); talon-braid pillar weakest-grounded;
  wide wing-span head competes with Kitsune fan + Catrina brim for the wide-top read.

## Clash rulings among the kept 5
- **Krasue vs Ifra (must fix):** re-spec Krasue body COOL (dusk-mauve/grey-indigo), gold glow
  ONLY inside the orbs. Sharpens the "cool head / warm guts" story.
- **Nukekubi vs Leyak (mandatory pin):** porcelain face is the one adjacency — face base warm
  powder `(236,212,196)`, face mass ≤25% of head; lacquer-black top-knot + gold pins dominate;
  coral-flame collar + black/gold crown carry the 32px read, not the face.
- **Nukekubi coral-flame vs Ifra coral:** deepen collar toward coral-RED `(232,108,64)`.
- **Mokoi ochre vs Mariachi/Karakasa:** no clash IF charcoal stays the dominant mass and the
  pipeclay-white dot-pattern is preserved as the hue-blind tell; ember glow confined to cap.
- **Tzitzimitl vs Umibozu (both dark-cool):** keep Tzitzimitl's gold corona the dominant warm
  focal (radial rays) and Umibozu's amber as small biolum DOTS only — different glow geometry.

## Per-survivor locking briefs (re-specs to apply before per-concept loops)
- **Krasue:** body cool dusk-mauve `(150,128,150)` base / `(96,78,104)` shade / lilac sheen
  `(214,200,220)`; firefly-gold `(255,224,128)` contained inside 5–6 discrete orbs only;
  cracked bottom lantern-orb = gap-edge cap.
- **Tzitzimitl:** lock as-is. Corona = 8 rays max, long/short alternating, clearly readable
  starburst; gold corona = single brightest mass; banner-fall 2–3 panels per repeat; star-disk
  cap ~shaft+30% (no top-heavy medallion); indigo body blue-leaning night-cloth, NOT violet.
- **Umibozu:** lock as-is. Amber biolum = small DOTS only (never body wash); 6 tentacles max
  → clean tileable curtain; bell-jelly cap modest (~shaft+30%); pate-sheen = one hard rim-sheen
  lobe (procedural, no soft gradient).
- **Mokoi:** charcoal ground dominant; pipeclay-white dot-pattern protected hue-blind tell; big
  clean concentric ring-eyes; bark-ribbon shaft (1 dot-band + 1 hatch-band per repeat);
  totem-plaque cap with ember glow confined to cap; keep it FLAT (no 3D shading).
- **Nukekubi:** face mitigation HARD-pinned (warm powder face ≤25% head; lacquer-black top-knot
  `(34,28,38)` + gold kanzashi `(228,184,84)` dominant); collar coral-RED `(232,108,64)`;
  charm-cord pillar = one o-fuda + one bead-knot per repeat, tassel + warding-bell cap on-axis;
  demure half-lid eyes + bow-mouth carry scary-cute (no Leyak grin / no ash tone).

## Set coverage
Five distinct silhouette KINDs (radial star-corona / smooth jelly-dome / flat graphic plank /
ornate coiffed-head / glowing orb-on-string); none a Leyak comet; no collision with
Yurei/Necrarch/Karakasa/Hollow. Bodies span cool-indigo / cool-teal / charcoal / black-gold /
(re-specced) cool-mauve. Four warm glows tolerated because each glow GEOMETRY differs — do NOT
add a sixth warm-glow concept.

Why ITERATE: two mandatory palette re-specs (Krasue cool body, Nukekubi face/collar) must fold
into the locked briefs first; a final GD pass applies these and re-confirms the cool/warm body
spread, then the set is locked.
