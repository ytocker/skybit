## FIX (final round)
- **Eyes must read SLEEPY, not startled (priority 1 — the gate).** Right now the
  ink lid is a thin bar riding on top of a tall fully-open socket; the eye is an
  upright oval. Sleepy = the LID is the dominant shape and the socket is a WIDE,
  SHORT slot. Drop the ink upper-lid down so it covers the top ~55-60% of the
  socket, and flatten the remaining gold glow into a wide low crescent (wider than
  it is tall). The half-closed read is a SHAPE, and that shape must be "heavy lid
  over a thin smile-curve of glow," not "small lid over a big round eye."
- **Day-1x head identity (priority 2).** On the day chip the head is a near-blank
  pale blob — the grille and lids vanish and only the two dark sockets survive, so
  it drifts toward generic-skull. The night/dusk chips are fine; the day chip is
  the failing one. Nudge the skull base ~1 value-step cooler/darker so the ink
  features (lid slots + grille) hold a hair more contrast against the bright blue
  day sky, and thicken the central grille post by ~1px so the jack-o'-lantern tell
  reads as a tell, not a smudge, at true day-1x.
