# LEYAK-LINEAGE EPIC BOSSES — GD BRAINSTORM (8 directions, no full render)

Brief: 5 NEW "epic boss" concepts INSPIRED BY shipped **Leyak** (Bali
flying-head-trailing-entrails devil; ash-white + hot-pink; the trailing
viscera-RIBBON IS the pillar). Propose ~8 distinct NEW bosses for the AD to
cull to 5.

## LINEAGE DNA (what every direction inherits, loosely)
Leyak's shared genes — NOT a repaint:
- **Bodiless / floating / detached-head OR streaming form.** No torso-with-legs.
- **A downward STREAM that IS the pillar** — the trailing form doubles as the
  repeatable shaft, with a glowing gap-edge cap derived from the creature's own
  anatomy (Leyak: rib-bead viscera-ribbon shaft + heart-pendant lantern cap).
- **Clean top↔bottom mirror** — stream is naturally vertical + symmetric.
- Accessibility tell: a SHAPE/value read that survives at 32px hue-blind.

## ELEVATED HOUSE STYLE (epic, per batch-2 header)
Inherited unchanged: CHIBI proportions (big head, compact/no body); FLAT
saturated fills + hard 1–2px ink keylines; form via the **dark-core →
flat-fill → top-left rim-sheen TRIAD**; 1px alpha-grown outline for silhouette
POP; supersample → smoothscale; scary-CUTE, never grim. PUSHED to EPIC:
bigger/more imposing 32px read, more geometric facet detail, richer triad
shading bands, stronger glow/menace halo — but STILL flat fills, hard
keylines, procedural-only (no gradients, no PNGs).

## HARD DISTINCTNESS PINS
- Must NOT collide with source **Leyak** (ash-white + hot-pink, viscera-lobe
  ribbon) — different folklore, silhouette, anatomy, palette each.
- Must NOT collide with shipped floaters/undead: **Yurei** (legless
  hair-curtain ghost, blue-cyan), **Necrarch** (floating crowned lich, violet),
  **Karakasa** (floating one-eyed umbrella, oxblood), **The Hollow** (batch-1
  faceless hooded floater).
- AVOID owned hue families: violet (Necrarch), ice-cyan (Draugr/Yurei), the
  green-bands (Cernun pine / Tlaloc grey-jade / Kappa yellow-green / Jiangshi
  jade-corpse), storm-grey (Raijin), mint + blue-cyan glows (Kitsune/Yurei),
  ash-white+hot-pink (Leyak), oxblood+amber (Karakasa).
- Each a unique BOTTOM-UP design: different creature-of, different streaming
  organ, different shaft motif. No "Leyak with X swapped."

## DIRECTION 1 — KRASUE, the lantern-gut bog-wisp
- **Thesis.** Thailand's Krasue reframed not as Leyak's grin-skull but as a
  CUTE GLOWING SKULL-EMBER whose dangling guts are a string of marsh
  will-o'-LANTERN orbs — a swamp-firefly head that lights its own way down.
- **32px read.** A small ROUND glowing head (lit from WITHIN, like a paper
  lantern — not bone-white like Leyak) over a vertical STRING OF GLOW-BEAD
  ORBS descending, each orb a little smaller — a luminous bead-chain, NOT
  Leyak's lobed viscera-ribbon. Reads as "glowing pearl on a light-string."
- **Construction.** Chibi translucent-look head: a near-spherical face with a
  soft sleepy half-lid eye and a tiny round o-mouth, rendered as flat triad but
  with a bright inner-core fill so it reads back-lit; two short trailing
  tendril-wisps off the jaw. BELOW: 5–6 round GLOW-ORBS strung on a thin sinew,
  each orb its own triad (dark rim → warm fill → top-left pin-sheen) with a
  pulse-glow halo cached behind. EPIC push: the orb halos stack into a vertical
  bloom-column of light + faceted "ember crackle" lines inside each orb.
- **Prop → pillar (grounded in OWN forms).** The GLOW-ORB STRING is the pillar:
  the strung marsh-lantern beads = repeatable shaft (each orb a band, sinew the
  through-line); the LARGEST bottom lantern-orb, cracked open and pouring
  ember-light, = glowing gap-edge cap. NOT a generic column — it's the
  creature's own light-string. Symmetric on-axis, clean mirror.
- **Palette (distinct).** SWAMP-EMBER: bog-teal-green is owned, so go WARM
  AMBER-CORAL not green — lantern-flesh `(244,176,96)` base · burnt-amber shade
  `(190,116,52)` · firefly-gold inner glow `(255,224,128)` (the warm bog-wisp
  glow — a YELLOW-GOLD, distinct from all cool glows) · dusk-rose accent
  `(214,120,108)` · deep-ember core `(150,70,40)` · ink `(30,22,18)` · sheen
  `(255,238,196)`. Reads "lit-from-within ember," nothing like Leyak's flat
  ash-white. Warm-gold glow is unclaimed (Raijin gold is a zag, not an orb).
- **Scary-cute-epic hook.** A sleepy little lantern-skull that strings its own
  guts into fairy-lights — adorable until you notice the bottom one is cracked
  and dripping fire.

## DIRECTION 2 — CHONCHON, the ear-winged flying head
- **Thesis.** Chile/Mapuche's Chonchon: a disembodied head that FLIES ON ITS
  OWN ENORMOUS EARS used as wings, talons tucked beneath. A bat-eared cute
  gremlin-head — the streaming form here is the TALON-TENDRILS dangling below.
- **32px read.** A head DOMINATED by two huge swept-back EAR-WINGS spread wide
  (a moth/bat read no Leyak has — Leyak is a tight comet, this is a WIDE
  wing-span head), with three thin TALON-CLAW tendrils trailing straight down.
  Wide-top, thin-trail silhouette — inverted-triangle, opposite of Leyak's blob.
- **Construction.** Chibi cross-eyed gremlin face, snub nose, snaggle-fang
  underbite, wide worried brows; the two EARS are big triad-lit membrane fans
  (faceted vein-ribs, hard panels) mid-flap; below the chin, three segmented
  BIRD-TALON tendrils hang and curl. EPIC push: ear membranes get layered
  facet-veins + a rim-sheen leading edge; talons get knuckle-joint banding.
- **Prop → pillar (grounded in OWN forms).** The TALON-TENDRIL braid is the
  pillar: the three segmented bird-talons twist into a scaly knuckle-banded
  shaft = repeatable body (each talon-segment a band); a clenched TALON-FIST
  gripping a stolen glowing soul-bead = gap-edge cap. The shaft is the
  creature's own claws, not a column. Symmetric braid on-axis, clean mirror.
- **Palette (distinct).** DUSK-PLUM-PINK is too near Leyak/violet, so go
  EARTHY RUSSET + SLATE: warm taupe-russet hide `(196,128,96)` base · deep
  cocoa shade `(126,76,52)` · slate-blue ear-membrane `(96,128,150)` accent
  (the cool counterweight — a steel-blue, NOT ice-cyan, NOT storm-grey body) ·
  bone-cream fang/claw `(228,216,190)` · sickly-chartreuse soul-bead glow
  `(196,214,120)` (a yellow-lime glow, unclaimed) · ink `(28,22,24)` · sheen
  `(236,200,170)`. Russet + slate-blue ears reads wholly apart from ash-white.
- **Scary-cute-epic hook.** A grumpy little head flapping away on its own ears,
  dragging a fistful of talons — looks like it's late for something and stole
  your soul on the way out.

## DIRECTION 3 — TZITZIMITL, the eclipse star-skull
- **Thesis.** Aztec Tzitzimitl: a skeletal STAR-being that descends during
  eclipses. A floating skull-FACE wreathed in a spiked star-corona, trailing a
  fall of star-banner streamers — a celestial omen, not a swamp horror.
- **32px read.** A skull-face centered in a radiating SPIKED STAR-CORONA (a
  sun-burst/starburst halo ring — wholly unlike Leyak's comet-tail), with a
  vertical fall of pennant/banner STREAMERS below. Reads as "skull inside a
  star," a radial silhouette vs Leyak's linear one.
- **Construction.** Chibi square-ish skull with hollow star-shaped eye-sockets
  and a stitched grin; a CORONA of 8–10 hard triad-lit spike-rays fanning all
  around the head (each ray a flat dark-core→fill→sheen sliver); below the jaw,
  3 stacked TASSELED BANNER-STREAMERS (Aztec paper-banner feel) descending and
  narrowing. EPIC push: the corona rays alternate long/short for a
  sun-disk-glyph density; banners get carved glyph-notch edges.
- **Prop → pillar (grounded in OWN forms).** The BANNER-STREAMER fall is the
  pillar: the stacked tasseled star-banners = repeatable shaft (each banner
  panel a band, knotted at the seams); a small SPIKED STAR-DISK medallion
  (a mini of the head's corona) blazing = gap-edge cap. Shaft is the creature's
  own banners + corona-echo cap. Symmetric on-axis, clean mirror.
- **Palette (distinct).** MIDNIGHT-INDIGO + BONE + BLOOD-CORAL: deep
  indigo-navy banner `(48,52,92)` base (a dark blue — NOT violet, NOT
  storm-grey; reads as night-sky cloth) · near-black indigo shade `(28,30,58)` ·
  bone-white skull `(224,216,200)` accent · molten-gold corona-ray `(244,196,84)`
  (gold rays as the focal blaze) · blood-coral tassel `(206,72,68)` · ink
  `(20,20,34)` · sheen `(248,242,224)`. Indigo + gold corona is a distinct
  cosmic family — no roster owns deep indigo cloth.
- **Scary-cute-epic hook.** A pocket eclipse-omen — a tiny star-skull blazing a
  corona two sizes too big for it, solemnly dropping doom-banners. Ominous, but
  its star-eyes are crossed.

## DIRECTION 4 — BAYKOK, the arrow-rib quiver-ghoul
- **Thesis.** Ojibwe Baykok: a near-skeletal flying hunter with glowing eyes
  who paralyzes warriors with invisible arrows. Reframed as a floating
  RIBCAGE-HEAD ghoul whose dangling spine doubles as a feathered ARROW-SHAFT
  quiver — the stream is bone + fletching, not viscera.
- **32px read.** A gaunt skull-on-ribcage (head fused to an open birdcage
  RIBCAGE, no limbs) over a single long dangling SPINE that ends in an
  ARROWHEAD, fletched with feathers along its length. A "rib-lantern over a
  spine-arrow" read — bony + linear, but the ribcage makes it read taller/wider
  at top than Leyak's smooth head.
- **Construction.** Chibi skull with sunken pin-prick GLOW eyes and a thin
  grimace; below it the head melts into a hollow RIBCAGE (4–5 hard triad-lit rib
  hoops, dark-core interior so it reads as a cage); a single vertebral SPINE
  drops from the cage. EPIC push: the ribs get a translucent-cage interior glow
  + the eye-glow is a hot pin-light halo; spine vertebrae are crisply faceted.
- **Prop → pillar (grounded in OWN forms).** The SPINE-ARROW is the pillar: the
  fletched vertebral spine = repeatable shaft (each vertebra a bone-band, paired
  feather-fletch barbs at intervals); a barbed bone ARROWHEAD nocked with a
  drawn-glow bowstring = gap-edge cap that "fires" into the gap. Shaft is the
  creature's own spine-arrow, not a column. Symmetric on-axis, clean mirror.
- **Palette (distinct).** GREY-BONE is risky vs Raijin storm-grey + Draugr
  ice-bone, so push WARM-DESATURATED BONE + a HOT-RED hunter glow: weathered
  greige-bone `(202,190,166)` base (warm taupe-bone, not cool/frost) · umber
  bone-shade `(140,122,92)` · charcoal cage-interior `(58,52,50)` · HOT
  hunter-red eye + bowstring glow `(228,72,56)` (a pure scarlet glow — distinct
  from Leyak's pink and Raijin's gold) · feather-grey fletch `(176,170,158)` ·
  ink `(26,24,22)` · sheen `(238,230,210)`. Warm-bone + scarlet glow is
  unclaimed; the open ribcage geometry is the hue-blind tell.
- **Scary-cute-epic hook.** A little hollow-rib hunter whose own spine is the
  arrow he's about to loose — pin-light eyes, a wobbly determined frown, way too
  small for the giant arrowhead he's aiming downward.

## DIRECTION 5 — PONTIANAK, the banana-bloom shroud-veil head
- **Thesis.** SE-Asian Pontianak: vengeful spirit tied to BANANA trees,
  white-dressed and long-haired. Reframed (distinct from Yurei's hair-curtain
  and Leyak's gore) as a floating head wrapped in a flowering BANANA-BLOSSOM
  SHROUD whose petal-streamers peel downward — a bloom that wilts into a pillar.
- **32px read.** A head cradled inside a teardrop BANANA-FLOWER BUD (overlapping
  petal-bracts hooding the face — distinct hood from The Hollow's empty cowl and
  Karakasa's umbrella), with peeling PETAL-STREAMERS and a ribbon of small
  banana-fruit "fingers" hanging below. A "blossom-pod head on a fruit-string."
- **Construction.** Chibi face peeking from inside layered petal-bracts (each
  bract a hard triad-lit teardrop, dark-core base → fill → top-left sheen tip);
  big sorrowful droop-eyes + a small pursed mouth (NOT Leyak's grin, NOT Yurei's
  hair); the bract-hood frames the face like a cobra-hood collar. EPIC push:
  petals layer in 3 overlapping ranks for depth + a faint bloom-glow at the
  flower's heart. NO long straight hair (Yurei-collision guard) — the hood is
  petals, not tresses.
- **Prop → pillar (grounded in OWN forms).** The PETAL/FRUIT-FINGER fall is the
  pillar: a stalk hung with paired banana-"finger" fruit + curling petal-bracts
  = repeatable shaft (each fruit-pair + petal-rank a band); the heavy purple
  BANANA-HEART bud (the flower's tip), split to glow, = gap-edge cap. Shaft is
  the creature's own bloom-stalk, not a column. Symmetric on-axis, clean mirror.
- **Palette (distinct).** Avoid violet body + ash-white. Go DEEP-MAROON BLOOM +
  PALE-JADE-CREAM petal interior + BUTTER-YELLOW fruit: maroon-bract `(146,52,68)`
  base (a wine-red, distinct from Karakasa oxblood by leaning crimson-rose and
  pairing with cream not bamboo) · deep-burgundy shade `(96,30,46)` · pale
  jade-cream petal-inner `(224,224,196)` accent · butter-banana fruit-yellow
  `(232,196,96)` (warm fruit accent) · soft bloom-pink glow `(238,156,168)` (a
  PALE rose glow, lighter/cooler than Leyak's hot-pink) · ink `(28,20,24)` ·
  sheen `(244,210,210)`. Maroon + cream + butter-yellow is a fresh family.
- **Scary-cute-epic hook.** A mournful flower-spirit blushing out of a banana
  bloom, dropping a wilting stalk of ghost-fruit — pretty and sad until the
  banana-heart at the bottom splits open like a fanged mouth.

## DIRECTION 6 — UMIBOZU, the ink-tide jelly-bell head
- **Thesis.** Japan's Umibozu — the giant black sea-monk that swells from calm
  water (jellyfish-origin lore). Reframed as a floating smooth DOME-HEAD (monk's
  bald pate) with a streaming JELLYFISH-TENTACLE curtain — a sea-spirit, all
  smooth dome + drifting tendrils, no bone at all.
- **32px read.** A big smooth round DOME-HEAD (glossy bald monk pate, the
  dominant smooth-orb read) with two huge round void-eyes, over a curtain of
  WAVY JELLY-TENTACLES streaming straight down (a soft tendril-fall, distinct
  from Leyak's beaded ribbon and Yurei's flat hair-panels). "Bell-dome over a
  tentacle veil."
- **Construction.** Chibi glossy dome-head (a single big triad sphere: dark-core
  bottom → fill → bold top-left pate-sheen, leaning into the EPIC rim-sheen);
  two huge round white-rimmed void eyes + a tiny calm o-mouth (eerily serene);
  below, 6–8 wavy TENTACLE-RIBBONS with frilled edges, each a triad-lit ribbon
  tapering to a curl. EPIC push: faint bioluminescent rim-dots along the
  tentacle frills + a heavier pate sheen to sell the wet dome.
- **Prop → pillar (grounded in OWN forms).** The TENTACLE-CURTAIN braids into
  the pillar: the bundled jelly-tentacles twist into a frilled ribbon-shaft =
  repeatable body (each tentacle-frill node a band, oral-arm ruffles at
  intervals); a heavy glowing BELL-JELLY medallion (a small echo of the
  dome-head, lit) = gap-edge cap pulsing at the gap. Shaft is the creature's own
  oral arms, not a column. Symmetric on-axis, clean mirror.
- **Palette (distinct).** DEEP-SEA: avoid storm-grey/ice-cyan. Go INKY
  TEAL-BLACK body + WARM-AMBER bioluminescence: abyss teal-black dome
  `(36,58,66)` base (very dark blue-teal — reads near-black at 32px, distinct
  from Raijin mid storm-grey) · black-teal shade `(20,36,42)` · slate-teal
  tentacle `(72,108,116)` accent · WARM bioluminescent amber glow `(248,200,120)`
  (the wet-glow dots — a warm gold-amber, opposite of cool jelly clichés) ·
  pale sea-foam rim `(180,210,206)` · ink `(14,24,28)` · sheen `(150,196,196)`.
  Near-black teal body with amber glow-dots is wholly unowned.
- **Scary-cute-epic hook.** A serene chubby sea-monk head the color of the deep,
  dragging a glowing-tipped tentacle curtain — placid and round and cute until
  the calm o-mouth is the last thing you see before the tide.

## DIRECTION 7 — MOKOI, the shadow-bark mask spirit
- **Thesis.** Yolngu (Arnhem Land) Mokoi: a dark night-spirit that moves unseen.
  Reframed as a floating BARK-PAINTING MASK (a flat carved/painted spirit-face
  in the X-ray/cross-hatch tradition) trailing a stream of dot-painted
  bark-ribbon — a flat woodcut mask, no skull, no gore.
- **32px read.** A tall narrow OVAL MASK-FACE (a flat painted plank-mask, very
  graphic — distinct from every round skull/dome in the set) with concentric
  ring-eyes and a hatched mouth, over a vertical DOT-PAINTED BARK-RIBBON
  streamer. "Painted plank-face on a patterned ribbon" — the most GRAPHIC,
  flattest read of the eight.
- **Construction.** Chibi elongated oval mask: flat plank-face with bold
  cross-hatch and DOT-PATTERN bands (Aboriginal art grammar) as hard triad
  shapes; two concentric ring-eyes (target-eyes) + a short hatched grin; tiny
  notch ears. Almost no 3D form — it's a painted board, so the triad reads as
  PAINTED BANDS more than carved relief. EPIC push: layered dot-band density +
  a single rim-sheen edge so the flat plank still pops off the sky.
- **Prop → pillar (grounded in OWN forms).** The BARK-RIBBON streamer is the
  pillar: a hanging strip of painted bark = repeatable shaft (each
  dot-pattern/cross-hatch panel a band, lashed at the seams); a small carved
  spirit-FIGURE plaque (a mini mokoi totem) glowing with ochre firelight =
  gap-edge cap. Shaft is the creature's own bark-painting, not a column.
  Symmetric on-axis, clean mirror.
- **Palette (distinct).** EARTH-OCHRE + CHARCOAL + PIPECLAY-WHITE: charcoal-black
  plank `(46,40,38)` base (a warm near-black ground) · pure black shade
  `(24,20,20)` · red-ochre band `(178,86,52)` accent · yellow-ochre band
  `(214,164,76)` accent · pipeclay-white dots `(236,228,210)` (the dot pattern —
  the hue-blind tell) · warm ember-orange glow `(244,150,72)` at the totem cap ·
  ink `(20,16,16)` · sheen `(200,180,156)`. Charcoal + twin-ochre + pipeclay
  dots is a distinct earth family — no roster owns ochre dot-painting.
- **Scary-cute-epic hook.** A flat little painted mask-board blinking its
  target-eyes, sliding down a strip of glowing bark-art — looks like a museum
  exhibit until it tilts its head and the hatched mouth grins wider.

## DIRECTION 8 — NUKEKUBI, the neck-flame paper-charm head
- **Thesis.** Japan's Nukekubi: a head that detaches at night, leaving the body
  behind, lit by a flame at the severed neck. Reframed as a tidy floating
  COURT-LADY head whose severed neck flares into a downward FLAME-COLLAR, with a
  trailing TASSEL-CORD of warding paper-charms — refined and prim, not gory.
- **32px read.** A neat oval face with a high TOP-KNOT / kanzashi hair-pin crown
  (a distinct ornate top-shape — not Leyak's bare skull, not Yurei's loose
  hair), a flaring FLAME-COLLAR ruff at the severed neck, over a trailing
  TASSEL-CORD strung with paper o-fuda charms. "Coiffed head over a charm-cord."
- **Construction.** Chibi powder-pale court face, demure half-lid eyes, a small
  red bow-mouth, a single beauty-spot; an ornate lacquer TOP-KNOT with two
  kanzashi pins as triad-lit shapes; at the neck, a flaring COLLAR of hard
  flame-petals (dark-core → fill → sheen). EPIC push: the flame-collar gets a
  layered petal ruff + a contained glow-halo; the top-knot gets faceted lacquer
  sheen. Prim, almost elegant — the menace is the floating-detachment itself.
- **Prop → pillar (grounded in OWN forms).** The CHARM-CORD is the pillar: a
  braided silk cord strung with rectangular paper O-FUDA wards + bead-knots =
  repeatable shaft (each charm + knot a band); a heavy SILK TASSEL set with a
  glowing warding-bell = gap-edge cap chiming at the gap. Shaft is the
  creature's own charm-cord + tassel, not a column. Symmetric on-axis, clean
  mirror.
- **Palette (distinct).** PORCELAIN + LACQUER-BLACK + IMPERIAL-GOLD with a
  WARM-FLAME collar: porcelain-cream face `(238,226,212)` base — RISK: too near
  Leyak ash-white. MITIGATION: the face mass is SMALL; the dominant masses are
  LACQUER-BLACK top-knot `(34,28,38)` + GOLD kanzashi `(228,184,84)` + a
  CORAL-ORANGE flame-collar `(240,132,72)` (the focal warm ruff, distinct from
  Leyak's flat pink trail). cinnabar-red charm-strokes `(198,56,46)` accent ·
  silk-cord olive-tan `(168,146,96)` · ink `(24,20,26)` · sheen `(252,244,232)`.
  Lacquer-black + gold + coral-flame collar reads wholly apart from ash+pink.
  (If AD finds the pale face still drifts toward Leyak, swap face base toward a
  warmer powder `(236,212,196)` and lean harder on the black/gold mass.)
- **Scary-cute-epic hook.** A prim little court-lady head, hair perfectly
  pinned, drifting down on a string of paper charms with a polite flame burning
  where her neck used to be — she'd be lovely if she weren't, you know, a head.

## CROSS-CHECK (GD self-distinctness pass, NOT a critique)
- **Silhouette KINDs, all distinct at 32px:** (1) glowing orb on a bead-string;
  (2) wide ear-wing head + talon-braid (inverted-triangle); (3) skull inside a
  radial star-corona + banner-fall; (4) hollow ribcage-head over a spine-arrow;
  (5) blossom-pod hooded head over a fruit-string; (6) smooth jelly dome-head
  over a tentacle-curtain; (7) flat graphic painted plank-mask over a bark-ribbon;
  (8) coiffed court-head with flame-collar over a charm-cord. Eight different
  "what is it" reads — none is Leyak's smooth-skull + lobed-viscera comet.
- **Palette lanes, all distinct:** (1) warm amber-coral + firefly-gold glow;
  (2) russet + slate-blue ears + chartreuse glow; (3) midnight-indigo + bone +
  gold corona; (4) warm-bone + scarlet glow; (5) maroon-bloom + cream +
  butter-yellow; (6) abyss teal-black + warm-amber biolum; (7) charcoal +
  twin-ochre + pipeclay dots; (8) lacquer-black + gold + coral-flame.
  None reuses violet / ice-cyan / the green-bands / storm-grey / mint+blue-cyan
  glows / ash-white+hot-pink / oxblood+amber. Glow hues spread: firefly-gold,
  chartreuse-lime, gold-corona, scarlet, pale-rose, warm-amber, ember-orange,
  coral-flame — eight readable glows, no two the same lane.
- **Pillar grounded in OWN forms, all confirmed:** (1) glow-orb light-string +

  umbrella/oxblood, or The Hollow's empty faceless cowl (all 8 have a visible
  face or mask). Source Leyak's ash-white + hot-pink + viscera-lobes is used by
