# Karakasa — Round 2 critique (art-director, final)

Round 2 nails it. Every priority-1 fix from round 1 landed, and the single
read the brief lives or dies on — "one-eyed umbrella ghost," not tent/mushroom
— is now unmistakable down to the 24px chip. This is the cleanest single-object
prop=pillar in the batch and it ships.

## Strongest / weakest
- **Strongest:** the canopy re-profile is the win. The tall conical wagasa
  silhouette with the top finial spike + scalloped rib-tipped flared rim reads
  PARASOL instantly; nothing mushroom/tent survives. The blown-up central amber
  eye is now the hero focal and carries all the scary-CUTE charm the round-1
  version lacked — at 32px your eye lands on the eye first, exactly right. The
  single central leg → geta is a clean vertical signature; the twig-stub arms
  recede so the "on ONE leg" read holds. Alternating oxblood/cream panels
  between radiating gold ribs give real value structure and the candy-stripe
  wagasa look the cream was missing in round 1. Tongue now tapers + flaps as a
  ribbon, not a slab. Pillar mirror is still the cleanest in either batch.
- **Weakest (all polish, none blocking):** the 32px-on-night chip is the
  softest read — oxblood canopy sinks into the dark blue and the silhouette
  leans almost entirely on the gold ribs + amber eye + pink leg to survive. It
  works as focal logic but the canopy EDGE nearly disappears. Minor.

## Per-aspect KEEP / FIX (FIX items are optional polish, not ship-blockers)
- **Silhouette/construction — KEEP all of it.** Canopy profile, finial, one leg,
  geta, twig-stubs: locked. Don't touch.
- **Eye/tongue — KEEP.** Eye size, amber iris on white, ink keyline, sheen dot,
  ribbon tongue: all on-target.
- **Color — KEEP** the oxblood/cream/ochre/amber/pink pins. FIX (optional): on
  the night biome, push the top-left rim-sheen `(220,128,108)` ~one step hotter
  along the upper canopy edge so the parasol's outer silhouette edge stays
  legible on dark-blue night sky — right now it's carried by the ribs alone.
- **Mirror/pillar — KEEP.** Shaft rib-banding repeats cleanly, canopy seats
  on-axis, finial points into the gap as the bloom detail. Exactly the bar.
- **Accessibility — PASS.** The read survives on shape (tall conical canopy +
  single eye + one leg) independent of hue; value contrast eye-to-canopy is
  strong. Good colorblind behavior.
