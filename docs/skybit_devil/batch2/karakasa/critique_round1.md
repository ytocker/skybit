VERDICT: ITERATE

# Karakasa — Round 1 critique (art-director)

Solid first pass on palette and triad lighting, but the **silhouette misses the
brief's single most important read**: this is a *parasol on ONE leg*, and round 1
reads as a striped CIRCUS TENT / mushroom on two arms. The canopy shape and the
arm/leg construction are the core fixes; everything else is polish.

## Strongest / weakest
- **Strongest:** the oxblood+ochre+amber palette is on-pin and pleasant; the
  tongue color is right; the triad (dark-core → flat-fill → top-left rim-sheen)
  is present on the canopy panels; the geta clog at the base is charming and
  clearly reads. The pillar mirror is genuinely the cleanest single-object
  mirror in the set — that promise from the brief is delivered.
- **Weakest:** the canopy is a low, wide, peaked TENT dome — not a paper
  parasol. Combined with two splayed stick-arms it reads "big top circus tent"
  or "toadstool," not "umbrella ghost." The one-eye is tiny and lost. At 32px
  (see insets) it collapses to an indistinct red mushroom cap — the umbrella
  identity is gone.

## Per-aspect KEEP / FIX

**Silhouette & construction (priority 1)**
- FIX — CANOPY PROFILE. Right now it's a squat tent dome (wide flat peak, low
  rise). A karakasa parasol canopy is TALLER, more conical/onion-domed, with a
  visible top FINIAL spike and a scalloped rib-tipped lower rim that flares
  slightly OUT (the classic wagasa flare). Raise the canopy height ~30%, narrow
  the base flare, add the top finial. The brief says "closed-ish paper umbrella
  standing vertically" — taller-than-wide, not wider-than-tall.
- FIX — ONE LEG, NOT TWO ARMS. The brief read is "umbrella-on-ONE-leg." The two
  big splayed stick-arms currently dominate the lower silhouette and create a
  symmetric two-legged tent-pole read. Drop the arms to TINY vestigial twig-stubs
  (or cut them — they're optional), and make the SINGLE central leg the
  unmistakable vertical spar down to the geta. The leg+geta is the signature, not
  a tripod of limbs.
- FIX — RIB STRUCTURE. The gold ribs read as a flat triangle/star overlay on a
  tent, not as radiating umbrella ribs. Make ribs radiate from the top finial
  down to each scalloped rim tip (a clear paper-parasol fan), and let alternating
  oxblood/cream panels sit BETWEEN ribs (you have cream in the palette but it's
  barely used — the panels should alternate oxblood and cream for the wagasa
  candy-stripe).

**The eye & tongue (priority 2)**
- FIX — THE EYE IS THE FACE. The single huge central eye is the creature's whole
  personality and it's currently a tiny dot lost in the canopy. Make it BIG —
  occupy ~40% of the canopy face width, centered where panels meet, amber iris on
  a clear white, a hard ink keyline, top-left sheen dot. This is the
  scary-CUTE hook; right now there's no cute.
- KEEP — tongue color and the lolling-below placement are right. FIX — taper it
  and add a slight curl/flap at the tip so it reads as a floppy tongue, not a
  pink rectangular bib. It currently reads as a slab.

**Color & value (priority 3)**
- KEEP — oxblood, shade, amber, tongue-pink are on-pin.
- FIX — use CREAM. The cream panel `(232,216,182)` is in the palette but the
  canopy is nearly mono-oxblood. Alternate oxblood/cream panels for value
  structure and the wagasa look — this also dramatically improves the 32px read.
- FIX — night-biome read. In the night inset the oxblood canopy sinks into the
  dark sky and the gold ribs + amber eye are the only things popping. That's
  acceptable focal logic, but push the rim-sheen `(220,128,108)` a touch hotter
  on the top-left canopy edge so the silhouette edge survives on dark blue.

**32px / 24px legibility (priority 1, tied to silhouette)**
- The whole fix above is in service of this: at 32px the current chip is an
  anonymous red blob. A taller conical canopy + one central leg + big single eye
  is what will make it read "umbrella ghost" at gameplay scale. Re-test the chip
  after the canopy/leg fix — that's the gate.

**Identity / sibling drift**
- KEEP — the chibi proportion intent and ink keylines match house grammar.
- FIX — currently closer to a generic tent/mushroom than to the tsukumogami
  object-spirit lane; the taller wagasa canopy + dominant single eye are what
  separate it from anything mushroom-ish and lock its one-of-a-kind "object that
  woke up" identity.

**Mirror / pillar**
- KEEP — excellent. Handle shaft repeats cleanly with rib banding; the canopy as
  detachable gap-cap is the cleanest mirror in the batch. When you raise the
  canopy height, just confirm the cap still seats on-axis with the new finial —
  the finial should point INTO the gap as a nice bloom detail.

## Prioritized punch list for round 2
1. Re-profile the canopy: taller conical/onion dome, top finial spike, scalloped
   rib-tipped rim with a slight outward flare — taller than wide. Kill the
   circus-tent read.
2. Make it ONE leg: reduce/cut the two stick-arms to vestigial stubs; the single
   central spar to the geta is the signature silhouette.
3. Blow up the single central eye to ~40% canopy width, amber iris + white +
   ink keyline + sheen dot — this is the scary-CUTE hook.
4. Alternate oxblood/cream panels between radiating gold ribs (use the cream
   that's currently sitting unused in the palette) for value + wagasa stripe.
5. Taper + curl the tongue tip so it flaps; lose the rectangular-slab read.
6. Re-render the 32px and 24px chips after 1–4 and confirm "umbrella ghost"
   survives — that is the ship gate.
7. Keep the pillar mirror exactly as is; just re-seat the new taller canopy cap
   on-axis with the finial dropping into the gap.

## References
