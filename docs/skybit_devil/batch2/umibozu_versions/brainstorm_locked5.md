# Umibozu-versions — LOCKED 5 (per-concept briefs)

Spin-off epic bosses off the shipped **Umibozu** (serene abyss sea-monk jelly-dome). Deep-sea /
oceanic-yokai lane; five disjoint creature KINDs, none the source's smooth jelly-dome-with-amber-
dots. Full rationale + cuts in `brainstorm_ad.md` (canonical).

**House style (elevated):** chibi + scary-CUTE + flat procedural triad (dark-core→flat-fill→
top-left rim-sheen) + 1–2px ink keyline + 1px outline; procedural-only; pushed EPIC at SS=5–6 →
smoothscale. Lineage renderer template: `docs/skybit_devil/batch2/leyak_epic/umibozu/render_umibozu.py`
(imports `game.draw` — run with `PYTHONPATH=/home/user/skybit`). Pillar grounded in each design
(2 body-as-pillar, 1 rib-cage, 2 grounded props); cap ~shaft+30%, on-axis, no top-heavy. Legible
at true 32px day+night.

**CROSS-SET PINS:** five disjoint KINDs (lure-on-a-stalk angler / nine-arm radial kraken / hung
whale rib-cage / horizontal clam-mouth / vertical spiral egg) + five separated lanes (oil-black+
coral / sunset-red+gold / cool sea-bone+amber / sea-rust+pearl+pale-green / teal-indigo+bone-
whorl+sour-green). Avoid: source jelly-dome, Tzitzimitl star-corona, Kappa yellow-green, Necrarch
violet, Draugr/Yurei ice-cyan, Kitsune mint. The four HARD re-specs below are pins, not options.

---

### 1. chochin-anko — abyssal anglerfish lure-fiend  (KIND: lure-on-a-stalk; ANCHOR, most epic)
- **Palette.** Oil-black body `(38,52,58)` / charcoal shade `(22,32,38)`; blood-CORAL belly+gum
  flush `(196,86,72)` (BELLY ONLY — never full-body, so it can't twin Akkorokamui's red);
  cold-steel sheen `(150,170,176)`; hot-white-peach lure glow `(255,224,168)` (sole brightest
  pip); ink `(20,26,30)`.
- **Pillar.** Illicium STALK body-as-pillar — segmented filament, knuckle-node per repeat,
  barbel-feelers; gap-cap = the glowing esca lure-bulb (~+30%) radiating into the gap.
- **Hook.** A perpetually-annoyed deep grump wagging a nightlight; cute underbite → endless
  needle-maw.
- **Re-spec.** None. Keep the body near-silhouette DARK so the single lure pip is the only focal.

### 2. akkorokamui — sunset-red kraken-deity  (KIND: nine-arm radial; the sole RED lane)
- **Palette.** Sunset-vermilion `(206,84,64)` / wine-oxblood shade `(132,42,46)`; warm-coral
  sheen `(238,150,120)`; cream sucker-rings `(228,210,184)`; divine-GOLD eye+sucker glow
  `(244,200,96)`; ink-beak `(30,20,22)`.
- **Pillar.** Single ARM tentacle-column body-as-pillar — tapering sucker-banded shaft (suckers
  = repeats); gap-cap = curled arm-TIP coil (~+30%) with a glow-sucker into the gap. Other arms
  stay in the head-mass.
- **Hook.** A benevolent one-eyed little sea-god, a bloom of friendly waving arms — that sink ships.
- **RE-SPEC (cross-set #1, important).** Arms read as 8–9 FAT, CURLING, TAPERING rays with
  sucker-dots + asymmetric curl — NOT thin straight spokes — and mass sits in the rounded
  mantle-head, so the 32px read is "octopus deity," clear of Tzitzimitl's rigid bone star-corona
  and Raijin's round drum-ring.

### 3. bake-kujira — skeletal ghost-whale  (KIND: hung rib-cage; vast leviathan)
- **Palette (RE-SPECCED).** COOL sea-rot teal-GREY dominant body value `(96,124,118)` (rib-gaps +
  membrane) over greyed bone `(160,170,158)` (cooler/greyer than warm calaca cream); drowned-grey
  shade `(118,130,124)`; pale sea-AMBER socket glow `(236,196,120)` (two large sockets, sole warm
  focal); ink-baleen `(28,30,30)`.
- **Pillar.** Rib-CAGE body-as-pillar — one curved rib-pair per repeat on a ghost-spine keel;
  gap-cap = blunt whale-SKULL nub (~+30%) dropping one socket-orb glow. Spine axis = clean mirror.
- **Hook.** A colossal sky-whale ghost, two big sad lantern-eyes, chibi-blunt and almost gentle —
  until you see it's only bones, hunting whalers.
- **RE-SPEC (skeleton-gate, important).** Body reads clearly COOL drowned sea-bone (teal-grey
  membrane dominant), NOT warm cream bone — keeps it off the Catrina/Mariachi/Necrarch warm-bone
  lane. 3 tiny ghost-bird companions for charm, small enough not to clutter the 32px read.

### 4. hamaguri-shinkirou — mirage clam, mouth of a drowned city  (KIND: horizontal clam-mouth)
- **Palette.** Sea-rust shell `(178,142,96)` / umber-shell shade `(120,86,52)`; pearl-cream sheen
  `(236,224,196)`; ghost PEARL-GREEN mirage glow `(170,222,186)` (the PALER/greyer green — pinned
  apart from Tehom); slate hinge-eyes `(60,72,74)`; ink `(28,26,24)`. Reads brown-warm, never teal.
- **Pillar.** Mirage-TOWER thread — exhaled drowned-city spire stacks into a translucent pagoda
  shaft (tier per repeat); gap-cap = single mirage-pagoda roof finial (~+30%) glowing at the gap.
- **Hook.** A serene old clam softly humming a whole ghost-city out of its mouth; the city is full
  of tiny drowned souls.
- **RE-SPEC.** Shell relief = bold RADIATING fan-ribs (distinct from Tehom's spiral whorl). Keep
  the body the ONLY HORIZONTAL-grin silhouette — the wide horizontal split is the whole
  distinctness; don't let the shell halves round up into a vertical blob.

### 5. tehom-no-tamago — abyssal leviathan-egg  (KIND: vertical spiral egg; cosmic outlier)
- **Palette.** Deep teal-indigo shell `(46,66,84)` (held BACK as backdrop) / near-black shade
  `(26,40,54)`; bone-cream WHORL ridge `(214,206,182)` (the bright structural read — eye lands on
  the spiral); abyss embryo-GREEN crack glow `(150,206,140)` (the DEEPER/SOURER green — pinned
  apart from Hamaguri); barnacle bone-grey `(170,172,164)`; ink `(20,28,36)`.
- **Pillar.** Birth-cord / kelp-tether — knotted cord shaft, barnacle-cluster per repeat; gap-cap
  = small cracked shell-shard egg-stone (~+30%) leaking one glow line into the gap.
- **Hook.** A cosmic deep-sea egg, one curious baby slit-eye blinking through the crack —
  impossibly cute, and what's coiled inside is the end of the world.
- **RE-SPEC.** Shell relief = a single continuous SPIRAL whorl (distinct from Hamaguri's fan-ribs);
  keep the dark shell value LOW so the bone whorl + green crack carry the read and it never twins
  the teal-black source Umibozu.
