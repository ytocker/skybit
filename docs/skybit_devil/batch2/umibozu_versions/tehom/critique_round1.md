# Tehom-no-Tamago — Round 1 critique (Umibozu-versions #5, abyssal leviathan-egg)

A strong, distinctive cosmic-outlier read with the bones of an epic here. The hero
silhouette is genuinely its own KIND — a vertical, dark, ovoid shell with a single
bone spiral and a curious slit-eye is unmistakable next to the four siblings and the
serene jelly-dome source. But Round 1 has not yet shipped the three things the brief
PINS hardest: the spiral does not read as a continuous logarithmic whorl, the
gap-cap egg-stone is a blob with an antenna instead of a cracked shard leaking a
crack-glow, and the shell value is not held low enough to keep the bone+green doing
the carrying at true 32px. All three are fixable in one round. Iterate.

## Strongest / weakest

- **Strongest:** the HERO concept and the slit-eye charm focal. At SS the curious
  baby eye peering through the crack lands exactly on the "impossibly cute / cosmic
  dread" hook. The vertical egg silhouette is the most original KIND in the set.
- **Weakest:** the gap-CAP egg-stone. At true scale (panel c / the 4x value blow-up)
  it is a soft pale blob with a thin straight green stalk — it reads as a lozenge with
  an antenna, NOT a cracked shell-shard leaking a crack. This is the single biggest
  miss and the GD flagged it correctly.

## Ruling on the GD's three flagged concerns — all THREE upheld

1. **Cap reads too rounded/blobby — UPHELD.** Confirmed. The cap is a smooth pale
   ovoid. An egg-stone that has CRACKED should have an angular, faceted, broken-shell
   silhouette, not a pebble. Right now it twins a generic blob and loses the egg story.

2. **Green "leak line" reads as an antenna — UPHELD, this is the worst offender.** The
   glow is a thin, straight, vertical line sitting ON TOP of / above the cap centroid.
   At 32px it reads as a mast/antenna, not as a crack leaking green INTO the gap. A
   crack glow must originate from a JAGGED split in the shell mass and bleed downward
   into the gap as a tapering, slightly irregular wedge of light — not a clean stick.

3. **Cap bone-whorl nearly vanishes at true scale — UPHELD.** Correct call. At 32px
   the cap's whorl is gone; the cap should NOT try to carry a whole spiral. Let the
   cap carry the CRACK + shard shape; reserve the spiral whorl as the HERO read only.

4. **Hero whorl could read as a more obviously continuous SPIRAL — UPHELD.** At SS the
   whorl currently reads as two or three separate crescent ribbons (a near-fan), not
   one unbroken logarithmic coil winding to a center. This is the RE-SPEC's core
   distinctness gate vs. Hamaguri's fan-ribs, and right now the two are too close to
   risk. Make it one continuous line that visibly spirals inward.

## RE-SPEC compliance check

- **Single continuous SPIRAL whorl (vs Hamaguri fan-ribs):** NOT yet met. Reads as
  segmented crescents. Must become one unbroken inward-winding coil — see directive 1.
- **Shell value held LOW as backdrop:** NOT met. The shell currently sits at a MID,
  fairly bright pale-green value across the whole body (especially the cap and the
  lower pillar tiles), so the bone whorl and green crack do not pop off it — they
  compete with it. The body needs to drop toward the dark teal-indigo `(46,66,84)` /
  near-black `(26,40,54)` so the bone-cream `(214,206,182)` whorl and the embryo-green
  crack are the only bright masses. Right now the whole egg glows; nothing leads.
- **Never twins the teal-black source Umibozu:** at risk BUT defensible once the shell
  goes dark — the spiral whorl + slit-eye + green crack are enough separation from the
  smooth amber-dotted jelly-dome. Keep the green SOUR/deep, not toward the source's
  warmth. No twin today because the body is too LIGHT; ironically, darkening it (the
  fix above) increases twin-risk, so lean on the bone spiral as the anti-twin.

## CROSS-SET compliance check

- **Embryo-green crack = the DEEPER/SOURER green (apart from Hamaguri pale pearl):**
  the hue family is right `(150,206,140)`, but because the whole shell is pale-green-ish
  the green does not read as a distinct SOURER pip — it blends. Darkening the shell
  fixes this automatically; verify against Hamaguri's `(170,222,186)` so Tehom stays the
  deeper, yellower-sour, less-grey green.
- **Curious baby slit-eye is the charm focal:** met at SS, working well. At 32px it
  shrinks — make sure it survives (directive 5).
- **Distinct from source + roster:** met on silhouette KIND.

## Pillar check

- Mirror is clean and bottom-rooted — good. The knotted birth-cord/kelp twin-braid
  shaft with a barnacle-cluster per knot is legible and on-theme.
- BUT the egg-stone cap inherits all the hero's problems (blob + antenna). The cap is
  also slightly oversized/bulbous in the 2x zoom — keep it ~shaft+30% and shard-shaped,
  not a second body. The "mirror axis green leak" currently doubles the antenna read on
  both gap edges — fix the crack-glow shape and both ends improve together.

## Iteration directives (most important first)

1. **Re-cut the cap as a CRACKED SHARD silhouette.** Replace the smooth ovoid with an
   angular broken-egg shape: 1–2 hard concave notches in the outline + a visible jagged
   split seam. At 32px the cap must read "broken shell," not "pebble." Keep it
   ~shaft+30%, on-axis.
2. **Make the glow a CRACK, not an antenna.** Kill the straight vertical stalk. Originate
   the green from the jagged split in the shard and bleed it DOWNWARD into the gap as a
   short, tapering, slightly irregular wedge/teardrop of light (wider at the crack,
   narrowing into the gap). No stick poking up; the light leaks out and DOWN.
3. **Rebuild the hero whorl as ONE continuous logarithmic spiral.** A single unbroken
   bone-cream line winding from the outer shell inward to a center point (1.5–2 turns).
   It must not break into separate crescents. This is the distinctness gate vs Hamaguri —
   if a viewer could call it "ribs," it has failed.
4. **Drop the shell value ~30–40% toward dark teal-indigo `(46,66,84)` / near-black
   `(26,40,54)`.** The body is a dark BACKDROP; the bone whorl, the slit-eye, and the
   sour-green crack must be the only bright masses. Today the whole egg is mid-value pale
   and nothing leads the eye. Recheck the 4x value blow-up: bone + green should be the
   sole high-value pips on a dark shell.
5. **Protect the slit-eye at 32px.** After darkening, confirm the curious baby eye still
   reads as the charm focal on both day and night chips — give it a touch more value
   contrast (dark sclera-slit on a bone-lit eyelid) so it survives smoothscale and never
   relies on the green hue alone.
6. **Verify green separation post-darken.** Side-by-side the 32px chip against Hamaguri's
   pearl-green so Tehom reads as the deeper/sourer green; nudge yellower/lower-grey if it
   drifts toward the pale pearl lane.
