# Umibozu-versions — GD BRAINSTORM (8 directions, no full render)

**Lineage anchor (shipped Umibozu, concept #3 of leyak_epic):** a serene chubby
deep-sea MONK head the color of the abyss — smooth large rounded jelly-DOME, calm
heavy-lidded/closed eyes, warm-AMBER biolum as SMALL DOTS only (never a body wash),
dragging a 6-tentacle glowing curtain that becomes the pillar; modest bell-jelly
gap-cap. Palette: abyss teal-black body (cool); amber dots are the sole warm focal.

**House style (inherited, ELEVATED):** CHIBI proportions + scary-CUTE menace; FLAT
saturated procedural triad **dark-core → flat-fill → top-left rim-sheen**; 1–2px ink
keyline `(28,22,30)` + 1px alpha-grown outline; supersample → smoothscale.
Procedural ONLY — no gradients, no PNGs. Pushed EPIC: bigger render scale, more
geometry, richer triad, stronger glow cache (`make_glow_surface`). Each boss HAS a
mirrorable pillar grounded in its own forms (body-as-pillar OK) with a clean top↔bottom
mirror and a modest, non-top-heavy gap-cap.

**Distinctness contract (per `distinct-design-variants`):** 8 genuinely NEW bosses,
each its own bottom-up creature KIND, folklore, anatomy, silhouette and palette — NOT
recolored Umibozus. They share Umibozu's DNA only *loosely* (deep-sea / oceanic-yokai
/ floating-bell / drowned / abyssal-spirit flavor). The set owns the **abyss-teal +
biolum lane**. NONE may collide with: shipped **Umibozu** (teal jelly-dome + amber
dots), **Kappa** (bright yellow-green water-imp), **Tzitzimitl** (midnight-indigo
star-skull sibling), **Necrarch** (royal-violet lich), **Draugr** (frost-bone +
ice-cyan jaw), **Yurei** (blue-cyan hitodama), **Kitsune** (mint foxfire), **Leyak**
(ash-white trailing-head), **Tlaloc** (grey slate-jade + coral). Hard avoids across the
8: no Kappa yellow-green, no Necrarch violet, no Draugr/Yurei ice-cyan / mint / blue-cyan
glows — pick warm-amber, sodium-orange, blood-coral, ghost-green-CHARTREUSE, pearl,
sulfur, sea-rust, bone-cream glows instead so the 8 don't read alike or like the source.

Folklore grounding (WebSearch): umi-nyobo (sea-wife, fish-wife of the umibozu) ·
bake-kujira (skeletal ghost-whale of revenge) · funayurei (drowned sailors demanding a
ladle to swamp ships) · isonade (hooked shark-fiend) · ningyo (claw-fingered mermaid) ·
akkorokamui (giant sunset-red octopus deity of Uchiura Bay) · anglerfish/siphonophore
naturals · abyss = Hebrew *tehom* / Babylonian *Tiamat* primordial-chaos egg.

Distinctness gate at 32px — the 8 silhouette KINDs are deliberately disjoint:
**1 saucer-bell** · **2 fish-wife portrait** · **3 hung whale-rib cage** · **4 lantern-pole crowd** ·
**5 lure-on-a-stalk** · **6 9-arm radial star** · **7 banded clam-shell mouth** · **8 spiral egg-shell**.
No two share a top read.

## 1. Kurage-no-Daibutsu — the drowned-temple bell-jelly Buddha
- **Thesis:** the Umibozu's sibling re-imagined as a SUNKEN BRONZE BELL that grew a
  serene Buddha face — a tolling temple-jelly that floats up from the drowned shrine.
- **32px read:** a wide flat-shouldered **SACRED-BELL / mokugyo SAUCER** dome (squared
  bell-lip flaring at the base, NOT Umibozu's smooth round pate) with a tiny ushnisha
  knob finial on top — bell-silhouette KIND, instantly not a jelly-dome.
- **Chibi+triad anatomy:** chubby cast-bell head, calm lowered Buddha eyes + faint
  half-smile; bell surface broken into hard triad-lit relief BANDS and lotus-petal
  panels (dark-core groove → verdigris fill → top-left bronze rim-sheen); a hanging
  clapper-tongue glows below the lip. *Elevated:* embossed sutra-glyph studs march the
  bands; one crisp bronze sheen lobe on the crown knob; a faint hairline crack lets the
  inner glow leak.
- **Pillar (prop, on-axis):** the bell-ROPE / striking-beam column — braided rope shaft
  with a wooden striker-log banded into each repeat; gap-cap = a small lotus-pad bell-boss
  (~shaft+30%) with a single glow stud. Clean vertical mirror.
- **Palette (verdigris-bronze, warm-rust focal — NOT abyss teal):** patina-verdigris
  bronze body `(74,116,104)` · deep oxide shade `(40,74,68)` · pale verdigris sheen
  `(150,196,180)` · **sodium-RUST glow** inside the clapper + crack `(228,150,84)` (sole
  warm focal — different glow geometry + hue from Umibozu's amber dots) · old-gold
  glyph-stud `(214,176,96)` · ink `(24,32,30)`. Reads as aged metal, not living jelly.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook:** a beatific drowned bell that *bongs* — adorable lowered eyes,
  but you fly through the rope of a thing big enough to be a temple.

## 2. Umi-Nyobo — the scaled sea-wife, the umibozu's drowned bride
- **Thesis:** the Umibozu's literal folklore WIFE — a melancholy fish-faced sea-woman,
  pearl-pale and barnacled, who keens for her monk; a PORTRAIT-bust boss, not a dome.
- **32px read:** an oval **veiled FACE-BUST** framed by a wide fan of drifting kelp-hair
  and a high scallop-shell collar — a portrait/coiffed-head KIND (kin to Nukekubi's
  ornate-head read but pearl-pale + finned, never a jelly blob).
- **Chibi+triad anatomy:** big sorrowful half-lid eyes, a small downturned shark-tooth
  mouth (cute-sad, not a grin), gill-slits on the cheeks; flat black kelp-hair curtains
  as hard triad panels; webbed/finned hands clasped under the chin; a scallop-shell
  collar fanning behind. *Elevated:* barnacle clusters + pearl studs along the collar
  rim, fin-ray hatching in the kelp, one pearl sheen lobe on the brow.
- **Pillar (grounded prop):** a hanging **kelp-strand + pearl-bead cord** column — twined
  kelp shaft with a clasped pearl every repeat; gap-cap = a single open scallop-shell
  cradling one big pearl (~shaft+30%). On-axis mirror.
- **Palette (drowned pearl + ink-kelp, ghost-CHARTREUSE glow — clear of Yurei blue-cyan):**
  drowned-pearl skin `(196,206,194)` · cold sea-grey shade `(132,150,148)` · ink-kelp
  hair `(30,44,46)` (near-black green-black) · **abyss-CHARTREUSE keening glow** in the
  gill-slits + eyes `(178,214,120)` (a yellow-green LIGHT glow — distinct from Kappa's
  yellow-green BODY and from any cyan/mint) · nacre-pink shell flush `(220,168,176)` ·
  ink `(26,30,32)`. Pale face-mass kept ≤25% so the kelp+collar carry the read.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook:** a beautiful-sad widow of the deep — the cute is genuine
  longing, the scary is the shark mouth and the dead-pearl eyes when she finally looks up.

## 3. Bake-Kujira — the skeletal ghost-whale of vengeance
- **Thesis:** the bone-whale that haunts whalers — a vast translucent WHALE SKELETON
  drifting head-down, lantern-eyed, trailing strange birds; the most EPIC-scaled of the 8.
- **32px read:** a huge **arched RIB-CAGE / whale-skull** mass — a downward-tapering bone
  barrel of curved ribs under a long blunt skull — a hung skeletal-cage KIND nothing else
  in the roster has.
- **Chibi+triad anatomy:** chibi-blunt whale skull with hollow socket-eyes that hold two
  small glow-orbs, a stubby fluke-tail flick up top; the body is a faceted cage of curved
  triad-lit RIBS (dark-core gap → bone fill → top-left rim-sheen) with ghost-flesh
  membrane stretched faintly between them. *Elevated:* baleen-comb hatch on the jaw, three
  tiny ghost-bird silhouettes orbiting (the folklore companions), drift-membrane sheen.
- **Pillar (body-as-pillar):** the rib-CAGE IS the column — each repeat = one pair of
  curved ribs joined on a ghost-spine keel; gap-cap = the blunt whale-SKULL nub
  (~shaft+30%) with one socket-orb dropping glow into the gap. Spine axis = clean mirror.
- **Palette (drowned bone + sea-rot teal-grey, pale-AMBER socket glow):** ghost-bone
  `(214,212,196)` · drowned-grey shade `(140,148,142)` · **sea-rot teal-GREY tint**
  `(96,124,118)` in the rib gaps + membrane (cool, but greyed and lighter — distinct from
  Umibozu's saturated abyss teal-BLACK) · **pale sea-amber socket glow** `(236,196,120)`
  (kept large in two sockets, not Umibozu's many small dots) · ink-baleen `(28,30,30)`.
  Bone-pale dominant, so it never reads as a teal body.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook:** a colossal sky-whale ghost with two big sad lantern-eyes —
  chibi-blunt and almost gentle, until you notice it's only bones and it's hunting whalers.

## 4. Funayurei — the drowned-sailor lantern crowd, ladles raised
- **Thesis:** the funayurei legend — drowned sailors who rise demanding a ladle to swamp
  your boat; rendered as a CLUSTER-boss, a raft of little drowned heads holding up bottomless
  ladles and a hanging ship-lantern. A crowd silhouette, unlike any single-creature boss.
- **32px read:** a stacked **CLUMP of small round drowned heads** crowned by a swinging
  paper SHIP-LANTERN, little ladle-poles bristling out — a many-headed lantern-crowd KIND.
- **Chibi+triad anatomy:** 3–4 tiny waterlogged sailor heads (puffed cheeks, X / spiral
  closed eyes, seaweed fringe) bunched into one chibi mass; each clutches a stubby
  hishaku LADLE raised overhead; a battered round paper lantern bobs above on a pole.
  Triad on the bloated grey-green flesh + the lantern paper. *Elevated:* drip-streaks of
  brine off each chin, woven-bamboo ladle texture, a kanji-marked lantern face, bubbles
  rising between heads.
- **Pillar (grounded prop):** the **ladle-POLE / lantern-rope** column — a pole strung
  with hanging bottomless ladles (each a repeat band) and float-net knots; gap-cap = the
  paper ship-lantern (~shaft+30%) glowing into the gap. On-axis mirror.
- **Palette (drowned grey-green + paper-lantern sulfur glow):** waterlogged grey-green
  flesh `(116,138,116)` · deep brine shade `(72,94,80)` · pale bloat sheen `(170,188,164)`
  · **sulfur-YELLOW lantern glow** `(238,212,104)` (warm, but yellower/paler than
  Umibozu's amber and Bake-kujira's pale-amber — a paper-flame hue) · wet-bamboo ladle
  `(150,130,86)` · cinnabar lantern-kanji `(200,72,56)` · ink `(26,30,26)`. Crowd-grey
  dominant; one warm lantern focal.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook:** a bobbing huddle of pitiful little drowned guys politely
  begging for a ladle — heartbreaking-cute — except the ladle is how they drown you.

## 5. Chochin-Anko — the abyssal anglerfish lantern-fiend
- **Thesis:** a deep-sea ANGLERFISH cuted to a grumpy gulper with a glowing lure dangling
  over a too-big underbite; the lure-on-a-stalk is the whole gimmick and the pillar.
- **32px read:** a fat round **gulper HEAD with a forward esca LURE on a bent stalk**
  arcing over the snout, jagged needle-teeth underbite — a lure-on-a-stalk KIND
  (cousin to Pazul's over-head sting, but a fish-bulb-on-a-fishing-rod, not a scorpion arc).
- **Chibi+triad anatomy:** spherical blunt fish head, one huge round eye, a wide jagged
  fang-fringed maw (cute-grumpy, lower jaw jutting), tiny useless pectoral fins; the
  illicium stalk bends forward from the brow to a glowing bulb-LURE. Triad on the dark
  abyss-fish flesh. *Elevated:* needle-tooth rows top+bottom, pore-dot lateral line,
  translucent fin-ray fans, a hard sheen pip on the eye and on the lure.
- **Pillar (body-as-pillar):** the **illicium STALK** is the column — a segmented
  filament shaft (each repeat a knuckle-node) hung with tiny barbel-feelers; gap-cap = the
  glowing esca LURE-bulb (~shaft+30%) radiating into the gap. Naturally vertical, on-axis.
- **Palette (oil-black abyss-fish + blood-coral skin, lure as sole hot focal):** oil-black
  fish body `(38,52,58)` (very dark blue-black, NOT teal — reads near-silhouette) ·
  charcoal shade `(22,32,38)` · **blood-CORAL belly + gum flush** `(196,86,72)`
  (warm-coral mass — the body warm-counterweight, a hue no abyss boss here uses on flesh)
  · cold steel sheen `(150,170,176)` · **hot-WHITE/peach lure glow** `(255,224,168)` (the
  single brightest pip — esca bioluminescence) · ink `(20,26,30)`. Near-black body + one
  blinding lure = the clean angler read.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook:** a perpetually annoyed deep-sea grump wagging a nightlight in
  your face — adorable underbite, then the maw opens and the needle-teeth go on forever.

## 6. Akkorokamui — the sunset-red kraken-deity, nine arms radial
- **Thesis:** the Ainu giant-octopus KAMI of Uchiura Bay — a vast sunset-red cephalopod
  god, a radial nine-arm STAR around one solemn divine eye; the warm-RED counterweight
  boss of the set (no other abyss boss owns red).
- **32px read:** a domed cephalo-mantle over **a wide RADIAL burst of curling arms**
  fanning out around a single central eye — a many-arm radial-star KIND (distinct from
  the Tzitzimitl corona: this is fat tapering tentacle-rays, not bone star-spikes).
- **Chibi+triad anatomy:** rounded octopus mantle-head, one big calm cyclopean eye + a
  small beak, two stub horns of mantle; 8–9 fat tapering arms curling outward, each a hard
  triad-lit taper with a row of sucker-dots; chibi-compact, weight in the head.
  *Elevated:* graduated sucker-rings down each arm, web-skin between arm-bases, a
  chromatophore stipple, one bright sheen lobe on the wet mantle.
- **Pillar (body-as-pillar):** a single **ARM / tentacle column** drops as the pillar —
  tapering sucker-banded arm shaft (suckers = repeat bands); gap-cap = a curled arm-TIP
  coil (~shaft+30%) with a glow-sucker dropping into the gap. On-axis mirror; the other
  arms stay in the head-mass for the radial read.
- **Palette (sunset vermilion-red + deep wine, divine-GOLD eye — owns the red lane):**
  sunset-vermilion skin `(206,84,64)` · deep wine-oxblood shade `(132,42,46)` · warm
  coral sheen `(238,150,120)` · cream sucker-rings `(228,210,184)` · **divine-GOLD eye +
  sucker glow** `(244,200,96)` (warm-gold focal, but on a RED body — no teal anywhere, so
  zero collision with Umibozu) · ink-beak `(30,20,22)`. The sole warm-RED boss.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook:** a benevolent-looking little sea-god with one wise eye and a
  bloom of friendly waving arms — until you remember it's the size of a bay and the arms
  are sinking ships.

## 7. Hamaguri-Shinkirou — the mirage clam, a mouth full of drowned city
- **Thesis:** the legend of the clam that breathes a mirage city (shinkirou) — a giant
  BIVALVE yokai whose parted shell-lips exhale a tiny glowing drowned-pagoda skyline; a
  banded clam-mouth boss, calm and uncanny.
- **32px read:** a wide **fan-RIBBED CLAM** split into a horizontal grin, a sliver of glow
  + tiny tower-silhouettes between the lips, two dot-eyes nestled in the hinge — a
  bivalve-shell-mouth KIND (the only horizontal-grin clam silhouette).
- **Chibi+triad anatomy:** two chibi rounded shell-halves with bold radiating triad-lit
  growth-RIBS (dark-core groove → shell fill → top-left pearl sheen), a frilled mantle
  lip with small calm eyes, the mouth-gap exhaling a thread of glow + 3–4 minuscule
  mirage-pagoda spires. *Elevated:* concentric growth-rings, barnacle freckles on the
  shell back, a pearl bead on the lower lip, faint mirage-shimmer dots above the gap.
- **Pillar (grounded prop):** a **mirage-TOWER thread** column — the exhaled drowned-city
  spire stacks into a translucent pagoda shaft (each tier a repeat band); gap-cap = a
  single mirage-pagoda roof finial (~shaft+30%) glowing at the gap. On-axis mirror.
- **Palette (sea-rust shell-amber + pearl, ghost-PEARL-green mirage glow):** sea-rust
  shell `(178,142,96)` · deep umber-shell shade `(120,86,52)` · **pearl-cream sheen**
  `(236,224,196)` (large pearl-iridescent mass — the cool counterweight to the warm shell)
  · **ghost pearl-GREEN mirage glow** `(170,222,186)` inside the mouth-gap (a soft
  sea-green LIGHT — distinct from Kappa body-green, Kitsune mint, Yurei cyan; this is
  paler/greyer) · slate hinge-eyes `(60,72,74)` · ink `(28,26,24)`. Warm shell + pearl
  body, one pale mirage glow — reads brown-warm, never teal.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook:** a serene old clam softly humming a whole ghost-city out of its
  mouth — wondrous-cute — and the city is full of tiny drowned souls.

## 8. Tehom-no-Tamago — the abyssal leviathan-EGG, the chaos that hasn't hatched
- **Thesis:** the primordial deep (*tehom* / Tiamat) as an unhatched SPIRAL EGG-SHELL —
  a barnacled chaos-egg cracked just enough that a single drowned leviathan eye and a
  tongue of glow leak out; the most abstract, ancient, EPIC-omen boss of the 8.
- **32px read:** a fat **spiraled SHELL / ammonite ovoid** (offset coil whorl, blunt at
  base) with a jagged horizontal CRACK leaking glow and one slit-pupil eye peering
  through — a spiral-egg KIND owned by nothing else in the roster.
- **Chibi+triad anatomy:** a rounded chibi egg-shell body, a hard triad-lit spiral WHORL
  ridge winding around it (dark-core groove → shell fill → top-left rim-sheen), a ragged
  glowing crack-mouth, one big reptilian slit eye inside the dark, tiny pre-hatch tendrils
  curling out of the crack. *Elevated:* barnacle + tubeworm crust on the shell, embryonic
  vein-tracery faintly visible through thin shell, a sharp sheen lobe on the whorl crest.
- **Pillar (grounded prop):** a hanging **birth-cord / kelp-tether** column anchoring the
  egg to the deep — knotted cord shaft with a barnacle-cluster every repeat; gap-cap = a
  small cracked shell-shard egg-stone (~shaft+30%) leaking one glow line into the gap.
  On-axis mirror.
- **Palette (deep abyssal-INDIGO-teal shell + bone-cream whorl, embryo-GREEN glow —
  pushed off both Umibozu teal AND Tzitzimitl indigo):** deep teal-indigo shell
  `(46,66,84)` (bluer + darker than Umibozu's teal-black, but greyer/cooler than
  Tzitzimitl's pure midnight-indigo — sits between, low-chroma) · near-black shade
  `(26,40,54)` · **bone-cream whorl ridge** `(214,206,182)` (the bright structural read
  — the eye lands on the spiral, not the dark shell) · **abyss embryo-GREEN crack glow**
  `(150,206,140)` (a living chartreuse-green leak — warm-green, unlike any cyan/mint;
  distinct from Umi-nyobo's lighter chartreuse by being deeper + more sour) · barnacle
  bone-grey `(170,172,164)` · ink `(20,28,36)`. Bone whorl + green crack carry it; the
  shell stays a backdrop so it never twins Umibozu.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook:** a cosmic deep-sea egg with one curious baby eye blinking
  through the crack — impossibly cute — and what's coiled inside is the end of the world.

## Distinctness self-audit (for the AD cull to 5)

- **8 disjoint silhouette KINDs:** 1 sacred-bell saucer · 2 finned face-bust · 3 hung
  whale rib-cage · 4 drowned-head lantern crowd · 5 angler lure-on-stalk · 6 nine-arm
  radial star · 7 horizontal clam-mouth · 8 spiral chaos-egg. No two share a 32px read,
  and none is Umibozu's smooth round jelly-dome.
- **Palette lanes spread wide so the 8 don't twin:** 1 verdigris-bronze + rust ·
  2 drowned-pearl + ink-kelp + chartreuse · 3 ghost-bone + sea-rot grey + pale amber ·
  4 grey-green crowd + sulfur lantern · 5 oil-black + blood-coral + hot lure · 6 sunset
  vermilion-red + gold (sole red) · 7 sea-rust shell + pearl + pale mirage-green ·
  8 teal-indigo shell + bone whorl + embryo-green. Warm focal hues alone span rust /
  chartreuse / pale-amber / sulfur / hot-peach / gold / mirage-green / embryo-green — no
  two glows alike.
- **Roster-collision clears:** none uses Kappa yellow-green BODY, Necrarch violet, Draugr
  /Yurei ice-cyan or blue-cyan, or Kitsune mint as a *glow*. The two green-leaning glows
  (2 chartreuse, 8 embryo-green) are pushed apart in value/sourness and sit on totally
  different creatures + body-hues. Akkorokamui owns red; Bake-kujira owns bone; none
  duplicates Umibozu's teal-black jelly body + many-amber-dots combination.
- **Pillars all grounded + mirrorable:** bell-rope (1), kelp-pearl cord (2), rib-cage
  body (3), ladle-pole (4), illicium stalk body (5), tentacle-arm body (6), mirage-tower
  thread (7), birth-cord tether (8). Three are body-as-pillar (3, 5, 6); all clean on-axis
  with modest ~shaft+30% gap-caps — no top-heavy risk flagged.
