# Umibozu-versions — AD BRAINSTORM-CRITIQUE (cull 8 → LOCKED 5)

VERDICT: ITERATE

The set is unusually strong — 8 genuinely disjoint silhouette KINDs, glow hues
spread across the warm spectrum, and every brief already carries a grounded
mirrorable pillar. This is not a re-roll: it is a confident cull plus four
surgical re-specs so the 5 survivors hold their lanes against the WHOLE roster
(not just each other) at 32px. The GD self-audit checked the 8 against each
other and against the named avoids cleanly; the gaps I am closing are
roster-wide collisions the brainstorm under-weighted: skeleton density,
radial-top density, and lantern-pole pillar density.

---

## THE LOCKED 5 (keep)

**1. Chochin-Anko (5) — abyssal anglerfish lure-fiend.** The single most EPIC,
instantly-readable CHARACTER of the eight: near-black oil body + one blinding
peach lure + a needle-tooth underbite is a 32px silhouette nothing in the
roster touches, and the lure-on-a-stalk owns a body-as-pillar with a literal
hot gap-cap. Scary-cute is dead-on (grumpy nightlight → endless maw). Anchor of
the set.

**2. Akkorokamui (6) — sunset-red kraken-deity.** Owns the sole RED lane in the
entire abyss family — that alone earns the slot, and the warm-red counterweight
is exactly what a teal/bone/pearl-heavy set needs so the five don't read cold-on-
cold. One calm cyclopean eye + a bloom of nine arms is maximally epic-cute. The
radial top needs a re-spec to clear Tzitzimitl/Raijin (below), but the KIND
survives because it is fat tapering tentacle-RAYS, not bone spikes or a drum
ring.

**3. Bake-Kujira (3) — skeletal ghost-whale.** SURVIVES the skeleton-density
gate, but only on a hard re-spec (below). Ruling: the hung whale-RIB-CAGE +
blunt-skull-head-down read is a silhouette KIND no humanoid calaca/reaper/lich
and no Citipati skull-brood shares — it reads as a colossal hanging cage, not a
standing bone figure. It is also the most EPIC-SCALED character available and
the only "vast drifting leviathan" beat. Kept for distinctness of scale and
silhouette, conditioned on pushing it OFF generic bone.

**4. Hamaguri-Shinkirou (7) — mirage clam exhaling a drowned city.** The only
HORIZONTAL-grin silhouette in the roster and the only warm-brown sea-rust body
in this set — a wide ribbed bivalve mouth breathing a thread of tiny pagoda
spires is wondrous, original (not the obvious sea-monster reference), and the
mirage-tower pillar is the most imaginative of the eight. Pearl + sea-rust reads
brown-warm, never teal.

**5. Tehom-no-Tamago (8) — abyssal leviathan-egg.** The most ORIGINAL and most
ominous-epic of the set: a barnacled spiral chaos-egg with one baby slit-eye
blinking through a glowing crack is peak scary-cute, and the spiral-egg KIND is
owned by nothing else. Bone whorl carries the read so the dark shell never twins
Umibozu. Kept as the abstract/cosmic outlier that gives the five real range.

## THE 3 CUTS

**CUT — Kurage-no-Daibutsu (1), bell-Buddha.** Strongest of the cuts and a
genuinely lovely idea, but it is the most REPLACEABLE: a serene calm-eyed
floating dome-with-a-face is the closest of all eight to the SHIPPED Umibozu's
own beat (serene monk head, calm lowered eyes, hangs over the deep). The bell
re-skins the silhouette but keeps the source's emotional + compositional read,
and verdigris-bronze is the one survivor-adjacent palette I'd worry about beside
a teal source. Cut to protect maximum distance from the lineage anchor.

**CUT — Umi-Nyobo (2), sea-wife portrait.** A face-bust KIND collides head-on
with the roster's portrait-head density — Nukekubi (ornate coiffed head),
Catrina (skull under brim), Yurei (face under hair-curtains). The brief itself
concedes it is "kin to Nukekubi's read," and pearl-pale + black-curtain framing
sits dangerously near Yurei's pale-face/ink-hair lane at 32px. Least distinct
silhouette of the eight. Cut.

**CUT — Funayurei (4), drowned-sailor crowd.** Charming, but two collisions: (a)
its ladle-POLE/ship-lantern pillar is a third lantern-pole pillar alongside
Jiangshi (talisman-lantern pole) and Yurei (hitodama lantern-pole), and (b) a
clump of small round grey-green heads risks reading as undifferentiated mush at
32px and the sulfur-yellow lantern glow sits close to the source's amber. The
crowd-KIND is novel but is the weakest 32px CHARACTER (no single hero shape).
Cut.

## NEAR-DUPLICATE / COLLISION FLAGS (within the 5, and roster-wide)

- **Radial-top stack: Akkorokamui (6) vs Tzitzimitl (sibling, star-corona) vs
  Raijin (drum-ring halo).** Three "wide radial thing around a central head"
  reads now exist. Akkorokamui survives only if its rays read as a FEW (8–9) FAT,
  CURLING, TAPERING tentacle-arms with sucker-dots — soft, organic, asymmetric
  curl — versus Tzitzimitl's straight rigid BONE spikes and Raijin's ring of hard
  round DRUMS. RE-SPEC: keep arm count low and arms THICK + curling (no thin
  straight rays), and weight mass into the mantle-head so the read is
  "octopus-god," not "starburst." This is the single most important cross-set fix.

- **Bone collision: Bake-Kujira (3) vs the skeleton section (Catrina, Mariachi,
  Jiangshi, Draugr, Necrarch) + Citipati brood.** Ghost-bone `(214,212,196)` is
  nearly the SAME hue as Catrina ash-white `(238,230,222)`, Mariachi warm-bone,
  Necrarch bone-parchment. The whale survives on SILHOUETTE, but its palette must
  not also read "generic calaca bone." RE-SPEC: push the body toward the sea-rot
  TEAL-GREY membrane as the dominant value (cool grey-green bone, not warm cream
  bone), keep the pale-amber sockets as the sole warm focal, and let the cool
  rib-cage tint — not bone-cream — carry the 32px read. That tints it clearly
  "drowned sea-bone," off the warm-bone calaca lane.

- **Green-glow proximity: Tehom embryo-green `(150,206,140)` vs Hamaguri
  mirage-green `(170,222,186)`.** Two pale-green LIGHT glows now coexist in the
  five. They're on totally different bodies (dark teal-indigo egg vs warm-brown
  clam) and the brief already pushes Tehom deeper/sourer — that holds, but PIN
  it: Tehom's crack glow must be the DEEPER, more sour chartreuse-green; Hamaguri's
  must be the PALER, greyer pearl-green. Do not let them converge in saturation.

- **Egg-shell vs clam-shell texture: Tehom (8) vs Hamaguri (7).** Both lean on
  triad-lit RIBBED/whorled shell relief. Distinct at silhouette (vertical spiral
  ovoid vs horizontal split bivalve), but keep the relief LANGUAGE different:
  Tehom = a single continuous SPIRAL whorl; Hamaguri = bold RADIATING fan-ribs.
  Don't let both become "generic concentric shell grooves."

- **Palette-lane check across the 5 (clean):** angler oil-black + blood-coral +
  hot lure / kraken sunset-vermilion + gold / whale sea-rot teal-grey-bone +
  pale-amber / clam sea-rust + pearl + mirage-green / egg teal-indigo + bone-whorl
  + embryo-green. No two bodies share a lane; the warm counterweights (coral,
  red, amber, rust, sour-green) are spread. None uses Kappa yellow-green body,
  Necrarch violet, Draugr/Yurei ice-cyan/blue-cyan, mint, or Kitsune foxfire.
  Two reds (angler blood-coral belly vs kraken sunset-vermilion) sit on totally
  different silhouettes and values — acceptable, but keep angler's coral as a
  BELLY/gum flush under a near-black body, never a full-body red, so it can't twin
  the kraken.

- **Source-Umibozu clearance (clean):** none of the 5 is a smooth round
  jelly-dome with amber dots. The closest emotional beat (serene dome-Buddha) was
  CUT precisely to keep distance. Tehom's dark shell is held back behind a
  bone-whorl read so it never twins the teal source.

## LOCKING BRIEFS — the 5 survivors

### 1. Chochin-Anko — abyssal anglerfish lure-fiend
- **Palette (LOCK):** oil-black body `(38,52,58)` / charcoal shade `(22,32,38)` /
  blood-CORAL belly+gum flush `(196,86,72)` (BELLY ONLY — never full-body, so it
  can't twin Akkorokamui's red) / cold-steel sheen `(150,170,176)` / hot-WHITE-peach
  lure glow `(255,224,168)` (sole brightest pip) / ink `(20,26,30)`.
- **Pillar:** illicium STALK body-as-pillar — segmented filament, knuckle-node per
  repeat, barbel-feelers; gap-cap = the glowing esca lure-bulb (~shaft+30%)
  radiating into the gap. Clean.
- **Character hook:** perpetually-annoyed deep grump wagging a nightlight; cute
  underbite → endless needle-maw.
- **Re-spec:** none. Anchor concept. Keep the body near-silhouette dark so the
  single lure pip is the only true focal.

### 2. Akkorokamui — sunset-red kraken-deity
- **Palette (LOCK):** sunset-vermilion `(206,84,64)` / deep wine-oxblood shade
  `(132,42,46)` / warm-coral sheen `(238,150,120)` / cream sucker-rings
  `(228,210,184)` / divine-GOLD eye+sucker glow `(244,200,96)` / ink-beak
  `(30,20,22)`. Sole red boss — owns the lane.
- **Pillar:** single ARM tentacle-column body-as-pillar — tapering sucker-banded
  shaft (suckers = repeats); gap-cap = curled arm-TIP coil (~shaft+30%) with a
  glow-sucker into the gap. Other arms stay in the head-mass.
- **Character hook:** benevolent one-eyed little sea-god, a bloom of friendly
  waving arms — that sink ships.
- **RE-SPEC (cross-set #1, important):** arms must read as 8–9 FAT, CURLING,
  TAPERING rays with visible sucker-dots and asymmetric curl — NOT thin straight
  spokes — and visual mass must sit in the rounded mantle-head, so the 32px read
  is "octopus deity," firmly clear of Tzitzimitl's rigid bone star-corona and
  Raijin's round drum-ring.

### 3. Bake-Kujira — skeletal ghost-whale
- **Palette (RE-SPECCED, LOCK):** make the COOL sea-rot teal-GREY the dominant
  body value `(96,124,118)` (rib-gaps + membrane) over a greyed bone `(160,170,158)`
  (cooler/greyer than the brief's `(214,212,196)` so it does NOT read as warm
  calaca cream) / drowned-grey shade `(118,130,124)` / pale sea-AMBER socket glow
  `(236,196,120)` (two large sockets, sole warm focal) / ink-baleen `(28,30,30)`.
- **Pillar:** rib-CAGE body-as-pillar — one curved rib-pair per repeat on a
  ghost-spine keel; gap-cap = blunt whale-SKULL nub (~shaft+30%) dropping one
  socket-orb glow. Spine axis = clean mirror.
- **Character hook:** colossal sky-whale ghost, two big sad lantern-eyes,
  chibi-blunt and almost gentle — until you see it's only bones, hunting whalers.
- **RE-SPEC (skeleton-gate, important):** body must read clearly COOL drowned
  sea-bone (teal-grey membrane dominant), NOT warm cream bone — this is the line
  that keeps it off the Catrina/Mariachi/Necrarch warm-bone lane. Keep 3 tiny
  ghost-bird companions for folklore charm but small enough not to clutter the
  32px read.

### 4. Hamaguri-Shinkirou — mirage clam, mouth of a drowned city
- **Palette (LOCK):** sea-rust shell `(178,142,96)` / deep umber-shell shade

  PEARL-GREEN mirage glow `(170,222,186)` (the PALER/greyer green — pin it apart

### 5. Tehom-no-Tamago — abyssal leviathan-egg
- **Palette (LOCK):** deep teal-indigo shell `(46,66,84)` (held BACK as backdrop)

## SET-LEVEL CHECK (post-cull)

- **5 disjoint silhouette KINDs:** lure-on-a-stalk angler · nine-arm radial
  kraken · hung whale rib-cage · horizontal clam-mouth · vertical spiral egg. No
  two share a 32px top read; none is the source jelly-dome.
- **5 separated palette lanes:** oil-black/coral · sunset-red/gold · cool
  sea-bone/amber · sea-rust/pearl/pale-green · teal-indigo/bone-whorl/sour-green.
- **Character-first:** every survivor is a memorable, named EPIC creature with a
  scary-CUTE hook (grumpy nightlight, friendly ship-sinker, sad sky-whale,
  humming ghost-city clam, blinking apocalypse-egg). Range spans cute-grump →
  benevolent-god → gentle-giant → wondrous → cosmic-omen.
- **Pillars all grounded + mirrorable:** 2 body-as-pillar (angler stalk, kraken
  arm), 1 body-cage (whale ribs), 2 grounded props (mirage tower, birth-cord). All
  clean on-axis, ~shaft+30% caps, no top-heavy risk.
- **Roster clearance:** no jelly-dome (Umibozu), no star-corona (Tzitzimitl), no
  yellow-green (Kappa), no violet (Necrarch), no ice-cyan/blue-cyan (Draugr/Yurei),
  no mint (Kitsune). Skeleton-whale held to cool sea-bone, off the warm-calaca
  lane. Radial kraken held to fat curling arms, off the bone star-corona and
  drum-ring.

The cull is locked at these 5; the four re-specs above (radial-arm read, whale
cool-bone palette, the two green-glow pins, the spiral-vs-fan shell-relief split)
must enter the per-concept loops as HARD pins, not suggestions. With those
applied, the set is ship-ready to mature.
