# AD CRITIQUE — Hamaguri-Shinkirou — ROUND 1

VERDICT: ITERATE

Strong palette, strong hero-scale silhouette intent, and the fan-rib relief is
genuinely beautiful at SS. But the design loses its TWO defining ideas at the
only scale that ships: at true 32px it reads as a generic mushroom/lamp/UFO
with a grey-green stub on top — neither "horizontal-grin clam" nor "clam + city"
survives. That is the gate, and it is not cleared yet.

---

## RANKING — strongest → weakest aspect

1. **Palette & lane (STRONGEST).** Sea-rust shell, umber shade, pearl-cream
   sheen, pearl-green mirage — reads unmistakably brown-warm, never teal,
   exactly per the pin. Clean separation from Tehom's deeper sour-green. Keep.
2. **Hero-scale fan-rib relief.** The radiating valve fans are gorgeous and
   instantly distinct from a spiral whorl. The shell-half construction is right.
3. **Hinge-eye faces + pearl on the upper valve.** The two slate hinge-eyes
   and the single pearl give the "serene old clam" charm at hero scale.
4. **Pillar tiers.** Pagoda-tier shaft reads as a translucent tower at PILLAR
   scale and mirrors cleanly bottom-rooted; distinct from Umibozu + roster.
5. **32px CHARACTER read (WEAKEST).** Silhouette collapses to a lamp/UFO; the
   horizontal-grin distinctness and the city both vanish. See Fix #1–#3.

---

## THE RULING you asked for (item 4)

The GD's flag is correct and it understates the problem. At 32px the issue is
NOT merely "the mirage-tower is too slim to read as a city." It is that:

- The clam read does NOT carry it. Look at the chip + the 4x blowup + the
  grayscale check: the silhouette is a domed top + a flat horizontal lens +
  a wide bowl bottom = a **UFO / table-lamp / mushroom**. The wide horizontal
  valve-SPLIT — the entire point of this KIND — is present as a thin dark line
  but is NOT the dominant read; the eye reads the green dome on top first.
- So at 32px you currently get "lamp + glowing finial," not "clam," and
  certainly not "clam + city."

**Ruling: the CLAM read must carry the icon, and it does not yet — fix that
FIRST.** The city is correctly a hero/pillar feature and should NOT try to read
as a literal city at 32px (that way lies noise). But the mirage must still read
as a distinct GLOWING EXHALE rising from the PARTED LIPS — a single bright
pearl-green plume pip — not as a solid grey-green bottle-neck that fuses to the
shell and de-silhouettes the grin. Right now the mirage hurts the clam read
instead of completing it.

---

## ITERATION DIRECTIVES (most important first)

1. **Make the horizontal valve-SPLIT the dominant 32px read — the grin must
   win.** Currently the green dome + the symmetric dome/bowl pairing read as a
   vertical lamp. Widen the valves so the silhouette is clearly WIDER than tall
   (push aspect toward ~1.4:1 W:H of the shell mass), and deepen the dark
   split-line keyline between the valves so the "parted-lips grin" is the first
   thing the eye lands on. The shell must read horizontal-grin BEFORE the
   mirage is even noticed.

2. **Stop the mirage from de-silhouetting the clam.** At 32px the exhaled tower
   is a solid, near-shell-VALUE grey-green stub sitting ON the hinge, so it
   fuses into the body and just makes a lamp-neck. Two changes: (a) drop the
   mirage to a SINGLE narrow pearl-green PLUME pip rising from the split, not a
   tiered bottle-neck; (b) raise its value/chroma and translucency so it reads
   as LIGHT (a glow) distinctly brighter than the shell — it should be the sole
   bright focal pip floating just above the lips, the way the brief's other
   bosses use one glow pip. It should sit slightly ABOVE the split with air
   around it, not plug the hinge.

3. **Kill the UFO/lamp gestalt.** The symmetric upper-dome + lower-bowl reads
   as a saucer. Break that symmetry: the LOWER valve should be the heavier,
   rounder shell (bottom-rooted weight) and the UPPER valve flatter/thinner so
   the pair reads as a clam opening UPWARD, not as a lens between two domes.
   Asymmetric valve weight is what sells "mouth," and it also helps the 32px
   read avoid twinning a generic dome.

4. **Push the fan-ribs to survive downscale as DIRECTION, not texture.** At 32px
   the radiating ribs blur to a flat tan smear (confirmed in the blowup). You
   don't need every rib — bake 3–4 BOLD value-stepped fan wedges per valve
   (dark-core gaps between them) so the radiating fan-DIRECTION still reads as a
   few chunky rays at small size. That preserves the "fan vs Tehom's spiral"
   distinctness even when individual ribs are gone.

5. **Hinge-eyes: keep, but make them carry charm at 32px.** Right now the two
   slate dots nearly vanish on the chip. Nudge them slightly larger and seat
   them on the UPPER valve so the "serene face above a wide grin" reads — the
   eyes + the wide split together are the scary-CUTE character beat. The pearl
   can stay as a hero-scale-only delight (it's fine that it drops at 32px).

6. **Night read.** On the night chip the warm shell holds up well (good — the
   warm lane reads on dark blue), but the mirage stub goes muddy grey-green and
   loses its glow. Once it becomes a bright plume pip (Fix #2) it should pop on
   night sky; verify the pearl-green sits brighter than both day haze AND night
   indigo.

7. **Pillar — keep, minor.** The tiered pagoda shaft + finial is working and
   on-brief. Once the hero mirage becomes a brighter plume, carry that same
   raised value into the finial cap so the gap-cap glows as the brightest pip,
   matching the body's focal logic.

## CROSS-SET PIN CHECK

- Brown-warm, never teal: PASS. Cleanly apart from Tehom's sour-green and the
  source Umibozu's teal-black.
- Pearl/greyer green vs Tehom's deeper green: PASS at hero; must stay bright
  enough to remain a GLOW (not grey stub) once Fix #2 lands.
- Fan-ribs vs spiral whorl: PASS at hero, AT RISK at 32px until Fix #4.
- Horizontal-grin = the ONLY horizontal silhouette of the brood: this is the
  failing pin right now — see Fix #1/#3. It currently rounds toward a vertical
  lamp/UFO. This is the make-or-break.
- Pillar bottom-rooted, clean mirror, distinct from source + roster: PASS.

Land Fix #1–#3 and this becomes a memorable, ownable epic. The bones are good;
the 32px silhouette discipline is what's missing.
