# Bake-Kujira — skeletal ghost-whale — ROUND 1 critique

VERDICT: ITERATE

A strong, genuinely epic first pass. The hero is a memorable CHARACTER —
blunt sad sky-whale skull, two big amber lantern sockets, hung rib-cage
on a keel — and it clears the skeleton-gate decisively. It needs a focused
cleanup round, not a re-roll: the flagged tail artifact is real and worth
fixing, the 32px night chip is leaning too warm-white, and the rib read
softens at true scale. Nothing here is a dead end.

## Strongest / weakest
- **Strongest:** the head. Blunt-dome skull + the two sad amber sockets in
  white eye-mass = instant scary-CUTE, instant "ghost whale," and the sole
  warm focal lands exactly where the brief wants it. This is the keeper.
- **Weakest:** the rib-cage's structural read at true scale — the C-arcs go
  to mush/noise on the membrane and the bottom rib-pair dangles as stray
  hooks (the flagged artifact).

## Skeleton-gate — PASS
Body membrane backing samples cool teal-grey ~ (93,121,115) — dead-on the
brief's (96,124,118) dominant; bone ribs sit greyed ~ (149,159,147), cooler
than calaca cream. Amber sockets (232,192,117) are the sole warm pip. This
is unmistakably drowned SEA-bone, clear of the Catrina/Mariachi/Necrarch
warm-bone lane. Hold this palette exactly — do not warm the bone.

## Ranked fixes (most important first)

1. **RULING on the tail rib termination: CLEAN IT.** The bottom-most
   rib-pair's C-arc overshoots the square membrane backing and its tip curls
   into open teal as two loose hooks below the silhouette. Even though it's
   clipped at 32px and tiles fine in the pillar, at HERO scale it reads as a
   modeling error / stray hook, not intent. Two acceptable fixes: (a) extend
   the membrane backing / keel a touch so every rib tip terminates ON the
   body (tips tuck into the spine, no open-space curl), or (b) shorten the
   final rib-pair so its sweep closes inside the silhouette like the pairs
   above it. Prefer (a) — a whale tapering to a tail-stub keel reads more
   "leviathan" than a hard-cropped cage. Either way: no rib tip floats in
   the void at hero scale.

2. **Night 32px chip is drifting toward warm-white bone — pull it cool.**
   On the DAY chip the read is correctly grey-teal, but on NIGHT the head
   eye-mass + upper body blow out to near-pure warm white, which is exactly
   the calaca-cream impression the skeleton-gate exists to avoid, and it
   flattens the amber sockets (the warm focal loses its monopoly). Drop the
   eye-white / rim-sheen value on the night variant ~15-20% and push it
   cooler (toward the membrane teal-grey) so the only thing that stays warm
   at night is the two amber sockets. Verify the sockets still out-punch the
   skull on the night sky after the pull.

3. **Rib structure turns to noise at true scale — simplify the cage.** At
   the 32px chip and in the pillar shaft the stacked thin C-arcs read as
   horizontal hatching/noise rather than "ribs." For the gameplay read,
   reduce the rib count in the 32px/pillar variant (fewer, thicker, higher-
   contrast pairs — 3 clean ribs beats 6 thin ones) and let the dark
   rib-gap shadow do the legibility work. The hero can keep more ribs, but
   the small variant must read as a CAGE, not a comb.

4. **Spine knuckles vs. socket-orb confusion in the pillar.** The white
   spine-bead down the keel and the gap-cap socket-orb are both bright round
   pips, so the eye doesn't immediately know which is the cap focal. Make
   the skull-cap socket-orb clearly the largest + warmest pip (it's the only
   amber one — good), and knock the spine beads slightly cooler/smaller so
   they read as structure, not as competing eyes. The 2x skull-cap zoom is
   lovely; protect that hierarchy at true scale.

5. **Ghost-bird companions — fine, hold them.** The three little chevron
   ghost-birds are charming and correctly absent from the 32px + pillar
   reads, so they don't clutter gameplay. Keep them HERO-only and keep them
   this small/pale. One micro-note: they currently read as plain flapping-V
   marks; a single 1px amber pin-eye on each would tie them to the whale's
   focal language without adding clutter. Optional.

## House-style fidelity — PASS
Chibi proportion, scary-CUTE sad-eye read, flat triad (dark-core → flat-fill
→ top-left rim-sheen), ink keyline + 1px outline all present and consistent
with the Umibozu-lineage renderer. Reads elevated, not grim. Distinct from
the source jelly-dome and from the rest of the roster (the hung rib-cage
KIND + cool-bone+amber lane is unique in the set).
