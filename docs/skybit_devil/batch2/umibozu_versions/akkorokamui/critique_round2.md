## Ruling on the GD's flagged concern (left flank flatter than right) — ACCEPTABLE

The depth-layering being concentrated on the right with a slightly flatter left
flank does NOT break the read and does not gate ship. The octopus interpretation
is carried by the head-dominant mass + the front/behind value band + varied arm
length — all of which are present. A live octopus is asymmetric by nature; a
perfectly balanced depth treatment on both flanks would actually push back
toward the symmetric icon we fought out of in r1. At true 32px the left-flank
flatness is invisible. Leave it.
