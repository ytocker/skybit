# Jangseung-versions — GD brainstorm (8 carved-WOOD guardian-post bosses)

Lineage anchor: shipped **Jangseung** (locked brief #4) — a tall narrow carved
TOTEM-POST where the creature IS the pillar; oversized comic-fierce face filling
the top third (bug eyes, fat bulb-nose, fanged grin), tall official's hat-block, a
vertical cinnabar-hanja column down the front, NO limbs; weathered slate-WOOD +
cinnabar + moss-teal; folklore-true twin-face cap. House style every direction
inherits (ELEVATED): CHIBI proportions, scary-CUTE menace; FLAT procedural triad
(dark-core -> flat-fill -> top-left rim-sheen), 1-2px ink keyline `(28,22,30)` +
1px grown outline; procedural-only (NO gradients/PNGs). Pushed EPIC = bigger render
scale, SS=5-6, more carved geometry, richer triad, stronger glow. Pillar is a SOFT
requirement (the carved post/body IS the pillar for most) — spend energy on the
CHARACTER/FACE. Each pillar grounded in the creature's OWN forms; clean top<->bottom
mirror; no top-heavy cap.

These are 8 DISTINCT NEW bosses sharing Jangseung's DNA loosely — a carved-WOOD
guardian / totem / idol-post / ancestor-pole spirit — each its own creature, folklore,
silhouette KIND, anatomy, wood tone + paint accent. NOT recolored Jangseungs.

## Distinctness pins (HARD — must hold at 32px)

- **Hold the WOOD lane** for all 8 (no glaze, no stone, no ceramic). Re-lane any
  stone/ceramic source to CARVED PAINTED WOOD. Stay off owned non-wood lanes:
  Tlaloc (grey STONE goggle-mask), Zhenmushou (amber CERAMIC), Karakasa (oilpaper
  object-yokai).
- **Avoid owned hues:** Necrarch violet, Draugr ice-cyan, Raijin storm-grey, Kitsune
  mint, Yurei blue-cyan, Tlaloc grey-jade-with-coral, Cernun deep-pine. Each of the
  8 below gets a DISTINCT wood tone + a DISTINCT paint accent so no two read alike.
- **Don't collide with source Jangseung:** it owns the slate-grey driftwood +
  cinnabar-hanja-column + twin bug-eye face + official hat-block. None of the 8 may
  be "a Jangseung with a different hat" — each is a different silhouette KIND and
  face KIND (bird-topped pole / horned lion-beast / bellied grandpa / figurehead
  prow / stacked-face stack / fanged tongue-out warrior / clawed thunderbird-crest /
  ancestor-stack with canoe-prow base).
- **Wood-tone spread (8 distinct):** ash-grey-driftwood (source, excluded) -> these
  8 span: pale-bleached pine, warm-honey cedar, ruddy red-totara, blackened bog-oak,
  golden-tan teak, ash-rose weathered, cool-walnut umber, sun-bleached bone-white
  oak. Each pairs to ONE saturated paint accent that nobody else uses as a mass.

## 1. Sotjang — the sky-bird sentinel pole  [SOTTDAE bird-pole]
- **Folklore.** Korean **sotdae**: a tall pole topped by a carved DUCK/goose that
  bridges earth and heaven, warding fire and drought — erected right beside jangseung.
  The natural sibling-post: the totem whose CHARACTER lives at the TOP as a bird, not
  a face down the shaft.
- **Thesis.** The only BIRD-CRESTED guardian — a fat carved waterfowl perched fierce
  atop a slim pole; the whole read is "angry duck on a stick," top-weighted by a
  rounded body, never a face-column.
- **32px silhouette.** Slim vertical pole; at the top a plump round DUCK body in
  side-profile, blunt flat bill jutting forward, one big round bug-eye, a stubby
  upswept tail — a teapot-ish bird blob on a stick. Bird-on-pole is one-of-a-kind in
  the roster.
- **Chibi + triad carved anatomy (elevated).** Carved-WOOD duck: fat oval body
  (dark-core belly -> honey fill -> top-left wing-edge sheen), a flat wide bill
  carved as two beveled planks with a fanged underbite peg-teeth grin, huge round
  comic eye with a carved socket-groove ring, carved feather-scale rows on the wing
  (hard triad facets, sparse), tiny peg feet gripping the pole-cap. Elevated detail:
  beveled feather courses, bill wood-grain, eye-ring carve.
- **Pillar (grounded).** The pole IS the pillar — slim turned-WOOD shaft with carved
  ring-bands (lathe grooves) + a couple of tied prayer-cloth knots; cap = a SECOND
  smaller mirrored duck facing the gap, bill lit, eye glowing. On-axis, slim,
  symmetric — bird-finial mirror, bottom-weighted by the bands so the duck doesn't
  top-heavy the gap.
- **Palette (distinct).** PALE-BLEACHED PINE pole `(196,178,140)` base / `(150,128,92)`
  shade; duck body warmer HONEY-TAN `(206,160,96)`; one saturated paint accent =
  INDIGO-BLUE neck-band + bill stripe `(58,86,138)` (cool, nobody owns a flat indigo
  mass — clear of Yurei's blue-CYAN glow, this is a flat paint not a glow); cinnabar
  is absent here (separates from source) — accent is the indigo; warm-amber eye glow
  `(244,200,120)`; pale lichen-grey patches `(176,182,156)`. Reads pale-pine + indigo.
- **Scary-cute-EPIC hook.** A grumpy sacred DUCK glaring down off a heaven-pole — the
  comic underbite bill + saucer eye is pure scary-cute; epic via the carved feather
  courses and the glowing twin-duck cap.

## 2. Haedung — the fire-eating guardian-lion post  [HAITAI / haetae]
- **Folklore.** Korean **haetae/haechi**: a scaled lion-dog of justice that DEVOURS
  FIRE, bulging truth-seeing eyes, single brow-horn, a neck-bell. Re-laned from its
  usual stone to a CARVED-WOOD temple-post lion.
- **Thesis.** The only HORNED LION-BEAST face-post — a wide maned lion-mask with a
  single stubby brow-horn and a fire-eating grin, on a squat scaled column; broad and
  bottom-rooted (anti-source: source is a tall NARROW human face, this is a WIDE
  beast mask).
- **32px silhouette.** A wide carved lion-MASK at top — fat curled mane lobes ringing
  the face, a single short center horn, two round bulge eyes, a wide downturned
  fire-grin with up-fangs; below it a short scaled column. Wide-mask-with-one-horn is
  the tell, unmistakable vs source's narrow hat-block face.
- **Chibi + triad carved anatomy (elevated).** Carved cedar lion: face plate (dark
  socket cores -> warm-cedar fill -> top-left brow sheen), a RING of carved mane curls
  each a hard triad spiral, single beveled brow-horn, two big bug-eyes with carved
  eye-rings, wide grin with a tiny carved FLAME-CURL licking from one corner (the
  fire-eater gag), a carved neck-bell pendant. Elevated detail: mane-curl spirals,
  carved scale-rows down the column, horn ridges, bell.
- **Pillar (grounded).** Squat scaled WOOD column = the body: hard carved fish-scale
  courses as repeatable bands; cap = a smaller mirrored lion-mask at the gap, mouth lit
  with a swallowed-flame glow. Broad bottom-rooted base -> never top-heavy; on-axis.
- **Palette (distinct).** WARM-HONEY CEDAR `(198,150,84)` base / `(150,104,52)` shade;
  one saturated accent = JADE-GREEN painted scales + mane-tips `(72,150,118)` (a
  clean mid-green, kept off Cernun's deep pine and Kappa's yellow-green — this is a
  blue-leaning carved-paint jade as a SCALE-band, not a body fill); EMBER-ORANGE
  swallowed-flame mouth glow `(238,128,64)` (warm focal); gold bell `(222,184,86)`;
  ink eyes. Reads honey-cedar + jade scales + ember mouth.

## 3. Harubang — the bellied stone-grandfather post  [DOL HAREUBANG, re-laned to WOOD]
- **Folklore.** Jeju **dol hareubang**, "stone grandfather": pupil-less bulging eyes,
  a mushroom-cap hat, both HANDS resting on a round belly, stooped raised shoulders.
  Re-laned from porous volcanic stone to a carved-WOOD ancestor-post — the only one
  WITH visible hands/belly.
- **Thesis.** The squat round-bellied grandpa idol — a pot-bellied stubby post with
  two carved hands clasped on the gut and a fat mushroom-cap; bottom-heavy chibi
  weeble, the cuddliest-yet-stern of the 8.
- **32px silhouette.** A fat barrel BODY (the belly is the widest mass), a round
  mushroom-CAP hat on top, two bulging pupil-less owl-eyes, a closed-mouth stern peg
  grin, and two little carved hands meeting low on the belly — a belted weeble with a
  cap. Belly + hands + mushroom-cap is the unmistakable read (no hands on source).
- **Chibi + triad carved anatomy (elevated).** Carved bog-oak grandpa: barrel torso
  (dark-core under-belly -> fill -> top-left belly sheen), domed mushroom-cap with a
  carved brim-groove, two huge carved bug-eyes (pupil-less, a bright rim-sheen reads
  as the "stare"), fat carved bulb-nose, a stern flat grin, two stubby hands carved in
  low relief on the belly with finger-grooves. Elevated detail: belly grain-swirl,
  cap brim bevel, finger grooves, weather-pitting stipple (sparse).
- **Pillar (grounded).** The barrel body = the pillar — a stout WOOD column with a
  couple of carved belt-bands and the belly grain as the repeat; cap = a smaller
  mirrored grandpa-head (cap + eyes) at the gap, eyes glowing. Bottom-heavy barrel ->
  zero top-heavy risk; on-axis, fattest of the 8.
- **Palette (distinct).** BLACKENED BOG-OAK `(96,80,68)` base / `(60,48,42)` shade
  (the DARKEST wood of the 8, deep brown-charcoal); one saturated accent = TURMERIC-
  GOLD painted cap + belt-bands `(224,168,60)` (warm gold mass, pops hard on the dark
  oak); pale-LICHEN mint-grey patches `(168,176,150)` (aged tell, a hair greyer-green
  than any glow so it can't be read as Kitsune mint); warm-cream eye glow
  `(246,224,168)`. Reads dark bog-oak + turmeric gold.

## 4. Muljang — the prow-rider ship figurehead spirit  [SHIP'S FIGUREHEAD]
- **Folklore.** A carved-WOOD ship's **figurehead**: bug-eyed toothy apotropaic faces
  on Viking/Norse prows, the spirit of the vessel warding harsh seas. The forward-
  LEANING guardian — body pitched out over a curling prow, the only dynamic-lean
  silhouette of the 8.
- **Thesis.** The figurehead that leans INTO the wind — a carved bust thrust forward
  off a curling wave-scroll prow, wind-swept carved hair streaming back; reads as
  motion where every other post stands rigid (its anti-source distinctness).
- **32px silhouette.** A forward-pitched carved BUST at the top — face tilted up and
  out, a fan of swept-back carved hair/beard streaming behind, two wide eyes, a bold
  grin; below it a big carved C-SCROLL wave-curl (the prow) the bust rides. Lean +
  trailing-hair-fan + scroll-base is the tell.
- **Chibi + triad carved anatomy (elevated).** Carved teak figurehead: chunky chibi
  bust pitched ~15deg forward (dark-core jaw-under -> teak fill -> top-left cheek
  sheen), swept hair as hard triad blade-locks fanning back, wide bug-eyes with carved
  rings, a broad confident grin with carved teeth, a carved chest-medallion. Elevated
  detail: blade-lock hair facets, the C-scroll wave with carved foam-curls, gouge
  marks along the grain.
- **Pillar (grounded).** The prow = the pillar — a carved WOOD wave-SCROLL column,
  the repeat is stacked foam-curl scrolls (hard triad commas); cap = a smaller
  mirrored figurehead-head at the gap, eyes lit. The lean is balanced by the heavy
  scroll base so the gap-cap isn't top-heavy; on-axis spine through the scrolls.
- **Palette (distinct).** GOLDEN-TAN TEAK `(204,158,98)` base / `(154,108,56)` shade;
  one saturated accent = SEA-TEAL painted scroll-foam + eye-paint `(58,138,140)` (a
  deeper sea-teal, kept off the cool glows by being a flat carved-paint band on the
  prow); CORAL-RED carved lip + medallion `(216,96,76)` (small warm focal); pale
  salt-bleach `(212,200,170)` along the wind-edges. Reads teak + sea-teal + coral lip.

## 5. Totjang — the stacked-ancestor face-stack  [PNW-style TOTEM stack, generic]
- **Folklore.** The world's **stacked-face totem** form (PNW cedar poles + Asmat
  ancestor stacks share it): multiple carved faces standing one atop another up a
  single pole. The COLUMN-OF-FACES — the only multi-face-body silhouette; where
  source has ONE big face + a column, this IS a column made of faces.
- **Thesis.** A living totem whose whole BODY is a stack of three chibi ancestor
  faces, each a little different mood (fierce / surprised / grinning); reads as a
  carved "face tower," the most overtly TOTEM of the 8.
- **32px silhouette.** A single vertical post divided into 3 stacked square FACE-
  blocks, each with its own pair of eyes + grin, a small carved wing/ear flange
  poking out at each seam; topped by a flat carved beak-snout block. Three-stacked-
  faces is the unmistakable read.
- **Chibi + triad carved anatomy (elevated).** Carved cool-walnut totem: 3 face
  blocks, each triad-lit (dark socket cores -> walnut fill -> top-left brow sheen),
  bug-eyes + peg-tooth grins, a beaky top face with a flat jutting bill, small carved
  wing-flanges (hard triad lobes) at the seams. Elevated detail: deep carved seam-
  grooves between blocks, ovoid eye-carves (PNW formline nod, simplified), grain
  courses, the top beak bevels.
- **Pillar (grounded).** The face-stack IS the pillar — each face-block is the
  repeatable band; cap = one more mirrored face-block + a small beak finial at the
  gap, top eyes glowing. Naturally vertical + symmetric, evenly weighted -> cleanest
  modular mirror; never top-heavy.
- **Palette (distinct).** COOL-WALNUT UMBER `(126,96,72)` base / `(82,60,46)` shade
  (cooler/greyer brown than honey-cedar or teak); one saturated accent = OXIDE-RED
  painted face-paint stripes + eye-rings `(184,72,56)` (a brick oxide red as thin
  carved stripes, NOT a mass — kept off Karakasa oxblood by value/placement: thin
  stripes on walnut, not a body fill); BLACK-INK formline accents on the brows;
  TEAL-green seam-paint `(70,134,120)` (one cool stripe); warm eye glow
  `(240,206,140)`. Reads walnut + oxide-red stripes + teal seams.

## 6. Hyeoljang — the tongue-out warrior guardian  [MAORI-style TEKOTEKO / pou]
- **Folklore.** The carved warrior guardian-post form (Maori **pou/tekoteko**):
  bulged eyes, top-knot, a defiant PROTRUDING TONGUE (whetero, the haka challenge),
  challenging stance. The only TONGUE-OUT defiance silhouette — comedy + menace in one.
- **Thesis.** A pugnacious little carved warrior-post sticking its big flat TONGUE out
  at you — bug-eyes crossed in challenge, a top-knot crest; the funniest-fierce of the
  8, scary-cute by pure attitude.
- **32px silhouette.** A square fierce FACE-post: huge round goggle eyes, a top-KNOT
  bun crest, and a big flat carved TONGUE flopping down out of a wide grimace
  (dominant lower-face mass), short carved arms hugged to the chest. Tongue-flop +
  top-knot is the tell, distinct from source's closed fanged grin.
- **Chibi + triad carved anatomy (elevated).** Carved red-totara warrior: blocky chibi
  body, big square head, two bulge goggle-eyes with carved spiral rings, a carved
  top-knot crest (hard triad lobe), a wide grimace with a fat flat tongue carved as a
  beveled paddle (dark-core root -> red-totara fill -> top sheen tip), tiny carved
  clenched fists at the chest. Elevated detail: SPIRAL carved cheek-grooves (koru-ish,
  simplified, sparse), tongue bevel, top-knot binding grooves.
- **Pillar (grounded).** The warrior body = the pillar — a stout WOOD column carved
  with sparse spiral grooves as the repeat; cap = a smaller mirrored tongue-out face
  at the gap, eyes + tongue lit. Bottom-rooted blocky body -> not top-heavy; on-axis.
- **Palette (distinct).** RUDDY RED-TOTARA `(166,96,68)` base / `(120,62,44)` shade
  (a reddish-brown WOOD tone — the warmest-brown of the 8, distinct from oxblood by
  being a wood-brown body not a paint); one saturated accent = ABALONE eye-inlay,
  PAUA shimmer faked as a flat TEAL-to-violet two-tone eye-ring `(64,150,150)` rim +
  `(110,96,160)` inner (the iconic paua inlay — its cool counterweight); TONGUE-PINK
  carved tongue `(214,108,118)` (warm focal, the gag); cream binding. Reads red-totara
  + paua eyes + pink tongue.

## 7. Cheonjang — the thunderbird-crest sky guardian  [carved THUNDERBIRD post]
- **Thesis (composite-folklore: PNW thunderbird crest atop a guardian post).** The
  only WINGED-CREST post — a fierce carved face crowned by a wide spread-WING crest
  instead of a hat-block; the silhouette goes WIDE at the top like Cernun's antlers
  but in flat carved wing-planks, not horn.
- **Thesis line.** A guardian-post whose hat is a pair of outstretched carved wings —
  a thunderbird brooding over the gap, the widest-top read of the 8.
- **32px silhouette.** A narrow fierce carved FACE with a hooked beak-nose, two bulge
  eyes, and above/behind it a wide symmetric pair of carved WING-PLANKS spreading out
  (a flat fan-crown read), small carved talon-feet at the base. Wing-crown + hooked
  beak is the tell (wide top, but flat plank-wings, distinct from Cernun's birch-bone
  antler forks).
- **Chibi + triad carved anatomy (elevated).** Carved ash-rose weathered wood: chibi
  face plate (dark socket cores -> ash-rose fill -> top-left brow sheen), hooked carved
  beak-nose, bug-eyes with carved rings, wide grin; the WINGS are stacked rows of hard
  triad feather-planks fanning symmetrically, each plank dark-core -> fill -> tip
  sheen. Elevated detail: layered feather-plank courses, beak bevel, carved

- **Pillar (grounded).** The post body = the pillar — a WOOD column carved with the
  chest lightning-zigzag as the repeat band; cap = a smaller mirrored beak-face with a
  TIGHT folded two-feather crest (NOT the full wingspan — folded tight to the axis so

- **Palette (distinct).** ASH-ROSE WEATHERED WOOD `(178,150,140)` base / `(132,104,98)`
  shade (a unique greyed dusty-rose driftwood — clearly distinct from source's

  zigzag paint `(58,166,156)` BUT pinned as the prominent crown mass (its cool
  counterweight); RUST-OCHRE beak + brow paint `(196,118,58)` (warm); pale chalk
  feather-edges `(220,206,196)`; amber eye glow. Reads ash-rose + turquoise wings.

## 8. Josanjang — the ancestor-stack on a canoe-prow base  [ASMAT BISJ pole]
- **Folklore.** Asmat **bisj** ancestor-pole: stacked carved ancestor figures topped
  by an openwork curling PROW-FLANGE (the cemen), carved from one mangrove trunk to
  ferry the dead. The only OPENWORK-FLANGE silhouette — a lacy carved fin breaking the
  hard-block lane.
- **Thesis.** A single carved ancestor squatting atop a tall pole, crowned by a big
  openwork curling PROW-FIN that loops out to one side like a comma-banner; the most
  asymmetric-but-balanced, ornamental of the 8.
- **32px silhouette.** A slim pole; near the top a squat carved chibi ANCESTOR figure
  (knees up, hands on knees, big head), and ABOVE it a large openwork CURL-FIN (a flat
  carved spiral with cut-out holes) arcing up and over — the cut-out fin-curl is the
  unmistakable read, nobody else has negative-space carving.
- **Chibi + triad carved anatomy (elevated).** Carved bone-white oak: squat ancestor
  (dark-core lap -> bleached-oak fill -> top-left knee sheen), big head with bug-eyes
  + a calm peg grin + a carved nose-bar, knees drawn up with carved hands on them; the
  PROW-FIN is a flat spiral plank with 2-3 carved cut-out lobes, each lobe rim
  triad-lit. Elevated detail: cut-out lobe rims (the only openwork in the set), carved
  rib-lines on the torso, fin spiral grain.
- **Pillar (grounded).** The pole + ancestor = the pillar — a slim WOOD shaft, the
  repeat is carved horizontal lash-bands (rattan ties); cap = a smaller mirrored
  ancestor head + a tight mini prow-fin curl at the gap, eyes lit. The big fin is flat
  + side-arcing so it doesn't top-heavy (mass stays near the axis); on-axis shaft.
- **Palette (distinct).** SUN-BLEACHED BONE-WHITE OAK `(214,202,178)` base /
  `(168,154,124)` shade (the PALEST wood of the 8 — driftwood gone near-white,
  clearly distinct from source's grey and #1's pale pine by being warmer-bleached);
  one saturated accent = OCHRE-CLAY + WHITE-LIME painted ritual stripes
  `(200,128,52)` ochre + `(238,232,216)` lime (the bisj clay/lime two-tone, as carved
  stripe-paint not a mass); CHARCOAL-BLACK carved socket-pits `(46,40,38)`; warm-amber
  eye glow. Reads bleached bone-oak + ochre/lime stripes.
