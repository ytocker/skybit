# Jangseung-versions — LOCKED 5 (per-concept briefs)

Spin-off epic bosses off the shipped **Jangseung** (carved guardian totem-post). Carved-WOOD
lane; each is its own creature KIND + wood tone + paint accent. Full rationale + cuts in
`brainstorm_ad.md`; per-survivor briefs there are canonical — condensed here.

**House style (elevated):** chibi + scary-CUTE + flat procedural triad (dark-core→flat-fill→
top-left rim-sheen) + 1–2px ink keyline `(28,22,30)` + 1px outline; procedural-only; pushed
EPIC (bigger / more carved detail / glow) at SS=5–6 → smoothscale. Lineage renderer template:
`docs/skybit_devil/batch2/jiangshi_epic/jangseung/render_jangseung.py` (STANDALONE pygame — no
game.* import needed). Pillar is the carved body itself for all five; mirror clean, no top-heavy
cap. Legible at true 32px day+night.

**CROSS-SET HARD PIN (police every round):** three teal/green accents must stay separated by
PLACEMENT + VALUE, never grown into a second mass — **Haedung** jade = SCALE-BAND repeat,
**Muljang** sea-teal = PROW-FOAM band only (deeper, cooler), **Hyeoljang** paua = tiny two-tone
EYE-RING inlay (smallest). All eye glows stay WARM (amber/cream); no cool glows borrowed.
Keep Muljang's teak a hair cooler/greyer than Haedung's honey-cedar (closest wood pair).

---

### 1. haedung — fire-eating guardian-lion post
- **Palette.** Honey-CEDAR `(198,150,84)`/sh`(150,104,52)`; JADE scale+mane-tip band
  `(72,150,118)` (blue-leaning, band only); EMBER mouth glow `(238,128,64)` (warm focal);
  gold bell `(222,184,86)`; warm eye glow; ink eyes.
- **Pillar.** Squat scaled cedar column = body; fish-scale courses = repeat; cap = smaller
  mirrored lion-mask, ember mouth lit. Keep column visibly tall (wide mask must not overweight).
- **Hook.** The fire-EATER gag — a tiny carved flame-curl licking from ONE mouth corner; the soul.
- **Re-spec (HARD).** MATTE wood, no glaze/crackle (anti-Zhenmushou); simple round bug-eyes w/
  single carved ring, NOT concentric goggles (anti-Tlaloc); ONE stubby center brow-horn (no
  pair); mane = sparse ring of ~6 hard curl-lobes.

### 2. harubang — bellied grandfather post (re-laned to wood) — SET LEAD, most polish
- **Palette.** Blackened BOG-OAK `(96,80,68)`/sh`(60,48,42)` (dark value anchor); TURMERIC-GOLD
  cap+belt `(224,168,60)`; pale lichen-grey patches `(168,176,150)` (greyer than any mint);
  warm-cream eye glow `(246,224,168)`.
- **Pillar.** Barrel body = pillar; belt-bands + belly grain-swirl = repeat; cap = smaller
  mirrored grandpa-head, eyes glowing. Fattest, bottom-heavy — best mirror in the set.
- **Hook.** Pupil-less bulging owl-eyes w/ bright rim-sheen = THE STARE + two little hands
  clasped low on the belly. Hands + belly + mushroom-cap = whole identity; big and few.
- **Re-spec.** Mushroom-cap domed/modest, brim ≤ belly width (no top-heavy brim); weather-pitting
  stipple SPARSE.

### 3. muljang — prow-rider ship figurehead spirit — only MOTION read
- **Palette.** Golden-tan TEAK `(204,158,98)`/sh`(154,108,56)` (a hair cooler/greyer than cedar);
  SEA-TEAL scroll-foam+eye-paint `(58,138,140)` (PROW-FOAM band only, deeper than jade); CORAL-RED
  lip+medallion `(216,96,76)` (small focal); pale salt-bleach `(212,200,170)` wind-edges; warm eye glow.
- **Pillar.** Carved wave-SCROLL column = pillar; stacked foam-curl scrolls (hard triad commas) =
  repeat; cap = smaller mirrored figurehead-head, eyes lit. On-axis spine.
- **Hook.** Forward LEAN into wind + swept-back blade-lock hair fan — the only motion of the five.
- **Re-spec (HARD).** ~15° lean balanced by HEAVY scroll base so gap-cap is NOT top-heavy; teak
  cooler than cedar; hair = ~5 hard blade-locks max.

### 4. hyeoljang — tongue-out warrior guardian — funniest-fierce
- **Palette.** Ruddy RED-TOTARA `(166,96,68)`/sh`(120,62,44)` (warmest-brown WOOD); paua EYE-RING
  `(64,150,150)` rim + `(110,96,160)` inner (smallest teal, two-tone INLAY; violet stays sub-pixel,
  anti-Necrarch); TONGUE-PINK `(214,108,118)` (focal gag); cream binding; warm eye glow.
- **Pillar.** Stout warrior body = pillar; sparse spiral cheek/body grooves = repeat; cap = smaller
  mirrored tongue-out face, eyes+tongue lit. Bottom-rooted, on-axis.
- **Hook.** Big flat TONGUE flopping from a wide grimace + crossed goggle-eyes + top-knot; comedy
  IS the menace. Tongue dominates lower-face mass.
- **Re-spec.** Paua ring is a flat PAINT ring w/ hard edge, NOT a glow (anti-Yurei/Kitsune cool
  glow); spiral grooves SPARSE.

### 5. sotjang — sky-bird sentinel pole — only top-finial/bird read
- **Palette.** Pale-bleached PINE pole `(196,178,140)`/sh`(150,128,92)`; duck body warmer HONEY-TAN
  `(206,160,96)`; INDIGO neck-band+bill stripe `(58,86,138)` (flat carved PAINT, not a glow; clear
  of Yurei blue-cyan); warm-amber eye glow `(244,200,120)`; pale lichen-grey `(176,182,156)`. No cinnabar.
- **Pillar.** Slim turned-pine shaft = pillar; lathe ring-bands + a couple prayer-cloth knots
  BOTTOM-WEIGHT it; cap = a SECOND SMALLER mirrored duck facing the gap, bill lit.
- **Hook.** Grumpy sacred DUCK glaring down off a heaven-pole — comic underbite bill + saucer eye.
- **Re-spec (HARD — set's only top-heavy risk).** Gap-cap duck CLEARLY smaller (≈70%) tucked to
  axis; lathe ring-bands + knots visibly bottom-weight the shaft; body a clean OVAL blob,
  feather-courses SPARSE (3–4). If it reads top-heavy at thumbnail, slim the body before detailing.
