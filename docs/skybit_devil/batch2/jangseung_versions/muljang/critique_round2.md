## Verification against the brief (sampled the committed PNG)

1. **Cap value matches the teak body — CONFIRMED.** Cap-head teak median **L=162**, body
   teak median **L=162** — identical. The body fill samples on-spec at `(201,155,96)`.
   Zero cream-plate pixels (L>215) in either mass; the R1 L204 cream face plate is gone.
   Salt-bleach is now a thin warm light-teak rim at `(216,184,143) L=189` (~27 L over the
   fill, inside the ≤35 rule) confined to wind-edge slivers, not a full-cheek wash. The
   cap carries slightly more rim ratio than the body (~24% vs ~11%) — that is the intended
   warm night-lift, not a separate pale mask. The "creature IS the pillar" wood read holds.

2. **EPIC motion at a glance + 32px legible — CONFIRMED.** The ~15° forward lean + swept
   blade-lock fan is the only motion read in the set and lands instantly even in the night
   chip. The night-sky 32px chip reads as leaning-head + scroll-base with the warm eyes +
   coral lip anchoring the dark read; the day chip reads clean. Two-eye face survives both.

3. **House style intact — CONFIRMED.** Elevated chibi/scary-cute, flat procedural triad +
   ink keyline, no glaze/raster. Lean is balanced by the heavy stacked scroll base — not
   top-heavy. Teak reads a hair cooler/greyer than Haedung's honey-cedar will. Sea-teal is
   held to the prow-foam scroll commas + eye-paint band (deeper/cooler than jade), coral is
   a single medallion dot + lip focal, eye glow is warm. Cross-set pins clean.

4. **Pillar mirror + bottom-rooted — CONFIRMED.** On-axis spine; gap-cap is a smaller
   mirrored figurehead head; stacked foam-curl scroll mass weights the base. Distinct from
   the Jangseung source (it's the only leaning/motion member) and from the four siblings.
