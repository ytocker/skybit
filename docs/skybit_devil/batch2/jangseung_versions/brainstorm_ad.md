# Jangseung-versions — AD brainstorm-critique (cull 8 → LOCKED 5)

VERDICT: ITERATE

(Brainstorm-critique mode. The 8 are strong material and the wood-lane discipline
is genuinely good — distinct wood tones, distinct paint accents, mostly distinct
silhouette KINDs. But the SET as proposed does NOT clear the bar at 5-as-is:
two survivors collide with the existing roster at 32px, two are near-duplicate
wide-top reads against each other, and one is a top-heavy/pillar liability. The
cull below resolves all of it. Lock the 5 KEEPs with the re-specs and proceed.)

---

## The LOCKED 5 (keep)

1. **Haedung — fire-eating guardian-lion post** (warm-honey CEDAR + jade scales + ember mouth)
2. **Harubang — bellied stone-grandfather post, re-laned to wood** (blackened BOG-OAK + turmeric gold)
3. **Muljang — prow-rider ship figurehead spirit** (golden-tan TEAK + sea-teal + coral lip)
4. **Hyeoljang — tongue-out warrior guardian** (ruddy RED-TOTARA + paua eyes + pink tongue)
5. **Sotjang — sky-bird sentinel pole** (pale-bleached PINE + indigo band)

## The 3 CUTS

- **CUT #5 Totjang (three-stacked-face tower)** — direct silhouette-KIND collision with
  the shipped **Tlaloc-Tiki** pillar, whose prop→pillar is *literally* "stacked-face
  stone totem, each mini-mask a band, feathered-serpent finial." At 32px a column of
  stacked carved faces reads the same KIND whether it's wood or stone; the wood tone
  won't save it because the silhouette is identical. It's also the LEAST character-
  first of the 8 (it's a form, not a creature — "three moods" doesn't survive at
  gameplay scale). Cut.

- **CUT #7 Cheonjang (wide wing-crest beak-face)** — two problems. (a) Wide-top winged
  silhouette duplicates the wide-top read already owned by **Kitsune** (nine-tail fan)
  and **Cernun** (antler crown) — the brief itself concedes it "goes WIDE at the top
  like Cernun's antlers." (b) It carries the same top-heavy gap-cap liability that
  forced the awkward "fold the wings tight" workaround on Cernun and Raijin; we don't
  need a third instance of that fix in a sub-roster of 5. The thunderbird hook is nice
  but the silhouette lane is crowded and the pillar is a fight. Cut.

- **CUT #8 Josanjang (squat-ancestor-under-openwork-fin)** — the openwork cut-out fin is
  the most beautiful idea here AND the riskiest at 32px: negative-space carving turns to
  noise/ambiguous blob at gameplay scale, and the asymmetric side-arcing comma-fin is the
  hardest mirror in the set (the brief admits it's "the most asymmetric"). It's also the
  PALEST wood (bone-white oak) sitting one lane over from Sotjang's pale-pine — a value-
  proximity risk if both survive. Between the two pale woods, Sotjang has the stronger
  one-of-a-kind silhouette (bird-on-pole) and the cleaner mirror, so it wins the pale
  lane and Josanjang is cut. (If the user wants the openwork idea later, it's a great
  candidate for a one-off, but it doesn't belong in a legibility-first set of 5.)

## Ranking with rationale (survivors, strongest first)

1. **Harubang** — the standout. Bellied weeble-with-hands is the most original,
   most instantly-likable, most bottom-rooted (zero top-heavy risk), and the darkest
   wood anchors the whole set's value spread. Cuddliest-yet-stern = perfect scary-cute.
2. **Hyeoljang** — pure attitude. Tongue-out top-knot warrior is the funniest-fierce
   read, a one-of-a-kind silhouette, and red-totara + paua + pink tongue is a gorgeous,
   roster-clear palette. Character-first by construction.
3. **Haedung** — the fierce one the set needs. Wide one-horn lion-mask + ember-mouth
   fire-eater grin gives the menace counterweight to the cute three; honey-cedar + jade
   + ember is clean and bottom-rooted.
4. **Muljang** — the only motion silhouette (forward lean), which earns its slot on
   distinctness alone; teak + sea-teal + coral is lovely. Slight pillar caution (lean
   must not top-heavy the scroll cap — already addressed in its brief, hold the line).
5. **Sotjang** — wins the pale-wood lane and gives the set its only top-weighted/finial
   read. The grumpy sacred duck is charming; watch top-heaviness hardest here (it's the
   one genuinely top-weighted silhouette of the five — see re-spec).

## Cross-set checks on the surviving 5

**Silhouette KINDs — all distinct, no near-duplicate pairs:**
lion-mask (Haedung) / bellied-weeble-grandpa (Harubang) / leaning-figurehead-on-scroll
(Muljang) / tongue-out-square-warrior (Hyeoljang) / bird-blob-on-pole (Sotjang). None
reads as another at 32px; none reads as the source's tall-narrow-hat-block-face-column.
Cutting Totjang + Cheonjang removed both the stacked-face and the wide-wing-crest reads,
which were the two crowded lanes.

**Wood tones — well separated, good value spread:**
honey-CEDAR (Haedung, warm mid) / bog-OAK (Harubang, darkest) / teak (Muljang, golden-
tan) / red-TOTARA (Hyeoljang, ruddy/warmest-brown) / pale PINE (Sotjang, lightest).
Dark anchor + pale anchor + three warm-mids = a clean value ladder. **One watch:**
cedar (Haedung) and teak (Muljang) are the closest pair in hue/value — both warm golden-
browns. They DON'T collide because the silhouettes are wildly different (wide beast-mask
vs leaning figurehead) and the accents differ (jade+ember vs sea-teal+coral), but DO NOT
let either drift warmer-amber in execution or they'll converge. Keep Muljang's teak a
hair COOLER/greyer than Haedung's honey-cedar to bank the separation.

**Paint accents — all distinct, no shared hero mass:**
jade scales + ember mouth (Haedung) / turmeric gold (Harubang) / sea-teal + coral lip
(Muljang) / paua teal-violet eyes + pink tongue (Hyeoljang) / indigo band (Sotjang).
Greens/teals are the crowded family here: Haedung jade-scale, Muljang sea-teal,
Hyeoljang paua-teal-eye. **HARD pin (most important cross-set fix):** these three teal/
green accents must stay separated by PLACEMENT + VALUE, never grown into a second mass —
Haedung jade as a SCALE-BAND repeat (mid-green, blue-leaning), Muljang sea-teal as a
PROW-FOAM band only (deeper), Hyeoljang paua as a tiny two-tone EYE-RING inlay (smallest,
with the violet inner to pull it off pure teal). If any of the three becomes a body-area
fill they'll read as the same accent at 32px. This is the single thing most likely to
collapse the set — police it every round.

**Roster + source collisions — clear:**
- Source Jangseung (slate-wood + cinnabar-hanja column + twin-bug-eye hat-block face):
  none of the 5 reuses slate-grey, the cinnabar-hanja column, or the hat-block face KIND.
- Tlaloc (grey STONE goggle-mask / stacked-face totem): the colliding Totjang is CUT;
  the 5 all read WOOD, not stone. Haedung's lion-mask is a WIDE maned beast, not a flat
  goggle eye-ring mask — clear. Pin: Haedung's eyes are bug-eyes with carved rings, NOT
  concentric goggle eye-rings (that's Tlaloc's tell) — keep them simple round.
- Zhenmushou (amber CERAMIC/glaze beast): Haedung is the only other beast — keep it
  matte CARVED-WOOD cedar with hard bevel facets, NO glaze pooling / crackle seams /
  kiln sheen. Different body too (face-post vs seated quadruped). Clear if matte.
- Karakasa (oilpaper parasol object-yokai): no overlap.
- Owned hues avoided: no Necrarch violet-mass (Hyeoljang's violet is a sub-pixel eye-
  inner sliver only — keep it tiny), no Draugr/Yurei blue-cyan glow, no Kitsune mint
  glow, no Raijin storm-grey, no Cernun deep-pine (Haedung jade is blue-leaning mid-
  green, not pine). All eye glows stay WARM (amber/cream) — pin this so no survivor
  borrows a cool glow.

**Pillars — all mirror clean; one real watch:**
Haedung (bottom-rooted scaled column, beast-mask cap), Harubang (fattest barrel body,
grandpa-head cap), Muljang (scroll-weighted base, figurehead cap), Hyeoljang (bottom-
rooted blocky body, tongue-face cap) all ground in their own forms and mirror cleanly.
**Sotjang is the one top-heavy risk in the 5** (the duck blob IS top-weighted by design)
— its brief's fix (lathe ring-bands bottom-weighting the slim pole + a SMALLER mirrored
duck at the gap) must be held hard. See re-spec.

## Locking briefs (per survivor)

### 1. Haedung — fire-eating guardian-lion post
- **Palette (locked).** Warm-honey CEDAR `(198,150,84)` / shade `(150,104,52)`;
  accent JADE-GREEN scale + mane-tip band `(72,150,118)` (blue-leaning mid-green, as a
  SCALE-BAND repeat, never a body fill); EMBER-ORANGE swallowed-flame mouth glow
  `(238,128,64)` (the warm focal — eye goes here first); gold bell `(222,184,86)`;
  WARM eye glow; ink eyes.
- **Pillar.** Squat scaled cedar column = body; hard fish-scale courses as the repeat
  band; cap = smaller mirrored lion-mask at the gap, ember mouth lit. Bottom-rooted, on-
  axis. Hold the wide-mask top from overweighting by keeping the column visibly tall.
- **Character hook.** The fire-EATER gag — a tiny carved flame-curl licking from ONE
  mouth corner. That single detail is the soul; make it read at 1×.
- **Re-spec (HARD).** (a) MATTE carved wood only — no glaze/crackle/kiln sheen (anti-
  Zhenmushou). (b) Eyes are simple round bug-eyes with a single carved ring — NOT
  concentric goggle rings (anti-Tlaloc). (c) ONE center brow-horn, stubby — resist a
  horn PAIR (pair drifts toward Zhenmushou's twin horns). (d) Mane = a sparse RING of
  ~6 hard curl-lobes max, not a fuzzy fringe.

### 2. Harubang — bellied grandfather post (re-laned to wood)
- **Palette (locked).** Blackened BOG-OAK `(96,80,68)` / shade `(60,48,42)` (the set's
  dark value anchor); accent TURMERIC-GOLD cap + belt-bands `(224,168,60)` (warm gold
  mass, pops on the dark oak); pale lichen-grey patches `(168,176,150)` (keep this a
  hair GREYER than any mint glow — it must not read as Kitsune mint); WARM-cream eye
  glow `(246,224,168)`.
- **Pillar.** Barrel body = pillar; carved belt-bands + belly grain-swirl as the repeat;
  cap = smaller mirrored grandpa-head (cap + eyes) at the gap, eyes glowing. Fattest of
  the 5, bottom-heavy — zero top-heavy risk. Best mirror in the set.
- **Character hook.** Pupil-less bulging owl-eyes with a bright rim-sheen reading as
  THE STARE, plus the two little hands clasped low on the belly. Hands + belly + mushroom-
  cap is the whole identity — keep all three big and few.
- **Re-spec.** Mushroom-cap must NOT grow into a wide brim that top-heavies the read —
  domed and modest, brim ≤ belly width. Weather-pitting stipple stays SPARSE (no face-
  fuzz at 1×). This is the set's lead — give it the most polish budget.

### 3. Muljang — prow-rider ship figurehead spirit
- **Palette (locked).** Golden-tan TEAK `(204,158,98)` / shade `(154,108,56)` — keep a
  hair COOLER/greyer than Haedung's honey-cedar (banks the closest-pair separation);
  accent SEA-TEAL scroll-foam + eye-paint `(58,138,140)` (PROW-FOAM band only, deeper
  than Haedung's jade); CORAL-RED lip + medallion `(216,96,76)` (small warm focal); pale
  salt-bleach `(212,200,170)` along wind-edges; WARM eye glow.
- **Pillar.** Carved wave-SCROLL column = pillar; stacked foam-curl scrolls (hard triad
  commas) as the repeat; cap = smaller mirrored figurehead-head at the gap, eyes lit.
  On-axis spine through the scrolls.
- **Character hook.** The forward LEAN into the wind + swept-back blade-lock hair fan —
  the only motion read of the 5. That dynamism is its whole reason to survive; push it.
- **Re-spec (HARD).** The ~15° lean must be balanced by a HEAVY scroll base so the gap-
  cap is NOT top-heavy (the lean leans toward the gap, mass stays low). Keep teak cooler
  than cedar. Hair = ~5 hard blade-locks max, not a streaming fringe (noise at 1×).

### 4. Hyeoljang — tongue-out warrior guardian
- **Palette (locked).** Ruddy RED-TOTARA `(166,96,68)` / shade `(120,62,44)` (warmest-
  brown WOOD body — distinct from any oxblood PAINT by being a wood tone); accent paua
  EYE-RING `(64,150,150)` rim + `(110,96,160)` inner (smallest teal accent of the three —
  a two-tone INLAY, never a mass; the violet inner stays a sub-pixel sliver, anti-
  Necrarch); TONGUE-PINK `(214,108,118)` (warm focal, the gag); cream binding; WARM eye
  glow.
- **Pillar.** Stout warrior body = pillar; sparse spiral cheek/body grooves as the repeat;
  cap = smaller mirrored tongue-out face at the gap, eyes + tongue lit. Bottom-rooted,
  on-axis.
- **Character hook.** The big flat TONGUE flopping out of a wide grimace + crossed goggle-
  eyes + top-knot. Funniest-fierce in the set — comedy IS the menace. Make the tongue the
  dominant lower-face mass.
- **Re-spec.** Paua eye-ring is the riskiest teal (closest to a "cool inlay glow") — keep
  it a flat PAINT ring with a hard edge, not a glow, so it can't be confused with Yurei/
  Kitsune cool glows. Spiral grooves SPARSE.

### 5. Sotjang — sky-bird sentinel pole
- **Palette (locked).** Pale-bleached PINE pole `(196,178,140)` / shade `(150,128,92)`;
  duck body warmer HONEY-TAN `(206,160,96)`; accent INDIGO-BLUE neck-band + bill stripe
  `(58,86,138)` (flat carved PAINT mass, not a glow — clear of Yurei blue-cyan); WARM-
  amber eye glow `(244,200,120)`; pale lichen-grey `(176,182,156)`. No cinnabar (separates
  from source).
- **Pillar.** Slim turned-pine shaft = pillar; carved lathe ring-bands + a couple of
  prayer-cloth knots BOTTOM-WEIGHT it; cap = a SECOND SMALLER mirrored duck facing the
  gap, bill lit, eye glowing.
- **Character hook.** Grumpy sacred DUCK glaring down off a heaven-pole — comic underbite
  bill + saucer eye. The only top-finial / bird read of the 5; one-of-a-kind in the roster.
- **Re-spec (HARD — this is the set's only top-heavy risk).** The duck body is top-
  weighted by design, so: (a) the gap-cap duck must be CLEARLY SMALLER than the top duck
  (≈70%) and tucked tight to the axis; (b) the lathe ring-bands + knots must visibly
  bottom-weight the shaft so the silhouette doesn't tip; (c) keep the body a clean OVAL
  blob — feather-course facets SPARSE (3–4 courses) so the wing doesn't turn to noise at
  1×. If at thumbnail it reads top-heavy, slim the body before adding any detail.

## What's working across the set (keep)
- Wood-lane discipline is excellent — distinct tones + distinct accents + a real value
  ladder (dark bog-oak anchor → pale pine anchor → three warm-mids).
- Character-first hooks are concrete and survive at small scale (the stare, the tongue,
  the fire-curl, the lean, the grumpy duck).
- Pillar groundedness is largely earned by the carved-body-IS-pillar logic; four of five
  mirror trivially.

## Why ITERATE not SHIP-READY
The 8-as-proposed shipped two roster collisions (Totjang/Tlaloc stacked-face, Cheonjang/
Cernun-Kitsune wide-wing) and a pillar liability (Josanjang openwork). With those cut and
the three re-specs + the teal/green placement pin applied, the LOCKED 5 above DO clear the
bar. Lock these 5 and run the per-concept loops; the single thing to police across all
five is the **teal/green-accent placement+value separation** (Haedung jade scale-band /
Muljang sea-teal foam-band / Hyeoljang paua eye-ring) — none may grow into a second mass.
