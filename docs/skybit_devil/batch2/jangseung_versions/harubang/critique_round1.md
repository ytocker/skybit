# Harubang — ROUND 1 critique (SET LEAD)

VERDICT: ITERATE

This is a strong, characterful first pass that already nails the house style and
the night read. It is NOT ship-ready: the GD's own flag is correct and decisive —
the belly-hands die at 32px, and since the brief makes hands one of the three
identity pillars, the lead cannot ship until the hands survive gameplay scale.
One round of surgical fixes should get it there.

---

## RULING ON THE OPEN QUESTION (hands at 32px) — most important

**The hands MUST be enlarged and lifted off the belt-band. The stare+barrel+cap
do NOT carry the identity alone — and they especially can't for the SET LEAD.**

Reasoning:
- At true 32px (both day and night chips) the two clasped hands have already
  merged into the lower turmeric belt-band. They read as a slightly lumpy gold
  stripe, not as hands. That means the silhouette currently says "barrel totem
  with big eyes and a gold hat" — which is a perfectly good Jangseung descendant,
  but it is NOT yet "bellied GRANDFATHER." The hands are what make him a little
  old man hugging his own belly; lose them and you lose the warmth and the gag.
- Stare + barrel + cap alone is also dangerously close to the source Jangseung's
  own 32px read (bug-eyes + post + topper). The hands are a primary point of
  DISTINCTION from the parent, not a nice-to-have.
- The brief's "big and few" instruction was written precisely so that all three
  identity beats survive downscale. Right now only two of three do. That's a fail
  against the brief as written, not a judgment call.

**How to fix (do all of these):**
1. **Lift the hands OFF the belt.** Move the clasped hands UP into the mid-belly,
   well clear of the lower belt-band, and carve a hard dark BOG-OAK-shade gap
   (bevel-groove value) between the hands and the band so the two never touch and
   never merge at 1x. The band reads as the band; the hands read as a separate
   warmer/lighter mass above it.
2. **Fewer, fatter fingers.** Drop to 3 fat sausage fingers per hand (or even
   read them as two mitten-blobs with a single divider groove). The current 4–5
   thin fingers are pure noise at 32px. Big and few.
3. **Give the hands their OWN value, not the belt's gold.** Render the hands in
   the oak-sheen / lichen-touched wood family with a bright top-left rim-sheen so
   they pop as a pale-ish blob against the dark belly — do NOT paint them gold, or
   they will re-merge with the gold band. The gold stays cap + belt only.
4. After the fix, re-cut the 32px day+night chips and confirm THREE blobs read in
   the body: gold cap-eyes mass / dark belly / pale clasped-hands mass. If the
   hands still vanish, go bigger before you go more detailed.

## FIX (beyond the hands)

- **Weather-pitting is slightly too busy on the hero belly.** At full size the
  stipple is fine, but it adds low-value noise that competes with the hands you're
  about to enlarge. Thin it ~30% and cluster it (a few deliberate pits near edges
  and the lower belly), never an even all-over speckle. Sparse, per the re-spec.
- **Lichen patches read a touch BLUE/mint at full-res in the lower-left clusters.**
  They're correct in the palette swatch but the on-model patches drift cooler than
  the chip. Push them a hair warmer/greyer so there's zero risk of a Kitsune-mint
  reading on a green day-sky. Keep them clustered lobes, never noise.
- **Cap brim ↔ belly relationship is good but the brim's lower edge is a little
  flat/straight** — give it the same gentle dome curve as the crown so the whole
  cap reads unmistakably as a soft MUSHROOM, reinforcing the third identity beat.
