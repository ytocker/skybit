# Sotjang — sky-bird sentinel pole · ROUND 1 critique (Art Director)

VERDICT: ITERATE

Strong, distinctive lead — the only top-finial/bird read in the Jangseung set
lands as a genuinely memorable character, and the set's one top-heavy risk is
*nearly* solved. It clears RE-ROLL comfortably; it does not yet clear SHIP-READY
because the 32px top-heavy read is borderline-but-not-confirmed and two
house-style finish items need a pass. One more round nails it.

---

## Ranking of aspects (most important fix first)

### 1. TOP-HEAVY CHECK (the set's only risk) — BORDERLINE, must be confirmed
This is the gate and it is the closest call in the image. The hero (a) is fine —
the graduated lathe ring-bands, the two low prayer-cloth knots, and the splayed
plinth foot DO visibly bottom-weight the shaft, and the body is a clean honey-tan
oval as briefed. Good.

But the 32px gameplay chips (c) are where I'm not yet convinced:
- **Day chip:** the duck head at the top is reading as a small dark blob that is
  *wider than the shaft directly below it*. At 32px the eye sees a lollipop
  (heavy round top on a thin stick) for a beat before the ring-bands register.
  The bottom-weighting cues (rings/knots/foot) are the SMALLEST, lowest-contrast
  marks on the pole at this size — exactly the marks that wash out first. So the
  thing that's supposed to anchor the bottom is the first thing to disappear, and
  the head is the last thing to disappear. That is the classic top-heavy failure
  mode, even if the centroid math is fine.
- **Night chip:** slightly better because the amber eye pulls the read up and
  reads as "intentional glowing top," but the shaft below the head is so thin and
  low-contrast against the night blue that it nearly vanishes — which again leaves
  a floating head, not a rooted pole.

It is not tipping, but it is *flirting* with top-heavy at true scale. Fix by
pushing more dark VALUE mass low (not more detail): the lowest ring-band cluster
and the plinth foot need to be the highest-contrast, widest marks on the whole
pole so they survive downscale and visibly outweigh the head. Right now the foot
is too pale/narrow at 32px.

### 2. Gap-cap duck — confirm the 70% + axis-tuck at SIZE, not just in the hero
In the hero the cap-vs-hero duck ratio looks right (~70%) and bill-lit, good. But
in the assembled pillar strip (center column) the gap-facing cap duck still
overhangs the shaft edge slightly on the bill side. At the gap line — the most
scrutinized silhouette edge in the game — any overhang reads as a snag. Tuck the
cap bill fully inside the shaft's vertical envelope; let the eye/eye-glow be the
only thing that breaks the column width, never the bill.

### 3. Indigo neck-band at 32px — RULING: acceptable as a value break, do NOT chase hue
You flagged that the indigo compresses to ~1px and the HUE isn't legible until
~2x. Ruling: **that is fine, leave the band's JOB as a value separator.** The
amber eye + the underbite-bill duck-on-pole silhouette carry the character read
at 32px exactly as you said, and that's the correct hierarchy — a sacred duck
doesn't need its collar legible to read as a grumpy duck. Forcing the band wider
to make indigo "read" would (a) fatten the neck and worsen item #1's top-heavy
read, and (b) risk the indigo growing into a second mass, which the CROSS-SET PIN
forbids. So: keep it ~1px as a value notch. ONE small ask — make sure at 32px the
band stays a clean DARK notch and doesn't dither into a muddy mid-grey ring; a
crisp dark separation sells "carved collar" without needing the hue.

### 4. Cross-set / palette discipline — CLEAN, keep as-is
- Indigo reads as flat carved PAINT, no bloom — clearly separated from Yurei's
  blue-cyan GLOW. Confirmed clear.
- No cinnabar anywhere; separates cleanly from the source Jangseung (which owns
  the cinnabar hanja). Good.
- Warm-amber eye is the only glow, and it's the night anchor — exactly right.
- Pale-pine shaft vs honey-tan body two-tone reads at distance. Good.
This is the strongest part of the round; don't touch it.

### 5. House-style fidelity — mostly there, two finish items
- Chibi + scary-CUTE: lands. The saucer eye + comic underbite bill = instantly a
  grumpy sacred duck. This is the memorable hook and it's working.
- Flat triad + hard ink keyline + 1px outline: present on the hero. FIX: the
  feather-courses on the hero body are reading as 3 chevron seams as briefed, but
  the lowest seam is close enough to the body-shade core that at mid distance it
  muddies into a smudge rather than a crisp carved groove. Keep 3 seams, but space
  them and let each be a clean hard-edged groove (dark-core line + a thin top-left
  sheen lip), not a soft band.
- Elevated, not grim: yes.

## Iteration directives (prioritized punch list)

1. **Bottom-weight harder for the 32px read.** Make the lowest ring-band cluster +
   the plinth foot the WIDEST and HIGHEST-CONTRAST marks on the entire pole so they
   survive downscale. Widen the plinth foot ~15–20% and darken its core; the foot
   must out-mass the head blob at 32px. Re-shoot both chips to confirm the read
   flips from "lollipop" to "rooted pole."
2. **Slim the head/neck blob ~10% at the silhouette** (not the eye — the eye stays
   the saucer). The head currently reads marginally wider than the shaft at 32px;
   bring its outer envelope closer to shaft width so the top stops winning the
   value fight.
3. **Tuck the gap-cap bill fully inside the shaft envelope** in the assembled
   pillar — no overhang at the gap edge. Eye-glow may break width; bill may not.
4. **Keep the indigo band ~1px as a DARK value notch** — do not widen for hue. Just
   ensure it stays a crisp dark separation at 32px, not a muddy grey dither.
5. **Crisp the 3 feather-course seams** into clean hard grooves (dark-core + thin
   sheen lip); pull the lowest seam off the body-shade core so it doesn't smudge.
6. **Re-confirm both day + night 32px chips** after 1–4. Night is close; day is the
   one to prove. Show me the chip at true 32px (not just the 4x) so I can rule the
   top-heavy gate closed.

Everything in the palette / cross-set lane is locked — spend the round entirely on
the 32px bottom-weighting + finish, not on recoloring.
