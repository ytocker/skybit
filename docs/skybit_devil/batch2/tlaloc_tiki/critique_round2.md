demoted bronze goggle-rings: it is the brightest, warmest, eye-first mass on the

## Strongest / weakest

- Strongest: the focal flip. Coral mouth is now the hero mass with a hot sheen,
  the gold rings dropped to dark bronze read as SHAPE not glowing discs, and the
  warm-coral cue is uniquely Tlaloc's — the green-band separator the concept was
  built around is now doing its job.
- Weakest (cosmetic, not a blocker): the bronze rings are now slightly heavy/dark
  at the very bottom totem band, where reduced contrast lets the two stacked masks
  blur into one at 28px. Acceptable — the hero and gap-cap masks read fine.
