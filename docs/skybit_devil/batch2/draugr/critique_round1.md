# Draugr — Round 1 critique (Batch 2 / Skeletons)

A strong, genuinely COLD read and a clean bottom-heavy block — the helm + round
shield + axe-arm silhouette lands and it sits naturally beside Big Reapy. But two
palette balance issues keep it from ship: the **helm is too large and too neutral
silver**, eating the head and pulling the whole figure toward "grey knight" rather
than "frost-bone undead," and the **ice-cyan is over-applied** (jaw bars + rime
flecks everywhere) so it reads as decoration rather than a focal cold tell. Fix the
helm-to-skull ratio and discipline the cyan, and this ships next round.

## Strongest / weakest
- **Strongest:** the prop→pillar mirror. The bearded-axe haft-down with rune banding
  (\X / X\\ /\\ marks) and the broad crescent head as gap-cap is on-axis, repeatable,
  and centered — exactly the brief's "no top-heavy risk." Best part of the sheet.
- **Weakest:** value/color hierarchy on the hero head. The big smooth silver helm dome
  is the single largest, highest-value mass and steals focus from the frost-bone skull
  + ice jaw that are supposed to be the identity. At 32px it reads "metal helmet,"
  not "frosted barrow-undead."

## Per-aspect KEEP / FIX

**Readability & 32px silhouette — KEEP** the bottom-heavy stance; shield-circle +
helm-dome + axe make a clean three-blob read. **FIX:** at 16/24px the figure collapses
into "grey blob + brown circle" — the skull face and ice jaw vanish entirely, so the
single most identity-bearing element (the frosted dead face) is gone exactly where
legibility matters most. The two thin straight helm-horns also disappear by 24px and
add salt-and-pepper noise at 48px. Make the horns shorter, thicker, and angled like
stubby cattle-horn nubs (brief says "two short STUBBY horns") so they survive downscale.

**Color / PALETTE match — FIX (primary).** The pinned dominant family is frost-bone +
ice-cyan + rust, and the helm should be a *small* bronze-trim accent — but here a huge
cool-grey/slate helm dome dominates the upper half. Right now slate is doing the work
frost-bone `(216,222,224)` should do. Shrink the helm so more frost-bone skull shows,
and warm/darken the helm dome slightly or recolor it toward rust-iron + bronze trim so
the bone reads as the lightest mass, not the helm. Separately the **ice-cyan is too
liberally scattered** (jaw teeth-bars AND dozens of rime dots across mail, shield,
ground) — pin cyan to two places: the glowing jaw and a *restrained* cluster of rime
flecks on the bone/helm crown. As-is the cyan dots read as JPEG noise at 1x and dilute
the focal glow.

**Rust mail — KEEP** the warm rust `(150,96,62)` mail block; it's the right cold-vs-warm
counterweight and the value separates well from frost-bone. **FIX:** the white stud rows
on the mail are too bright and too regular — they're competing with the ice jaw for
attention and read as a second noise field at 32px. Drop their value or thin them.

**Shield — KEEP** the round wooden shield with bronze boss; good warm anchor and reads at
every scale. **FIX:** it currently rivals the head for visual weight because the head is
under-saturated. Once the face/jaw pop, the shield will settle into supporting role —
but also nudge its rim a hair darker so it doesn't tie the helm for "biggest grey-ish
disc."

**Moss crust — FIX:** the moss-green `(98,128,76)` is reduced to a thin sliver at the
feet/base. Brief calls it part of the dominant crust identity ("moss-crusted"). Bring a
few moss patches up onto the shoulders/helm crown so the "grave-mound, ancient" thesis
reads, not just a clean warrior.

**Scary-CUTE menace — KEEP** the wide hollow eye-sockets with tiny cyan pupils — genuinely
cute-creepy. **FIX:** the brow is a bit flat/neutral; a slight angry-arch to the socket
tops or helm nose-guard shadow would add menace without losing the chibi charm.

**Identity / sibling drift — OK.** Reads clearly COLD and distinct from the warm-bone
siblings (Mariachi, Jiangshi jade, Necrarch parchment). No green-band or violet conflict.
Just make sure the slate helm doesn't drift toward Raijin's storm-grey territory — keeping
helm small + bronze-trimmed and letting frost-bone dominate solves this.

**Feasibility — OK.** All triad-lit flat fills; nothing implies raster. Good.

**Accessibility — FIX:** the read currently leans on the ice-cyan hue for "this one is the
cold one." Add a non-hue tell — the frost rime as distinct WHITE-sheen geometry (crystalline
flecks) plus the high frost-bone value gives a value/shape cold-tell independent of cyan.

## Iteration directives (prioritized)
1. **Shrink the helm dome ~25–30% and reveal more frost-bone skull** so bone, not slate,
   is the lightest dominant mass. Recolor/darken the dome toward rust-iron+bronze if it
   still competes. (Palette match — primary fix.)
2. **Discipline the ice-cyan:** confine it to the glowing jaw + a small restrained rime
   cluster on the bone/helm crown. Kill the scattered cyan dots on mail/shield/ground —
   they read as noise at 1x.
3. **Stubbier, thicker, angled horns** that survive 24px downscale; current thin straight
   spikes vanish and add salt-and-pepper at 48px.
4. **Tone down the white mail studs** (lower value or thin the rows) so they stop competing
   with the jaw as a second bright field.
5. **Bring moss up onto shoulders/helm crown** so the "moss-crusted barrow-mound" thesis
   reads, not just a clean warrior; current moss is a base sliver only.
6. **Verify the 16/24px tier still shows a face** after the helm shrink + jaw focus — the
   frosted dead face must survive to the smallest gameplay scale.
7. Keep the axe-pillar exactly as is — it's the sheet's best element.
