# Mukha-Devi brood — LOCKED 5 (per-concept briefs)

Five new epic bosses spun off the shipped **Mukha-Devi** (six-armed wrathful bone-mother, KIND:
radial-fan). Mukha-Devi is this brood's **"Original."** Every concept KEEPS her DNA — **multi-arm +
skull-crown/tiara + elaborate arm-end ornaments with TINY SKULLS among them** — but takes a different
solid-black silhouette archetype, and the five deliberately **span both spread axes** the user chose:
proportion (elevated-chibi → monumental) and theme (pure skeletal → bone-core+distinct-accent).

**House style (citipati lineage):** chibi + scary-CUTE + flat procedural triad (dark-core→flat-fill→
top-left rim-sheen) + 1–2px ink keyline + 1px outline; procedural-only; pushed EPIC at SS=5–6 →
smoothscale. Each sheet: hero + bottom-rooted top↔bottom-mirrored pillar + true-32px day & night chips
+ blacked-out silhouette proof + palette strip. Legible at true 32px day+night.
**Clone DNA from** `docs/skybit_devil/batch2/citipati_versions/mukha_devi/render_mukha_devi.py`.

**CROSS-SET PINS (police every round):**
- Distinct from Mukha (wide symmetric OPEN sideways starburst, magenta-rose glow) and the 4 other
  citipati siblings (Nagaraja serpent-coil/emerald, Stupika stacked-tower/gilt-saffron, Asthi-Garuda
  spread-wing/blood-orange, Koschei seated-throne/chartreuse+indigo).
- Accents off ALL owned hues: Necrarch violet-glow, Draugr/Yurei cyan, Raijin gold+crimson, Kitsune
  mint, source Citipati cinnabar+ember, Mukha magenta-rose+teal.
- Differentiate by SILHOUETTE ARCHETYPE + proportion + accent + arm-config — never recolor/relic-swap.

---

### 1. asha-mukti — lotus bloom-mandorla bone-saint  (KIND: closed tall-almond mandorla · CHIBI pole)
- **Proportion.** Elevated-CHIBI — tiny idol / figurine; the small end of the axis.
- **Palette.** Warm bone-core; butter-saffron `(238,190,86)` → lotus-peach `(248,196,150)` soft
  bloom-glow (WARM-GOLD-INTO-PEACH, demonstrably NOT pink — anti-Mukha rose; soft, not Stupika gilt).
- **Silhouette tell.** EIGHT slender arms curve up and MEET in a clean teardrop point ABOVE the crown —
  blackout = an unmistakable vertical pointed almond, never a circle/open fan.
- **Arms / ornaments.** 8 thin arms (more + thinner than Mukha's six fat); arm-ends = lotus-pod relics;
  tiny skulls set in the lotus pods of the TWO LOWEST hands (arc-tips stay clean glow-caps).
- **Pillar.** Lotus-stalk reliquary, bottom-rooted, bloom at the cap.
- **32px risk.** 8 thin arms tangle — make the almond OUTLINE the priority read; soften interior gaps.

### 2. nagini-devi — serpent hood-ring bone-naga-queen  (KIND: arms-as-snakes hood-RING · mid)
- **Proportion.** Mid.
- **Palette.** Bone-core; verdigris-teal `(78,150,128)` → aged-bronze `(150,110,62)`, DULLER + GREENER-
  BRONZE (split from Nagaraja emerald `(72,196,142)` and Mukha teal `(64,170,166)`); BRONZE dominant.
- **Silhouette tell.** A RING of reared hood-lobes around the skull — lumpy, lateral, S-curved; blackout
  = a head ringed by bulbous hood-blobs, the antithesis of straight radial spokes.
- **Arms / ornaments.** SIX arms, each ending in a cobra-HOOD (not a hand); tiny skulls in alternating
  hood-jaws; bronze hood-rings with skull-bosses.
- **Pillar.** Caduceus twin-snake staff, bottom-rooted.
- **32px risk.** Writhing S-curves smear — FAT hoods + a clear negative gap between adjacent hoods.

### 3. chandra-mata — lunar crescent-arc bone-mother  (KIND: wide low crescent + rising horn · mid-tall)
- **Proportion.** Mid-tall, slim/elegant.
- **Palette.** Moon-silver bone + grey-lilac `(176,168,196)`, DESATURATED + GREY-leaning (never saturated
  purple = Necrarch, never blue-cyan = Draugr/Yurei). The only NIGHT-tuned accent in the roster.
- **Silhouette tell.** A WIDE LOW crescent smile-arc (arms sweep only the lower hemisphere) + a SINGLE
  large crescent-horn rising behind the crown; blackout = a low cupping smile under a rising horn.
- **Arms / ornaments.** SIX arms holding moon-phase discs; tiny skulls hang from the TWO OUTER
  crescent-tips as eclipse-charms.
- **Pillar.** Moon-gnomon, bottom-rooted.
- **32px risk.** Phases read identically — carry identity on the crescent-arc + rising horn; give disc
  cores a hair more value contrast so the accent pops dark-on-dark on the NIGHT biome.

### 4. rakta-ambu — coral-tree dendritic bone-canopy  (KIND: forked tree-canopy · mid-tall→monumental-via-width)
- **Proportion.** Mid-tall → monumental via WIDTH (the only organic blob; densely samples the axis).
- **Palette.** Briny indigo-blue `(54,86,140)` w/ a coral-pink HEART `(214,118,110)`; darker INDIGO
  (never cyan = Yurei), pink a DULL SALMON-CORAL (never Mukha hot magenta); indigo dominant, coral small.
- **Silhouette tell.** A FORKED dendritic canopy — arms split, then split again, into a spreading fractal
  tree-crown; blackout = an organic knobbly branching canopy, not discrete radiating limbs.
- **Arms / ornaments.** EIGHT arms forking to coral-polyp cups + conch relics; tiny skulls ENCRUSTED into
  the lower forks (death-as-growth).
- **Pillar.** Coral-reef bone-trunk, bottom-rooted (trunk roots it — no taper risk).
- **32px risk.** Fractal branching turns to noise — cap recursion at TWO fork levels; let the OUTER
  canopy silhouette carry it, not interior tracery.

### 5. asthi-samrat — temple-pylon colossus  (KIND: rigid architectural arm-grid · MONUMENTAL + PURE-bone poles)
- **Proportion.** MONUMENTAL — towering, the heaviest base in the brood; the high end of the axis.
- **Palette.** The PURE pole: bone-only. The ONLY non-bone note is the ink keyline + a COLD socket-pin
  (cold steel-grey / faint cold-white socket glow, NO thematic hue). The austerity IS the accent.
- **Silhouette tell.** A rigid architectural arm-GRID — three stacked tiers of four arms, each tier
  SHORTER than the one below, reading as horizontal cornices of a temple-pylon; blackout = a stepped
  vertical monolith with hard horizontal cornice-lines. Splits from Stupika (tapering stupa, ONE face,
  NO arms) by being explicitly MANY-ARMED; cornices SQUARE/AUSTERE (not Svarna's curved eaves).
- **Arms / ornaments.** TWELVE arms, each gripping a vertical bone-banner standard topped with a
  tiny-skull FINIAL — a colonnade of skull-poles (how the arm-end-skull DNA survives w/o a thematic accent).
- **Pillar.** Pylon-gate column, heaviest base in the brood, bottom-rooted.
- **32px risk.** Twelve arms collapse into a slab — the horizontal CORNICE-LINES (tier separations) are
  the must-survive read; hard dark grooves so it reads as steps; push the stepped silhouette; widest base.
