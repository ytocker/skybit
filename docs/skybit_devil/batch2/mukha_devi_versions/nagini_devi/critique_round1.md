# NAGINI-DEVI — Round 1 critique

Core problem is clear and serious: the headline silhouette tell — a head RINGED by bulbous lateral
hood-blobs — does not exist in this render. It reads as a six-armed seated skeleton clutching boxy relic-
plaques, structurally a near-cousin of Mukha. Re-roll NOT warranted (DNA, proportion, pillar are solid),
but this needs a genuine structural redraw of arms→hoods, not a recolor.

## Per-criterion KEEP / FIX
1. **Preserved DNA — KEEP.** Skull-crown of 3 small skulls, verdigris third-eye as brightest pixel, six
   limbs, tiny skulls inset in lower arm-ends. All present and on-brand.
2. **Silhouette tell — FIX (blocking).** The blackout shows a compact crouched body with stubby
   protrusions — could be Mukha or any seated skeleton. Causes: (a) arm-ends are flat rectangular RELIC-
   PLAQUES (picture-frames), not reared cobra-hoods (a hood = fat teardrop/spade lobe on an S-curved
   neck); (b) hoods don't encircle the skull — they cluster low/front (lap/belly), so the blackout is
   bottom-heavy + centered, not a radial ring around the head; (c) no air between lobes — bodies merge
   into one mass.
3. **Proportion — KEEP (mostly).** Mid head:body, fine; slightly bottom-clustered (fixing the ring rebalances).
4. **Accent — FIX (partial).** Bronze present + dominant (good, separates from Nagaraja emerald). BUT the
   verdigris accents read as clean cyan-teal, dangerously close to "Mukha's teal grown up." Cut chroma
   ~25–30%, push greener/greyer = oxidized copper, not a jewel.
5. **32px — FIX.** Both chips are mush (beige blob + teal dot); day chip loses bronze against blue sky.
   Can't pass until #2 fixed.
6. **Distinctness — FIX.** As drawn, too close to Mukha (six-armed bone-mother w/ ornament-tipped arms);
   the hood-ring is the ONLY separator and it's absent. Same blocking issue as #2.
7. **Pillar — KEEP.** Caduceus twin-snake spiral on bronze rod is genuinely good — clearly twin-snake,
   bottom-rooted, mirrored, no top-heavy cap. Best-resolved element. Just nudge accent to greener verdigris.

## Round-2 directives (prioritized)
1. Rebuild arm-ends as actual COBRA HOODS and RING them around the SKULL: each arm = S-curved neck →
   fat spade/teardrop hood-lobe; splay 3 per side arching around the head (top-lateral + mid-lateral) so
   the blackout = skull haloed by 6 bulbous lobes. Kill the rectangular relic-plaques entirely.
2. Force a NEGATIVE GAP between every adjacent hood (~1 hood-width of air); verify it survives blackout
   AND the 32px chip — discrete blobs, not one welded mass.
3. Move mass OFF the lap and UP into the ring; let the gap below the chin read open. Blackout = a flower
   of fat petals around a skull, not a crouching torso.
4. Re-tune verdigris GREENER + duller (toward oxidized-copper green-grey, chroma −25–30%); keep it ONLY
   as the brightest-pixel third-eye + thin oxide rims; bronze stays the dominant mass.
5. Put tiny skulls in the HOOD-JAWS, alternating (3 of 6 open jaws) so they punctuate the ring rhythmically.
6. Fatten hoods (largest forms on the body) for 32px; re-pull the day chip — if the ring dies on blue sky,
   deepen bronze value + thicken the ink keyline on outer hood edges.
7. Pillar: keep as-is; only restate its accent to the new greener verdigris.

Bring back round 2 with the blackout reading as a lobed ring before anything else is judged.
