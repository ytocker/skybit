VERDICT: ITERATE

# CHANDRA-MATA — Round 1 critique

Palette, proportion, and rising-horn are genuinely strong and on-brief. The concept is undermined by ONE
structural failure: the blackout does NOT read as a low cupping crescent-smile — it reads as a straight
downward radial spray, too close to Mukha's archetype to clear the distinctness gate. That forces ITERATE.

## Strongest aspects (KEEP)
- **Proportion.** Slim mid-tall nailed — long neck + narrow shoulders + extended limbs, elegant not squat.
  Best proportion in the brood so far; protect it.
- **Palette / accent.** Moon-silver + grey-lilac correctly desaturated/grey-leaning; clearly NOT Necrarch
  purple, NOT Draugr/Yurei cyan. Genuinely POPS dark-on-dark on the night chip at 32px. Constraint 4 met.
- **Rising crescent-horn.** The single large horn behind the crown is the most successful identity element
  — clean, asymmetric, instant in blackout + 32px night. The anchor; do not touch.
- **Eclipse-charm DNA.** Tiny skulls at outer arm-ends + skull-tiara on crown. DNA preserved.

## Weakest aspects (FIX) — ranked
1. **[BLOCKER] Blackout reads as a downward radial spray, not a wide LOW crescent smile-arc.** Six arms
   fan DOWNWARD with two near-vertical center limbs = starburst-spray, same family as Mukha. ROUND 2:
   re-pose all six into a true cradle — kill the two vertical-down center limbs; spread wide and lateral,
   each arm bowing DOWN at the midline then curving back UP at the tip (U/bowl whose width is ~1.6–1.8x
   its depth); outer tips at ~shoulder height or above. Target blackout: a low cupping crescent UNDER the
   horn, the two shapes echoing (small horn up, wide crescent down).
2. **At 32px DAY the slim arms turn to thin noise.** Night passes, day weak. ROUND 2: thicken arms
   ~25–30%, rely on the 1–2px ink keyline to hold dark-on-light; enlarge + evenly space the disc beads so
   the arc reads as a deliberate bead-string. Keep discs as identical flat-lilac beads (NO phase rendering
   — phases die at 32px).
3. **Disc value structure flat against bone on day chip.** ROUND 2: darker lilac core + brighter
   cold-silver rim so the bead-arc holds focal hierarchy on BOTH biomes.
4. **Crown/tiara skulls crowded under the horn at 32px (fuse into one mass on night chip).** ROUND 2: open
   a 1px negative gap between the tiara skull-cluster and the horn root so the crown reads as a band.

## Other constraints
- Distinctness: AT RISK vs Mukha only because of the spray pose (fix #1 resolves it); clear of siblings +
  roster on accent + horn. Once the crescent-cup lands, distinctness is solid.
- Pillar: moon-gnomon clean, bottom-rooted, mirrored, no top-heavy cap; full-moon cradle is a nice touch.
  Clipped label text behind the GAP block is purely cosmetic — ignore unless trivial to nudge.

## Round-2 punch list (in order)
1. Re-pose six arms from downward-spray into a WIDE LOW crescent cup (lateral span ~1.7x vertical depth;
   outer tips curve UP to shoulder height); delete the two vertical center limbs.
2. Hang the two eclipse-charm skulls from the UP-curved OUTER crescent tips so they cap the smile's horns.
3. Thicken arms ~25–30%; enlarge/even-space disc beads for a clean day-chip read; discs identical (no phases).
4. Increase disc core↔rim value contrast (darker lilac core, brighter silver rim) for day+night pop.
5. Open a 1px gap between the tiara skull-cluster and the horn root so the crown stays a distinct band.

Keep the proportion, palette, and rising horn exactly as they are — they already clear the bar.
