# Mukha-Devi-versions — GD brainstorm (8 directions, no full render)

## Brood thesis

Spin-off epic bosses descended from the shipped **Mukha-Devi** — the six-armed wrathful
bone-mother (`citipati_versions`, KIND: radial-fan). She is the **Original** of a new "many-armed
death-deity" brood. Where the Citipati five were five different CREATURE KINDS, this brood shares
one ancestral motif — **a multi-arm fan around a skull-crowned head** — so distinctness has to be
won on a harder axis: **silhouette ARCHETYPE + proportion + arm-count/configuration + thematic
accent**, never a recolor or a swapped relic. The user picked two spread axes the 8 must visibly
span: **proportion (elevated-CHIBI tiny-idol → MONUMENTAL towering colossus)** and **theme (PURE
skeletal death-deity → bone-CORE-with-a-distinct-accent)**. Bone always dominates the silhouette.

## Preserved DNA — EVERY direction below keeps all of these (non-negotiable)

- **Multi-arm** — a radiating arm configuration is the brood signature (count/geometry varies).
- **Skull-crown / skull-tiara** on the head.
- **Elaborate arm-end ornaments** — each arm ends in an ornate relic/weapon/implement, and **tiny
  skulls appear among those ornaments on at least some arms.**
- **House style** — chibi + scary-CUTE; flat procedural triad (dark-core → flat-fill → top-left
  rim-sheen); ink keyline (28,22,26-family) + 1px grown outline; procedural-only; pushed EPIC at
  SS=5–6 → smoothscale; **must read at true 32px day+night**; bottom-rooted mirrorable pillar.

## Distinctness ground rules I obeyed (blackout / swap / cover-label / one-sentence)

- Each direction is a **different SILHOUETTE ARCHETYPE**, not Mukha's symmetric peacock-fan
  re-dressed. Mukha herself owns the *wide symmetric STARBURST around a big chibi head*; none of the
  8 below repeats that exact shape.
- Distinct from the 5 citipati siblings' archetypes: **serpent-coil** (Nagaraja), **stacked-tower**
  (Stupika), **radial-fan** (Mukha), **spread-wing** (Asthi-Garuda), **seated-throne** (Koschei).
  Where a direction risks echoing one (e.g. a tower-tall colossus vs. Stupika), I split it by
  many-arm config + accent + the arm-end-skulls DNA Stupika never had.
- Distinct from the skeleton-DENSE roster (Catrina, Mariachi+variants, batch-1 reapers, Citipati).
- **Accents diverge off owned hues:** no Necrarch violet-glow, no Draugr/Yurei cyan, no Raijin
  gold+crimson, no Kitsune mint, no source Citipati cinnabar+ember, and **none repeat Mukha's
  magenta-rose** (the mother owns rose, so the children must diverge).

## The 8 directions

### 1. ASHA-MUKTI — the dawn-lotus reliquary saint  (KIND: bloom-mandorla / petal-aureole)
**Proportion:** elevated-CHIBI (tiny shrine-idol, ~chibi+ a hair shorter than Mukha).
**Accent:** lotus / relic — **butter-saffron + soft lotus-blush peach** (NOT magenta-rose: a warm
gold-into-peach, reads holy-warm).
**Arm config:** **EIGHT slender arms in a folded MANDORLA OVAL** — not a wide starburst but two
near-vertical almond curves of arms framing the body like a halo *outline*, four per side curving up
and meeting in a closed teardrop point ABOVE the crown. The silhouette is a tall pointed almond, not
a circle.
**Arm-end ornaments + skulls:** alternating budding-lotus cups and tiny prayer-bells; the **two
lowest hands cradle tiny skulls set INTO lotus pods** (skull-in-the-flower), so the death-DNA sits at
the base of the bloom while the tips glow gold.
**Pillar:** a **lotus-stalk reliquary** — a segmented green-bone stalk (banded) hung with alternating
lotus-pod + tiny-skull pendants = shaft; cap = a half-open lotus throne with a saffron relic-flame at
the gap. Bottom-rooted.
**Why distinct:** the only **closed tall-almond mandorla** silhouette (vs. Mukha's open wide
starburst, vs. Asthi-Garuda's X-wing). Saffron/peach is nowhere on the owned-hue list. Tiny-idol
proportion anchors the chibi end of the axis.

### 2. JVALA-KALI — the wrath-flame furnace-mother  (KIND: flame-corona / rising-tongue fan)
**Proportion:** mid (compact-chibi torso, but the silhouette reads taller via flame).
**Accent:** wrathful FLAME — **molten amber-orange core with a thin char-black outer tongue**
(distinct from Raijin gold+crimson and from source ember: this is amber→soot, no crimson, no gold
trim).
**Arm config:** **TEN arms splayed UPWARD and OUTWARD like rising flame-tongues**, asymmetric in
length (a flickering, uneven fan, longest at center) so the silhouette is a jagged upward
flame-crown — the opposite of Mukha's even, symmetric, sideways-bracketing fan.
**Arm-end ornaments + skulls:** each hand ends in a **flame-blade / trishula tip**; charred **tiny
skulls are threaded onto the three center arms like beads on the flame**, blackened at the edges.
**Pillar:** a **pyre-column** — a banded charcoal-bone shaft wrapped in a thin amber flame-ribbon =
shaft (skull-bead pendants on alternating bands); cap = a cupped flame-bowl with an amber ember at
the gap. Bottom-rooted.
**Why distinct:** **jagged, asymmetric, UPWARD** flame-fan — Mukha's fan is even/symmetric/sideways
and never reaches over the crown; Jvala's whole point is the spiky upward corona. Amber-soot accent
is unowned. Ten arms vs. Mukha's six.

### 3. MARU-DEVI — the desert charnel-djinn, lower body dissolving to bone-smoke  (KIND: vortex-tail / dust-funnel)
**Proportion:** mid-tall (chibi head + torso, but **no legs/base** — body tapers into a spiraling
dust-tail), so it reads taller than a squat idol without being a colossus.
**Accent:** ash / charnel — **bone-ash dust with a thin DUSTY-OLIVE-KHAKI haze** (desaturated
sand-olive; distinct from Leyak ash-white, Mukha rose, and the cool bones).
**Arm config:** **SIX arms but arranged in a slow SPIRAL / pinwheel** — each arm rotated a step
around the torso so the fan reads as a swirling pinwheel, not a mirror-symmetric starburst. The lower
body spins down into a **funnel/vortex tail** of bone-dust shards.
**Arm-end ornaments + skulls:** alternating curved khopesh-blades and incense-censers trailing dust;
**tiny skulls tumble loose in the dust-tail** and one is clutched in the topmost spiral hand.
**Pillar:** a **dust-funnel obelisk** — a tapering banded bone shaft that narrows downward into a
twist of dust-shards = shaft (censer + skull pendants); cap = a flared censer-mouth venting an
olive-ash glow at the gap. Bottom-rooted (the funnel tip roots it).
**Why distinct:** the **pinwheel/spiral + legless dust-funnel** silhouette is unique in the brood —
no other has a tapering vortex tail or rotational (non-mirror) arm symmetry; Mukha is rigidly
mirror-symmetric on a wide base. Olive-ash is unowned.

### 4. NAGINI-DEVI — the serpent-coil bone-priestess, arms ending in cobra-hoods  (KIND: arms-as-snakes / hydra-radial)
**Proportion:** mid (chibi figure, snakes give lateral spread).
**Accent:** naga / serpent — **verdigris-teal-into-bronze** (a green-bronze patina; distinct from
Nagaraja's *emerald* serpent-glow — this is a duller verdigris bronze, and distinct from Mukha's lone
teal bell-sliver by making it the dominant accent + bronze, not a sliver).
**Arm config:** **SIX arms that DON'T end in hands — each arm IS a snake**, the forearms thickening
into reared cobra-hoods so the fan is a ring of six hissing hood-heads around the priestess's skull.
Lateral, wavy, S-curved limbs — a writhing radial, not Mukha's rigid straight bone-arms.
**Arm-end ornaments + skulls:** the cobra-hoods themselves are the ornaments (flared, brow-jewelled);
**tiny skulls are gripped in the snakes' jaws** on alternating heads, and bronze hood-rings carry
skull-bosses.
**Pillar:** a **caduceus bone-staff** — twin snake-bodies wound up a central bone rod = shaft (the
weave is the band rhythm; skull pendants at the crossings); cap = a paired cobra-hood mouth with a
verdigris brow-jewel glowing at the gap. Bottom-rooted.
**Why distinct from Nagaraja:** Nagaraja is **one legless serpent-COIL body** (no arms); Nagini is an
**armed humanoid priestess whose ARMS are snakes** — fully different silhouette (upright figure +
radial hood-ring vs. a single reared coil) and verdigris-bronze ≠ emerald. Different from Mukha's
straight-bone fan by the writhing S-curve hood-ring.

### 5. CHANDRA-MATA — the moon-mother, arms cradling phase-discs  (KIND: crescent-halo / lunar-arc)
**Proportion:** mid-to-tall elegant (slimmer, taller chibi — a serene rising figure).
**Accent:** lunar / night — **pale moon-silver with a cool DUSTY-LILAC-GREY glow** (a desaturated
moonlit lilac; deliberately NOT Necrarch's saturated violet-glow and NOT Draugr/Yurei cyan — this is
a soft grey-lilac that still pops on the night biome).
**Arm config:** **SIX arms arching up and outward to hold a HALF-RING of moon-phase discs** — the
arms form the lower half of a big crescent/halo arc behind the head, so the silhouette is a wide low
crescent cupping the skull (a smile-shaped arc, not Mukha's full radiating star). A single large
crescent-moon horn rises behind the crown.
**Arm-end ornaments + skulls:** each hand holds a **moon-phase disc** (new → crescent → gibbous →
full, a lunar cycle around the arc); **tiny skulls hang from the two outer crescent-tips** like
lunar-eclipse charms.
**Pillar:** a **moon-gnomon pillar** — a banded pale shaft strung with descending phase-disc pendants
+ tiny-skull spacers = shaft; cap = a cupped crescent cradle with a lilac-glow full-moon at the gap.
Bottom-rooted.
**Why distinct:** the **wide low crescent-arc** (cupping smile) silhouette + the rising single moon-
horn is its own shape — Mukha's star radiates in all directions; Chandra's arms only sweep the lower
hemisphere into a crescent. Grey-lilac lunar accent is unowned. Tall-elegant marks the upper-mid of
the proportion axis.

### 6. ASTHI-SAMRAT — the colossal bone-emperor, arms as a war-banner array  (KIND: monumental tiered-colossus / pylon)
**Proportion:** **MONUMENTAL** (the towering end of the axis — a vast imposing pylon-bodied colossus,
deliberately top-of-range).
**Accent:** PURE skeletal death-deity, austere — **bone-only, no thematic glow**; the *only* color
note is the ink keyline and the cold socket-pins. This is the brood's austere bone-only pole.
**Arm config:** **TWELVE arms held in a rigid ARCHITECTURAL GRID** — three stacked tiers of four
arms, each tier shorter than the one below, so the arms read as the horizontal cornices/eaves of a
towering temple-pylon, not a fan. Vertical, monumental, ziggurat-of-arms.
**Arm-end ornaments + skulls:** each hand grips a **vertical bone-banner standard** topped with a
**tiny skull finial**; the standards line up like a colonnade of skull-poles flanking the colossus.
**Pillar:** a **pylon-gate column** — a massive stacked bone-block shaft with cornice bands = shaft
(skull-finial standards as bracket pendants); cap = a temple-gate lintel with the colossus's deep
cold socket-glow at the gap. Bottom-rooted, heaviest base in the brood.
**Why distinct from Stupika (stacked-tower):** Stupika is a **stupa-reliquary tower with ONE live
face on its ground floor and NO arms** — Asthi-Samrat is an explicitly **MANY-ARMED colossus** whose
twelve arms ARE the architecture (a colonnade), with the brood's skull-crown + arm-end skull-finials
Stupika lacks. Pure-bone austerity is its accent pole; monumental scale anchors the colossus end.

### 7. RAKTA-AMBU — the coral charnel sea-mother, arms branching like reef-bone  (KIND: branching coral-tree / dendritic radial)
**Proportion:** mid-tall, but **reads MONUMENTAL-ward** via a wide spreading coral canopy (a
spreading-tree colossus).
**Accent:** coral / sea — **bleached bone-coral with a deep BRINE-BLUE-INTO-CORAL-PINK glow**
(distinct from Draugr/Yurei *cyan*: a darker briny indigo-blue with a coral-pink heart, and the pink
is a dull salmon-coral, NOT Mukha's hot magenta-rose).
**Arm config:** **EIGHT arms that FORK and re-fork into dendritic coral branches** — each arm splits
into two, then two again, so the upper silhouette is a spreading branching coral canopy (a fractal
tree-crown) rather than discrete radiating limbs. Encrusted, knobbly, organic — anti-Mukha's clean
straight bones.
**Arm-end ornaments + skulls:** branch-tips end in **coral-polyp cups and conch-shell relics**;
**tiny skulls are encrusted into the lower branch-forks** like reef accretions, half-swallowed by
coral.
**Pillar:** a **coral-reef bone-trunk** — a knobbly branching shaft with conch + skull pendants on
alternating forks = shaft; cap = a fanned brain-coral crown with a brine-blue glow at the gap.
Bottom-rooted (the trunk roots it).
**Why distinct:** the **dendritic branching tree-canopy** silhouette is unlike any sibling or Mukha's
discrete-limb star — it's organic and fractal, not radial-spoke. Brine-blue+coral is unowned (cleanly
off Yurei cyan). Spreading canopy pushes toward the monumental pole via width.

### 8. SVARNA-RANI — the gilt-temple empress on a tiered bone-dais  (KIND: gilt-pagoda enthroned-monolith)
**Proportion:** **MONUMENTAL** (a towering gilt-temple monolith — the most ornamented colossus, top
of the proportion axis alongside #6 but a totally different mass).
**Accent:** gilt-temple — **deep BURNISHED-GOLD-INTO-INDIGO-LACQUER** (a gilt + dark-indigo lacquer
pairing; distinct from Raijin gold+CRIMSON by swapping crimson for indigo-lacquer, and richer/darker
than Mukha's thin gold relic-trim which never becomes a mass).
**Arm config:** **FOURTEEN arms in a STACKED PAGODA-FAN** — multiple horizontal tiers of arms, each
tier a shallow shorter fan, stacked vertically like the eaves of a pagoda so the whole reads as a
tiered golden temple-roof silhouette with the skull-empress enthroned at its base.
**Arm-end ornaments + skulls:** each hand holds a **gilt temple-bell / lamp-finial**; **tiny gilt-
capped skulls cap the corner arms of each tier** like roof-ridge guardian figures, indigo-lacquered
in their hollows.
**Pillar:** a **pagoda-spire pillar** — a stacked tiered bone-and-gilt shaft (each eave a band) hung
with bell + gilt-skull pendants = shaft; cap = a gilt finial-spire with an indigo-lacquer lamp-glow
at the gap. Bottom-rooted, broad tiered base.
**Why distinct from Asthi-Samrat (#6) AND Stupika:** #6 is austere PURE-bone rigid-grid pylon;
Svarna is the **opulent gilt-and-lacquer tiered PAGODA-fan** — same monumental pole, completely
different mass (ornate curved eaves vs. austere square cornices) and opposite accent (heavy gilt vs.
bone-only). Vs. Stupika's armless single-face stupa, Svarna is an explicitly MANY-armed
(fourteen-arm pagoda-fan) empress with arm-end skull-ridge guardians. Gilt+indigo is unowned.
