# Mukha-Devi brood — AD brainstorm cull (8 → 5)

VERDICT: STRONG set — 8 genuinely distinct silhouette archetypes, all preserving the brood DNA
(multi-arm + skull-crown + arm-end ornaments with tiny skulls), both spread axes over-covered. This is
a real bottom-up brainstorm, not one idea shown eight ways; the cull is about trimming overlap and
protecting both poles, not rescuing a weak set. Proceed to per-concept loops on the 5 below.

## How I read the SET against the distinctness tests

**Blackout test (solid-black silhouette).** Eight read cleanly: 1 tall closed almond/teardrop · 2 jagged
upward flame-crown · 3 spiral pinwheel + tapering funnel tail (legless) · 4 lateral writhing hood-RING ·
5 wide low crescent smile-arc + single rising horn · 6 rigid stacked horizontal-cornice pylon · 7
spreading fractal tree-canopy (forked) · 8 stacked tiered curved-eave pagoda. No two collapse into the
same blob. None repeats Mukha's wide symmetric OPEN sideways starburst. **Pass.**

**Where the set crowds (the cull pressure):**
- **2 (Jvala flame-corona)** is an UPWARD spiky fan — closest of the eight to "a Mukha fan re-aimed up,"
  and amber→soot is the survivor nearest the owned Raijin gold+crimson / source cinnabar+ember zone.
- **6 (Asthi-Samrat pylon) vs 8 (Svarna pagoda)** — BOTH monumental, BOTH stacked-tier-of-arms; the two
  most ADJACENT archetypes. Split is legit but we keep only one monumental.
- **3 (Maru vortex)** novel (only spiral) but its LEGLESS funnel tail puts mass UP HIGH and tapers to a
  point at the base — highest top-heavy/pillar-rooting risk against the bottom-weighted-mirror rule.
- **1 (Asha mandorla) vs 5 (Chandra crescent)** read as opposite arcs — no clash.

## RANKED CULL — the 5 to PURSUE
1. **#6 ASTHI-SAMRAT (monumental, PURE bone).** FORCE-KEEP. Anchors BOTH mandated extremes — monumental
   proportion pole AND pure-skeletal theme pole. Rigid architectural arm-grid is a blob no sibling owns.
2. **#1 ASHA-MUKTI (elevated-chibi, lotus saffron+peach).** FORCE-KEEP. The ONLY chibi/tiny-idol
   candidate — anchors the small end. Closed tall-almond mandorla is unique; saffron/peach unowned.
3. **#4 NAGINI-DEVI (mid, verdigris→bronze serpent).** Strongest mid archetype: arms-as-snakes hood-RING
   is the most viscerally different silhouette; reads NOTHING like Nagaraja's single legless coil.
4. **#5 CHANDRA-MATA (mid-tall, lunar silver+grey-lilac).** Best "arc" candidate; wide low crescent +
   rising horn is its own blob; grey-lilac is the only night-tuned accent in the roster.
5. **#7 RAKTA-AMBU (mid-tall→monumental, coral brine-blue+coral-pink).** The only ORGANIC silhouette;
   bridges mid→monumental via WIDTH (not height), keeping the proportion axis densely sampled.

## RANKED CULL — the 3 to CUT
- **CUT #8 SVARNA-RANI (gilt-pagoda monumental).** Duplicate archetype + axis already covered + accent
  crowding (gilt+indigo crowds Raijin gold and Stupika gilt-saffron). Keep only one monumental; the
  PURE-bone pole is worth more than a second gilt colossus.
- **CUT #2 JVALA-KALI (flame-corona).** Weakest distinctness (upward fan nearest Mukha's own) + amber→soot
  closest to owned Raijin/ember + its mid slot already held by Nagini.
- **CUT #3 MARU-DEVI (vortex-funnel).** Hardest cut. Legless tapering tail fights the bottom-rooted
  mirrorable-pillar rule (highest top-heavy risk); dusty olive-khaki muddiest at 32px on busy day skies.

> If the spiral config is explicitly wanted, swap #3 IN for #5 — but that loses the unowned lunar night
> accent, so advised against. Maru is the right cut.

**Proportion:** chibi (#1) → mid (#4) → mid-tall (#5) → mid-tall/wide-monumental (#7) → monumental (#6).
Both extremes nailed by the force-keeps; middle sampled three times without clustering. **Covered.**
**Theme:** PURE skeletal (#6) → bone-core+accent (#1 lotus, #4 serpent, #5 lunar, #7 coral); accents span
WARM (saffron) and COOL (verdigris/lilac/brine) so they don't all glow the same temperature. **Covered.**
> Both poles survive ONLY because #6 and #1 are force-kept. Hold them.

**Distinct from the 5 citipati siblings:** serpent-coil (Nagini = armed humanoid w/ snake-arms, not

repeats the open sideways starburst) ✓ · spread-wing (none winged) ✓ · seated-throne (none enthroned) ✓.

## PER-SURVIVOR re-spec notes — lock BEFORE render r1

**#1 ASHA-MUKTI (chibi / lotus).** Arm-curves must MEET in a clean teardrop point ABOVE the crown —
blackout = unmistakable vertical pointed almond, not a circle/open fan. Accent: butter-saffron
`~(238,190,86)` → lotus-peach `~(248,196,150)`, WARM-GOLD-INTO-PEACH, demonstrably NOT pink (rose drift
collides w/ Mukha), soft bloom-glow not Stupika hard gilt. EIGHT slender arms (more+thinner than Mukha's
six fat); tiny skulls in lotus pods in the TWO LOWEST hands so arc-tips stay clean glow-caps. Pillar:
lotus-stalk reliquary, bottom-rooted, bloom at cap. 32px: make the almond OUTLINE the priority read.

**#4 NAGINI-DEVI (mid / serpent).** A RING of reared hood-lobes around the skull — lumpy, lateral,
S-curved; blackout = head ringed by bulbous hood-blobs, antithesis of straight radial spokes. Accent:
verdigris-teal `~(78,150,128)` → aged-bronze `~(150,110,62)`, DULLER + GREENER-BRONZE to split from
Nagaraja emerald `(72,196,142)` and Mukha teal bell-sliver `(64,170,166)`; BRONZE the dominant mass.
SIX arms each ending in a cobra-HOOD not a hand; tiny skulls in alternating jaws. Pillar: caduceus
twin-snake staff, bottom-rooted. 32px: FAT hoods + clear negative gap between adjacent hoods.

**#5 CHANDRA-MATA (mid-tall / lunar).** WIDE LOW crescent smile-arc (arms sweep only the lower
hemisphere) + a SINGLE large crescent-horn rising behind the crown; blackout = low cupping smile under a
rising horn. Accent: moon-silver bone + grey-lilac `~(176,168,196)`, DESATURATED + GREY-leaning (never
saturated purple = Necrarch, never blue-cyan = Draugr/Yurei). Verify it pops dark-on-dark on the night
biome at 32px — it's the only night-tuned accent; give disc cores a hair more value contrast. SIX arms
holding moon-phase discs; tiny skulls hang from the TWO OUTER crescent-tips as eclipse-charms. Pillar:
moon-gnomon, bottom-rooted. 32px: carry IDENTITY on the crescent-arc + rising horn, not legible phases.

**#7 RAKTA-AMBU (mid-tall→monumental / coral).** FORKED dendritic canopy — arms split, then split again,
into a spreading fractal tree-crown; blackout = organic knobbly branching canopy, the set's only organic
blob. Accent: briny indigo-blue `~(54,86,140)` w/ a coral-pink HEART `~(214,118,110)`, darker INDIGO
(never cyan = Yurei), pink a DULL SALMON-CORAL (never Mukha hot magenta); indigo dominant, coral a small
warm core. EIGHT arms forking to coral-polyp cups + conch relics; tiny skulls ENCRUSTED into lower forks.
Pillar: coral-reef bone-trunk, bottom-rooted (trunk roots it — no taper risk). 32px: cap recursion at TWO
fork levels; let the OUTER canopy silhouette carry it, not interior tracery.

**#6 ASTHI-SAMRAT (monumental / PURE bone).** Rigid architectural arm-GRID — three stacked tiers of four
arms, each tier SHORTER than the one below, reading as horizontal cornices of a temple-pylon; blackout =
stepped vertical monolith w/ hard horizontal cornice-lines. This splits it from Stupika (tapering stupa,
ONE face, NO arms) and from cut Svarna (curved ornate eaves) — keep cornices SQUARE/AUSTERE and the
silhouette explicitly MANY-ARMED. Accent = the PURE pole: bone-only; the ONLY non-bone note is the ink
keyline + a COLD socket-pin (cold steel-grey / faint cold-white socket glow, NO thematic hue). The
austerity IS the accent. TWELVE arms, each gripping a vertical bone-banner standard topped with a
tiny-skull FINIAL (a colonnade of skull-poles — how the arm-end-skull DNA survives without a thematic
accent). Pillar: pylon-gate column, heaviest base in the brood, bottom-rooted. 32px: the horizontal
CORNICE-LINES (tier separations) are the must-survive read — hard dark grooves so it reads as steps,
not a slab; push the stepped silhouette hard and keep the base widest.
