# ASHA-MUKTI — Round 1 critique

A competent chibi skull-saint with a strong face, but it misses three load-bearing pins — the silhouette
tell, the accent color, and the chibi proportion. Needs a real round 2, not a polish pass.

## Ranked notes
### 1. [CRITICAL — breaks a CROSS-SET PIN] Accent rendered TEAL, not warm saffron→peach.
Third-eye, every arm-end pod rim, neck collar, and pillar boss read teal/verdigris in hero + both chips.
Teal is owned by Mukha (rose+TEAL) and adjacent to Nagaraja emerald / Draugr-Yurei cyan. Worst miss on
the sheet. R2: recolor ALL accents to the warm ramp — gem + pod-cores butter-saffron `(238,190,86)`,
soft outer bloom lotus-peach `(248,196,150)`; third-eye = single brightest WARM pixel; zero teal; add a
faint peach outer-glow halo so the "bloom" in bloom-mandorla reads at a glance.

### 2. [CRITICAL — fails the silhouette tell] Blackout is NOT a clean almond — reads as a bug/spider.
Apex teardrop is correct, but below the crown the outline explodes into discrete knobby pods on both
sides + a lumpy double-tier base. Only the top ~30% is almond. R2: pull arm-pods INWARD so their outer
edges fall ON a smooth mandorla curve apex→bottom (beads strung along the rim, not bumps past it); tuck
the lowest two arms toward ONE clean bottom point. Blackout must trace as a single teardrop, no concave gaps.

### 3. [MAJOR] Proportion reads mid-figure, not elevated-chibi tiny-idol.
Big head (good) but the torso + wide square arm-blocks add mid-size mass; competes with Nagini's mid slot.
R2: shrink the body (bigger head:body ratio, shorter torso, daintier arms); make arm-ends small ROUND
lotus beads, not square blocks. Should feel like a little carved netsuke idol.

### 4. [MAJOR] 32px: almond outline does NOT survive — interior tangle wins; only the (wrong-hue) teal dot reads.
R2: once #2 closes the almond, add a continuous 1px warm-PEACH rim so the almond edge is the strongest
32px read; simplify interior at small scale (arm-tangle + grille drop out), leaving almond + warm third-eye.

### 5. [MINOR] Pillar construction right (bottom-rooted, mirrored, no top-heavy cap) but accent teal again.
R2: recolor pendants saffron; make the bloom gap-caps a clear soft peach glow so "lotus bloom caps the gap" reads.

## KEEP
- The face: scary-CUTE, big round sockets, clean tooth-row, strong chibi appeal — the best asset. Keep
  nearly as-is (just recolor third-eye warm).
- Crown of tiny skulls — clear, DNA preserved. Apex teardrop point — exactly right; extend that quality
  down the whole outline. Lowest-pod skulls — DNA present. Pillar construction passes §7.

## Distinctness (§6)
Once accent goes warm-saffron, distinct from Mukha — but ONLY then. As rendered, teal makes it read like
a recolor-sibling of Mukha/Nagaraja (exact failure the PINS police). The almond archetype is the right
differentiator but must actually read as an almond (#2) for distinctness to land at gameplay scale.

## Round-2 punch list (prioritized)
1. Recolor ALL accents warm: saffron `(238,190,86)` cores → peach `(248,196,150)` bloom; zero teal;
   third-eye = brightest warm pixel.
2. Close the silhouette: arm-pods onto a smooth almond rim; tuck lowest arms to ONE bottom point —
   blackout traces as a single teardrop.
3. Shrink body / round arm-ends to beads — push elevated-chibi tiny-idol proportion.
4. Continuous 1px warm rim on the almond edge so the outline is the 32px priority read.
5. Recolor pillar pendants saffron; make the peach bloom gap-caps clearly visible.
