# Ifra — round 1 critique (AD)

Strong palette and triad discipline; one structural miss that must be fixed before
anything else. The render reads as a floating HEAD with a smoke base — but the locked
brief pins an ifrit with a **crossed-beefy-arms TORSO** above the smoke-curl. As drawn,
Ifra has no torso and no arms, which collapses its silhouette toward the **Leyak**
sibling (the bodiless flying-head devil) and throws away the "cocky genie swagger"
that is the whole thesis. That is the headline.

## Strongest aspects (KEEP)
- **PINNED PALETTE — exact.** Sampled the showcase: coral-ember (238,108,72),
  deep-rust (176,62,42), violet smoke (118,86,150), hot-saffron (255,196,96), sheen
  (255,182,138), ink (34,20,24) all land on-pin. Coral+violet+saffron reads as the
  sole "live fire" hue in the set. Don't touch the swatches.
- **Triad on the smoke-plume lobes** is textbook: dark-core violet, flat-fill violet,
  saffron/coral top-left sheen, hard-edged — no soft gradient. This is exactly the
  house grammar.
- **Violet smoke-curl base** is the unmistakable legless blob the 32px read asked for,
  and it's the cool counterweight to the fire body. Holds at the 32px chip.
- **Day/night value separation** is good: coral body pops on the pale day sky and the
  saffron flame-core keeps a focal on the dark night sky. Glow restraint on the
  coal-eyes is correct (quiet embers, not glare).
- **Lamp-pillar shaft** has clean vertical fluting that will tile as a repeatable body,
  and the wick-spout fire-curl cap is on-axis. Mirror direction is right.

## Weakest aspects (FIX)
1. **No torso, no crossed arms — reads as a head, not a genie. [BLOCKER]**
   The brief: "Broad crossed-arms upper torso... tapering at the waist into a billowing
   legless smoke-curl." What's drawn is a near-square head with two small cheek/jaw nubs
   and zero arms. This is the single most important fix: it both violates the locked
   construction AND drifts straight into Leyak's bodiless-flying-head lane. Build the
   chibi ember TORSO with two beefy arms folded across the chest (the classic genie
   fold), shoulders wider than the head, then taper the waist into the existing
   smoke-curl. The crossed-arm horizontal mass is what will distinguish Ifra from every
   other floating creature in the set at 32px.
2. **The face is too large relative to body and too literal a cube.** Even at chibi
   big-head proportions, the head currently IS the whole upper creature. Once the torso
   exists, shrink the head to ~55-60% of torso width and round the hard cube corners a
   touch — right now the flat-front box plus round goggle-eyes reads more "robot/owl"
   than "smug fire-jinn."
3. **Flame-HORNS are missing.** Brief pins "two flat back-swept flame-horns." The render
   has pointed elf-EARS instead. Replace (or add above) with two flat, back-swept
   saffron-tipped flame-horns — they're a key part of the silhouette and currently read
   as ears, which is a different archetype.
4. **Coal-eyes are reading as round spectacles.** The black ink rings around the saffron
   pupils look like glasses, not glowing coals set into the face. Drop the heavy full
   ink ring; let the coal-eyes be saffron-core lobes with only a partial lower ink
   keyline, so they read as embers, not goggles. (Bonus: this further separates from
   Tlaloc's goggle eye-rings.)
5. **Nose-ring placement.** The gold ring currently sits centered over the mouth area
   reading like a lip-ring/septum hoop in an ambiguous spot. Pin it clearly on the
   nose/septum so the "single gold nose-ring" tell is unambiguous at 1x; one clean
   brass hoop, not overlapping the smug-mouth line.
6. **Grayscale tell is weak up top.** In the desaturated check the head+goggles carry,
   but with no arms the upper-body value structure is flat and could be any imp. The
   crossed-arm torso will fix this — confirm in next round that the arm-fold creates a
   readable horizontal shadow band so the value read survives colorblind/low-contrast.
7. **Pillar fluting contrast is slightly muddy.** The vertical flutes on the lamp shaft
   are close in value to the brass fill; deepen the deep-rust groove lines ~10-15% so
   the banding reads as fluting at obstacle scale and not a plain gold tube. Cap fire-curl
   is good — keep it dropping INTO the gap.

## Distinctness / drift check
- vs **Leyak** (sibling, bodiless head + trailing viscera): CURRENTLY TOO CLOSE because
  Ifra lost its torso. Fixing #1 resolves this — Ifra = crossed-arm fire torso over a
  compact smoke blob; Leyak = pure head over a long streaming ribbon. Make sure Ifra's
  base stays a short rounded blob, NOT a long descending trail.
- vs batch-1 bosses / Tlaloc goggle-eyes: the spectacle-look eyes (#4) are the only
  collision risk; coal-ember eyes fix it.
- Live-fire hue ownership: confirmed unique — keep coral+saffron exclusive to Ifra.

## Priority order for round 2
1. Add the crossed-beefy-arms chibi TORSO between head and smoke-curl (blocker).
2. Shrink/soften the head; swap elf-ears for back-swept flame-horns.
3. Re-do coal-eyes (kill the spectacle rings); re-seat the nose-ring on the septum.
4. Deepen lamp fluting grooves for obstacle-scale read.
