# Ifra — round 2 critique (AD, final)

Big progress. The round-1 BLOCKER is cleanly resolved: Ifra now has a broad
crossed-beefy-arms ember torso, the head shrank and softened, elf-ears became
back-swept flame-horns, the spectacle rings are gone, and the smoke-curl stayed
short. It now reads as a genie, not a floating head, and it no longer collides
with Leyak. It is close — but two things still hold it back from ship-ready at
32px, and one is a structural read miss that the final round-3 pass must land.

## Strongest aspects (KEEP)
- **Crossed-arm torso solves the blocker.** Shoulders now sit wider than the head,
  the folded-arm horizontal mass is the dominant silhouette feature, and the
  grayscale chip confirms a readable horizontal shadow band across the fold — the
  value read survives without hue. This is exactly the Big-Reapy weight-shift
  grammar the brief wanted. Don't touch the torso construction.
- **Leyak separation: confirmed clean.** Ifra = crossed-arm fire torso over a
  short, rounded twin-lobe smoke blob; Leyak = bare head over a long streamer.
  At the 32px chip there is zero confusion. The smoke-curl staying SHORT was the
  right call — keep it short.
- **Palette still pin-exact.** Coral-ember, deep-rust, violet plume, hot-saffron
  core, brass, sheen all on-pin. Coral+violet+saffron remains the sole live-fire
  family. Triad on the violet lobes is textbook hard-edged.
- **Coal-eyes fixed.** The full ink spectacle ring is gone; partial lower keyline
  reads as embers now, and the Tlaloc-goggle collision is resolved.
- **Day/night both hold.** Coral pops on day sky, saffron core holds focal on
  night. Glow restraint correct.
- **Pillar.** Fluting grooves are deeper and read as a lamp shaft at obstacle
  scale; the wick fire-curl cap drops into the gap on-axis. Mirror is good. The
  pillar is effectively ship-ready as-is.

## Weakest aspects (FIX)
1. **The crossed arms read as a flat horizontal SAUSAGE, not a confident fold. [PRIMARY]**
   At showcase the arms are legible, but at the 32px chip the fold collapses into
   a single smooth ellipse with no internal break — you can't tell it's two arms
   crossed. The "cocky genie swagger" needs the fold to read. Add ONE hard ink
   break where the forearms overlap (a short diagonal keyline + a small value step
   between the over-arm and under-arm), and push the near shoulder/fist nub
   slightly proud of the silhouette so the crossed-arm geometry survives at 1x.
   Right now it's an arm-shaped bar; make it unmistakably two crossed arms.
2. **The head is now slightly too SMALL / sunk into the shoulders.** Round 1 over-
   sized it; round 2 over-corrected — the head reads ~50% of torso width and sits
   low between the shoulders, so the smug face (the charm carrier) is the smallest
   element and nearly pinched out at 32px. Nudge back to ~60% torso width and lift
   it a few px clear of the shoulder line so the face gets breathing room. The
   smug expression is the appeal; don't let the torso eat it.
3. **Flame-horns read as soft nubs, not flame.** They're back-swept and correctly
   placed, but they look like rounded ears/buds — the saffron flame-tip and the
   tapered flame shape aren't landing. Sharpen each horn to a clear back-swept
   point with a saffron top-edge sheen so they read as flame, not knobs. This is
   a silhouette tell, so it must survive at the chip.
4. **Nose-ring sits on the mouth line, not the septum.** Still ambiguous — the
   brass hoop overlaps the smug-mouth, reading as a lip-ring. Lift it onto a small
   defined nose/septum so the single-gold-nose-ring tell is unambiguous; keep the
   mouth line clear below it.

## Distinctness / drift check
- vs Leyak: RESOLVED. Keep the blob short.
- vs Tlaloc goggle-eyes: RESOLVED (coal-eyes).
- Live-fire hue ownership: unique, keep coral+saffron exclusive to Ifra.

## Priority order for round 3 (final pass)
1. Break the crossed-arm fold so it reads as two arms at 32px (ink overlap + value
   step + a proud fist nub).
2. Bump the head back up ~10-15% and lift it clear of the shoulders so the smug
   face carries.
3. Sharpen the flame-horns to pointed saffron-tipped flames.
4. Re-seat the nose-ring on the septum, clear of the mouth.

These are finishing notes, not a re-build — #1 and #2 are the difference between
"reads as a genie blob" and "reads as a cocky crossed-arm fire-jinn" at gameplay
scale. Land them and Ifra ships.
