# Take-Tsuchigumo — AD critique round 1

VERDICT: ITERATE

Hero is genuinely strong: believable REALISTIC non-chibi spider-yokai with culm-segment legs, an
amber eye-cluster that anchors hard, confident multi-band stepped shading — clears the realism bar at
hero. But the concept's defining job is to read as a radial bamboo STAR at true 32px on night sky, and
that is exactly where round 1 fails.

**Strongest (KEEP):** hero culm-leg construction (node-rings segment each joint, dusty-rose collars at
knuckles, muddy-tan node-band); amber 8-eye + fang focal hierarchy; hard-stepped shading; warm ambush
temperature (the #7-vs-#3 gap holds wide). Pillar SHAFT is the best in the set (jointed culm + rose
collars = clean repeat band).

**Weakest (blocking):** at 32px the silhouette collapses to a CRAB/BEETLE lozenge — legs read as short
lateral nubs, not a radial splay; blacked-out chip reads as a horned beetle/boar, not a spider; on
night the green body + green legs fuse (only amber eyes survive). Root cause: hero legs come off the
sides in two near-vertical bilateral fans with a bare front gap — heraldic, not radial.

## Round-2 punch list (prioritized)
1. **Re-pose legs into a TRUE radial splay** around the body (front pair forward/down, mids out, rear
   back); kill the bare front gap. Blacked-out chip must read as an asterisk of limbs, not a lozenge.
2. **Front-leg-pairs LARGEST (the AD fallback):** make the front 2–3 pairs clearly longest/boldest
   (~1.3–1.5× body width so they project past the body at 32px); rear pairs taper/recede. A few long
   dominant legs read as "spider" far better than eight equal nubs.
3. **Separate legs from body on NIGHT:** put the brighter muddy-tan node-band on the OUTER leg segments
   (leg edges catch light against the dark body) OR widen leg-vs-body value gap by one band. Verify on
   the night chip directly.
4. **Lift whole-form value vs night sky ~15–20%** (lit body band + rose collars/tan nodes as mid-value
   scaffold) so the body silhouette doesn't dissolve into night.
5. **Scale up the pillar gap-cap head ~30–40%** + push amber eye-glints + fang triangle so the cap reads
   as a fanged spider-head at 32px; make the lower-mirror curled claw + web-wisp a clear hooked shape.
6. KEEP hero shading/palette/eye-focal/culm-node construction exactly — this is a silhouette/legibility
   re-pose, NOT a redesign.
