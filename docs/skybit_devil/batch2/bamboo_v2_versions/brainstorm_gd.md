# Bamboo v2 — BRAINSTORM (8 directions, GD)

A SECOND bamboo boss set. The first set (Take-Ryu / Madake-Oni / Kurochiku-Nushi / Yukitake /
Kaguya — see `../bamboo_versions/brainstorm_locked5.md`) shipped CHIBI + flat-fill + scary-cute and
read **too cartoony / not bamboo-forward enough**. This set is a deliberate departure:

- **REALISTIC, not chibi.** True tall-thin culm proportions, regular node rings with node-branch
  whorls (Phyllostachys throws a pair of branches per node, alternating sides; Sasa throws a single
  branch), papery culm sheaths with brown speckle/hairs, lance-shaped foliage, real growth habit.
  Researched: moso (thick-walled, short basal internodes, strong taper, no visible nodal ridge, yellow-
  /purple-brown speckled hairy sheaths), madake (dark→bright green, two distinct rings per node, long
  internodes, straight), kurochiku/Phyllostachys nigra (green-then-blackening culm), sasa (snow-laden
  groundcover, single-branch nodes), golden P. aurea (compressed basal "tortoise-shell" internodes),
  takenoko sprout (fat sheath-wrapped cone), 67-year mass flowering→die-off. Folklore: Kaguya/take-
  tori, kadomatsu (3 diagonal-cut culms welcoming the year-god), Seven Sages of the Bamboo Grove,
  Daoist hollow-vessel emptiness, panda-as-bamboo-eater, snow-bamboo aesthetic.
- **Technique:** PROCEDURAL only, hard-edged flat shading, but the house triad pushed to **4–6 HARD
  STEPPED value bands per form** for a sculpted near-volumetric read. NO smooth gradients (protects
  true-32px legibility). Radial glow for accents only. SS=6. Hard ink keyline `(28,22,30)` + 1px
  alpha-mask outline. Realistic at hero scale, unmistakably bamboo at 32px.
- **Pillar law:** a jointed culm IS the natural pillar — node-segment = the repeat band, shaft +
  detachable gap-edge cap, bottom-rooted, never top-heavy.

## Collision map (what these 8 must dodge)

ROSTER GREENS (do not collide): Cernun deep desaturated PINE + birch-bone antler; Kappa bright
yellow-green + turtle-bronze; Kitsune snow-white + MINT foxfire; Haedung jade scale-band; Tlaloc/
Citipati grey slate-jade + coral/teal. OLD BAMBOO 5 (do not clone the concept): Take-Ryu grove-coil
dragon, Madake-Oni dry-timber ogre+club, Kurochiku-Nushi black-lacquer noble-stack, Yukitake bowed-
snow-stalk, Kaguya split-stalk-with-inner-figure.

So this set may revisit the bamboo THEME pool but must bring fresh CONCEPTS, fresh palette lanes,
fresh silhouette KINDs. Below: 8 directions, each tagged BODY-IS-BAMBOO or CREATURE-WITH-BAMBOO.

---

### 1. Takenoko-Warashi — the armored sprout-child that erupts from the earth  (CREATURE-WITH-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** A fierce young earth-spirit sheathed head-to-foot in the layered, overlapping husk-armor
  of a bamboo SHOOT (takenoko) — the moment of violent emergence, lacquered like samurai plate.
- **Centrality.** CREATURE clad in bamboo: the creature's whole armor IS real takenoko sheath-scale.
- **32px read (KIND: SHEATH-SCALE CONE-WARRIOR).** A fat upward-tapering CONE of overlapping pointed
  husk-plates with a small fierce face peeking from the apex notch and two stubby arms — a rocket-
  shaped armored figure bursting up. Bottom-WIDE, apex-narrow: the inverse of the old Madake ogre's
  squat mass, and unlike any straight-culm sibling.
- **Realistic botany.** True takenoko sheaths: broad triangular overlapping scales, each tipped with a
  curling hair-fringe and a dark speckle field (moso sheaths are brown-speckled and hairy), a pale
  ligule line at each scale-top, soil crumbs clinging at the base where it tore out of the ground. The
  emerging culm-tip shows one or two fresh green node-rings at the apex. NOT a smooth cone — every
  plate is a discrete stepped facet.
- **Palette lane (TAKENOKO sprout-tan/cream, warm earth).** husk-tan `(198,168,112)` base / umber-
  speckle shade `(140,104,58)` / cream ligule-edge `(236,222,176)` / deep soil-umber root `(96,70,44)`.
  **Accent: a single fresh-shoot lime apex `(150,196,96)`** (the only green, tiny, at the crown) +
  **dark-speckle field as texture, not a value band.** Holds clear of Madake's gold-leaf straw by being
  cooler/earthier and matte (no metallic glint) and by the lime apex.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The shoot IS the pillar: stacked sheath-plate rings = the repeat band
  (each husk-overlap a node-equivalent groove); gap-edge cap = the fresh green apex-tip with its tiny
  node-rings poking out (lower mirror = the torn soil-root ball). Bottom-rooted, mass low — clean.
- **Hook.** It grew a foot overnight; by dawn it had armor and a temper.

### 2. Sennin-no-Take — the grove hermit-sage walking on a single culm-staff  (CREATURE-WITH-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** One of the Seven Sages of the Bamboo Grove as an immortal: a gaunt, serene Daoist
  hermit of sun-bleached robes who has become half-bamboo, leaning on a tall jointed culm-staff,
  embodying the hollow-vessel emptiness of the grove.
- **Centrality.** CREATURE with bamboo: a humanoid sage whose staff + sprouting joints are real
  bamboo; his hollow sleeves echo the hollow culm.
- **32px read (KIND: TALL ROBED SAGE-WITH-STAFF).** A slender vertical robed elder, long beard, tall
  culm-staff held to one side rising past his head — a narrow human pillar quite unlike Kurochiku's
  noble-STACK (that was a body built of stacked black segments; this is a robed figure beside a
  separate staff) and unlike any creature-only sibling.
- **Realistic botany.** The staff is anatomically correct madake: long straight internodes, the
  signature TWO rings per node, a papery sheath-scar collar, real lance leaves clustered at the top.
  A live green node-shoot sprouts from his shoulder where the man is turning into grove. Robe folds
  are hard stepped fabric bands, not bamboo — the contrast sells "creature WITH bamboo."
- **Palette lane (SUN-BLEACHED weathered straw-grey, the palest warm).** bleached-culm `(206,196,158)`
  base / weathered-grey-tan shade `(150,138,108)` / parchment-robe cream `(230,222,196)`. **Accent: a
  deep persimmon sash `(196,96,52)`** (warm, saturated, small — the focal) + **a thin live-jade node-
  shoot `(96,158,104)`** kept tiny on the shoulder. The bleached near-grey body is its own lane,
  distinct from Madake's gold straw (warmer/yellower) and Sennin reads cooler, dustier, greyer.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The culm-STAFF is the pillar: madake node-segment shaft (two-ring nodes)
  = repeat band; gap-edge cap = a leaf-cluster crook with a hanging gourd (or lower mirror = the
  staff's iron ferrule + a single curled shoot). Slim, on-axis, symmetric.
- **Hook.** He sat in the grove so long the grove sat down inside him.

### 3. Sasa-Yuki-Onna — the snow-grass drift-spirit of the dwarf bamboo  (BODY-IS-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** A low, drifting winter spirit whose body is a tumbling MOUND of snow-laden SASA (dwarf
  groundcover bamboo) — many small single-branch culms fanning out, blades bowed flat under powder,
  a face forming in the drift. The horizontal snow-grass spirit.
- **Centrality.** BODY IS bamboo: the spirit is literally a thicket of living sasa.
- **32px read (KIND: WIDE LOW DRIFT-MOUND with fanning blades).** A broad low billowing mound bristling
  with many thin upswept leaf-blades, a soft face in the middle — a WIDE horizontal silhouette. This
  is deliberately the opposite of Yukitake's single tall BOWED stalk: Yukitake = one bowed culm + snow-
  loaf cap; Sasa-Yuki = a low wide fanning THICKET of many small blades. Different KIND, shared season
  but split silhouette.
- **Realistic botany.** True sasa: small, single-branch nodes (vs Phyllostachys's paired branches),
  broad lance leaves with the characteristic dry pale-margin "snow-edge" necrosis that real sasa shows
  in winter, blades fanning in flat tiers, fine culms. Snow sits as discrete hard powder-caps on each
  blade-tier, not a smooth blanket.
- **Palette lane (SNOW-on-SASA: cool blue-white over muted sage — distinct from Yukitake's white).**
  snow-powder white `(230,238,244)` over **muted sage-green sasa body `(120,156,118)`** / blue-shadow
  snow `(158,182,206)` / dry-margin pale `(214,220,194)`. **Accent: a cold periwinkle frost-glow
  `(176,200,236)`** (radial, on the face only). Reads as a green-bodied winter mound — Yukitake was
  white-DOMINANT with green only a sliver; here the muted sage is genuinely the body, snow is the
  overlay, so palette dominance is flipped. Held off roster greens by the heavy snow-desaturation +
  periwinkle accent (no roster green pairs with periwinkle).
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** A single tall sasa culm rising from the mound IS the pillar: thin single-
  branch node-segments = repeat band; gap-edge cap = a snow-capped leaf-fan tuft (lower mirror = the
  drift-mound base). The wide creature sits at the bottom; the culm tiles upward.
- **Hook.** Step into the dwarf grove in winter and the snow watches you back.

### 4. Take-no-Hone-Musha — the bone-armored grove warrior in a culm-stalk cuirass  (CREATURE-WITH-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** A dead warrior of the grove risen in samurai armor LACED FROM SPLIT BAMBOO STAVES — a
  do-maru cuirass of bound culm-slats (real Japanese armor used bamboo/lacquered slat lamellae), gaunt
  and martial, fresh shoots growing through the joins where flesh used to be.
- **Centrality.** CREATURE with bamboo: a humanoid warrior whose ARMOR is bound bamboo lamellae.
- **32px read (KIND: BROAD-SHOULDERED LAMELLAR WARRIOR).** A squared, broad-shouldered standing
  warrior, horned kabuto helm, a cuirass of horizontal slat-rows, holding a bamboo naginata upright —
  a martial humanoid block. Distinct from Madake-Oni (squat ogre with a CLUB) by being upright,
  armored, slat-laced, and weapon = bladed pole not knot-club; distinct from Sennin (frail robed sage)
  by martial mass.
- **Realistic botany.** The lamellae are convincingly split-culm: each slat shows the inner-wall pale
  cream face vs outer green skin, a node-ring crossing the slat, lacing-cord holes — real bamboo armor
  construction. A live green shoot with node-rings + lance leaves erupts from the helmet crest and one
  pauldron, sheath-collar at its base.
- **Palette lane (MOSO-BROWN cured culm, warm mid-brown — own lane).** moso-brown slat `(150,108,64)`
  base / dark-umber lacing shade `(96,68,40)` / pale split-inner cream `(218,200,158)`. **Accent: an
  indigo lacing-cord + sashimono `(64,78,128)`** (the cool counterweight, deliberately NOT a roster
  green) + **a single live-jade helm-shoot `(110,168,110)`** tiny. Moso-brown is darker and redder
  than Madake's gold straw and Sennin's bleached grey — a third distinct tan/brown lane. Indigo accent
  is unique in both bamboo sets.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The bamboo NAGINATA is the pillar: lacquered culm haft with node-rings =
  repeat band; gap-edge cap = the curved blade lashed to a split-culm collar (lower mirror = the iron
  ishizuki butt-cap). Slim pole, mass on-axis, weapon-blade modest — clean, non-top-heavy.
- **Hook.** They buried him in the grove; the grove kept the armor and gave back the soldier.

### 5. Kurochiku-Garasu-Tengu — the black-bamboo crow-tengu of the mountain grove  (CREATURE-WITH-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** A karasu-tengu (crow-billed mountain tengu, guardian-yokai of forests) feathered in the
  glossy BLACK of mature kurochiku, gripping a black-bamboo staff, wings of split-culm quills — the
  fierce winged grove-guardian.
- **Centrality.** CREATURE with bamboo: a bird-yokai whose quills/staff are blackened bamboo.
- **32px read (KIND: WINGED BEAKED TENGU with spread quill-wings).** A hunched crow-headed figure with
  a sharp beak, a tall single feather-tuft, and two angular SPLIT-CULM WINGS spread — a winged
  silhouette no bamboo sibling has, and the only WINGED form in the set. Different from Kurochiku-Nushi
  (a calm slim noble-STACK with no wings) despite the shared black-bamboo palette family — the wing +
  beak KIND fully separates them at 32px.
- **Realistic botany.** Quill-feathers are split blackened culm-slats with visible node-rings and the
  faint violet-bloom waxy sheen real Phyllostachys nigra carries; the staff is a true black-bamboo culm
  with paired branch-whorl stubs. A small ring of fresh green node-shoots collars the staff-top (the
  living vs blackened contrast).
- **Palette lane (BLACKENED kurochiku — shared family with Nushi but re-keyed cooler).** black-culm
  `(46,40,50)` base / ink-violet shade `(28,24,36)` / **steely blue-grey quill-sheen `(132,140,162)`**
  (cooler/steelier than Nushi's violet-white). **Accent: a vermilion tengu-nose/beak-wattle
  `(206,68,52)`** (the focal) + **a thin gold staff-band `(214,176,90)`**. NOTE: to satisfy the no-two-
  share-BOTH-palette-AND-KIND gate, this shares the black family with Nushi from the OLD set — but
  Nushi is in the OLD set, not this one, and the KIND (winged tengu vs noble-stack) differs; within
  THIS set of 8 it is the SOLE black lane. AD may swap the steely sheen if it wants more daylight from
  Nushi.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The black-bamboo STAFF is the pillar: black node-segment shaft = repeat
  band; gap-edge cap = a feather-tuft crook hung with a small hand-drum/shide (lower mirror = the
  staff's gold ferrule + curled shoot). Slim on-axis.
- **Hook.** The mountain grove keeps a guardian; it has a beak and it does not warn twice.

### 6. Kadomatsu-Shin — the New-Year gate-god of three diagonal-cut culms  (BODY-IS-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** The Toshigami (year-welcoming god) manifest as a living KADOMATSU: three fresh-green
  culms cut on the steep diagonal, bound at the waist, pine + plum sprigs at the base, a serene
  auspicious face glowing where the bindings cross. The festive grove-shrine boss.
- **Centrality.** BODY IS bamboo: the god literally is the three-culm kadomatsu arrangement.
- **32px read (KIND: TRI-CULM DIAGONAL-CUT CLUSTER).** Three vertical culms of stepped heights, each
  ending in a bright OVAL diagonal-cut mouth at the top (the unmistakable kadomatsu slant-cut),
  bound by a woven straw collar mid-body — a clustered tri-stalk read. Totally distinct from every
  single-stalk sibling and from Kaguya's split-OPEN single stalk: this is three SEPARATE slant-cut
  culms bundled.
- **Realistic botany.** Fresh madake culms: bright living green, two-ring nodes, the diagonal cut
  showing the pale hollow ring-wall and the inner cavity (real kadomatsu cuts reveal the hollow). Woven
  rice-straw rope binding (shimenawa-style) at the waist; pine needle-fans + red plum blossoms tucked
  at the base — botanically the real kadomatsu companions. Steep slant-cuts, hard stepped.
- **Palette lane (FRESH-CUT festive green + auspicious red/straw — a bright lane, not the cool jade of
  Take-Ryu).** fresh-culm green `(110,176,96)` base / deep node-green shade `(64,124,66)` / pale cut-
  ring cream `(222,212,168)` / straw-rope `(206,176,104)`. **Accent: auspicious vermilion plum + face-
  glow `(212,76,58)`** + **a soft pale-gold blessing glow `(244,224,150)`** (radial, on the bound
  face). Distinct from Take-Ryu's COOL saturated jade (Take-Ryu held cool + lacquer-red whisker beads,
  serpent body); Kadomatsu green is warmer/yellower and the silhouette is a static tri-cluster, not a
  coil — split on both axes.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The center culm IS the pillar: fresh node-segments = repeat band; gap-
  edge cap = the bright diagonal-CUT mouth with its hollow ring + a pine sprig (lower mirror = the
  straw-bound base with plum). Bottom-rooted bundle, the tallest culm tiles up — clean.
- **Hook.** Set it at the gate and the year itself comes to stand there, watching.

### 7. Take-Tsuchigumo — the grove spider-yokai woven into a culm-thicket web  (CREATURE-WITH-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** A tsuchigumo (earth-spider monster yokai) lurking in the deep grove, its long legs each a
  jointed bamboo culm-segment, its web strung between standing culms — the eight-legged grove ambusher.
- **Centrality.** CREATURE with bamboo: a spider-yokai whose legs ARE jointed culm-segments and whose
  trap is woven through the grove.
- **32px read (KIND: RADIAL MULTI-LEG SPIDER).** A compact bristled body with a cluster of long
  jointed legs arcing out and down, fangs below, set against vertical culm-stems — a radial arachnid
  star, the ONLY radial/many-limbed silhouette in the set. Reads instantly different from every
  vertical-stalk and every humanoid sibling.
- **Realistic botany.** Each leg is a real culm: distinct node-rings segmenting the leg-joints (the
  node IS the leg-knuckle — botany and anatomy fuse), a sheath-collar at each joint, fine leaf-hairs as
  the spider's bristles. The body carapace shows split-culm plating with paired branch-stub spinnerets.
- **Palette lane (DEEP FOREST-GROVE green-black, the shadowed deep-grove lane).** deep grove-green
  `(58,90,62)` base / near-black bark shade `(34,54,38)` / muddy-tan leg-node `(150,120,72)`. **Accent:
  a venom-amber eye-cluster + fang-tip `(232,176,72)`** (warm focal, eight tiny eye-glints) +
  **a dusty-rose sheath-collar `(178,108,98)`**. This deep grove-green is darker and bluer than
  Kadomatsu's fresh green and warmer-brown-tinged vs Cernun's blue-pine — held apart by the amber+rose
  accent pairing (no roster green uses amber-rose). Radial spider KIND keeps it clear regardless.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** A single culm-LEG (or the strung dragline-culm) is the pillar: node-
  segment leg = repeat band (each node a leg-knuckle); gap-edge cap = the fanged head-carapace with
  eye-glints (lower mirror = a curled leg-tip claw + a wisp of web). Slim jointed leg, on-axis.
- **Hook.** The grove is quiet because something in it is patient.

### 8. Take-Ryu-no-Tama — the dew-pearl grove-koi rising in a single luminous culm  (BODY-IS-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** A pale, translucent ascending KOI-spirit whose body is a single luminous green culm
  beaded with morning dew, fins of leaf-blades, rising vertically as if leaping the grove toward
  heaven — a serene glowing fish-of-the-grove. (Distinct from Take-Ryu's coiling DRAGON: this is a
  straight rising KOI, calm not reared.)
- **Centrality.** BODY IS bamboo: the koi's body is literally a single jointed culm; fins are leaf-
  blades; "scales" are node-rings.
- **32px read (KIND: VERTICAL LEAPING-KOI STALK).** A straight upright culm-stalk with a rounded koi
  HEAD finial at the top (round eye, whisker-barbels, soft mouth) and paired leaf-blade FINS flaring
  at the sides, a fan-tail at the base — a fish-headed vertical stalk. The round koi head + side-fins
  differ from Take-Ryu's whiskered CRANIAL dragon head + coil body, and from Kaguya's split-aperture
  (no figure inside — here the stalk IS the creature).
- **Realistic botany.** A genuine green culm: even node-rings (read as koi scale-bands), a translucent
  dew-wet sheen with discrete hard dew-bead highlights at node joints (not a smooth gloss), real lance
  leaf-blades as the dorsal/pectoral fins with visible parallel veining. Fresh sheath-collars at two
  nodes.
- **Palette lane (DEW-PALE luminous jade-mint + pearl-cyan glow — a glowing lane held off Kitsune mint
  & Kaguya gold).** dew-pale jade `(150,196,158)` base / cool node-green shade `(92,148,108)` / pearl-
  highlight cream `(224,236,212)`. **Accent: a cool pearl-CYAN dew-glow `(168,224,224)`** (radial, on
  head + dew-beads — a teal-leaning cyan, distinct from Kitsune's warm MINT `(150,224,196)` and from
  Yurei blue-cyan and Kaguya gold-white) + **a single coral koi-eye/barbel-bead `(224,120,96)`** focal.
  Pale-luminous-jade lane is distinct from Take-Ryu's saturated cool jade (heavier value) and from
  Kadomatsu's opaque fresh green (this one glows).
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The luminous culm IS the pillar: dew-beaded node-segments = repeat band;
  gap-edge cap = the round koi-HEAD finial with barbels + a pearl dew-glow (lower mirror = the fan-tail
  fin curl). Straight symmetric stalk — cleanest mirror after Kadomatsu.
- **Hook.** Once a century the grove's dew gathers a fish and sends it up the sky.

**No two of the 8 share BOTH palette family AND silhouette KIND.** Palette families span eight distinct
bamboo lanes: takenoko-tan / bleached-grey-straw / snow-on-sage / moso-brown / blackened-kurochiku /
fresh-cut-green / deep-grove-green-black / dew-pale-luminous-jade. Silhouette KINDs span eight distinct
reads: cone-warrior / robed sage-staff / wide drift-mound / lamellar warrior / winged tengu / tri-culm
cluster / radial spider / leaping-koi stalk.

**Vs the OLD bamboo 5** (coil-dragon / ogre+club / noble-stack / bowed-snow-stalk / split-stalk-inner-
figure): none repeat a KIND, none repeat the concept. Closest adjacencies deliberately split — Sasa-Yuki
vs Yukitake (wide drift-mound vs single bowed stalk; sage-body vs white-body); Take-no-Hone-Musha vs
Madake-Oni (upright slat-lamellar warrior + bladed pole vs squat ogre + knot-club); Take-Ryu-no-Tama vs
Take-Ryu (straight koi stalk + round head vs reared dragon coil + cranial head); Sennin vs Kurochiku-
Nushi (robed figure beside a staff vs body of stacked black segments). Kadomatsu (tri-cluster) vs Kaguya
(single split-open stalk) split on cluster-vs-single.

**Vs roster greens:** Takenoko/Sennin/Hone-Musha are warm tans/browns (not green bodies at all). Sasa-
Yuki's muted sage is snow-dominated + periwinkle-accented (no roster green pairs with periwinkle).
Kadomatsu fresh-green carries vermilion+pale-gold (warmer/yellower than Cernun pine, less yellow than
Kappa, and a festive tri-cluster KIND). Take-Tsuchigumo deep grove-green carries amber+rose on a radial
spider (no roster green is dark-grove + amber-rose + arachnid). Take-Ryu-no-Tama's dew-pale jade glows
pearl-CYAN, held clear of Kitsune mint / Yurei blue / Haedung jade-band / Kaguya gold. Kurochiku-Garasu-
Tengu is black, not green — no green-lane conflict.
