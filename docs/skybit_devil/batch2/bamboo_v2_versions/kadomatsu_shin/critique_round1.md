# Kadomatsu-Shin — AD critique round 1

VERDICT: ITERATE

The hero reads beautifully — the cleanest, most convincing kadomatsu in the set (three stepped culms,
serene gold-glow bound face, base-anchored pine+plum). Blocked by 32px collapse: the diagonal-cut
hollow mouths (the whole concept's signature) vanish at gameplay scale, and the tri-culm stepping
mushes toward a single-stalk read.

**Strongest:** hero readability + charm; warm yellow-green correctly clear of roster greens; honest
hard-stepped bands. **Weakest:** the cut-mouths collapse at 32px (cream oval too small, dark cavity
eats its area); night read goes muddy-dark (only the gold face-glow carries); back culms merge.

KEEP: hero tri-culm proportions, the cut-CREAM `(222,212,168)` ring-wall + cavity at hero, base-
anchored pine/plum, the bound gold-glow face, warm temperature + hard bands.

## Round-2 punch list (prioritized)
1. **Make cut-mouths survive 32px (top):** enlarge the cream cut-oval on the tallest front culm ~1.6–1.8×;
   shrink/lighten the dark inner cavity so the dominant small-scale read is a BRIGHT CREAM DISC. The cream
   ring must be the single brightest value on the form.
2. **Front-back hierarchy:** front culm's mouth large + bright, the two stepped-back mouths smaller cream
   nubs. One unmistakable cut + two hints reads as a cluster; three equal smudges read as noise.
3. **Hold the night read:** lift green body value/warmth ~15% + thin warm rim-keyline on culm edges so the
   three-culm stepping survives at 32px on night.
4. **Widen/brighten the stepping at small scale** (more horizontal offset, or darken back-culm node-green
   one step) so back culms don't merge into the center — the stepped-cluster KIND must survive the blur.
5. **Rework pillar gap-cap** as an explicit fresh diagonal-CUT (fatter cream cut-oval at a clear slant +
   pine tuft to one side), not a symmetric arrow-point.
6. **Anchor the lower-mirror plum cap** in value (brighten/enlarge plum + cream ring) so it doesn't vanish
   into the green pillar on day.
7. Keep the straw bind reading as a horizontal BIND, not protruding arms (protects body-is-bamboo + KIND).
