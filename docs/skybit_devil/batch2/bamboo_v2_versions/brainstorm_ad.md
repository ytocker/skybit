# Bamboo v2 — AD brainstorm-critique (cull 8 → 5)

VERDICT: KEEP 1 Takenoko-Warashi, 3 Sasa-Yuki-Onna, 5 Kurochiku-Garasu-Tengu, 6 Kadomatsu-Shin,
7 Take-Tsuchigumo. CUT 2 Sennin-no-Take, 4 Take-no-Hone-Musha, 8 Take-Ryu-no-Tama.

## Cut rationale
- **2 Sennin-no-Take** — sun-bleached straw-grey is the blandest, lowest-contrast lane (near-grey
  body washes out at 32px); a frail robed-figure-beside-a-staff is silhouette-fragile; stacks a third
  warm-pale lane onto #1/#4.
- **4 Take-no-Hone-Musha** — the THIRD warm-tan/brown body lane; broad-shouldered humanoid block sits
  between Sennin and the old Madake-Oni; most generic yokai-boss silhouette. (Indigo-cord idea could
  fold forward later if a martial entry is ever wanted.)
- **8 Take-Ryu-no-Tama** — dew-pale luminous jade-mint is the closest lane in either set to Kitsune's
  MINT foxfire (a glowing pale-green-cyan creature is Kitsune's territory), AND reuses the old
  Take-Ryu name/koi-dragon concept space. Thinnest distinctness margin of the four green-bodied
  directions; the green to thin out.

## Surviving-5 checks
- **Mix: PASS** — 3 CREATURE (1, 5, 7) + 2 BODY (3, 6); neither kind culled out.
- **Palette spread: PASS, clean** — takenoko-tan / snow-on-sage / blackened-kurochiku / fresh-cut-
  green / deep-grove-green-black. Cutting Sennin+Hone-Musha removed warm-tan crowding; cutting #8
  removed the pale-jade-vs-Kitsune risk.
- **Silhouette KINDs: all five distinct** — cone-warrior / wide drift-mound / winged tengu / tri-culm
  cluster / radial spider. Strongest axis of the set.
- **Thin margin to watch — #3 vs #7** (both "green creature in the grove"): split on KIND (wide mound
  vs radial spider) and palette (cool snow-sage vs deep grove-green-black), so they clear the gate;
  widen the gap by emotional temperature — #3 HARD cool winter-spirit, #7 HARD warm ambush-predator.
