# Bamboo v2 — LOCKED 5 (per-concept briefs)

A SECOND bamboo boss set, a deliberate departure from the first (chibi / flat / scary-cute, in
`../bamboo_versions/`) which read too cartoony and not bamboo-forward enough. This set is
**REALISTIC and unmistakably bamboo**. GD brainstormed 8 directions (`brainstorm_gd.md`); the AD
culled to these 5 and cut **Sennin-no-Take** (blandest sun-bleached straw-grey lane, silhouette-
fragile robed stalk, a third warm-pale lane), **Take-no-Hone-Musha** (a third warm-tan/brown lane,
generic broad humanoid block between Sennin and the old Madake-Oni), and **Take-Ryu-no-Tama** (dew-
pale jade-mint rides Kitsune's mint lane + reuses the old Take-Ryu name/koi-dragon concept space).
The surviving 5 split clean on both palette family AND silhouette KIND, hold a 3 creature / 2 body
mix, and dodge the roster green lanes and the old bamboo 5.

## House grammar (REALISM-elevated)
REALISTIC proportions — NO chibi big-head. Botanical accuracy is the whole point: true tall-thin
culm proportions, regular node rings with node-branch whorls (Phyllostachys = paired branches/node,
Sasa = single), papery culm sheaths with brown speckle/hairs, lance/sasa foliage, real growth habit.
**Technique:** PROCEDURAL only (no PNGs); hard-edged flat shading but the house triad pushed to
**4–6 HARD STEPPED value bands per form** for a sculpted, near-volumetric realistic read — **NO
smooth gradients** (protects true-32px legibility). Radial glow for accents only. **SS=6** →
smoothscale. Hard ink keyline `(28,22,30)` + 1px alpha-mask outline for silhouette POP. Realistic at
hero scale, unmistakably bamboo at true 32px (day AND night).

**Pillar law:** a jointed culm IS the natural pillar — node-segment = the repeat band; shaft +
detachable gap-edge cap; bottom-rooted; never top-heavy. Mirror top↔bottom cleanly.

## CROSS-SET PINS (police every round)
- KIND spread: SHEATH-SCALE CONE-WARRIOR / WIDE-LOW DRIFT-MOUND / WINGED BEAKED TENGU / TRI-CULM
  DIAGONAL-CUT CLUSTER / RADIAL MULTI-LEG SPIDER — each unmistakable at 32px.
- Palette lanes: takenoko-tan (1) / snow-on-sage (3) / blackened-kurochiku (5) / fresh-cut-green (6)
  / deep-grove-green-black (7). The two greens (6 warm fresh-cut tri-cluster vs 7 deep cool green-
  black radial spider) are split on BOTH value/temperature AND KIND — hold that gap.
- **#3 vs #7 thin-margin watch** (both "green creature in the grove"): lean #3 HARD into the COOL
  snow-white + periwinkle winter-spirit temperature; lean #7 HARD into the WARM amber-eye/dusty-rose
  ambush-predator temperature on near-black. Different emotional temperature, not just shape.
- Clear of roster greens (Cernun pine / Kappa yellow-green / Kitsune mint / Haedung jade /
  Tlaloc-Citipati teal) and of the old bamboo 5 (Take-Ryu / Madake-Oni / Kurochiku-Nushi / Yukitake
  / Kaguya).

### 1. takenoko-warashi — the armored sprout-child that erupts from the earth  (CREATURE-WITH-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** A fierce young earth-spirit sheathed head-to-foot in the layered overlapping husk-armor
  of a bamboo SHOOT (takenoko), lacquered like samurai plate — the moment of violent emergence.
- **Palette lane (TAKENOKO sprout-tan/cream, warm earth, MATTE).** husk-tan `(198,168,112)` base /
  umber-speckle shade `(140,104,58)` / cream ligule-edge `(236,222,176)` / deep soil-umber root
  `(96,70,44)`. **Accent: a single fresh-shoot lime apex `(150,196,96)`** — the ONLY green, tiny, at
  the crown. Holds clear of Madake's gold-leaf straw by being cooler/earthier and MATTE (no metallic
  glint).
- **Silhouette KIND.** SHEATH-SCALE CONE-WARRIOR — a fat upward-tapering CONE of overlapping pointed
  husk-plates, small fierce face peeking from the apex notch, two stubby arms; bottom-WIDE, apex-
  narrow. A rocket-shaped armored figure bursting up.
- **Botany.** Broad triangular overlapping sheath-scales, each tipped with a curling hair-fringe and a
  pale ligule line at the scale-top; dark brown speckle field; soil crumbs clinging at the torn base;
  one or two fresh green node-rings at the emerging apex. Every plate a discrete stepped facet.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The shoot IS the pillar: stacked sheath-plate rings = repeat band; gap-
  edge cap = the fresh green apex-tip with tiny node-rings (lower mirror = the torn soil-root ball).
  Bottom-rooted, mass low.
- **AD HARD CONSTRAINT.** Must NOT drift toward "pinecone": KEEP the curling hair-fringe and the pale
  ligule line on every plate, and make the single lime apex node-ring genuinely the ONLY green so the
  eye locks there. Keep the speckle as texture INSIDE a fill band, NEVER as its own value step (it
  boils into noise at 32px).
- **Hook.** It grew a foot overnight; by dawn it had armor and a temper.

### 3. sasa-yuki-onna — the snow-grass drift-spirit of the dwarf bamboo  (BODY-IS-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** A low, drifting winter spirit whose body is a tumbling MOUND of snow-laden SASA (dwarf
  groundcover bamboo) — many small single-branch culms fanning out, blades bowed under powder, a face
  forming in the drift. The horizontal snow-grass spirit.
- **Palette lane (SNOW-on-SASA: cool blue-white over muted sage — flipped from Yukitake's white).**
  snow-powder white `(230,238,244)` over **muted sage-green sasa body `(120,156,118)`** / blue-shadow
  snow `(158,182,206)` / dry-margin pale `(214,220,194)`. **Accent: a cold periwinkle frost-glow
  `(176,200,236)`** (radial, on the face only) — the identity anchor; no roster green pairs with
  periwinkle.
- **Silhouette KIND.** WIDE-LOW DRIFT-MOUND with fanning blades — a broad low billowing mound
  bristling with upswept leaf-blade tiers, a soft face in the middle. A WIDE horizontal silhouette,
  the deliberate opposite of Yukitake's single tall bowed stalk.
- **Botany.** True sasa: small single-branch nodes, broad lance leaves with the dry pale-margin
  "snow-edge" necrosis real sasa shows in winter, blades fanning in flat tiers; snow as discrete hard
  powder-caps on each tier, NOT a smooth blanket.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** A single tall sasa culm rising from the mound IS the pillar: thin single-
  branch node-segments = repeat band; gap-edge cap = a snow-capped leaf-fan tuft (lower mirror = the
  drift-mound base). Wide creature at the bottom; culm tiles upward.
- **AD HARD CONSTRAINT.** The whole margin vs Yukitake lives in FLIPPED DOMINANCE: HOLD the sage-green
  as visibly the body mass and keep snow as discrete hard powder-caps, NEVER a blanket — if snow ever
  reads dominant you've rebuilt Yukitake. Cap the fanning blades at a readable 5–7 blade-clumps (not a
  fuzz) or the mound dissolves at 32px. Lean HARD into the cool snow-white + periwinkle winter-spirit
  temperature (the gap-widener vs #7's warm spider).
- **Hook.** Step into the dwarf grove in winter and the snow watches you back.

### 5. kurochiku-garasu-tengu — the black-bamboo crow-tengu of the mountain grove  (CREATURE-WITH-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** A karasu-tengu (crow-billed mountain guardian-yokai) feathered in the glossy BLACK of
  mature kurochiku, gripping a black-bamboo staff, wings of split-culm quills — the fierce winged
  grove-guardian.
- **Palette lane (BLACKENED kurochiku, re-keyed COOLER/steelier than the old Nushi).** black-culm
  `(46,40,50)` base / ink-violet shade `(28,24,36)` / **steely blue-grey quill-sheen `(132,140,162)`**.
  **Accent: a vermilion tengu-nose/beak-wattle `(206,68,52)`** (the focal) + **a thin gold staff-band
  `(214,176,90)`**. The only black lane in this set; the winged KIND fully separates it from the old
  Nushi noble-stack.
- **Silhouette KIND.** WINGED BEAKED TENGU — a hunched crow-headed figure, sharp beak, a tall single
  feather-tuft, two angular SPLIT-CULM WINGS spread. The ONLY winged form in the set.
- **Botany.** Quill-feathers are split blackened culm-slats with visible node-rings and the faint
  violet-bloom waxy sheen real Phyllostachys nigra carries; staff is true black-bamboo with paired
  branch-whorl stubs; a small ring of fresh green node-shoots collars the staff-top.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The black-bamboo STAFF is the pillar: black node-segment shaft = repeat
  band; gap-edge cap = a feather-tuft crook hung with a small shide/hand-drum (lower mirror = the gold
  ferrule + curled shoot). Slim, on-axis.
- **AD HARD CONSTRAINT.** Black-on-black NIGHT legibility is the real risk: push the steely blue-grey
  quill-sheen as a FIRM value band (not a whisper) so the wing edges hold against a night biome, and
  let the vermilion beak-wattle + thin gold staff-band carry the focal (those two warm accents keep it
  off Nushi AND readable). Confirm the split-culm node-rings on the quills survive at 32px; if not,
  use FEWER, BIGGER quills.
- **Hook.** The mountain grove keeps a guardian; it has a beak and it does not warn twice.

### 6. kadomatsu-shin — the New-Year gate-god of three diagonal-cut culms  (BODY-IS-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** The Toshigami (year-welcoming god) manifest as a living KADOMATSU: three fresh-green
  culms cut on the steep diagonal, bound at the waist, pine + plum at the base, a serene auspicious
  face glowing where the bindings cross. The festive grove-shrine boss.
- **Palette lane (FRESH-CUT festive green + auspicious red/straw — warm, not the cool jade of Take-
  Ryu).** fresh-culm green `(110,176,96)` base / deep node-green shade `(64,124,66)` / pale cut-ring
  cream `(222,212,168)` / straw-rope `(206,176,104)`. **Accent: auspicious vermilion plum + face-glow
  `(212,76,58)`** + **a soft pale-gold blessing glow `(244,224,150)`** (radial, on the bound face).
- **Silhouette KIND.** TRI-CULM DIAGONAL-CUT CLUSTER — three vertical culms of stepped heights, each
  ending in a bright OVAL diagonal-cut mouth at the top, bound by a woven straw collar mid-body. Three
  SEPARATE slant-cut culms bundled.
- **Botany.** Fresh madake: bright living green, two-ring nodes, the diagonal cut showing the pale
  hollow ring-wall + inner cavity (real kadomatsu cuts reveal the hollow); woven rice-straw rope at
  the waist; pine needle-fans + red plum at the base. Steep slant-cuts, hard stepped.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** The center culm IS the pillar: fresh node-segments = repeat band; gap-
  edge cap = the bright diagonal-CUT mouth with its hollow ring + a pine sprig (lower mirror = the
  straw-bound base with plum). Bottom-rooted bundle; tallest culm tiles up.
- **AD HARD CONSTRAINT.** This is the CLEANEST pillar mirror in the set — protect it. The diagonal-cut
  hollow-ring mouths are the signature: make them bright cut-CREAM against the green so they pop at
  32px. Keep the three culms at clearly STEPPED heights so the cluster never reads as a single fat
  stalk. Pine/plum stays base-anchored mass, NEVER top-heavy.
- **Hook.** Set it at the gate and the year itself comes to stand there, watching.

### 7. take-tsuchigumo — the grove spider-yokai woven into a culm-thicket  (CREATURE-WITH-BAMBOO)
- **Thesis.** A tsuchigumo (earth-spider monster yokai) lurking in the deep grove, its long legs each
  a jointed bamboo culm-segment, web strung between standing culms — the eight-legged grove ambusher.
- **Palette lane (DEEP FOREST-GROVE green-black, the shadowed deep-grove lane).** deep grove-green
  `(58,90,62)` base / near-black bark shade `(34,54,38)` / muddy-tan leg-node `(150,120,72)`.
  **Accent: a venom-amber eye-cluster + fang-tip `(232,176,72)`** (warm focal, eight tiny eye-glints)
  + **a dusty-rose sheath-collar `(178,108,98)`**. Darker/bluer than Kadomatsu's fresh green, warmer-
  brown-tinged vs Cernun's blue-pine; held apart by the amber+rose pairing (no roster green uses it).
- **Silhouette KIND.** RADIAL MULTI-LEG SPIDER — a compact bristled body with a cluster of long jointed
  legs arcing out and down, fangs below, set against vertical culm-stems. The ONLY radial/many-limbed
  silhouette in the set.
- **Botany.** Each leg is a real culm: distinct node-rings segmenting the leg-joints (the node IS the
  leg-knuckle — botany and anatomy fuse), a sheath-collar at each joint, fine leaf-hairs as bristles;
  body carapace shows split-culm plating with paired branch-stub spinnerets.
- **Pillar (cap + mirror).** A single culm-LEG (or the strung dragline-culm) is the pillar: node-
  segment leg = repeat band (each node a leg-knuckle); gap-edge cap = the fanged head-carapace with
  eye-glints (lower mirror = a curled leg-tip claw + a wisp of web). Slim jointed leg, on-axis.
- **AD HARD CONSTRAINT.** Deep grove-green-black risks merging with night skies AND with its own legs
  at small scale: make the venom-amber eye-glints + fang-tips your BRIGHTEST values and the readability
  anchor. Keep leg count + arc spread legible at 32px — the radial star MUST read as a star, not a
  blob; if eight legs muddy, suggest the silhouette with the FRONT leg-pairs largest. Verify the single
  culm-LEG pillar reads as bamboo on its own. Lean HARD into the warm amber-eye/dusty-rose ambush-
  predator temperature (the gap-widener vs #3's cool winter mound).
- **Hook.** The grove is quiet because something in it is patient.

## DISTINCTNESS MATRIX (all 5 differ on palette family AND silhouette KIND)

| # | Boss | Palette family + accent | Silhouette KIND | Centrality |
|---|------|------------------------|-----------------|------------|
| 1 | Takenoko-Warashi | takenoko-tan/cream + lime apex (matte) | sheath-scale cone-warrior | CREATURE |
| 3 | Sasa-Yuki-Onna | snow-on-muted-sage + periwinkle frost-glow | wide-low drift-mound | BODY |
| 5 | Kurochiku-Garasu-Tengu | blackened kurochiku + vermilion/steel/gold | winged beaked tengu | CREATURE |

Five distinct palette families × five distinct silhouette KINDs — no two share either axis. Mix:
3 creature / 2 body. The two greens (6 warm fresh-cut cluster vs 7 deep cool green-black radial) are
split on value/temperature AND KIND; #3 and #7 (both grove greens) are split by emotional temperature
(cool winter vs warm ambush) as well as KIND. None clones the old bamboo 5 or a roster green lane.
