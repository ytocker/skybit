## ITERATION DIRECTIVES (prioritized)

1. **De-weight the pillar gap-cap (RE-SPEC A, top priority).** Pull the cap's
   ochre arc TIGHTER and NARROWER toward the shaft axis (target arc span ≈ shaft
   width +30%, no wider), and DROP the dot-finials DOWN below the arc toward the
   gap line rather than riding along the top of the arc — so the heaviest pipeclay
   mass falls INTO the gap, not above the shaft. The finial should read as a slim
   on-axis arc with dots hanging down, not as a wide horizontal smile-bar.

2. **Protect the face at true 32px.** The ring-eyes are merging toward solid discs
   and the bar-mouth thins. Hold the eye as a clear charcoal pupil inside the
   ochre ring (keep the hole open) and keep the bar-mouth ≥2px tall at 32px. If a
   pixel must go, drop the chest/brow dot COUNT before you let the eyes or mouth
   degrade — the face is the entire chip read.

3. **Thin the ochre at icon scale to lock the value pin (RE-SPEC B).** On the
   32px/1x chip, trim total warm-ochre AREA ~15-20% (narrower brow band, thinner
   mouth bar) so the chip stays an unambiguous DARK charcoal block with yellow as
   accent — pushing it further from any future warm/light drift toward Mimi. Hero
   scale can keep the fuller banding; this is a chip-only trim.

4. **Hero arms — keep, minor polish.** The wide double-arc reaching arms read well
   at hero scale; keep them HERO-ONLY (already correct). Minor: the two arc-hands
   at the bottom currently read slightly as detached mitts — tie them to the arc
   with one more keyline beat so the "reaching to scoop you" gesture is unmistakable.

5. **Antenna-horns.** The two dot-tipped horns are charming but thin; at 32px they
   nearly vanish. Either thicken them ~1px so a hint survives, or accept they drop
   at chip scale (fine — the brow band + crown silhouette already say "crowned").
   Make it a deliberate call, not an accident.
