## RULING on the GD's organ-lozenge flag

**Acceptable as-is — do NOT add a brighter organ.** The GD is right that the warm
organ-lozenge is the least robust element at the very smallest bake and goes
near-threshold against the dark belly. But the spine + 3 ribs carry the x-ray read
completely on their own — the chips prove the tell survives without the organ doing
any work. Brightening the organ now risks two regressions: (a) a second warm focal
competing with the spine for the brightest-mass slot, muddying the clean
order-of-read we just won; (b) ember/warm creep beyond the cap-confined discipline.
The organ reading as a faint warm hint at 32px and only resolving at hero scale is
the CORRECT behaviour for a see-through-fish — the skeleton is the headline, the
organ is the reward for looking closer. Leave it. The bones earned the focal; don't
split it.
