# Mokoi-versions — LOCKED 5 (per-concept briefs)

Spin-off epic bosses off the shipped **Mokoi** (flat-graphic painted plank-mask spirit). This
lineage stays intentionally FLAT-GRAPHIC: saturated flat fills, hard 1–2px ink keyline
`(28,22,30)` + 1px outline, detail via PATTERN DENSITY (dots / hatch / bands) — NOT heavy 3D
triad shading. Every survivor carries the pipeclay-white DOT/linework tell as the hue-blind
read. Full rationale + cuts in `brainstorm_ad.md` (canonical).

**House style (elevated flat-graphic):** chibi + scary-CUTE; pushed EPIC via richer graphic
pattern + scale; procedural-only; SS=5–6 → smoothscale. Lineage renderer template:
`docs/skybit_devil/batch2/leyak_epic/mokoi/render_mokoi.py` (imports `game.draw` — run with
`PYTHONPATH=/home/user/skybit`). Pillar grounded in each design (painted board/strip/staff =
pillar); cap ~shaft+30%, on-axis mirror, ember stays cap-confined. Legible at true 32px day+night.

**CROSS-SET PINS:** five distinct KINDs (radial-halo face / thin stick-figure / knob-lollipop /
side-on x-ray fish / wide-arc authority) + five separated earth-pigment lanes (white-clay /
warm-yellow-light / charcoal+red / hematite-rust / charcoal+yellow-dominant). The one live
clash is **Mimi (yellow-DOMINANT, LIGHT body)** vs **Baiame (CHARCOAL-DOMINANT, yellow ACCENT)**
— separate by VALUE structure, not just hue. No grey-stone goggle-mask (Tlaloc), no cyclops
object (Karakasa); avoid Necrarch violet, Draugr/Yurei cyan, green-bands/jade, Raijin grey,
Kitsune mint.

### 1. wandjina — white-clay radial rain-ancestor  (KIND: radial-halo face; cleanest survivor)
- **Palette.** Pipeclay-WHITE dominant `(232,226,212)` (the LIGHT one — inverts source ground);
  charcoal keyline-mass `(44,40,46)`; brick-red-ochre rays `(176,84,60)`; soft yellow-ochre
  dot-field `(214,168,96)`; ember warm, cap-rim only.
- **Pillar.** Rain-streak board shaft = 1 pipeclay rain-dot column band + 1 red-ochre ray-hatch
  band per repeat; cap = small haloed face-disk (~+30%), ember at cap rim. Clean on-axis.
- **Hook.** The calm mouthless stare inside a too-huge raining halo — the weather-god that
  watches and never speaks.
- **Watch.** Halo = crisp graphic STARBURST (alternating long/short ray-tips), NEVER a soft glow
  ring or a Raijin drum-ring; rays radiate from the band, not a circle of discrete objects.

### 2. mimi — thin crosshatch stick-spirit  (KIND: thin stick-figure)
- **Palette.** Warm yellow-ochre body `(204,158,86)` (LIGHT-ground / yellow-DOMINANT); charcoal
  hatch-keyline `(42,38,44)`; pipeclay-white dot-joints `(232,226,212)`; faint red-ochre spear
  accent `(168,86,58)`; ember at spear-tip cap only.
- **Pillar.** Thrown spear + woomera shaft = 1 hatch-banded spear-shaft + 1 dot-knot binding per
  repeat; cap = barbed spear-tip plaque (~+30%), ember-tipped. On-axis.
- **Hook.** A frantic gangly scribble-spirit, all speed — cute like a doodle come alive.
- **Re-spec / PIN.** Yellow-DOMINANT, overall LIGHT value (opposite of Baiame). **Oversized
  high-contrast dot-HEAD anchors the silhouette** so the thin hatch body doesn't dissolve at
  32px — head carries the read, hatch is texture.

### 3. quinkan-imjim — knob-headed ambush-imp  (KIND: knob-lollipop)
- **Palette.** Charcoal body dominant `(48,42,46)` (RED-ochre lean); brick-red-ochre dot/stamp
  `(180,84,58)`; pipeclay-white concentric knob-bands `(232,226,212)`; thin yellow-ochre rim
  `(206,150,72)`; ember cap only.
- **Pillar.** Knob-tipped club / digging-stick shaft = 1 dot-column band + 1 red-ochre
  handprint-stamp band per repeat; cap = round knob-head finial (~+30%), ember. On-axis.
- **Hook.** A goofy bulb-head bobbing adorably — until you notice it's been lurking by the trail
  to thump you.
- **Watch.** Keep the neck-stalk THIN and the knob FAT so the lollipop silhouette stays
  unmistakable at 32px; body detail must not compete with the knob.

### 4. barramundi-spirit — x-ray fish-beast  (KIND: side-on x-ray animal; most original)
- **Palette.** Hematite-RUST body `(158,72,52)` (the RED-EARTH one); charcoal bone-keyline
  `(40,36,42)`; pipeclay-white rib/organ lattice `(232,226,212)`; yellow-ochre fin-tips
  `(206,150,72)`; ember cap only.
- **Pillar.** Fish-trap / weir-pole shaft = 1 rib-ladder band + 1 dot-row band per repeat
  (echoes the body lattice); cap = small whole x-ray fish-medallion (~+30%), ember. On-axis.
- **Hook.** A chubby wide-eyed fish, adorable until you see right through it to its glowing bones.
- **Re-spec.** SPINE = one bold thick charcoal line, 3–4 ribs MAX per side + one clear pipeclay
  organ-lozenge; NO dense rib-comb (turns to grey mush at 1×). The see-through read must survive
  at gameplay scale.

### 5. baiame-allfather — horned sky all-father  (KIND: wide-arc authority; the monumental one)
- **Palette.** Charcoal body `(46,42,48)` (CHARCOAL-DOMINANT); bright yellow-ochre bands/arcs
  `(214,162,82)` (ACCENT only); pipeclay-white dot-rows `(232,226,212)`; thin brick-red-ochre rim
  `(172,92,60)`; ember cap only.
- **Pillar.** Banded ceremonial staff / message-stick shaft = 1 ochre band-stripe + 1 pipeclay
  dot-row per repeat; cap = SLIM single on-axis arc-finial (see re-spec A).
- **Hook.** A stern but oddly endearing big-eyed elder whose reaching arms could scoop you up or
  smite you — the monumental, authoritative read the set otherwise lacks.
- **RE-SPEC A (top-heavy pillar, REQUIRED).** Wide double-arc crown stays on the CHARACTER render
  ONLY; the PILLAR cap = a SINGLE compact arc-finial tucked tight to the shaft axis, dot-tipped
  finials dropped DOWN toward the gap so mass falls into the gap, not above it.
- **RE-SPEC B (palette pin vs Mimi, REQUIRED).** Read as a DARK charcoal authority block with
  yellow as accent — opposite value structure from Mimi's light body. If it drifts warm/light
  toward Mimi at 32px, push body charcoal darker + thin the ochre banding.
- **Watch.** Keep the big concentric ring-eyes + severe bar-mouth — that face is what makes the
  monument scary-CUTE, not just imposing.
