# Mokoi-versions — BRAINSTORM (GD, brainstorm mode; no full render)

Lineage anchor: shipped **Mokoi** (concept #4 of leyak_epic) — a deliberately
FLAT-graphic painted plank-MASK spirit. Charcoal-dominant ground
`(46,42,48)`; twin-OCHRE accents `(206,150,72)`/`(170,108,52)`; pipeclay-white
`(238,232,220)` DOT-pattern as the protected hue-blind tell; big concentric
ring-eyes; a bark-art ribbon pillar; ember confined to the cap. Detail comes
from PATTERN DENSITY, NOT 3D triad shading.

These 8 are DISTINCT NEW bosses that share Mokoi's DNA *loosely* — a
FLAT-graphic painted-spirit / mask / rock-art / dot-painting aesthetic — each
its own creature (different folklore, silhouette KIND, anatomy, palette). NOT
eight recolored Mokois.

## House style this lineage inherits (the Mokoi twist on the roster grammar)

CHIBI + scary-CUTE; **stays FLAT-GRAPHIC** — saturated flat fills, hard 1–2px
ink keyline `(28,22,30)`, 1px grown outline, detail via PATTERN DENSITY (dot
rows, hatch bands, banding, concentric rings) NOT the dark-core→fill→sheen 3D
triad. Procedural-only (no gradients/PNGs). Pushed EPIC via richer graphic
pattern + bigger render scale + more geometry, NOT via shading. Must still read
as BUILT like the roster (keylined, outline-popped) — not a foreign cutout.

## Distinctness guardrails (per `distinct-design-variants`)

Each a unique bottom-up design. None may collide with the source Mokoi or the
shipped roster. Hold the charcoal + ochre + pipeclay-dot LANE but make each a
distinct mask/painted-spirit GRAPHIC + distinct silhouette KIND + distinct
palette lane. HARD-avoid collisions:
- **Tlaloc** (grey stone goggle-mask, coral mouth focal) — no grey-stone
  goggle masks here.
- **Karakasa** (object-yokai, single-eye) — no single-cyclops-eye object.
- Owned hues to stay clear of: Necrarch violet, Draugr ice-cyan, the three
  green-bands (pine/grey-jade/yellow-green) + Jiangshi jade, Raijin storm-grey,
  Kitsune mint / Yurei blue-cyan glows, Catrina/Mariachi/Ifra warm-festive
  coral-pink. Each of the 8 must read distinct at 32px from each other AND from
  the source Mokoi's charcoal+twin-ochre.

The 8 spread across the charcoal/ochre/earth-pigment family by leaning each to
a DIFFERENT ground tone (red-ochre, white-clay, black-charcoal, hematite-rust,
yellow-ochre, dark-umber) + a DIFFERENT pattern grammar (dots / x-ray-bone /
crosshatch / concentric-halo / scarification-relief / striped-banding).

## 1. Wandjina — the haloed mouthless rain-ancestor, dot-rain crown
- **Thesis:** a serene white-clay rain-spirit face ringed by a colossal radiant
  HALO of pipeclay rays, no mouth, raining dot-pattern down its own body — the
  weather-ancestor as a calm, eerie sun-disk face.
- **32px read:** a big rounded white face filling a WIDE radiant halo-ring of
  short rays (a sunburst-halo KIND — distinct from Mokoi's rectangular plank);
  no mouth at all (the eerie tell); huge dark ring-eyes. Wide-radial top read.
- **Flat-graphic chibi anatomy:** round pipeclay-white face mass; thick charcoal
  keyline halo-band studded with a dense RING of red-ochre rays; two oversized
  black almond-eyes joined by a single broad nose-bar (the Wandjina signature);
  NO mouth. Elevated pattern detail: rows of pipeclay rain-dots streaming down
  the cheeks + a dense ochre dot-field inside the halo band; ray-tips alternate
  long/short for a crisp graphic starburst — density, not shading.
- **Grounded pillar:** rain-streak BOARD shaft = 1 vertical band of pipeclay
  rain-dot columns + 1 red-ochre ray-hatch band per repeat; gap-edge cap = a
  small haloed face-disk (~shaft +30%) with ember confined to the cap rim.
  On-axis, clean mirror.
- **Palette (white-clay ground — the LIGHT one):** pipeclay-white dominant
  `(232,226,212)`; charcoal keyline-mass `(44,40,46)`; brick-red-ochre rays
  `(176,84,60)`; soft yellow-ochre dot-field `(214,168,96)`; ember cap-glow
  warm only on cap. (Inverts Mokoi: white-DOMINANT vs charcoal-dominant.)
- **Scary-cute-but-EPIC hook:** the calm mouthless stare + a too-huge raining
  halo = beautiful and unnerving; the weather-god that watches and never speaks.

## 2. Mimi — the impossibly-thin stick-spirit, single-line crosshatch body
- **Thesis:** a wiry trickster cliff-spirit drawn as elongated dynamic
  stick-figure linework, all motion and crosshatch — the rock-art figure that
  *moves*, a thin scampering ancestor.
- **32px read:** an extremely THIN elongated figure mid-stride, limbs splayed in
  dynamic running/dancing motion (the dynamic-thin-figure KIND — the slim
  counter to every blocky mask in the set); tiny dot-head atop a long
  hatch-lined torso. A reads-as-LINEWORK silhouette no other take has.
- **Flat-graphic chibi anatomy:** "chibi" here = an oversized round dot-head on
  a deliberately spindly hatch-striped body; thin charcoal-keyline limbs frozen
  in a leaping pose; ring-eyes shrunk to two bright dots. Elevated pattern
  detail: the whole body filled with parallel yellow-ochre HATCH-lines + cross-
  ties (Mimi figures are defined by their internal line-fill), dot-joints at
  every elbow/knee — density via linework, not mass.
- **Grounded pillar:** the figure's own thrown SPEAR + woomera as the shaft =
  1 hatch-banded spear-shaft + 1 dot-knot binding per repeat; gap-edge cap = a
  barbed spear-tip plaque (~shaft +30%) ember-tipped only at the cap.
  Naturally vertical, on-axis.
- **Palette (yellow-ochre ground — the WARM-light one):** warm yellow-ochre
  body `(204,158,86)`; charcoal hatch-keyline `(42,38,44)`; pipeclay-white
  dot-joints `(232,226,212)`; faint red-ochre accent `(168,86,58)` on the
  spear; ember at spear-tip cap only.
- **Scary-cute-but-EPIC hook:** a frantic, gangly scribble-spirit that's all
  speed — cute like a stick-doodle come alive, epic in its restless,
  hatch-charged motion; the thin one that darts where the heavy masks loom.

## 3. Quinkan-Imjim — the knob-headed stalker, big knob-club totem
- **Thesis:** a squat ambush-imp with a club-knob crowning its head, the
  mischievous Imjim of the rock shelters — short, lurking, bulb-topped.
- **32px read:** a stubby compact body topped by an unmistakable round KNOB on a
  short neck-stalk (the knob-headed KIND — a lollipop-on-a-body read nothing
  else has); wide stance, low and lurking. Bottom-compact, knob-top.
- **Flat-graphic chibi anatomy:** squat charcoal body, short bowed legs planted
  wide; a thin neck-stalk lifting a fat round dot-knob "head"; small ring-eyes
  on the knob, sly half-lid; stubby grabber arms. Elevated pattern detail: the
  knob ringed in concentric pipeclay dot-bands; body filled with dense
  vertical dot-columns; red-ochre hand/footprint stamps as accents — density
  via stamped marks.
- **Grounded pillar:** knob-tipped CLUB / digging-stick shaft = 1 dot-column
  band + 1 red-ochre handprint-stamp band per repeat; gap-edge cap = the round
  knob-head finial (~shaft +30%) ember-glow confined to the cap.
- **Palette (charcoal ground, RED-ochre lean — distinct from Mokoi's twin-ochre
  balance):** charcoal body dominant `(48,42,46)`; brick-red-ochre dot/stamp
  `(180,84,58)`; pipeclay-white concentric knob-bands `(232,226,212)`; thin
  yellow-ochre rim `(206,150,72)`; ember cap only. (Red-DOMINANT accent vs
  Mokoi's balanced twin-ochre.)
- **Scary-cute-but-EPIC hook:** a goofy bulb-head that bobs adorably — until you
  notice it's been lurking by the trail to thump you; ambush-cute, knob-club epic.

## 4. Barramundi-Spirit — the x-ray fish-beast, visible-bone latticework
- **Thesis:** a great painted river-fish ancestor rendered x-ray style — its
  spine, ribs and organs drawn as a glowing pattern-lattice INSIDE the
  silhouette; the totem-beast brought to life by showing its insides.
- **32px read:** a broad fat fish-body seen flat side-on, big lobed tail and
  dorsal fin, a wide round eye — and a visible internal SPINE+RIB ladder running
  through it (the x-ray-skeleton KIND — the only see-through anatomy in the
  set). A leaf-shaped beast read, not a mask.
- **Flat-graphic chibi anatomy:** chibi = an oversized round head-eye on a short
  fat body; flat hematite-red fish silhouette; a single thick charcoal spine-
  line with evenly spaced rib-bones forking off; a stylized organ-shape (heart/
  gut) as a pipeclay lozenge mid-body. Elevated pattern detail: the rib-ladder +
  cross-fill hatch between ribs + a dot-row along the lateral line — the x-ray
  lattice IS the detail, dense and graphic.
- **Grounded pillar:** a strung FISH-TRAP / weir-pole shaft = 1 rib-ladder band
  + 1 dot-row band per repeat (echoes the body's own bone-lattice); gap-edge
  cap = a small whole x-ray fish-medallion (~shaft +30%) ember at cap only.
- **Palette (hematite-rust ground — the RED-EARTH one):** hematite-rust body
  `(158,72,52)`; charcoal bone-keyline `(40,36,42)`; pipeclay-white rib/organ
  lattice `(232,226,212)`; yellow-ochre fin-tips `(206,150,72)`; ember cap only.
- **Scary-cute-but-EPIC hook:** a chubby, wide-eyed fish that's adorable until
  you realize you can see right through it to its bones — the totem made
  "alive" by its glowing skeleton; cute-creepy x-ray epic.

## 5. Gope — the carved-board river-spirit, scarification relief grooves
- **Thesis:** a Papuan-Gulf spirit BOARD that woke up — an ovoid painted plank
  whose face is built from nested U-grooves and chevron scar-bands; a
  carved-and-painted shield-spirit (distinct KIND from Mokoi's rectangular
  plank: this is the rounded ovoid board with relief-line grammar).
- **32px read:** a tall rounded-rectangle / ovoid board, its surface a stack of
  nested U-shapes and concentric chevrons framing a small face near the top
  (the scarification-relief-board KIND); a totem-board silhouette.
- **Flat-graphic chibi anatomy:** ovoid charcoal board; a small heart-shaped
  face near the top with two ring-eyes + a flat bar-mouth; the body filled with
  nested U-curves, chevron rows and lozenge-eyes drawn as FLAT keyline grooves
  (carved-relief LOOK done with line, not shaded bevels — important: stays
  flat, "relief" is implied by parallel keylines). Elevated pattern detail:
  white-clay outline-lines doubled inside the grooves; ochre fill alternating
  band-to-band for a woven graphic rhythm.
- **Grounded pillar:** the BOARD itself is the pillar — ovoid board shaft = 1
  chevron-groove band + 1 nested-U band per repeat; gap-edge cap = a small
  scallop-topped board-finial (~shaft +30%) ember at cap rim only. The cleanest
  single-object mirror of the set.
- **Palette (dark-umber ground — the DEEP-BROWN one):** dark-umber board
  `(74,52,40)`; charcoal groove-keyline `(38,32,34)`; pipeclay-white relief-
  lines `(232,226,212)`; muted red-ochre band-fill `(166,90,62)`; ember cap
  only. (Browner + darker than Mokoi's charcoal-grey.)
- **Scary-cute-but-EPIC hook:** a wide ancient shield-face that seems to study
  you from a single high-set pair of eyes — patient, carved, eternal; the board
  that has been watching the river for a thousand years.

## 6. Eland-Therianthrope — the antelope-headed trance-shaman, dot-aura body

- **32px read:** an upright slim figure with a long down-curved ANTELOPE head +

- **Grounded pillar:** a hunting-BOW + arrow-shaft as the pillar = 1 dot-aura

- **Palette (yellow-to-red ochre blend, BLOOD-OCHRE lean — the dual-ochre one,

## 8. Gable-Spirit — the woven banded mask-stack, basketry hatch pattern

  from woven banded fibre and a long hooked beak-nose; the ceremonial-house
  spirit that loomed over the dance-ground, here a stacked-band mask-tower.
- **32px read:** a TALL NARROW elongated face, much taller than wide, with a
  long downward hooked NOSE/beak running its full length and small eyes flanking
  the top (the tall-narrow-beaked-mask KIND — the vertical inverse of Wandjina's
  wide halo); a totem-tower silhouette.
- **Flat-graphic chibi anatomy:** narrow tall charcoal mask-face; one long
  central down-hook nose dividing the face; two small ring-eyes high up; a
  flat band-mouth low. Elevated pattern detail: the whole face filled with
  woven BASKETRY hatch — alternating pipeclay + ochre diagonal weave-bands,
  concentric eye-rings, a dot-row down the nose-ridge — density via woven
  cross-hatch (a fibre-weave grammar no other take uses).
- **Grounded pillar:** the mask is itself a tower — gable-mask shaft = 1
  diagonal weave-band + 1 pipeclay dot-ridge per repeat (the basketry weave
  tiles perfectly); gap-edge cap = a small hook-nosed mask-finial (~shaft +30%)
  ember at cap only. Naturally vertical, cleanest tall-mask mirror.
- **Palette (charcoal ground, DUAL pipeclay+ochre weave — closest to Mokoi's
  family, separated by the WEAVE grammar + tall-beaked KIND, and a cooler
  greyer charcoal):** cool-charcoal `(50,48,54)`; pipeclay-white weave

- **Scary-cute-but-EPIC hook:** a long solemn face peering down from on high

  imposing as a looming tower; the watcher that hangs above the gate.
