# Mokoi-versions — AD BRAINSTORM-CRITIQUE (cull 8 → locked 5)

VERDICT: ITERATE

The set is strong and genuinely 8-wide on KIND — better spread than most
brainstorms reach. But it is NOT a locked 5: there are too many FRONTAL
MASK-FACES competing in one cluster, two of the eight sit closest to the
SOURCE (one of which the GD mis-flagged), and two warm/yellow palette lanes
collide once you collapse to five. ITERATE because the survivors need three
surgical re-specs (one top-heavy pillar, two palette pins) before the briefs
lock — not a re-roll; the bones are good.

One correction up front: the GD flagged **#8 Gable** as the leanest-distinctness
/ closest-to-source pick. That is backwards. The source Mokoi is a *rectangular
painted PLANK-MASK that is its own pillar with dot+hatch bands.* The true
closest cousin is **#5 Gope** — an ovoid painted BOARD with a face and
relief-band grammar whose board IS the pillar. That's the same sentence as
Mokoi with the corners rounded off. Gable is a *tall narrow hook-nosed
mask-tower* with a fibre-WEAVE grammar — a different KIND and a different
pattern language; it is further from source than Gope. So the distinctness cut
falls on Gope, not Gable. (Gable still gets cut, but for cluster reasons below,
not source-collision.)

Five distinct KINDs: radial-halo face / thin stick-figure / knob-lollipop /
side-on x-ray fish / wide-arc authority figure. None reads alike at 32px.
Five separated lanes: white-clay / warm-yellow-light / charcoal+red /
hematite-rust / charcoal+yellow-dominant.

## THE 3 CUTS

- **CUT #5 Gope** — closest cousin to the SOURCE Mokoi (painted board + face +
  relief bands + board-IS-pillar). Same design sentence with rounded corners;
  fails the distinctness gate against the lineage anchor it sprang from. Its
  dark-umber lane also crowds the charcoal source. (This is the GD's mis-flag
  corrected — Gope, not Gable, is the source-collision cut.)
- **CUT #8 Gable** — a third frontal MASK-FACE in a set that already keeps
  Wandjina (halo-face) and Baiame (authority-face). The tall-narrow + weave
  grammar is real, but at 32px against busy sky it lands in the same
  "patterned mask staring out" family as the two face-survivors; cool-charcoal
  lane also sits nearest the source charcoal. Cut to protect the face cluster,
  not for source-collision.
- **CUT #6 Eland-Therianthrope** — two problems. (a) It is a SECOND slim
  upright standing FIGURE alongside Mimi — both thin, both ringed by a halo of
  pipeclay dots; at 32px they blur into "skinny figure with dotted aura."
  (b) Its blood-ochre / red-leaning warm lane CLASHES with Quinkan's
  charcoal+red lean. Lovely concept, but it duplicates a kept KIND and a kept
  lane. Park it as the first alternate if any survivor falls in the per-concept
  loop.

## NEAR-DUPLICATE PAIRS / CLASHES FLAGGED (and how the 5 resolve them)

- **Frontal-mask-face cluster** (Wandjina / Baiame / Gable / Gope-face): four
  frontal patterned faces is too many. Resolved by keeping only Wandjina
  (WHITE, mouthless, radial-halo) + Baiame (wide-ARC crown, authority stance)
  — two reads that diverge hard on ground tone (white vs charcoal) AND
  silhouette envelope (radial disc vs arc-span). Cutting Gable + Gope clears
  the cluster.
- **Thin-upright-figure pair** (Mimi / Eland): resolved by cutting Eland;
  Mimi owns the thin-figure lane alone.
- **Warm/yellow lane pair** (Mimi / Baiame): both lean yellow-ochre. SURVIVES
  but must be pinned apart — see Baiame re-spec: Mimi is yellow-DOMINANT on a
  LIGHT ground; Baiame is CHARCOAL-ground with yellow as the dominant ACCENT.
  Different value structure (light figure vs dark figure), not just hue.
- **Red-lean pair** (Quinkan / former Eland): resolved by cutting Eland;
  Quinkan owns the charcoal+red lane alone.
- **Roster check (clean):** no grey-STONE goggle-mask (Tlaloc) — Wandjina is
  white-clay + radial rays, not grey stone; Baiame is charcoal+yellow, not
  grey. No single-cyclops-eye object (Karakasa) — all five have paired
  ring-eyes. None touches Necrarch violet, Draugr ice-cyan, the three
  green-bands / Jiangshi jade, Raijin storm-grey, Kitsune mint / Yurei
  blue-cyan, or the warm-festive coral-pink family. Ember stays cap-confined
  on all five. The protected pipeclay-white DOT/linework tell is present on
  every survivor as the hue-blind read.

## LOCKING BRIEFS (per survivor)

### 1. Wandjina — white-clay radial rain-ancestor  [LOCKED]
- **Palette (WHITE-clay ground — the LIGHT one, inverts source):** pipeclay-white
  dominant `(232,226,212)`; charcoal keyline-mass `(44,40,46)`; brick-red-ochre
  rays `(176,84,60)`; soft yellow-ochre dot-field `(214,168,96)`; ember warm,
  cap-rim only.
- **Character hook:** the calm mouthless stare inside a too-huge raining halo —
  beautiful and unnerving; the weather-god that watches and never speaks.
- **Pillar:** rain-streak board shaft = 1 pipeclay rain-dot column band + 1
  red-ochre ray-hatch band per repeat; cap = small haloed face-disk (~shaft
  +30%), ember at cap rim. Clean on-axis mirror — no change.
- **Re-spec:** none. This is the cleanest survivor. WATCH only: keep the halo a
  crisp graphic STARBURST (alternating long/short ray-tips), never a soft glow
  ring — it must stay flat-graphic, and it must not creep toward Raijin's
  drum-RING or a corona read. Halo is RAYS radiating from the band, not a
  circle of discrete objects.

### 2. Mimi — thin crosshatch stick-spirit  [LOCKED, palette pin]
- **Palette (yellow-ochre, LIGHT-ground / yellow-DOMINANT):** warm yellow-ochre
  body `(204,158,86)`; charcoal hatch-keyline `(42,38,44)`; pipeclay-white
  dot-joints `(232,226,212)`; faint red-ochre spear accent `(168,86,58)`;
  ember at spear-tip cap only.
- **Character hook:** a frantic gangly scribble-spirit, all speed — cute like a
  doodle come alive; the thin one that darts where the heavy masks loom.
- **Pillar:** thrown spear + woomera shaft = 1 hatch-banded spear-shaft + 1
  dot-knot binding per repeat; cap = barbed spear-tip plaque (~shaft +30%),
  ember-tipped at cap. On-axis — no change.
- **Re-spec / PIN vs Baiame:** Mimi is YELLOW-DOMINANT on a near-LIGHT read —
  the body itself is the ochre mass. Keep the figure's overall VALUE light/warm
  so it never reads as Baiame's dark-charcoal authority block. WATCH: at 32px a
  thin hatch-only figure risks dissolving into noise against busy sky — keep
  the dot-HEAD oversized and high-contrast (the chibi anchor) so the read holds
  even when the limbs thin out; the head carries the silhouette, the hatch is
  texture.

### 3. Quinkan-Imjim — knob-headed ambush-imp  [LOCKED]
- **Palette (charcoal ground, RED-ochre lean):** charcoal body dominant
  `(48,42,46)`; brick-red-ochre dot/stamp `(180,84,58)`; pipeclay-white
  concentric knob-bands `(232,226,212)`; thin yellow-ochre rim `(206,150,72)`;
  ember cap only.
- **Character hook:** a goofy bulb-head bobbing adorably — until you notice it's
  been lurking by the trail to thump you; ambush-cute, knob-club epic.
- **Pillar:** knob-tipped club / digging-stick shaft = 1 dot-column band + 1
  red-ochre handprint-stamp band per repeat; cap = round knob-head finial
  (~shaft +30%), ember at cap. On-axis — no change.
- **Re-spec:** none. WATCH: the knob-on-stalk is the whole read — keep the
  neck-stalk thin and the knob fat so the lollipop silhouette stays
  unmistakable at 32px; don't let body detail compete with the knob for the eye.

### 4. Barramundi-Spirit — x-ray fish-beast  [LOCKED]
- **Palette (hematite-RUST ground — the RED-EARTH one):** hematite-rust body
  `(158,72,52)`; charcoal bone-keyline `(40,36,42)`; pipeclay-white rib/organ
  lattice `(232,226,212)`; yellow-ochre fin-tips `(206,150,72)`; ember cap only.
- **Character hook:** a chubby wide-eyed fish, adorable until you see right
  through it to its glowing bones — the totem made "alive" by its skeleton;
  cute-creepy x-ray epic. The most original character of the five — lean into it.
- **Pillar:** fish-trap / weir-pole shaft = 1 rib-ladder band + 1 dot-row band
  per repeat (echoes the body's own lattice); cap = small whole x-ray
  fish-medallion (~shaft +30%), ember at cap. On-axis — no change.
- **Re-spec:** none. WATCH: the x-ray rib-lattice is the hero detail but is the
  highest noise-risk at 1× — keep the SPINE one bold thick charcoal line with
  3–4 ribs MAX per side and one clear pipeclay organ-lozenge; resist a dense
  rib-comb that turns to grey mush small. The see-through read must survive at
  gameplay scale, or the concept's whole point is lost.

### 5. Baiame-Allfather — horned sky all-father  [LOCKED, TWO re-specs]
- **Palette (charcoal ground, YELLOW-ochre DOMINANT accent):** charcoal body
  `(46,42,48)`; bright yellow-ochre bands/arcs `(214,162,82)`; pipeclay-white
  dot-rows `(232,226,212)`; thin brick-red-ochre rim `(172,92,60)`; ember cap only.
- **Character hook:** a stern but oddly endearing big-eyed elder whose reaching
  arms could scoop you up or smite you — grandfatherly-cute, sky-father epic;
  the monumental, authoritative read the set otherwise lacks.
- **Pillar:** banded ceremonial staff / message-stick shaft = 1 ochre
  band-stripe + 1 pipeclay dot-row per repeat; cap = double-arc finial.
- **RE-SPEC A (top-heavy pillar — REQUIRED):** the wide double-arc crown is a
  genuine top-heavy gap-cap risk (same failure mode the roster flagged on
  Cernun's antler-crook and Raijin's drum). Render the CAP as a SINGLE compact
  arc-finial tucked tight to the shaft axis (NOT the full wide double-arc head
  span), with the dot-tipped finials dropped DOWN toward the gap line so visual
  mass falls into the gap, not above it. The wide double-arc stays on the
  CHARACTER render only; the pillar cap is a slim on-axis arc-finial.
- **RE-SPEC B (palette pin vs Mimi — REQUIRED):** Baiame must read as a DARK
  charcoal authority block with yellow as the dominant accent — the opposite
  value structure from Mimi's light yellow-dominant body. Keep the charcoal body
  mass clearly dominant in VALUE; the yellow lives in the arc-dots, band-stripes
  and crown, never as a body fill. If at 32px it drifts warm/light toward Mimi,
  push the body charcoal darker and thin the ochre banding.
- **WATCH:** keep the big concentric ring-eyes + severe bar-mouth — that face is
  what makes the monument scary-CUTE rather than just imposing.

## CROSS-SET, MOST IMPORTANT FIX (one line)
Re-spec Baiame's pillar cap to a slim on-axis arc-finial (kill the top-heavy
wide double-arc cap) AND pin Baiame charcoal-DOMINANT / dark-value so it
separates from Mimi's light yellow-dominant body — that single survivor carries
both the only top-heavy pillar risk and the only surviving palette clash.

## SUMMARY OF RE-SPECS BEFORE LOCK
1. **Baiame pillar cap** → slim single on-axis arc-finial, mass dropped into the
   gap (top-heavy fix). REQUIRED.
2. **Baiame palette** → charcoal-DOMINANT dark body, yellow accent-only, to
   separate from Mimi (value, not just hue). REQUIRED.
3. **Mimi** → oversized high-contrast dot-HEAD anchors the silhouette so the
   thin hatch body doesn't dissolve at 32px. (carry-forward watch)
4. **Barramundi** → cap rib count to 3–4 per side + one bold spine + one organ
   lozenge; no dense rib-comb. (carry-forward watch)

With those applied the five lock as five distinct EPIC characters, five
silhouette KINDs, five separated earth-pigment lanes, all flat-graphic
+ keyline + pipeclay-dot tell, clean of the source and the roster.
