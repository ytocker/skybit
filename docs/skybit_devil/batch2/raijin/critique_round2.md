# Raijin — Round 2 critique (Design loop, AD pass 2 — FINAL)

The gate item landed. Round 1's fatal flaw was that the unique 32px read — a
drum-RING halo cresting over the head — wasn't on screen. Round 2 delivers it:
the four larger drums now arc OVER the top of the skull as a continuous crown,
with night sky visible in the gap between head and ring, so the CIRCLE shape is
legible. At both the 32px and 24px night chips the crimson rims survive as a
distinct cluster of dots riding above a small grey body — this silhouette is now
owned by Raijin and nothing else in the 15. The concept's moat is built.

## Strongest / weakest

- **Strongest:** the night-biome focal read AND the now-delivered halo. On the
  dark-blue swatch the storm-grey body genuinely sinks and the crimson rims +
  gold zag are the first and only things the eye catches — exactly the pinned
  behavior. The shape-tell (ring of red dots + zag) carries independent of hue,
  so the colorblind/accessibility gate is also satisfied.
- **Weakest (polish-only, non-blocking):** at the smallest 24px crop the four
  rims begin to touch and read as one continuous red band rather than four
  beads. It still reads as "halo of drums," so it does not block ship — but if a
  GD round-3 pass happens, a hair more sky between adjacent drums would let each
  rim breathe at the very smallest scale.

## KEEP (do not touch)

1. **Palette stays on-pin** — body `(108,124,144)`, gold `(248,212,96)`, crimson
   `(206,62,52)`, cloud-white base. Hold every hue.
2. **The overhead halo staging** — sky-gap between head and ring is the whole
   win. Do not re-cluster the drums toward the shoulders again.
3. **Gold committed to radiating zags** (mallets demoted to plain sticks) — gold
   now speaks one language and the zag reads as the focal. Correct call.
4. **Pillar fix applied** — the cap drum is now modest, front-on, seated tight on
   the haft axis with the crimson rim + gold zag dropping into the gap. The
   matchstick-balloon top-heaviness from round 1 is gone; mirror reads balanced
   like Big Reapy's bone-bident. Shaft banding calmed to 2-3 wood bands — it will
   no longer strobe against scrolling.
5. **Spiky storm-hair + open crimson shout** give the head wild-god energy and a
   second crimson note tying rims to face.

## FIX (optional polish for a round-3 pass ONLY — none of these block ship)

1. **24px rim-merge.** Add ~1px more negative space between the four drums so
   each crimson bead stays discrete at the absolute smallest scale (see the 24px
   night chip — rims kiss into a band). Minor; the read survives without it.
2. **Mallet sticks now near-vestigial.** With gold moved to zags, the two brown
   sticks the creature holds add little at 32px and can blur into the body
   outline. Consider dropping them entirely (the zags carry the "beating" energy)
   or shortening them so the body silhouette stays clean. Designer's call.

## Sibling-drift check

Clean. Sole storm-grey holds; gold+crimson focal stays distinct from Tlaloc
coral, Karakasa oxblood, and Mariachi rust-red in both hue and context. Crucially
the silhouette drift risk from round 1 is resolved — the overhead drum-ring is
now wholly unique and no longer edges toward a generic horned-demon read.

## Verdict rationale

This was the silhouette gate, and it is cleared at gameplay scale on both day and
night biomes, with the palette and pillar pins satisfied and the accessibility
shape-tell carrying. SHIP-READY. The two FIX items are optional finish, not
conditions of ship — integrate this version.
