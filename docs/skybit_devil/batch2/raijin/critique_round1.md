VERDICT: ITERATE

# Raijin — Round 1 critique (Design loop, AD pass 1)

Strong first pass on palette discipline and the night-biome focal read — but
the single defining promise of the brief, the **drum-RING halo arcing overhead**,
is not yet on screen. That is the gate item. Everything else is secondary.

## Ranking of the design's aspects

- **Strongest:** the night-biome focal hierarchy. On the dark-blue night swatch
  the storm-grey body genuinely recedes and the gold zags + crimson drum-rims are
  the first thing the eye lands on. The palette is on-pin (body recedes, gold +
  crimson pop). This is the hardest part of the brief and it largely works.
- **Weakest:** the drum-ring SILHOUETTE. The unique 32px read — a *halo arc of
  drums over the head* — is not delivered. The drums currently read as two
  shoulder-mounted clusters flanking the head, not a ring arcing overhead. At
  32/24px this collapses into a generic "horned/winged grey blob," which is the
  exact silhouette this concept was supposed to own. The concept's whole reason to
  exist is at risk.

## KEEP

1. **Palette is on-pin.** Body `(108,124,144)`, gold `(248,212,96)`, crimson
   `(206,62,52)`, cloud-white base all match. Do not retune the hues.
2. **Night recede + focal pop works.** Grey sinks, gold/crimson lead. Hold this.
3. **The crimson open-shout mouth** is a nice second crimson note low on the body
   that ties the rims to the face — keep it, it reads even at 24px.
4. **House triad + ink keyline** is correctly applied; the chibi proportions and
   cloud-puff perch are on-style and sit beside the batch-1 grammar.

## FIX (priority order)

1. **THE DRUM-RING IS NOT A HALO — fix this first.** Re-stage the drums as a
   continuous ARC riding OVER the top of the head (an actual crown/halo curve, like
   a rainbow of 3-5 drums cresting above the skull), not two side clusters at
   shoulder height. Right now the negative space reads "grey head with bunny-ear
   tufts." The arc must clear the top of the head with sky visible between head and
   ring so the CIRCLE shape is legible. This is the silhouette gate — nothing else
   ships until the halo reads.
2. **Cut the drum count and enlarge each.** Six small drums at native scale turn
   to crimson mush by 32px (see the night 32px crop — the rims smear into one red
   smudge). Drop to 3-4 LARGER drums on the arc so each crimson rim survives as a
   distinct dot. Fewer, bigger, cleaner.
3. **The mallets vanish at scale.** The two raised mallets read as gold zags at
   full size but disappear into the rim mass by 32px. Either commit the gold to
   LIGHTNING-ZAGS (the pinned focal) radiating from the ring, or make the mallets
   read as clear diagonal sticks — pick one gold language; right now gold is split
   between mallet-shafts and zags and neither lands.
4. **PILLAR is top-heavy — the brief's own fix is not applied.** The pillar cap
   drum is round and oversized, sitting like a lollipop on a thin shaft (see 48px
   pillar crop). The locked fix was: front-on drum at ~shaft-width +30%, seated
   TIGHT on the haft axis, crimson rim + a short gold zag dropping DOWN INTO the
   gap. Currently the zag points up/out and the mass floats at the very top. Pull
   the drum down onto the shaft, shrink it ~30-40%, and drop the zag into the gap
   line so the cap reads like Big Reapy's bone-bident, not a matchstick balloon.
5. **Shaft banding too busy.** The mallet-shaft pillar body has thin crimson
   rings that, repeated vertically, will strobe against scrolling. Use 2-3 calm
   wood bands max; let the cap carry the crimson.
6. **Flame-hair is missing.** Brief calls for wild spiky storm-hair as part of
   the fierce silhouette; current head is a smooth grey dome. Add 3-4 hard spiky
   hair-shards (grey, ink-keyed) so the head silhouette has the wild-god energy
   even before color reads.
7. **Tiger-print wrap** is currently a flat gold band — fine at 32px, but add 2
   short ink dashes so it doesn't read as a plain gold belt (minor).

## Accessibility note

The brief's shape-tell is "drum-ring CIRCLE + zag." Neither is currently legible
at 32px (no ring, zag too thin). Fixing #1 and #3 above also fixes the colorblind
read — the crimson rims and gold zag must carry as SHAPES, not just hues, and
right now they carry as neither. This is a hard gate, not a polish item.

## Sibling-drift check

No palette collision — sole storm-grey holds, gold+crimson focal is distinct from
the roster's other warm accents (Tlaloc coral, Karakasa oxblood, Mariachi
rust-red all read clearly different in hue+context). The RISK is silhouette drift:
without the overhead halo, a grey chibi with side-tufts edges toward a generic
"horned demon" read shared by nothing specific but owned by nothing either. The
halo is the entire moat — land it.

## Next-round brief (one line)

Re-stage the drums as a true overhead HALO ARC (3-4 large drums cresting above
the head, sky visible between), apply the pinned top-heavy pillar fix, and add
spiky storm-hair — keep the palette and night focal exactly as-is.
