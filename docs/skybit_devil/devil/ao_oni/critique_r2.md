# AO-ONI — round 2 critique (art-director)

VERDICT: SHIP-READY

This is there. Every one of the four round-1 blockers is resolved cleanly, and
nothing new broke in the fixing. The concept that always had the strongest
reason to exist in the ten — the max-mass cobalt slab — now ships with a pillar
half that finally matches the figure. Lock it.

## Strongest / weakest
- STRONGEST: the PROP->PILLAR turnaround. Last round it was a thin grey pole
  stranded in empty sky — the one thing keeping it off ship. The widened ~84%
  shaft now reads as a full, heavy studded iron kanabo at true obstacle scale,
  and the 1x-native crop proves it: three big high-contrast rivets per row plus
  the full-width dark groove band give a genuine banding RHYTHM that survives
  smoothscale instead of washing to a blank bar. The spiked club-head cap is an
  unmistakable gap-edge token. This is now the best-resolved pillar in the set.
- WEAKEST (cosmetic only, do NOT gate ship): at the very smallest in-game scale
  the brow/eye spacing on the head is a hair tight, so the scowl reads a touch
  more "surprised" than "grumpy" until ~1.3x. Minor; the grayscale panel still
  carries the read on brows + tusks + slab.

## KEEP (do not lose these in the final pass)
- The full-width studded shaft + full-width inter-row groove band. This is the
  identity. Keep the 3-rivet-per-row cadence and the rivet light/dark contrast
  exactly where it is — it is what makes the banding survive 1x.
- Cobalt holds on NIGHT now. Killing the additive halo for the crisp cool
  rim-sheen was the right call — the body stays unmistakably blue on the dark
  sky, so AO-ONI still owns the BLUE lane in both biomes. Held.
- Ox horn now reads as a blunt AMBER nub with a seat-ring, clearly off the
  topknot — reads as a horn in colour AND grayscale. Exactly the fix asked for.
- Simplified single topknot + darkened ink brows: the head is less cluttered and
  the scowl is the loudest face cue. Scary-cute grumpy-sumo, not grim.
- UP-tusks, sulky pout, tiger-gold loincloth as the lone warm accent — all the
  round-1 keeps are intact.

## Optional micro-polish for the final GD pass (none gate ship)
1. Nudge the two eyes ~1px closer to the nose-bridge / drop the brows a hair so
   the scowl reads grumpy rather than wide-eyed at the smallest gameplay scale.
2. The club-head cap's inner rivet cluster is slightly busier than the shaft
   rows; if it reads noisy at 1x, drop it to ~4-5 studs so the cap stays a clean
   silhouette token. Judge at 1x — if it holds, leave it.
3. Confirm the rim-sheen tick doesn't catch on the loincloth gold on night (a
   cool sheen over warm gold can mud); if it does, mask the sheen to the cobalt.

These are taste-level. The brief is met — ship it.
