## What landed (the round-1 blockers, now closed)

1. **PROP -> PILLAR — FIXED, the headline win.** At true obstacle scale the
   obstacle now reads as a continuous banded indigo POLE: a dark-indigo shaft
   with a violet sheen stripe up the left flank, gold cuff bands tiling at the
   46px pitch, and a small warm-gold brazier flame flaring INTO the gap at each
   gap-edge. Top and bottom posts mirror as one coherent torch-pole pair. The
   floating chess-pawn is gone. This was the #1 mandate failure last round and it
   is now genuinely solved — the cap is a small detachable gap-edge flourish and
   the body dominates, exactly the inversion the brief asked for.
2. **MUZZLE — reads GOAT.** The flat coin-slot face-plate is gone. It is now a
   forward-tapering snout with a rounded tip, two nostrils at the FRONT of the
   tip, and a down-curved frown. Dropping its value ~15% means it no longer
   out-shouts the eyes/horns — the sulphur eyes are correctly the brightest
   shapes now. Critically, the grayscale cell proves it: the goat reads by FORM,
   not by the horns carrying it. Group-A skull firewall holds (this is a muzzle,
   not a skull); it is the only goat-muzzle face in the set.
3. **CROWN-TORCH — reads as flame between horns.** Lifted to a tall narrow gold
   teardrop on a cradle ring, with a clear band of dark-indigo cranium beneath it
   between the horn bases. It no longer fuses into a bald gold skullcap, and at
   1x it stays distinct from the horn bases. Warm-gold only — not converging with
   Glitchfiend neon or Pyrecrown green. Guardrail clean.

## Micro-polish for the final GD pass (all optional, none blocking)

1. **Eye spacing vs. snout root.** At 1x night the two eyes + the bright snout
   highlight form a tight cluster that very slightly reads as one wide mass.
   Consider nudging the eyes ~1px apart or deepening the keyline between eye and
   snout-root so the three shapes stay individuated on the night cell.
2. **Snout highlight value.** The cream specular blob mid-snout is the second
   brightest shape after the eyes; on day it competes a touch with the eye read.
   A ~5-8% drop on that single highlight keeps the eyes unambiguously first.
3. **Legs at 1x.** The two thin indigo legs nearly vanish against the night sky
   and the dark wing roots. They are non-essential to the silhouette (wings carry
   the width), so this is fine to leave — but a 1px lighter keyline or a faint
   hoof-tip catch would let them survive if you want the full body read.
4. **Lower cuff band on a short bottom post.** Confirm the 46px tile doesn't clip
   a half-band right at the brazier on the shortest bottom-post case; if it does,
   phase-align the tile to the gap-edge so the brazier always sits on a clean
   band rather than a sliced one. (Looks correct in the shown case — just verify
   the short-post extreme.)
