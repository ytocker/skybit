# GLITCHFIEND (B6) — round 2 critique

VERDICT: SHIP-READY

The headline guardrail is now met. Round 1's two failing diagnostic cells —
the DAY 1x pink-blob and the flat-mid-gray glow-strip — both pass in round 2.
This concept now does the thing it exists to prove: a DARK angular devil that
carries its own read, with neon as an accent, not as the construction. Lock it.

## Did it clear the six judge criteria?

1. **DARK body, neon ACCENT — PASS (the make-or-break).** The value flip
   landed. On the DAY (danger-case) cell the figure now reads as a near-black
   angular devil with magenta confined to a lit-side wedge and thin cyan/pink
   piping — no pink blob, the silhouette holds against the bright sky. In the
   glow-STRIPPED grayscale cell the body is a clear near-black mass with a few
   bright filaments; squint and it is unmistakably a horned devil with a
   trident. That is exactly the pass condition I set in r1. Both cells now read
   the same way the night cell does — consistent across biomes.

2. **Glow = crisp filaments — PASS.** The bloom is gone. Bands read as discrete
   lit lines at 1x, not a haze. House-style tight additive glow, not soft bloom.

3. **Prop -> pillar — PASS.** At 82px true obstacle width the post now reads as
   a DARK void conduit with magenta side-rails and bold flat cyan pulse rungs —
   a neon-railed post, not the r1 thermometer/white-stick. The prong cap reads:
   shorter, thicker tines with a wider splay and a bright caught orb as the
   focal point. The up/down mirror is clean and the gap silhouette is legible.

4. **Reads as a DEVIL, cocky-arcade not grim — PASS.** Swept laser horns +
   trident + glitch-spade tail + laser-yellow narrowed eyes + the bolder cyan
   zig grin land the scary-cute tech-demon attitude.

5. **Sharp horns, no palette collision — PASS.** Single swept laser-blade horns
   (no ram pair). Electric magenta/cyan only with the one warm laser-yellow eye
   note; no warm torch-gold (Baalgoat), no green soul-fire (Pyrecrown).

6. **Distinct — PASS.** The only neon/synthwave/geometric devil in the set;
   no silhouette or palette overlap with the other nine or the roster.

## KEEP (carry into final untouched)

- The dark-conduit pillar with bold flat cyan rungs + magenta rails.
- The beefed prong cap + larger caught orb.
- Head geometry, glitch-doubled cyan ghost-horn (now reads), bolder grin.
