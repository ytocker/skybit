# IMPLET (B8 "pocket gremlin imp") — Round 2 critique

VERDICT: SHIP-READY

Both R1 blockers are cleared, and nothing new broke in the fix. The 1x
silhouette now reads "tiny green winged imp" — body-dominant, sky cutting
between wing and torso — and the comedy-of-scale gag is in the POSE, not the
caption. The two-lobe plum membrane survives night, the prop reads as flame at
the cap, and the distinctness firewall against shipped Grim Sprout (and the
warm-vs-green-flame separation from Pyrecrown) is fully intact. This is a
ship-ready lead; the notes below are micro-polish for the optional final pass,
not gating.

## Strongest / weakest

- **Strongest:** the 1x read FIX is decisive. In the (c) DAY/NIGHT/B-W insets
  the acid-green body is now the dominant value and the wings are a clean
  two-lobe shape with real sky negative-space between membrane and torso — no
  more "dark wedge with a green dot." The body holds on luminance alone in the
  B-W panel, eyes carrying the focal read. This was the blocker; it's gone.
- **Weakest (cosmetic only):** the straining pose is *almost* perfect but the
  spear's drag-line could be sold a hair harder — the arms-overhead grip is
  great, the body counter-lean is great, but the shaft still hangs close to
  vertical in the showcase so the "weight is dragging me sideways" vector is
  softer than it could be. Not a blocker; a finishing touch.

## Per-lens KEEP / FIX

### 1. Readability & silhouette (FIXED — the R1 blocker)
- KEEP: the bold two-lobe membrane, the higher wing hinge, and the ~18% reach
  pull-in. The sky now cuts cleanly between wing and body at 1x, so green is the
  dominant mass and the figure reads as an imp WITH wings, not a winged blob.
  Exactly the anchor R1 asked for — shape, not rib detail.
- KEEP: huge orange-on-green eyes still carry the focal read at the smallest
  size. That instinct from R1 is preserved.

### 2. Appeal & charm (FIXED)
- KEEP: the gag now lives in the picture — both stub arms thrown overhead to a
  high grip, head counter-lean, knee-buckled brace, spear tilted so its weight
  drags. "Can barely lift its own spear" finally reads without the caption.
- KEEP: face is untouched and still pure gremlin glee — fang, tongue-tip,
  asymmetric eyes. Don't lose any of it.

### 3. Color (FIXED for night)
- KEEP: the desaturated plum membrane (86,62,96) — it's now a HUE, not a value
  hole. On the NIGHT inset the wing edge stays crisp and the lower figure no
  longer dissolves into the dark sky. Body triad still harmonious on both skies.

### 4. Identity & consistency (PASS)
- House-faithful: chibi pear, flat fills, grown ink keyline, triad, scary-cute
  goblin-mischief read. Sits naturally beside Grim Sprout and the parrot.

### 5. Distinctiveness (CRITICAL — PASS, re-verified)
- Checked directly against shipped Grim Sprout R3: that is a violet HOODED blob
  with a curved teal-edged SCYTHE; Implet is an acid-green PEAR with spread
  BAT-WINGS and a warm fire-SPEAR. No hood, no skull, no scythe — fully clear.
  Warm orange flame stays separate from Pyrecrown's green soul-fire. No
  collision with the other 9.

### 6. Feasibility (PASS)
- Fully procedural, real game helpers, no sprite-sheet thinking.

### 7. Accessibility (FIXED)
- The B-W inset now reads as an imp on luminance alone — body is the dominant
  mass, eyes register. No longer reliant on the wing-black for silhouette.

### 8. Polish (PASS)
- KEEP: flame prongs reshaped to teardrops with a pale core — the cap and prop
  now read FLAME, not iron/bone fork, at both showcase and pillar cap. Tail
  reduced to one bold S-stroke + spade — the 1x bead-noise is gone.
