# CLOVENPATE — round 2 critique (art-director)

VERDICT: SHIP-READY

Round 2 lands the gag that round 1 missed. The figure now reads, instantly and
at gameplay scale, as a wee bone-imp teetering on ABSURD oversized cloven devil
hooves — not "a skeleton in red pants." This is the concept's whole reason for
beating A1 in the cull, and it is now the silhouette payoff. All six ranked FIX
items from the r1 critique are resolved, the protected wins (skull, sulphur eye,
stitched grin, femur-shaft pillar) are intact, and the distinctness guardrails
hold. This concept is locked.

## Strongest aspect
The hoof gag + grayscale test. In the blackout inset (and on my own desaturation
of the boss) the two widest, darkest masses at the floor are unmistakably the
hooves — splayed black cloven wedges with a hard center ink cleft, flaring wider
than any leg segment above them, capped by a bone-white crown rim so they read as
HOOVES, not feet. That was the r1 headline directive and it is fully delivered.

## Weakest aspect
The lifted-leg maroon facets and the down-hook tail are the least load-bearing
elements — fine as shipped, just the place I'd spend any remaining polish budget
(see optional notes). Nothing here blocks ship.

## KEEP (protect through the final pass)
- HOOVES as the boldest lower-half shape: width ~lw*1.9, deep central cleft,
  bone-rim base, brass ankle-ring. Do not shrink them. This is the concept.
- Reverse-knee GOAT legs: fat thigh kicks out to a digitigrade knee notch, slim
  cannon kicks in to the 3-lobe fetlock tuft, then hoof. Now reads beast-leg.
- Hoof/leg VALUE separation: bone-white hoof crown rim + brass ring break the
  black hoof off the maroon leg. Verified popping on BOTH day and night insets —
  no maroon/sky merge, no night blackout. The r1 mud-at-1x problem is gone.
- Skull dome + squared chin + sulphur eye-dots + heart-nose + stitched grin:
  still small, still scary-CUTE, still the charm anchor up top.
- Femur-shaft 3-tine PILLAR (cell B): clean, tileable, top<->bottom mirror,
  condyle banding, distinct from Big Reapy's 2-prong. Unchanged and still
  ship-quality — do not touch the cap geometry.
- Bottom-heavy chibi proportion, flat fills + hard ink keylines, bone/oxblood/
  brass/sulphur triad. House style honoured; no gradient streaks.
- No second curved-horn pair; brow-nub tiny. Distinct from the other 9 + roster.

## Optional polish (final GD pass — none are blockers)
1. Lifted-leg facet sheen: the flat maroon facets are correct grammar, but the
   single brightest facet on the raised thigh sits a touch hot — knock it down a
   half-step so it doesn't pull focus from the hooves at 1x.
2. Tail down-hook: reads cleaner than r1's stray-limb spade, but at true 1x it can
   still flirt with "third leg" against a dark hip. If trivially cheap, nudge its
   curl tighter to the hip so it never separates; otherwise leave it.
3. Balance-arms: low-held now, no longer crowning the skull as antennae — good.
   No change needed.

These are taste-level only. If the final pass changes nothing, this ships as is.

## Distinctness / identity check (passing)
Bottom-heavy + giant cloven hooves + no horn pair + bone spade-hook tail is a
clean inversion of Big Reapy and unmistakable against the other nine and the
existing roster. Palette stays clear of Brimstone basalt and Reapy ash-blue.
Shareable silhouette achieved.

## Top line
SHIP-READY. The gag lands, the grayscale + day/night tests pass, the prop/pillar
is locked, and the house style + guardrails are respected. Proceed to mature/
integrate; the optional notes above are the only discretionary polish left.
