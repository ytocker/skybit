# CLOVENPATE — round 1 critique (art-director)

VERDICT: ITERATE

Round 1 nails the SKULL and the PILLAR, but misses the concept's headline gag.
The brief is "absurd oversized cloven HOOVES" — the hooves are the silhouette
payoff and the whole reason this concept beat A1 in the cull. As built, the
bottom-heavy mass lives in two smooth maroon TUBES that read as red trousers/
pyjama pants, and the actual hooves are small muddy brown blobs at the floor.
The figure currently reads "skeleton in red pants," not "bone-imp teetering on
giant devil hooves." That is a concept miss, not a polish miss — fixable in one
round, hence ITERATE not RE-ROLL. The skull, the sulphur-eye charm, the stitched
grin, and the femur-shaft pillar are genuinely good and should be protected.

## Strongest aspect
The bone PITCHFORK -> PILLAR (cell B). The femur-condyle banding gives the shaft
a real bone identity, the 3-tine cap is unmistakable and clearly distinct from
Big Reapy's 2-prong, and the top<->bottom mirror is clean. This is ship-quality.

## Weakest aspect
The legs/hooves. The "oversized cloven hoof" gag — the concept's entire reason to
exist — does not land. The maroon columns read as clothing, not goat-leg; the
hooves are undersized and value-muddy (black hoof on oxblood leg goes to brown
mush at 1x). The bottom-heaviness is currently carried by trouser-mass, which any
skeleton-in-pants would have, so the DEVIL read and the DISTINCTNESS hook are
both soft.

## KEEP
- Skull: dome + squared chin reads "skull" small; sulphur eye-dots are charming
  and the night-glow keeps them lit. Heart-nose + stitched grin = scary-CUTE, on
  brief. Keep all of this.
- Femur shaft + 3-tine fork (the whole prop/pillar). Don't touch the cap geometry.
- 3-bold-rib ribcage — survives 1x, doesn't turn to noise. Guardrail honoured.
- Bottom-heavy proportion target (legs ~55%) is the right call; the EXECUTION of
  what fills that 55% is the problem, not the ratio.
- No second horn pair; brow-nub stays tiny. Distinctness guardrails respected.

## FIX (ranked)
1. THE HOOVES MUST BECOME THE GAG. Right now they're ~1.35*lw and read as feet.
   Make them genuinely ABSURD — at least as wide as the thigh is long, splayed
   out past the leg silhouette so the BOTTOM corners of the blackout are the
   hooves, not the legs. The black cloven wedge + center cleft should be the
   single boldest shape in the lower half. Test: in the grayscale blackout, the
   two darkest, widest masses at the floor should obviously be hooves.
2. KILL THE "TROUSERS" READ. The legs are smooth featureless maroon tubes. Give
   them goat-leg ARTICULATION: a clear thigh -> reverse (backward) digitigrade
   knee -> narrow cannon -> hoof, so the silhouette says "beast leg," not "pant
   leg." The current knock-knee bow is too subtle to register at 1x. Bend it
   harder and make the reverse-knee joint a real silhouette notch.
3. HOOF VALUE SEPARATION. Black hoof on oxblood leg muddies to brown at 1x and
   vanishes at night (cell C, night). The brass ankle-ring is the right idea but
   too thin to do the job — widen it into a real value break, OR lighten the leg
   maroon a step and/or add a hard bone-white hoof rim so the black hoof always
   pops off the leg. Verify on the NIGHT sky specifically.
4. FETLOCK TUFT ISN'T READING. The 5-lobe ruff above the hoof currently blends
   into the leg as a slight bulge. Either commit to it as a bold, hard-lobed
   ragged ring that clearly says "goat-leg fur" (a recognised devil tell), or cut
   it and put that silhouette budget into the hooves. Half-measure = noise.
5. TAIL + ARMS at 1x. In cell C the spaded bone tail reads as a stray third limb
   and the balance-arm knob-hands read as pins/antennae sticking off the skull.
   Either thicken/curl the tail so it clearly belongs to a hip, or drop it from
   the 1x silhouette; pull the arms in tighter so they don't crown the head.
6. LEG SHEEN GRAMMAR. The diagonal sheen streaks down the thighs read as soft
   airbrushed lines, which drifts toward the gradient look the house style bans.
   Make the rim-sheen a HARD-edged flat plane on the lit (top-left) side of each
   leg segment, consistent with the triad on the skull and pillar.

## Distinctness / identity check (passing — keep it that way)
- Bottom-heavy + no horn pair + bone spade tail: compliant, the inversion of Big
  Reapy is conceptually clear. The risk is purely EXECUTION: once the hooves
  dominate, the silhouette becomes un-shareable. Don't let fix #2's articulation
  sneak in anything that reads as a second curved-horn pair anywhere.
- Palette (bone + oxblood + brass + sulphur) is distinct from Brimstone basalt
  and Reapy ash-blue. Good — just resolve the leg/hoof value clash within it.

## Top 3 directives (the next-round headline)
1. Blow up the cloven HOOVES so they are the widest, boldest, darkest masses in
   the figure — the silhouette payoff, not just feet.
2. Re-cut the legs as articulated reverse-knee GOAT legs so they read beast-leg,
   never red trousers.
3. Force hard hoof/leg value separation (rim + wider brass break) so the black
   hooves pop on BOTH day and night — verify in the night inset.
