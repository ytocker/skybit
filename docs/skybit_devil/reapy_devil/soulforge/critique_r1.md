# SOULFORGE (A8) — round-1 critique

VERDICT: ITERATE

A strong, characterful first round that nails the workman thesis and the
prop->pillar mirror. It is held back by ONE structural problem (the giant open
ember socket has hijacked the face and is bleeding the forge-orange past the
"contained accent" guardrail) plus a few 1x legibility losses. None is fatal;
this is a fix-the-face-and-tighten-the-glow round, not a re-roll.

---

## Strongest / weakest aspects

- **Strongest:** the SILHOUETTE GAG and the PILLAR. The stocky apron-block body
  with ONE absurd stone mitt clamped on the haft is instantly legible, genuinely
  scary-cute, and shares zero silhouette DNA with the other 9 or the roster. The
  sledgehammer->pillar mirror in (b) is the cleanest in the set so far: the
  riveted grip-bands survive the 82px downscale, the anvil-head cap reads as a
  cap, and the hot strike-face firing sparks INTO the gap is a real "juice" beat
  that doubles as the gameplay gap-edge tell. Ship that pillar nearly as-is.
- **Weakest:** the FACE READ and the GLOW BUDGET. The open right eye is a huge
  filled forge-orange disc with a white-hot core and a wide additive halo. At
  showcase it reads as a cyclops target-reticle, not a skull socket; the squint
  eye on the left is so much smaller/darker that the face loses bilateral skull
  symmetry and the "two coal sockets, one squinting" intent doesn't land — it
  reads "one big glowing eye + a scratch." That single disc is also the largest
  warm area on the figure, which is exactly what the BRIMSTONE-separation
  guardrail says to avoid: contained-accent should mean coals + sparks + seam,
  not a headlight.

---

## Per-lens KEEP / FIX

**1. Readability & silhouette**
- KEEP: block-body + giant-fist + raised hammer is a top-tier 1x silhouette; the
  grayscale panel confirms the read survives without ember. Good.
- FIX: the FILED HORN-STUMPS are the named identity primitive and they nearly
  vanish at 1x (day and night). They sit too low/small and the bone-pale fill is
  close in value to the cool sheen on the cranium, so the flat sawn-off top
  doesn't register. They are the thing that says "devil" — they must read at 1x.
- FIX: the four-tooth band + tongue-tip + nose-triangle is a lot of small
  high-frequency mouth detail that muddies into a single dark smear at 1x. Drop
  to a bolder, lower-count read.

**2. Appeal & charm**
- KEEP: the concentration beat (squint + tongue-tip, lining up the swing) is a
  lovely idea and very on-brand scary-cute.
- FIX: it doesn't survive scale — at 1x the squint just looks like the figure is
  missing an eye. Make the asymmetry legible as "scrunched aiming eye" vs "wide
  open eye," not "big eye vs no eye."

**3. Colour**
- KEEP: the soot-on-cool-iron value structure is correct and distinctive; the
  ox-blood apron is a good warm anchor that is NOT orange (smart — it carries
  warmth without spending the forge budget).
- FIX: the single big ember socket is over-saturated and over-sized. Forge-orange
  must be the smallest-area warm on the figure, not the largest. See directives.

**4. Identity & consistency**
- KEEP: flat fills + ink keyline + triad are honoured; sits beside the reaper
  family naturally.
- FIX: the brass buckle + brass collar trim introduces a SECOND warm-metal hue
  (brass-gold) competing with forge-orange. On a soot/iron figure that is one
  warm family too many; consider muting the buckle toward dark iron so orange
  owns "hot."

**5. Distinctness (CRITICAL — Brimstone separation)**
- KEEP: the construction is unmistakably different from a faceted magma boulder —
  stocky body, apron, fist, hammer. Good.
- FIX: the GLOW AREA is the collision risk, not the construction. Right now the
  big socket disc + its halo + the hammer strike-face glow + the spark burst add
  up to a lot of orange real estate clustered at the head/hammer — at a thumbnail
  glance it can read "hot-glowing skull," which is Brimstone's lane. Cut total
  ember area meaningfully and keep it as DISCRETE points (coals/sparks/seam),
  never a fill or a wide halo.

**6. Feasibility** — all procedural, no concerns. Good.

**7. Accessibility**
- KEEP: grayscale panel proves the read doesn't depend on the ember hue — exactly
  the right check to have included.
- FIX: ensure the squint-vs-open asymmetry survives in grayscale too (right now
  it's mostly carried by the orange). The socket SHAPE must do the work.

**8. Polish**
- FIX: the squint eyebrow arc, the open-eye bowed brow, and the cheekbone hollows
  are competing dark marks crowding the upper face; simplify so the two sockets
  + two stumps are the only events up there. The apron scuff/fold grooves are
  invisible at 1x — fine to keep for showcase, but don't add more.

---

## Iteration directives (prioritized punch list)

1. **Tame the open socket (highest priority, fixes face AND Brimstone risk).**
   Shrink the open right eye to roughly socket-sized parity with the squint,
   make it a DARK ink cavity with a small contained ember coal + a tiny white-hot
   pinprick (point, not disc). Cut the additive halo radius by ~half day / keep a
   modest push only at night. The dark socket shape, not the glow, carries the
   eye. Net: forge-orange becomes the smallest warm area on the figure.
2. **Land the filed stumps at 1x.** Raise them ~15-20% larger and seat them a
   touch higher/prouder on the brow; push the flat sawn-off top a clear value
   step lighter than the cranium sheen so the cut-bone ellipse reads as a flat
   top (the "filed, not pointed" tell). They are the devil signature — verify in
   the 1x day inset, not just showcase.
3. **Make the squint read as a scrunched eye, not a missing one.** Give it a
   clear lid-line/lash crease and a small coal glint so at 1x it reads "winking
   to aim," matched in size to the open socket so the skull keeps bilateral
   symmetry. Confirm in grayscale.
4. **Simplify the mouth.** Drop to 3 bold teeth (or a single bowed tooth band
   with 2 dark separators) and enlarge them slightly so the grin survives 1x;
   keep the tongue-tip but make it a hair bigger and higher-contrast so it reads
   as a tongue, not a stray red pixel.
5. **Resolve the second warm hue.** Mute the brass buckle/collar toward dark
   iron (or a single small brass dot) so forge-orange is the only "hot" on the
   figure — protects both the focal hierarchy and the Brimstone separation.
6. **Trim the head clutter.** Remove or soften the cheekbone hollows and one of
   the brow arcs so the upper face has exactly four events: two stumps, two
   sockets. Let the silhouette breathe.
7. **Pillar: keep, with one tweak.** The pillar pair is essentially ship-ready.
   Only nudge: the strike-face glow on the cap is the one place ember is welcome
   at size (it's the gap-edge tell) — but make sure it doesn't grow now that
   you're cutting the face glow. Keep cap glow and face glow in balance so the
   eye still goes to the gap edge in-game.
