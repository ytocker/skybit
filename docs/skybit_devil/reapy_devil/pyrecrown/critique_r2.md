Every round-1 failure is fixed, and the concept now delivers on its full thesis.

## RANKING — strongest to weakest aspect

1. **STRONGEST — the candle crown read.** Round 1's mohawk is gone. Five distinct
   near-cylindrical wax columns, bold height variation (centre tallest), fat
   overhanging melted-lip collars + visible drips, and clear sky between tapers.
   At 1x in cell (c), day AND night, you count five candles. This is now the
   signature silhouette. Fully solved.
2. **The prop->pillar payoff on NIGHT.** The CAP-band zoom proves the headline:
   top cap flame points down, bottom cap flame points up, both green flames
   rim-light the gap edges against the dark sky, skull-knobs visible. The
   "flame LIGHTS the gap, not Hollow's snuffer" idea finally reads in-frame, and
   the wax-drip banding tiles cleanly on the shaft. Highest-value fix, landed.
3. **The serene skull + grayscale read.** Closed-eye crescents, heart-nose, quiet
   tooth band, calm brow — the scary-cute beat holds, and the grayscale cell
   proves face + black tapers carry the silhouette with zero green.
4. **Color / night pop + distinctness.** Bone / wax-black / green triad is
   harmonious on both skies; green lifts on night without neon. Lane is clean
   vs the set and roster. No notes.
5. **WEAKEST — the bone bib (formerly praying hands).** Cleaner than r1's mush,
   but it now reads as a mitre/shield plaque, not praying hands. Minor; see FIX.

## FIX (optional polish — none are gates)

1. **(MINOR) The bone bib doesn't read as praying hands.** At showcase scale the
   pentagon plaque reads as a pope's mitre / shield; the "praying" tell is lost.
   If a final pass has budget, add a faint centre seam + a hint of two fingertip
   peaks at the top so it suggests a clasped steeple — or lean fully into the
   bone-bib read and drop the "praying" language from the thesis line. As-is it
   is acceptable and does not block ship.
2. **(NIT) Showcase flame add-glow slightly hot on the centre candle.** The
   tallest centre flame's halo is the one place the contained-glow rule is
   stretched closest to a wash. Hold it at or just below the side flames' halo
   radius so the crown stays five contained teardrops, not a central bloom.
3. **(NIT) The lower (bottom-pillar) cap flame in the 1x native strip is read as
   a wide heart/diamond** vs the crisper teardrop on the top cap. Match the
   bottom-cap flame shape to the top-cap teardrop for consistency at true scale.
