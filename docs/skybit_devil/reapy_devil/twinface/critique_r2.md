# A7 TWINFACE — round 2 critique (art-director, final critique of loop)

VERDICT: SHIP-READY

Round 2 lands every directive from r1. The concept that got TWINFACE locked —
a clean asymmetric two-mood split that survives 1x — is now actually on screen.
The two failures that held r1 back (the split dying at gameplay scale, and
several elements rendering as the *opposite* of spec) are both resolved. This
is shippable; the notes below are polish for the final GD pass, not blockers.

## What landed (KEEP — do not regress)

1. **Devil eye is now the wink.** The loud yellow disc is gone; the bold INK
   closed-arc reads as a sly cocked wink against the calm hollow bone socket.
   The "two halves disagree" gag now works at a glance — this was the single
   most important fix and it is clean.
2. **Red-half value structure.** Deepened DEVIL_SH + inked brow/smirk/fang/
   goatee give the devil side crisp dark marks instead of a smooth lump. The
   pink sheen shrunk to a tick no longer reads as a wound. The red half finally
   matches the bone half's internal contrast.
3. **The horn reads as a horn.** The solid dark up-and-back tapering wedge with
   the ink back-keyline registers as a horn in the blackout silhouette and stays
   in frame. The lopsided one-horn gag — the locked distinctness hook — survives.
4. **The gold seam is now the gag.** Widened ~2x with dark valley + bright ridge
   + catch-light, and the devil sheen pulled off it. At 1x the head reads as two
   halves cut by a gold line — exactly the target.
5. **Finials fixed.** Skull end = one clean reaper crescent on a gold collar (no
   more snowman/second-face read); fork end = a bold, obvious two-prong spade.
   Each reads as its own shape and neither rivals the boss head.
6. **1x legibility passes, day AND night.** Split, seam, and dark horn all
   survive in both insets. The night red half no longer sinks into the sky.
7. **Distinctness + house style hold.** One horn only, no ram pair, no drift
   toward A1's spiral; chibi flat-fill + ink-keyline triad intact; scary-cute,
   not grim.

## Polish for the final GD pass (nice-to-have, NOT ship-blockers)

1. **Night horn tip.** The dark wedge against the dark night sky loses ~the
   last few px of its tip. A 1px bone/gold rim-keyline along the horn's leading
   edge (lit side only) would keep the point reading on night without touching
   the day read or the silhouette.
2. **Finial mass separation at smallest scale.** The crescent and the spade are
   close in overall blob mass at the tiniest inset. Consider nudging the spade
   prongs slightly longer/narrower (or the crescle slightly more open) so the
   two gap-caps stay tellable apart when both appear in one column. Minor.
3. **Devil-side fang.** Reads well at showcase; at 1x it nearly merges with the
   smirk line. If trivial, give it one extra px of bone value so it survives —
   otherwise leave it, the wink + seam already carry the devil read at 1x.

No re-shoot required. The concept clears the bar — proceed to mature/integrate.
