# FAR-EAST-LANDMARKS — Phase A brainstorm critique (cull 8 → 6)

Locked set of six directions, distinctness gate cleared, per-survivor round-1 FIX
folded in. Proceed to mature each on its own loop. This is a critique only — no
render exists yet, so the notes below judge the IDEAS and set them up to land tiny.

The starting 8 are a genuinely strong, well-researched spread — the GD did the
distinctness homework up front (blackout/swap/nearest-neighbour pins are real, not
decoration). The job here is not to rescue a weak set; it is to shed the two that
either fight the fill gate or read generic small, and to protect the modern/historic
+ East/SE-Asia balance while doing it.

---

## RANKING (all 8, best → cut)

1. **`oriental_pearl`** — circles-on-a-stick is the most unmistakable blackout in the
   whole row; nothing else in the game (or the 25 pagodas) is discrete beads on a
   mast. Fills clean, modern/East anchor. Lock.
2. **`marina_bay_boat`** — the only top-heavy horizontal-cap-on-legs pole; the "ship
   on stilts" tell survives at 58px even as a blob. Feasibility is work (two leg
   gaps, wide crown) but the fix is clean and the silhouette payoff is the highest
   in the set. Lock.
3. **`potala_fortress`** — owns the widest/flat-topped solid-massif pole nothing else
   occupies; the red-core-in-white-wings + gold roofs is a colour+construction tell,
   not just a trapezoid. Trivial fill. Lock (with a tell-sharpening FIX so it isn't
   "just a trapezoid" small).
4. **`petronas_twins`** — the only DOUBLED silhouette; twin-towers is one of the most
   globally legible shapes there is. Killzone slot is the one real risk but the dim
   recessed-backing fix is cleaner than Canton's waist pinch. Lock.
5. **`himeji_heron`** — jagged fanned-gable white pyramid; strong historic/East pole.
   Nearest-neighbour risk to both `angkor` (tapering historic mass) and the existing
   25 pagodas — needs the wide battered castle base + gable fan pushed hard to
   separate. Lock with that FIX.
6. **`angkor_lotus`** — the only scalloped/redented curvy-cone; adds the SE-Asia
   historic slot that keeps the set from skewing all-modern-glass. Borderline: the
   redenting must land as a scalloped edge tiny or it slumps into "generic pointed
   spire / pagoda cone." Lock with a silhouette FIX; it earns its place on balance,
   not on ease.
7. **`canton_twist`** — CUT. Best-looking on paper, weakest small: a see-through
   twisting mesh loses both its tells (the 45° twist AND the waist) at 58px and reads
   as a tapered tube — the "generic tower" the brief told me to cut. And it is the
   single worst feasibility case: a pinched waist is the one construction that
   actively wants to open a sub-collision-width band / hollow column. Double strike.
8. **`merlion`** — CUT. Fine wild-card in isolation, but the game already ships a
   wall of organic creature-statue pillars (`totems`: moai/jade_serpent/kota;
   `japanese_totems`: daruma/tengu/oni/kappa/kitsune). Another creature adds the
   least NEW silhouette space to the overall roster even though it's this set's
   outlier. Plus it double-books Singapore with `marina_bay_boat`, and its asymmetric
   head-heavy figure + spout overhang fights the "solid centred core, mirror = clean
   vertical flip" contract harder than any architectural pick.

## THE SIX TO PURSUE — silhouette poles + why the sextet is maximally distinct

| slug | silhouette pole (blackout) | era / region |
|---|---|---|
| `oriental_pearl` | beaded spire — discrete circles on a thin mast, tripod splay at base | modern / East (Shanghai) |
| `petronas_twins` | doubled — two shafts + mid skybridge, thin vertical slot, paired needles | modern / SE (Kuala Lumpur) |
| `marina_bay_boat` | top-heavy — one long horizontal boat cap on three splayed legs | modern / SE (Singapore) |
| `himeji_heron` | jagged tiered pyramid — stacked flared eaves/gables, wide battered base | historic / East (Japan) |
| `potala_fortress` | broad flat-topped massif — inward-battered trapezoid, red core in white wings | historic / East (Tibet/China) |
| `angkor_lotus` | scalloped curvy cone — redented ogival lotus bud on a stepped base | historic / SE (Cambodia) |

**Why the sextet holds under the gate:**
- **Blackout:** six mutually unmistakable poles — beads-on-stick / twin-slot /
  horizontal-cap / jagged-pyramid / flat-trapezoid / scalloped-cone. No two share a
  base-to-top edge KIND. The only cluster worth policing is the three historic masses
  (himeji/potala/angkor), and their edges differ in kind — jagged-stepped vs
  straight-slanted-flat-top vs scalloped-curved — which is exactly the separation the
  GD's nearest-neighbour pin already names. Keep it.
- **Balance:** cutting Canton + Merlion lands a clean **3 modern / 3 historic** and
  **3 East / 3 SE-Asia** — no skew to modern-glass, no double-Singapore, and the only
  repeated country (China: Shanghai-modern vs Tibet-historic) sits in opposite era AND
  region cells. This is the single biggest reason the cull is these two and not, say,
  Petronas or Angkor (cutting either historic would tip the set modern-heavy).
- **Cover-the-label:** "the pearl one / the twin towers / the boat one / the white
  castle / the red palace / the lotus temple." Six distinct nicknames, none colliding
  with an existing pillar family.
- **Cross-family:** none duplicates a pagoda, a windmill, a totem, or a Japanese
  yokai; `himeji` and `angkor` are the only pagoda-adjacency risks and both carry a
  FIX below to open the gap.

## KEEP + FIX per survivor (round-1 brief)

**`oriental_pearl` — KEEP:** the bead-string is the strongest blackout; don't dilute
it. **FIX:** (1) commit to exactly TWO large spheres + one small capsule bead — a
third mid sphere will merge into a lumpy sausage at 58px; the gap of bare mast
between the two big spheres IS the tell, protect it. (2) Render the tripod legs as
lit relief struts over a dim recessed core, never as an open triangle — the base
band must stay ≥ collision width. (3) Keep the rose-glass sphere hue distinct from
Petronas's cool steel so the two China/modern-ish spikes never trade at a glance.

**`petronas_twins` — KEEP:** the doubled silhouette; it's the set's rarest shape.
**FIX:** (1) the slot between the shafts is the whole risk — make it a NARROW dark
recess (shadowed curtain-wall backing at collision width), never a see-through gap;
if the slot ever reads as a hole the pillar is a killzone. (2) At 58px two full
shafts is ~2×20px + slot — verify the twin read survives; if it muddies, widen the
shafts and thin the slot rather than lose the pair. (3) The paired needle pinnacles
are the top tell — keep two distinct spikes, not a merged crown.

**`marina_bay_boat` — KEEP:** the horizontal boat cap is the highest-value tell in
the set and lands even as a silhouette blob. **FIX:** (1) solve the two inter-leg
gaps first — centre leg solid + dim recessed atrium backing across the full width;
prove the fill gate before any deck detail. (2) The boat crown is gutter-overhang
only; keep it emphatically HORIZONTAL and long (cantilever past the end leg) so it
never reads as a fat tower cap. (3) Skip the palm nubs / pool-lip filigree — noise
at 1×; the ship-on-stilts silhouette carries it.

**`himeji_heron` — KEEP:** jagged fanned-gable pyramid; a lovely historic pole.
**FIX:** (1) this is the top pagoda-adjacency risk — separate it by making the base
emphatically a WIDE battered stone keep block, not a slim tiered stack; a castle
sits on a broad masonry foot, a pagoda doesn't. (2) Cap the tiers at ~4–5 with
corner eaves that flare UP (the heron tell); more tiers = pagoda mush. (3) Gold
shachihoko finials read as two small bright nicks on the top ridge — keep them, they
say "castle keep," but keep them tiny.

**`potala_fortress` — KEEP:** owns the broad flat-topped massif pole; trivial fill.
**FIX:** (1) the brief's generic-trapezoid warning applies here — the tell that saves
it is the deep-RED central block set into WHITE battered wings; make that two-tone
split bold and high-contrast so it reads at 58px, not a subtle stain. (2) Cluster the
gold flat pavilion roofs as a distinct bright crown so the flat top isn't a bare
edge. (3) Ensure the two-tone still separates under night biome retint (don't let the
red and white converge in value at night — accessibility/value check).

**`angkor_lotus` — KEEP:** the only scalloped curvy-cone; holds the SE-historic slot.
**FIX:** (1) borderline pick — the redenting MUST read as a scalloped/lobed OUTLINE,
not just surface ribs, or it collapses into a plain pointed spire; exaggerate the
lobes on the silhouette edge. (2) Ground it on a broad, unmistakably STEPPED square
base with the steep central stair line — that base is what separates it from a
pagoda cone AND from himeji's tiers. (3) Warm grey-gold weathered sandstone + moss in
the recesses; keep it clearly stone, not the whites of himeji/potala.

## THE TWO CULLS (why they go)

- **`canton_twist`** — the one to read "generic tower" small (twist + waist both need
  fine detail that dies at 58px → tapered tube) AND the worst fill-gate fight (a
  pinched waist is the exact construction that opens a sub-collision band). Cutting it
  costs nothing the set needs: Petronas already carries modern/SE, Oriental Pearl
  already carries the modern China spike, and both are stronger tiny. This is the
  clean "cut the generic-tower risk" the brief asked for.
- **`merlion`** — redundant against the game's existing creature-statue families
  (totems + japanese_totems already ship ~8 organic figures), double-books Singapore
  with `marina_bay_boat`, and its asymmetric top-heavy figure + spout overhang fights
  the centred-core / clean-vertical-mirror contract more than any architectural pick.
  Its outlier value inside THIS 8 is real, but it adds the least new silhouette space
  to the FULL pillar roster — and dropping it is what delivers the perfect 3/3, 3/3
  balance.
