# Japanese-totem family — brainstorm critique (AD, Phase A)

The seven are strong, genuinely Japanese, and palette-clean. The brief was to
cull to a distinct, feasible, authentic **5** — and this set gives me a clean
one without a re-roll. I'm locking **4 masks + 1 non-mask**, each on its own
silhouette pole, all feasible against the 58px band + flip harness. No gap, no
promotion needed. Proceed to mature the five per-concept.

## The distinctness read (family caveat first)

This whole family is deliberately re-skinned off ONE skeleton
(`moai_ancestor`'s stacked-head driver). So **construction is the family's
held-constant axis by design** — the four masks all share "few big carved
relief heads." That means the `distinct-design-variants` gate can NOT ride on
construction here; it has to ride on **silhouette + shape-language + concept +
material**, and that's exactly where I'm policing. Daruma is the one genuine
construction/shape-language outlier (round painted doll, no relief carving),
which is why it earns the non-mask slot. `yokai_hyakki_pole` was the only OTHER
construction variant (many-small) — but it fights the very skeleton it re-skins,
and its feasibility caveats sink it (below).

## Ranking — all 7

1. **`oni_kanabo`** — KEEP. The anchor. Boldest blackout, most instantly-"Japanese
   demon," full-width brute jaw, owns the horned pole outright.
2. **`kitsune_inari`** — KEEP. Crisp tall-ear silhouette, the only cool-white face
   — carries huge value/hue spread against the reds.
3. **`kappa_suijin`** — KEEP. The inward concave dish is the single most unique
   topper in the set; the only cool-green face. A true silhouette + color outlier.
4. **`tengu_yamabushi`** — KEEP. The forward nose spur is a top-tier unmistakable
   Japanese read, unlike anything else in the set.
5. **`daruma_gankake`** — KEEP. The round bulbous counterpoint to four angular
   masks; cleanest flip; eyeless gold-rim face reads instantly different.
6. **`hannya_grudge`** — CULL. Beautiful concept, but it's a SECOND horned mask on
   Oni's pole, and its bone-flesh lands it in the pale-trio with Kitsune. Costs a
   pole slot for spread it doesn't add.
7. **`yokai_hyakki_pole`** — CULL. Most distinct construction, but fights the
   fixed skeleton and is the least feasible in the set (its own flagged caveats).

Bench: **`menpo_bushi`** stays the named spare — but I do NOT promote it. An iron
war-mask is a broad faceted face closest to Oni's brute read; it adds a new
material but no new SILHOUETTE pole. Hold it only if the user later wants a 6th
mask or a swap.

## The 5 to pursue — one pole each, why the quintet is maximally distinct

| Slug | Silhouette pole | Shape language | Base material (color pole) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `oni_kanabo` | BACKSWEPT-HORNS brute | heavy angular knuckled | bright vermilion + iron horns |
| `tengu_yamabushi` | LONG-NOSE spur | smooth post + forward spear | dark cool crimson/lacquer |
| `kitsune_inari` | UPRIGHT FOX-EARS | sleek pointed triangular | cool porcelain-WHITE + vermilion |
| `kappa_suijin` | BEAK + INWARD DISH | rounded domed + concave | moss-JADE green |
| `daruma_gankake` | ROUND-BEADED (non-mask) | pure round bulbous | glossy lacquer-red + gold face |

**Blackout:** five mutually unmistakable solids — rearward antler-V / skewering
nose spur / tall thin ear-pairs / domed head with a concave-dish top / column of
near-circles. No head drops legibly into another pole. PASS.
**Swap:** an oni head in a daruma column shatters instantly (horns vs round);
every swap is wrong. PASS.
**Cover-the-label:** each names itself — demon / mountain-goblin / fox-shrine /
water-imp / wish-doll. PASS.
**One-sentence:** five different yōkai/folk concepts, five different theses. PASS.

Color spread is deliberately engineered here: white (Kitsune) and green (Kappa)
are the palette-breakers, and — importantly — the SET is **silhouette-first
differentiated**, so the read never leans on hue. That's colorblind-safe by
construction: horns / nose / ears / dish / round carry identity, color is only
the second guard.

## KEEP + FIX per survivor (sharpen into round 1)

### `oni_kanabo` — the anchor
- KEEP: bold backswept-horn brute silhouette, bright vermilion, full-width jaw.
  The most legible blackout in the set — don't soften it.
- FIX: make horns **THICK, blunt, and wide-set** with an unmistakable rearward
  rake — thin horns turn to noise at 58px; the V-opening-rearward must survive as
  two dark nubs. Snarl + fangs collapse to a dark bar small — rely on the 2-dot
  eye fallback + a single mouth-notch, don't count on individual teeth. Own the
  **brightest, highest-sat** end of the three reds so Tengu and Daruma can't twin.
  Keep the studded kanabō cap a SOLID wide drum at the gap rim — studs must not
  fray the rim silhouette.

### `tengu_yamabushi` — long-nose
- KEEP: the single forward nose spur — a top-tier unmistakable Japanese read.
  tokin cap + maedate feather is an authentic, quiet topper.
- FIX: nose length IS the tell — push it clearly into the gutter but keep it
  ABOVE the neck-waist seam so it never pinches the collision column or opens a
  gap. Push the face to a **dark, cool crimson/oxblood, distinctly lower-value
  than Oni** — this is the primary red-twin guard. Keep the nose a straight
  horizontal SPUR (long, thin, pointing down), never a short wedge, or it flirts
  with Kappa's beak — LENGTH disambiguates. Feather is narrow gutter-overhang;
  the pillbox tokin is the solid rim presenter.

### `kitsune_inari` — fox-ears (white)
- KEEP: tall upright ears = crisp distinct silhouette; the sole cool-white face
  brings the biggest value/hue break in the set.
- FIX: ears must be **TALLER and THINNER than any horn, near-vertical** so the
  blackout never twins Oni. Watch the real risk: **porcelain-white on a bright
  DAY sky is low-contrast** — anchor the head with the vermilion liner/cheek-swirl
  markings + a darker jaw-shadow so it doesn't wash out at gameplay scale; verify
  value contrast against the LIGHTEST day sky. Keep the mouth simple; nose-dot +
  slant eyes carry the fox read small.

### `kappa_suijin` — beak + dish
- KEEP: the inward concave sara dish — the only inward-domed topper in the set,
  genuinely unique. Moss-jade is the sole cool-green face and a superb red-run
  breaker.
- FIX: the dish is the tell AND the feasibility risk — it must present a **SOLID
  wide rim at the gap line** (fill the bowl underside); prove no >12px empty run
  opens under it and the fill-gate passes. Keep the beak SHORT (forward wedge),
  never a spur — length is Tengu's. Pull the green firmly to amphibian moss-jade
  (toward stone_mid), away from porcelain tile-blue, so it never twins Kitsune or
  reads as sky. Dish water-glint = a single bright dot, not detail that noises up.

### `daruma_gankake` — round non-mask
- KEEP: the pure round bulbous column — maximal counterpoint to four angular
  masks; cleanest flip; eyeless gold-rim discs read instantly different.
- FIX: round-vs-gaunt is the guard against retired moai — keep the waist pinch
  SHALLOW, the body squat and round, and lead with the flat gold face + brows so
  it never reads as the carved moai profile. Make the gold eye-rims **bold enough
  to hold the blank discs at 58px** via the 2-dot fallback — eyeless is charming
  but the eyes must not vanish. Own a glossy lacquer-red distinct from Oni by
  leaning on the **cream face patch + gold belly medallion** so it reads
  "red+gold doll," not "another red demon." Shallow pinch + hidden full-width core
  so the round taper never opens a >12px seam run.

## The 2 culls

- **`hannya_grudge`** — the headline collision. Forward-vs-backswept horn
  direction is too subtle to survive the 58px blackout as a SECOND horned mask,
  and its bone-flesh face lands it in the pale-trio next to Kitsune. Its killer
  tell (gold serrated grin) degrades to a dark noise-bar at gameplay scale. Great
  mask, wrong economics — Oni already owns the horned pole. Cull for spread.
- **`yokai_hyakki_pole`** — the most distinct construction, but the family is
  built on ONE stacked-mask skeleton, so construction is the constant it can't
  vary without fighting the harness. Its own flagged caveats confirm it as least
  feasible: asymmetric body won't mirror on the flip, >12px neck-gap risk under
  small faces, collapses to 1–2 faces + lantern at 70px, and leans hardest on the
  2-dot fallback. Cull for feasibility — the round Daruma covers the non-mask slot
  far more safely.

**Replacement:** none needed. The five occupy five distinct poles with no gap;
`menpo_bushi` remains benched, not promoted (no new pole).

## One SET-level directive for all five round-1s
Three of the five carry red (Oni bright vermilion / Tengu dark crimson / Daruma
glossy lacquer). Silhouette already separates them hard, and players see one
landmark at a time — but enforce **three distinct value/temperature bands** for
the reds so a scroll-run of red pillars never feels monochrome, and lean
Kitsune-white and Kappa-green as the deliberate palette breakers. Silhouette
stays the primary identity channel (colorblind-safe); hue is the second guard
only.
