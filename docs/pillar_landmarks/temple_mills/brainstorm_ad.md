VERDICT: SHIP-READY

# TEMPLE-MILL family — Phase A brainstorm critique (art-director)

Five locked, two culled — no re-roll and no replacement needed. This is a strong,
disciplined set: the material contract is already at the pagoda/waterwheel fidelity
bar, and the cross-set Blackout table proves seven distinct crown KINDS. The cull is
NOT about craft. It is about the two non-negotiable gates in the brief — (a) **does
it read as a WIND-DRIVEN MILL at a glance** (something that spins/catches wind, not a
decorated tower), and (b) **is it MORE eye-catching at ~58px than the retired
windmills**. Exactly two directions fail one or both of those gates and nothing else
does, so they go, and the surviving quintet already spans the interesting space with
maximum silhouette + mechanism spread. Set locked; proceed to per-concept maturation.

One thing to name up front: **eye-catch is the user's explicit ask here**, so I have
weighted raw wow/read-at-58px above pure silhouette-novelty. That is why the unique
pendant-bell fringe (a genuinely distinct shape) still loses to five bolder,
more-obviously-spinning crowns.

## Ranking (all 7, best → worst as a DIRECTION)

1. **`vane-star-mill`** — the single strongest "this SPINS in the wind" read in the
   set: a face-on pinwheel is the most literal spin-icon there is, and rendering it
   as a saturated gilt rosette makes it the highest-value, most reward-screaming hit
   of the seven. Clean centred flip. Family anchor. **PURSUE.**
2. **`sail-fan-mill`** — the boldest, most saturated silhouette FOOTPRINT; the
   scalloped 180° sweep is unmistakable at 58px and it's plainly a sail array
   catching wind. Clean bilateral flip. **PURSUE.**
3. **`phoenix-vane-mill`** — the highest raw WOW/charm in the set: a single hero gilt
   creature that glows like a shrine icon at night, the only figural crown, the most
   premium silhouette. The flip is the price of admission, and it is payable (see
   FIX). Directly serves the "more eye-catching" mandate. **PURSUE.**
4. **`parasol-crown-mill`** — the boldest single convex shape; warm, regal,
   sheltering, and the cleanest flip of the seven. Holds the "one smooth dome" pole
   alone once the eave-stack is culled. **PURSUE.**
5. **`streamer-whirl-mill`** — the only soft / linear / actively-fluttering pole and
   the richest colour story; it buys the set its motion + `_vermilion` juice. Carries
   the set's biggest 58px thin-mass risk, so it's the weakest survivor — but the pole
   it holds is irreplaceable and worth policing. **PURSUE.**
6. **`roof-turbine-mill`** — architectural grandeur, but it fails BOTH gates: a stack
   of flared pagoda eaves reads as *architecture*, not a spinning mechanism (weakest
   "it turns" tell alongside the bells), AND it is the crown CLOSEST to the 11 shipped
   pagodas + Songyue towers — the least differentiated thing this family could add.
   Cover the label and it says "another pagoda finial." **CULL.**
7. **`furin-ring-mill`** — the pendant-teardrop fringe is a genuinely unique
   silhouette KIND, but hanging bells say *temple decoration / lantern fringe*, not a
   MILL: a bell hangs and chimes, it doesn't visibly catch wind and spin. This is the
   prayer-flag failure from the windmill cull repeating — soft decoration reads, not a
   mechanism. It is also the fussiest crown at 58px (a row of tiny cups sparkles into
   noise) and the WORST mirror in the set after the phoenix (bells invert to point
   up). Fails the eye-catch gate (fussy) and the mill gate (decoration). **CULL.**

## The 5 to pursue — one silhouette + mechanism pole each

| # | Slug | CROWN pole (blackout) | BODY pole (blackout) | Wind mechanism | Shape language |
|---|------|-----------------------|----------------------|----------------|----------------|
| 1 | `vane-star-mill` | pointed 360° RADIAL STAR rosette | stepped ziggurat | face-on pinwheel | rigid gilt radial, pointed |
| 2 | `sail-fan-mill` | filled 180° convex FAN ARC (flat base) | tapered cone | swept sail-leaf array | rigid canvas half-sweep |
| 3 | `phoenix-vane-mill` | a FIGURAL CREATURE (spread wings) | slender needle spire | weathervane that turns to wind | organic, figural |
| 4 | `parasol-crown-mill` | one smooth convex DOME + scalloped skirt | squat wide pedestal | rotating canopy | smooth 3-D convex canopy |
| 5 | `streamer-whirl-mill` | airy SPRAY of trailing TAILS | mini-pavilion box | whirling ribbon spray | soft, linear, fluttering |

**Why the quintet is maximally distinct AND maximally eye-catching:**
- **Blackout:** pointed-star / flat-fan-arc / creature / domed-skirt / airy-tails —
  five different solid-black shapes, no two share a mass KIND. PASS.
- **Swap:** each crown is welded to a body whose proportion sells it — the star needs
  the ziggurat's flat top step, the fan needs the cone's shoulder to spread from, the
  phoenix needs the slim spire to lead the eye up to the perched figure, the parasol
  needs the squat pedestal's inverse proportion to dominate, the ribbons need the
  pavilion mast. No crown transplants without breaking the read. PASS.
- **Cover-the-label:** "the gold star," "the fan," "the phoenix," "the umbrella," "the
  streamers" — five nameable-from-silhouette-alone terminations. PASS.
- **One-sentence:** pinwheel / sail-fan / weathervane-bird / temple-parasol /
  prayer-streamers — five different real wind/temple objects, no shared noun. PASS.
- **Eye-catch spread:** two high-value GOLD hero crowns (#1 reward-star, #3
  hero-creature) + the boldest FOOTPRINT (#2 fan) + the boldest CONVEX shape (#4 dome)
  + the only MOTION/colour crown (#5 ribbons). Every one out-punches the retired
  windmills' quieter sail-X / drum-stack / paper-disc / bamboo-cage. GATE MET.

### Set-level FIX — the ONE cluster to police in round 1
Three of five crowns (**#1 star, #2 fan, #4 parasol**) are **wide, bilateral,
symmetric masses that spill into both gutters**. This is the exact structure of the
retired set's three-circle cluster — acceptable BECAUSE #3 (figural) and #5 (airy
tails) break it, and BECAUSE the three wide crowns are pinned on independent edge
signatures. But every round-1 render must protect those pins so the trio never
collapses:
- **#1 star = POINTED radial tips + GOLD** (a spiky sunburst rosette; if the tips
  soften it drifts toward the fan).
- **#2 fan = FLAT-BASED scalloped half-sweep + CANVAS/light** (a 2-D fan with a hard
  bottom edge; if it domes it becomes the parasol).
- **#4 parasol = ROUNDED convex dome + a hanging SCALLOPED SKIRT** (3-D canopy; the
  skirt fringe is its unique tell — lean on it so it never reads as a flat fan).

## KEEP + FIX per survivor (fold directly into round 1)

**1. `vane-star-mill` — KEEP:** the most literal spin-read and the highest-value gold
hit; the ziggurat's flat top step is the perfect pinwheel mount and its stepped
blackout is unique. Clean centred flip.
**FIX:** at 58px, ~10 curved vanes will fuse into a gold blob — cap to **6–8 bold
swept vanes** with a clear lit→shadow value break edge-to-edge so it reads as *caught
mid-spin*, not a static medallion. Keep the `_vermilion` back-disc peeking between
vanes for the rotation tell. Guard the star vs the shipped/retired open sail-X: it
must stay a SOLID gilt rosette with air only as thin slots between vanes, never four
bare canvas arms.

**2. `sail-fan-mill` — KEEP:** the boldest silhouette footprint and an unmistakable
scallop edge; plainly a wind-catching sail array; clean bilateral flip.
**FIX:** 11 overlapping leaves will smear into one solid arc at 58px and lose the "fan
of blades" read — drop to **7–9 leaves** and make the SCALLOP of the leading edge the
carried tell (deep, regular notches), with a per-leaf lit→shadow ramp across the sweep
so it reads as one rotating vane-sheet, not a painted arc. Keep the `_vermilion`
tip-band as the focal that ties fan to shrine. Confirm the fan hub is dead-centred on
`cx` so the hung twin keeps a clean fan.

**3. `phoenix-vane-mill` — KEEP:** the set's charm ceiling and its only creature — a
premium, characterful hero the others can't touch, and a night-halo showpiece.
**FIX (pin the mirror NOW, do not discover it in render):** build the phoenix as a
**spread-winged, vertically-near-symmetric MEDALLION** — wings sweeping UP, tail
streamers trailing DOWN, head/neck centred in low profile — so the vertical flip still
reads as a phoenix on the hung twin. Do **NOT** ship the "diving-phoenix upside-down
hung twin" as the primary plan: an inverted bird glimpsed at 58px in a scrolling field
is a charm/legibility gamble the whole consistent family can't take. Second: the
figure must carry on OUTLINE alone at 58px — silhouette the spread wings + tail plume
boldly; treat feather detail as close-up reward only. Batter-cap the slim spire so its
shoulder still holds ≥PIPE_W/2 and the masonry (not the bird) fills the collision
column.

**4. `parasol-crown-mill` — KEEP:** the boldest single convex shape, warm and regal,
and the cleanest flip in the set. Holds the "one smooth dome" pole alone.
**FIX:** the mill-read is the softest of the four keepers — a static dome can read as
just a roof/umbrella. Sell ROTATION: give the ribbed panels a clear lit→shadow
banding that implies a turning canopy, and make the **scalloped hanging fringe** deep
and rhythmic (it's both the unique tell vs the fan AND the "it turns" cue as the skirt
catches wind). Watch the opposite fill risk the GD flagged — keep the squat pedestal
from over-widening the plinth beyond the 58px column.

**5. `streamer-whirl-mill` — KEEP:** the only soft/motion/colour pole; it's what keeps
a scrolling field of otherwise-rigid crowns from feeling metallic and samey, and it
owns the richest `_vermilion`/`horizon` story.
**FIX (highest 58px risk in the quintet — this is the prayer-flag trap):** thin tapering
ribbons are the first thing to alias/vanish at gameplay scale and to read as festival
bunting, not a mill. So: cap to **~5 BOLD ribbons**, each wide enough at the mast to
survive 1× with a high-contrast `_vermilion` core, some snapped taut to imply the
WHIRL (rotation), not limp sag. Anchor the crown with a solid silhouette mass — the
pavilion box + bronze mast must read on their own so the crown never disappears when
the ribbons thin. Lay the whirl BALANCED/radial about the mast so the vertical flip
still reads as a whirl on both halves.

## The 2 culls — why, and gap coverage

- **CULL `roof-turbine-mill` (#6):** fails both gates — a nested pagoda-eave stack
  reads as ARCHITECTURE, not a spinning mechanism (weakest "it turns" tell of the
  seven), and it is the crown NEAREST the 11 shipped pagodas + Songyue towers, so it's
  the lowest-differentiation add in the whole set. Its poles are covered: the
  "rounded/conical crown mass" reading is held by #4's dome, and the faceted-tower body
  is the least valuable body to lose precisely because towers already ship in force.
  **No gap; no replacement.**
- **CULL `furin-ring-mill` (#7):** fails the mill gate (hanging bells = decoration, not
  a wind mechanism — the prayer-flag failure repeating) and the eye-catch gate (a row
  of tiny cups is the fussiest crown at 58px and sparkles into noise), with the
  second-worst mirror in the set. Its one genuine asset — a scalloped/pendant HANGING
  edge — survives in #4's scalloped parasol skirt. Its straight-drum body reading is
  the least missed (drums risk reading as stacked pots). **No gap; no replacement.**

I am deliberately NOT back-filling to a sixth. Five obviously-a-mill, maximally-spread,
high-wow crowns beat seven where two whisper. **If** the user later wants a sixth to
break the wide-bilateral-crown cluster, the right future add is a **LATERAL /
asymmetric horizontal mechanism** — a side-mounted paddle bank or tilting
louver-shutter vane, echoing the off-axis energy of the retired waterwheel — NOT a
rescue of the bells or the eave-stack. Not needed to lock this set.

## Guard vs the retired windmill sibling set (police in every round)
- retired pavilion sail-X (open 4-arm cross) → #1 stays a SOLID 360° gilt rosette, #2
  stays a FILLED overlapping fan sweep. Neither is four bare open sails.
- retired mani drum-stack → none of the five stack copper cylinders (the culled
  furin/roof-turbine were the only stack risks).
- retired shoji glowing paper disc → nothing here is a flat emissive disc; glow stays
  clamped to the gated night halo on METAL only.
- retired junk-sail bamboo cage → no open lattice/cage crown in the five.
