VERDICT: SHIP-READY

# TOTEM / ancestral-idol family — Brainstorm Critique (art-director, Phase A)

Strong, disciplined set. All seven are genuinely conceived KINDS with their own
signature primitive and a stated column-fill story, and the GD did the honest
work of pre-flagging the moai/olmec basalt collision. The set does NOT yield
seven distinct-enough towers — it clusters two basalt heavy-mass heads, and it
carries two carved-wood poles that share a material lane. But it cleanly yields
**5 maximally-distinct, feasible, on-brief towers** that keep both required
anchors and span six finish families across five silhouette poles. Brainstorm
locks — proceed to mature the five below.

One hard external fact drives the top cull: the family ALREADY SHIPS a
`moai-monolith` landmark whose blackout is a *bulbous, knobbly stacked-ovoid
head-stack* (brow/nose bumps breaking the outline). That is almost exactly
`olmec_colossal`'s silhouette (a bulbous bead-string of helmeted heads). So
`olmec` doesn't just twin the internal `moai_ancestor` — it twins a tower
already on the field. That makes it the uncontested first cut, and it also
green-lights the NEW `moai_ancestor` precisely because it is gaunt/smooth/planar,
i.e. the opposite of the bulbous shipped moai. Keep that contrast sacred in the
per-concept loop: the new moai must read gaunt in blackout, or it collapses into
the tower we already have.

---

## Ranking (all 7)

1. **`totem_formline`** — REQUIRED anchor (the painted Pacific-NW "amazing native
   faces" pole) and the only winged/painted lane. Highest color juice in the set;
   its wings-out zig-zag is the most un-pagoda blackout here. **PURSUE.**
2. **`moai_ancestor`** — REQUIRED anchor (high-fidelity dark basalt). Only
   gaunt-smooth-planar lane; silhouette-carried read (nose-ridge + jaw notch +
   offset pukao), safest column fill. **PURSUE.**
3. **`jade_serpent`** — Uncontested angular-stepped-glossy lane. Only lapidary
   finish, only 90° fret profile, bold high-juice face (gold rings + fang bar).
   Reads apart from every carved-wood/stone concept. **PURSUE.**
4. **`kota_reliquary`** — Only metallic finish and the only open-frame body; the
   oval-and-crescent-on-a-diamond blackout exists nowhere else, and the all-accent
   palette gives a free night-glow landmark. Carries the one real FILL risk (the
   lozenge void) — solvable, see FIX. **PURSUE.**
5. **`tiwanaku_stele`** — Only flat-slab format: the single most contrasting
   blackout in the family (a wide tablet against a field of vertical poles/heads),
   and therefore the highest-value silhouette to keep. Lowest intrinsic juice and
   the one face at real 58px risk — needs the hero-face + bold-halo FIX to avoid
   the "blank grey domino" trap. **PURSUE (conditional on round-1 juice).**
6. **`sepik_spirit`** — Genuinely excellent single concept (the spiky/hooked lane
   is high-juice and its own primitive), but the SET is stronger without it: its
   material (carved wood) is already owned by the required `totem_formline`
   anchor, and in gross blackout it is still a vertical pole, not a new format.
   Redundant-material + pole-shaped = the cheapest lane to drop for maximum spread.
   **CULL — first alternate / replacement (see gap note).**
7. **`olmec_colossal`** — Bulbous basalt head-stack. Collides on TWO axes: with
   the internal `moai_ancestor` (both basalt heads) and, more damningly, with the
   ALREADY-SHIPPED `moai-monolith` landmark (both bulbous stacked heads). The
   greener/warmer tint is not enough to save a silhouette we already own.
   **CULL.**

## The 5 to pursue — silhouette poles + why the quintet is maximally distinct

| Slug | Silhouette pole (blackout) | Finish family | Tip / gap-end |
|---|---|---|---|
| `totem_formline` | winged zig-zag (wings + beaks out) | painted wood (red/black/teal) | thunderbird wing-crown |
| `moai_ancestor` | gaunt smooth vertical (nose-ridge + jaw notch) | raw grey basalt + one red drum | offset scoria pukao |
| `jade_serpent` | stepped-angular (90° fret notches + fang snout) | glossy jade-green + gold + obsidian | fret crown |
| `kota_reliquary` | oval-and-crescent on an open diamond | hammered brass + copper (metal) | half-moon lunette |
| `tiwanaku_stele` | flat broad slab (tablet + ray spokes) | bare grey andesite + cinnabar traces | ray-haloed lintel |

**Blackout:** winged-zig-zag / gaunt-pole / stepped-orthogonal / oval-crescent-
diamond / flat-wide-slab — five unmistakable solid shapes, and crucially only ONE
is a flat non-pole (tiwanaku) and only one is an open frame (kota), so the field
never reads as five verticals. PASS.
**Cover-the-label (finish):** painted wood / raw grey stone / glossy mineral /
sheet metal / incised grey stone. Six of the six named finish poles are present
across five slots; the only material repeat is grey stone (moai + tiwanaku) and
those two are silhouette-polarized (round gaunt pole vs wide flat slab) and
palette-separated (monochrome basalt vs cinnabar-traced andesite). PASS.
**Swap:** `_ovoid`/`_u_form`, plane-relief+`_pukao`, `_fret_band`/`_serpent_jaw`,
`_crescent`/`_lozenge_frame`, `_ray_halo`-on-slab — no primitive survives on
another body. PASS.
**One-sentence:** "totem with its wings out" / "smooth stone ancestor with a red
topknot" / "green fanged serpent behind stone frets" / "a shining brass mask on a
diamond" / "a carved standing stone tablet." Five nouns, no overlap. PASS.

Distinct from shipped: none is a pagoda (no eave stacks/finials), none is a
skull/bone column, and none is the tiny single-face `stone_face` dressing or the
plain `menhir` — every one here is a full stacked-idol tower with its own
material. Confirmed clear of the shipped `moai-monolith` too, via the gaunt-vs-
bulbous pin above (police it hard in `moai_ancestor` round 1).

## Per-survivor KEEP + FIX (sharpen into round 1)

### `totem_formline`  (anchor — do not dilute)
- **KEEP:** the wings-out zig-zag blackout (its whole identity); the ovoid-built
  bold rounded shape language; teal borrowing `horizon` so it warms at dawn/dusk;
  the 2-dot teal-on-black thumbnail fallback (survives 58px cleanly).
- **FIX:**
  1. Drive the red/black/teal off VALUE, not hue alone — accessibility + night
     survival. Confirm ~25%+ value separation between `_formline_red` and
     `_formline_black` at 1x on the night palette; a colorblind viewer must read
     the crest by value even if red/teal muddy.
  2. Wings/beaks are gutter overhang ONLY — verify the cedar core still fills the
     full 58px column behind them so the flyable gap never reads as bridged.
  3. Formline ovoids will crowd at 58px — cap each face to eye-pair + one lip
     `_u_form`; let interior split-U feathering be close-up reward, not small-size
     load. The wing-span silhouette must carry the read, not the paint detail.

### `moai_ancestor`  (anchor — gaunt is the whole point)
- **KEEP:** trapezoid heads butting edge-to-edge (safest fill in the set); the
  hollow `_lit_niche` socket as the "living eye" night-glow; the single red pukao
  as the only hue (clean focal); height-adaptive head count with a hero head at 70px.
- **FIX:**
  1. **Pin gaunt vs the shipped `moai-monolith`.** In blackout the new tower MUST
     read as a smooth near-straight vertical with sharp nose-ridge/jaw notches —
     NOT a knobbly bulbous ovoid stack, or it becomes the moai we already ship.
     Keep facets chisel-flat and the edge tight.
  2. Resolve the ceiling mirror now: an inverted stack of upside-down faces reads
     creepy. Either root the top heads facing "up" from the ceiling or commit to a
     symmetric totem — decide before render, don't discover it.
  3. Relief (sockets, nose, lip) mushes at 58px — guarantee the ancestor reads on
     OUTLINE + brow-shadow + 2 socket dots alone; treat carved planes as close-up
     reward.

### `jade_serpent`
- **KEEP:** the orthogonal fret-notch blackout (only hard-geometric lane); glossy
  `_jade_lit` specular as the finish tell; the fanged `_serpent_jaw` with a
  `_lit_niche` throat (night = lit maw); the 2 gold-ring-dots + fang-bar fallback.
- **FIX:**
  1. Keep the fret steps CRISP, regular and square (machine-cut) so the stepped
     edge can never be misread as organic lumps — this is what buys it distance
     from every carved concept in blackout.
  2. Gloss restraint: one raking specular band per mask, not per facet, or the
     jade goes plasticky and noisy at 1x. Value structure must still read the mask
     if the green desaturates at night.
  3. Fang snout is the only overhang — keep jade block + fret-bands full-width
     behind it; confirm no gutter substitution for column fill.

### `kota_reliquary`
- **KEEP:** the oval-and-crescent-on-a-diamond blackout (unique open-frame); the
  metal finish as the only non-earthen material; all-accent palette self-glowing at
  night; crescent + 2 rivet-dots thumbnail (the crescent alone IDs it).
- **FIX:**
  1. **The lozenge is the one real FILL risk.** The GD's shadow-box fill is the
     right call — but pin it: the recessed `_gradient_rect` inside the diamond must
     carry enough VALUE that no band exceeds 12px of dead space at 58px, AND the
     brass diamond struts must stay inside the 58px column (let only the outer
     points overhang the gutters). Prove the collision column is continuous
     70–355px before adding rivets.
  2. Make the crescent+dished-oval the HERO-at-gap unit (widest, most legible);
     drop the lozenge first when short (70px), exactly as proposed.
  3. Hammered-rivet `_tile_hatch` stipple will alias to noise at 1x — keep it a
     sparse specular sparkle, not a full texture field; let the concave dish
     rim-light carry the "metal" read.

### `tiwanaku_stele`  (highest-value silhouette, highest juice risk)
- **KEEP:** the flat-slab format itself — the single most distinct blackout in the
  family and the reason it earns a slot; square-eyed rectilinear face (only
  non-almond face); slab-IS-the-column fill (robust); cinnabar-in-recess as the
  one focal hue that survives night.
- **FIX (this is the make-or-break brief):**
  1. **Beat the "blank grey domino" trap.** A flat incised plank is exactly the
     low-juice failure the obelisk was culled for in the landmark round. Round 1
     must PUSH: one big proud hero staff-god face (not a timid grid), real relief
     read through raking light, and a bold `_ray_halo` overhanging the slab edges
     so the BLACKOUT is a rayed tablet, not a bare rectangle. If round 1 still
     reads as a plain plank at 58px, this is the concept to swap out (see gap note).
  2. The square-eyed low-relief face is the one face at genuine 58px risk — force
     the 2-square-shadow-pit + halo fallback to carry it; deepen the incision
     value delta so the eyes don't vanish on the night palette.
  3. Pin it apart from `moai_ancestor` (shared grey-stone finish): lean on FORMAT
     (flat wide slab vs round gaunt pole), the square-vs-almond eye, and the
     cinnabar traces so cover-the-label still separates them.

## The 2 culled + gap coverage

- **`olmec_colossal`** — bulbous basalt head-stack that collides with BOTH the
  internal `moai_ancestor` and the already-shipped `moai-monolith` landmark.
  Redundant on silhouette AND material; the green tint can't rescue a profile the
  roster already owns. No gap left — bulbous stacked heads are covered on the field.
- **`sepik_spirit`** — excellent concept, but redundant-material (carved wood,
  already the `totem_formline` anchor's lane) and pole-shaped in gross blackout, so
  it costs the least SPREAD to drop.
  **Gap + replacement:** culling sepik removes the spiky-organic lane. That is the
  deliberate trade to keep the far-more-distinct flat-slab format. BUT `sepik_spirit`
  is the first alternate: if `tiwanaku_stele` fails its round-1 juice/read test
  (still a blank plank at 58px after FIX #1), promote `sepik_spirit` into the slot —
  it swaps a high-juice spiky pole for the at-risk flat slab, at the cost of a
  second wood finish. Hold it warm; do not start it unless tiwanaku falls.
