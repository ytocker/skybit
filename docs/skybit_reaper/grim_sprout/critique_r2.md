# GRIM SPROUT — Art-Director critique, Round 2 (final)

`VERDICT: SHIP-READY`

Round 2 closes the two gaps that held round 1 back, and it does so without
touching the one thing that was already solved. This clears the bar. Take the
residual clip into the final GD pass as a 10-minute polish item, but it is NOT
a blocker — ship-ready stands.

---

## What landed (compared to R1)

- **Comedy-of-scale — FIXED, the headline win.** Standing the snath near-vertical
  and stacking the blade TALL above the imp is exactly the correction. In the
  showcase the scythe now reads as a genuine 4–5× great-scythe dwarfing a roly-poly
  baby — the "tiny terror, huge blade" gag is instant and unmistakable, and
  critically it now survives the 1× DAY and NIGHT cells. In R1 the blade was a
  delicate ~1.3× hook leaning across the body; that "generic small reaper" risk is
  gone. This is the single most important read of the concept and it now works.
- **Blade legibility on day sky — FIXED.** The darkened spine + thicker ink keyline
  + mint under-rim do the job: in the B/W cell the crescent holds as a clearly
  separated dark-edged shape, and on the DAY inset it no longer dissolves into the
  light sky bottom. The load-bearing edge is now actually load-bearing.
- **Scary-cute face survives to 1× — FIXED.** The B/W cell confirms it: the dark
  eye-cavity + socket pair + the one oversized offset fang carry a readable FACE in
  grayscale, not two gold pinpricks that vanish. The asymmetric upward-tilted eyes
  land "cheeky baby" rather than "grim frown." The accessibility note (dark socket
  shape, not gold hue, carries the read) is satisfied.
- **Prop→pillar system — KEPT INTACT.** This is the thing I most worried the pose
  change would break. It didn't: (b)/(b') still show the snath as a clean banded
  vertical post, blade detaching to the gap-edge only, top/bottom mirroring as one
  matched obstacle, mid-strip tiling with no blade bleed. The pose work was done
  without disturbing the already-solved tiling. Good discipline.
- **House-style fidelity — KEPT.** Flat fills, ink keyline + outline pop, saturated
  orchid-violet/mint, triad sheen. Zero drift back toward the grim/desaturated
  finish. The differentiated mitts now give the "hauling something 5× my size"
  body language R1 lacked.
- **Distinctness — KEPT.** No collision with Big Reapy (giant skull), Dr. Quill
  (plague-bird), Tick-Tock (hourglass clerk), or The Hollow (void-shroud). The
  baby-imp-under-a-monster-scythe silhouette is unique in the set, and the
  scale-up sharpened rather than blurred that identity.

---

## Residual (the GD's flag) — confirmed minor, fix in final pass

The high-grip fist sitting close to the face and the snath clipping the
lower-right of the face crescent IS visible in the showcase, and it's the only
blemish I'd touch. It reads as a small "is the pole going through his cheek?"
ambiguity on close inspection. It does NOT hurt the 1× read (it disappears at
gameplay scale), so it does not gate ship-ready — but since the GD gets one more
pass, spend it here.

---

## Final-round directives (priority order, all polish — none gate ship)

1. **Resolve the snath/face-crescent clip.** Nudge the snath 2–4px right (or the
   imp 2–4px left) so the pole runs cleanly BESIDE the head silhouette instead of
   kissing the lower-right of the face crescent. The "held beside the head" read
   is the goal; right now it ambiguously overlaps. This is the one must-do.
2. **Give the high grip a hair more breathing room from the face.** Either drop the
   fist ~3px down the snath or shrink it slightly so it doesn't crowd the cheek.
   Reinforces #1 and makes the upper-grip mitt read as "fist on pole," not "fist on
   face."
3. **(Optional, if time) Confirm the hood/head-top still reads.** With the body
   shrunk, double-check the hood droop-curl + mint pom hasn't merged into a
   featureless blob at 1×; a touch more value separation at the hood seam would
   help, but it's currently acceptable.

No other changes. Do not re-tune scale, palette, blade, or the pillar cells —
they're locked. This is a single clean-up pass, then it's done.
