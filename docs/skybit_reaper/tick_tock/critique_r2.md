## FIX — final-round punch list (prioritized, all cheap)

1. **WIN THE FACE OVER THE HOURGLASS AT 1x — top priority.** The head now
   reads at showcase scale, but in BOTH 1x insets the eye still lands on the
   bright amber hourglass first and the face second. Two-part fix: (a) nudge
   the hourglass glow/amber alpha down one more notch so it stops being the
   single brightest mass, and (b) confirm the smirk's two half-lid slivers +
   one cocked mouth line survive at the 1x inset — if they smear to a flat
   smudge, thicken/space them by ~1px and drop any remaining nose tick. The
   character is the SMIRK; it must be the first thing read, even tiny.

2. **PUSH MENACE INTO THE BROW/LID, not the palette.** At showcase the face
   currently reads more "pleasant" than "smug-clerk-who-runs-out-your-clock."
   Steepen the half-lid bar a hair and deepen the cocked mouth corner so the
   expression is unmistakably a knowing smirk, not a neutral smile. This is
   the last few percent of scary-cute; the construction is all there.

3. **CONFIRM HOURGLASS HALF-WIDTH PARITY** between the boss staff and the two
   pillar piers (carried over from R1 #7 — verify it actually landed; the
   piers still look marginally wider than the staff glass). The "one literal
   shape" promise must read identical at a glance.

4. **CRADLING-SLEEVE SEAM.** The sleeve is now continuous (good) but the
   elbow where it meets the shoulder has a slightly flat dark facet that
   reads as a stuck-on tab at showcase scale. Soften that one seam so the arm
   springs cleanly from the shoulder mass.

5. **GRAYSCALE RE-CONFIRM.** After 1-2, desaturate: face should be the
   highest-contrast read on the figure, hourglass second. If the glass still
   wins in grayscale, pull its value down until the head leads.

If 1 and 2 land — face leads the read and the smirk reads as smug DEATH at
1x — this ships. Everything else is verification.
