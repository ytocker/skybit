# THE HOLLOW — round 2 critique (art director)

VERDICT: SHIP-READY

Round 2 cleared every gate from R1. The night-sky legibility fix is the headline
win: the lifted shroud (58,49,100) plus the new interior cool-violet separation
rim (132,120,205) makes the figure pop off the dark 1x NIGHT inset on its own —
the silhouette no longer depends on the ink outline, which was the brief's #1
risk and the seed's killing flaw. The squat is real (W184 > H160, lowered
shoulders, cowl lean + dropped shoulder): it now reads as an unmistakable
big-hood chibi BELL, not a tall generic reaper, and it's clearly distinct from
Grim Sprout / Big Reapy / Dr. Quill / Tick-Tock. The fattened snuffer-pole reads
as a genuine obstacle, the enlarged bell + soul-flame register at the gap edge,
and the 3-value triad sheen gives the form real dimension while staying FLAT and
saturated. Zero drift back to the soft/desaturated void. This ships.

## Strongest / weakest

STRONGEST — the night-sky separation rim. This single move converts the figure
from "half-lost on dark sky" to "self-supporting silhouette." It's the identity
of this take working at gameplay scale. KEEP exactly as tuned.

ALSO STRONG — the squat bell proportion + lowered, dropped-shoulder lean; the
flat-black star-void hood with cyan pinprick eyes (still the charm); the hard
scalloped bone hem; the fattened pole that finally reads as a real pillar post.

WEAKEST (polish-only, not blocking) — the soul-flame on the pole top now glows
large enough that on the night pillar inset the cyan-eye / pink-flame pairing is
the only thing carrying the gap cap; the brass rail bands on the pillar are a
touch close in value to the bone shaft on the darkest part of the gradient.

## Optional final-round polish (none are blockers — ship as-is is acceptable)

1. PILLAR BAND CONTRAST. On the lower (dark) portion of the night pillar, nudge
   the brass rail bands ~1 value brighter so the repeatable mid-banding stays
   crisp where the sky gradient is darkest. Minor.
2. EYE/RIM HARMONY. The cyan eyes and the violet separation rim sit close in hue
   family on the night inset; consider holding the eyes a hair brighter/cooler so
   the two cyan pinpricks remain the clear focal point over the rim. Optional.
3. FLAME RESTRAINT. The soul-flame glow is now ideal at the gap cap; resist
   growing it further next pass — any more bloom and it starts to feather, which
   would drift toward the seed's soft look. Hold the line where it is.

If the GD touches nothing but items 1-2, great; if it ships the R2 render as-is,
that is also fine. The bar is met.
