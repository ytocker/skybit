# THE HOLLOW — round 1 critique (art director)

VERDICT: ITERATE

A real recovery from the seed: the void IS flat-black with hard star pixels, the
hem IS crisply scalloped, and the cyan pinprick eyes land the "scary-cute" beat.
But the figure is fighting the dark night sky exactly where the brief said it
must win, the chibi proportions have crept toward "tall ghost," and the
prop->pillar mirror is the weakest panel on the sheet. None of these are
re-roll problems — the bones are right. One focused round fixes it.

## Ranking of what's working -> what's broken

1. STRONGEST — the hood void. Flat-black field + crisp white/cyan star pixels +
   two cyan pinprick eyes reads cosmic and eerie, not a transparency dropout.
   The night blink inset proves the eyes are load-bearing and charming. KEEP
   this whole subsystem; it's the identity.
2. STRONG — the scalloped hem + bone trim band. Hard lobes, zero feathering,
   the deliberate anti-seed correction. Reads clean even in the 1x insets. KEEP.
3. WEAK — value separation from the NIGHT sky. The shroud fill (42,35,72) sits
   only a hair above the night sky's lower gradient (toward 35,55,115). On the
   night half of the boss panel and in the 1x NIGHT inset the lower bell nearly
   melts into the sky; the 1px ink outline is doing ALL the lifting and it's not
   enough at gameplay scale. This is the brief's #1 risk and it's currently
   half-lost.
4. WEAK — chibi proportions have drifted tall. H=190/W=150 plus a 42% hood reads
   as a slender hooded figure, not a squat chibi. Next to Grim Sprout's tiny-imp
   and Big Reapy's giant-skull it doesn't own a distinct chibi mass — it reads
   "generic tall reaper," the very silhouette we're trying to re-cut.
5. WEAKEST — the PILLAR panel. The pole is a thin bone stick with brass bands;
   at 1x against the night sky it's a near-invisible vertical hairline. The
   snuffer-bell + flame cap is so small it barely registers as the prop. This
   does not yet prove a clean tileable pillar pair — it proves a thin pole.

## KEEP / FIX by lens

HOUSE-STYLE FIDELITY
- KEEP: flat triad fills, hard ink keylines, saturated cosmic palette (no
  desaturation drift — the seed problem is gone), crisp scallops.
- FIX: the top-left sheen polyline on the body/hood is so thin and so close in
  value to the fill that it barely reads — the triad's third step is nearly
  invisible at 1x. Widen the sheen band and push SHROUD_HI brighter so the form
  steps in three clear values, not two.

SCARY-CUTE MENACE
- KEEP: pinprick eyes + blink. This is the charm.
- FIX: the head tilt / weight-shift is too subtle to read as a "presenting"
  pose — the figure stands stiff and symmetrical. Exaggerate the hood lean and
  the dropped shoulder so the chibi actually cocks toward its prop.

PROP->PILLAR
- FIX: thicken the pole substantially and give the bone rail a brighter
  highlight so the post holds value as an obstacle on night sky. Enlarge the
  snuffer-bell cap and the soul-flame so the gap-edge flourish actually reads as
  a snuffer at 1x. Right now the mirror is technically present but visually thin.

1x LEGIBILITY (the gate)
- FIX: this is the round's must-fix. The lower bell loses to the night sky.
  Lift the shroud fill value and/or add a subtle cooler-violet rim/halo so the
  silhouette pops on dark sky without relying on the hairline outline.

DISTINCTNESS
- FIX: squat the proportions (see directives) so THE HOLLOW owns the "big-hood
  chibi bell" silhouette and can't be confused with a tall generic reaper or
  with the other four roster takes.

ACCESSIBILITY
- The figure currently leans on one value (dark indigo on dark sky) to carry
  legibility — that fails low-contrast and small-screen viewing. The value lift
  + sheen widening fixes this; the cyan eyes already give a safe second cue.

## Top 3 directives (in priority order)

1. WIN THE NIGHT SKY. Lift the shroud fill ~15-20% in value and add a thin
   cooler-violet rim/halo just inside the silhouette so the lower bell separates
   from the night gradient WITHOUT depending on the 1px ink outline. Prove it in
   the 1x NIGHT inset, not just the big panel.
2. SQUAT THE CHIBI. Drop H or widen W so the body reads clearly wider-than-tall
   at the hem; keep the big hood but lower the whole center of gravity so the
   silhouette is an unmistakable chibi bell, distinct from the other 4 reapers.
3. FATTEN THE PROP + ITS PILLAR. Thicken the snuffer-pole and enlarge the
   bell-cap + soul-flame so the post reads as a real obstacle and the cap reads
   as a snuffer at 1x; then re-shoot the pillar pair to actually prove a clean
   tileable mirror. While here, widen the triad sheen so the form steps in three
   visible values.
